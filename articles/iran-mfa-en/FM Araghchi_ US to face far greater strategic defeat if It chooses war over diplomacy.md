# FM Araghchi: US to face far greater strategic defeat if It chooses war over diplomacy

Published: 2026-10-21 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796425

Iran is ready to give diplomacy another chance if the US demonstrates a sincere willingness to build trust and reach a fair solution, Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi said, warning that Washington would face a far greater strategic defeat than before if it chooses war over diplomacy in a post on X.
