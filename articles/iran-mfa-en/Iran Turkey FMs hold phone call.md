# Iran Turkey FMs hold phone call

Published: 2026-09-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794504

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who was in Beijing on Wednesday, held a phone conversation with Hakan Fidan, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Turkey, during which they discussed and exchanged views on the latest developments and regional issues.
