# Baqaei: Iran does not need to adopt aggressors terrorist methods to defeat them

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797798

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baqaei said in a post on X that the Israeli regime's media apparatus and psychological warfare machine are orchestrating a campaign of false allegations to spread fear and portray Iran as a threat to ordinary citizens, stressing that Iran does not need to imitate its aggressors to defeat them.
