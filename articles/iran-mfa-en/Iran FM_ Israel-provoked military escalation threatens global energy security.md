# Iran FM: Israel-provoked military escalation threatens global energy security

Published: 2026-10-28 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796719

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi wrote on X that the destructive impact of any military adventure in the Persian Gulf, provoked by Israel, on international markets remains a major concern for gas-exporting and gas-consuming countries.
