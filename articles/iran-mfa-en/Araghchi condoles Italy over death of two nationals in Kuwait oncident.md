# Araghchi condoles Italy over death of two nationals in Kuwait oncident

Published: 2026-11-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798400

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and his Italian counterpart, Antonio Tajani, held a phone conversation on Thursday evening to discuss issues of mutual concern. During the call, Araghchi expressed regret over the deaths of two Italian nationals in a drone incident in Kuwait and extended his condolences and sympathy to the Italian government and people. Rejecting as baseless the accusations against Iran over the incident, Araghchi stressed that Iran had no role or involvement whatsoever in it. He also announced Tehran's readiness to cooperate with the governments of Kuwait and Italy to establish the origin of the incident and investigate its various aspects.
