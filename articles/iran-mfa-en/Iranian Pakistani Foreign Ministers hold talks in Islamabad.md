# Iranian Pakistani Foreign Ministers hold talks in Islamabad

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797845

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi, who is in Islamabad to attend the meeting of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO), met with Pakistani Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar on Monday afternoon. Araghchi thanked Pakistan for its warm hospitality and the successful organization of the 30th meeting of the ECO Council of Foreign Ministers, emphasizing the need to strengthen multilateral cooperation, enhance economic and trade interactions, and expand coordination among member states. The two foreign ministers exchanged views on developing and expanding bilateral relations in areas of mutual interest. Araghchi and Ishaq Dar also reviewed regional and international developments and discussed the latest diplomatic efforts aimed at safeguarding regional stability and security.
