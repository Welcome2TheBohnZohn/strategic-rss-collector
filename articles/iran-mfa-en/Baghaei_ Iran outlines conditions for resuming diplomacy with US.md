# Baghaei: Iran outlines conditions for resuming diplomacy with US

Published: 2026-09-24 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794961

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baghaei said on X that Iran and the United States exchanged messages through a Qatari mediator on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, with Iran conveying its clear conditions for reviving diplomacy, including an end to US aggression and the naval blockade, economic terrorism, and the war on all fronts.
