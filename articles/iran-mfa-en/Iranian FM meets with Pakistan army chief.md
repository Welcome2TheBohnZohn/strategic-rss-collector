# Iranian FM meets with Pakistan army chief

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797844

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi met and held talks with Field Marshal Asim Munir, Pakistan's Chief of Army Staff, in Islamabad on Monday evening. Araghchi is in Islamabad to attend the 30th Meeting of the ECO Council of Foreign Ministers. During the meeting, the two sides reviewed regional and international developments and discussed ways to expand bilateral relations in areas of mutual interest. They also exchanged views on existing challenges and threats to regional peace and security, as well as ways to strengthen cooperation to safeguard peace and stability.
