# Statement by Irans Foreign Ministry on escalation of genocide in occupied Palestine

Published: 2026-11-22 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798126

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly condemns the crimes of the Zionist regime against the oppressed people of Palestine, particularly in Gaza, and emphasizes the urgent need for effective action by the international community to stop the genocide and to hold the Zionist criminals accountable and punish them. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs also strongly condemns the crimes of the occupying regime and the terrorist acts of Zionist settlers against Palestinians in the West Bank—the most recent of which was the burning of the Al-Dahi Mosque. While Gaza has been turned into a pile of rubble and debris as a result of extensive bombings, and the displaced people of Gaza have been deprived even of access to tents to protect themselves from the cold and rain in the winter cold, Zionist military forces, by storming the center of Gaza, are seeking to intensify the genocide plan and kill more people. The inaction imposed on international institutions by the United States and the supporters of the occupying regime has led to greater insolence by this regime and the continuation of the gross violation of the fundamental rights of the Palestinian people. Without a doubt, the United States and other supporters of the occupying regime—especially Germany as the second-largest arms seller to this regime, as well as the United Kingdom, France, and Canada, which in various ways have shielded this regime from any accountability and punishment—are partners in the crimes committed in Gaza. Furthermore, all parties that were present and participated in the ceasefire agreement of 9 October 2025 in Sharm El-Sheikh bear direct responsibility for stopping the crimes of the occupying regime. The Islamic Republic of Iran recalls the responsibility of all states, in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations and international law, to support the oppressed people of Palestine in realizing their right to self-determination and liberation from the evil of occupation, apartheid, and brutal colonialism, and emphasizes that all countries, pursuant to the relevant international instruments, particularly the “Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide” and the “Four Geneva Conventions of 1949,” are obligated to take action to stop the genocide and war crimes that are continuously taking place in Gaza and the West Bank. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs also condemns the continuous obstruction by the American ruling establishment of the process of addressing the crimes committed by the Zionist regime, especially through the sanctions and intimidation of the judges of the International Criminal Court, and reminds all countries of their responsibility to prosecute and punish the leaders of the Zionist regime for the commission of international crimes. The Islamic Republic of Iran, while emphasizing its all-out support for the Palestinian people until the realization of their right to self-determination and the establishment of an independent Palestinian state with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital, calls on the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) and all Islamic countries to take immediate measures to utilize all the capacities of this organization and other international institutions to confront the genocide of the Palestinians.
