# Iranian ambassador to Poland meets FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-04 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797243

Isa Kameli, Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Poland, met and held talks with Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi on Wednesday afternoon. During the meeting, Kameli presented a report on Iran-Poland bilateral relations and developments concerning the European Union. The Foreign Minister, recalling the Islamic Republic of Iran’s policy of strengthening diplomatic approaches, the rule of law, and multilateralism at the international level, stressed the importance of making use of all opportunities to articulate Iran’s positions and expand relations with other countries.
