# Iran categorically rejects France’s baseless accusations

Published: 2026-11-10 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797490

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran has categorically rejected the French government's baseless and malicious accusations against Iran over alleged involvement in cyber activities in France, strongly condemning the French government's discredited policy of making accusations against Iran. The latest accusations against Iran, which follow a series of repeated allegations and claims aimed at diverting public attention from France's destructive policies toward Iran and the West Asian region, particularly its decades-long hosting of terrorist groups and support for the genocide of Palestinians, constitute an extremely irresponsible and despicable attempt to tarnish Iran's image and undermine its relations with other countries. Such actions serve only to please the United States and the Israeli regime, the world's biggest violators of international law. The French government's baseless allegations amount to an attempt to deflect attention from its own governance failures, its inability to meet the people's demands, and its failure to address shortcomings in the country's management systems. These are shortcomings so severe that the French authorities are even unable to control the temperature of refrigerators, yet seek to shift the blame onto foreign actors by making despicable accusations. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly advises French officials to spend their resources on addressing the livelihood and educational needs of their own people, rather than allocating huge budgets to interfere in other countries, including through the abuse of diplomatic cover and ostensibly cultural projects. The Islamic Republic of Iran, while reminding the French government of its international responsibility for actions that violate international law and France's illegal interventions against Iran, emphasizes that it will not hesitate to take the necessary and appropriate measures to safeguard its national interests and security against unlawful foreign activities.
