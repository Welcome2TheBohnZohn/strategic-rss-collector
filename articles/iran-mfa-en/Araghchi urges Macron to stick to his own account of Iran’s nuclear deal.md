# Araghchi urges Macron to stick to his own account of Iran’s nuclear deal

Published: 2026-10-23 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796481

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi, reacting to French President Emmanuel Macron’s contradictory statements on Iran’s nuclear activities, wrote on X that Macron had pledged in 2018 that France would not withdraw from the JCPOA because it had signed the deal, but now claims to have always opposed any nuclear activity in Iran. “Stick to your own account, Mr. President!” Araghchi wrote.
