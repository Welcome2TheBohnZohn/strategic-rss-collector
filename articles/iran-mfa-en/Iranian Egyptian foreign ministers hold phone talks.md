# Iranian Egyptian foreign ministers hold phone talks

Published: 2026-11-04 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797244

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty held a telephone conversation on Tuesday night. During the call, the two sides discussed regional developments and exchanged views on the latest status of ongoing diplomatic efforts to de-escalate tensions in the region.
