# ECO Foreign Ministers’ Council Meeting opens in Islamabad

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797786

The 30th session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) opened in Islamabad on Monday morning, November 16, under the chairmanship of Pakistan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar, with the participation of foreign ministers from member states, including Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi. The meeting, attended by foreign ministers and senior diplomats from ECO member states, will discuss the organization’s efforts to expand economic, trade, transportation, energy, tourism and other forms of cooperation among its 10 member countries. Discussions will also cover agenda programs and ongoing initiatives aimed at strengthening ties among member states. The ECO Vision 2035 document, which seeks to outline the organization’s cooperation prospects over the next decade, will also be reviewed. In addition to representatives of member states, senior officials from several international and regional organizations are attending the meeting, including the Secretary-General of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC), the Deputy Secretary-General of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), and the Director General of the Conference on Interaction and Confidence-Building Measures in Asia (CICA). The ECO chairmanship will also be handed over from Kazakhstan to Pakistan at the summit. Pakistan will assume the organization's rotating chairmanship for a two-year term covering 2026–2027. The Islamic Republic of Iran is scheduled to host the 18th ECO Summit in Tehran in December this year.
