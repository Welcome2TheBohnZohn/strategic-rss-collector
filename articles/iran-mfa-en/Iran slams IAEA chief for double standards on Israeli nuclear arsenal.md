# Iran slams IAEA chief for double standards on Israeli nuclear arsenal

Published: 2026-11-20 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798057

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baqaei said IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi cannot use Israel’s non-membership in the Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) to evade scrutiny of the agency’s indifference toward the Israeli regime’s nuclear arsenal and its double standards toward Iran’s nuclear program. Baqaei made the remarks in a post on X, criticizing Grossi’s justification of the agency’s different approaches to Iran’s peaceful nuclear program and Israel’s weapons-related nuclear activities.
