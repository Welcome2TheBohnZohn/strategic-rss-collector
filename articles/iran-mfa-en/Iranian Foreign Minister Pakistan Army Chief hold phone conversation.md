# Iranian Foreign Minister Pakistan Army Chief hold phone conversation

Published: 2026-09-11 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794202

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Field Marshal Asim Munir, Commander of the Pakistani Army, held a phone conversation on Thursday evening. During the call, the two sides reviewed the latest regional developments and exchanged views on the escalation of tensions and increasing insecurity in the region and their consequences for regional peace and stability. The two sides also, stressing the need for vigilance and coordination among regional countries regarding ongoing developments, emphasized the use of shared capacities to help establish lasting peace and security in the region.
