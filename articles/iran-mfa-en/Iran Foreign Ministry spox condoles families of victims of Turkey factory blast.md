# Iran Foreign Ministry spox condoles families of victims of Turkey factory blast

Published: 2026-11-11 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797627

Esmail Baqaei, Spokesperson for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, referring to the explosion incident at an iron and steel factory in Izmir Province in western Turkey, expressed the condolences and sympathy of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the families of the deceased, and to the government and people of the neighboring and friendly country. Baqaei asked God Almighty to grant mercy and forgiveness to the victims of the incident and health to the injured.
