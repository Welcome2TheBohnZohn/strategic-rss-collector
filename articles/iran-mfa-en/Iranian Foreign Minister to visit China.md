# Iranian Foreign Minister to visit China

Published: 2026-09-15 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794394

Iranian Foreign Minister to visit China Esmail Baghaei, Spokesperson for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, told reporters that Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi will travel to Beijing, China. According to Baghaei, the foreign minister will travel to Beijing on Wednesday for an official visit. During the visit, the foreign minister will hold talks and exchange views with his Chinese counterpart on bilateral relations as well as important regional and international issues.
