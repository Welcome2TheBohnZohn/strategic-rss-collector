# Iranian ambassador to Ireland meets with Foreign Minister

Published: 2026-10-14 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/795944

Es’haq Ale-Habib, Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Ireland, met with Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi on Tuesday. During the meeting, the Iranian ambassador briefed the foreign minister on the latest developments in his host country, the state of bilateral relations, and the main issues on the agenda of relations between the Islamic Republic of Iran and Ireland. The foreign minister, referring to Iran’s commitment to utilizing all diplomatic capacities to safeguard its national interests, stressed the continuation of active consultations, effectively articulating the Islamic Republic of Iran’s principled positions, and pursuing bilateral issues.
