# Iran FM to Attend Annual Meeting of Foreign Ministers of Islamic Countries

Published: 2026-10-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796087

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, departed for Baghdad, the capital of Iraq, at the head of a diplomatic delegation on Saturday morning to attend the meeting of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC). In addition to attending the meeting and explaining the positions of the Islamic Republic of Iran, the Iranian foreign minister will also hold bilateral meetings with a number of ministers participating in the gathering. The 52nd Session of the OIC Council of Foreign Ministers, themed “Dialogue and Solidarity as Two Essential Pillars for Advancing Peace and Sustainable Progress,” will be held in Baghdad on Saturday and Sunday.
