# Iranian Egyptian FMs discuss regional stability by phone

Published: 2026-10-28 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796661

Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty held a phone conversation with Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi on Tuesday morning. During the phone call, the two sides referred to the quadrilateral meeting of Iran, Pakistan, Turkey, and Saudi Arabia held in Tehran on Monday, October 26, and stressed the need to continue close consultations among regional countries to reduce tensions and establish security and stability in the region.
