# Baghaei: False-flag operations cannot conceal illegal US-Israeli wars

Published: 2026-10-08 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/795687

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baghaei wrote on X that false-flag operations cannot conceal the illegal and criminal nature of the endless wars waged by the United States and the Israeli regime, saying the perpetrators are resorting to lies as they struggle to justify their aggression against Iran. The message is as follows: False flags are an old craft: manufacture the incident, assign the blame, and harvest the consequences. In a world where the American and international public is weary of an unlawful war of aggression against Iran, and where the aggressors have no honest way to justify a war of choice, even a raft made of lies can look like salvation to those who are drowning.
