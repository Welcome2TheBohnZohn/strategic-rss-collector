# Iranian South Korean FMs discuss bilateral ties regional developments

Published: 2026-11-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798397

South Korean Foreign Minister Cho Hyun held a phone conversation with his Iranian counterpart, Seyed Abbas Araghchi, on Thursday morning to discuss bilateral issues and the latest regional developments. During the call, Araghchi warned against any foreign military presence in the region, stressing that the deployment of foreign forces would not help establish stability and security, but would instead escalate tensions and widen the scope of conflicts. He also briefed his South Korean counterpart on the latest diplomatic efforts and initiatives aimed at preserving regional peace and stability, outlining Iran's firm positions and views on the requirements and framework for achieving peace in the region. Cho, for his part, reaffirmed South Korea's commitment to supporting regional peace and stability and assured Araghchi that Seoul had no intention of intervening in or participating in the war and had no plans to deploy troops to the region. He expressed hope that a peaceful solution could be found to end the ongoing war and tensions.
