# Norwegian Deputy Foreign Minister meets with top Iranian diplomat

Published: 2026-09-23 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794798

On Tuesday at noon, on the sidelines of the annual session of the United Nations General Assembly, Andreas Motzfeldt Kravik, Deputy Foreign Minister of Norway, met with Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran. During the meeting, the latest regional and international developments, ongoing developments in West Asia, and issues of mutual interest were discussed and views were exchanged.
