# Iranian Turkmen FMs discuss regional developments in phone call

Published: 2026-10-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796611

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and his Turkmen counterpart, Rashid Meredov, held a telephone conversation on Monday afternoon to discuss key regional issues and the latest international developments. During the call, the two ministers reviewed the latest regional developments and emphasized the importance of continuing diplomatic consultations and political efforts to de-escalate tensions and establish lasting stability and security in the region.
