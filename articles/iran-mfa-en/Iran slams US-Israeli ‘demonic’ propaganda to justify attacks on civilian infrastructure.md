# Iran slams US-Israeli ‘demonic’ propaganda to justify attacks on civilian infrastructure

Published: 2026-11-18 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797921

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baqaei has condemned US-Israeli media campaigns alleging links between Iran’s missile bases and dams and spreading claims about a so-called “dirty bomb,” calling them a sinister attempt to manufacture pretexts for aggression and war crimes.
