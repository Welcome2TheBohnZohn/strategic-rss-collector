# ECO foreign ministers back Iran’s initiative to end US-Israeli imposed war

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797841

The 30th Meeting of the Council of Ministers of Foreign Affairs of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) concluded in Islamabad with the adoption of a final declaration. In one of the paragraphs of the declaration, ECO member states expressed concern over the security situation in the region (due to the US and Israeli regime’s military aggression against Iran). They voiced support for Iran’s recent initiative aimed at bringing the imposed war to a complete and permanent end. Highlighting the importance of diplomatic engagement, they also expressed support for the good offices and mediation efforts of Pakistan and Qatar. The Islamic Republic of Iran recently, as part of its responsible approach to using diplomacy to end the imposed war, conveyed through mediators its two-day plan, which includes Iran’s seven demands and conditions, as well as practical solutions for establishing peace and stability in the region. ECO member states also underscored the Islamic Republic of Iran’s position as one of the founding members of the organization and emphasized its important role in effectively contributing to the advancement of the organization’s objectives.
