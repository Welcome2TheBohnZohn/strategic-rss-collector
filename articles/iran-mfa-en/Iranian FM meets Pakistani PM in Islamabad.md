# Iranian FM meets Pakistani PM in Islamabad

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797843

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi met with Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif in Islamabad on Monday afternoon, along with other foreign ministers and senior officials attending the 30th Meeting of the ECO Council of Foreign Ministers. Sharif welcomed the foreign ministers of ECO member states, stressing the importance of strengthening regional cooperation and utilizing the member countries' extensive capacities to expand economic, trade and transit ties among neighboring and regional countries. The foreign ministers thanked Pakistan for hosting the summit and successfully organizing its 30th session, emphasizing the need to strengthen multilateral cooperation, expand economic and trade relations, and promote greater convergence among member states. Araghchi, referring to the productive discussions at the summit, particularly on Palestine and the strong condemnation of the genocide in the occupied Palestinian territories, stressed the urgent need for the international community and Islamic countries to address the catastrophic humanitarian situation in Gaza and the West Bank. Referring to a clause in the summit's final statement supporting Iran's proposed initiative to end the imposed war, Araghchi also praised Pakistan's efforts as a mediator in Iran-US negotiations. Sharif, in turn, underscored Pakistan's and the Muslim world's commitment to the Palestinian cause, describing the crimes committed against the Palestinian people in Gaza and the West Bank as a catastrophe for humanity and emphasizing everyone's moral and humanitarian responsibility to confront the continuation of these crimes.
