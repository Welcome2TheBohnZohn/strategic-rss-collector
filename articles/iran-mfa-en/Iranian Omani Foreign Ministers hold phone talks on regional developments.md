# Iranian Omani Foreign Ministers hold phone talks on regional developments

Published: 2026-11-05 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797245

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, and Sayyid Badr Albusaidi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Sultanate of Oman, held a telephone conversation on Thursday. During the call, the Iranian and Omani foreign ministers discussed regional developments and exchanged views on ongoing diplomatic efforts to de-escalate tensions in the region.
