# Phone call between Foreign Ministers of the Islamic Republic of Iran and Saudi Arabia

Published: 2026-09-11 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794200

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a phone conversation with Saudi Foreign Minister Faisal bin Farhan, during which they exchanged views and discussed the latest regional developments. During the conversation, the two sides, referring to the escalation of tensions and increasing insecurity in the region, stressed the need to continue cooperation and diplomatic efforts to prevent the tensions from spreading further and to restore stability and security to the region.
