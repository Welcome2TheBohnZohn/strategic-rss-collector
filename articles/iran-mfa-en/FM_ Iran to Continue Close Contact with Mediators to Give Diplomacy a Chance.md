# FM: Iran to Continue Close Contact with Mediators to Give Diplomacy a Chance

Published: 2026-10-28 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796779

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi said in a post on X that Iran would continue close and sustained contact with mediators to explore ideas for implementing the seven-day plan in less than a week, while the country’s armed forces remain fully prepared to defend the homeland.
