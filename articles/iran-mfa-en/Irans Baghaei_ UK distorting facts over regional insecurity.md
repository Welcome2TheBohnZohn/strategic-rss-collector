# Irans Baghaei: UK distorting facts over regional insecurity

Published: 2026-10-15 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796052

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baghaei said on X that Britain is distorting facts to conceal its role in regional insecurity, rejecting UK Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband’s characterization of Iran’s defensive actions as “aggression.” The chaos engulfing our region is the consequence of unlawful interventions, including those backed or contributed to by the UK. For the UK Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband to portray Iran as the “aggressor” after two rounds of unlawful aggression against it in just nine months, even as it pursued negotiations, is a grotesque reversal of victim and aggressor. It marks yet another low in the UK’s long record of hostile campaigns against Iran. It is as absurd as portraying Nazi Germany as a “defender of peace” while casting the UK and its allies in WWII as the aggressors.
