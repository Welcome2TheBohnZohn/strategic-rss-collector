# Iranian Foreign Ministry statement condemning Israeli military attacks on Lebanon

Published: 2026-11-25 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798391

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly condemns the Israeli regime’s airstrikes and artillery attacks on various areas in southern Lebanon and the destruction of civilian infrastructure in the country, and emphasizes Lebanon’s inherent right to defend its territorial integrity and national sovereignty. The escalation of the Israeli regime’s acts of aggression against Lebanon, coinciding with the celebrations marking the country’s independence, constitutes a flagrant violation of the UN Charter and the November 2024 ceasefire agreement. It also lays bare the undeniable truth that the Zionist enemy poses the greatest threat to Lebanon’s independence, territorial integrity, security and stability. Undoubtedly, the Israeli regime’s commission of grave international crimes, including genocide, and its flagrant violations of international law in Gaza and the West Bank, Lebanon, Syria and other countries across the region warrant a firm response from the United Nations and the international community. However, the United States, as Israel’s biggest supporter and accomplice, has prevented any effective action in this regard. The Security Council’s inaction in the face of the occupation of vast areas of Lebanon and the continuation of acts of aggression against the country, carried out with the support and complicity of the United States and certain other Western countries, has not only undermined the credibility of this body but has also emboldened the Israeli regime to further violate the law and commit grave crimes. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs also condemns the United States’ destructive interference in Lebanon’s internal affairs and warns that Washington’s moves to establish mechanisms lacking national consensus in Lebanon, aimed at paving the way for the advancement of the Israeli regime’s sinister and expansionist objectives, not only fail to serve Lebanon’s national rights and interests and cannot prevent the Israeli regime’s occupation, expansionism, aggression and terrorism against Lebanon, but will also pose serious threats to the country’s independence, security, stability and national cohesion. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, while paying tribute to the steadfastness and resilience of the noble people of Lebanon in confronting the Israeli regime’s military aggression and expansionism, reaffirms the Islamic Republic of Iran’s support for Lebanon in its efforts to defend its independence, national sovereignty and territorial integrity.
