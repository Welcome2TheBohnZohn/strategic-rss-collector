# Iranian Foreign Ministry spox expresses sympathy with families of Egypt fire victims

Published: 2026-11-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798396

Following a fire caused by the overturning and subsequent ignition of a fuel tanker in Egypt’s Giza Governorate, which left more than 50 people dead and injured, Esmail Baqaei, Spokesperson for the Iranian Foreign Ministry, expressed the condolences and sympathy of the government and people of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the bereaved families, as well as to the government and people of Egypt. He also prayed to Almighty God to grant patience and fortitude to the bereaved families and a speedy recovery to those injured.
