# Algerian Minister of State for Foreign Affairs meets Iranian Foreign Minister

Published: 2026-10-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796149

As part of the Iranian foreign minister’s consultations with countries participating in the annual session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC), Sofiane Chaib, Algeria’s Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, met and held talks with Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Iran’s Foreign Minister, on Saturday afternoon. The two sides exchanged views on Iran-Algeria bilateral relations and issues concerning the Muslim world, particularly Palestine and the expansionism of the Zionist regime. Referring to the continued occupation and genocide perpetrated by the Zionist regime in occupied Palestine, Araghchi described the Palestinian issue as the most important issue facing the Muslim world and humanity, stressing the need to strengthen Islamic unity and solidarity to end the Zionist regime’s impunity. The Iranian foreign minister also briefed the Algerian side on developments in West Asia and the United States’ interventionist and destabilizing actions in the Persian Gulf region.
