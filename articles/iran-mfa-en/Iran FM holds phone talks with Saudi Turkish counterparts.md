# Iran FM holds phone talks with Saudi Turkish counterparts

Published: 2026-09-06 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793942

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held separate phone calls with Saudi Foreign Minister Faisal bin Farhan and Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan on Saturday evening to discuss bilateral issues, regional developments, and the latest diplomatic efforts to restore stability and address insecurity imposed on the Strait of Hormuz as a result of US aggressive actions.
