# Baghaei: World Will Not Be Fooled by US False Narratives Hypocritical Propaganda

Published: 2026-09-18 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794522

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Bagaei, in a post on X, said the world would not be fooled by Washington’s “false narratives and hypocritical propaganda,” criticizing the US for waging an economic war against Iran while making a show of restricting Iranian officials from shopping at a wholesale store in New York during the upcoming UN General Assembly.
