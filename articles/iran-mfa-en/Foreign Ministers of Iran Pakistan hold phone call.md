# Foreign Ministers of Iran Pakistan hold phone call

Published: 2026-10-18 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796190

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who is in Baghdad to attend the 52nd Session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC), held a phone conversation with Mohammad Ishaq Dar, Pakistan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs, at noon on Sunday. During the phone call, the two sides reviewed a number of bilateral issues and the latest regional developments, and exchanged views on diplomatic efforts aimed at de-escalating tensions and establishing stability in West Asia.
