# Iran spox: Media hype cannot shield US from responsibility for war crimes state terrorism

Published: 2026-11-12 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797628

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has rejected renewed baseless accusations of terrorism against Iran, saying on X that the United States cannot evade responsibility for its war crimes and acts of state terrorism against Iran through media campaigns and victim-playing.
