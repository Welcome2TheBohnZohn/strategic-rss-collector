# Iranian Foreign Minister condemns Israeli regime’s lies about Iran’s Pharmaceutical industry

Published: 2026-11-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798394

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi issued a statement condemning the Israeli regime’s fabrications concerning Iran’s pharmaceutical industry. The statement reads: The Israeli regime has refused to join any international convention prohibiting nuclear, chemical, or biological weapons. It possesses hundreds of nuclear warheads, has attacked seven countries in the region, and occupied parts of the territories of three countries. Over the past year alone, Israel has twice dragged the United States into a failed war against Iran. Now, this same warmongering regime shamelessly accuses Iran, a country that is a member of the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) and the Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW), and which was itself a victim of chemical weapons supplied by West Germany to Saddam Hussein’s regime, of producing and concealing such heinous weapons in pharmaceutical factories!! The purpose behind making such an outrageous allegation is clear: to deceive the United States into being dragged into another war in pursuit of Israel’s interests, while simultaneously putting Iranian patients at risk.
