# Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan held a phone conversation.

Published: 2026-09-13 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794247

During the conversation, the latest regional developments were reviewed, and the two sides stressed the need to prevent an escalation of tensions and the expansion of the crisis in the region, as well as the need to continue consultations and diplomatic efforts to help establish stability and calm
