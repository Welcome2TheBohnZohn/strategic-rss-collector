# Foreign Ministers of Iran Brazil hold phone conversation

Published: 2026-10-20 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796299

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, and Mauro Vieira, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Brazil, held a telephone conversation to exchange views on the most important regional issues and the latest international developments. During the call, Iran’s Foreign Minister briefed Mauro Vieira on the latest regional developments. The two sides emphasized the importance of continuing diplomatic consultations and political efforts to de-escalate tensions and establish lasting stability and security in the region.
