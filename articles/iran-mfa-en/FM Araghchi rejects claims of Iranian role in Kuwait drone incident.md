# FM Araghchi rejects claims of Iranian role in Kuwait drone incident

Published: 2026-11-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798399

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a phone conversation with his Kuwaiti counterpart, Sheikh Jarrah Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah, on Thursday afternoon. During the call, Araghchi categorically rejected claims that Iran had carried out a drone attack on Kuwait, stressing that the Islamic Republic had no involvement whatsoever in the incident. Expressing regret over the human casualties and damage caused by the incident, Araghchi also announced Iran's readiness to conduct a joint investigation with the Kuwaiti side to thoroughly examine the incident and identify its source and the parties involved.
