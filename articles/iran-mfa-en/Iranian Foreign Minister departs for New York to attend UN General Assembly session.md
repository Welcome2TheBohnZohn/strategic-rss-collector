# Iranian Foreign Minister departs for New York to attend UN General Assembly session

Published: 2026-09-20 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794623

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, left Tehran for New York on Sunday afternoon to attend the United Nations General Assembly session. It should be noted that the Foreign Minister will make a brief stop in Doha en route to New York, during which he will hold consultations on the latest regional developments.
