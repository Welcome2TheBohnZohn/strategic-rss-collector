# Baghaei slams Canada PM’s Defense of Trump’s war rhetoric

Published: 2026-09-24 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794962

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baghaei, responding on X to Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney’s remarks defending the US president’s military aggression and war rhetoric against Iran, said Carney should have condemned the aggression and warmongering instead of justifying it and describing threats to destroy a country as the “language of wartime.”
