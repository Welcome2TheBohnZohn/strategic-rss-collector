# Iran FM slams US for undermining global chemical weapons watchdog

Published: 2026-11-25 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798315

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi wrote on X that the United States is effectively paralyzing the global watchdog for chemical weapons by obstructing the OPCW's work, warning that Washington is knowingly endangering international peace and security. He called the move “the height of shamelessness.”

The U.S. is so concerned about chemical arms that it’s crippling the world’s chemical weapons watchdog. OPCW may be forced to suspend work and scale back vital programs. By obstructing its mandate, the U.S. is deliberately putting global peace and security at risk. Shameful. https://t.co/4MdSkUODze — Seyed Abbas Araghchi (@araghchi) November 24, 2026
