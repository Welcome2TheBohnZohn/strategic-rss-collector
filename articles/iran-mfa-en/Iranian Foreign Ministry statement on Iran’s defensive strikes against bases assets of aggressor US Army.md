# Iranian Foreign Ministry statement on Iran’s defensive strikes against bases assets of aggressor US Army

Published: 2026-11-19 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798053

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly condemns the aggressive actions carried out by the terrorist US Army against locations along the southern coasts of Iran over the past several days. In response to these acts of aggression, the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran, in exercise of their inherent right to self-defense and in line with their duty to safeguard the country’s national sovereignty and territorial integrity, launched forceful and decisive defensive strikes against Patriot air defense systems and drone and missile launch platforms belonging to the US Army at two of its bases in the region, which had served as the origin and support bases for aggressive operations against the Islamic Republic of Iran. The defensive measures taken by the Islamic Republic of Iran in response to the US armed attacks have been carried out within the framework of its inherent right to self-defense and with the aim of countering the sources and supporting infrastructure of the aggression. It is self-evident that the legal and international responsibility for the consequences of this situation rests with the United States, which, by violating the fundamental principles of international law and resorting unlawfully to the use of force, has endangered regional peace and security. While reaffirming its respect for the national sovereignty and territorial integrity of regional countries, the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly urges the governments of the region to take the necessary measures to prevent aggressors from continuing to use their territories, bases, and facilities located in their countries to plan, prepare, and carry out acts of aggression against Iran. The failure of regional countries to prevent the United States from using their territories and facilities to prepare and carry out acts of aggression against Iran is not only contrary to the principle of good neighborliness, but also constitutes an “act of aggression” under Paragraph (f) of Article 3 of the Annex to United Nations General Assembly Resolution 3314, fully entitling and authorizing Iran to take defensive measures against them. It should be noted that, to date, the Islamic Republic of Iran has acted with the utmost restraint, out of consideration for its relations with neighboring countries and the interests of the Islamic Ummah, and has exclusively targeted US military bases and assets in those countries in its defensive strikes. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran once again underscores the responsibility of the United Nations, particularly the Security Council and the Secretary-General, to fulfill their duties in accordance with the UN Charter. Silence and inaction in the face of the continued violation of the Charter’s fundamental principles, particularly the prohibition on the use of force enshrined in Article 2(4), by a permanent member of the Security Council will not only undermine the credibility and standing of the United Nations, but will also pave the way for further acts of aggression and an escalating threat to international peace and security. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs also condemns the irresponsible approach of certain regional parties which, disregarding the realities on the ground and turning a blind eye to the US acts of aggression against Iran, criticize Iran’s legitimate self-defense operations. It calls on these countries to prevent the United States from using their territories and bases in the region to prepare and carry out acts of aggression against Iran, rather than complaining about Iran for exercising its right to self-defense. The Islamic Republic of Iran, reaffirming its firm determination to defend its sovereignty, independence, and territorial integrity, declares that any repetition of aggression or hostile action against Iran will be met with a resolute and regret-inducing response from the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran.
