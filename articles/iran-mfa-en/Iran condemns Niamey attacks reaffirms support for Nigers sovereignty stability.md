# Iran condemns Niamey attacks reaffirms support for Nigers sovereignty stability

Published: 2026-09-03 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793747

Esmail Baghaei, the Spokesperson for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, condemned the violent incidents and attacks in Niamey, the capital of Niger, which resulted in the death and injury of a number of military personnel and civilians. Expressing sympathy with the families of the victims and declaring solidarity with the people and government of Niger, the Foreign Ministry spokesperson emphasized the Islamic Republic of Iran's support for the Nigerien government's efforts to protect the country's independence and national sovereignty, maintain security and stability, and advance its national development programs.
