# Iranian Jordanian foreign ministers hold phone talks

Published: 2026-11-05 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797289

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a phone conversation with Ayman Safadi, Jordan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister, on Thursday afternoon. During the call, Araghchi offered his condolences on the passing of Safadi’s mother, and the two sides exchanged views on the latest regional developments.
