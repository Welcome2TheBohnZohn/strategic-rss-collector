# Iran offers condolences to families of victims of Islamabad hospital fire

Published: 2026-08-26 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793299

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baqaei, referring to a fire in the maternity ward of the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) hospital in Islamabad, expressed the condolences and sympathy of the Iranian government and people to the families of the victims, as well as to the government and people of the neighboring and friendly country. Baqaei prayed to Almighty God to grant patience and endurance to the grieving fathers and mothers and wished the injured a swift recovery and good health.
