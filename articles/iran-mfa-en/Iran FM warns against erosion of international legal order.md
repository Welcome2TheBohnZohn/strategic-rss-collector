# Iran FM warns against erosion of international legal order

Published: 2026-11-13 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797654

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi stressed the need to uphold international law at a time when war criminals are seeking to destroy it, posing the question of what would remain of the international legal order if states were free to attack one another based on their own assessments of potential threats. He made the remarks in an article published by Japan’s Kyodo News, which he highlighted in a post on X.
