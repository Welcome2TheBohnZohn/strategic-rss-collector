# Iran strongly condemns Bahraini rulers’ issuance of unjust sentences against clerics citizens

Published: 2026-11-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797847

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran considers the issuance of unjust sentences against a group of Bahraini clerics and citizens on spurious pretexts to be a systematic and widespread violation of human rights and strongly condemns it. The imposition of unjust life and lengthy prison sentences and the confiscation of religious scholars’ property, shortly after the disgraceful remarks made by the US Secretary of State against clerics and prominent figures of the Shia world, demonstrate the Bahraini rulers’ alignment with the US establishment’s efforts to foment religious discord. Undoubtedly, holding show trials and using the judicial system as a cover for the collective punishment and harassment of clerics and ordinary Bahraini citizens can in no way conceal the inhumane nature of this action or its incompatibility with the fundamental principles and norms of human rights. The leveling of entirely baseless and unfounded accusations, including alleged links with Iran, as grounds for the criminal prosecution of Bahraini citizens and their collective sentencing to lengthy prison terms, is a clear indication of the Bahraini rulers’ abuse of the judiciary to suppress any rights advocacy. This, in turn, will undermine the credibility of Bahrain’s judicial system, render the issued verdicts null and void, and further erode the legitimacy and public acceptance of the ruling class in the country. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, while firmly rejecting the baseless allegations leveled against Iran, calls on the Bahraini rulers to end their repression of the people and religious scholars and to respect their human and civil rights in accordance with human rights standards and Islamic teachings. It also emphasizes that a policy of repression and intimidation can in no way prevent the people of Bahrain from pursuing their legitimate and rightful demands.
