# Iran Oman FMs hold phone talks on bilateral regional issues

Published: 2026-08-21 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793009

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, and Sayyid Badr Albusaidi, Foreign Minister of the Sultanate of Oman, held a telephone conversation on Friday, exchanging views on bilateral relations and regional developments. The Iranian and Omani foreign ministers underscored the need to continue consultations between the two countries to help strengthen peace and stability in the region.
