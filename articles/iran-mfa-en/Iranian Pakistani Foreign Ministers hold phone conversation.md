# Iranian Pakistani Foreign Ministers hold phone conversation

Published: 2026-09-19 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794580

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Pakistani Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar held a phone conversation at noon on Saturday. During the call, the two sides discussed issues of mutual interest and exchanged views on the latest regional developments.
