# Baghaei: Soleimani’s image at UNGA seat further rattled ‘butcher of Gaza’

Published: 2026-09-25 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/795017

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baghaei said on X that the Iranian delegation left an image of martyred General Qassem Soleimani on Iran’s seat at the UN General Assembly to further unsettle the “Butcher of Gaza” during his speech.
