# Iran FM Araghchi receives Qatari delegation in Muscat

Published: 2026-11-18 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797920

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who is visiting Muscat for an official visit to Oman, received a Qatari delegation on Tuesday afternoon. The Qatari delegation, headed by an adviser to Qatar’s prime minister, traveled to Oman and held talks with Iran’s foreign minister on the latest regional developments. Referring to Qatar’s continued role in efforts to ease tensions in the region, Araghchi stressed the importance of continued consultations among regional countries to counter the destabilizing and insecurity-inducing activities of actors from outside the region
