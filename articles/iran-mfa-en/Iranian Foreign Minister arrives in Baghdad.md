# Iranian Foreign Minister arrives in Baghdad

Published: 2026-10-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796089

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi arrived in Baghdad to attend the meeting of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC), where he was received by Iraq’s deputy foreign minister. Upon arrival at Baghdad International Airport, Araghchi paid tribute to the martyred Resistance commanders, Lieutenant General Qassem Soleimani and Abu Mahdi al-Muhandis, as well as their companions, at their memorial site.
