# Iranian FM arrives in Ashgabat for official visit to Turkmenistan

Published: 2026-11-02 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796959

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi arrived in Ashgabat, the capital of Turkmenistan, on Monday morning for an official visit to the country. During the visit, the foreign minister is scheduled to meet with senior Turkmen officials to discuss ways to strengthen cooperation and expand bilateral relations in various political and economic fields.
