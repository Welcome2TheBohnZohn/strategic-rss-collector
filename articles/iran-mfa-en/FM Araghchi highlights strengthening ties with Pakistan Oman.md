# FM Araghchi highlights strengthening ties with Pakistan Oman

Published: 2026-11-22 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798125

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi, in a post on Instagram, highlighted his recent visit to Pakistan and Oman, emphasizing the importance of expanding bilateral cooperation, strengthening regional economic ties, and enhancing coordination to promote peace and stability in the region.
