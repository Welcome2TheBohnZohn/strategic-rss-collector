# Iranian MFA Spokesperson writes on X after meeting new German ambassador

Published: 2026-10-07 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/795686

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baghaei wrote on X that he had held candid and useful talks with Germany’s new ambassador to Iran, Axel Dittmann, on developments in international relations and shared responsibility for protecting the rule of law and international peace and security. His message reads as follows: Today, I received the new German Ambassador to Iran, Amb Alex Dittmann, and we had an engaging and frank discussion on prevailing trends in international relations. Countering the threats and challenges that jeopardize the rule of law and endanger international peace and security remains a shared responsibility - one that calls for insight, courage, and cooperation.
