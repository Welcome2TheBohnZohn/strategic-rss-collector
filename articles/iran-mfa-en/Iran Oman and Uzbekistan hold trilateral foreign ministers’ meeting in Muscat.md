# Iran Oman and Uzbekistan hold trilateral foreign ministers’ meeting in Muscat

Published: 2026-11-18 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798056

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who is visiting Muscat for an official visit to Oman and consultations with senior Omani officials on bilateral relations and regional developments, attended a trilateral meeting of the foreign ministers of Iran, Oman and Uzbekistan to discuss the potential of the North-South Corridor. The meeting, hosted by Sayyid Badr Al-Busaidi, Foreign Minister of the Sultanate of Oman, was attended by Bakhtiyor Saidov, Foreign Minister of the Republic of Uzbekistan, and Araghchi. The three sides discussed ways to expand interregional cooperation in various economic, trade, transportation and transit sectors. Highlighting the considerable potential of the three countries to expand economic ties, particularly through strengthening trade exchanges along the Central Asia–Persian Gulf/Gulf of Oman route (the North-South Corridor), the foreign ministers of Iran, Uzbekistan and Oman emphasized the importance of planning to expand regional transportation networks and complete and upgrade the relevant infrastructure.
