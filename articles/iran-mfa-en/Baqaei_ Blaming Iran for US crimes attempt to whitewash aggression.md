# Baqaei: Blaming Iran for US crimes attempt to whitewash aggression

Published: 2026-10-30 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796810

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei, reacting to claims by the so-called Iran Human Rights Reporting Mechanism, wrote on X that using the mechanism to blame Iran for crimes committed by the United States and Israel was a deliberate attempt to whitewash aggression and crimes. "Regardless of the politically motivated nature of the mandate imposed on the Human Rights Council by the UK، exploiting the so-called Special Rapporteurship on the Situation of Human Rights in Iran to blame Iran for the crimes committed by the US and Israel amounts to nothing less than a deliberate attempt to whitewash aggression, atrocities, and unilateral coercive measures. Such an approach is deeply misleading and profoundly disingenous."
