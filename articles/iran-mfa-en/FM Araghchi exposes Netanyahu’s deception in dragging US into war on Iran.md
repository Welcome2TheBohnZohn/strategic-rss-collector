# FM Araghchi exposes Netanyahu’s deception in dragging US into war on Iran

Published: 2026-09-01 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793551

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi has exposed Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s deception and role in dragging the United States into war with Iran, writing on X that Netanyahu openly boasts in Hebrew about deceiving the US government into entering the war on Israel’s behalf.
