# Iranian FM holds separate phone talks with Saudi Qatari counterparts

Published: 2026-11-05 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797290

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held separate phone conversations with Saudi Foreign Minister Faisal bin Farhan and Qatari Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani on Thursday afternoon. During the calls, the sides discussed regional developments and the latest diplomatic efforts aimed at establishing stability in the region.
