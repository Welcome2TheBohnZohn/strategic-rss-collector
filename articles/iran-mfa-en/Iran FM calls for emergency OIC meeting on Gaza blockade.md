# Iran FM calls for emergency OIC meeting on Gaza blockade

Published: 2026-11-22 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798122

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi has called for an emergency OIC foreign ministers’ meeting, urging immediate action to lift the siege on Gaza and ensure the delivery of humanitarian aid. He made the call in a post on X.
