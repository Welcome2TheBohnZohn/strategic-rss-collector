# Meeting held between Iranian Pakistani Foreign Ministry spokespersons in Islamabad

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797799

Esmail Baqaei, Spokesperson for the Iranian Foreign Ministry, who is accompanying the foreign minister at the ECO Council of Foreign Ministers meeting, held talks with his Pakistani counterpart, Sajjad Haider Khan, in Islamabad on Monday morning. During the meeting, the two sides highlighted the shared historical, civilizational, religious and cultural bonds between the two neighboring Muslim nations and stressed the importance of leveraging the two countries' strong relations and mutual trust to strengthen ties across various fields, including media and culture. The Iranian and Pakistani foreign ministry spokespersons also emphasized the importance of deepening mutual understanding and friendship between the two nations through media and underscored the need to establish appropriate mechanisms to enhance coordination on media-related issues.
