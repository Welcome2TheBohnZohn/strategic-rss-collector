# Iranian Foreign Minister meets Sultan of Oman in Muscat

Published: 2026-11-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797908

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who is visiting Oman on an official trip, met and held talks with Sultan Haitham bin Tariq Al Said of Oman on Tuesday morning. Referring to the long-standing and positive history of relations between Iran and Oman, Araghchi emphasized the Islamic Republic of Iran's determination to further expand relations with Oman in all areas of mutual interest. The Iranian foreign minister also recalled the Islamic Republic of Iran's principled policy of strengthening trust and cooperation among regional countries, stressing the importance of comprehensive efforts by countries in the region to advance their shared interests and regional security. The two sides also discussed the latest regional developments, particularly the prevailing insecurity and tensions resulting from the United States' aggressive and interventionist actions. Araghchi briefed the Sultan of Oman on the latest situation, including Iran's diplomatic initiative to end the US-imposed war. Appreciating Oman's good offices and mediation efforts, he stressed that the Islamic Republic of Iran has always been serious about pursuing diplomacy, just as it has acted firmly and resolutely in defending its territorial integrity and national interests. The Sultan of Oman, for his part, highlighted the importance of the historic and friendly relations between Oman and Iran, expressed appreciation for Iran's responsible approach toward regional peace and stability, and stressed the need for continued consultations among regional countries to manage the current critical situation and prevent further escalation of tensions.
