# Iranian FM arrives in Muscat for official visit to Oman

Published: 2026-11-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797846

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi arrived in Muscat, the Omani capital, for an official visit following his participation in the 30th meeting of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO). During the visit, the Iranian foreign minister is scheduled to hold talks with senior Omani officials on bilateral relations and regional developments.
