# Iran FM mocks Israeli envoy’s remarks on US military

Published: 2026-11-22 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798124

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi sarcastically reacted to remarks by the Israeli ambassador to Washington, writing on X: “We didn’t know Israel’s ambassador to Washington was the commander-in-chief of the US armed forces!”
