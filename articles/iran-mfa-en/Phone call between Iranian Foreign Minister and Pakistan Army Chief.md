# Phone call between Iranian Foreign Minister and Pakistan Army Chief

Published: 2026-09-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794507

Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, held a phone conversation with Field Marshal Asim Munir, Chief of Army Staff of Pakistan, in Beijing yesterday. During the call, the latest regional developments and issues of interest to the two countries were discussed.
