# Iran FM Araghchi slams western media’s ‘Iraq War-style’ disinformation

Published: 2026-11-19 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/798054

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi has dismissed Western media reports alleging Iranian plots to use chemical weapons in Washington and the construction of missile bases beneath dams as “utter nonsense,” saying that Israel-linked media outlets are spreading Iraq War-style propaganda. He made the remarks in a post on X.
