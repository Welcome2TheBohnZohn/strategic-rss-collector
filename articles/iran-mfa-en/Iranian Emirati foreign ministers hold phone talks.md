# Iranian Emirati foreign ministers hold phone talks

Published: 2026-11-06 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797294

During a phone conversation on Thursday night, Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and UAE Foreign Minister Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan discussed and exchanged views on the latest regional developments in light of diplomatic efforts aimed at maintaining stability in the region.
