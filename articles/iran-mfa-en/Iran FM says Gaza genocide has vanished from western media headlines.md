# Iran FM says Gaza genocide has vanished from western media headlines

Published: 2026-11-13 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797656

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi has condemned the lack of media attention to the catastrophic humanitarian situation in Gaza, saying a “silent genocide” is underway with virtually no attention being paid to it. In a post on X, he said Israel had succeeded, at least in one aspect of its efforts to wage war and divert public attention from its failures.
