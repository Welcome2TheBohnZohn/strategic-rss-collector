# Iranian Foreign Minister to visit Pakistan Oman in coming fays

Published: 2026-11-15 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797787

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baqaei announced that Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi will visit Islamabad and Oman in the coming days. Baqaei said that Araghchi will attend the 30th Meeting of the Council of Ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) on Monday. The meeting will focus on discussing and exchanging views on economic and trade cooperation among the organization's 10 member states. The Foreign Ministry spokesperson also announced Araghchi's visit to Oman, saying that the foreign minister will travel to Muscat on Tuesday to meet and hold talks with his Omani counterpart.
