# Pakistan Army chief to visit Tehran

Published: 2026-08-23 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793020

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmail Baghaei, speaking to reporters, announced that Field Marshal Asim Munir, Chief of Army Staff of Pakistan, will travel to Tehran. According to the Foreign Ministry Spokesperson, Field Marshal Asim Munir will visit Tehran on Monday at the head of an official delegation. The Spokesperson stated that the visit aims to strengthen bilateral cooperation between Iran and Pakistan and to continue Pakistan’s good offices in support of peace and security in the region. During the visit, Asim Munir will meet and hold talks with senior officials of the Islamic Republic of Iran.
