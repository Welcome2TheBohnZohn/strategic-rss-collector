# Iranian Omani Foreign Ministers hold talks in Muscat

Published: 2026-11-17 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797876

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi, who is visiting Muscat for an official visit to the Sultanate of Oman, met with Omani Foreign Minister Badr Al-Busaidi on Tuesday morning. During the meeting, Araghchi congratulated Oman in advance on its National Day and underscored the importance of friendly relations and cooperation between the two countries in all fields. The two sides discussed and exchanged views on Iran-Oman bilateral relations. They also reviewed the regional situation and exchanged views on the need to strengthen consultations and cooperation among regional countries to safeguard their common interests and preserve regional peace and stability.
