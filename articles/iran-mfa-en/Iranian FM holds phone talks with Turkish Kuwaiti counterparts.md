# Iranian FM holds phone talks with Turkish Kuwaiti counterparts

Published: 2026-11-05 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797292

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held separate phone conversations on Thursday afternoon with Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan and Kuwaiti Foreign Minister Sheikh Jarrah Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah. During the calls, they exchanged views on the latest regional developments and diplomatic initiatives aimed at maintaining stability in the region.
