# Iran Qatar FMs hold phone talks on regional developments

Published: 2026-08-25 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793257

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani, Qatar’s Prime Minister and Foreign Minister, held a telephone conversation on Tuesday evening. During the call, the two sides reviewed the latest regional developments and emphasized the importance of utilizing diplomatic channels and continuing coordination to prevent further escalation of tensions in the region.
