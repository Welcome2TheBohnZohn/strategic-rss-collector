# Iranian FM says warned European officials against supporting US Israeli war crimes

Published: 2026-09-23 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794898

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi wrote on X that he had warned EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas and other European officials about the severe, long-term consequences of facilitating and supporting US and Israeli war crimes in Gaza and Iran.
