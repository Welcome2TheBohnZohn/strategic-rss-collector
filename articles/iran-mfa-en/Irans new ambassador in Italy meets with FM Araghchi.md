# Irans new ambassador in Italy meets with FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-21 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796422

Ali Akbar Rezaei, the new Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran in Italy, met today, Wednesday, with Seyed Abbas Araghchi, the Minister of Foreign Affairs. During this meeting, Ali Akbar Rezaei outlined his proposed plans regarding bilateral relations between Tehran and Rome. While wishing success to Iran’s new ambassador in Rome, the Iranian Foreign Minister emphasized the importance of using all opportunities to strengthen Iran’s relations with other countries. Prior to his appointment as Iran’s new ambassador to Italy, Ali Akbar Rezaei served as Director-General of the Office of Strategic Planning and Monitoring of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
