# Iran FM Pakistan Army Chief hold phone talks on regional peace security

Published: 2026-08-25 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793260

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, Pakistan’s Chief of Army Staff, held a telephone conversation to review the latest regional developments and matters of mutual interest. The two sides emphasized the need to continue consultations and joint efforts to establish peace and security in the region.
