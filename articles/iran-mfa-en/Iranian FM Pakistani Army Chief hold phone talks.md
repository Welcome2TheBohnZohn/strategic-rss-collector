# Iranian FM Pakistani Army Chief hold phone talks

Published: 2026-08-20 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/792904

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Pakistan’s Chief of Army Staff, Field Marshal Asim Munir, held a telephone conversation on Wednesday. During the call, the two sides discussed and exchanged views on the latest regional developments.
