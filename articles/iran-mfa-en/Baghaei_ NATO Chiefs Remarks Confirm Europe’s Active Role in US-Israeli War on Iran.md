# Baghaei: NATO Chiefs Remarks Confirm Europe’s Active Role in US-Israeli War on Iran

Published: 2026-09-25 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/795016

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baghaei said on X that NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte’s remarks about the extensive use of European facilities by the US amount to an explicit admission of Europe’s active involvement in the US-Israeli military aggression against Iran.
