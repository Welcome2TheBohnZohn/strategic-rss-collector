# Iranian Tunisian foreign ministers hold phone talks

Published: 2026-11-06 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797295

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a telephone conversation with Tunisian Foreign Minister Mohamed Ali Nafti on Friday afternoon. During the phone call, Araghchi briefed his Tunisian counterpart on diplomatic efforts and initiatives aimed at de-escalating tensions and strengthening stability in the region. The two ministers also exchanged views on the latest regional developments and events.
