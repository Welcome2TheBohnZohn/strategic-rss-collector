# Iran warns IAEA chief against turning technical reports into pretext for war

Published: 2026-09-12 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794210

Iranian Foreign Ministry Spokesman Esmail Baghaei, in a post on X, warned that the IAEA chief’s use of technical reporting to create political and legal pretexts against Iran risks turning the Agency into a tool for justifying and facilitating military aggression.
