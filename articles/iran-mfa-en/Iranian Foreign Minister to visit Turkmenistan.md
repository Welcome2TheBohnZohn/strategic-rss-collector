# Iranian Foreign Minister to visit Turkmenistan

Published: 2026-11-01 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796955

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi will travel to Ashgabat on Monday at the head of a diplomatic and economic delegation. During the visit, the Iranian foreign minister will hold talks and consultations with Turkmen officials on bilateral relations in various areas of mutual interest.
