# MFA Spokesman: Iran Will Never Forget Minab’s “Angels” Killed in US Strike

Published: 2026-09-23 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/794897

Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesman said, in a post on X, the Iranian people will forever remember the Minab schoolchildren killed in a US Tomahawk missile strike on February 28, 2026, and will never forgive those responsible for their deaths.
