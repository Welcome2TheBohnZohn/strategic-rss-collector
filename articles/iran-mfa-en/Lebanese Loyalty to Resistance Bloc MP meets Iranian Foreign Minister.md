# Lebanese Loyalty to Resistance Bloc MP meets Iranian Foreign Minister

Published: 2026-08-23 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/793114

Sayyed Hassan Fadlallah, a member of the Loyalty to the Resistance Bloc in the Lebanese Parliament, met this Sunday afternoon with Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi. During the meeting, the Lebanese MP conveyed the greetings of Sheikh Naim Qassem, Secretary-General of Hezbollah, and praised the heroic defense of the brave Iranian nation against the American-Zionist military aggression, as well as Iran’s firm diplomacy. He described these as factors that have solidified the authority of the Islamic Republic of Iran and a source of pride for Iran’s friends, the peoples of the region, and free people around the world. Fadlallah also provided a report on the latest situation in Lebanon and the ongoing aggression and occupation by the Zionist regime against the country. Expressing appreciation for the Islamic Republic of Iran’s support for the Lebanese people—particularly the efforts of the Iranian Foreign Minister in backing the Lebanese people and resistance in diplomatic processes—he stressed the need for concerted and decisive action by Islamic countries to confront the expansionism and crimes of the Zionist regime in Lebanon, Gaza, and the West Bank. In the meeting, the Iranian Foreign Minister praised the honorable resistance and steadfastness of the Lebanese people in the face of the Zionist regime’s brutal aggressions and reaffirmed the Islamic Republic of Iran’s comprehensive support for the Lebanese people in defending their territorial integrity, dignity, and national sovereignty.
