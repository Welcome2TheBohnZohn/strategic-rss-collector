# Iran FM Reaffirms Right to Defend National Sovereignty Security

Published: 2026-11-07 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797313

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi said, in a post on Instagram, that in telephone conversations with 12 regional and international counterparts over the past 48 hours, he had stressed Iran’s legitimate right to defend its national sovereignty and security, calling for regional security arrangements without interference from extra-regional powers.
