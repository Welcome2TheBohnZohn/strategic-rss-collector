# Iranian FM congratulates Algeria on National Day

Published: 2026-11-01 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796915

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi, in a message to Algerian Foreign Minister Ahmed Attaf, congratulated the Algerian nation and government on the 72nd anniversary of the launch of the liberation revolution and Algeria’s National Day. In his message, the Iranian foreign minister wished prosperity and well-being for the government and great people of Algeria and expressed hope that Iran and Algeria, by continuing their active and constructive approach in supporting justice-oriented causes, would see their joint efforts strengthened and make greater use of their existing capacities to further expand cooperation at the bilateral, regional, and international levels.
