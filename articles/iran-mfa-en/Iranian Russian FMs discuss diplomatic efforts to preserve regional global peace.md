# Iranian Russian FMs discuss diplomatic efforts to preserve regional global peace

Published: 2026-11-16 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/797842

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi held a phone conversation with his Russian counterpart, Sergey Lavrov, at noon on Monday to discuss the latest political and diplomatic efforts and initiatives aimed at preserving regional and global peace and security. Araghchi, who is in Islamabad to attend the 30th Meeting of the ECO Council of Ministers of Foreign Affairs, held the telephone conversation with Lavrov during his visit.
