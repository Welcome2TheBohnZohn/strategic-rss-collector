# Iranian Jordanian FMs hold phone talks discuss regional developments

Published: 2026-10-28 00:00:00

Source: https://en.mfa.gov.ir/portal/newsview/796662

Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Jordanian Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ayman Safadi held a phone conversation on Tuesday morning to discuss regional developments. During the conversation, Araghchi outlined Iran’s positions on regional developments and stressed the need for Islamic countries and the international community to take serious action to counter the continued genocide and expansionism of the occupying Zionist regime. Recalling the devastating consequences of the US and Israeli military aggression against Iran for the region and the world, the Iranian foreign minister emphasized that restoring security to the region requires an end to the United States’ aggressive and interventionist actions, as well as containing the Zionist regime’s expansionism and warmongering. Araghchi also condemned the terrorist attack on a church in southern Jordan and expressed his condolences to the families of those killed in the incident. The foreign ministers of the two countries underscored the importance of making use of diplomacy and continuing political consultations to safeguard peace and stability in the region.
