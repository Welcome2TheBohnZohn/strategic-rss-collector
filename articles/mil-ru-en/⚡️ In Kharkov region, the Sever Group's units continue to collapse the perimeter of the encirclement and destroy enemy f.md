# ⚡️ In Kharkov region, the Sever Group's units continue to collapse the perimeter of the encirclement and destroy enemy formations

Published: 2026-09-07 10:20:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29261

⚡️
In Kharkov region,
the Sever Group's units continue to collapse the perimeter of the encirclement and destroy enemy formations
close to Varvarovka, Malaya Volchya, Nefedovka, and Chernoye (Kharkov region).
📊
At the moment, about 1,700 militants are encircled.
During the day, the enemy losses amounted to up to 40 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, two pick-up trucks, and three quadricycles. The enemy did not perform attempts to break out of the encirclement.
💥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Malaya Rybitsa, Ulanovo, and Hoten (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy lost up to 190 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, ten motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
↗️
The Zapad Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade close to Boldyrevka, Kovalevka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Mayaki, Raygorodok, and Rubtsy (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 170 Ukrainian troops, one U.K.-made Snatch armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, and three artillery guns.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the tactical situation along the front line.
Formations of two mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades, and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Vysokoivanovka, Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Krasny Molochar, Nikolayevka, Novoselovka, Semenovka, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 200 troops, one U.K.-made Husky TSV armoured fighting vehicle, 23 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
