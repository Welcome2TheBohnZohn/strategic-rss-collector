The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one jaeger brigade, one air assault brigade, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, two marine brigades, two national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade close to Novoandreyevka, Svyatogorovka, Roganskoye, Kalinino, Belozerskoye, Maryevka, Novofedorovka, Verovka (Donetsk People's Republic), Chuguyevo, Novopavlovka, and Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
⏩
In Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army
advanced in the northern and central parts of the city.
Marine units completed mopping-up of residential building in the eastern areas of the city and continued offensive in its northern part.
📊
Over the past 24 hours, 53 buildings have been liberated, up to 20 Ukrainian servicemen have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, more than 435 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibilitys.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade have been hit close to Prosyanaya, Katerinovka, Orestopol, Shevchenko, Ivanovka, Dibrova (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasinovka, Dubovy Gay, Obshcheye and Nikolskoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 290 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, and five motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces' units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU have been hit near Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, Kamyshevakha, Dimitrov, and Zapasnoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 18 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation destroyed a radar station for detecting air targets, and inflicted damage
on the transport infrastructure facilities of Ukraine used for the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, military equipment depots, UAV storage sites, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 147 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four guided aerial bombs, three U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 1,222 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
