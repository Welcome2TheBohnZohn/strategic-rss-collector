↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian elements engaged manpower and hardware of an air assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Trudoolenovka, Tersyanka, Volnyanka, Barvinovka, Vasilevskoye in Zaporozhye region, and Gavrilovka in Dnepropetrovsk region.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 195 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, three motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the AFU were hit near Preobrazhenka and Orekhov of Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 30 troops and nine motor vehicles of the AFU were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage
on transport infrastructure facilities used by the AFU, ammunition depots, production workshops, and storage areas of unmanned ground and long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 148 areas.
▫️

two guided aerial bombs and 645 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
