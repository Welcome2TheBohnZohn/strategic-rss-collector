The Vostok Group's units advanced into the depth of the enemy's defence.
Strikes have been delivered at manpower and hardware of one assault brigade, two air assault brigades, and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Podgavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Storchevoye, Zalivnoye, Vasilevskoye, Novorozovka, and Kirovo (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 205 troops, two motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
↗️
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian elements delivered strikes at one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU close to Preobrazhenka, Yurkovka, and Grigorovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 35 troops, 22 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun of the AFU have been neutralised.
✈️
