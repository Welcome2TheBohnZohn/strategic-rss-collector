# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by UAVs against power facilities of Ukraine and ships used in the interests of the AFU.

Published: 2026-11-06 06:42:07

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30460

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by UAVs against power facilities of Ukraine and ships used in the interests of the AFU.
▫️
As a result of the strikes, a
battery energy storage park
that supplied electricity to defence industry facilities of Ukraine was hit in Belaya Tserkov (Kiev region).
▫️
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo to Ukraine was hit in the port of Odessa.

Russian Defence Ministry
