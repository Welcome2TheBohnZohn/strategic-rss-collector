CSTO's joint fist: allied forces put words into action
📍
Chebarkulskiy training ground
As part of the Interaction 2026 exercise,
the CSTO Collective Rapid Reaction Force
began preparing a joint operation
to localize a border armed conflict.
💬
Commander of the CRRF Colonel Aleksey Sgibnev
reported the Decision, which determined:
🔹
the main efforts directions during the joint operation;
🔹
tasks for military contingents and special forces formations;
🔹
the main issues of management organisation, interaction and comprehensive support.
✅
After the approval of the Decision, the CSTO CRRF management bodies
began planning the operation.
➡️
Subordinate formations, military units and detachments
were given combat orders to prepare and conduct combat (special) operations.
📊
Troops of the Republic of Belarus, the Republic of Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic, the Russian Federation and the Republic of Tajikistan are taking part
in the CSTO exercises
at the Chebarkulskiy training ground. In total, about 2,000 troops and 350 units of weaponry, military and special hardware are involved.
