Taking care of our future

The servicemen of the Tsentr Group of Forces and volunteers from the Volga region handed over backpacks with office supplies and school uniforms to the students of the Donetsk Rehabilitation Centre on the occasion of the Knowledge Day.
📕
The centre was also gifted with a school album found under the rubble in Avdeyevka: after restoration, it will be sent to the museum as a symbol of the return to peaceful life.
The children sang to their guests, and our servicemen promised to continue to support them.
