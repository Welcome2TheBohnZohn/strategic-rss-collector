# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres and port infrastructure of Ukraine, as 

Published: 2026-10-20 16:40:58

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30174

❗️
Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres and port infrastructure of Ukraine, as well as against vessels
used to deliver military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike,
the Epicentr K logistics centre in the settlement of Dubno in Rovno region was hit.
There is a warehouse on its territory used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔥
In addition,
a container ship was damaged at sea delivering cargo to the port of Odessa
to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
