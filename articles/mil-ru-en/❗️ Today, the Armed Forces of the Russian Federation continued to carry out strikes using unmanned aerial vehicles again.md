# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of Ukraine’s defence industry, port infrastru

Published: 2026-10-27 16:20:30

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30311

❗️
Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of Ukraine’s defence industry, port infrastructure facilities, and vessels used to deliver military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike, the electronic industry enterprise (STE "UKRSPETSYSVYAZ" LLC) was damaged. It is located in the settlement of Nadtochii of Kharkov region, and manufactures automation systems and communication equipment for the AFU.
💥
In addition, a cargo ship delivering supplies to the port of Odessa for the AFU was damaged at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
