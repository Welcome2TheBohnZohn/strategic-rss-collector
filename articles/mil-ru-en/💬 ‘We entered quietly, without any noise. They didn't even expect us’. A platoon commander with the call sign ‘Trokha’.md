Published: 2026-10-21 09:30:01

‘We entered quietly, without any noise. They didn't even expect us’.
💬
A platoon commander with the call sign ‘Trokha’
gave details of the liberation of
Timoshevka
in Zaporozhye region.
▫️
According to the serviceman,
the most difficult part was to cross a six-kilometre open area
in front of the settlement. The assault detachments managed to get through it thanks to the rain, which thwarted the enemy UAVs' flights.
