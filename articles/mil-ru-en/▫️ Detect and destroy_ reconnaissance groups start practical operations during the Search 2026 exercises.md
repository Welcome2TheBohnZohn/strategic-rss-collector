Detect and destroy: reconnaissance groups start practical operations during the Search 2026 exercises
📍
Chebarkulskiy training ground
💥
Reconnaissance groups have already moved to the designated areas to search for a mock enemy. To defeat the enemy, the servicemen use reconnaissance and attack FPV drones, as well as adjust artillery and aviation fire.
➡️
At the same time, the reconnaissance data units collect, analyse, and generalise the information acquired and transmit it to the Command of the CSTO CRRF. It enables the 'detect and destroy' principle to be implemented.
The goal of the exercise is to conduct various types of reconnaissance in the interest of deploying the CSTO CRRF, as well as to improve the practical skills of the command and reconnaissance unit personnel.
