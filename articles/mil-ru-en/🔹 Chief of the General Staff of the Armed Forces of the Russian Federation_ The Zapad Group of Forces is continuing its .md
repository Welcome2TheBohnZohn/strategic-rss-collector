Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation: The Zapad Group of Forces is continuing its offensive
Report
by Valery Gerasimov:
The Zapad Group of Forces is continuing its offensive in the northern part of the Donetsk People's Republic and in the east of Kharkov Region having completed the liberation of the Lugansk People's Republic in March 2026.
In Krasny Liman direction, the enemy, attempting to halt Russian advance, made unprecedented efforts to conduct counter-offensive operations, redeploying for this purpose its best-trained and motivated formations, staffed by nationalists and foreign mercenaries. However, it failed to achieve its objectives.
Against the backdrop of a lack of visible successes on the battlefield and severe losses, the enemy command once again resorted to staging false, media-driven victories, organising a series of publicity stunts as part of a planned information campaign. Nevertheless, on this direction alone, during summer and autumn, the Armed Forces of Ukraine lost more than 3,500 manpower from their most highly trained and combat-capable formations, killed or seriously wounded.
Formations of the 20th and 25th Combined Arms Armies have repelled attacks of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and have resumed offensive.  They are simultaneously conducting search operations to detect and neutralise scattered enemy groups that had attempted to infiltrate the rear of Russian forces.
In Kupyansk direction, units of the 6th Army, having thwarted enemy attempts to drive us out of Kupyansk, are advancing westwards, towards Shevchenkovo. The settlements of Palamaryovka and Kovalyovka have been liberated. The liberation of Zaporozhye region is proceeding according to plan.
