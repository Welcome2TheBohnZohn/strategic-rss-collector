Anti-submarine ships of Northern Fleet show firepower
The formation of anti-submarine ships of the Kola Flotilla all-arms forces (Northern Fleet), as part of the planned exercise, carried out artillery and anti-aircraft fire.
🎯
The crews
practised searching for and destroying a mock enemy
submarine in cooperation with submarines and anti-submarine aviation of the Northern Fleet. They also performed air defence missions.
⚓️
The Severomorsk and Vice Admiral Kulakov anti-submarine ships
are part of the ship's search and strike group
.
