Breathtaking: For the first time in the Russian Navy history, the Northern Fleet vessels pass through the Shokalsky Strait
📍
The route passed between the islands of the Severnaya Zemlya archipelago.
🚁
Ice surveillance:
a Ka-29 helicopter took off from the icebreaking patrol ship Ivan Papanin to reconnoitre the route from the air.
🌊
Manoeuvring among icebergs:
the crew faced a difficult ice situation of the strait. The Northern Fleet's sailors quickly changed their course and speed to ensure complete navigation safety.
▫️
Combat readiness:
training alert was announced to pass through the strait by ships, and crews successfully assessed this new area for futher actions of the Russian Navy.
