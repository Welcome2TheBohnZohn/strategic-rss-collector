# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch UAV strikes

Published: 2026-10-26 06:40:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30280

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to launch UAV strikes
against
logistics centres and power industry facilities
that provide their operation, as well as
Ukrainian ports and vessels
used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets were hit:
📍
in Kharkov region:
▫️
a depot of medium- and long-range UAVs,
as well as their components, in the settlement of Olshany;
▫️
the Zalyutino PS-330 power substation
used to provide functioning of defence industry enterprises of Ukraine in Kharkov;
📍
in Khmelnitskiy region:
▫️
the PS-330 power substation
used to provide logistics infrastructure functioning in Kamenets-Podolskiy;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
loading and unloading infrastructure facilities
of three berth terminals in the port of Chernomorsk, as well as four depots containing military and dual-use cargo to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
▫️
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo to the port of Odessa to support the AFU was hit at sea.

Russian Defence Ministry
