↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four air assault brigades, two assault brigades, and an assault regiment of the AFU near Mayak, Tikhoye, Prosyanaya, Malomikhailovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Yasnaya Polyana, Odarovka, and Veseloye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 220 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, six motor vehicles, one U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's elements hit formations of three mechanised brigades and a mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Dimitrovo of Zaporozhye region, Belozerka, and Mikhaylovka of Kherson region.
▪️
Up to 20 troops, 24 motor vehicles, one 155-mm Bogdana howitzer, and one counter-fire radar station of the were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces destroyed
a missile launcher of a Neptune coastal missile system, hit energy and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, production areas, storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and launch areas, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 144 areas.

four guided aerial bombs and 792 fixed-wing UAVs.
