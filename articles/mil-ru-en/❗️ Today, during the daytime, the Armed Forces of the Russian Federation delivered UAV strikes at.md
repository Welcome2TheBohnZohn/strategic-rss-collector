Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes at
power infrastructure facilities, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets were hit:
three 110 kV power substations providing operation of defence industry facilities of Ukraine in Vinnitsa, in Obolonye (Chernigov region) and Rakitnoye (Rovno region).
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo to support the AFU
was hit at sea in the Black Sea waters south-west of Odessa.
