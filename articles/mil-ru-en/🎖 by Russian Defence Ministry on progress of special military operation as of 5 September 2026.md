↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, two assault brigades, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Moskovka, Berestovoye, Barvinovka, Omelnik, Obshcheye, Nikolskoye of Zaporozhye region, and Dibrova of Dnepropetrovsk region.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 310 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, and unmanned systems brigade of the AFU near Preobrazhenka, Yurkovka, Orekhov of Zaporozhye region, and Urozhaynoye of Kherson region.
▪️
Up to 35 troops, 21 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
the transport infrastructure of Ukraine used by the AFU, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 158 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 763 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
