# ‘ The one, whose will is stronger, wins’ The marines of the 40th Separate Guards Brigade (the Vostok Group of Forces)

Published: 2026-10-13 12:05:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30015

‘
The one, whose will is stronger, wins’
💬
The marines of the 40th Separate Guards Brigade (the Vostok Group of Forces)
told about fighting to liberate the settlement of
Nikolskoye
(Zaporozhye region).
▫️
A serviceman with the call sign ‘Ten’ noted that
in the battle everything was decided by the solid will of the fighters and competent support from the commanders:
‘The will has a very big influence. The one, whose will is stronger, wins the battle. The situations are different, but everyone wants to live, everyone is scared. That's why at this moment the commander's very first duty is to support his soldier, to show him, that the commander is close. We need to stay strong and go on’.
📊
During the battles, assault detachments
established control over an area of about 13 square kilometres and mopped up more than 200 buildings.
The following AFU elements were neutralised:
▪️
up to a company of manpower,
▪️
15 armoured fighting vehicles,
▪️
19 motor vehicles,
▪️
29 ground robotic systems,
️️
