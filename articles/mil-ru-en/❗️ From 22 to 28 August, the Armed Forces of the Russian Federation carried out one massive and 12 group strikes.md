# ❗️ From 22 to 28 August, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out one massive and 12 group strikes

Published: 2026-08-28 10:16:35

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29083

❗️
From 22 to 28 August,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out one massive and 12 group strikes
with high-precision weapons and attack drones, which hit:
▫️
enterprises of defence, metallurgical, and petroleum industries of Ukraine,
▫️
logistics centres,
▫️
fuel-power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine,
▫️
military airfields,
▫️
workshops manufacturing unmanned aerial vehicles and components,
▫️
ammunition, fuel and materiel depots,
▫️
temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition, the strikes disabled infrastructure of the Ukrainian ports, which were used to received and store military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants intended for use by the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔥
During the week,
a patrol boat
of the Ukrainian Navy, as well as
eight vessels
operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine,
including six dry cargo ships
and
two tankers,
were neutralised.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
