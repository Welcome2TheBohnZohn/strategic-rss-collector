# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-10 09:51:17

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29315

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces
have taken control over Zarubinka in Kharkov region
as a result of intensive actions to expand the control zone on the outer ring of the encirclement of the enemy's formations in Kharkov region.
▫️
Units of two enemy battalions
of up to 180 troops
have been blocked
near Olkhovatka of Kharkov region. AFU units have been hit inside the 'pocket' close to Malaya Volchya and Buzovo of Kharkov region.
▫️
All attempts to break through the encirclement have been thwarted.
▪️
During the day, the enemy losses amounted to up to 40 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, three pickup trucks, two ATVs, and three unmanned ground vehicles.
🚩
In the course of resolute assault actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces
took control over the settlement of Goptovka
in Kharkov region.
▪️
The Group's units inflicted losses on the formations of a mechanised brigade, two motorised infantry brigades of the AFU, a territorial defence brigade, and a national guard brigade near Lozovaya, Shevchenkovo, Novoaleksandrovka, and Annopolye in Kharkov region.
📍
In Sumy region, losses were inflicted on
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades close to Volnaya Sloboda, Esman, Semenovka, and Doroshovka (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy lost up to 230 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 18 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade close to Kovalevka, Vyssheye Solenoye, Chervony Oskol, Osinovo (Kharkov region), Krymki, Rubtsy, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 215 troops, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, 18 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one U.S.-made AN/TPQ-50 counter-fire radar station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line.
The Group's units hit formations of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, and a mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Slavyansk, Orekhovatka, Kramatorsk, Belenkoye, Klinovoye, Shabelkovka, Nikolayevka, Krasny Molochar, and Druzhkovka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 160 troops, two Butsefal armoured personnel carriers, two armoured fighting vehicles, 19 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one MLRS combat vehicle.
