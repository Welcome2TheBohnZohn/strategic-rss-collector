# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-10 10:34:45

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30527

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
As a result of active actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces liberated Nikolskoye in Sumy region.
💥
Formations of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Sumy and Putivl (Sumy region).
🔥
In addition,
strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade near Rubezhnoye, Veseloye, Liptsy, and Izbitskoye (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 305 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, 15 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Manpower and materiel of three mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Izyum, Volchiy Yar, Berezovka (Kharkov region), Mayaki, and Ilyichovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 210 servicemen, an armoured fighting vehicle, 13 motor vehicles, five artillery guns, and seven electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Russian units inflicted damage on formations of two mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Raygorodok, Nikolayevka, and Krasny Molochar (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 190 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 21 motor vehicles, and one self-propelled artillery system.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Yakovlevka, Sergeyevka, Kuritsino, Roganskoye, Novopavlovka, Kamenka, and Verovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 340 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced into the depth of the enemy's defence
. Manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade have been hit near Malinovka, Ivanovka, Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Tavriyskoye, Novorozovka, and Novonikolayevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 215 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and three motor vehicles.
