# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-03 11:04:58

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29190

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
As a result of decisive actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces took control over Vasilevka, Blagodatnoye, and Dolzhanka (Kharkov region).
💥
Russian troops inflicted losses
on formations of one mechanised brigade, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Goptovka, Novoaleksandrovka, Olkhovatka, Pogoreloye, Prikolotnoye, Khotomlya, and Shevchenkovo (Kharkov region).
📍
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Kiyanitsa, Ulanovo, and Khothen (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy has lost more than 315 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, four artillery guns, one counter-fire radar station, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian troops launched strikes at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade close to Grushevka, Petrovka, Prilyutovo, Studenok (Kharkov region), Krymki, and Prishib (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to over 210 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
🚩
As a result of active actions, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces liberated Izhevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
The Group's units engaged
formations of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Verolyubovka, Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Semenovka, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 205 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 26 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Bokovoye, Gruzskoye, Dobropolye, Novotroitskoye, and Sergeyevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 385 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, one electronic warfare station, and one electronic reconnaissance station.
