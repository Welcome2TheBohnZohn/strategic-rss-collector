# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-02 10:05:27

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29174

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
As a result of resolute actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces took control over Kazachya Lopan in Kharkov region.
📍
Russian troops
inflicted losses
on formations of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Novoaleksandrovka, Prikolotnoye, Posredneye, and Sosnovy Bor of Kharkov region.
💥
In Sumy region, the Group's units engaged
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade of the AFU and a territorial defence brigade near Ulanovo, Khoten, and Malaya Rybitsa (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 200 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one counter-fire radar station.
🚩
As a result of intensive actions, units of the Zapad Group of Forces liberated the city of Svyatogorsk in the Donetsk People's Republic.
🔥
Currently, the city is being mopped up from the Ukrainian militants hiding in basements. Russian elements inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Studenok (Kharkov region), Raygorodok, Mayaki, Sidorovo, Krymki, and Prishib (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 205 troops, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, 20 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line.
Russian troops engaged formations of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigades near Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Semenovka, Druzhkovka, Verolyubovka, and Alekseyevo-Druzhkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 140 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 28 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units engaged manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Gruzskoye, Novogrigorovka, Belozerskoye, Dobropolye, and Sergeyevka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 410 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 16 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations.
