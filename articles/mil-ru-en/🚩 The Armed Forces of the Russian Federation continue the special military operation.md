# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-13 10:40:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29370

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement around the enemy's formations
and expand the external control zone in Kharkov region. As a result of active actions,
the control over the settlement of Chernyakov
(Kharkov region)
was established by the Russian troops.
💥
Strikes were delivered at
the encircled AFU's manpower and hardware near Malaya Volchya, Budarki, and Buzovo (Kharkov region).
🔥
The enemy's manpower and hardware were hit
on the outer perimeter of the encirclement
close to Novoaleksandrovka and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region), as well as the destruction of the AFU's units continue to be carried out in the area of Olkhovatka (Kharkov region).
The enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement
near Sliznevo and Gogino (Kharkov region)
were thwarted.
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, the enemy has lost up to 55 militants and one ground robotic system.
🔥
In addition,
in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at
formations of three mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades near Shevchenkovo, Staritsa, Novoselovka, Pogoreloye, Khatneye, and Fedorovka.
📍
In Sumy region,
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigade
were neutralised
close to Doroshovka, Velikaya Chernetchina, Boyaro-Lezhachi, Sennoye, Esman, and Krovnoye.
▪️
In total, up to 230 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, 18 motor vehicles, and one 155-mm Bogdana howitzer were neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of tree mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one presidential brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near Karpovka, Rubtsy, Beloye, Starodubovka (Donetsk People's Republic), Sobolevka, Solenoye, and Peski Radkovskiye (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 220 troops, one Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, 15 motor vehicles, one U.K.-made Braveheart self-propelled artillery system, and one Polish-made Krab self-propelled artillery system, as well as one electronic reconnaissance station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near the settlements of Yasnogorka, Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, Rayskoye, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Novoselovka, Nikolayevka, and Orekhovatka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 205 troops, two Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, and 17 motor vehicles. Two field artillery guns, including one 155-mm U.S.-made Paladin self-propelled artillery system, were neutralised.
