# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-08-29 11:00:13

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29105

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
As a result of active actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces established control over Khrapovshchina (Sumy region).
💥
Manpower and hardware
of a mechanised brigade, two airmobile brigades of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Sadki, Vakalovshchina, Ulanovo, and Kiyanitsa (Sumy region).
🔥
In Kharkov region
, strikes were delivered at formations of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade close to Sosnovy Bor, Berezhnoye, Podsredneye, Tokarevka, and Sliznevo (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 250 troops, nine motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Studenok (Kharkov region), Svyatogorsk, Sidorovo, Tatyanovka, Krymki, and Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to over 220 troops, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, 14 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line
. Formations of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit close to Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Krasny Molochar, Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Druzhkovka, and Vysokoivanovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy has lost more than 200 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 24 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns.
