# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-21 10:40:29

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29537

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region tighten the encirclement
and expand the external control zone. As a result of decisive actions,
the settlements of Polnaya and Novoaleksandrovka (Kharkov region) have been brought under control.
💥
Inside the encirclement, strikes were delivered at
enemy manpower and hardware close to Buzovo, Krasny Yar, Reznikovo, Varvarovka, and Khripuny (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU's blocked formations suffered losses of more than 35 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, one artillery gun, and one ground robotic system.
▫️
Active combat operations are conducted to expand the external control zone
near Petropavlovka and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
Three attempts to deploy the AFU's reinforcement groups
from Velikiy Burluk to Olkhovatka (Kharkov region)
were thwarted.
📍
In addition,
in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades and assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine close to Sosnovka, Podsredneye and Sosnovy Bor.
🔥
In Sumy region,
formations of three mechanised brigades and one airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine
have been hit
close to Kiyanitsa, Vakalovshchina, Khoten, and Velikaya Chernetchina.
▪️
In total, the enemy has lost more than 265 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 24 motor vehicles, one MLRS, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade have been hit near Lozovoye, Volchiy Yar, Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic), Lesnaya Stenka, Nizsheye Solenoye and Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, 11 motor vehicles, four field artillery guns, including one U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer, one MLRS, and one U.S.-made AN/TPQ-36 counter-fire radar.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of four mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade have been hit near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, Nikolayevka, Krasny Molochar, Vysokoivanovka, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, and Chervonozaryanoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 215 troops, one tank, one armoured fighting vehicle, 19 motor vehicles, and eight field artillery guns.
