# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-23 09:40:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29571

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten encirclement and expand the external control zone.
The Russian troops have established control over Krasny Yar and Potikhonovo (Kharkov region) in the encirclement area.
💥
The battles for Buzovo (Kharkov region) continue.
Strikes were delivered at the enemy's formations near Varvarovka and Grachevka (Kharkov region).
The enemy's attempt to break through
the encirclement from the village of Chernoye (Kharkov region) was thwarted.
▪️
The AFU's blocked formations have lost more than 35 troops during the day.
💥
Active combat operations are underway to expand the external control zone
in the areas of Petropavlovka, Lozovaya, and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware
of three mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades near Podsredneye, Martynovka, Berezhnoye, Revolyutsionnoye, and Doroshenkovo (Kharkov region).
🔥
I
n Sumy region, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware
of a mechanised brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and four territorial defence brigades near Pershe Travnya, Yastrebinoye, Ulanovo, Ugroyedy, Doroshovka, Boyaro-Lezhachi, Radkovka (Sumy region), and Sumy.
▪️
In total, up to 210 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, and 13 motor vehicles have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a marine brigade near Rubtsy, Mayaki, Volchiy Yar, Lozovoye (Donetsk People's Republic), Monachinovka, Andreyevka, Grushevka, and Izyum (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 220 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 15 motor vehicles, as well as one Polish-made 155-mm Krab self-propelled artillery system, and one 155-mm Bogdana self-propelled artillery system.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group's elements took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on units of five mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades, a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade near Belenkoye, Novoselovka, Kramatorsk, Semenovka, Slavyansk, and Druzhkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 225 troops, a Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, 20 motor vehicles, and three field artillery guns.
↗️
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one UAV regiment of the AFU, two marine brigades, three National Guard brigades, and the Azov Special Forces Brigade near Belozerskoye, Andreyevka, Verovka, Maryevka, Novoandreyevka, Starorayskoye, Petrovskoye, Novofedorovka (Donetsk People's Republic), Mezhevaya and Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
💥
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), units of the 51st Army advancing along converging axes extended the outer ring of encirclement in the north-western part of the city.
The Group's marine assault detachments and units of the 76th Air Assault Division continued to destroy the AFU troops blocked in the western and eastern parts of Dobropolye.
🔥
They also foiled three attempts
of the 4th, 15th National Guard brigades, and 425th Assault Regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine to break through from an encirclement towards the settlement of Svyatogorovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, more than 40 Ukrainian troops have been neutralised, and 158 buildings have been mopped up.
▪️
In total, more than 410 troops, 10 armoured fighting vehicles, six motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
