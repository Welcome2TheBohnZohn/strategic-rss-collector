# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-01 10:10:24

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29734

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces tighten the encirclement around the AFU formations in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone. When conducting active offensive operations in the encirclement, Russian troops
liberated Nesternoye and Rublenoye in Kharkov region
.
💥
Fights are underway for the liberation of
Varvarovka, Grachevka, Reznikovo, and Serdobino (Kharkov region). Moreover, enemy formations are engaged near Nefedovka, Nikolayevka, and Khatneye (Kharkov region).
▪️
In the encirclement, up to 30 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and one unmanned ground vehicle have been neutralised during the day.
💥
While expanding the external control zone, Russian troops are operating to liberate Prikolotnoye in Kharkov region
.
🚩
Moreover, as a result of resolute actions, Vysokaya Yaruga in Kharkov region has been taken under Russian control
.
🔥
Manpower and hardware of
a heavy mechanised brigade, two motorised infantry brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a territorial defence brigade, and a national guard brigade have been hit near Zarozhnoye, Slatino, Liptsy, Pitomnik, Sosnovy Bor, and Tsirkuny (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at formations of
a heavy mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade, two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades close to Boyaro-Lezhachi, Velikaya Rybitsa, Korenek, Nikolskoye (Sumy region), and the city of Sumy.
▪️
In total, over 250 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, one field artillery gun, 26 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and a territorial defence brigade near Blagodatovka, Grushevka, Izyumskoye, Kovalevka, Osinovo, Proletarskoye in Kharkov region, Mayaki, and Prishib of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, one Canadian-made Senator armoured fighting vehicle, 10 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Troops of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, an unmanned systems brigade of the AFU, and a marine brigade have been hit near Belenkoye, Vasilyovskaya Pustosh, Druzhkovka, Klinovoye, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Semenovka, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to over 205 troops, a Bucephalus armoured personnel carrier, and 16 motor vehicles.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation
. Manpower and and materiel of three mechanised brigades, an unmanned systems brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades have been hit near Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Bokovoye, Gornyak, Lidino, Maryevka, Novogrigorovka, Novoyavlenka, Sergeyevka, and Shilovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic),
assault groups of the 51st Army are advancing in the northern, north-western, central, and eastern parts of the city. Over the past 24 hours, 93 buildings were liberated and more than 30 AFU militants were neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 460 troops, eight motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
