# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-22 09:45:14

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30731

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
As a result of intensive actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces gained control over the settlement of Sosnovy Bor in Kharkov region.
💥
Russian troops engaged
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades close to Novoselovka, Khatneye, Ploskoye, Rubezhnoye, Podsredneye, and Fedorovka in Kharkov region.
🔥
In Sumy region,
the Group's units
inflicted losses on
formations of a mechanised brigade, two airmobile brigades of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades near Khoten, Radkovka, Pushkarevka, Krasnopolye, Stepanovka, and Spasskoye (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost over 120 troops, three motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops hit manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades close to Khomino, Chervony Oskol, Gusinka, Staroverovka, and Peski-Radkovskiye of Kharkov region.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 210 troops and 10 motor vehicles.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's elements engaged formations of four mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades, and an assault regiment of the AFU near Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Slavyansk, Belenkoye, Raygorodok, Novoselovka, and Nikolayevka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 145 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, one Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, 21 motor vehicles, and one 155-mm Bogdana howitzer.
↗️
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out active offensive operations.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade of the AFU, two marine brigades, four national guard brigades, and the Azov special forces brigade close to Rayskoye, Privolye, Novokrivorozhye, Andreyevka, Sergeyevka, Novodonetskoye, Stepanovka, Lvovka, Kuroyedovka, and Kamenka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 345 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
