# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-08 09:15:14

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29276

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to expand the outer ring of the encircled enemy, as a result, the control over the settlement of Dolgenkoye of Kharkov region has been gained.
▫️
The inner ring continues to shrink, and
Berezniki of Kharkov region has been taken under control by Russian troops.
💥
In addition,
inside the 'pocket' units of the enemy were hit
close to Malaya Volchya, Varvarovka, Buzovo, and Nefedovka in Kharkov region.
▪️
During the day, the AFU losses amounted to up to 30 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, two ATVs, and three unmanned aerial vehicle command posts. All attempts of the enemy to break through the encirclement have been thwarted.
🔥
In Sumy region, Russian elements engaged
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Luzhki, Ulanovo, Ivolzhanskoye, and Kiyanitsa (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, up to 180 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and eight motor vehicles were neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade near Osinovo, Petrovka, Kovalevka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Glubokaya Makatikha, Krymki, Bogorodichnoye, Sidorovo, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 190 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted fire damage on formations of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the AFU near Slavyansk, Cherevkovka, Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Nikanorovka, Nikolayevka, and Alekseyevo-Druzhkovka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 190 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 17 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
