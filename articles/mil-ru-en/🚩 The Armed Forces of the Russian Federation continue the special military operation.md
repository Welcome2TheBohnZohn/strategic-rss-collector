# 🚩 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-09 10:47:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29296

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
🚩
In Kharkov region, units of the Sever Group of Forces are shrinking the inner ring of the enemy's formations.
As a result of active actions, Ozernoye (Kharkov region) was taken under control.
💥
In addition,
the AFU units were hit in the 'pocket' of Malaya Volchya, Commissarovo, and Buzovo (Kharkov region).
🔥
On the outer ring
, enemy manpower and hardware were neutralised close to Novoaleksandrovka, Prikolotnoye, and Olkhovatka (Kharkov region).
▪️
During the day, the AFU losses amounted to up to 35 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, two ATVs, and four ground-based robotic systems. Assault groups thwarted two enemy attempts to break the ring close to Sliznevo and Dolzhanka (Kharkov region).
💥
In Kharkov region
, units of two mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades of the AFU, one territorial defence brigade, and one national guard brigade have been hit near Lozovaya, Ternovaya, Doroshenkovo, Karasevka, Moskalevka, and Liptsy (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Partizanskoye, Chervonoye, Nikolskoye, Stepanovka, and Stetskovka (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 205 troops, 22 motor vehicles, and one RADA RPS-42 counter-fire radar station in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
📍
The Zapad Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades near Nizhsheye Solennoye, Chervony Oskol, Andreyevka, Monachinovka (Kharkov region), Glubokaya Makatikha, Karpovka, Raygorodok, and Starodubovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 200 troops, 17 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line.
Formations of five mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, three airmobile brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one air assault brigade of the AFU have been hit near Slavyansk, Gornyak, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Druzhkovka, Klinovoye, Vysokoivanovka, Nikolaypolye, and Krany Molochar (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 210 troops, two Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, 18 motor vehicles, and one 122-mm D-30 howitzer.
