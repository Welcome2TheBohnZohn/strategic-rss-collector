🚩
During the week, as a result of active offensive actions, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Novovodyanoye and Veseloye Pole
in the Donetsk People's Republic.
🔥
The losses were inflicted on
formations of five mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one unmanned systems brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and four national guard brigades.
▫️
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), units of the 51st Army, developing an pincer movement in the west and east of the city, continue to tighten the encirclement of the formations of 425th Assault Regiment of the AFU, the 4th and 15th National Guard Brigades. All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement and perform personnel rotation were thwarted.
▪️
Since the beginning of the enemy's encirclement in Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), more than 375 militants and two pick-up trucks have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, more than 3,125 troops, 17 armoured fighting vehicles, 74 motor vehicles, seven field artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
🚩
Over the past week, the Vostok Group's units have continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence, the settlement of Yegorovka (Zaporozhye region) was liberated.
💥
Russian elements hit
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,740 troops, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, 55 motor vehicles, and five field artillery guns.
📍
Last week, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Up to 220 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 74 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and seven electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
▫️

air defence systems shot down
37 guided aerial bombs, four U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, four Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 5,031 fixed-wing UAVs.
