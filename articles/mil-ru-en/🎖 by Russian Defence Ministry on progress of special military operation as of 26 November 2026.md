# 🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 26 November 2026

Published: 2026-11-26 12:01:27

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30792

🎖
🎖
by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 26 November 2026
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, strikes have been delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one national guard brigade close to Sosnovka, Velikiy Burluk, Kolodeznoye, and Pitomnik.
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigade
have been hit
close to Obody, Vakalovshchina, Zhovtnevoye, and Novopetrovka.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 155 troops, six motor vehicles, three electronic warfare stations, and one electronic reconnaissance station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Osadkovka, Vasilevka, Maliyevka (Kharkov region) and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 15 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU have been hit near Cherevkovka, Vysokoivanovka, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, and Belenkoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 175 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 11 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out active offensive operations.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades near Kalinino, Kuritsyno, Krinitsy, Novopavlovka, and Zavido-Kudashevo (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 340 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, seven armoured fighting vehicles, six motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one air assault brigade, one assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Veseloye, Novotroitskoye, Novoivanovka, Novorozovka (Zaporozhye region) and Gavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 200 troops and eight motor vehicles.
↗️
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted fire damage on formations of one mechanised brigade of the AFU near Kamyshevakha (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 20 troops and 16 motor vehicles of the AFU have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have hit
power infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, production workshops and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and ground robotic systems, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries in 146 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four U.S.-made JDAM aerial bombs, two Neptune long-range guided missiles, and 872 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
