🚩
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Chervonaya Krinitsa (Zaporozhye region).
💥
Strikes were launched at
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two assault brigades, two air assault brigades, four assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Barvinovka, Vasinovka, Nikolskoye, Obshcheye, Vasilyevskoye (Zaporozhye region), and Shevyakino (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▫️
The enemy has lost more than 295 troops, three motor vehicles, and one U.S.-made AN/TPQ-50 counter-fire station.
🚩
As a result of the decisive actions of the Dnepr Group of Forces units, Novopavlovka (Zaporozhye region) was liberated.
💥
Fire damage was inflicted on
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one artillery brigade, and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine close to Orekhov, Chervony Zhovten, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▫️
Up to 65 troops, 16 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
Ukrainian logistics centres, energy and transport infrastructure facilities, used by the AFU, UAV assembly workshops and depots of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 156 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
13 guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 1,050 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
