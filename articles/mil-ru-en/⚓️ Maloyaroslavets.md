:
5th in the series
▫️
The Project 677 Lada diesel-electric submarine was
laid at the Admiralty Shipyards in St. Petersburg.
💬
Commander-in-Chief of the Russian Navy Admiral of the Fleet Aleksandr Moiseyev
stressed that the submarines of the project are the most modern in their class, thanks to their tactical and combat specifications.
✅
The fifth bastion:
the submarine Maloyaroslavets is the fifth serial vessel of this project developed by the Rubin Central Design Bureau for Marine Engineering.
📑
A name with background:
according to the tradition of the Lada Project, ships are named after Russian cities. The new submarine is named after a City of Military Glory in Kaluga region. It played a key role in the battles of 1812 and the Great Patriotic War.
