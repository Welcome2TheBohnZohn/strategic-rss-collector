'The military signalmen always come to the rescue'
Military signalmen of the 20th Combined Arms Army (Zapad Group of Forces) shoulder to shoulder with local specialists are repairing what the enemy has destroyed. Maintenance of high towers, repair of fibre-optic lines after shelling takes enormous efforts and time.
💬
Andrei, the chief engineer of a telecommunication company,
does not hide emotions, telling about such interaction:
'Military signalmen always come to the rescue if there is some sort of emergency situation in which it requires to restore lines or communication equipment. Though their work is mostly behind the scenes, it’s what keeps us feeling safe and connected to the rest of the world'.
