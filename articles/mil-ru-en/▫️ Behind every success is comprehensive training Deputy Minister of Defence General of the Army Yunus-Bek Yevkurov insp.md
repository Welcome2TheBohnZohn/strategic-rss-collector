Behind every success is comprehensive training
Deputy Minister of Defence General of the Army Yunus-Bek Yevkurov inspected the facilities of the training and material base of the Combat Training Centre.
He was presented with the following:
▫️
software and hardware systems for training military personnel,
▫️
advanced models of unmanned systems, weapons, and equipment,
▫️
training systems from industrial enterprises.
💬
Head of the Combat Training Directorate of the Main Directorate of Combat Training of the Russian Armed Forces Major General Vyacheslav Kozlovsky reported to the Deputy Minister of Defence on the results of testing of items developed within the framework of the 'Innovators of the Armed Forces 2026' competition.
📊
A total of
62
items were presented, including UGVs, UAVs, counter-UAV systems, and electronic components. Thirty of them successfully passed the trials.
🤝
The developer enterprises confirmed the novelty of the projects presented and examined the possibilities for integrating them into serial production.
✅
Based on the results of the field trials, the new training aids
were found to be highly effective;
recommendations for their further refinement were issued, and the procedure for their subsequent acceptance into service was defined.
