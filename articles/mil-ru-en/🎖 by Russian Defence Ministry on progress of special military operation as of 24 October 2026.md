↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence
. Russian elements inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Brovki (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vesely Gai, Blakitnoye, Volnyanka, Barvinovka, and Voskresenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 205 troops and 11 motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation
. Strike were delivered at formations of a mechanised brigade, a mountain assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Kirovo, Orekhov, and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, 15 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on energy and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, depots and launch sites of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 147 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
one French-made SCALP air-launched cruise missile, four Neptune long-range cruise missiles, and 1,297 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
