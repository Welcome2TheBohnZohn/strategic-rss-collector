# ✈️ Strategic altitude Tu-95ms aircraft crews performed a planned flight over the neutral waters of Bering and Chukchi seas.

Published: 2026-10-26 12:30:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30290

Strategic altitude
✈️
Tu-95ms aircraft crews
performed a planned flight over the neutral waters of Bering and Chukchi seas.
⏱️
The flight
of the Russian Aerospace Forces' long-range aircraft
lasted more than 13 hours and the aircraft were refuelled right in the sky
. It was escorted by Su-35s fighter jets.
▫️
At certain stages of the route, the aircraft were escorted by foreign fighter jets.
Crews of long-range aviation regularly conduct flights over the neutral waters of the Arctic, the North Atlantic, the Pacific Ocean, Black and Baltic seas. All Russian Aerospace Forces aircraft flights are carried out in strict compliance with international rules on the use of airspace.
