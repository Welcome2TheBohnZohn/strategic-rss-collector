# 🤝 Long voyage, warm welcome Ships of the Pacific Fleet arrived with a business call in Indonesia.

Published: 2026-10-01 14:21:13

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29738

🤝
Long voyage, warm welcome
Ships of the Pacific Fleet arrived with a business call in Indonesia.
As part of the visit, working meetings, cultural and sporting events are planned with Indonesian counterparts.
A ship detachment of the Pacific Fleet comprising the frigate Marshal Shaposhnikov, the large anti-submarine ship Admiral Tributs, and the tanker Pechenga left Vladivostok on 10 September for missions in the Asia-Pacific region.
