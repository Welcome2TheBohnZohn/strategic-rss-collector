# ❗️ Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch UAV strikes

Published: 2026-10-23 20:01:02

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30246

❗️
Today, during the daytime,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch UAV strikes
against logistics centres and port infrastructure facilities, as well as vessels used to deliver military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike, in the city of Chernigov,
the Aviation New Technologies LLC transport-logistical centre,
which territory was used store military materiel, UAVs and their components, as well as dual-use cargo intended to support the AFU,
was hit.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering cargoes to the port of Odessa to support the AFU
was hit
at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
