# ▫️ Ready to perform tasks In accordance with the Strategic Missile Forces 2026 training plan, the Strategic Missile Forces Command Commission conducts

Published: 2026-10-08 07:04:42

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29911

Ready to perform tasks
▫️
🚀
In accordance with the Strategic Missile Forces 2026 training plan, the Strategic Missile Forces Command Commission conducts
a comprehensive inspection of the Strategic Missile Forces formation headquartered in Vladimir
including a command post exercise with a silo-based missile formation led by the Strategic Missile Forces Commander
Colonel General Sergei Karakayev.
📑
The Strategic Missile Forces Command Commission will assess the combat training level of the command, control bodies, and crews. In addition, support forces (with deployment in the assigned areas) as well as assigned units of the Moscow Military District were involved in the practical tasks performance.
🔥
In addition to a silo-based rocket formation,
autonomous rocket launchers of mobile-based missile formations of the Strategic Missile Forces were deployed
on combat patrolling routes.
▫️
During the exercise,
the Yars transporter erector launcher crews
train to perform marches, missile divisions dispersion including field positions change, overcoming contaminated areas, use of engineering equipment, as well as camouflage and security organisation.
🎮
The Army aviation and the Strategic Missile Forces' UAV units
are used to reconnoitre the routes of hardware, to search and detect mock saboteurs.
🔹
The main purpose of the exercise is
to improve the coherence of military command and control bodies.
⬆️
During the inspection, the formation's personnel demonstrate
a high level of combat training and readiness to perform tasks according to their purpose.
