‘We were sent to the slaughter’: Ukrainian POWs reveal AFU hidden side
💬
The servicemen of the 78th Air Assault Brigade and the 28th Mechanised Brigade of the AFU, captured in Slavyansk-Kramatorsk direction,
spoke about the difficult situation on the front line and how their command treated them.
▫️
According to Vladimir Yankov, the AFU's rifleman,
the victims' bodies are not evacuated from the roads
. The prisoner believes that the command intentionally leaves the corpses there in order to avoid paying compensation to the families and to keep money for themselves.
▫️
His comrade, Aleksandr Tishchenko,
complained about a severe food supply shortage
: the fighters have been living without food or water for weeks and were forced to search for food in abandoned basements.
▫️
Another prisoner, Dmitry Gorobtsets, the 28th Mechanised Brigade's serviceman, said that company commander sent a group to the position, ensuring that it was safe there, but the servicemen immediately got under heavy fire. Before the deployment,
the officer confiscated the subordinates' passports and bank cards and stated sincerely that they would no longer need money,
as there was no chance to survive.
