# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched UAV strikes against Ukrainian

Published: 2026-10-21 06:15:18

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30180

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
launched UAV strikes against Ukrainian
logistics centres, port infrastructure and power substations which provide their functioning, as well as vessels
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets were engaged:
▫️
in Poltava,
a depot centre
used to store medium- and long-range UAVs;
▫️
in Kiev,
the Nivki PS-330 power substation
used to provide functioning of defence industry enterprises of Ukraine and logistics centres.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo to Ukraine
was hit at sea on its way to the port of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
