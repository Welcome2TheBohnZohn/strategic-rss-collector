Destroyed without a hitch
The liberation of Polnaya in Kharkov region was the result of clear and cohesive actions of the 82nd Motorised Rifle Regiment. It was extremely difficult to approach to the settlement due to the abundance of enemy drones.
To outwit the enemy, the servicemen advanced in small groups and used camouflage.
💥
The operation success was provided by powerful air and ground support.
‘Sure, not without the help of our UAV teams: FPVs, quadcopters, reconnaissance drones. Then, artillery fired. We mopped the enemy,’ said the commander of the assault company with the call sign 'Yelets'.
