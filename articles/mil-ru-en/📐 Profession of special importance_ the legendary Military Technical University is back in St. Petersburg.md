Profession of special importance: the legendary Military Technical University is back in St. Petersburg
🤝
On Knowledge Day, the Deputy Russian Minister of Defence Pavel Fradkov
inspected the readiness of the re-established Military Engineering-Technical University
and congratulated its cadets.
💬
Pavel Fradkov stressed that great prospects for service to the benefit of the Fatherland lay ahead of them, and
their professionalism would directly strengthen the country's defence capabilities.
More than 400 people entered the university in 2026. The cadets will undergo training at 21 modern training complexes.
▫️
Godspeed, future engineers!
