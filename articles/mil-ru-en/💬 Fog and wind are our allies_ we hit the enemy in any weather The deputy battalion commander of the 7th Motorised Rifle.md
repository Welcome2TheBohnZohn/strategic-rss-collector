Fog
and wind are our allies: we hit the enemy in any weather
💬
The deputy battalion commander of the 7th Motorised Rifle Brigade with the call sign 'Sokol' spoke about the battles for forest belts and settlements in Slavyansk-Kramatorsk agglomeration:
'We come out winners after every fight. They are focusing on drones. If weather conditions begin to change, foggy or very windy weather, which prevents 'birds' from flying, then that's the end. The enemy is deprived of eyes and hands'.
