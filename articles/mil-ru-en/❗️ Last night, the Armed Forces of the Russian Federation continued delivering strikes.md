# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes

Published: 2026-10-31 06:25:53

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30368

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes
by UAVs against logistics and power infrastructure facilities used in the interests of Ukraine's defence industry, as well as ships employed for the needs of the AFU.
💥
As a result of strikes, the following targets
were hit:
📍
in Kiev:
▫️
combined heat and power plant (CHPP-6)
, which supplies electric power to military logistics facilities and storage sites of armament and military hardware;
📍
in Kharkov:
▫️
workshops within the Proton industrial zone
, where components for AFU armaments and military hardware were produced;
▫️
depots
equipped as assembly and storage sites for unmanned aerial vehicles.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering cargo intended for the AFU was hit at sea 18 km south of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
