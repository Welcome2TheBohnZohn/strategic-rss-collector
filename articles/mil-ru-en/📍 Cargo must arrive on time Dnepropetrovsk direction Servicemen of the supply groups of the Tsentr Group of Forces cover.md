Cargo must arrive on time
📍
Dnepropetrovsk direction
🚛
Servicemen of the supply groups of the Tsentr Group of Forces cover over 100 kilometres a day
in Dnepropetrovsk direction. Every tour to the front line takes place under the sights of enemy drones.
▫️
The enemy hides 'awaiter' ambush drones along the roads and also attacks the convoys with fixed-wing strike drones.
💥
To break through,
the crews continuously scan the sky
. Upon spotting an 'awaiter' drone or an incoming attack drone, gunners immediately open heavy fire and shoot down the targets on approach.
🤝
Thanks to the composure of the drivers and the marksmanship of the gunners,
the cargo reaches the front line right on schedule.
🔹
