# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a group strike using high‑precision ground‑based weapons and long‑range attack unmanned aerial vehicles agains

Published: 2026-09-05 07:15:19

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29225

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a group strike using high‑precision ground‑based weapons and long‑range attack unmanned aerial vehicles against
metallurgical plants in the Dnepropetrovsk region that supply military defence enterprises, as well as against logistics centres and power substations in the city of Nikolayev, Nikolayev and Odessa regions.
📍
In Dnepropetrovsk region, the following facilities were damaged:
▪️
the Dneprodzerzhinsk metallurgical plant ('Kametstal') and the Dnepr metallurgical plant (Dnepr Metallurgical Plant), which are the largest suppliers of metal components for Ukraine’s military production.
📍
In Nikolayev and Nikolayev region, the following were hit:
▪️
a logistics center used to store military property, and the Nikolayevskay power substation.
📍
In Odessa region, the following were hit:
▪️
in the settlement of Dachnoye – a logistics center used to store military goods;
▪️
in the port of Chernomorsk, a dry cargo ship carrying weapons and military equipment for the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
📍
On the evening of 4 September, the following facilities were hit in Kiev region:
▪️
the Svyatopetrovsky logistics center, which was used to store military equipment;
▪️
two fuel storage and distribution sites for the Armed Forces of Ukraine in the military town of Blistavitsa, near the Gostomel airfield.
📍
In addition, a radio and radio‑technical intelligence center
was hit in settlement of Brovary.
