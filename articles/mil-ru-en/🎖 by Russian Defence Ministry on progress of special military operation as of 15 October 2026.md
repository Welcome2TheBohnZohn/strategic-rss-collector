Units of the Tsentr Group of Forces inflicted damage
on personnel and equipment of four mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Ukrainian Armed Forces, a marine brigade and two national guard brigades near Krutoyarovka in Dnepropetrovsk region, and Belozerskoye, Novoyavlenka, Druzhkovka, Starovarvarovka, Veselaya Gora, Roganskoye, Yuryevka and Gornyak in the Donetsk People's Republic.
⚡️
In Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army advanced in the north-western part of the city. The Group's assault units continued their offensive in the western part of the settlement.
📊
Over the course of the day, 31 buildings were liberated and up to 15 Ukrainian servicemen were neutralised.
▪️
In total, in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group, the losses of Ukrainian armed formations amounted to up to 345 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, and 12 motor vehicles.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted damage on personnel and hardware of two air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Ukrainian Armed Forces and two marine brigades close to Tarasovka, Ivanovka, Kolomiytsy, Chernenkovo, Vasilkovka, and Podgavrilovka in Dnepropetrovsk region, and Mirolyubovka and Rayskoye in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
The Ukrainian Armed Forces lost over 190 troops, six motor vehicles, a Grad MLRS combat vehicle, and an electronic warfare station.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They hit formations of two mechanised brigades and a mountain assault brigade of the Ukrainian Armed Forces close to Orekhov, Balabino and Kushugum in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 30 Ukrainian troops and 13 motor vehicles were neutralised.
