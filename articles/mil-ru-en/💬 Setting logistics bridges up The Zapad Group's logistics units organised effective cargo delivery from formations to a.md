Setting logistics bridges up
The Zapad Group's logistics units organised effective cargo delivery from formations to assault groups using drones.
💬
A UAV platoon commander with the call sign ‘Zawgar’
noted that
the logistics units' effectiveness increased significantly since the beginning of using drones:
‘We increased twice delivery volumes, reduced the manpower and motor vehicles losses risks. While a ground robotic system is delivering a large volume of supply, FPV drones distribute to detachments small but very important cargo: medical supplies, communications, water, food supplies.’
