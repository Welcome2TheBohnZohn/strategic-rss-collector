# ⚓️ Corvette Grozny becomes fifth diamond of Pacific Fleet A ceremonial launch of the Project 20380 corvette Grozny took place

Published: 2026-10-24 09:03:02

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30252

Corvette Grozny becomes fifth diamond of Pacific Fleet
⚓️
A ceremonial launch of the Project 20380 corvette Grozny took place
at the OJSC Amur Shipbuilding Plant. This is already the fifth serial-production vessel built for the Pacific Fleet to a design developed by designers of the Almaz Central Marine Design Bureau.
👉
The ceremony was also attended by Governor of Khabarovsk region Dmitry Demeshin and Commander of the Pacific Fleet Admiral Viktor Liina.
▫️
The OJSC Amur Shipbuilding Plant
is currently building 10 vessels
of various projects for the Pacific Fleet.
