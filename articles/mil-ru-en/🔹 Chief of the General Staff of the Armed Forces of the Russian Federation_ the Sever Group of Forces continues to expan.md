Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation: the Sever Group of Forces continues to expand the security zone
Report
by Valery Gerasimov:
The Sever Group of Forces continues to expand the security zone along the Russian-Ukrainian border in the border areas of Sumy and Kharkov regions of Ukraine. The elimination of the 2,000-strong enemy task force surrounded in the north-eastern part of Kharkov region has been completed. A total of 461 square kilometres and 28 settlements have come under Russian control.
The offensive in Sumy region is continuing. Assault units of the Sever Group of Forces are advancing towards Sumy. A large enemy task force has been destroyed in the vicinity of Sadki.
