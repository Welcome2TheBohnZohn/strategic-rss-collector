# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against energy infrastructure facilities in Ukraine and vessels involved 

Published: 2026-11-08 14:50:39

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30500

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to carry out strikes
using unmanned aerial vehicles against energy infrastructure facilities in Ukraine and vessels involved in supporting the AFU.
💥
In the daytime, the strikes hit:
▫️
the Kamenskaya 330 kV substation
, which provided power to defence industry enterprises in Dnepropetrovsk region;
▫️
in the port of Chernomorsk (Odessa region),
a container ship
that had delivered dual-use equipment and cargo to Ukraine;
▫️
in the Black Sea, south-west of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship
carrying weapons and military equipment for the AFU.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
