# ❗️ Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against transport infrastructure facilities

Published: 2026-10-15 16:04:34

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30066

❗️
Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued delivering strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against transport infrastructure facilities
used in the interests of the Ukrainian Armed Forces, as well as ships involved in delivering military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes
in the port of Yuzhny in Odessa region
,
a transport infrastructure facility
used in the interests of the Ukrainian Armed Forces was destroyed.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship was hit
at sea; it had been delivering military and dual-use cargo to the port of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
