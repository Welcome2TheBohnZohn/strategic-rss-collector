↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Shevyakino, Prosyanaya (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasinovka, Timoshevka, Nikolskoye, and Chervony Yar (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 325 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, three armoured fighting vehicles, and six motor vehicles.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade near Kamyshevakha, Malokaterinovka, Antonovka, Dimitrovo, Grigorovka, Novoyakovlevka, and Orekhov (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 50 troops, 22 motor vehicles, three electronic warfare stations, and one electronic reconnaissance station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on the Ukrainian transport and logistics infrastructure used by the AFU, assembly and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in the 144 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four guided aerial bombs and 400 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
