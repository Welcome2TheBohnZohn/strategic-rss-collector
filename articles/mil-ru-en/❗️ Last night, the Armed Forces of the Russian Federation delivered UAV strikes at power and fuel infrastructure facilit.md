# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes at power and fuel infrastructure facilities, as well as vessels

Published: 2026-11-24 06:02:07

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30755

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
delivered UAV strikes at power and fuel infrastructure facilities, as well as vessels
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result,
the following targets were hit:
▫️
a 110 kV power substation
providing operation of the defence industry facilities of Ukraine in the settlement Priluki (Chernigov region);
▫️
a dry cargo ship
at sea delivering military cargo south-west of Odessa.

Russian Defence Ministry
