Across waves of Caspian Sea under St. Andrew’s flag
📑
More than three centuries ago, on 15 November 1722, Peter I founded the Caspian Flotilla to protect the southern borders.
Today, its modern ships and marines continue the traditions of their ancestors, fighting with honour in the most dangerous areas in the special military operation zone.
💬
Commander-in-Chief of the Russian Navy Admiral of the Fleet Aleksandr Moiseyev congratulated the sailors of the Caspian Flotilla
and noted that the sailors and marines of the Caspian Flotilla are an example of valiant service to the Fatherland during the special military operation, and they demonstrate bravery, courage, fortitude and self‑sacrifice in fierce battles.
Congrats, Caspian sailors!
