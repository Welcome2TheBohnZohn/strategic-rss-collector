# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against power facilities and logistics infrastructure, as well as vessels used in 

Published: 2026-11-25 15:01:05

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30780

❗️
Today,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched strikes
by unmanned aerial vehicles against power facilities and logistics infrastructure, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
a 330 kV power substation in Kharkov, a 150 kV power substation in Verkhnedneprovskoye of Dnepropetrovsk region and a 110 kV power substation in Malin of Zhitomir region,
supplying electric power to the defence industry of Ukraine;
▫️
the Krivorozhskaya thermal power plant in Zelenodolsk of Dnepropetrovsk region
, which supports the operation of enterprises of defence industry of Ukraine;
▫️
the Nerubayskoye logistics centre in the village of Nerubayskoye and the Blum logistics centre in the village of Mirnoye of Odessa region
, which stored and distributed military cargo to the AFU;
▫️
the Novaya Pochta logistics terminal in the village of Belaya Tserkov in Kiev region,
used to store dual-use cargo supplied to the AFU by Western countries.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo to the AFU was hit east of Odessa in the Black Sea waters.
