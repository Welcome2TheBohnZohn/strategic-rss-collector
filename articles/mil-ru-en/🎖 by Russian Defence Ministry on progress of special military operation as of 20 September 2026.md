The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation
. Russian troops hit manpower and materiel of two mechanised brigades, an unmanned systems regiment, an assault battalion of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, the Azov Special Forces Brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Verovka, Dobropolye, Maryevka, Novogrigorovka, Novofedorovka, Novoyavlenka, and Starorayskoye (Donetsk People's Republic), Mezhevaya and Novopavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 455 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, one radar, and two electronic warfare stations.
💥
The Vostok Group of Forces continue to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence
. Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, four air assault brigades, one airmobile brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade have been hit close to Gavrilovka, Velikomikhaylovka, Shevchenko in Dnepropetrovsk region, Dudnikovo, Novokasyanovka, and Obshcheye of Zaporozhye region.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 280 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and eight motor vehicles.
↗️
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Troops of two mechanised brigades and one unmanned systems brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Kushugum, Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, Yurkovka (Zaporozhye region), and Novoraysk (Kherson region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, 21 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, strike UAVs, Missile Troops and Artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on transport infrastructure facilities, ammunition depots, military cargoes, drone manufacturing shops and their storage facilities as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations, and foreign mercenaries in 146 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
eight guided aerial bombs, four U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, eight Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 1,951 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicle.
