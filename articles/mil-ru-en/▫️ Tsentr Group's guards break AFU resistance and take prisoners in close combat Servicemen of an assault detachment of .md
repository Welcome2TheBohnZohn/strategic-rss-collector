Tsentr Group's guards break AFU resistance and take prisoners in close combat
▫️
Servicemen of an assault detachment of the 80th Guards Tank Regiment (90th Guards Tank Division, Tsentr Group of Forces)
captured several AFU servicemen
in Dnepropetrovsk direction of the special military operation.
▫️
During a combat mission, the assault detachments
detect the enemy's camouflaged positions with manpower and destroy them
both from the air with FPV drones and in direct contact at the positions with fire from organic weapons.
❗️
Before destroying the enemy,
Russian servicemen always offer Ukrainian soldiers the choice to surrender voluntarily.
The stormers try to take the captured servicemen to their unit,
keeping them alive even under the strikes of AFU drones.
