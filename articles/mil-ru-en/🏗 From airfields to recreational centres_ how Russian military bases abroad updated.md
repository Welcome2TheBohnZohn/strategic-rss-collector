From airfields to recreational centres: how Russian military bases abroad updated
Deputy Defence Minister of the Russian Federation Lieutenant General Pavel Fradkov inspected the development of the infrastructure of Russian military bases in the Republic of Kyrgyzstan and the Republic of Tajikistan.
🏗
Lieutenant General Pavel Fradkov
inspected the development of airfield infrastructure and social facilities
at the 999th Guard Air Base. Specialists of the Military Construction Complex
reported on the implementation of the development plan at the airbase.
🏛
In the Republic of Tajikistan, on the territory of the 201st Russian military base,
the construction of a cultural and leisure centre
for servicemen and their families
has been completed.
The Deputy Defence Minister of the Russian Federation
has ordered to complete the construction of a new modern canteen
for military personnel by the end of the year.
🏋️
In the area of the 1109th optical-electronic hub,
construction of a cultural and leisure centre has also been completed.
It is planned to be opened immediately after the delivery of sports equipment.
📋
Following the working visit, Lieutenant General Pavel Fradkov
gave a series of instructions for the implementation of plans to develop military and military social infrastructure.
