Anna Tsivileva: level of medical care provided to Russian servicemen meets the highest global standards
The fifth Scientific and Practical Forum on Surgery of Armed Conflicts ‘Gunshot wound. Surgery of injuries’ has been started in Moscow. Leading doctors of the Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Health, along with specialists from 16 countries, have gathered at the venue.
💬
State Secretary — Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Anna Tsivileva, speaking at the ceremonial opening of the International scientific and practical forum, noted that
the level of medical care provided to Russian service members meets the highest global standards.
‘The current level of medical care provided to servicemen and civilian population fully meets the highest global standards’, said Anna Tsivileva.
▫️
The Deputy Head of the Russian Military Department also noted that
during the special military operation, the system of providing care to the wounded has undergone comprehensive modernisation.
To maintain and further raise this standard, Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov ordered to initiate a large-scale project entitled ‘Military Medicine of the Future’, aimed at comprehensive improvement of the quality of medical care at all stages – from the frontline to restorative treatment and rehabilitation.
🏥
In addition,
a programme to establish modern high-tech prosthetics centres is being actively implemented
in all regions of Russia.
‘All the necessary conditions have been created in the country's military hospitals for performing the most complex reconstructive operations
using additive manufacturing and tissue engineering methods. Russian science today demonstrates a high level of professionalism, which is confirmed by the scale and depth of fundamental research conducted at the country's leading research institutions’, said the deputy head of the Russian military department.
