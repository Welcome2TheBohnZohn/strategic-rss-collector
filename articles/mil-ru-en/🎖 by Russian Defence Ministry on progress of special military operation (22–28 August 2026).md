🚩
During the week, as a result of intensive offensive actions by units of the Tsentr Group of Forces,
the settlements of Annovka and Kucherov Yar, the Donetsk People's Republic, were liberated.
💥
Strikes were launched at formations
of three mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, an airmobile brigade, an airborne brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and four national guard brigades.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,540 troops, 12 armoured fighting vehicles, 50 motor vehicles, eight field artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare and counter-fire stations.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continue to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Last week, the settlements of Dolinka, Nezhenka, and Shevchenkovskoye in Zaporozhye region were liberated.
💥
The Russian troops inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware
of a mechanised brigade, two assault brigades, two air assault brigades, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
The AFU losses in the area of responsibility of the Vostok Group of Forces amounted to more than 2,260 troops, 19 armoured fighting vehicles, and 37 motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
The Group hit manpower and military hardware of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Over the past week, the enemy sustained losses of more than 260 troops, 118 motor vehicles, three uncrewed surface vehicles, and 16 electronic warfare and counter-fire stations.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
80 guided aerial bombs, 15 U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, 14 Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 5,989 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
