the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike with ground-launched high-precision weapons and long-range strike drones. As a result of the strike, enterprises of the defence industry
, facilities storing cruise missiles, unmanned aerial vehicles, and a data centre in Kiev and Kiev region were hit.
📍
The following targets
were hit
in Kiev:
▫️
a Datagroup's Data Centre;
▫️
a Fire Point logistics hub
, which produces and stores long-range fixed-wing UAV components as well as engines for Flamingo FP-5 ground-launched cruise missiles;
▫️
an industry enterprise (Khimvolokno Open Joint-Stock Company)
. It produces components for long- and medium-range UAVs;
▫️
a motive power depot
in Kiev where Flamingo ground-based FP-5 cruise missiles as well as FP-9 ballistic missile components are warehoused and stored.
📍
In Irpen (Kiev region), the Planet Plastic factory,
which produces polymers for the defence industry, was hit.
💥
Moreover, 130 km south-west of the port of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship was engaged
. It was transporting military and dual-use cargo.
