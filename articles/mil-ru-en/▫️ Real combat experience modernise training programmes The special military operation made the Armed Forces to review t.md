Real combat experience modernise training programmes
The special military operation made the Armed Forces to review their training system. The main goal is to use modern technologies and save the soldiers' lives. There is no longer room for outdated theory at the training grounds: the programmes are changed by returning assaultmen, and instructors get versatile.
