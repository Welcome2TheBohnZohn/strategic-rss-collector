CIS defence ministers pay tribute to Soviet soldiers in Cholpon-Ata
🤝
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov and the heads of delegations of the Council of Defence Ministers of the member states of the Commonwealth of Independent States
laid flowers at the monument to Soviet soldiers
in the Central Park of Culture and Recreation in the city of Cholpon-Ata.
The participants in the ceremony observed a minute of silence to honour the memory of the soldiers who fell during the Great Patriotic War. The ceremony concluded with a solemn march by a company of the Honour Guard.
🌳
In addition, the defence ministers of the Commonwealth countries took part in a joint tree-planting ceremony.
