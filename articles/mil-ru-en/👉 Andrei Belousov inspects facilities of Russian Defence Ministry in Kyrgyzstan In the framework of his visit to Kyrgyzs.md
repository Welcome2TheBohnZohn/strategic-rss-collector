# 👉 Andrei Belousov inspects facilities of Russian Defence Ministry in Kyrgyzstan In the framework of his visit to Kyrgyzstan, Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov

Published: 2026-10-14 16:01:52

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30038

Andrei
Belousov inspects facilities of Russian Defence Ministry in Kyrgyzstan
👉
In the framework of his visit to Kyrgyzstan, Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov
inspected facilities of the testing base
located on Lake Issyk-Kul.
✅
The Minister
checked the state of the base equipment
, which includes boats and vessels of various projects prepared for testing.
➡️
The head of the testing base reported to the Russian Defence Minister that the main task of the base
is to conduct serial tests of anti-submarine weapons of the Russian Navy
as well as systems of defence against torpedoes mounted on surface ships and submarines.
🎮
Modern unmanned aerial vehicles enabled to carry out real-time control and surveillance tests.
📍
The base is a unique Russian military facility
located at an altitude of 1,609 metres above mean sea level. Lake Issyk-Kul remains unfrozen all year round and its water is close to sea. It is ideal for testing sea weapons and diving gear.
💬
At the end of the inspection, the Russian Defence Minister
held a meeting with the base command
. He heard a report on current and future tasks, the progress of weapons testing, and development of infrastructure.
