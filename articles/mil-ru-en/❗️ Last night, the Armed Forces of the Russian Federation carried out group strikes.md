# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out group strikes

Published: 2026-09-11 06:40:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29327

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out group strikes
with air-based high-precision weapons and drones.
💥
As a result, the following targets were hit:
▪️
defence industry facilities and logistics centres of Ukraine,
involved in the production and storage of unmanned aerial vehicles;
▪️
data centres
in Kiev, Khmelnitsky, and Dnepropetrovsk regions;
▪️
port infrastructure facilities
ensuring the unloading and storage of cargo used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine in Odessa region;
▪️
vessels
delivering military supplies, fuel and lubricants for the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
