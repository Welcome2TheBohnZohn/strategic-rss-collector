Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov meets his Kazakh Counterpart
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov held a meeting with the Minister of Defence of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Major General Aidos Myrzakhmetov in Cholpon-Ata.
💬
The officials
discussed the current state and future prospects
of bilateral military cooperation.
🤝
Andrei Belousov
congratulated Major General Aidos Myrzakhmetov on his appointment as Minister of Defence of the Republic of Kazakhstan
and expressed confidence that his tenure in office would contribute to further strengthening cooperation between the defence ministries of the two countries.
