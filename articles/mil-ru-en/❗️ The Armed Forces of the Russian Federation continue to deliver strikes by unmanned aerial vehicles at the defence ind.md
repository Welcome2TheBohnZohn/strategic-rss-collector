# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to deliver strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles at the defence industry enterprises and logistics centres, as well as ships

Published: 2026-09-25 15:12:30

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29615

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to deliver strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles at the defence industry enterprises and logistics centres, as well as ships
operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets were hit:
▫️
in Kiev region – the logistics centre
(HAVI Logistics Ukraine) in Trebukhov, the territory of which was used by units of the Ministry of Defence and other security structures of Ukraine to store dual-use goods;
▫️
in Kiev – DataGroup data centre,
which was responsible for processing and transmitting intelligence data in the interests of the AFU;
▫️
in Odessa region – a dry cargo ship
that delivered military cargo to the port of Chernomorsk.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
