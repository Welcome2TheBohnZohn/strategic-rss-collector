# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes against power, fuel and transport infrastructure facilities, as well as ships

Published: 2026-11-28 07:01:44

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30823

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
delivered UAV strikes against power, fuel and transport infrastructure facilities, as well as ships
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
Zarechye 110 kV electrical substation
in Belaya Tserkov (Kiev region), which supports the operation of Ukraine's defence industry enterprises;
▫️
a fuel tank
in Kiliya (Odessa region), which stored fuel for the AFU;
▫️
four railway locomotives in Sinelnikovo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Korosten (Zhitomir region), and in Lyubotin (Kharkov region), used for transporting armaments and military hardware;
▫️
in the Black Sea waters south of Odessa –
a dry cargo ship,
delivering military-purpose cargo to Ukraine.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
