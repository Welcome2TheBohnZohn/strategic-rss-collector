Published: 2026-10-11 17:45:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29977

❗️
Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres, port infrastructure facilities in Ukraine, and vessels
involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike, a logistics centre in Ternovka of Nikolayev region was hit. It stored military and dual-use cargo intended to support the AFU.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship delivering military cargo to the port of Chernomorsk was hit
while underway at sea.

Russian Defence Ministry
