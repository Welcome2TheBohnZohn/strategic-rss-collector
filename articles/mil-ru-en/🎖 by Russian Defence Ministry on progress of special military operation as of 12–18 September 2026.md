🚩
The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence,
liberated Rozovka in Zaporozhye region.
💥
Russian troops inflicted damage
on the formations of one mechanised brigade, three air assault brigades, three assault brigades, three assault regiments, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade.
▪️
The AFU losses in the Vostok Group's area of responsibility amounted to more than 1,955 servicemen, five armoured fighting vehicles, 65 motor vehicles, and four artillery guns.
🚩
The Dnepr Group's units improved the tactical situation and
liberated Novoandreyevka in Zaporozhye region.
💥
They inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
Over 280 servicemen, an armoured fighting vehicle, 129 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and 11 electronic warfare and counter-fire radars have been neutralised during the week.
▫️
From 12 to 18 September, air defence systems shot down
44 guided aerial bombs, eight U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, two Neptune long-range guided missiles, and 5,706 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
