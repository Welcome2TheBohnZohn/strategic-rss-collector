↗️
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Sergeyevka, Varvarovka, Bokovoye, Krivorozhye, Yakovlevka, Belozerskoye, and Kamenka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 345 troops, nine armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Vostok Group's units advanced into the depth of the enemy's defence
. Manpower and hardware of an air assault brigade, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two marine brigades have been hit close to Chernenkovo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Kirovo, Zheltaya Krucha, Vasilevskoye, and Novorozovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 215 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and four motor vehicles have been neutralised.
↗️
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Formations of a mechanised brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a territorial defence brigade were hit close to Orekhov and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 25 troops, 11 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on energy facilities, transport infrastructure employed by the AFU, ammunition depots, production workshops and storage areas of unmanned ground vehicles and unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 145 areas.
