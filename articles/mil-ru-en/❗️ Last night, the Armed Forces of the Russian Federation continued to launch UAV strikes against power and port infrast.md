# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch UAV strikes against power and port infrastructure facilities, as well as vessels

Published: 2026-11-19 06:40:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30675

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to launch UAV strikes against power and port infrastructure facilities, as well as vessels
used to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike,
in Odessa region, the following targets were neutralised:
▫️
the PS-110 Karolino power substation in the settlement of Karolino-Bugaz and a PS-110 in the settlement of Belgorod-Dnestrovskiy.
These power substations distribute electricity to ensure the operation of port infrastructure facilities and logistics centres in Odessa region;
▫️
infrastructure facilities
intended to store the AFU's POL materials
in the port of Chernomorsk
;
▫️
a dry cargo ship
at sea delivering dual-use cargo to the port of Odessa.
🔥
In addition,
the PS-330 Sumy Severnaya power substation was hit in the city of Sumy.
It was used to distribute electricity to the Ukrainian defence industry enterprises and logistics centres in Sumy region.
