Preparation is over, we are ready for active phase
💬
Defence Minister Andrei Belousov reported to the Supreme Commander-in-Chief on the progress of the final stage of the Centre 2026.
Comrade Supreme Commander-in-Chief,
In accordance with the Training Plan of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, the strategic command and post exercise Centre 2026 has been under way since 28 September.
The exercise involves troops of the Central Military Region, formations and military units of the Aerospace Forces, the Airborne Troops, formations and military units of central subordination, as well as forces of the Caspian Sea Flotilla.
In addition, representatives of almost 60 states and the forces and assets of the CSTO collective security system are taking part in the exercise. Seven countries have dispatched their military contingents, the defence ministry of the Congo is represented by an operational group, and 51 states are represented by observers. The total number of troops at the Chebarkulskiy training ground stands at around six thousand servicemen; around 700 pieces of hardware, more than 600 UAVs of various modifications and upwards of 60 robotic systems are being used.
The training tasks are being carried out within the framework of an operation by a grouping of troops on the Central Asian strategic direction. The troops and forces have completed joint preparation and are ready for the active phase of the exercise.
