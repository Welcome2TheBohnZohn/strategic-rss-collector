# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes against transport infrastructure facilities and ships used in the interests of the AFU.

Published: 2026-11-23 15:42:31

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30751

❗️
Today,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes
against transport infrastructure facilities and ships used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
near Merefa (Kharkov region) –
a railway locomotive,
used for transporting armaments and military hardware;
▫️
in the Black Sea waters east of Odessa –
a dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo to support the AFU.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
