↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued advancing into the depth of enemy defence.
Russian troops hit manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, three air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Omelnik, Vasinovka, Nikolskoye, Samoylovka (Zaporozhye region), Pravda, Ivanovka and Gavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 340 troops and four motor vehicles.
💥
The Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Preobrazhenka, Yulyevka, and Orekhov (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 Ukrainian troops, 16 motor vehicles, a 122-mm D-30 howitzer, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Russian Groups of Forces have inflicted damage on
the preparation and launch site of the Flamingo long-range cruise missile, the fuel and power infrastructure of Ukraine, the ammunition production workshop, logistics centres used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, assembly and storage areas of UAVs and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 143 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
14 guided aerial bombs, five HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 804 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
