# ❗️ Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, First Deputy Defence Minister, General of the Army Valery Gerasimov

Published: 2026-08-27 08:00:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29064

❗️
Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, First Deputy Defence Minister, General of the Army Valery Gerasimov
inspected the course of combat missions carried out by formations and units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces.
💬
In the framework of the inspection, at the command post of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, General of the Army Valery Gerasimov
was briefed by the Group's Commander, Colonel General Sergei Medvedev,
on the current situation in the area of responsibility
as well as by commanders of formations
on the results of combat missions.
✅
The Chief of General Staff summing up results
noted the success of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces
in liberating the Donetsk People's Republic and
set tasks for further action.
▫️
Moreover, General of the Army Valery Gerasimov
presented state awards to the most distinguished servicemen
and thanked them for their courage and heroism during combat operations.
