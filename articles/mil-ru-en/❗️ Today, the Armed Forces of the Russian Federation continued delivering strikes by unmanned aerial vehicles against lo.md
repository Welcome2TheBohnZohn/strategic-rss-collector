Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres, energy infrastructure facilities, as well as ships involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
As a result of the strike, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
a logistics centre
in Kharkov, where the storage of military and dual-use cargo intended to support the AFU has been organised;
▫️
the PS-330 Lutsk-Severnaya electrical substation
in Lutsk and
a PS-330 electrical substation
in Kovel (Volyn region), which support the operation of defence industry enterprises producing UAVs and their components, as well as logistics centres used for storing military equipment.
💥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering cargo intended to support the AFU to the port of Chernomorsk was hit at sea.
