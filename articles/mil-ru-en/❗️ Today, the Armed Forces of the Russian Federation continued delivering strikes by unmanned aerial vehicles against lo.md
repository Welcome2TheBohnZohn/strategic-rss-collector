# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres, port infrastructure facilities of Ukraine a

Published: 2026-10-12 14:58:00

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30001

❗️
Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres, port infrastructure facilities of Ukraine and ships
involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
📍
As a result of the strike, the following targets
were hit in Nikolayev region:
▫️
in Ternovka, the Novya Pochta logistics centre, used for storing dual-use cargo intended to support the AFU;
▫️
at sea,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military-purpose cargo to the port of Nikolayev.
💥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military and dual-use cargo to the port of Odessa was hit at sea.

Russian Defence Ministry
