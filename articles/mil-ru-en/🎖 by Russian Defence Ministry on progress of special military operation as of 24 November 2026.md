The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Barvinovka (Zaporozhye region).
🔥
They inflicted fire damage on
manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, one assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU near Kolomiytsy, Gavrilovka, Zelenaya Dolina (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novotroitskoye, Zheltaya Krucha, Veseloye, and Schastlivoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU have lost more than 205 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, and three motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian elements delivered strikes at formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Kirovo and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 25 troops, 12 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station have been neutralised.

power and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, military airfields, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed forces and foreign mercenaries in 143 areas.
