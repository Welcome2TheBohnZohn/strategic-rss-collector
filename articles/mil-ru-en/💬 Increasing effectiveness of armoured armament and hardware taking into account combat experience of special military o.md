# 💬 Increasing effectiveness of armoured armament and hardware taking into account combat experience of special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-26 12:02:13

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29634

Increasing
effectiveness of armoured armament and hardware taking into account combat experience of special military operation.
Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation General of the Army Aleksandr Sanchik held a technical conference on improving the design of armoured weapons and motor vehicles as well as testing new systems of active protection against enemy UAVs.
💬
Aleksandr Sanchik noted that the feedback from the troops, organised by manufacturers
, makes it possible to quickly solve issues of modernisation of supplied to units weapons and equipment.
❗️
At the same time, repair battalions
are creating their own effective innovations
. The best of such inventions should be produced at factories.
In practical part of the conferences, were presented
👇
▫️
samples of military motor vehicles, which are employed on the front line in the most effective way,
▫️
defensive aids systems against UAVs of the enemy on different chassis.
➡️
General of the Army Aleksandr Sanchik was informed
that after testing at the training ground, anti-drone systems would be sent to the special military operation zone.
