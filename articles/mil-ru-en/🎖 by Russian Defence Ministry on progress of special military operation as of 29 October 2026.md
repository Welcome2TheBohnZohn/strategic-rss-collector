📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades near Mezhevaya (Dnepropetrovsk region), Stepanovka, Belozerskoye, Shilovka, Andreyevka, Roganskoye, Starovarvarovka, Veselaya Gora, and Novopavlovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU formations have lost more than 340 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, one assault brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Gavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Sergeyevka, Barvinovka, Vasilevskoye, Tersyanka, and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 175 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, seven motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade have been hit close to Orekhov, Yurkovka, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 15 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and two motor vehicles have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, UAV launch sites, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 139 areas.

1,425 fixed-wing UAVs.
