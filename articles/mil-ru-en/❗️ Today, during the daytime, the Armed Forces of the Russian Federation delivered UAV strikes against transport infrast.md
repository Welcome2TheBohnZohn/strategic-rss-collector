# ❗️ Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes against transport infrastructure facilities and vessels

Published: 2026-11-27 16:45:27

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30811

❗️
Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
delivered UAV strikes against transport infrastructure facilities and vessels
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes,
three railway locomotives
used to deliver military cargo have been hit close to Pershotravensk, Kamenskoye, and Morozovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo to support the AFU has been hit at sea south-west of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
