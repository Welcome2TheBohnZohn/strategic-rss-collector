Russian Defence Minister arrives on working visit to Republic of Kazakhstan
📍
Astana
The Ministry of Defence of the Republic of Kazakhstan held
a solemn ceremony of the official meeting of the Russian military delegation
with the participation of the guard of honour company.
The head of the Russian Defence Ministry laid wreaths at the monument of the Defenders of the Fatherland
and paid tribute to the heroes with a minute of silence.
