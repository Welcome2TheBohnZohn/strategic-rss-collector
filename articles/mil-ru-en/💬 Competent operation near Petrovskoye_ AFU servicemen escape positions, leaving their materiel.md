Competent operation near Petrovskoye: AFU servicemen escape positions, leaving their materiel
💬
Deputy platoon commander with the call sign ‘Osetiya’
gave details of the liberation of
Petrovskoye
(the DPR).
‘It was a competent operation planned by the command. We delivered FAB and UAV strikes, then we just entered and mopped up the settlement. It was small, so there were no troubles to get everyone out of there,' noted the serviceman.
▫️
‘Osetiya’ demonstrated a trophy NATO weapon and equipment that was left at the enemy's positions
‘We looted NATO weapons already damaged by fragments. All stuff was left. As always, the enemy rode off into the sunset’.
📍
The liberation of Petrovskoye allowed the Group's units
to establish control over the highway between Dobropolye and Kramatorsk.
It cut off the AFU's supply routes, deprived them of safe routes to withdraw, and created conditions for a further offensive in Dobropolye direction.
