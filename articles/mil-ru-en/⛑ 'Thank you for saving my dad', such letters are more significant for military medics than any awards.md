# ⛑ 'Thank you for saving my dad', such letters are more significant for military medics than any awards.

Published: 2026-11-24 09:27:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30759

'Thank you for saving my dad', such letters are more significant for military medics than any awards.
⛑
Doctors of the forward medical group of the 74th Separate Guards Motorised Rifle Brigade (Tsentr Group of Forces)
save the servicemen's lives in Dnepropetrovsk direction.
In a forward hospital, the wounded servicemen are given first aid, operated and escorted during evacuation to the rear area.
🫶
They are treated not only with medicines, but also with people's warmth.
‘We don't care whether a serviceman has minor or moderate injury, I send every doctor with him to talk to him, to cheer him up. This factor really supports the servicemen,’ says Mamadi Gadzhiyev, the medical company commander.
💌
The most valuable reward for medics are children's letters of gratitude for saving their fathers and random meetings with their patients already in peaceful life, on vacation.
Thank you for your hard work, Guardian Angels!
