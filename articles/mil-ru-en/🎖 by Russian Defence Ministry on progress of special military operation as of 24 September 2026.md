The Vostok Group's units continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated the settlement of Svetlaya Dolina (Zaporozhye region).

Russian troops inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, two air assault brigades, and two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine close to Pokrovskoye, Yablonovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vladimirovka, Sofiyevka, and Tarasovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 240 troops, five motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.

The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Orekhov and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 45 troops, 16 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.

have inflicted losses on ammunition and fuel depots, energy facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, long- and medium-range unmanned aerial vehicles and their components as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 140 areas.

244 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
