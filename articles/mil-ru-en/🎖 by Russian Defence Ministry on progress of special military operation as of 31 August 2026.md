↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued advancing into the depth of enemy defence.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware, two assault brigades, two assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade close to Barvinovka, Listovka, Obshcheye, Rozovka, Samoylovka, and Nikolskoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 325 troops, one Bogdana howitzer, and 11 motor vehicles.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of the AFU mechanised brigades and a territorial defence brigade near Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 16 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on the fuel and power infrastructure of Ukraine, transport infrastructure, logistics centres used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, assembly and storage sites of unmanned aerial vehicles, ammunition depots, fuel and military equipment, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, three HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 766 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
