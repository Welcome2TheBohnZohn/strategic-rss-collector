# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-25 10:47:00

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29607

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
From 19 to 25 September, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two mass strikes and seven group strikes
, as a result of which fire damage was inflicted on military plants, metallurgical industry enterprises, along with fuel and power facilities of Ukraine that kept them running, logistics and telecommunication centres,️ transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU,️ military airfields,️ UAV production and storage areas,️ ammunition, fuel, and military hardware depots,️ temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition, the strikes disabled a number of facilities of the infrastructure of the Ukrainian ports
, which provided for the reception, storage and distribution of military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants used in the interests of the AFU.
▪️
During the week, Russian troops hit 18 ships, including 15 dry cargo ships and three tankers involved in supplying Western weapons, military hardware, and fuel to the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement of the enemy's formations
in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
🚩
Last week, as a result of active and resolute actions, the settlements of Buzovo, Komissarovo, Krasny Yar, Loshakovo, Novoaleksandrovka, Polnaya, Potikhonovo, and Khiznyakovo (Kharkov region) were taken under Russian control. All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
▪️
Over the past week, the enemy has lost over 250 militants, seven armoured fighting vehicles, three motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and five UGVs.
▪️
A total of 212 square kilometres of territory and 12 settlements have been liberated
since the beginning of the AFU group's encirclement in the north-east of Kharkov region.
More than 960 troops, 23 armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, 13 ATVs, and 28 UGVs were neutralised.
💥
In addition,
seven mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, one airmobile brigade, one artillery brigade, two assault regiments, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, six territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade were hit in Sumy and Kharkov regions.
▪️
Over the past week, in the Sever Group's area of responsibility, the enemy has lost more than 1,490 troops, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, 107 motor vehicles, four field artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
During the week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces have taken more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades were hit.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,510 troops, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, including five U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carriers, 92 motor vehicles, 13 field artillery guns, and five electronic warfare stations and counter-fire radars.
📍
Last week, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault brigade, one mountain assault brigade, two airmobile brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, one marine brigade, two territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade were hit.
▪️
In total, in the Yuzhnaya Group's area of responsibility, the enemy has lost more than 1,340 troops, three tanks, including one German-made Leopard tank, 10 armoured fighting vehicles, 148 motor vehicles, and 20 field artillery guns.
