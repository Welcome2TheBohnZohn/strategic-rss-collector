# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-15 08:49:40

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29419

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten the encirclement of the enemy's formations
and expand the external control zone.
🚩
Control over Olkhovatka in Kharkov region has been established in the encirclement,
and AFU formations blocked east of the settlement are being neutralised.
💥
Combat operations to liberate Malaya Volchya in Kharkov region are carried out.
The coordinated fire damage was inflicted on manpower close to Khripuny and Buzovo of Kharkov region.
▪️
Within the past 24 hours, the enemy lost up to 35 troops in the encirclement. All attempts of the militants to break through the encirclement have been thwarted.
📊
Since the beginning of the month, up to 490 militants, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, eight pickup trucks, 13 ATVs, and 21 ground robotic systems have been neutralised.
💥
As a result of resolute actions to expand the external control zone, the enemy was hit
close to Prikolotnoye, Novoaleksandrovka, and Petropavlovka of Kharkov region.
▪️
In Kharkov region, two assault groups of the AFU attempting to counter-attack near Kazachya Lopan (Kharkov region) were neutralised.
🔥
In addition, Russian troops inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of a tank brigade, a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades close to Zolochev, Martynovka, Chernoglazovka, Metallovka, and Revoliutsionnoye of Kharkov region.
💥
In Sumy region,
units of a tank brigade, a mechanised brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades were hit near Boyaro-Lezhachi, Gnilitsa, and Khoten (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, up to 195 troops, three tanks, 11 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations of the enemy have been neutralised in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted losses on formations of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the AFU, two marine brigades, and a territorial defence brigade close to Stetskovka, Chervony Oskol, Putnikovo, Petrovka, Studenok (Kharkov region), Bogorodichnoye, Prishib, and Sidorovo (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 200 troops, 14 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
