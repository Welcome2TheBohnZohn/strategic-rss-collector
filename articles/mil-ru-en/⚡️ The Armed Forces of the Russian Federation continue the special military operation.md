# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-26 09:49:48

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29631

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces
continue to tighten the encirclement of the AFU formations
in the north-east of Kharkov region and
expand the external control zone
.
🔥
Combat operations to liberate
Khripuny, Grachovka, and Rublenoye in Kharkov region are carried out. The enemy units are also being neutralised near Nesternoye, Shevchenkovo, and Varvarovka (Kharkov region).
📍
Russian troops
thwarted two attempts to escape from the encirclement
by
a group of up to 10 militants close to Nefedovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
More than 50 servicemen, one armoured fighting vehicle, two motor vehicles, one ATV, and one MLRS launcher have been neutralised in the encirclement.
🚩
When expanding the external control zone,
Russian troops liberated Petropavlovka and Lozovaya in Kharkov region.
💥
In addition, in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade near Svetlichnoye, Kovali, Morozova Dolina, Shapovalovka, Zhukov Yar, and Velikiy Burluk (Kharkov region).
🚩
In Sumy region, Russian units took Mariyno under control during active offensive operations
.
💥
Manpower and materiel
of an airomobile brigade, a mechanised brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades have been hit near Esman, Kiyanitsa, Vakalovshchina, and Boyaro-Lezhachi (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 245 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one MLRS launcher in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade near Glushchenkovo, Andreyevka, Lesnaya Stenka, Chervony Oskol, Grushevka, Yaremovka (Kharkov region), and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, one tank, one U.S.-made MaxxPro armoured vehicle, 14 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Druzhkovka, Slavyansk, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Nikolayevka, Kramatorsk, Belokuzhminovka, and Chervonozaryanoy (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 160 troops, 26 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
