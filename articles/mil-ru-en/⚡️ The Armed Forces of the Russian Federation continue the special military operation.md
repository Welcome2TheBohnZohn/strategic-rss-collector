# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-12 09:46:16

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29352

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten the enemy's encirclement
and expand the external control zone.
💥
Over the past 24 hours,
hostile manpower and hardware have been hit close to Novoaleksandrovka, Prikolotnoye, Malaya Volchya, and Buzovo, as well as blocked the enemy's formations near Olkhovatka (Kharkov region).
➡️
Russian troops thwarted two attempts by assault groups of the AFU to escape from the ring of encirclement
close to Sliznevo and Gogino in Kharkov region.
▪️
Up to 125 militants, four armoured fighting vehicles, three ATVs, and 12 UGVs were neutralised. Two Ukrainian servicemen surrendered.
🔥
In addition, strikes were delivered
at formations of one mechanised brigade, one motorised infantry brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Shevchenkovo, Goptovka, and Lozovaya (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades close to Ulanovo, Velikaya Rybitsa, Khoten, Esman and Kiyanitsa.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 215 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, and 17 motor vehicles in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units hit formations of three mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Chervony Oskol, Grushevka, Senkovo (Kharkov region), Raygorodok, Krymki, and Prishib (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 220 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 20 motor vehicles, and three field artillery guns, including one UK-made Braveheart self-propelled artillery system and one U.S.-made M114 howitzer.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the AFU, and one marine brigade near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, Belenkoye, Nikolayevka, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, and Orekhovatka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 180 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and 25 motor vehicles. Three field artillery guns, including one 155-mm Polish-made Krab self-propelled artillery system and one U.S.-made M777 howitzer, were neutralised.
