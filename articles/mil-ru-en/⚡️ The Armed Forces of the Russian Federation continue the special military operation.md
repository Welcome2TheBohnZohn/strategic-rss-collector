# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-27 09:39:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29650

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces
continue to tighten the encirclements of the AFU formations
in the north-east of Kharkov region
and expand the external control zone.
🚩
As a result of active offensive actions,
control has been established over the settlement of Khripuny
(Kharkov region).
📍
Combat operations are conducted to liberate
Nesternoye, Lukashovo, and Grachovka. Formations of the enemy are being destroyed close to the settlements of Varvarovka and Rublenoye (Kharkov region).
🔥
Russian troops
thwarted two enemy's attempts to escape from the encirclement
near Nefedovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
During the day, the enemy lost up to 60 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, one motor vehicle, one ATV, and two ground robotic systems.
📍
While expanding the external control zone,
the combat actions are underway for the liberation of Prikolotnoye
(Kharkov region).
💥
In addition,
in Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one unmanned systems brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade near Kupino, Fedorovka, Zhovtnevoye, and Maly Burluk (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Sumy region,
strikes were delivered at
formations of four mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two territorial defence brigades, and one border detachment of the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine near Sukhodol, Sumy, Khoten, Kiyanitsa, and Velikaya Rybitsa (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, more than 300 troops, one T-72 tank, one U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carrier, one Tisa armoured fighting vehicle, 15 motor vehicles, and four electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one territorial defence brigade, and one national guard brigade close to Shevchenkovo, Pervomayskoye, Maleyevka (Kharkov region), Donetskoye, Lozovoye, Rubtsy, Karpovka, and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 230 troops, 12 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns, including one U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of five mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade have been hit near Druzhkovka, Slavyansk, Nikolayevka, Kramatorsk, Klinovoye, Krasny Molochar, and Nikonorovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 230 troops, one Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, 28 motor vehicles, and three artillery guns.
