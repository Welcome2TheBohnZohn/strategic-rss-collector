# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-15 11:03:03

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30061

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region are tightening the encirclement and expanding the outer control zone.
🚩
Within the encirclement, as a result of the active actions of our units,
control has been established over the settlements of Nefyodovka and Chernoye in Kharkov region.
💥
Fighting continues for the liberation of Nikolayevka and Varvarovka in Kharkov region.
👊
As part of the expanding of the outer control zone,
fighting continues to establish control over the settlements of Prikolotnoye, Kotovka and Gogino in Kharkov region.
🔥
Fire strikes were delivered against strongholds of a heavy mechanised brigade and an assault regiment of the Ukrainian Armed Forces close to Fedorovka and Maly Burluk in Kharkov region.
💥
Also in Kharkov region,
personnel and equipment of three mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades and two assault regiments of the Ukrainian Armed Forces were hit close to Liptsy, Velikiye Prokhody, Zamulovka, Shirokoye, Zarechnoye, Pershotravnevoye, Kurgannoye, Veliky Burluk, Vysoky and Gontarovka.
🔥
In Sumy region,
formations of a mechanised brigade of the Ukrainian Armed Forces and two territorial defence brigades were hit in the areas of the settlements of Leninskoye, Barilovka and Pervomayskoye in Sumy region.
▪️
In total, in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group the enemy lost over 175 servicemen, 16 motor vehicles and two electronic warfare stations.
📍
Units of the Zapad Group improved their position along the forward edge.
They inflicted damage on personnel and equipment of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Ukrainian Armed Forces and a territorial defence brigade in the areas of the settlements of Mayaki, Karpovka, and Donetskoye in the Donetsk People's Republic, and Chervony Oskol and Maliyevka in Kharkov region.
▪️
Ukrainian Armed Forces losses amounted to up to 220 servicemen, three armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, three artillery pieces and a Grad MLRS combat vehicle.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted damage on formations of a mechanised brigade, a mountain assault brigade and an airmobile brigade of the Ukrainian Armed Forces in the areas of the settlements of Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Semenovka, Vysokoivanovka, and Krasny Molochar in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 145 servicemen, 33 motor vehicles and two field artillery pieces.
