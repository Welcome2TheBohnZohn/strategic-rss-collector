# ⚡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-19 10:25:27

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29503

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
⚡️
The Sever Group's units in the north-eastern part of Kharkov region
tighten the encirclement ring and expand the external control area.
💥
The neutralisation of the encircled enemy units continues
close to Grachovka, Krasny Yar, and Khripuny (Kharkov region).
🚩
In the course of decisive actions, the settlements of Loshakovo and Komissarovo (Kharkov region) have been brought under control, and combat actions to liberate Buzovo (Kharkov region) are carried out.
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, up to 30 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and two motor vehicles have been neutralised.
↗️
During the external control area expansion,
the AFU's formations
are being neutralised
near Petropavlovka, Novoaleksandrovka, and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
🔥
In addition, in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade close to Korbiny Ivany, Chernoglazovka, Bratenitsa, Podsredneye, and Velikiy Burluk.
💥
In Sumy region,
clusters of manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine
have been hit
close to Khoten, Rudnevka, and Pushkarevka (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, over the past 24 hours, the enemy lost up to 245 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and 12 motor vehicles in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Bazaliyevka, Berezovka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), and Rubtsy (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 210 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, including two U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carriers, 12 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade, and one mountain assault brigade near Vysokoivanovka, Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, and Shabelkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 145 troops, 19 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
