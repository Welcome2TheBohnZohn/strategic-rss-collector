# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched UAV strikes against defence industry enterprises and ports of Ukraine, as well as vessels

Published: 2026-09-16 06:35:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29432

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
launched UAV strikes against defence industry enterprises and ports of Ukraine, as well as vessels
used in the interests of the AFU.
The following targets were engaged:
▪️
in Kiev,
an industrial enterprise
(JSC Kiev motor transport enterprise) which produces specialised motor vehicles supplied to the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▪️
in Odessa region, in the port of Chernomorsk,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo.

Russian Defence Ministry
