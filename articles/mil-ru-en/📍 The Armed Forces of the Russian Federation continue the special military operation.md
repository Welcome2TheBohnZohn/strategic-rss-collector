# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-18 10:47:27

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30663

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Golubovka, Lebedevka, Muravskoye, Ploskoye, Podsredneye and Chuguyev (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
the Group's units inflicted losses on formations of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and three territorial defence brigades near Bititsa, Konotop, Korenek, Putivl, Tokari, Ulyanovka, and Yastrebinoye of Sumy region.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 225 troops, one self-propelled artillery system, 11 motor vehicles, and six counter-battery and electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the AFU, one territorial defence brigade near Monachinovka, Peski-Radkovskiye, Proletarskoye (Kharkov region), and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, four field artillery guns, including two self-propelled artillery systems, and 19 motor vehicles.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian troops hit three mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one national guard brigade have been hit near Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Toretskoye, and Cherevkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 155 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, three artillery guns, and 26 motor vehicles.
↗️
Units of the Tsentr Group of Forces continue to carry out intensive offensive operations.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised assault brigades, one assault regiment, one assault regiment of the AFU, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Zavido-Borzenka, Novoviktorovka, Novoiverskoye, Ocheretino (Donetsk People's Republic), Zoryanoye, and Fedorovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 345 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, ten motor vehicles, six electronic warfare and electronic reconnaissance stations.
