# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-07 10:30:38

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29896

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten encirclement and expand the external control zone. Battles are underway in the encirclement for the liberation of Nikolayevka, Varvarovka, Reznikovo, Nefedovka, and Lobanovka (Kharkov region).
💥
Strikes were delivered at the enemy's formations near Kupino and Chernoye (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 30 troops and an unmanned ground vehicle in the encirclement. Two command posts of the enemy's unmanned aerial vehicles were neutralised.
🔥
While extending the external control zone,
there is active fighting for the settlements of Annopolye, Prikolotnoye, and Kotovka (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition, Russian troops hit manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade near Chernoglazovka, Dementiyevka, Velikiy Burluk, Novoselovka, Belashi, Cherkasskaya Lozovaya, and Goryanoye in Kharkov region.
💥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a territorial defence brigade near Grunovka, Kiyanitsa, Boyaro-Lezhachi (Sumy region) and the city of Sumy.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost up to 260 troops, one Tisa armoured fighting vehicle, 19 motor vehicles, a 155-mm Bogdana self-propelled artillery system, and an electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line
and inflicted losses on the manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a territorial defence brigade, and a border detachment of the Ukrainian border service near Chervony Oskol, Maliyevka (Kharkov region), Volchiy Yar, Raygorodok, and Karpovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 220 troops, a U.S.-made HMMWV armoured fighting vehicle, a 155-mm Bogdana self-propelled artillery system, 15 motor vehicles, and an electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group's elements took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on three mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade near Belenkoye, Semenovka, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Slavyansk, Nikolayevka, Druzhkovka, and Kramatorsk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 155 troops, a Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, 23 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and an electronic warfare station.
