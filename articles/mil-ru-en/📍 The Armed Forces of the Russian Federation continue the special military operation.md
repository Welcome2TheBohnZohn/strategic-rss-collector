# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-03 10:13:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30415

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The Sever Group of Forces improved its tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, Russian troops inflicted losses on formations of three mechanised brigades, one unmanned systems brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Aleksandrovka, Tomakhovka, Fedorovka, Bugayevka, Rubezhnoye, Sonino, and Primorskoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition, in Sumy region,
strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades near Korenek, Gorki, Spasskoye, Putivl, Markovka, and Nikolskoye (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 140 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 16 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
⏩
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Izyum, Chervony Oskol, Ivanovka, Studenok, Monachinovka, Smorodkovka (Kharkov region), and Rubtsy (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 210 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian troops inflicted fire damage on formations of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault brigade, one airmobile brigade, and two assault regiments of the AFU close to Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Nikolayevka, Druzhkovka, Vysokoivanovka, Krasnotorka, Starodubovka, and Kondratovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 175 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 36 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, two air assault brigades, one assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades have been hit near Krivorozhye, Golubovka, Yakovlevka, Andreyevka, Sergeyevka, Belozerskoye, Varvarovka, Shavrovo (Donetsk People's Republic), and Ukrainka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
Up to 340 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, five motor vehicles, one Grad MLRS and one electronic warfare station have been neutralised.
