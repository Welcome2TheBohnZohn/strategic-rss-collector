# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-29 11:40:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30342

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces
took more advantageous lines and positions.
💥
In Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, three motorised infantry brigades, and two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Zolochev, Golubovka, Nikolskoye, Izbitskoye, Lobanovka, and Dergachi.
🔥
Besides, in Sumy region,
formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two territorial defence brigades
have been hit
close to Yastrebinoye, Vakalovshchina, Putivl, and Radkovka.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 260 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces and one territorial defence brigade near Mayaki, Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic), Podvysokoye, Glushchenkovo, Studenok, and Monachinovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, 13 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, one air assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one marine brigade close to Druzhkovka, Nikolayevka, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Semenovka, Slavyansk, and Kramatorsk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 145 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 30 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns.
