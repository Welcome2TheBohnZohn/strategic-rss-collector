# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-22 10:09:22

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30213

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit near Revolyutsionnoye, Novouzhvinovka, Kolodeznoye, Bugayevka, Kotovka, and Izbitskoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of one AFU airmobile brigade and two territorial defence brigades have been hit near Krovnoye, Khoten, Barilovka, and Luzhki (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 230 troops, 21 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Pleskachovka, Osinovo, Tamarganovka (Kharkov region), Raygorodok, and Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 200 troops, 16 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at formations of five mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one air assault brigade of the AFU, two marine brigades, and one territorial defence brigade close to Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Gavrilovka, Kondratovka, and Vasilyevskaya Pustosh (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 185 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 32 motor vehicles, four Western-made field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements hit five mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one assault brigade, one air assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, three marine brigades, five national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade close to Krinitsa, Roganskoye, Novodonetskoye, Shilovka, Kamenka, Starovarvarovka, Belozerskoye, Novoyavlenka (Donetsk People's Republic) and Leninskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 345 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, four motor vehicles, five field artillery guns, and six electronic and counter-fire radars.
