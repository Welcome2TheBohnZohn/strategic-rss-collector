# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-21 10:31:07

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30714

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, strikes have been delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine close to Muravskoye, Ploskoye, Kolodeznoye, and Lebedevka.
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of three mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade
have been hit
near Knyazhichi, Yastrebinoye, Krovnoye and Sumy.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 260 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, one electronic warfare station, one counter-fire radar station, and one electronic reconnaissance station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, one territorial defence brigade, and one border detachment of State Border Guard Service of Ukraine have been hit near Mayaki, Lozovoye, Starodubovka, and Karpovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 200 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 13 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near Barvenkovo (Kharkov region), Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 165 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 32 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
👊
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out active offensive operations.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one airborne brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, one marine brigade, and four national guard brigades have been hit near Kuritsyno, Verovka, Novokrivorozhye, Andreyevka, Sergeyevka, Novofedorovka, Mikhaylovka, Novopavlovka, and Belozerskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 335 troops, nine armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
