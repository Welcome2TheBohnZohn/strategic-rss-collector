# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-26 10:00:13

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30286

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade close to Podsredneye, Prikolotnoye, Doroshenkovo, and Fedorovka (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Khoten, Barilovka, and Nikolskoye.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 300 troops, 19 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades have been hit close to Lesnaya Stenka, Donetskoye (Kharkov region), Raygorodok, Cherkasskoye, and Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, 20 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
They launched strikes against formations of five mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, and Kondratovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 185 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 33 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and four electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades near Stepanovka, Krinitsy, Belozerskoye, Shilovka, Starovarvarovka, and Malotaranovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 340 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, one U.S.-made Paladin self-propelled artillery system, and two electronic warfare stations.
