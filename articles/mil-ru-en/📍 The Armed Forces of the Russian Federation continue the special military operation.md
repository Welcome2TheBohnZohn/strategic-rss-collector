# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-06 10:25:20

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29858

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone in Kharkov region.
Fights are underway in the encirclement for the liberation of settlements in Varvarovka, Lobanovka, Nikolayevka, and Reznikovo (Kharkov region). Moreover, enemy formations are neutralised close to Kupino and Chernoye in Kharkov region.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 35 troops during the day in the encirclement ring.
📍
While expanding the external control zone, Russian troops
continue active fighting in order to establish control over
Annopolye, Kotovka, and Prikolotnoye in Kharkov region
🔥
The enemy's attempts to deblock surrounded units
north of Kurgannoye (Kharkov region) were thwarted.
💥
In addition, in Kharkov region,
manpower and hardware
of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU were hit close to Belashi, Slatino, Tsirkuny, Cherkasskaya Lozovaya, and Chuguyev (Kharkov region).
📍
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered
at formations of two mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Gnilitsa, Gruzskoye, Krolevets, Maryino in Sumy region, and Sumy.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost over 250 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and 17 motor vehicles in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line and inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Blagodatovka, Izyum, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region) and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 235 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, one artillery gun, and 15 motor vehicles.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units hit formations of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU have been hit near Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Vysokoivanovka, Kramatorsk, Krasny Molochar, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 195 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 27 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Russian troops inflicted damage on formations of two mechanised brigades, one air assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and two national guard brigades have been hit close to Belozerskoye, Maryevka, Roganskoye, Svyatogorovka (Donetsk People's Republic) and Novopavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
⏩
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army are advancing in the central part of the city.
Marine units are advancing in its northern part, while also continuing to destroy the enemy on the territory of the Dobropolskaya mine. Over the past 24 hours, 52 buildings have been liberated, up to 20 Ukrainian servicemen and a motor vehicle have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, more than 535 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, and an electronic warfare station have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
