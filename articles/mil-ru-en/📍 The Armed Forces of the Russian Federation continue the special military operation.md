# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-06 10:36:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29245

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The Sever Group of Forces improved its tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Yastrebinoye, Khoten, Radkovka, and Volnaya Sloboda (Sumy region).
💥
In Kharkov region
, strikes were delivered at formations of one mechanised brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, three territorial defence brigades, and national guard brigades close to Grigorovka, Tsupovka, Prikolotnoye, Novouzhvinovka, Svetlichnoye, and Borshchevaya (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 200 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, three motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near Berezovka, Staroverovka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Raygorodok, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 190 troops, one Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, 16 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line.
Formations of three mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit near Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Slavyansk, Semenovka, Klinovoye, Kondratovka, and Druzhkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 145 troops, one Novator armoured fighting vehicle, 20 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, two marine brigades, three national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade close to Novoandreyevka, Novotroitskoye, Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novovodyanoye, Verovka, Andreyevka, and Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 405 troops, two Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and one 122-mm D-30 howitzer.
