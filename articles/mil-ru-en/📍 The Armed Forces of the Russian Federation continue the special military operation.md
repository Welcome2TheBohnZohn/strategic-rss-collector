# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-29 10:10:28

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29692

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces
continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone
in Kharkov region.
🚩
As a result of active offensive actions, the settlement of Kreydyanka (Kharkov region) has been liberated.
💥
Combat operations
are conducted
inside the encirclement
to liberate Nesternoye, Lukashovo, and Grachevka, Rublenoye, and Reznikovo (Kharkov region).
A complex fire damage was inflicted on
the concentration areas of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade close to Khatneye (Kharkov region).
▪️
During the day, the enemy lost more than 60 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and two motor vehicles.
📍
While expanding the external control zone, the combat actions are underway
for the liberation of Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition,
in Kharkov region
,
strikes were delivered at
manpower concentration areas and strongholds of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one border detachment of the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine close to Ivashki and Konstantinovka.
🔥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigade near Khoten, Kiyanitsa, Velikaya Rybitsa, and Malaya Rybitsa.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 315 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 25 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU, one territorial defence brigade, and one national guard brigade near Brusovka, Mayaki, Sidorovo, Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic), Maliyevka, and Osinovo (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 230 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 15 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one marine brigade close to Belenkoye, Druzhkovka, Nikolayevka, Kramatorsk, and Semenovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 205 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 19 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
