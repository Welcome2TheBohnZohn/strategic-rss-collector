# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-08 10:01:19

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29916

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone in Kharkov region.
Battles are underway in the encirclement for the liberation of Nikolayevka, Varvarovka, Lukashovo, Reznikovo, Lobanovka, and Nefedovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 40 troops and a field artillery gun in the encirclement.
🚩
During the expansion of the external control zone, the Group's units established control over the settlement of Annopolye (Kharkov region).
In addition, Russian elements continue combat actions to establish control over the settlements of Prikolotnoye and Kotovka (Kharkov region).
💥
Also, in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of
one mechanised brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade near Chernoglazovka, Mishchenki, Dergachi, Velikiy Burluk, Artemovka, and Novy Burluk.
🔥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Kiyanitsa, Grunovka and Sumy.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost up to 280 troops, 19 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line
and inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Maliyevka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Volchiy Yar, Karpovka, and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 210 troops, a UK-made Snatch armoured fighting vehicle, a U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS launcher, a Gvozdika self-propelled artillery system, 12 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade near Krasny Molochar, Belenkoye, Kramatorsk, Gavrilovka, Nikolayevka, and Semenovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 160 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 34 motor vehicles, and a counter-fire radar.
