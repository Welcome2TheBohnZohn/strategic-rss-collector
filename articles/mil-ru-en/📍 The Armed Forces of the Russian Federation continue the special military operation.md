# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-05 09:50:26

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29228

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The Sever Group of Forces improved its tactical situation.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Ulanovo, Khoten, and Kiyanitsa of Sumy region.
💥
In Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at formations of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and three territorial defence brigades close to Khotomlya, Kotovka, Polnaya, Goptovka, and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 205 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, one anti-aircraft missile system, two electronic warfare stations, and an Israeli-made RADA RPS-42 counter-fire radar station.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group’s units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the AFU and a territorial defence brigade near Krymki, Sidorovo, Bogorodichnoye, Prishib of the Donetsk People's Republic, and Chervony Oskol of Kharkov region.
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to over than 210 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 16 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
🚩
As
a result of intensive actions, the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces liberated Kurtovka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
💥
Russian troops hit
formations of five mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, and Verolyubovka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 205 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 16 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns, including one U.S.-made 155-mm Paladin self-propelled artillery system.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Novovodyanoye, Andreyevka, Novogrishino, and Dobropolye of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 425 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and one U.S.-made M777 howitzer.
