# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-02 10:34:22

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30400

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
💥
In Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one national guard brigade near Kolodeznoye, Shipovatoye, Vodyanoye, and Martovoye.
🔥
Besides, in Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade close to Ivolzhanskoye and the city of Sumy.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 110 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, five motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one marine brigade near Studenok, Glushchenkovo, Vishnevka (Kharkov region), and Rubtsy (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 115 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
↗️
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian units hit formations of three mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one territorial defence brigade close to Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Nikolayevka, and Vasilevskaya Pustosh (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
More than 150 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and 25 motor vehicles of the AFU have been neutralised.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one special forces brigade, and one national guard brigade have been hit near Stepanovka, Sergeyevka, Krinitsy, Kamyshevakha, Andreyevka, and Kamenka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 335 troops, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations.
