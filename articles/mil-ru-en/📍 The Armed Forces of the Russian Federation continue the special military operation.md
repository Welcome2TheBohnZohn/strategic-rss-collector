# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-04 11:00:42

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30434

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The Sever Group of Forces improved its tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, losses were inflicted on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Berezhnoye, Garbuzovka, Doroshenkovo, and Kolodeznoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition, in Sumy region,
Russian troops engaged formations of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Markovka, Nikolskoye, Putivl, and Spasskoye (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 195 troops, 31 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
⏩
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade near Monachinovka, Smorodkovka (Kharkov region), Bogorodichnoye, Krymki, and Raygorodok of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, 14 motor vehicles, and four electronic warfare stations.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian troops hit formations of three mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Druzhkovka, Kondratovka, Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Starodubovka, and Yasnogorka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU lost more than 180 troops and 31 motor vehicles.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and four national guard brigades close to Belozerskoye, Verovka, Veselaya Gora, Kamenka, Krivorozhye, Roganskoye, and Yakovlevka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 335 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations.
