# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-25 09:31:24

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30269

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, and an airmobile brigade of the AFU close to Rubezhnoye, Metallovka, and Berezhnoye in Kharkov region.
💥
In Sumy region, formations of three mechanised brigades of the AFU were hit near Khoten, Ivolzhanskoye, and Nikolskoye (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 165 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, and one radar station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and a territorial defence brigade near Rubtsy, Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic) and Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 230 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 21 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
The Groups' units inflicted losses on formations of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade of the AFU, and a marine brigade near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Cherevkovka, and Druzhkovka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 165 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 24 motor vehicles, and three artillery guns.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements engaged manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades near Krivorozhye, Belozerskoye, Novopavlovka, Varvarovka, Novodonetskoye, Malotaranovka, Krinitsy, and Starovarvarovka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 345 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
