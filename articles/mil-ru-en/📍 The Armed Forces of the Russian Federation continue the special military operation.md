# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-16 11:45:26

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29440

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten the encirclement of the enemy's formations and expand the external control area.
💥
Combat operations
to liberate Malaya Volchya (Kharkov region)
continue in the encirclement ring. Russian elements inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of the enemy close to Serdobino, Khripuny, and Buzovo (Kharkov region).
The AFU assault group's attempt to leave the encirclement
near Gogino (Kharkov region)
was thwarted
.
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, the enemy has lost up to 50 troops, two Western-made armoured fighting vehicles, one motor vehicle, and two ground robotic systems.
📊
Since the beginning of the month, more than 600 troops, 15 armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, 13 ATVs, and 23 ground robotic systems have been neutralised in the encirclement ring.
🔥
During the external control area expansion,
strikes were delivered at
AFU formations close to Prikolotnoye, Petropavlovka, and Novoaleksandrovka (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition, in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of two tank brigades, one mechanised brigade, and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine close to Maly Burluk, Korbiny Ivany, Rassokhovatoye, Varvarovka, Izbitskoye, and Rubezhnoye.
📍
In Sumy region,
units of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two territorial defence brigades
were hit
near Tolstodubovo, Studenok, Krovnoye, Vakalovshchina, and Doroshovka.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 220 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and one Grad MLRS vehicle in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade were hit close to Putnikovo, Petrovka, Izyum, Proletarskoye, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Prishib, Sidorovo, Krymki, and Shchurovo (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 210 troops, 12 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns, including one UK-made 155-mm Braveheart self-propelled artillery system.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Nikolayevka, Semenovka, and Vasilyevskaya Pustosh (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 155 troops, 20 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
