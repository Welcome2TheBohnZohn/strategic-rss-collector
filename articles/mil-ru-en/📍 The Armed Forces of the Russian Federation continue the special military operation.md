# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-30 09:50:23

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29716

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces tighten the encirclement around the AFU troops in the north-eastern part of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
In the course of resolute offensive actions in the encirclement, the settlement of Shabelnoye (Kharkov region) has been liberated.
💥
There are battles underway for the settlements of Grachevka, Nesternoye, Rublenoye, and Reznikovo
(Kharkov region). In addition, the enemy's formations are being routed near Nefedovka, Nikolayevka, and Khatneye (Kharkov region).
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, the enemy has lost more than 30 Ukrainian troops in the encirclement.
↗️
In expanding the external control zone, Russian troops carry on fighting for the liberation of Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition, in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware
of a mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, a UAV regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine (AFU), a territorial defence brigade, and a National Guard brigade near Dergachi and Zarozhnoye (Kharkov region).
🚩
In Sumy region, with intensive offensive actions, the Russian troops have liberated Tolstodubovo.
💥
Strikes were delivered at
formations of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two territorial defence brigades near Turya, Rovnoye, Boyaro-Lezhachi, Vakalovshchina, and Khoten (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy has lost more than 340 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and 25 motor vehicles in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near Grushevka, Berezovka, Osinovo, Izyumskoye (Kharkov region), Volchiy Yar, Prishib, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 200 troops, a U.S.-made HMMWV armoured fighting vehicle, 13 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and an Anklav electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group's elements took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on units of four mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, a UAV brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two marine brigades near Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Semenovka, Nikolayevka, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, and Vysokoivanovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to more than 195 troops, a Canadian-made Senator armoured fighting vehicle, 20 motor vehicles, and an artillery gun.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an UAV brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and four National Guard brigades near Chuguyevo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Belozerskoye, Starorayskoye, Lidino, and Roganskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
↗️
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic),
the 51st Army's assault groups continue offensive operations in the north-eastern, central, and southern parts of the city. Over the past 24 hours, 164 buildings have been liberated, up to 35 militants of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, Ukrainian armed formations sustained losses of more than 455 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, and an electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
