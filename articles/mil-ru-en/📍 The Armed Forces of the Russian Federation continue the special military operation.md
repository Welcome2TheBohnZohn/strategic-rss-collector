# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-17 10:46:31

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30122

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The units of the Sever Group of Forces have completed neutralisation of the enemy
in the north-eastern part of Kharkov region and are carrying out actions to identify and destroy scattered, isolated groups of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔥
In Kharkov region,
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one motorised infantry brigade, and two assault regiments of the AFU have been hit close to Sosnovka, Makarovo, Velikiye Burluk, and Svetlichnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
strikes were delivered at formations of the AFU mechanised brigade and one territorial defence brigade close to Khoten and Bondarevshchina (Sumy region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 160 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, five motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations.
🚩
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line and, as a result of active actions, took control over the settlements of Palamarevka and Kovalevka (Kharkov region).
💥
Russian troops inflicted damage
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades and one assault brigade of the AFU near Andreyevka (Kharkov region), Lozovoye, Karpovka, and Rubtsy (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 230 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 16 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
🚩
As a result of resolute actions, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces liberated Alekseyevo-Druzhkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
🔥
The Group's units hit
formations of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade, and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Kramatorsk, Krasny Molochar, Semenovka, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 145 troops, one infantry fighting vehicle, 36 motor vehicles, one Bogdana self-propelled artillery system, and one electronic warfare station.
🚩
As a result of active and decisive actions,
the Tsentr Group's units liberated the settlements of Dobropolye, Pervomayskoye, and Sergeyevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of two mechanised assault brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, two marine brigades, and two national guard brigades close to Novopavlovka, Chuguyevo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Malotaranovka, Starovarvarovka, and Belozerskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 345 troops, one Italian-made Puma armoured personnel carrier, four armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations.
