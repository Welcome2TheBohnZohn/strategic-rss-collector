# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-17 10:10:56

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29457

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region
continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone.
🚩
As a result of resolute actions, Russian troops took control over Malaya Volchya in Kharkov region.
💥
Strikes were delivered
at enemy manpower close to Khripuny and Buzovo (Kharkov region). All attempts of the AFU units to break through from the encirclement have been thwarted.
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, up to 40 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and one self-propelled artillery system have been neutralised.
📊
Since the beginning of the month, more than 650 militants, 16 armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, 13 ATVs, and 25 UGVs have been neutralised.
💥
During the external control perimeter expansion, enemy groups
attempting to break through the fortifications
were eliminated
near Prikolotnoye, and Novoaleksandrovka (Kharkov region).
🔥
In addition, in Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of a heavy mechanised brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, a mechanised brigade, and an assault regiment of the AFU near Zolochev, Dementiyevka, Revoliutsionnoye, Grachevka, Bratenitsa, and Liptsy (Kharkov region).
📍
In Sumy region
, clusters of manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades have been hit near Grunovka, Kucherovka, Boyaro-Lezhachi, and Khothen (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy lost up to 220 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, five motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Formations of three mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Nizhneye Solenoye, Chervony Oskol, Izyum, Putnikovo, Nechvolodovka (Kharkov region), Krymki, Raygorodok, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to over 200 troops, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, seven motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade have been hit near Krasny Molochar, Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Nikolayevka, and Kondratovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 170 troops, one U.S.-made MaxxPro armoured vehicle, and 14 motor vehicles.
