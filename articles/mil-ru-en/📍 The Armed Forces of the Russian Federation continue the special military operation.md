# 📍 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-15 09:58:38

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30621

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The Sever Group of Forces improved its tactical situation.
In Kharkov region,Russian troops hit manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Berezniki, Novoselovka, Ploskoye, Sadovod, and Khatneye (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Sumy region, the Group's units inflicted losses on
formations of four mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades near Bititsa, Vakalovshchina, Krovnoye, Luzhki, Tokari, and Shtepovka of Sumy region.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 150 troops, six motor vehicles, one counter-fire radar station, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades near Malinovka, Monachinovka (Kharkov region), Brusovka, Karpovka, and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 210 troops, nine motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's elements engaged formations of four mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades, and an assault regiment of the AFU close to Druzhkovka, Klinovoye, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, Orekhovatka, and Slavyansk of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 180 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 26 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and four electronic warfare stations.
↗️
Units of the Tsentr Group of Forces continue to carry out intensive offensive operations.
Losses were inflicted on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an airborne brigade, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, three marine brigades, the Azov special operations brigade, and four national guard brigades close to Verovka, Golubovka, Mikhaylovka, Novodonetskoye, Novopavlovka, Sergeyevka, Starorayskoye, Uritskoye, and Yakovlevka of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
Up to 350 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, two motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
