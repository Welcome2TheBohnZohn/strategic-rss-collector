Published: 2026-10-22 20:28:05

🏎
Formula 1 of the skies
The new F-1 interceptor drone has been used for the first time
by the Tsentr Group's anti-aircraft missile brigade. In four days, drone teams
neutralised more than 20 AFU strike drones
.
📍
Dnepropetrovsk direction
'Good streamlining, wing aerodynamics, more stable. Good manufacturing material. Upgraded engine and regulator. Smaller form, comparatively light weight, but despite its lightness, it behaves quite steadily and is stable in any weather,' the team leader noted.
This drone
has shown high effectiveness in neutralising high-speed targets.
F-1 drone intercepts enemy reconnaissance and strike UAVs before they get a chance to attack the positions of our troops.
