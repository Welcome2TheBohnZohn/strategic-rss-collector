The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units engaged manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, the Azov special forces brigade, and four national guard brigades close to Andreyevka, Svyatogorovka, Belozherskoye, Novovodyanoye, Dobropolye, and Novotroitskoye of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 415 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade, two air assault brigades, and two assault regiments of the AFU near Mechetnoye, Podgavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasilkovoye, Vasinovka, Omelnik, and Chervony Yar (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 360 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and six motor vehicles.
💥
The Dnepr Group's units inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Yulyevka, Kushugum, and Grigorovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 50 troops and 14 motor vehicles of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
the Ukrainian transport and logistics infrastructure, used by the AFU, storage areas of UAVs and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 146 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
six guided aerial bombs, seven U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, one Neptune long-range cruise missile, and 1,030 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet eliminated
three uncrewed surface vehicles of the AFU.
