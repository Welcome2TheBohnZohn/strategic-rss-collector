# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles

Published: 2026-10-17 16:00:59

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30126

❗️
Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles
against military industry enterprises, energy infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, as well as ships involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
in Lutsk –
the Kromberg & Schubert enterprise
, which carried out repairs of automotive equipment in the interests of the AFU;
▫️
in Kolodnoye, Ternopol region –
an electrical substation
that supports the operation of military industry enterprises.
🔥
In addition, 40 km south of the port of Odessa, at sea,
a dry cargo
ship
carrying military and dual-use cargo was hit.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
