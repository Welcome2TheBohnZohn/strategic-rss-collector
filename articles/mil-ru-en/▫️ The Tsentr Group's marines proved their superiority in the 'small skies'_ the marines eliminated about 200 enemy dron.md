# ▫️ The Tsentr Group's marines proved their superiority in the 'small skies': the marines eliminated about 200 enemy drone command posts during the liberation of Dobropolye.

Published: 2026-10-20 13:02:35

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30170

▫️
The Tsentr Group's marines
proved their superiority in the 'small skies':
the marines eliminated about 200 enemy drone command posts during the liberation of Dobropolye.
Drones and artillery joint strikes in the city neutralised:
▫️
more than 20 ammunition and food depots;
▫️
about 150 temporary deployment areas and shelters of the enemy.
💬
The Unmanned Systems Service chief of the 177th Regiment with the call sign 'Sapsan'
spoke about the interaction of units:
'We work with an artillery commander online. After detection, we report the coordinates, inflict fire damage in cooperation'.
💥
By suppressing enemy drones and communications, the marines provided safe passage for assault groups.
The Unmanned Systems Service chief of the 61st Marine Brigade with the call sign 'Babay'
explained:
'At first, we hit their hardware, then their teams. Only after that it was possible to isolate the area of combat operations and give our infantry an opportunity to move forward'.
