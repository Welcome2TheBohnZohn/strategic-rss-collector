Footage from liberated Konstantinovka, filmed by fighters of the 84th Motorised Rifle Division and the 72nd Separate Motorised Rifle Brigade (Yuzhnaya Group of Forces).
Troops in designated sectors of the city are inspecting buildings on the territory of industrial enterprises, residential yards, and roads in their assigned sectors of the city for unexploded ordnance or signs of remote mining, which is still being carried out by long-range heavy AFU drones.
➡️
Consolidation units are paying particular attention to industrial areas:
the city's metallurgical plant, monitoring the situation in the treelines of Yubileyny Park, near the Iosif Stalin tank monument, and in the area of one of Konstantinovka's thoroughfares – Oleksa Tykhy Avenue, next to the Orthodox church and the railway station.
🎮
Reconnaissance drone operators
are maintaining 24/7 surveillance over key facilities and city streets
, tracking all movements of consolidation units and providing them with real-time situational updates within their operational areas.
