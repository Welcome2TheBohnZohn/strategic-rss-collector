Published: 2026-11-23 10:34:31

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30746

Road to life:
the Yuzhnaya Group's servicemen evacuated nine civilians from Konstantinovka.
▫️
First, the group was led along cleared paths under drone protection. When the weather turned, servicemen of the 78th Sever-Akhmat Regiment came out to meet them:
the elderly were supported by the arm, and when their strength ran out, fighters carried them on their backs.
'Ali carried an old lady 15 km on his back. Through mud like that. They are iron men!
We'll be praying for them for the rest of our lives,' one of the evacuees shares.
🤝
The final part of the journey they finished on an Ulan ATV, and safely reached a temporary accommodation.
The worst is now behind – these people are safe and warm.
▫️
And our lads head out for another task – they are needed in the city.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
