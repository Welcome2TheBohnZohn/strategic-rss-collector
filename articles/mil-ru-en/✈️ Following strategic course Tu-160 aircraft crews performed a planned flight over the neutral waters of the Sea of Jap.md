# ✈️ Following strategic course Tu-160 aircraft crews performed a planned flight over the neutral waters of the Sea of Japan.

Published: 2026-11-24 12:05:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30764

Following strategic course
✈️
Tu-160 aircraft crews
performed a planned flight over the neutral waters of the Sea of Japan.
⏱️
The flight of the Russian Aerospace Forces' long-range aircraft
lasted around 10 hours.
It was escorted by Su-35s and Su-30sm fighter jets.
➡️
At certain stages of the route, the aircraft were escorted by foreign fighter jets.
Crews of long-range aviation regularly conduct flights over the neutral waters of the Arctic, the North Atlantic, the Pacific Ocean, Black and Baltic seas. All Russian Aerospace Forces aircraft flights are carried out in strict compliance with international rules on the use of airspace.
