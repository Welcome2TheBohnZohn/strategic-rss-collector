Arctic amphibious assault: Northern Fleet's daily routine
A detachment of Northern Fleet ships
conducted a tactical landing exercise episode on the Sedov Archipelago in the Kara Sea.
According to the exercise scenario, an armed group of a mock enemy has taken up positions on Sredniy Island.
▫️
The Northern Fleet's navymen were effective and well-coordinated:
▫️
Helicopter manoeuvre:
from the patrol ship Ivan Papanin, a Ka-29 helicopter deployed an air assault detachment from the Separate Guards Marine Brigade on the coast, as well as a post to adjust the ship's artillery fire.
▫️
Technology patrol:
before the main assault, the coastline was studied with the help of robotic systems.
▫️
Dry landing:
the large landing ship Kondopoga approached the shore, and DT-10PN Vityaz two-section tracked all-terrain vehicles disembarked from its tweendeck.
▫️
Forced march:
servicemen successfully gain a bridgehead and moved to the designated area. On the way, a mine action group promptly eliminated all detected mines and munitions.
💥
The servicemen performed all tasks with flying colours. Keep it up!
