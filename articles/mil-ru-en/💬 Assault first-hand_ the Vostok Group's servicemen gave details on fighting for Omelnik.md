Assault first-hand: the Vostok Group's servicemen gave details on fighting for
Omelnik
in Zaporozhye region.
▫️
Assault detachments of the 38th Guards Motorised Rifle Brigade (35th Combined Arms Army)
overcame the prepared defence system of the enemy and took control over a large fortified area
of 27 square kilometres. The deputy platoon leader with call sign 'Med' spoke about the preparation of the enemy's routes and defence.
➡️
Russian troops entered the settlement area from several directions at once.
Some of the groups performed distracting actions while others approached the enemy's positions and entered the flank. The section leader with the call sign 'Dag' told about the assault and the militants' attempts to hold the settlement.
