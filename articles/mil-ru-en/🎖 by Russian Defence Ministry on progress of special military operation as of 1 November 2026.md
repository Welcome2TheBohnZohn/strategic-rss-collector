▫️
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an air assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, four national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Forces Brigade have been hit near Belozerskoye, Kamyshevakha, Andreyevka, Krinitsy, Starorayskoye, Sergeyevka, Verovka, Zelenoye, and Kamenka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 350 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station have been neutralised.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence
. Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, three air assault brigades, an assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit close to Podgavrilovka, Kuninova, Sidorenko, Velikomikhaylovka, Gavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novorozovka, and Zheltaya Krucha (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 205 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, and seven motor vehicles have been neutralised.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Orekhov, Yulyevka, Kirovo, Kushugum, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 20 troops and eight motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on the energy and transport infrastructure of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, assembly workshops, storage and launch sites of unmanned aerial vehicles, ammunition depots, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 139 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
391 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
