First military oath of the Moscow Guards Higher Combined Arms Command School
Minister of Defence Andrei Belousov took the oath of the first-year cadets of the Moscow Higher Combined Arms Command School. These cadets had already completed the basic training programme. Since now, Russian legendary Higher School has officially turned into the Guards Higher School.
💬
Speaking to the first-year cadets, the head of the Ministry of Defence stressed that the oath of loyalty to the Motherland
makes servicemen be counted not only with their comrades, but also with all times defenders for the independence of the country.
‘Be worthy of the glorious achievements of your predecessors, our country's reliable support. Hold proudly the title of serviceman of the Russian Army,’ said Andrei Belousov.
