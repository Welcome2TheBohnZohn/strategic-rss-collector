From basements to captivity: Tsentr Group's special forces push militants out of their shelters
Servicemen of the Tsentr Group's special operations unit told about performing tasks in Dnepropetrovsk direction.
▫️
The servicemen
conduct reconnaissance, reveal positions, UAV control posts, hardware and enemy manpower
, then transmit the coordinates for fire damage.
🎮
Reconnaissance drone operators
record all movements of militants and their hardware,
while strike UAV teams
promptly engage the targets
, thwarting the enemy rotation and delivery of ammunition.
🎯
During the assault, the special forces clear settlements and streets, operating in small groups.
Their combat work includes both fire damage and taking of prisoners:
fighters get into close contact with the enemy in basements and shelters, keep the situation under control and ensure the evacuation of the surrendered.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
