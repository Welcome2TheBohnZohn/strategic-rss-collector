# ❗️ During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered strikes against Ukrainian ports and ships used in the interests of the AFU.

Published: 2026-09-14 15:50:26

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29406

❗️
During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered strikes against Ukrainian ports and ships used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of UAV strikes, a dry cargo ship that had delivered cargo intended to support the AFU was hit in the port of Odessa.
💥
In addition,
strikes continued against railway infrastructure facilities
in the western regions of Ukraine used to transport military-purpose cargo from European countries.
