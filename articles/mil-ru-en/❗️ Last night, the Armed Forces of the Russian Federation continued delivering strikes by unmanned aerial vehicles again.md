Published: 2026-09-25 06:32:01

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against defence industry enterprises and logistics centres, as well as ships used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets
were hit:
📍
in Kiev region
– the assembly and storage site of unmanned aerial vehicles (Fire Point LLC) in the settlement of Borispol, where Honeywell TFE731 engines used as the powerplant for Flamingo long-range cruise missiles were stored;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
two logistics centres in Odessa and Nerubayskoye, which stored and distributed military and dual-use cargo arriving in Ukraine from European countries;
▫️
in the port of Reni – berthing infrastructure facilities and three depots with military equipment intended to support the AFU.
💥
In addition, Russian troops
hit a dry cargo ship
delivered military materiel and dual-use cargo to the port of Odessa in order to support the AFU at sea.
