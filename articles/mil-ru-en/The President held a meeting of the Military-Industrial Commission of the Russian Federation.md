# The President held a meeting of the Military-Industrial Commission of the Russian Federation

Published: 2026-09-18 20:01:52

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29492

The President held a meeting of the Military-Industrial Commission of the Russian Federation
Today, we will talk about the new draft State Armament Programme and the defense and industrial complex development programme. They will become the backbone for designing and serial production of modern weapons systems and hardware for our army and other defence and law enforcement agencies for them to be ready for a rapid and effective response to potential external threats. And, as is known, these threats are not diminishing.
