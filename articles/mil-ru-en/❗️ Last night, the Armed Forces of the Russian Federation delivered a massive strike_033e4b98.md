# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike

Published: 2026-09-12 06:06:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29347

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike
employing high-precision air-, ground- and sea-based weapons, as well as strike unmanned aerial vehicles,
as a result of which the following targets were hit:
▫️
Ukrainian metals industry enterprises
producing military-purpose products, in the cities of Zaporozhye, Dnepropetrovsk and Krivoy Rog,
▫️
port infrastructure facilities and a strategically important transport infrastructure facility in Odessa region
, which ensured the unloading and storage of military-purpose cargo and a significant share of foreign traffic of weapons and ammunition into Ukraine.

Russian Defence Ministry
