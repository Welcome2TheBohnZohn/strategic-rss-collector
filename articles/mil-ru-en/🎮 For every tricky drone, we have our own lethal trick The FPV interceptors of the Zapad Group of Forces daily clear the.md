For every tricky drone, we have our own lethal trick
🎮
The FPV interceptors of the Zapad Group of Forces daily clear the sky over the eastern bank of the Krasno-Oskol Reservoir.
🎯
The enemy goes all the way to reach our positions.
For example, they send large UAVs, 'motherships', filled with small attack drones. But our guys showed some ingenuity and
figured out how to fight back:
'We don't destroy the dropped drones, but the mothership drone itself. We hit it and attack sub-units fall from it,' said the drone operator with the call sign 'Mot'.
