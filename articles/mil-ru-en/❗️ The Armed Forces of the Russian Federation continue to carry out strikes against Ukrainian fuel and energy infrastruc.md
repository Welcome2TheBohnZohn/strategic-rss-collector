The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to carry out strikes
against Ukrainian fuel and energy infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, as well as maritime vessels involved in the delivery of military-related cargo.
💥
Last night, unmanned aerial vehicles
hit a 330 kV substation
near Beloshitsy of Zhitomir region, which supplied electricity to Ukrainian defence industry enterprises.
🔥
In addition, in the Black Sea south-west of Odessa, a
container ship
delivering weapons and military equipment to Ukraine
was hit.
