📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one Unmanned Systems Forces assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, four national guard brigades, and the Azov special forces brigade close to Belozerskoye, Malotaranovka, Andreyevka, Maryevka, Verovka, Pervomayskoye (Donetsk People's Republic), Novopavlovka, and Chuguyevo (Dnepropetrovsk region).
⚡️
In the settlement of Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Combined Arms Army
carried out offensive operations in the north-western direction
and, cooperating with marine units, continued to deliver strikes at the AFU's encircled units in the west and east areas of the city. More than 30 militants have been neutralised, and 94 buildings have been mopped up. Over the past 24 hours, the enemy has not attempted to break out of the encirclement.
▪️
In total, up to 420 troops, four Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, four field artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Pokrovskoye, Gavrilovka, Orly, Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Tarasovka, Yegorovka, and Barvinovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 290 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, and three artillery guns.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Malokaterinovka, Novoyakovlevka, Kirovo, Kamyshevakha, Orekhov, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 45 troops, 19 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
transport, fuel and power infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, manufacturing workshops producing unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries in 149 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four guided aerial bombs, one HIMARS-made MLRS projectile, and 505 fixed-wing UAVs.
