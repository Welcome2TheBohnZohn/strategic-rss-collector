Russia and Kyrgyzstan approve military cooperation plan for 2027
The cooperation plan between the defence ministries of Russia and Kyrgyzstan for 2027 was signed during a meeting between the heads of the two countries' military departments.
💬
During the meeting, Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov noted that Russian-Kyrgyz military cooperation
is developing dynamically.
He also emphasised that particular attention in the interaction between the two defence ministries is given to the operational and combat training, personnel education and sharing experience, functioning of the joint Russian military base on the territory of Kyrgyzstan, and military-technical cooperation.
🤝
In turn, Ruslan Mukambetov
thanked his Russian counterpart for the assistance
provided to the Republic by the Russian Ministry of Defence in strengthening combat readiness of the Kyrgyz Armed Forces, including military and military-technical cooperation, personnel training and other areas of interaction.
👉
He also
expressed gratitude to the Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation
for inviting the Kyrgyz Armed Forces to take part in the recently completed Centre 2026 strategic command post exercise.
The talks between the heads of the Russian and Kyrgyz military departments preceded the meeting of the Council of Defence Ministers of the CIS member states.
