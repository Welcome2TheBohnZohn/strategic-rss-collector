# 👇 Here is how our Armed Forces have been reinforced Su-57 and Su-35S fighter jets.

Published: 2026-10-06 16:53:55

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29874

Here is how our Armed Forces have been reinforced
👇
✈️
Su-57 and Su-35S fighter jets.
The aircraft were delivered in a new technical configuration which helps expand their operational tasks, while the advanced capabilities built into the aviation weapons system allow for the use of new types of ordnance.
💥
T-90M, T-80BVM, and T-72B3M tanks.
The accepted batches of tanks of various modifications were delivered from the factories with additional protection systems that were designed taking into account experience of the special military operation.
🎮
Troops were reinforced with
Supercam, Skat, Orlan, and Molniya reconnaissance UAVs
of various modifications, FPV strike drones and hexacopters, as well as multi-purpose unmanned ground vehicles.
▫️
Radar stations, Zubr air target engagement systems, ZAK-30 Citadel systems
, and other ones capable of detecting, tracking, and engaging aerial targets, which have proven their effectiveness in the special military operation zone and border regions.
⚓️
As part of the Navy's re-equipment program, the latest
minesweeper Semen Agafonov (Project 12700)
built for the Northern Fleet was floated out in September.
⚔️
According to the Procurement Schedule,
over one million units of ordnance
were supplied to the troops, including a wide range of artillery ammunition, aircraft missiles and bombs, UAV warheads, anti-drone cartridges for servicemen's standard small arms, and others.
