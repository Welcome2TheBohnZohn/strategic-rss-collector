The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Belozerskoye, Roganskoye, Andreyevka, Novovodyanoye, Sergeyevka, Bokovoye, Veseloye Pole, and Gornyak (Donetsk People's Republic).
🔥
In Dobropolye of the Donetsk Donetsk People's Republic,
units of the 51st Combined Arms Army continue to destroy surrounded AFU formations
in the west and east of the city. Up to 40 militants were neutralised. Over the past 24 hours, the enemy has not attempted to break out of the encirclement.
▪️
In total, up to 415 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence
. Manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, two air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade have been hit close to Vesely Kut, Gavrilovka, Ivanovka, Orly (Dnepropetrovsk region), Granichnoye, Obshcheye, Yegorovka, and Omelnik (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 275 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and five motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. A mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Dimitrovo, Orekhov, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 35 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 22 motor vehicles were neutralised.
