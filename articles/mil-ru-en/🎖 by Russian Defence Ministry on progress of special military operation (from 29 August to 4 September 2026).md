🚩
The Vostok Group of Forces continue to advance into the depth of the enemy's defence.
Last week, the settlement of Chervonaya Krinitsa (Zaporozhye region) was liberated.
💥
The Russian troops inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware
of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, two airborne brigades, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU's losses in the area of responsibility of the Vostok Group of Forces amounted to more than 2,230 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, and 50 motor vehicles.
🚩
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation and
liberated Novopavlovka (Zaporozhye region).
💥
Three mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one unmanned systems brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade were hit.
▪️
Over the past week, the enemy sustained losses of more than 330 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 120 motor vehicles, and 15 electronic warfare stations.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
49 guided aerial bombs, 19 U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, eight Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 6,093 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
💥
In the Black Sea waters, the Black Sea Fleet eliminated 14 uncrewed surface vehicles of the Ukrainian Navy.
