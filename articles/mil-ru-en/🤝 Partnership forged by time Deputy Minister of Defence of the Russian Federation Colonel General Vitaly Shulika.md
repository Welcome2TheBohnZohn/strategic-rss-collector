Partnership forged by time
🤝
Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Colonel General Vitaly Shulika
took part in a solemn reception on the occasion of the 78th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between the Russian Federation and the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.
👉
Deputy Head of the Russian military department emphasised that today Russian-Korean ties are going through one of the best periods in their entire history.
The strategic partnership is based on the coincidence of fundamental national interests, common views on the international situation and world order, a high level of mutual trust, and the preservation of long-term good-neighbourliness for the benefit of the peoples of the two countries.
💬
Colonel General Vitaly Shulika, on behalf of the Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation,
expressed gratitude to the leadership of the DPRK and personally to the Chairman of State Affairs of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, Comrade Kim Jong Un
, noting the special role of Korean servicemen in the liberation of the Kursk region from the Kiev regime's armed formations, in demining and restoring Russian infrastructure facilities.
‘I am convinced that the Russian-Korean partnership's strengthening meets the fundamental interests of our countries. It makes an invaluable contribution to ensuring stability not only in the region, but throughout the world,’ Colonel General Vitaly Shulika concluded.
