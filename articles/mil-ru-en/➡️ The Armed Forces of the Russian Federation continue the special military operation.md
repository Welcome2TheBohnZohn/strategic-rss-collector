# ➡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-13 10:33:14

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30013

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
➡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region tighten
the encirclement and expand the external control zone.
💥
Fights are underway in the encirclement
for the liberation of Nikolayevka, Varvarovka, and Nefedovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU's encircled units lost up to 60 troops, one motor vehicle, and five ground robotic systems during the day.
➡️
In the course of combat operations to establish control over the settlements
of Prikolotnoye, Kotovka, and Gogino (Kharkov region), Russian elements continue to expand the external control zone.
🔥
In addition, in Kharkov region,
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigade
have been hit
near Velikiye Prokhody, Khotomlya, Pogoreloye, Russkaya Lozovaya, Tsirkuny, and Velikiy Burluk (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two territorial defence brigades close to Glukhov, Yuryevo, Barilovka, Maryino, Konotop, Nikolskoye, and Sumy.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 270 troops, one tank, eight motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
📍
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line
and inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades close to Grushevka, Blagodatovka, Berezovka, Maliyevka, Osinovo (Kharkov region), and Volchiy Yar (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 230 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 17 motor vehicles, three artillery guns, and four electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and one marine brigade near Kramatorsk, Semenovka, Slavyansk, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 160 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 31 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
