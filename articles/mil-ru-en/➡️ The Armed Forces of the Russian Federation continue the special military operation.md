# ➡️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-14 13:07:57

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30033

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
➡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region tighten the encirclement
and expand the external control zone.
💥
Fights are underway
in the encirclement
for the liberation of Nikolayevka and Varvarovka
(Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU surrounded task force has lost up to 40 troops during the day.
▫️
While extending the external control zone, Russian units operate
to seize Prikolotnoye, Kotovka, and Gogino (Kharkov region).
💥
Manpower and hardware
of a heavy mechanised brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a detachment of the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine have been hit near Kurgannoye, Fedorovka, Maly Burluk, and Khatneye (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Kharkov region
, manpower and hardware of two motorised infantry brigades and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit close to Rubezhnoye, Velikiye Prokhody, Bugayevka, Izbitskoye, and Velikiy Burluk (Kharkov region).
📍
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at formations of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades close to Velikaya Rybitsa, Nikolskoye, Barilovka, Kucherovka, Glukhov (Sumy region), and the city of Sumy.
▪️
In total, more than 225 troops, 17 motor vehicles, one Grad MLRS and four electronic warfare stations have been neutralised by the enemy in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line
and inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades close to Tamarganovka, Blagodatovka, Maliyevka, Osinovo (Kharkov region), Bogorodichnoye, and Volchiy Yar in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 240 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 23 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, an airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Vysokoivanovka, Semenovka, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 155 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 33 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
