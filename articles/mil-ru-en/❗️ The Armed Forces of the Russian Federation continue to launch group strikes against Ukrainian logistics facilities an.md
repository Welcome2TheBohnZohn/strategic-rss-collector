# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to launch group strikes against Ukrainian logistics facilities and vessels,

Published: 2026-09-01 14:30:46

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29155

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to launch group strikes against Ukrainian logistics facilities and vessels,
used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
During the day,
the following targets were neutralised by attack drones:
▫️
in Usatovo (Odessa region),
a logistics centre
used to store and distribute military cargo;
▫️
in the port of Yuzhny,
a dry cargo ship
with military equipment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▫️
in the Black Sea waters,
a dry cargo ship
with weapons and ammunition.

Russian Defence Ministry
