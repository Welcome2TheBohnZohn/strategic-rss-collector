The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Omelnik in Zaporozhye region.
💥
Strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, three air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Kolomiytsy, Podgavrilovka, Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Volnyanka, and Zheltay Krucha (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 165 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and five motor vehicles.
↗️
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation
. Formations of a mechanised brigade of the AFU and a marine brigade have been hit near Orekhov and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 25 troops, one tank, 18 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on drone workshops, depots and launch sites of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 147 areas.

405 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
