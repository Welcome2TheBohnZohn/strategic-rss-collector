↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian elements engaged manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Barvinovka, Zalivnoye, Tersyanka, Listovka, Vasilevskoye, Sergeyevka (Zaporozhye region), Kolomiytsy, Gavrilovka, and Katerinovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 215 troops, three armoured personnel carriers, and five motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian units hit formations of one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Kamyshevakha, Grigorovka, and Orekhov (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 25 troops, 15 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on port infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, launch and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 144 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four Neptune long-range guided missiles and 1,265 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
