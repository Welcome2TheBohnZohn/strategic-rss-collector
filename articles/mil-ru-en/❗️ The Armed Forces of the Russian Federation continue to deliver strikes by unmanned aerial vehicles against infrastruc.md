# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to deliver strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against infrastructure facilities of Ukraine and ships

Published: 2026-10-04 14:43:51

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29826

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to deliver strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against infrastructure facilities of Ukraine and ships
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
Today, as a result of a strike
in Kiev, the Northern Road Bridge across the Dnepr River
, which supports the transfer of troops and the logistics of military cargo, was hit.
🔥
In addition,
two dry cargo ships
delivering military equipment to the port of Odessa
were hit at sea in the north-western part of the Black Sea
.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
