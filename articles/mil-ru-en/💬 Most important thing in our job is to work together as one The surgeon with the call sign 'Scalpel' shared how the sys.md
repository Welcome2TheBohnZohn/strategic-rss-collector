Most important thing in our job is to work together as one
💬
The surgeon with the call sign 'Scalpel' shared how the system of saving wounded soldiers is built.
It's a tough but perfectly organised mechanism:
first, servicemen are stabilised on the front line, then they are provided with first-aid care and immediately passed to evacuation brigades, who take them to a hospital.
There are no extra people or secondary tasks in this sequence. Every stage is important: bandaging, injection, boarding into a vehicle. The main weapons of military medics is mutual trust and team work.
