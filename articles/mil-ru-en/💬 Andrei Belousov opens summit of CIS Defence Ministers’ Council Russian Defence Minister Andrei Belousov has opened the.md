Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov has opened the meeting of the Council of Defence Ministers of the member states of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS), which is being held in Cholpon-Ata in the Kyrgyz Republic.
💬
In opening the meeting, the head of the Russian military department stated that it is taking place
amid a complex military-political situation in the world and along the perimeter of the CIS borders
, which requires coordinated action and joint efforts to ensure the security of the CIS countries.
'In this connection, we attach particular importance to enhancing the readiness of the armed forces to respond to emerging challenges and threats. It is on this basis that the agenda of the meeting has been prepared,' noted the Russian Defence Minister.
📑
During the joint work,
the attendees will present reports on approaches to building national armed forces, use of new types of weaponry, and current trends in the organisation of operational and combat training
, as well as sign documents aimed at improving interaction and strengthening the potential of joint military systems.
