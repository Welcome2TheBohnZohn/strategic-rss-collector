📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian troops inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Druzhkovka, Orekhovatka, Kondratovka, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Slavyansk, Starodubovka, Kramatorsk, and Nikolayevka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 165 troops, one Canadian-made Senator armoured fighting vehicle, 17 motor vehicles, and one British-made 155-mm Braveheart self-propelled artillery system.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades near Novovodyanoye, Dobropolye, Gulevo, Maryevka, Andreyevka, Belozerskoye (Donetsk People's Republic), and Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 345 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an air assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade near Ivanovka, Orestopol (Dnepropetrovsk region), Samoylovka, Omelnik, Vasilevskoye, and Obshcheye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 285 troops, 13 motor vehicles, and a counter-fire station.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades near Orekhov, Malokaterinovka, Yulyevka, Yurkovka, and Grigorovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 35 troops, 15 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
Ukrainian energy and transport infrastructure used by the AFU, storage and assembly facilities of unmanned aerial vehicles, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 139 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
six guided aerial bombs and 589 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
