# 💬 'We hit everything that moves' A drone operator of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces described his combat work in Druzhkovka direction.

Published: 2026-11-11 12:33:17

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30552

'We hit everything that moves'
💬
A drone operator of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces
described his combat work in Druzhkovka direction.
🎮
According to the serviceman, our drone operators
engage every target without exception:
'We hit infantry, motor vehicles, Baba Yaga drones. Basically, anything that moves, we hit it'.
👉
As the operator himself admits,
the nerves of the first combat sorties long ago gave way to cold, precise work,
where the main task is to keep improving the results.
'When you hit your first unit of hardware, it's always incredibly exciting and always memorable. Then you simply get used to it all, and it becomes like a job. You just try to do it better and better each time'.
▫️
This calm attitude to the job is what shows true professionalism.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
