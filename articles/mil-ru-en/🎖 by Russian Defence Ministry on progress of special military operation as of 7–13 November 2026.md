🚩
Last week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence, as a result of resolute actions they liberated the settlements of Vasilkovoye and Voskresenka (Zaporozhye region).
💥
They inflicted fire damage
on the AFU manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, four air assault brigades, two assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two marine brigades.
▪️
In total, the AFU lost more than 1,415 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, and 51 motor vehicles in the area of responsibility of the Vostok Group of Forces.
↗️
During the week, the Dnepr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted fire damage on three mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Up to 230 Ukrainian troops, 126 motor vehicles, five artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
🎯
Air defence systems have shot down 18 guided aerial bombs,
four U.S.-made Rusty Dagger cruise missiles, four Neptune long-range cruise missiles, four U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, as well as 6,177 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet destroyed
two uncrewed surface vehicles of the Ukrainian Navy in the north-western part of the Black Sea.
