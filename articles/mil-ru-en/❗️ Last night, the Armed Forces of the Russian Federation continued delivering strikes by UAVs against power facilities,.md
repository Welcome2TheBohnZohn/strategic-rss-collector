# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by UAVs against power facilities, port and railway infrastructure, as well as ships involved 

Published: 2026-11-14 06:39:05

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30600

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by UAVs against power facilities, port and railway infrastructure, as well as ships involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
As a result of the strike, the following targets
were hit:
▪️
an electrical substation
in Priluki (Chernigov region), which supports the operation of defence industry enterprises, as well as logistics centres used for storing military equipment;
▪️
a dry cargo ship
at sea, delivering military cargo to the port of Odessa.
▪️
In addition,
two railway locomotives
were hit near the Matveyevka railway station in Zaporozhye region, used for transporting cargo intended to support the AFU.
