🚩
During the week, the Tsentr Group's units have liberated Dobropolye, Pervomayskoye, and Sergeyevka (Donetsk People's Republic) as a result of active actions.
💥
Strikes have been delivered at
formations of five mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one jaeger brigade, one air assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, five national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade.
▪️
In total, more than 2,405 troops, 44 armoured fighting vehicles, 53 motor vehicles, 16 field artillery guns, and 15 electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
🚩
Over the past week, the Vostok Group's units have continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence and, as a result of decisive actions, have liberated Omelnik, Timoshevka, Chervony Yar and Mirovka (Zaporozhye region) and established control over the settlement of Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
💥
Strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware
of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade. The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,380 troops, 22 armoured fighting vehicles, 38 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
🚩
Last week, the Dnepr Group's units improved the tactical situation and liberated Malokaterinovka and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
💥
Russian elements have inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade.
▪️
Up to 215 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 127 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and eight electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
five guided aerial bombs, three U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, seven Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 5,593 fixed-wing UAVs.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet eliminated
five uncrewed surface vehicles of the Ukrainian Navy
in the Black Sea waters.
