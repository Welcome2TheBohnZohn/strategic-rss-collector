Good luck! Leonid Gornin congratulated the Military University on Knowledge Day.
💬
The first Deputy Defence Minister of the Russian Federation
congratulated cadets and teachers of the Military University of the Russian Defence Ministry on the start of the new academic year.
He wished everyone success and noted that
an important project, 'Military education in the service of the Fatherland,' is actively developing on the initiative of the Minister of Defence.
📌
There's a lot of interesting and important work ahead, because
the main goal is to make studying even more modern and high-quality.
Here is what Leonid Gornin said about the project:
The key courses of the project are:
military education adaptation to new requirements of combat operations involving the use of modern military hardware, material and technical facilities development, creating conditions for continuous vocational education.
📚
May this academic year bring new and useful knowledge, loyal friends, and pride for your work! Good luck on your way to new heights!
