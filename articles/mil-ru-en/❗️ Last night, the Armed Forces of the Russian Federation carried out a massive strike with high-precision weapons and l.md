# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike with high-precision weapons and long-range attack unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprise

Published: 2026-09-01 08:02:17

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29141

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out a massive strike with high-precision weapons and long-range attack unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of military-industrial and fuel-power systems in Kiev and Kiev region,
as well as infrastructure facilities in Odessa, Odessa region, the ports of Odessa and Chernomorsk used to store and deliver military supplies, fuel and lubricants for the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
📍
The following targets were hit in Kiev:
▫️
an industry enterprise of Fire Point LLC.
It produced components and combat units for FP-5 Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, as well as solid-fuel accelerators;
▫️
an industry enterprise of Aerobavovna LLC.
It produced aerostats to deliver and provide control of long- and medium-range unmanned aerial vehicles, including the Hornet loitering munitions;
▫️
a locomotive shed of Darnitsa.
It repaired railway locomotives, and it was one of the key service centres of the locomotive fleet involved in delivering military cargo.
📍
The following targets were hit in Kiev region:
▫️
a uncrewed surface vehicle
manufacturing and storing workshop
in the settlement of Khotov;
▫️
an oil refinery
delivering POLs to the AFU in the settlement of Borispol.
📍
The following targets were hit in Odessa and Odessa region:
▫️
an electric power plant and three electric substations,
which operated the port infrastructure, as well as a UAV assembly workshop;
💥
16 depots
with military weaponry and hardware and
six POL tanks
supplied to the Armed Forces of Ukraine by Western countries in the port of Odessa;
💥
two depots with military equipment intended for supplying the Armed Forces of Ukraine in the port of Chernomorsk;
💥
a border
checkpoint
and
a ferry crossing
in Orlovka.

Russian Defence Ministry
