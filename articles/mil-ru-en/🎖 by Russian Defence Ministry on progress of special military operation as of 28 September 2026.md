🚩
The Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Novovodyanoye (Donetsk People's Republic) by active actions.
💥
Strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Belozerskoye, Sergeyevka, Yuryevka, Novofedorovka, Novogrigorovka, Roganskoye, Maryevka, Pervomayskoye, Veseloye Pole (Donetsk People's Republic), Novopavlovka, and Novolozovatovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▫️
Over the past 24 hours, assault groups of the 51st Army have advanced in a western direction in north-eastern, central and southern parts of Dobropolye in the Donetsk People's Republic.
Marine units were operating in the eastern part of the city, destroying the enemy's fortified stronghold. Over the past 24 hours, 70 buildings have been liberated, up to 30 troops have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 460 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, and one Grad MLRS were neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence
. Russian troops inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade, two air assault brigades, and one assault regiment of the AFU near Omelnik, Vasinovka, Mirolyubovka, Kievskoye, Obshcheye, Golubkovo, Samoylovka (Zaporozhye region), Orly, Dibrova, Vsesvyatskoye, and Podgavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 180 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
The losses were inflicted on one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade close to Preobrazhenka, Yurkovka, Kirovo, Orekhov, Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region) and Zaporozhye.
▪️
Up to 25 troops and eight motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on production workshops and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 151 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
10 guided aerial bombs and 442 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
