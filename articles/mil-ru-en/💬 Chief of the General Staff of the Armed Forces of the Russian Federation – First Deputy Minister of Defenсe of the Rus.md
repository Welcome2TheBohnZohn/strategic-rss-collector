Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation – First Deputy Minister of Defenсe of the Russian Federation General of the Army Valery Gerasimov
’In relation to the conduct of a humanitarian operation in the area of the settlement of Aleshki, Kherson region, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation are ceasing fire on the Kherson direction and introducing a 'silence regime' from 04:00 Moscow time on 27 October 2026.
In order to ensure the safety of the population and the delivery of socially important cargo, engineer reconnaissance and safe movement of the humanitarian convoy to the settlement of Aleshki, Kherson region, will be organised and ensured’.
