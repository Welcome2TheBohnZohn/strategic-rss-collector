The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, an airmobile brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Ivanovka, Kolomiytsy, Lysaya Balka, Orly (Dnepropetrovsk region), Moskovka, Nikolskoye, Obshcheye, Samoylovka, and Svoboda (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 270 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 12 motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces' units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements hit a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine close to Dimitrovo, Orekhov, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops and four motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have hit military airfields, energy infrastructure facilities used for the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, assembly and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 148 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs and 650 fixed-wing UAVs.
