# ❗️ From 14 to 20 November, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two massive and 11 group strikes,

Published: 2026-11-20 11:14:14

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30697

❗️
From 14 to 20 November, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two massive and 11 group strikes,
which hit Ukrainian defence industry enterprises used in the production of cruise missiles, long- and medium-range UAVs, logistics centres, power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the AFU, production stations, and storage areas of robotic systems, including unmanned aerial vehicles, military airfields, ammunition depots, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
🔥
In addition, the facilities of the Ukrainian port infrastructure, which receive, store, and distribute of military cargo,
were disabled
. Over the week,
12 dry cargo ships
delivered military hardware and dual-use goods to Ukraine for the AFU
were neutralised.
💥
During the week, units of the Sever Group of Forces inflicted losses on
formations of five mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, an airmobile brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, three territorial defence brigades, and a national guard brigade.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces, the enemy lost more than 1,150 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 66 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and 15 electronic and counter-fire warfare stations.
↗️
During the week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces have taken advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units hit manpower and military hardware of five mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,495 troops, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, 116 motor vehicles, eight field artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on formations of five mechanised brigades, an assault brigade, two airmobile brigades, a mountain assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, a marine brigade, and a national guard brigade.
▪️
In total, in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,150 troops, 15 armoured fighting vehicles, 176 motor vehicles, nine field artillery guns, and five electronic warfare stations.
