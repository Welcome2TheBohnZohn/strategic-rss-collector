Pushing through the mine barrier at Golubkovo

A fighter of the 36th Separate Motorised Rifle Brigade of the 29th Combined Arms Army told how they liberated Golubkovo in Zaporozhye region:
'The enemy blanketed the area with mines and obstacles, including seeded ones dropped by Baba Yaga drones. We had to watch our steps. So the advance was slow'.
But this did not stop our assault detachments. During the liberation of Golubkovo, the enemy lost:
