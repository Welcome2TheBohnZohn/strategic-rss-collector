# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched group UAV strikes

Published: 2026-10-18 06:40:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30131

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched group UAV strikes
against logistics centres and ports of Ukraine, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result,
in Rovno, the Novaya Pochta logistics centre,
where dual-use cargo was stored and distributed, uncluding long- and medium-range UAVs, was hit.
🔥
In addition, 30 kilometres east of the port of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military and dual-use cargo was hit at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
