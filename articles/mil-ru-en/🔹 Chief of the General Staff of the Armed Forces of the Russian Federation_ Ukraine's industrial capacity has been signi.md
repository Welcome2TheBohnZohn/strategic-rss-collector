Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation: Ukraine's industrial capacity has been significantly reduced
Report
by Valery Gerasimov:
On 7 October, a massive strike was carried out against Ukrainian defence industry enterprises involved in the production and storage of Flamingo cruise missiles and long- and medium-range Fire Point unmanned aerial vehicles.
As a result of direct strikes, the machining, testing, and assembly workshops as well as specialised equipment, finished products and components for the production of cruise missiles and unmanned aerial vehicles, were destroyed at the Yuzhmash Machine-Building Plant in Dnepropetrovsk.
Following secondary detonations, two production buildings and the Flamingo cruise missiles stored inside them were completely destroyed, along with components for FP-5 operational-tactical missiles at the premises of the Smart Ammunition LLC in Kiev. In addition, four production workshops manufacturing components for missiles and unmanned aerial vehicles, together with specialised equipment and finished products, were destroyed at the premises of the Kiev Cardboard and Paper Mill in Obukhov and the Mechanical Plant in Lozovaya, Kharkov Region. The operations of these enterprises have been suspended for an extended period.
In total, 12 massive strikes were carried out in September and October, targeting 30 defence industry enterprises and logistics centres involved in the production and storage of missiles and strike drones as well as 14 fuel, energy and transport infrastructure facilities. As a result, Ukraine's industrial capacity to manufacture long-range weapons, including cruise and ballistic missiles, has been significantly reduced. Its ability to procure the equipment required to restore the country's industrial and energy sectors has also been diminished.
At the same time, key enterprises in the metallurgical sector are being targeted in order to undermine Ukraine's military and economic potential. Their products are used in military manufacturing and are also exported to pay for deliveries of weapons and ammunition.
As of today, it can be stated that steel production in Ukraine has halted. The strikes have hit nine Ukrainian metallurgical enterprises.
