# 🤝 Today, Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Colonel General Vitaly Shulika congratulated cadets and academic staff of the Military Academy for Information Securit

Published: 2026-09-01 14:02:45

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29152

🤝
Today, Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Colonel General Vitaly Shulika congratulated cadets and academic staff of the Military Academy for Information Security named after Army General S.M. Shtemenko.
📆
This academic year is symbolic for the Academy.
Just recently, on July 1, 2026, the legendary Krasnodar Higher Military School was granted the status of Military Academy. This is where unique cyber security specialists study in the interests of Russian army and foreign colleagues.
💬
For Vitaly Shulika, this institute is special; he graduated in 1992. After presenting state awards to officers,
the Deputy Minister held the first lecture for first-year cadets.
Vitaly Shulika told cadets about the importance of the Fatherland's defender profession.
✅
He stressed that
‘the development of military education and the improvement of the quality of military personnel formation
is one of our priorities today’.
▫️
The Ministry of Defence continues to implement a long-term
large-scale project 'Military education in the service of the Fatherland'
, which is aimed at improving the military education system, widespread introduction of innovations, creating a comfortable educational environment, and strengthening the prestige of military service.
📚
At the end, Vitaly Shulika
wished the cadets luck in mastering science and achieving their goals.
▫️
Comrades, study hard, make your dreams come true confidently and remember:
the future of our Motherland is in your trusted hands!
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
