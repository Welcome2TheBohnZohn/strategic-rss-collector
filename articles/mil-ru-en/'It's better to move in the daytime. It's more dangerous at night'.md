'It's better to move in the daytime. It's more dangerous at night'
💬
The storming soldier with the call sign 'Vysota'
shared such an unusual detail of the assault. He took part in
liberation
of Nezhenka
in Zaporozhye region.
▫️
It's all about a lot of mines and artificial obstacles,
which the serviceman may not be able to see at night.
▫️
At the same time,
it is impossible to stop in the open,
and hostile drones are constantly spinning around in the sky.
'There was an open terrain of up to a kilometre. It was literally five to ten minutes to overcome them,' the storming soldier recalls.
▫️
Despite this,
Russian troops did all they could and completed the task.
Well done!
