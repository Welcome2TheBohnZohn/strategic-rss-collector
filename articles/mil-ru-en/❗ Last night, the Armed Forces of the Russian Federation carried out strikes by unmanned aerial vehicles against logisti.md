Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres, warehouses, and ports in Ukraine as well as vessels used to support the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes,
the following targets were hit
:
📍
in Kiev region:
▫️
a warehouse belonging to the FIRE POINT company in Boyarka (11 km south-west of Kiev), used to store components and launch boosters for long-range UAVs;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
a dry cargo ship in the port of Odessa, which had delivered military cargo;
▫️
a Novaya Pochta logistics centre (1.5 km north of Odessa), that stored and distributed military cargo delivered to Ukraine.
