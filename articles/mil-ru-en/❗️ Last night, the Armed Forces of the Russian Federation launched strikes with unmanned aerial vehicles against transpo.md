Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched strikes
with unmanned aerial vehicles against transport infrastructure and defence industry facilities, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
▪️
a 330 kV power substation in Zaporozhye
, which provides support for Ukrainian defence industry enterprises;
▪️
two railway locomotives
in Dnepropetrovsk transporting weapons and military hardware;
▪️
a 330 kV power substation in Zelenodolsk of Dnepropetrovsk region
supporting the operation of Ukrainian defence industry enterprises;
▪️
an industrial enterprise in the village of Snovsk in Chernigov region
, where UAV components were produced;
▪️
a dry cargo ship in the Chernomorsk port,
which delivered military cargo to Ukraine.
