↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
The Group's units engaged manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, an air assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and a marine brigade near Velikomikhaylovka, Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Golubkovo, Obshcheye, Vasilkovoye, Yegorovka, Nikolskoye, and Vasinovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 270 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 10 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of two mechanised brigades of the AFU were hit near Orekhov, Yurkovka, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region). The enemy lost up to 40 troops and 21 motor vehicles.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on logistics centres, production workshops of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 148 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
six guided aerial bombs, one HIMARS-made MLRS projectile, and 629 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
