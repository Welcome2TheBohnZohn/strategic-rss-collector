# ❗️ From 10 to 16 October, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out massive and seven group strikes

Published: 2026-10-16 11:11:18

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30084

❗️
From 10 to 16 October, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out massive and seven group strikes
. As a result, they hit enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry, logistics and telecommunication centres, transport infrastructure facilities used for the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, production and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles, ammunition and military hardware depots as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
💥
Moreover,
a number of infrastructure facilities of Ukrainian ports
, which received, stored, and distributed military cargo, have been disabled.
▪️
Over the week, 15 dry cargo ships involved in supplying Western weapons, military hardware, fuel, and materiel to the Armed Forces of Ukraine were neutralised.
⏩
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continued to tighten the encirclement
around enemy's formations in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
🚩
Last week, as a result of active and decisive actions, Russian troops took under control
Lukashovo, Lobanovka, Reznikovo, Kupino, Nefedovka
, and
Chernoye
. Yesterday,
Varvarovka
and
Nikolayevka
(Kharkov region) were taken under controlled. All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
💥
In Kharkov and Sumy regions
, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of six mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, two airmobile brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, four territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,675 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 82 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare stations.
↗️
During the week, the Zapad Group of Forces have taken more advantageous lines
and positions. Russian units inflicted losses on formations of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,550 troops, 18 armoured fighting vehicles, 94 motor vehicles, 12 field artillery guns, and 11 electronic warfare stations.
↗️
Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Russian units inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, a mountain assault brigade, an airmobile brigade, an unmanned systems brigade, a marine brigade, and territorial defence brigade.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 1,155 troops, nine armoured fighting vehicles, 198 motor vehicles, 13 field artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces.
