The Tsentr Group of Forces inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an airborne brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Starovarvarovka, Verovka, Belozerskoye, Svyatogorovka, Veselaya Gora, Shilovka, Novoyavlenka (Donetsk People's Republic), and Krutoyarovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▫️
Near Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army advanced in the central and northern-western parts of the city. Assault detachments of marines units continued the offensive in the western part of the settlement.
📊
Over the past 24 hours, 142 buildings have been liberated, up to 25 servicemen have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 350 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, five armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence
. Manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, two air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two marine brigades have been hit close to Tarasovka, Kolomiytsy, Chernenkovo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Rayskoye, Mirolyubovka, Barvinovka, Omelnik, and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 265 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions
. Russian troops hit formations of two mechanised brigades and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Orekhov and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 18 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on drone manufacturing shops, storage areas of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations in 146 areas.
