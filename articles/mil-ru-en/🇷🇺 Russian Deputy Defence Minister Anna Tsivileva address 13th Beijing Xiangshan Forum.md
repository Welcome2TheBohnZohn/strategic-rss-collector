# 🇷🇺 Russian Deputy Defence Minister Anna Tsivileva address 13th Beijing Xiangshan Forum

Published: 2026-09-16 11:20:01

Russian Deputy Defence Minister Anna Tsivileva address 13th Beijing Xiangshan Forum
State Secretary – Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Anna Tsivileva took part in the 13th Beijing Xiangshan Forum.
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
The relationship between Russia and China is at an unprecedentedly high level and meets the key interests of the peoples of both states.
State Secretary - Deputy Russian Defence Minister Anna Tsivileva said that during the forum.
'Today, this respected discussion platform has gained particular importance.
The Forum has become the most important centre of attraction for those seeking an objective view of global processes and striving for dialogue
'.
🤝
Anna Tsivileva noted that
the Russian-Chinese comprehensive partnership and strategic interaction are dynamically developing
in all spheres, including in the field of military cooperation, based on the principles of equality, reciprocal support in key issues, mutual understanding and consideration of each other's interests. The Deputy Defence Minister of the Russian Federation expressed confidence that
such principles should be the base of a multipolar world order,
which, according to her, has already become a reality.
'
The multipolar world order
creates the conditions for strengthening the global security architecture and guaranteeing strategic stability – a key factor that will ensure development and prosperity in the world'.
💬
The interaction between the military agencies
of different countries should be aimed at creating conditions for the preservation of global and regional stability. Russia-China security cooperation in this context is important, Anna Tsivileva said.
▫️
Anna Tsivileva also noted that
the sphere of humanitarian cooperation and assistance to states affected by natural disasters or epidemics offer great opportunities for building confidence among all countries.
‘Russia is always ready to come to the aid of any country affected by natural disasters, catastrophes or epidemics’.
❗️
The Deputy Defence Minister stressed that
the priority must be given to dialogue and interaction at the level of all departments,
including defence agencies.
‘Russia has always been in favour of peaceful coexistence and the search for compromises. We are proposing to establish a just world order that takes into account the interests of all nations, and a dialogue between countries based on mutual respect and equality’.
