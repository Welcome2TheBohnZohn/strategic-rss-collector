# ▫️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-17 10:08:14

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30649

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
▫️
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades and an assault regiment of the AFU close to Podsredneye, Novoselovka, and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
the Group's units inflicted losses on formations of two motorised infantry brigades, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and a national guard close to Nikolayevka, Bititsa, and Krasnopolye (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 175 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 16 motor vehicles, two electronic warfare stations, and one counter-fire radar station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian units engaged manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Monachinovka, Izyum, Novonikolayevka, and Petrovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 230 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 24 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted damage on formations of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, and two assault regiments of the AFU near Krasny Molochar, Druzhkovka, and Nikolayevka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 180 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 26 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
💥
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out intensive offensive operations.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade, an airborne brigade, an air assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, three national guard brigades, and a special forces brigade close to Novopavlovka, Krivorozhye, Sergeyevka, Novokrivorozhye, Sofiyevka, Svyatogorovka, Mikhailovka (Donetsk People's Republic), and Novopodgorodnoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 340 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and four electronic warfare stations.
