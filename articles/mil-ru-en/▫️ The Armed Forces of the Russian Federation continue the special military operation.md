# ▫️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-20 09:31:33

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29519

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
▫️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region
tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone
.
💥
In the encirclement, strikes were delivered at enemy manpower
close to Varvarovka, Grachevka, Reznikovo, Khizhnyakovo, and Khripuny (Kharkov region).
The fighting is underway for the liberation of Buzovo
(Kharkov region).
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, more than 30 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, one motor vehicle, and one unmanned ground vehicle have been neutralised.
🔥
Active combat operations are underway to expand the external control zone
near Novoaleksandrovka, Petropavlovka, Polnaya, and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
📍
Moreover,
in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an artillery brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades close to Artemovka, Berezhnoye, Doroshenkovo, Martynovka, Novoselovka, and Podsredneye, Revolyutsionnoye, and Sosnovka (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region
, three territorial defence brigades have been neutralised near Volnaya Sloboda, Studenok, and Sumy.
▪️
In total, up to 195 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Formations of four mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigades have been hit near Vyssheye Solenoye, Grushevka, Maleyevka, Monachinovka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Karpovka, Lozovoye, Mayaki, Starodubovka, and Yatskovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 230 troops, two U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carriers, one Kazak armoured vehicle, 15 pickup trucks, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a national guard brigade were hit near Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Vysokoivanovka, Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Krasny Molochar, Nikolayevka, Novoselovka, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 195 troops, three Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, 26 motor vehicles, and three artillery guns.
