# ▫️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-03 10:06:24

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29798

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
▫️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces tighten the encirclement around the AFU formations in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
💥
Fights are underway in the encirclement for the liberation of Varvarovka, Lobanovka, and Reznikovo (Kharkov region). Moreover, enemy formations are being destroyed near Nikolayevka, Nefedovka, Kupino, and Chernoye in Kharkov region.
▪️
In the encirclement, the enemy lost up to 35 troops and an armoured fighting vehicle during the day.
👉
While expanding the external control zone, Russian troops are operating to liberate Prikolotnoye and Sosnovy Bor in Kharkov region
. Strikes are delivered at enemy strongholds in Khatneye and Fedorovka (Kharkov region).
🚩
Moreover, as a result of resolute offensive actions
, Russian troops took control over Novaya Kazachaya in Kharkov region.
💥
Strikes were delivered
at manpower and materiel of a mechanised brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a national guard brigade near Slatino, Sosnovka, Velikiy Burluk, Metallovka, Russkiye Tishki, and Bezruki (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at formations of two airmobile brigades, two mechanised brigades of the AFU, two territorial defence brigades, and one national guard regiment near Doroshovka, Rudnevka, Khoten, Ivolzhanskoye, Nikolskoye, Bititsa, and Lipnyak (Sumy region).
▪️
In total, the enemy lost over 250 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 19 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and a territorial defence brigade near Sobolevka, Chervony Oskol, Grushevka, and Andreyevka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and 14 motor vehicles.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Formations of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigades have been hit near Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy has lost more than 195 troops, 18 motor vehicles, and a Polish-made 155-mm Krab self-propelled artillery system.
