# ▫️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-28 10:48:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29667

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
▫️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces tighten the encirclement around the AFU formations in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
💥
Inside the encirclement, there are battles for the settlements of Nesternoye, Lukashovo, Grachevka, and Rublenoye
in Kharkov region. Russian elements also neutralise enemy units near Varvarovka, Chernoye, Nefedovka, and Kupino (Kharkov region).
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, the enemy lost up to 60 troops, two ATVs, and one unmanned ground vehicle.
📍
While expanding the external control zone, Russian troops continue combat actions for Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
🔥
In addition,
in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of three heavy mechanised brigades, two mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, one territorial defence brigade, and one national guard brigade close to Slatino, Liptsy, Pitomnik, Tsupovka, Zhukov Yar, Velikiy Burluk, Mikhaylovka, Kutuzovka, and Shestakovo (Kharkov region).
🚩
In Sumy region, as a result of decisive actions of the Group's units established control over Ulanovo (Sumy region).
💥
Strikes were delivered
at enemy manpower and UAV command posts of two mechanised brigades and an airmobile brigade of the AFU close to Kiyanitsa, Khoten (Sumy region), and Sumy.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 285 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 21 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU, one marine brigade, one territorial defence brigade, one national guard brigade close to Volchiy Yar, Bogorodichnoye, Sidorovo, Donetskoye, Lozovoye, Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic), Kovalevka, Osinovo, Grushevka, Maleyevka, Chervony Oskol, and Shevchenkovo (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to over 210 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of three mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades of the AFU, and one marine brigade were hit close to Vysokoivanovka, Druzhkovka, Slavyansk, Nikolayevka, Kramatorsk, Chervonozoryanoye, Krasny Molochar, and Nikonorovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 190 troops, one U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carrier, four armoured fighting vehicles, 23 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one Grad MLRS vehicle.
