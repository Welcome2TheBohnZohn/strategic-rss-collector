# ▫️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-10 10:33:10

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29955

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
▫️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone.
🚩
In the encirclement, as a result of active combat operations, the settlements of Lukashovo and Lobanovka (Kharkov region) have been brought under control.
🔥
Battles are underway for the liberation of
Nikolayevka, Varvarovka, Reznikovo, Nefedovka and Chernoye (Kharkov region).
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, the enemy lost up to 45 troops and three ground robotic systems in the encirclement.
💥
While extending the external control zone, there is active fighting for the settlements of Prikolotnoye and Kotovka (Kharkov region).
🔥
In addition,
in Kharkov region,
Russian troops
hit
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two motorised infantry brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one national guard brigade near Slatino, Berezovka, Zamulovka, Martovoye, Vodyanoye, Tsirkuny, Cherkasskaya Lozovaya, and Russkaya Lozovaya.
📍
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of three mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Studenok, Ivolzhanskoye, Yastrebinoye, Topolya, Stetskovka, and Osoyevka.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost up to 270 troops, 11 motor vehicles, and one Akatsiya self-propelled artillery system in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Zapad Group's units improved the situation along the front line and inflicted losses on
the manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Glubokaya Makatikha, Yatskovka, and Karpovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 220 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, three artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on formations of one mechanised brigade, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigades near Krasny Molochar, Kramatorsk, Belenkoye, Slavyansk, and Semenovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 150 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 28 motor vehicles, and five field artillery guns.
