# ❗️ From 21 to 27 November, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two massive and 12 group strikes, which hit

Published: 2026-11-27 11:20:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30807

❗️
From 21 to 27 November, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two massive and 12 group strikes, which hit
Ukrainian defence industry enterprises used to produce cruise missiles, missile fuel, long- and medium-range UAVs, logistics centres, warehouses, power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the AFU, production workshops and storage areas of robotic systems, including UAVs, military airfields, ammunition depots, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition,
Ukrainian port infrastructure facilities
used to receive, store, and distribute military cargo
have been disabled.
🔥
During the week,
14 dry cargo ships delivering military hardware and dual-use cargo to Ukraine to support the AFU
have been neutralised.
🚩
During the week, as a result of intensive offensive operations, units of the Sever Group of Forces established control over the settlement of Sosnovy Bor (Kharkov region).
💥
Formations of five mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, four territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade
have been hit.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,125 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 56 motor vehicles, and 25 electronic warfare and counter-fire stations.
↗️
During the week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces have taken advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements delivered strikes at manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,490 troops, two tanks, five armoured fighting vehicles, 72 motor vehicles, six field artillery guns, and five electronic warfare stations.
📍
Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades, one mountain assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade.
▪️
In total, in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,145 troops, 17 armoured fighting vehicles, 132 motor vehicles, four field artillery guns, and four electronic warfare stations.
