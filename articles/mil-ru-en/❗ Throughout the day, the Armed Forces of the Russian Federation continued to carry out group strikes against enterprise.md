# ❗ Throughout the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out group strikes against enterprises of Ukrainian defence industry

Published: 2026-08-29 17:00:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29109

❗
Throughout the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to carry out group strikes against enterprises of Ukrainian defence industry
and seaports used to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
The following targets
were hit
by strike unmanned aerial vehicles:
📍
in Kiev, an assembly facility of the shipbuilding industry engaged in the assembly and maintenance of remotely controlled boats;
📍
in the port of Nikolayev, a maritime transhipment terminal and four storage facilities containing military equipment intended to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
In addition,
an electrical substation in Trikhaty of Nikolayev region, which provides power to the port of Nikolayev, was hit
.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
