# 💬 We block roads, cut off reserves A storming soldier with the call sign 'Dobro' told about the liberation of Vasilkovoye

Published: 2026-11-10 14:01:25

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30531

We block roads, cut off reserves
💬
A storming soldier with the call sign 'Dobro'
told about the liberation of Vasilkovoye
in Zaporozhye region.
▫️
According to the servicemember, assault groups moved from different directions through a treeline,
moving under drone fire
on the way to well-built positions on the highgrounds.
👊
After seizing the village, the
servicemen mopped up all enemy's foxholes
and held the lines till the consolidation group arrived.
🚩
The liberation of Vasilkovoye
made it possible to block supply routes and the approach of AFU reserves.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
