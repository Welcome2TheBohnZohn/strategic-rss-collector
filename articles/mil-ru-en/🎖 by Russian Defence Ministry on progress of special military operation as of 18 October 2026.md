↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Gavrilovka, Pavlovka, Pokrovskoye, Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasinovka, Zalivnoye and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU has lost more than 195 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, one pick-up truck, one Grad MLRS fighting vehicle, and one artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Formations of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been neutralised near Orekhov, Kamyshevakha, and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 14 motor vehicles have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
have inflicted damage on
transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, a military hardware depot, assembly workshops, long-range UAVs storage and launch sites, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 151 areas.

seven Flamingo long-range cruise missiles and 1,256 fixed-wing UAVs.
