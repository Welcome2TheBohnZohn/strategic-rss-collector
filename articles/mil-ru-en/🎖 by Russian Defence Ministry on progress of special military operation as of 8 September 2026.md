📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades near Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Veseloye, Shilovka, Novotroitskoye, Dobropolye, Novogrishino (Donetsk People's Republic), and Novopavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
Up to 380 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade, three air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade close to Mechetnoye, Ivanovka, Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Zelenaya Dibrova, Nikolskoye, Chervony Yar, and Omelnik (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 300 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and five motor vehicles.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Orekhov, Yurkovka, Malokaterinovka, Yulyevka, Novoyakovlevka, and Preobrazhenka in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
More than 45 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 14 motor vehicles of the AFU were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
the transport and logistics infrastructure of Ukraine used by the AFU, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 148 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
12 guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 713 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
