Malaysia is over stern: Pacific Fleet's ship detachment completes visit to Butterworth
A ship detachment of the Pacific Fleet comprising the frigate Marshal Shaposhnikov, the large anti-submarine ship Admiral Tributs, and the tanker Pechenga have completed a business call at the Malaysian port of Butterworth.
🤝
The command of the detachment
met with representatives of the Malaysian Navy and the Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of Russia in Malaysia
Nail Latypov.
▫️
On the island of Jerejak,
Russian sailors paid tribute to the crew of the cruiser Zhemchug,
laying flowers and holding a memorial service. They also
took part in cultural events
and
sporting competitions.
The Pacific Fleet ship detachment left Vladivostok on 10 September to perform tasks in the Pacific Rim.
Previously
, the crews made a business call at the port of the city of Jakarta of the Republic of Indonesia.
