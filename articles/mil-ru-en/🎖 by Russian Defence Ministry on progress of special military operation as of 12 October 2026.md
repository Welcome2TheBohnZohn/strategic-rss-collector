🚩
As a result of active actions, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Lidino (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Russian troops engaged
manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one air assault brigade of the AFU and three national guard brigades near Starorayskoye, Novoandreyevka, Maryevka, Novoyavlenka, Svyatogorovka, Belozerskoye, Veselaya Gora (Donetsk People's Republic), Andronovka, and Tarasovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
⚡️
In Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army
advanced in the central and northern-western parts of the city.
Marine units
continued to mop up the western quarters of the city and the territory of the Dobropolskaya mine.
💥
The enemy's attempt to deblock the AFU's encircled units by troops of up to a platoon without armour was thwarted.
📊
Over the past 24 hours, 216 buildings,
including four production facilities,
have been liberated
and up to 35 Ukrainian servicemen have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 330 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, two electronic warfare stations, and one electronic reconnaissance station have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
🚩
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance to the depths of the enemy defence and liberated Nikolskoye in Zaporozhye region by resolute actions.
🔥
Manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two air assault brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade have been hit near Ivanovka, Podgavrilovka, Kolomiytsy, Orestopol, Skotovatoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasinovka, Ternovka, and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 275 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 12 motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one air assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit near Orekhov, Kushugum, Zapasnoye, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 40 troops, 19 armoured fighting vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on the transport infrastructure facilities of Ukraine used for the interests of the AFU, the production workshops of unmanned aerial vehicles and their launching sites, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 149 areas.

five guided aerial bombs and 646 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
