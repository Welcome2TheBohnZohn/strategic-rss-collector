# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles

Published: 2026-11-05 06:30:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30446

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles
against facilities of the Ukrainian energy industry, logistics centres, and vessels involved in the support of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following were hit:
📍
In Novy Korotych of Kharkov region
, a Novaya Pochta logistics terminal;
📍
in Dnepropetrovsk region
, a 330 kV power substation.
📍
Furthermore,
a dry cargo ship with military materiel was hit
in the Black Sea 52 km south-west of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
