# 🤝 Lending a helping wing In the course of the battles for Khizhnyakovo , the assault detachments of the 344th Motorised Rifle Regiment (6th Guards Combined Arms Army)

Published: 2026-09-30 13:30:40

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29719

Lending a helping wing
🤝
▫️
In the course of the battles for
Khizhnyakovo
, the assault detachments of the 344th Motorised Rifle Regiment (6th Guards Combined Arms Army)
acted under the watchful eye and support of UAV operators.
A UAV platoon elaborated on it:
'We detected hostile strongholds, hit them to make it easier for our assault detachments to get through. We guided them with the use of the 'birds', and helped them in all sorts of ways. We engaged the enemy by munitions, destroyed a stronghold so that they easily entered the area and mopped up the adversary in the settlement. There were reconnaissance units and Baba Yaga drones of the enemy, all of which were eliminated by our 'birds'. We were also backed by air defence systems. Our scouts, as well as our commander, monitor our units' movement and help to navigate in the area.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
