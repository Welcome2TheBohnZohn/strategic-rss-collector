# ➡️ Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov arrives on working visit to Cholpon-Ata in Kyrgyz Republic. There, meeting of Council of Ministers of Defence of the CIS will take place

Published: 2026-10-14 14:30:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30036

Russian
Defence Minister Andrei Belousov arrives on working visit to Cholpon-Ata in Kyrgyz Republic. There, meeting of Council of Ministers of Defence of the CIS will take place.
➡️
The event is to be visited by delegations
from Azerbaijan, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Russia, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan as well as representatives of Council of Ministers of Defence of the CIS Secretariat.
📑
It is planned to
discuss topical issues of the development of military cooperation
in the CIS as well as to agree on joint activities of the CIS armed forces in 2027.
🤝
On the sidelines of the event, the
Russian Defence Minister will have bilateral meetings with heads of delegations
of defence ministries of the CIS member states.
📍
Moreover, Andrei Belousov
will visit Russian military bases located in the Republic.
The CIS Defence Ministers Council meets in follow-up to the agreements reached within the framework of the CIS Summit with the participation of President of the Russian Federation Vladimir Putin.
