# ❗️ From 5 to 11 September, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out one massive and six group strikes with high-precision weapons and long-range unmanned aerial vehic

Published: 2026-09-11 10:25:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29334

❗️
From 5 to 11 September, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out one massive and six group strikes with high-precision weapons and long-range unmanned aerial vehicles,
which hit enterprises of defence industry of Ukraine, logistics and distribution centres of military cargo, fuel, power, and transport infrastructure facilities, a DATA-centre used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, drone manufacturing workshops and warehouses, ammunition, fuel, and materiel depots, radio and technical reconnaissance centres, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
🔥
In addition, a number of the Ukrainian ports infrastructure facilities,
which provided reception and storage of military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants used in the interests of the Ukrainian Armed Forces,
have been disabled.
🔥
During the week, 13 dry cargo ships, two tankers, two tugboats, and two boats operating in the interests of the AFU were hit.
💥
Last week, units of the Sever Group of Forces
continued to tighten the inner perimeter of the encirclement and expand the outer one around the enemy's formations in Kharkov region.
🚩
As a result of decisive actions,
control has been established over the settlements of Berezniki, Ozernoye, Dolgenkoye, and Zarubinka (Kharkov region).
💥
Two enemy battalions were blocked near Olkhovatka (Kharkov region). All attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
▪️
Over the past week, more than 180 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, five motor vehicles, 11 ATVs, and eight ground robotic systems were neutralised.
🚩
In addition, during active assault actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces
took control over the settlement of Goptovka (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy and Kharkov regions, fire damage was inflicted on manpower and hardware of
five mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, two airmobile brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, four territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 1,430 troops, 12 armoured fighting vehicles, 83 motor vehicles, five field artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare and counter-fire radar stations in the Sever Group's area of responsibility.
During the week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces took more advantageous tactical position.
Strikes were delivered at formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The enemy's losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,395 troops, 10 armoured fighting vehicles, 108 motor vehicles, 12 field artillery guns, and seven electronic warfare and counter-fire radar stations.
🚩
Last week, as a result of active actions, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces
liberated Kurtovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of
six mechanised brigades, three airmobile brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one air assault brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,330 troops, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, 127 motor vehicles, and 11 field artillery guns.
🚩
Yesterday, on 10 September, as a result of active offensive actions, the Tsentr Group's units
liberated the settlement of Novogrishino (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
During the week, strikes were delivered at
formations of five mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one jaeger brigade, one unmanned systems brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, two marine brigades, and four national guard brigades.
▪️
The enemy's losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,820 troops, 20 armoured fighting vehicles, 64 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, 10 electronic warfare stations, and a counter-fire radar system.
