# ▫️ On Land, in Sky and at Sea: Indra 2026 Exercise starts The Indra 2026 joint Russian-Indian inter-service exercise

Published: 2026-10-14 17:50:25

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30042

▫️
On Land, in Sky and at Sea: Indra 2026 Exercise starts
➡️
The Indra 2026 joint Russian-Indian inter-service exercise
has begun in Chelyabinsk region and the Russian Far East. The exercise involves ground force units from both countries, Russian Navy and Indian Navy warships as well as aircraft operated by the Russian Aerospace Forces.
▫️
The land phase of the exercise will run until 23 October
at a Central MD training ground in Chelyabinsk region. Servicemen of the 90th Guards Tank Division (Central MD) and their Indian counterparts will
practise command and control and neutralising illegal armed formations.
⚓️
The maritime phase will take place in the Sea of Japan
on 16 and 17 October. Warships operating as part of a joint task force will practise tactical manoeuvring and conduct naval gunfire exercises. They will also carry out a boarding operation, liberate a vessel seized by a mock enemy, and repel attacks of unmanned aerial vehicles and uncrewed surface vessels.
✈️
Russian Aerospace Forces and Indian Air Force crews flying Su-30SM aircraft will conduct manoeuvring dog fights and practise engaging small low-altitude targets
as well as aircraft carrying air-launched weapons. Mi-8 helicopter crews will deploy special forces and conduct search-and-rescue operations in hard-to-reach areas.
