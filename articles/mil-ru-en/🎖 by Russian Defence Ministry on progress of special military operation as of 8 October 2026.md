The Tsentr Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of
three mechanised brigades of the AFU, one marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Stepanovka, Belozerskoye, Sergeyevka, Roganskoye, Novoyavlenka, and Kamyshevakha (Donetsk People's Republic).
↗️
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army were advancing in central and northern parts of the city.
Marine units continued the offensive in the south-western direction. They've established control over the sports centre, mopped up the stadium and the remaining part of the park area. Over the past 24 hours, 103 buildings have been liberated and more than 25 troops have been neutralised.
▪️
Ukrainian armed formations sustained losses of up to 535 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one radar station in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Orestopol, Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Krinovka, Mirovka, and Zalivnoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 295 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, nine motor vehicles, and two artillery systems.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Orekhov and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 30 troops, 20 motor vehicles, a U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian groups have inflicted fire damage on
production workshops of UAVs and their depots, artillery and missile ammunition depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 1,062 fixed-wing UAVs.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet destroyed one uncrewed surface vehicle of the Ukrainian Navy in the Black Sea waters.
