Tsentr's strike force: marines share details of the liberation of Dobropolye
👊
During the liberation of the city, fighters of the 61st Marine Brigade and the 177th Marine Regiment of the Tsentr Group of Forces
employed infiltration tactics and building-up assault groups to create a strike fist.
✂️
The marines began
cutting off the enemy's supply routes
around Dobropolye
back in May, before forward detachments entered the city.
Fire teams blocked the delivery of ammunition and reserves of the AFU
from the neighbouring settlements of Svyatogorovka, Annovka and Belozyorskoye.
🔥
The infantry's advance was supported daily by artillerymen and drone operators. They
fired on enemy positions and fortifications
both in Dobropolye itself and on its outskirts.
