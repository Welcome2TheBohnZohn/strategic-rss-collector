Enemy can't catch a break: our lads are working beyond Krasny Liman, with UAVs reaching as far as Slavyansk
Our UAV operators follow the enemy beyond Krasny Liman. The Seversky Donets is under our control, and our drones are reaching as far as Slavyansk. This was reported by the Zapad Group's commander of a Molniya-2 strike UAV team of the 25th Combined Arms Army with call sign Druzhba.
'Two-storey, brick-built, solid houses that the enemy often uses for cover — they come down without any bother,' the serviceman noted.
