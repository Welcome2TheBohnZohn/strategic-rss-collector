# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-14 09:50:22

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30605

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Dergachi, Lyutovka, and Martovoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
units of one AFU mechanised brigade and two territorial defence brigades have been neutralised near Bititsa, Vakalovshchina, and Radkovka (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 175 troops, five motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two AFU mechanised brigades and one territorial defence brigade close to Prosyanka (Kharkov region), Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic), and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 200 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 17 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Losses were inflicted on formations of four mechanised brigades and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Novoselovka, Slavyansk, and Cherevkovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 130 troops, 16 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
⏩
Units of the Tsentr Group of Forces continue to carry out active offensive operations.
Russian troops hit manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one national guard brigade close to Kuritsyno, Novoiverskoye, Ocheretino, and Sergeyevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 335 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, one self-propelled artillery system, and two electronic warfare stations.
🚩
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Tersyanka in Zaporozhye region as a result of active actions.
💥
Manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one air assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit near Podgavrilovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novorozovka, and Odarovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 215 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, one artillery gun, and five motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted losses on the formations of one AFU mechanised brigade and one territorial defence brigade near Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region) and Zaporozhye.
▪️
Up to 30 Ukrainian troops, one tank, one armoured fighting vehicle, 15 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️

on the power and transport infrastructure used for the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, unmanned aerial vehicles, their storage and launch sites, ammunition depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of the Ukrainian Armed Forces in 142 areas.
