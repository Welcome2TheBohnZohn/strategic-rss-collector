# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-19 09:59:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30680

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
In Kharkov region, strikes have been delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades and two assault regiments of the AFU close to Katerinovka, Podsredneye, Malinovka, Ploskoye, Chuguyev, and Turovo.
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of two mechanised and one airmobile brigades of the AFU
have been neutralised
close to Yastrebinoye, Krovnoye, Vakalovshchina, and Vyazovoye.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 110 troops and seven motor vehicles.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian elements have inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Peski-Radkovskiye, Studenok, Monachinovka, Smorodkovka, and Nechvolodovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to over 210 troops, one field artillery gun, and 16 motor vehicles.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes have been delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades and one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one national guard brigade near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhovka, Nikolayevka, and Toretskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 180 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, one artillery gun, and 28 motor vehicles.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out intensive offensive operations.
Russian units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Verovka, Samarskoye, Kuritsino, Novokrivorozhye, Kalinino, Starorayskoye, Kamenka, and Belozerskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU formations have lost more than 340 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
The Group's elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one air assault brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU near Novotroitskoye (Zaporozhye region), Podgavrilovka, Kolomiytsy, Ivanovka, and Kirichkovo (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 210 troops and five motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU have been hit close to Kirovo, Kamyshevakha, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 15 troops and 17 motor vehicles have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered strikes at
power and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, military airfields, production and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and ground robotic systems, ammunition depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed forces in 148 areas.

eight Neptune long-range guided missiles and 268 fixed-wing UAVs.
