# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-21 10:45:26

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30189

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade and one motorised infantry brigade of the AFU near Kurgannoye, Izbitskoye, Bugayevka, and Velikiy Burluk (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region, strikes were delivered at
formations of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and the territorial defence brigade close to Pustogorod, Khoten, and Gnilitsa.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 230 troops, one tank, 15 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Maliyevka, Andreyevka, Blagodatovka, Monachinovka, Nechvolodovka, Tamarganovka, Osinovo, Pleskachovka (Kharkov region) and Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 210 troops, three Western-made armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, one air assault brigade, one motorised infantry brigade, one airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near the settlements of Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, and Vasilevskaya Pustosh (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 160 troops, one Italian-made Puma armoured vehicle, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, and 30 motor vehicles.
↗️
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and two national guard brigades have been hit near Krinitsa, Kuritsino, Roganskoye, Belozerskoye, Shilovka, Novoyavlenka, Starovarvarovka (Donetsk People's Republic) and Novopavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 335 troops, one U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carrier, six armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations.
