# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-31 10:12:47

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30373

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
In Kharkov region, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one motorised infantry brigade, one unmanned systems brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Duvanka, Velikiye Prokhody, Tsupovka, Martovoye, and Velikiy Burluk (Kharkov region).
💥
In addition,
units of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades have been hit near Khoten, Velikaya Rybitsa, Osoyevka, Gnilitsa (Sumy region) and Sumy.
▪️
The enemy lost more than 270 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and eight motor vehicles.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades close to Monachinovka, Smorodkovka, Glushchenkovo, Studenok (Kharkov region), and Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 210 servicemen, an armoured fighting vehicle, 21 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and four electronic warfare stations.
📍
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade of the AFU and one marine brigade near Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Druzkovka, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Semenovka, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 190 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, two armoured fighting vehicles, 20 motor vehicles, and one self-propelled artillery gun.
⏩
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, and four national guard brigades close to Belozerskoye, Shilovka, Andreyevka, Roganskoye, Starovarvarovka (Donetsk People's Republic), Novopavlovka, and Mezhevaya (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 340 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
