# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-24 10:31:38

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30254

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade close to Karasevka, Veseloye, Khatneye, Stary Saltov, Velikiy Burluk, Shevchenko, and Martovoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at formations of three mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and three territorial defence brigades close to Luzhki, Sennoye, Vakalovshchina, Tokari, and Ivolzhanskoye (Sumy region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 200 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, six motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Russian units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade near Mayaki, Donetskoye, Ilyochovka, Bogorodichnoye (Donetsk People's Republic), and Nechvolodovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 215 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and 17 motor vehicles.
↗️
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation
. Formations of three mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a motorised infantry brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade were hit near Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Slavyansk, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Nikolayevka, and Kondratovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 180 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 34 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade, a jaeger brigade, an airborne brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Starorayskoye, Belozerskoye, Shilovka, Starovarvarovka, Sergeyevka, Roganskoye, Novoyavlenka, Svyatogorovka, Blagodat, and Novodonetskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 350 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
