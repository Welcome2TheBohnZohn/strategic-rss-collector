# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-22 10:48:43

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29553

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone in Kharkov region.
💥
In the encirclement,
enemy manpower and equipment were defeated close to Krasny Yar, Potikhonovo, Buzovo, Reznikovo, Khripuny, Varvarovka, and Grachovka (Kharkov region). All attempts to break out of the encirclement have been thwarted.
▪️
Up to 40 troops and one armoured fighting vehicle have been neutralised.
↗️
Active combat operations are underway to expand the external control zone
near Podsredneye, Novoselovka, Petropavlovka, and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware
of four mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Staritsa, Svetlichnoye, Izbitskoye, Liptsy, Mikhaylovka, Kirillovka, and Veliky Burluk (Kharkov region).
🔥
In Sumy region,
manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades have been hit close to Khoten, Stetskovka (Sumy region) and Sumy.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 215 servicemen, an armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, a field artillery gun, and an electronic warfare station in the Sever Group 's area of responsibility.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the AFU, and a national guard brigade near Chervony Oskol, Putnikovo, Blagodatovka, Osinovo (Kharkov region), Lozovoye, and Volchy Yar (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 200 troops, one tank, 13 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian troops engaged formations of two mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade close to Bylbasovka, Vysokoivanovka, Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, Vasilyevskaya Pustosh, Nikolayevka, and Klinovoye in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The enemy lost up to 170 troops, one German-made Marder infantry fighting vehicle, two U.S.-made HMMWV armoured fighting vehicles, 19 motor vehicles, and three field artillery guns.
↗️
As a result of resolute actions, the Tsentr Group's units completed encirclement of a large AFU centre of resistance in Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic).
Units of the two national guard brigades and the Skala Assault Regiment were encircled. Over the past 24 hours, up to 50 servicemen have been neutralised. All attempts to break out of the enemy environment have been thwarted.
💥
In addition, Russian elements hit
manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, a unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades close to Belozerskoye, Starorayskoye, Andreyevka, Gornyak, Yakovlevka, Novovodyanoye, Pervomayskoye, Svyatogorovka, and Novogrigorovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
In total, more than 420 troops, an armoured personnel carrier, five armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and five electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
