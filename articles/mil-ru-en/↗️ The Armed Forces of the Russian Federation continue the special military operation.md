# ↗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-18 11:45:02

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30137

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces took advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one border detachment of the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine have been hit near Tomakhovka, Vysheye, Martovoye, Velikiy Burluk, Izbitskoye, and Kurgannoye (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two territorial defence brigades
have been hit
near Gniliitsa, Sennoye, Sukhodol, and Pustogorod.
▪️
The enemy has lost more than 195 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 17 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, two assault brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigade near Maliyevka (Kharkov region), Donetskoye, Volchiy Yar, Strodubovka, and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 240 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 20 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
↗️
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted damage on formations of four mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, two air-mobile brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades near Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Semyonovka, Orekhovatka, Druzhkovka, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 175 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 35 motor vehicles.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one airborne brigade, one unmanned systems assault brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, five national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Brigade near Novopavlovka, Raypole, Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novoyavlenka, Roganskoye, Shilovka, Krivorozhye, Starovarvarovka, Maryevka, Svyatogorovka, and Malotaranovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 350 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, one Grad MLRS, one field artillery gun, and two electronic warfare stations.
