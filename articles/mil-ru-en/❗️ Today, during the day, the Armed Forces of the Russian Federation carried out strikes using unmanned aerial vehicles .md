Today, during the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against facilities of the port and railway infrastructure, as well as against vessels used to support of the AFU.
As a result of the strike, the following targets
were hit
:
▪️
a dry cargo ship
at sea, carrying military cargo to the port of Odessa;
▪️
a railway locomotive
in the area of the Krivoy Rog railway station, used for transporting cargo used for the AFU.
▪️
In addition,
three PS‑330 power substations were damaged
in the cities of Rovno and Grabov in Rovno region, Kamenskoye in Dnepropetrovsk region, as well as
one PS‑110 power substation
in the city of Chernovtsy in Chernovtsy region. These substations ensure the distribution of electricity for the operation of enterprises of the Ukraine’s defence industry and logistics centres used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
