# 🔹 Moscow and Beijing get aligned Russian Deputy Defence Minister Vasiliy Osmakov took part in the first round of strategic consultations

Published: 2026-11-26 14:49:58

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30795

Moscow and Beijing
get aligned
Russian Deputy Defence Minister Vasiliy Osmakov
took part in the first round of strategic consultations
between the defence departments of Russia and China.
Under the coordinating participation of the Main Directorate for International Military Cooperation of the Russian Defence Ministry,
issues of mutual interest concerning the development of the situation in different regions of the world were discussed.
As a result of the consultations, the parties
stressed the importance and relevance of this dialogue mechanism
and agreed to regularise it.
According to the event's participants,
the talks were productive and took place in a warm and friendly atmosphere.
