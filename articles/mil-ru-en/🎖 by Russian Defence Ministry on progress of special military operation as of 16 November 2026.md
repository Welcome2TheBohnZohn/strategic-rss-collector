↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade have been hit near Demurino, Dibrova, Vasilkovskoye, Sidorenko, Pokrovskoye in Dnepropetrovsk region, Novotroitskoye, Novorozovka, and Odarovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 230 troops and nine motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Russian elements inflicted losses on units of a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade near Dimitrovo, Kamyshevakha, Chervony Zhovten, Natalovka, and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 15 troops and 19 motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on the energy and transport infrastructure used for the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, on the storage and launch sites of unmanned aerial vehicles as well as on temporary deployment points of the Ukrainian Armed Forces in 141 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, four Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 2,049 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
