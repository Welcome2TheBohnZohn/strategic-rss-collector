Grandmasters of the seas
Indra 2026

During the exercise, anti-terrorism units of the Russian Navy and the Indian Navy
liberated a ship captured by mock pirates.
The ships' crews also
carried out artillery fire and repelled attacks of uncrewed surface vehicles and UAVs
