# ⚓️ Perekop off coast of Seychelles The training ship of the Baltic Fleet Perekop arrived at the port of Victoria of the Republic of Seychelles

Published: 2026-09-26 14:11:32

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29637

Perekop off coast of Seychelles
⚓️
The training ship of the Baltic Fleet Perekop arrived at the port of Victoria of the Republic of Seychelles
for the first time as part of the maritime practice of officer cadets of Russian Navy schools.
▫️
The crew is going to enjoy cultural and sporting events as well as a visit the grave of the compatriot, senior ship's doctor Aleksandr Krupenin.
The navigational voyage of the Perekop training ship began in early July and will last until mid-December 2026. The ship will pass about 30 thousand nautical miles. The route comprises four oceans, 14 seas, 23 straits, and 9 bays.
