During the week, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces took advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, an airborne brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, three marine brigades, and five national guard brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,400 troops, 38 armoured fighting vehicles, 67 motor vehicles, five field artillery guns, and 13 electronic warfare stations.
🚩
Last week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance to depths of the enemy's defence and, as a result of resolute actions,
liberated the village of Obshcheye in Zaporozhye region.
💥
Russian troops
inflicted damage on
manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, three air assault brigades, three assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade.
▪️
In total, more than 1,390 troops, 12 armoured fighting vehicles, 42 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and three electronic and counter-fire stations were neutralised in the of the Vostok Group's area of responsibility.
📍
Over the past week, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units inflicted damage on formations of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Up to 205 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 65 motor vehicles, and 10 electronic warfare and counter-fire stations were neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
six guided aerial bombs, three air-to-surface cruise missiles, including two UK-made Storm Shadow missiles, and one French-made SCALP missile, six Neptune long-range cruise missiles, as well as 6,900 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
