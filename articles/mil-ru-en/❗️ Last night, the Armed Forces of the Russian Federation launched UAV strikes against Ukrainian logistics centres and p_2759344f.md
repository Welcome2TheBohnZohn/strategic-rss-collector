# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched UAV strikes against Ukrainian logistics centres and ports, vessels operating in the interests of the Armed Forces

Published: 2026-09-19 06:40:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29494

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
launched UAV strikes against Ukrainian logistics centres and ports, vessels operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, as well as transport infrastructure facilities.
The following targets were engaged:
📍
in Kiev region:
▫️
the Kopylov Logistik Park customs and logistics centre
in the settlement of Kopylov (6 km west of Kiev) used for storing and distributing military cargo arriving in Ukraine from European countries.
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
a pontoon bridge on the Dniester river
used for the transit of military cargo delivered to Ukraine from Romania and the port of Izmail;
▫️
a dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo in the port of Chernomorsk.
💥
In addition,
two dry cargo ships
delivering military supplies intended for the support of the AFU
were hit
on their way to the port of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
