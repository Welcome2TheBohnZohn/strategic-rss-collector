Military Union strengthening: Russian Deputy Defence Minister Vasily Osmakov holds a series of talks with the Mongolian Defence Ministry's leadership.
📑
Long-term
work
The meeting between Vasily Osmakov and his Mongolian counterpart, Major General Baasandamba Dashtseden, resulted in the signing of the fundamental document – the 2027–2031 Russian-Mongolian Strategic Partnership Plan in the Military Field.
🤝
All levels' sustainable interaction
A vector to strengthen ties was approved at the meeting between Vasily Osmakov and Mongolia's Defence Minister Dambyn Batlut. To date, a solid working relationship has been worked out between the countries: general staffs, frontier military districts, various branches of the armed forces, and various military higher schools are effectively cooperating.
🧭
Revised our priorities and determined common vector
In addition, Vasily Osmakov held talks with Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Mongolia Lieutenant General Sunrev Ganbyamba. The Russian side highly commended the accuracy of the April agreements implementation. Lieutenant General Sunrev Ganbyamba assured that Mongolian General Staff personally oversees the implementation of the new five-year plan.
