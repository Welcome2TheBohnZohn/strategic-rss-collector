# 🎖 by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation as of 25 November 2026

Published: 2026-11-25 10:32:39

🎖
by Russian Defence Ministry on progress of special military operation
as of 25 November 2026
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Kolodeznoye, Mikhaylovskoye, and Novoselovka in Kharkov region.
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of a mechanised brigade and an airmobile brigades of the AFU were neutralised near Vakalovshchina, Katerinovka, Korenek, Novaya Sloboda, Petrovskoye (Sumy region), and in the city of Sumy.
▪️
The enemy lost over 110 troops and three motor vehicles.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Vasilevka, Maleyevka, Monachinovka, Nechvolodovka, Osadkovka, Smorodkovka (Kharkov region), and Raygorodok (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 210 troops, one tank, one armoured fighting vehicle, five motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units hit formations of three mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Druzhkovka, Kramatorsk, Nikolayevka, and Slavyansk of the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU lost up to 150 troops and 13 motor vehicles.
↗️
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out intensive offensive operations.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades near Andreyevka, Belozherskoye, Verovka, Kuritsino, Lvovka, Mikhaylovka, Novoviktorovka, Novokrivorozhye, and Sergeyevka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 345 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and five motor vehicles.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian elements inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, an assault brigade, and two assault regiments of the AFU close to Gavrilovka, Pokrovskoye, Tsikovo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novoivanovka, Novonikolayevka, Novorozovka, and Schastlivoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU have lost up to 220 servicemen.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops hit formations of a mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Kamyshevakha and Kirovo of Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 15 troops and five motor vehicles of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation inflicted damage on
power and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, production, storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and ground robotic systems, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations, and foreign mercenaries in 149 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
863 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
