🚩
The units of the Vostok Group of Forces advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated the settlement of Obshcheye (Zaporozhye region).
🔥
Russian elements hit
manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Gavrilovka, Vsesvyatskoye, Pokrovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Volnyanka, and Vasilevskoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 210 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, seven motor vehicles, and one counter-fire station.
↗️
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit close to Orekhov, Dimitrovo, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 12 motor vehicles, and one counter-fire station have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
defence industry enterprises, power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the AFU, ammunition depots, production workshops, storage areas of ground robotic systems and long-range UAVs, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 151 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
two guided aerial bombs, two U.K.-made Storm Shadow air-to-surface cruise missiles, and 581 fixed-wing UAVs.
