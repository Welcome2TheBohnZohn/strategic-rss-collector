# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch group strikes

Published: 2026-09-10 06:25:17

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29308

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch group strikes
against the logistics centres and seaports of Ukraine, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of UAV strikes,
the following
targets were neutralised
:
📍
in the city of Nikolayev,
a depot of unmanned aerial vehicles
used to support the AFU;
📍
in the port of Chernomorsk of Odessa region,
the infrastructure of the distribution centre of the customs terminal
used for unloading and storing military cargo supplied by foreign countries.
🔥
In addition, at sea crossing to the port of Odessa,
two dry cargo ships delivering materiel
to the Armed Forces of Ukraine and
one tugboat
accompanying these ships
were hit.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
