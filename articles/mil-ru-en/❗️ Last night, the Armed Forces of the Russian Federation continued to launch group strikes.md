# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch group strikes

Published: 2026-09-09 07:52:33

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29292

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch group strikes
against military industry facilities, sea ports of Ukraine, and ships, as well as logistics centres operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
Ground-based and air-based
high-precision weapons and drones hit
:
📍
in Kiev:
▫️
a depot
of enterprise manufacturing uncrewed surface vehicles and fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as their components;
📍
in Nikolayev and Nikolayev region:
▫️
the Nika Terra cargo terminal
used for storage of fuel and lubricants and military equipment of the AFU (three fuel and lubricants tanks intended for supply of the AFU, and three military cargo depots), as well as
a UAV production workshop and storage depot
located on the site of the terminal;
▫️
the Metro logistics centre
, which is used to store military cargo and materiel intended to support the AFU;
▫️
the ATP STC logistics centre
used to store military cargo and materiel intended to support the AFU;
▫️
the
Novaya Pochta logistics centre
used to deliver, distribute and store military supplies and equipment intended to support the AFU;
▫️
the Nikolayev-2 logistics centre
used to store military cargo and materiel intended to support the AFU;
▫️
the Electrotekhnika logistics centre
used to store and distribute UAVs and their spare parts;
▫️
the Dimetra warehouse terminal
used to store AFU military materiel;
▫️
the Zhovtnevoye logistics depot
used to storage fuel, lubricants, and military materiel of the AFU (six fuel and lubricants tanks intended for supply of the AFU, and five depots with military supplies have been destroyed);
▫️
the Trikhaty power substation
, which operates the port of Nikolayev;
📍
in the port of Nikolayev – four tanks with fuel and lubricants and one depot with military equipment
intended to support the AFU.

:
▫️
at sea crossing to the port of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship

▫️
the logistics facilities
of the Starokazachye road crossing point used to deliver military supplies to Ukraine.
