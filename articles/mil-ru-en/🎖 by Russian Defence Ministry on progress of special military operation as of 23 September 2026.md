↗️
The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, four air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade near Velikomikhailovka, Gavrilovka, Shevchenko (Dnepropetrovsk region), Nikolay Pole, Obshcheye, Kirovo, Zalivnoye, Tarasovka, and Novokasyanovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 275 troops, a tank, an armoured fighting vehicle, and 11 motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
They engaged units of two mechanised brigades and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Orekhov, Kamyshevakha, Kushugum, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 Ukrainian troops and 20 motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian groups of forces inflicted losses on
logistics centres, fuel depots, energy facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, workshops producing unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 143 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems
shot down three guided aerial bombs and 868 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
💥
The Black Sea Fleet's aviation
destroyed three AFU uncrewed surface vehicles in the north-western part of the Black Sea.
