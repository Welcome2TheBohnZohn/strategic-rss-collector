# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike

Published: 2026-11-20 08:50:05

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30695

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike
with ground-, sea-, air-based high-precision weapons, unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry, warehouses, power facilities, transport and port infrastructure, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes,
the following targets were hit:
📍
in Kiev and Kiev region:
▪️
a manufacturing enterprise of explosives and powder (state powder metallurgy plant) in Brovary,
which produces components of solid missile fuel for FP-9 operational-tactical missiles;
▪️
a UAV assembly enterprise
that prepares fixed-wing UAVs;
▪️
road and rail bridges
used for the transit of AFU weapons, military and special hardware to the combat operations zone in Donbass;
▪️
the assembly plant (Fire Point LLC) in Petrovskoye,
which assembles, prepares for combat use, and delivers FP-7 Flamingo cruise missiles to launch sites;
📍
in Dnepropetrovsk region:
▪️
the PS-330 Terny-Pervomayskaya electric substation in Terny,
which distributes electricity to support the operation of defence industry enterprises and military facilities of the AFU in Dnepropetrovsk region;
📍
in Chernigov region:
▪️
the PS-110 electric substation in Mena,
which distributes electricity to ensure the operation of enterprises of the defence industry and military facilities of the AFU in Chernigov region;
📍
in Ivano-Frankovsk region:
▪️
the Burshtynskaya thermal power plant in the village of Burshtyn
, which produces electricity, including for the operation of enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry and military facilities of the AFU;
📍
in Lvov region:
▪️
the Lvov aviation industry repair facility (Lvov State Aviation Repair Plant)
, which produces FP-9 tactical missiles and FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles, as well as long- and medium-range unmanned aerial vehicles;
▪️
the PS-750 Zapadnoukrainskaya electric substation in Khodorov
, which distributes electricity to support the operation of the defence industry and military facilities of the AFU in Lvov region;
📍
in Nikolayev region:
▪️
a depot in Bereznegovatoye
, where cruise missiles are stored and used to hit civilian objects in the Russian Federation;
📍
in Odessa region:
▪️
the Zatoka road bridge
used for the transit of military goods by road and rail.
🔥
In addition, a dry cargo ship delivering military cargo
to the port of Odessa
at sea
, as well as
three railway locomotives near Dnepropetrovsk station, and one locomotive near Korosten station of Zhitomir region,
used to transport freight trains with military and dual-use cargo for the AFU, were hit.
