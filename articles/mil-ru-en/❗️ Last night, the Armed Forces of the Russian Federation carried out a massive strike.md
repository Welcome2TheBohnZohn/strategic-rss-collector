# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike

Published: 2026-10-27 07:00:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30303

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike
with ground-based high-precision weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry and transport infrastructure in Kiev, a logistics centre in Kharkov region, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result, the following targets were hit
📍
in Kiev:
▫️
an industrial enterprise
producing and storing long- and medium-range unmanned aerial vehicles;
▫️
the Oakline LLC unmanned aerial vehicle assembly workshop,
which produces and stores long- and medium-range FP-1 and FP-2 unmanned aerial vehicles of Fire Point company;
▫️
an unmanned aerial vehicle assembly workshop
producing and storing long- and medium-range fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles;
▫️
the Yuzhny road-rail bridge
used for the transit of military cargo by road and rail;
📍
in Kharkov region:
▫️
the unloading and customs terminal of the Regional Logistics Centre LLC
used to store and distribute materiel, dual-use cargo intended for supply of the AFU, as well as reconnaissance and attack UAVs.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo vessel
, delivering cargo to the port of Chernomorsk in Odessa region to support the AFU, was hit at sea.
