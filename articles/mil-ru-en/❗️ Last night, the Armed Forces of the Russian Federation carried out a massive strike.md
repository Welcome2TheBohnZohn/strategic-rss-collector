Published: 2026-11-17 06:40:21

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30644

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike
using high‑precision ground‑ and air‑based weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles.
💥

enterprises of Ukrainian military defence industry used in the production of cruise missiles and long‑ and medium‑range UAVs, transport infrastructure facilities in the cities of Kiev and Zaporozhye, in Cherkassy and Dnepropetrovsk regions, as well as energy facilities, port infrastructure, and ships used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine,
were hit.
