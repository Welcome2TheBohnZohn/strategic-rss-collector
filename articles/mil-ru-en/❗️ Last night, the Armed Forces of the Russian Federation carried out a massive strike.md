# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike

Published: 2026-10-30 07:03:33

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30353

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a massive strike
by ground-based high-precision weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry and energy facilities that support its operation, as well as port infrastructure facilities and ships used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
📍
in Kiev and Kiev region:
▫️
the missile unit and component plant (JSC Kiev Radio Plant, LLC Trimen-Ukraine),
which produces components for reconnaissance and attack UAVs, including long-range ones;
▫️
the unmanned aerial vehicle assembly and storage workshop (PJSC Fanplit),
which produces and stores FP-1, FP-2 long- and medium-range UAVs of the Fire Point company;
▫️
the PS-330 Novokievskaya power substation
in the settlement of Khotov, ensuring the operation of enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry in Kiev;
📍
in Dnepropetrovsk region:
▫️
the PS-330 Svetloye power substation in the settlement of Svetloye,
which ensures the operation of defence industry enterprises in the city of Dnepr;
▫️
in the Black Sea waters,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military materiel and dual-use cargo for the AFU to the port of Odessa.

Russian Defence Ministry
