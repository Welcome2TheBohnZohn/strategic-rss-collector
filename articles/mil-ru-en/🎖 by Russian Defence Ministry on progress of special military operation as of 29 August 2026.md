🚩
As a result of resolute actions, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Rubezhnoye and Novoandreyevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Manpower and hardware
of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades have been hit close to Belozerskoye, Veseloye Pole, Svyatogorovka, Zolotoy Kolodez, Sergeyevka, Dobropolye, Novogrigorovka (Donetsk People's Republic), and Novopavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
Up to 395 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and seven motor vehicles have been neutralised.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued advancing into the depth of enemy defence
. Russian troops hit manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade, an air assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade near Ivanovka, Vasilkovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Volnyanka, Nikolskoye, Obshcheye, Vasilyevskoye, Rozovka, and Zorevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 310 troops and six motor vehicles.
💥
The Dnepr Group of Forces
inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade and a UAV brigade of the AFU near Preobrazhenka, Orekhov (Zaporozhye region), and Antonovka (Kherson region).
▪️
Up to 50 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on the fuel and power infrastructure of Ukraine, logistics centres used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, assembly workshops, storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles, and their components as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 147 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
five guided aerial bombs, two HIMARS-made MLRS projectiles, eight Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 715 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
