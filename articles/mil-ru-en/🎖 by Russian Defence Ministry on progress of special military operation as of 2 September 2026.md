↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, three air assault brigades, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Vasilkovka, Kolomiytsy, Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novorozovka, Obshcheye, Mirovka, and Omelnik (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 325 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
🔥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades and a brigade of unmanned aerial vehicles of the AFU near the settlements of Vysokoye in Dnepropetrovsk region, Malokaterinovka and Orekhov in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 50 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 18 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations of the AFU were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
distribution and logistics centres, a military airfield, fuel, power, and transport infrastructure facilities of Ukraine, used in the interests of the AFU, production workshops and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 152 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four guided aerial bombs, two U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 464 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
