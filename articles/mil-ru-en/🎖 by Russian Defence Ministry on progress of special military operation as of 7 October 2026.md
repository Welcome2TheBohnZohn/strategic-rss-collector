The Tsentr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, a UAV brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, the Azov special brigade, and two National Guard brigades near Svyatogorovka, Belozerskoye, Sergeyevka, Stepanovka, Novoyavlenka, Roganskoye, Lidino, Novoandreyevka (Donetsk People's Republic), Petropavlovka, and Chuguyevo (Dnepropetrovsk region).
↗️
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army were advancing in central and northern parts of the city.
Marine units continued the offensive in the north-western direction along a railway. Control was established over the park area, culture house in the northern part of the settlement, and two production facilities on the territory of the Dobropolskaya mine
💥
An attempt by the 25th Airborne Brigade (AFU) to unblock an encircled enemy—with up to a platoon-sized force supported by armoured vehicles—has been repelled.
▪️
Over the past 24 hours, 82 buildings have been liberated, more than 25 Ukrainian troops, an armoured vehicle, and an pickup truck have been neutralised.
▪️
Ukrainian armed formations sustained losses of up to 520 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, 10 motor vehicles, a field artillery gun, four electronic warfare stations, and a radar in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, two air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade near Chernenkovo, Ivanovka, Gavrilovka, Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Volnyanka, Nikolskoye, and Listovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 255 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, 11 motor vehicles, and two 155-mm Paladin self-propelled artillery systems.
↗️
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on two mechanised brigades and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Orekhov, Kushugum, Kanevskoye, Yulyevka, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 30 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, 22 motor vehicles, and a U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian groups of forces have inflicted fire damage
on production workshops of unmanned aerial vehicles and their depots, ammunition and military materiel depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 146 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down four guided aerial bombs and 764 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
