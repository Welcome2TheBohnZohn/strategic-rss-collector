‘This Order will take up an honorary place on the brigade's combat banner’

The Vostok Group's 305th Guards Brigade was presented with the Order of Zhukov in the special military operation zone.
‘This is not just an award, it's the outcome of the efforts of every serviceman in our brigade, their dedication and their merit before the Fatherland’, said the deputy brigade commander for military-political work with the call sign ‘Tambov’.
