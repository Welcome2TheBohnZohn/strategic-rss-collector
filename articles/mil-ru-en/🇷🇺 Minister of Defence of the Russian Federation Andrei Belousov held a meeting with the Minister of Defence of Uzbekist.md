Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov held a meeting with the Minister of Defence of Uzbekistan
Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov held a meeting with the Minister of Defence of the Republic of Uzbekistan, Major General Botir Kudratkhodjaev, who has been recently appointed to the post.
🤝
The Russian Defence Minister
congratulated his Uzbek counterpart on his appointment
and noted that it would give a fresh push to the development of bilateral military cooperation.
💬
The officials
discussed issues
of mutual interest during the meeting.
