🔥
The Tsentr Group's units inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one air assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two national guard brigades close to Liman, Verovka, Belozerskoye, Svyatogorovka, Veselaya Gora, Maryevka, and Novoyavlenka (Donetsk People's Republic).
📍
In Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army advanced in the central and northern-western parts of the city. Marine units continued to mop up the controlled quarters of the city and the territory of the Dobropolskaya mine.
🚩
Over the past 24 hours, 286 buildings,
including three production facilities,
have been liberated,
up to 65 troops were neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 345 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and one Grad MLRS combat vehicle have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one air assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Gavrilovka, Tarasovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Dudnikovo, Zalivnoye, Blakitnoye, and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 240 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Orekhov and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, 20 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
the defence industry enterprises and transport infrastructure facilities of Ukraine used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the production workshops of unmanned aerial vehicles and their depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
