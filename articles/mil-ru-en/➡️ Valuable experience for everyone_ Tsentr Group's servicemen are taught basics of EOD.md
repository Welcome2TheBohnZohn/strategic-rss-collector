Valuable experience for everyone: Tsentr Group's servicemen are taught basics of EOD
New EOD specialists are being prepared in the Tsentr Group of Forces.
The training takes place on the territory of a special Centre
created under the aegis of the 12th Guards Separate Engineering Brigade.
➡️
The programme lasts a week:
during this time, the servicemen learn EOD theory and improve skills in modern training classrooms and at training grounds.
