# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to launch UAV strikes against fuel-power infrastructure facilities of Ukraine used in the interests of the AFU and vessels us

Published: 2026-11-07 15:50:30

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30486

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to launch UAV strikes
against fuel-power infrastructure facilities of Ukraine used in the interests of the AFU and vessels used to deliver military cargo.
💥
Today, during the daytime, the following targets have been hit in Kiev region:
▫️
fuel tankers at the parking lot
used to deliver fuel to the AFU's units close to the settlement of Irpen;
▫️
the Severnaya 330 kV power substation with a capacity of 750 kV,
which supplied electricity to Ukraine’s defence industry enterprises close to the settlement of Novye Petrovtsy.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military materiel and dual-use cargo to Ukraine
was hit
in the Black Sea waters east of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
