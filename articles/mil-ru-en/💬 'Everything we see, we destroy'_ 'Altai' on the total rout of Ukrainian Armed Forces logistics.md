'Everything we see, we destroy': 'Altai' on the total rout of Ukrainian Armed Forces logistics
Drone operators from the special forces of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces are wiping out the enemy in the settlements of the Slaviansk-Kramatorsk agglomeration.
UAV operators respond instantly to every attempt by the enemy to change or secure their routes. The result — a huge amount of burnt-out equipment and disrupted logistics for Ukrainian Armed Forces units.
💬
Details of the combat work
were shared by the deputy commander of a group of the 58th Separate Special Purpose Battalion, call sign 'Altai':
'We keep watch on their logistics routes. Both with awaiter drones and simply on free-hunt missions. The enemy tries to change their logistics routes somehow, tries to change the timing. We register all these changes immediately and start destroying them on the new routes. A lot of what we destroy is Western-made, of course. Everything from some Volvo trucks to American Paladin self-propelled guns, or just some Hummers. Everything we see, we destroy'.
