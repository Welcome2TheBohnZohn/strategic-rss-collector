Through the fire: for the life in his arms
In the liberated settlements, the Sever Group's servicemen
found civilians who had been taken hostage by the AFU
. Ukrainian troops used people as human shields and brutally suppressed any attempt to escape towards Russia.
🔹
Local resident Olga recalls in tears how she and her family tried to escape by car. A Ukrainian FPV drone struck their vehicle. Olga's husband died instantly, she suffered severe shrapnel wounds, and a scared little child remained in her arms.
Russian assault detachments have come to rescue.
▫️
Under non-stop fire, fighters carried a woman and her baby out in their hands.
In those moments, as the servicemen themselves admit, fear completely fell away – all that remained was the clear understanding that a fragile childhood life was in their arms and must be saved at any cost.
▫️
Now, the rescued persons are safe
under medical supervision.
