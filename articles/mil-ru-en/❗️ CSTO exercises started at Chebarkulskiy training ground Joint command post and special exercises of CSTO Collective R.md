CSTO exercises started at Chebarkulskiy training ground
Joint command post and special exercises of CSTO Collective Rapid Reaction Forces Cooperation 2026, Search 2026 and Echelon 2026 were started in the Russian Federation.
▫️
Colonel General Andrei Serdyukov, Chief of the CSTO Joint Staff, Deputy Secretary General of the Organisation Mirlan Turgunbekov, and military contingents commanders took part in the opening ceremony.
💬
Addressing the participants of the exercise, Chief of the CSTO Joint Staff Andrei Serdyukov noted that
high-quality organisation of joint operational and combat training, taking into account the experience of modern armed conflicts, as well as the development of the combat potential of the CSTO Collective Forces, their equipping with modern weapons, military and special hardware, including unmanned systems, reconnaissance means, and electronic warfare systems, and the improvement of the air defence system organisation
are priority directions of activity during the Russian Federation's chairmanship of the Organisation.
‘The urgency of this is due to the extremely difficult military and political situation in the world. The further degradation of international relations, as well as the ‘zones of instability’ approaching the borders of our states, contribute to the growth of challenges and threats to military security. The prevailing conditions dictate us that we must promptly react to changes of the combat operations' nature. Guided by our priorities, we are refining the command elements and formations of the Collective Forces, and their readiness for operations that replicate as closely as possible the real conditions of modern armed conflicts,' said Colonel General Andrei Serdyukov.
✅
‘I am sure that
our exercises will serve to further improvement and the readiness increasing of the Collective Forces to perform their tasks, will demonstrate the determination of the Organisation and the force component's readiness to ensure collective security
,’ the Chief of the CSTO Joint Staff noted.
