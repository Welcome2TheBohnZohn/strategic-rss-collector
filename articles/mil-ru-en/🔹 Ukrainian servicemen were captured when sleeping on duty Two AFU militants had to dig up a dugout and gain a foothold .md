Ukrainian servicemen were captured when sleeping on duty
Two AFU militants had to dig up a dugout and gain a foothold in it on order from the command. At night, Russian servicemembers woke them up:
'I finished my shift. I woke up my comrade, told him it was his turn, and went to sleep. Then, I woke up, saw him sleeping next to me. I heard voices near the dugout. Someone shouted for us to surrender. Otherwise, they would threw two grenades,' tells the POW.
