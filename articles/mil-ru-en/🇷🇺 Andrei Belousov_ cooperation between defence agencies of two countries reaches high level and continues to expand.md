Andrei Belousov: cooperation between defence agencies of two countries reaches high level and continues to expand
Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov held talks with the Minister of Defence of the Republic of Uzbekistan Major General Botir Kudratkhodjayev as part of a working visit to Uzbekistan.
'The cooperation between the defence agencies of the two countries has reached a high level and continues to expand. Nowadays, this is especially important for strengthening overall security in the Central Asian region,' said Andrei Belousov.
💬
Andrei Belousov stressed that Russia and Uzbekistan
are partners and long-standing friends,
and the main vector of their interaction is determined by warm and friendly relations between the presidents of the two countries.
'Currently, we are implementing the Strategic Partnership Programme in Military Sphere for 2026–2030. In accordance with it, we hold more than 60 events annually. Particular attention is paid to operational and combat training of troops and joint exercises. In October, Russian and Uzbek troops practised joint missions at the Termez training ground,' the Russian Defence Minister noted.
▫️
The head of the Russian defence department added that
personnel training and regular consultations on the activities of the armed forces are another important areas of bilateral cooperation.
'We attach great importance to the training of Uzbek servicemen in our special training centres of the Russian Armed Forces, as well as to comprehensive exercises and training sessions led by Russian instructors with real combat experience,' he said.
📊
Andrei Belousov noted that the Russian defence department is aimed at further active cooperation to increase the combat potential of the Armed Forces of the Republic of Uzbekistan and ensure collective security.
🤝
In turn, Major General Botir Kudratkhodjayev conveyed words of welcome from the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
'Thanks to the political will of the heads of our states, the relations between Russia and Uzbekistan have moved to a qualitatively new level. It should be noted that today, there is positive development of cooperation in the military field,' he noted.
🌐
The head of the Uzbek Defence Ministry stressed that
Uzbekistan is interested in further expanding bilateral cooperation
with Russia in the field of defence and ensuring regional security.
✅
At the end of the talks, the
sides signed the 2027 Cooperation Plan
between the Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation and the Ministry of Defence of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
