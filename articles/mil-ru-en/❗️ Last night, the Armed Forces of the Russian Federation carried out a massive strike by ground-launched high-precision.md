Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out a massive strike by ground-launched high-precision weapons and long-range drones against communication and energy infrastructure facilities
in Kiev, Kiev and Kharkov regions, which are used in the interests of the defence industry of Ukraine and the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
The following targets
were engaged:
📍
in Kiev:
▪️
a data processing centre (JSC Ukrsibbank),
which provided internet traffic for intelligence gathering on the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation;
▪️
the Darnitskay Power Station (TPP-4)
, which provides electric power to the defence industry in Kiev;
▪️
the Kievskaya power station (TPP-5)
supplying military facilities and enterprises of the defence industry in Kiev and Kiev region;
📍
in Kiev region:
▪️
the Kievskaya power station (TPP-6)
providing energy for military logistics facilities, weapons, and military hardware storage areas in the north-eastern part of Kiev region;
▪️
the Tripolskaya power station
providing electricity to defence industry facilities in Kiev and Kiev region;
▪️
the Kievskaya electric substation
, which ensures the transfer of electricity from the western regions of Ukraine and its distribution to the facilities of the Ukrainian Armed Forces in Kiev region;
📍
in Kharkov and Kharkov region:
▪️
the Kharkovskaya power station
providing electric power to the defence industry in Kharkov and the western part of Kharkov region;
▪️
the Zmievskaya power station
supplying electricity to AFU facilities in the south-eastern part of Kharkov region.
