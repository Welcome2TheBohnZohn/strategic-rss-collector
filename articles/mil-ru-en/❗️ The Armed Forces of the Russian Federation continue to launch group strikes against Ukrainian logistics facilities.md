# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to launch group strikes against Ukrainian logistics facilities

Published: 2026-09-02 15:20:02

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29178

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to launch group strikes against Ukrainian logistics facilities
and vessels,
used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
During the day,
air-based high-precision weapons and attack drones hit the following targets:
▪️
the Eridon logistics centre
in Vishnevoye of Kiev region, used for storage and distribution of military cargo;
▪️
the FM Logistik Dnepr logistics centre
near Dudarkov of Kiev region, where warehouses were used to store dual-purpose goods as well as components of unmanned aerial vehicles;
▪️
the logistics centre in Korchevatoye
of Kiev region, where cargo for the Armed Forces of Ukraine was stored and distributed;
▪️
a dry cargo ship
with military equipment for the AFU in the port of Chernomorsk;
▪️
a dry cargo
with weapons and ammunition in the Black Sea waters;
▪️
depots with military hardware
in the port of Odessa.

Russian Defence Ministry
