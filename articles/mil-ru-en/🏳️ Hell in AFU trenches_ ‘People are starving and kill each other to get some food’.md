Hell in AFU trenches: ‘People are starving and kill each other to get some food’
A captured Ukrainian serviceman, Anatoly Kropotov, told about the command's total indifference to the servicemen:
‘We didn't have any training, all we had is a few days of dry fire trainings. They wanted to send us to the frontline ASAP. The only mentioned reason was insufficient manpower there. The brigade commander sends us with some rancour, he doesn't send food supplies, servicemen are starving and killing each other to get some food, to try to survive.’
Leaving the AFU's encircled formations defeated in Dobropolye, the Tsentr Group's servicemen continue to neutralise Ukrainian militants on the line of contact.
The only chance to stay alive is to surrender to the Russian servicemen.
