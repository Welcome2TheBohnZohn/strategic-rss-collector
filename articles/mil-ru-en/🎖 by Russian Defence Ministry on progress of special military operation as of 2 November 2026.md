↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Novorozovka, Tersyanka (Zaporozhye region), Pokrovskoye, Ivanovka, and Pavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 195 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, three motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units inflicted losses on the formations of one mechanised brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and one territorial defence brigade close to Preobrazhenka and Orekhov (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 20 troops and eight motor vehicles of the AFU have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
the power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, production workshops and depots of unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.

658 fixed-wing UAVs.
