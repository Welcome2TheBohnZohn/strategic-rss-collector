📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and four national guard brigades close to Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Gornyak, Dobropolye, Novovodyanoye, Novogrishino (Donetsk People's Republic), Ivanovka, Novoandreyevka, and Novotroitskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 360 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, eight motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade, one airborne brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Barvinovka, Vasinovka, Nikolskoye, Obshcheye, Omelnik, and Chervony Yar (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 275 troops and ten motor vehicles.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU near Antonovka, Kushugum, and Razumovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 30 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, and 21 motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
the Ukranian transport and logistics infrastructure, used by the AFU, storage areas of UAVs and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
two guided aerial bombs and 498 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
