We
took what's ours!
During the assault of
Obshcheye
, the Vostok Group's servicemen had to break through the enemy's powerful defence system.
▫️
Our lads liberated the settlement,
moved more than 5 kilometres forward, crossed the Solenaya River, and mopped up an area of more than 12 square kilometres from the AFU.
💬
The deputy platoon leader with the call sign 'Berkut'
spoke about advancing to the settlement and the enemy's equipped positions:
'Sometimes, you come over 500 metres in five minutes, but sometimes, it takes a whole day. The enemy intensively operates with FPV drones and heavy drones with drop systems, and mines approaches. They had well-equipped dugouts, strongholds, machine gun positions'.
