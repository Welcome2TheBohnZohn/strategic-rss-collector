The T-80BVM at work: combat power is forged at the training grounds of the special military operation
▫️
The Zapad Group's tank crews are honing their skills at training grounds in the rear of the special military operation. Crews of the T-80BVM tanks are training in firing from closed positions and with direct fire.
👉
Particular attention is paid to coordination, protection against UAVs, and countering electronic warfare. Commanders analyse every shot so that units are ready to destroy enemy equipment and fortifications around the clock.
