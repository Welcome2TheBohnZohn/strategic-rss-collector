# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-04 10:55:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29213

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
From 29 August to 4 September, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out one massive and 17 group strikes with high-precision weapons and long-range drones,
which hit enterprises of defence industry of Ukraine, logistics centres, fuel-power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, drone manufacturing workshops and warehouses, ammunition, fuel and materiel depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
🔥
In addition, a number of the Ukrainian ports infrastructure facilities,
which provided reception and storage of military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants used in the interests of the Ukrainian Armed Forces,
have been disabled
.
🔥
During the week, eight dry cargo ships and one container ship operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, were neutralised.
🚩
Last week, as a result of decisive actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces
established control over the settlements of Khrapovshchina, Sadki and Velikiy Prikol (Sumy region), Kazachya Lopan, Vasilevka, Dolzhanka, and Blagodatnoye (Kharkov region).
💥
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware
of five mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, three territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 1,660 troops, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, 63 motor vehicles, 10 field artillery guns, and nine electronic and counter-fire stations in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
🚩
During the week, as a result of active actions, units of the Zapad Group of Forces
liberated Svyatogorsk (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Strikes were delivered at
formations of four mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault brigade, one airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,415 troops, 15 armoured fighting vehicles, 112 motor vehicles, and 10 field artillery guns.
🚩
Last week, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and
liberated Izhevka and, on 3 September, they liberated Nikanorovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of
three mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault brigade, one mountain assault brigade, one airmobile brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, has lost more than 1,365 troops, 20 armoured fighting vehicles, 181 motor vehicles, and 17 field artillery guns.
🚩
During the week, as a result of active offensive actions of the Tsentr Group of Forces,
the settlements of Rubezhnoye, Novoandreyevka, and, today, Gruzskoye (Donetsk People's Republic) have been liberated.
💥
Strikes were delivered at
formations of four mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one airborne brigade, one assault brigade, one jaeger brigade, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and three national guard brigades.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,685 troops, 18 armoured fighting vehicles, 80 motor vehicles, eight field artillery guns, and six electronic warfare stations.
