# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-23 09:43:45

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30744

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
↗️
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one national guard brigade close to Veseloye, Makarovo, Ploskoye, and Golubovka (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region,
formations of three mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade have been hit near Spasskoye, Pushkarevka, Vakalovshchina, Simonovka, and Novaya Sloboda (Sumy region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 160 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, one electronic reconnaissance station, and nine counter-fire and electronic warfare stations.
⏩
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU and two territorial defence brigades near Peski-Radkovskiye, Khomino (Kharkov region), Raygorodok, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 220 troops, one field artillery gun, and nine motor vehicles.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's elements engaged formations of four mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades, and one assault regiment of the AFU close to Nikolayevka, Druzhkovka, and Belenkoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 175 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 22 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Tsentr Group's units continued to carry out active offensive operations.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one infantry fighting brigade of the AFU, and two national guard brigades near Rayskoye, Viktorovka, Mikhaylovka, Novopavlovka, Sergeyevka, Kamenka and Ocheretino (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 340 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, one artillery gun, ten motor vehicles, and five electronic warfare stations.
