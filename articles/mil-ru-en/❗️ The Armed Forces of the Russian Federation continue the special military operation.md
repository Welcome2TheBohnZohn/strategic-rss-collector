# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-14 11:05:07

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29394

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces in Kharkov region continue to tighten the encirclement
of the enemy's formations and expand the external control zone.
🚩
I
n the encirclement ring, Russian troops have liberated Shirokoye (Kharkov region)
and actively carry out intensive operations to liberate Malaya Volchya (Kharkov region).
💥
The coordinated fire damage was inflicted
on enemy manpower and hardware close to Rybalkino and Buzovo (Kharkov region). The Group's units continue to neutralise blocked units of the AFU near Olkhovatka in Kharkov region.
▪️
Within 24 hours, the enemy lost up to 60 troops and one armoured fighting vehicle with manpower during an attempt to leave the area of Chernoye (Kharkov region).
▪️
Since the beginning of the month,
up to 460 militants, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, eight pickup trucks, 13 ATVs, and 21 ground robotic systems have been neutralised.
🚩
As a result of resolute actions to expand the external control zone,
Russian troops liberated Shevchenkovo (Kharkov region)
and inflicted losses on the enemy's formations near Novoaleksandrovka and Prikolotnoye (Kharkov region).
🔥
In addition,
units of one motorised infantry brigade and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit close to Martynovka and Chernoglazovka (Kharkov region).
💥
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two enemy's territorial defence brigades close to Boyaro-Lezhachi, Barilovka, and Radkovka (Sumy region).
📊
In total, within 24 hours, in the Sever Group's area of responsibility, the enemy has lost
up to 200 troops, one U.S.-made M113 armoured personnel carrier, two armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and ten UGVs.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Losses were inflicted on formations of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Starodubovka, Karpovka, Rubtsy (Donetsk People's Republic), and Peski Radkovskiye (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 215 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, one field artillery gun, and one electronic reconnaissance station.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades of the AFU and one marine brigade near Druzhkovka, Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Slavyansk, Klinovoye, Kramatorsk, Rayskoye, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 170 troops, two field artillery guns, including one U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer, and 21 motor vehicles.
