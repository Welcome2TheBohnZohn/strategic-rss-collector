# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-13 12:26:50

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30590

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
From 7 to 13 November, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two massive and 12 group strikes
to hit enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry, logistics centres, energy and transport infrastructure facilities used by the AFU, production facilities and storage areas of cruise missiles and robotic systems, including unmanned aerial vehicles, ammunition depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
🔥
Over the past week,
15 dry cargo ships
delivering weapons, military hardware, and dual-use products to the Ukrainian Armed Forces have been neutralised.
🚩
Over the past week, units of the Sever Group of Forces have established control over Nikolskoye (Sumy region) and yesterday over Kotovka (Kharkov region).
💥
Strikes were delivered
at formations of six mechanised, one motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades, two unmanned systems brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and four territorial defence brigades.
▪️
Over the past week, the enemy has lost more than 1,480 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, 101 motor vehicles, five field artillery guns, and 31 electronic warfare and counter-fire stations in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
↗️
Over the past week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces have taken advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, an assault brigade, an airmobile brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,510 troops, nine armoured fighting vehicles, 97 motor vehicles, 12 field artillery guns, and 21 electronic warfare and counter-fire stations.
📍
Last week, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted fire damage on formations of six mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
In total, the enemy has lost more than 1,325 troops, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, 180 motor vehicles, five field artillery guns, and 13 electronic warfare stations in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces.
🚩
During the week, the Tsentr Group's units liberated Shilovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
🔥
They inflicted fire damage
on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an airborne brigade, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, three marine brigades, and five National Guard brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,390 troops, 50 armoured fighting vehicles, 59 motor vehicles, eight field artillery guns, and 24 electronic warfare stations.
