# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-11-11 10:34:48

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30550

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units engaged units of two mechanised brigades, two airmobile brigades of the AFU, and four territorial defence brigades near Yamnoye, Olshanka, Bititsa, Pushkarevka, Stetskovka, Fotovizh, and Velikaya Rybitsa (Sumy region).
💥
In addition,
in Kharkov region
, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU near Kolodeznoye, Alisovka, Izbitskoye, Velikiye Prokhody, and Sosnovka (Kharkov region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 315 troops, 18 motor vehicles, 105-mm U.S.-made M101 howitzer, and 12 electronic warfare stations.
⏩
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, one airmoble brigade, one assault brigade of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade near Lesnaya Stenka, Studenok, Vasilevka (Kharkov region), Mayaki, Lozovoye, Starodubovka, and Volchiy Yar (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 210 troops, one U.S.-made HMMWV armoured vehicle, and 16 motor vehicles.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Formations of three mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, one mountain assault brigade, and two assault regiments of the AFU have been hit near Vasilevskaya Pustosh, Kramatorsk, Slavyansk, Druzhkovka, Raygorodok, Nikolayevka, and Krasny Molochar (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 175 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 36 motor vehicles, one Grad MLRS, one radar station, and four electronic warfare stations.
🚩
As a result of resolute actions, the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Shilovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
🔥
Russian elements inflicted damage
on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one air assault brigade, one assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, four national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade close to Yakovlevka, Sergeyevka, Krinitsy, Kuritsino, Zelenoye, Zolotye Prudy, Roganskoye, Andreyevka, Belozerskoye (Donetsk People's Republic), and Maliyevo (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 345 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 12 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station.
