# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-09 10:35:27

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29936

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
From 3 to 9 of October, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out one mass and seven group strikes,
which hit defence industry enterprises, logistics and telecommunication centres, energy and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, military airfields, production and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles, ammunition depots of fuel and military-technical equipment, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
▪️
In addition, the strikes disabled a number of Ukrainian port infrastructure facilities used for receiving, storing, and distributing military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants destined for the AFU.
💥
During the week, 14 dry cargo ships and one tanker engaged in supplying Western weapons, military hardware, fuel, and materiel intended to support the AFU were hit.
➡️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continued to tighten the encirclement
of enemy's formations in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
🚩
Last week, as a result of resolute actions, the settlement of Annopolye (Kharkov region) was taken under control.
All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
▪️
Over the past week, the enemy has lost more than 275 militants, three armoured fighting vehicles, two motor vehicles, and three UGVs.
▪️
In total, 414 sq km of territory and 20 settlements have been liberated since the beginning of the AFU group's encirclement in the north-east of Kharkov region. More than 1,360 troops, 35 armoured fighting vehicles, 21 motor vehicles, 18 ATVs, and 34 UGVs have been neutralised.
🚩
Moreover, Russian troops established control over Novaya Kazachya (Kharkov region) and yesterday took under control Kiyanitsa (Sumy region).
💥
In Sumy and Kharkov regions, strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, two airmobile brigades, one unmanned system brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, three territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade.
▪️
Over the past week, in the Sever Group's area of responsibility, the enemy lost more than 1,850 troops, 15 armoured fighting vehicles, 134 motor vehicles, six field artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare and counter-fire radars.
📍
During the week, the Zapad Group of Forces have taken more advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units inflicted losses on formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,565 troops, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, 93 motor vehicles, nine field artillery guns, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS launcher, and five electronic warfare stations.
📍
Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one mountain assault brigade, two airmobile brigades of the AFU, one marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 1,215 troops, ten armoured fighting vehicles, 162 motor vehicles, nine field artillery guns, and three electronic and counter-fire radars in the Yuzhnaya Group's area of responsibility.
