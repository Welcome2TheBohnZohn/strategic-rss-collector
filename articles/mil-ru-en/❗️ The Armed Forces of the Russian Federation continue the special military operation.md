# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-30 09:25:36

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30357

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
From 24 to 30 of October, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two massive and nine group strikes
, which hit enterprises of the Ukrainian defence industry, logistics centres, energy and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, production workshops and storage areas of ground robotic systems, unmanned aerial vehicles, and uncrewed surface vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
🔥
In addition,
the facilities of the Ukrainian port infrastructure
, which provided for the reception, storage and distribution of military cargo
, were disabled.
💥
Eleven dry cargo ships
delivering military hardware and dual-use goods to Ukraine for the AFU
were neutralised
.
🚩
During the week, as a result of intensive offensive actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces
liberated settlements of Alisovka in Kharkov region and Barilovka in Sumy region.
💥
Russian troops
inflicted losses on
formations of eight mechanised brigades, three motorised infantry brigades, two airmobile brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and four territorial defence brigades.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,575 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, 68 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and six electronic warfare stations.
📍
During the week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces have taken advantageous lines and positions.
The Group's units hit manpower and military hardware of four mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, an assault brigade of the AFU, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,585 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, 108 motor vehicles, six field artillery guns, and 12 electronic warfare stations.
📍
Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Russian troops inflicted damage on formations of five mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, an air assault brigade, a mountain assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, a marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
In total, in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,155 troops, 24 armoured fighting vehicles, 217 motor vehicles, 12 field artillery guns, and eight electronic warfare stations.
