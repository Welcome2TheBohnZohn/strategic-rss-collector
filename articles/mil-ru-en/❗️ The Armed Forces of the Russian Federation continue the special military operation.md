# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-08-28 10:20:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29084

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
From 22 to 28 August, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out
one massive and 12 group strikes
with high-precision weapons and attack drones, which hit enterprises of defence, metallurgical, and petroleum industries of Ukraine, logistics centres, fuel-power and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, military airfields, workshops manufacturing unmanned aerial vehicles and components, ammunition, fuel and materiel depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition, the strikes
disabled infrastructure of the Ukrainian ports
, which were used to received and store military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants intended for use by the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔥
During the week,
a patrol boat
of the Ukrainian Navy, as well as eight vessels operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, including
six dry cargo ships and two tankers
, were neutralised.
🚩
Last week,
units of the Sever Group of Forces established control over Pershe Travnya and Pisarevka (Sumy region).
💥
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of seven mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, five territorial defence brigades, and one National Guard brigade.
▪️
In total, the enemy lost more than 1,550 troops, three tanks, 10 armoured fighting vehicles, 73 motor vehicles, four field artillery guns, and five electronic warfare and counter-fire stations in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
📍
During the week,
units of the Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
They hit units of four mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,510 troops, 12 armoured fighting vehicles, including five Western-made vehicles, 136 motor vehicles, 16 field artillery guns, and 11 electronic warfare stations.
📍
Last week,
units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
They hit manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an airmobile brigade, an assault brigade, a mountain assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Over the past week, in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, the enemy has lost more than 1,490 troops, 10 armoured fighting vehicles, 180 motor vehicles, and 19 field artillery guns.
