# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-10-02 11:40:20

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29762

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
❗️
From 26 September to 2 October,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out one massive and seven group strikes, which hit
logistics and telecommunication centres, power, transport and port infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, military airfields, UAV manufacturing workshops and storage areas, ammunition, Flamingo ground-based cruise missiles, fuel, and military hardware depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed forces and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition,
the strikes disabled a number of facilities of the infrastructure of the Ukrainian ports,
which provided reception and storage of military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants used in the interests of the Ukrainian Armed Forces.
🔥
During the week,
17 dry cargo ships
used to deliver Western-made weaponry, military hardware, and materiel intended to support the AFU
were hit.
▫️
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement around the enemy's formations in the north-east of Kharkov region and expand the external control zone.
Last week, as a result of active and resolute actions, the settlements of Petropavlovka, Lozovaya, Khripuny, Kreydyanka, Shabelnoye, Nesternoye, Rublenoye (Kharkov region) were brought under control,
and yesterday, the Russian control was established over Grachevka and Serdobino (Kharkov region). All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
▪️
Over the past week, the enemy has lost over 265 troops, nine armoured fighting vehicles, six motor vehicles, five ATVs, and three ground robotic systems.
➡️
A total of 352 square kilometres of territory and 20 settlements have been liberated since the beginning of the AFU group's encirclement in the north-east of Kharkov region.
More than 1,085 troops, 32 armoured fighting vehicles, 19 motor vehicles, 18 ATVs, and 31 UGVs were neutralised.
🚩
In addition,
Russian elements established control over Maryino, Ulanovo, and Tolstodubovo (Sumy region), and yesterday the settlement of Malaya Rybitsa (Sumy region) was brought under Russian control. In Kharkov region, control was established over the settlement of Vysokaya Yaruga.
🔥
Also, six mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, one airmobile brigade, one unmanned systems brigade, one assault regiment, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, four territorial defence brigades, and one national guard brigade
were hit in Sumy and Kharkov regions.
▪️
Over the past week, in the Sever Group's area of responsibility, the enemy has lost more than 2,000 troops, 21 armoured fighting vehicles, 144 motor vehicles, four field artillery guns, two Grad MLRS combat vehicles, 11 electronic warfare stations.
↗️
During the week, units of the Zapad Group of Forces have taken more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at formations of four mechanised brigades, one assault brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, one national guard brigade, and two territorial defence brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,510 troops, 13 armoured fighting vehicles, 86 motor vehicles, nine field artillery guns, and five electronic warfare stations.
📍
Last week, units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Manpower and hardware of five mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one assault brigade, one mountain assault brigade, two airmobile brigades, one unmanned system brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and two marine brigades were hit.
▪️
In total, in the Yuzhnaya Group's area of responsibility, the enemy has lost more than 1,360 troops, nine armoured fighting vehicles, 164 motor vehicles, and ten field artillery guns.
