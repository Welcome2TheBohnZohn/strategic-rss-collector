# The President of Russia, in his capacity as the Supreme Commander-in-Chief, ordered Russian forces to cease attacks on Kiev for three days starting at midnight today, Presidential 

Published: 2026-09-05 14:49:50

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29234

The President of Russia, in his capacity as the Supreme Commander-in-Chief, ordered Russian forces to cease attacks on Kiev for three days starting at midnight today, Presidential Press Secretary Dmitry Peskov announced. According to Mr Peskov, this is due to the talks — and preparations thereof — with American negotiators to be held in Kiev
