🚩
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence and took control of the settlement of Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
💥
Manpower and hardware of one air assault brigade, one airmobile brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade have been hit near Malomikhaylovka, Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Volnyanka, Tersyanka, and Yasnaya Polyana (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 135 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, five motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
🚩
As a result of decisive actions, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces liberated Malokaterinovka in Zaporozhye region.
🔥
Formations of two mechanised brigades of the AFU were hit near Orekhov and Balabino in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 25 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and seven motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, UAV storage areas, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed forces and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile and 654 fixed-wing UAVs.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet destroyed
one uncrewed surface vehicle of the Ukrainian Navy in the Black Sea waters.
