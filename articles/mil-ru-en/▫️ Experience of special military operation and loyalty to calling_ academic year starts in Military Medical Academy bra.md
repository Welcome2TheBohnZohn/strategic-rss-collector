Experience of special military operation and loyalty to calling: academic year starts in Military Medical Academy branch
🤝
Russian Deputy Defence Minister Anna Tsivileva
congratulated cadets and instructors of a branch of the Military Medical Academy named after S.M. Kirov on the beginning of the academic year
stressing that military medics are the real elite of the Army.
▫️
A high level of teaching, combined with real combat experience, helps
to prepare officers and medical personnel for modern combat conditions.
It raises the prestige of the entire military service.
⏩
The fact that
more than 30% of those who entered the Academy this year are participants of the special military operation
plays a huge role. Moreover, it is not only the future medical officers who have the combat experience, but also those who have come to secondary and junior medical school.
