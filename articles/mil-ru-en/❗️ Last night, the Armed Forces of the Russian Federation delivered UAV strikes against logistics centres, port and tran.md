Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes against logistics centres, port and transport infrastructure facilities, communication centres, and vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
As a result, the following targets were engaged:
📍
in Kiev, a data processing centre of the PLC Vodafone Group,
which is one of the biggest companies providing mobile communication and high-speed internet for the AFU in order to transmit the reconnaissance data;
📍
in Kiev region,
in Krasilovka,
the Zammler Group logistics centre,
where Western military assistance was stored and distributed in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
📍
in Nikolayev, two depots
of unmanned aerial vehicles and their combat units;
📍
in Odessa region,
in Ilyichovka,
the Dron Kalinsh logistics centre
where FPV interceptor drones were stored;
📍
in
the port of Kiliya:
▫️
the headquarters of the Vostok naval base of the Ukrainian Navy,
▫️
the Ukrainian State Border Service's home site,
▫️
four depots with military equipment intended to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine,
▫️
mooring infrastructure facilities.
💥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military materiel and dual-use cargo to the port of Odessa to support the AFU was hit at sea.
