# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch strikes

Published: 2026-11-16 06:30:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30629

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch strikes
by unmanned aerial vehicles against port and railway infrastructure facilities as well as vessels engaged in providing support to the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets were hit:
▫️
a dry cargo ship
at sea delivering military and dual-use cargo for the Armed Forces of Ukraine to the port of Chernomorsk;
▫️
a railway locomotive
near the Zaporozhye railway station employed to transport goods intended for being supplied to the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
