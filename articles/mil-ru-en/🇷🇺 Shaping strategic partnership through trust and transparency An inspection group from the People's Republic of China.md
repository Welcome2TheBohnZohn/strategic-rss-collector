# 🇷🇺 Shaping strategic partnership through trust and transparency An inspection group from the People's Republic of China

Published: 2026-08-28 12:00:56

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29086

Shaping
strategic partnership through trust and transparency
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
An
inspection group from the People's Republic of China
visited facilities of the
Eastern Military District
in Primorsky Krai under multilateral agreements.
📑
The event was conducted as part of the implementation of the Shanghai (1996) and Moscow (1997) agreements
between the Russian Federation, the Republic of Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic, the Republic of Tajikistan, and the People's Republic of China
on confidence-building
in the military field in the border area and on mutual reduction of armed forces in the border area.
🤝
These agreements
became an important step in the development of strategic partnership
relations between the states, laid a reliable foundation for military cooperation and helped ensure lasting peace and tranquillity on the borders of the participating countries.
📍
One of the key events was an inspection of a Guards rocket artillery formation of the Eastern Military District,
stationed in Primorsky Krai.
✅
During the visit, the Chinese inspection group
verified the Russian Federation's compliance with its international obligations and confirmed the high level of effectiveness
of the control mechanisms provided for under the agreements with the PRC.
🕯
During the visit, the inspection group laid flowers at the Eternal Flame memorial complex. The joint work concluded with performances by creative ensembles of the Eastern Military District.
