Trap for enemy birds
▫️
Servicemen of the 30th Separate Motorised Rifle Brigade (the Tsentr Group of Forces)
organised an echeloned system to counter enemy drones
in Dobropolye direction.
💬
A sniper platoon commander with the call sign 'Gora' told about
how the servicemen at the front line work as a single body.
‘Our main task it is to secure the sky, against small UAVs, such as FPV and kamikaze drones, and against large agricultural drones, such as Baba Yaga. We are always on guard, we always stay alert and keep doing our best to benefit our Motherland!’ said the serviceman.
