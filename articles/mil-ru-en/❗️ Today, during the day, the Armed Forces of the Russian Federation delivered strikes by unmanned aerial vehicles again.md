# ❗️ Today, during the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against data processing centres

Published: 2026-09-23 16:34:41

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29576

❗️
Today, during the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
delivered strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against data processing centres
(data centres), as well as logistics and warehouse terminals
used in the interests of supporting the activities of the Ukrainian Armed Forces.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following were hit:
📍
in the city of Kiev:
▫️
the New-Telco data processing centre
, one of the key Internet traffic exchange facilities of the Ukrainian Ministry of Defence;
▫️
the United DS data processing centre,
which provides data processing and transmission, as well as international communications for Ukraine's special services;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
two logistics centres
in the city of Odesa and the settlement of Iliichivka, which store and distribute military cargo arriving in Ukraine from European states.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
