Andrei Belousov to the servicemen who liberated Dobropolye: This victory will become a symbol of the courage and resilience of Russian troops and will go down in the history of the military exploits of these brigades and regiment
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov congratulated the command and personnel of the 110th Separate Guards Motorised Rifle Suvorov Order Brigade, the 114th Separate Guards Motor Rifle Brigade, the 61st Separate Guards Kirkenes Red Banner Marine Brigade and the 177th Separate Guards Marine Regiment on the liberation of the settlement of Dobropolye in the Donetsk People's Republic.
‘During the special military operation, these formations have been operating on some of the most challenging fronts. In fierce fighting, the servicemen of these units have demonstrated outstanding courage and bravery, decisively defeating the enemy on Donetsk soil,' the Russian Defence Minister noted.
Andrei Belousov
thanked the servicemen for their loyalty to their military duty and oath.
He also expressed confidence that the personnel of these units would continue to carry out all assigned tasks with honour, reliably defend Russia national interests and ensure the country security.
