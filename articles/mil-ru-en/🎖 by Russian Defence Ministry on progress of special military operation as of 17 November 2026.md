🚩
As a result of resolute actions, units of the Vostok Group of Forces liberated the settlement of Zalivnoye in Zaporozhye region.
🔥
The Group's units inflicted damage
on manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, two air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU near Podgavrilovka, Pokrovskoye, Rusakovo (Dnepropetrovsk region), Odarovka, and Sergeyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 120 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
units of a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Dimitrovo, Kamyshevakha, and Kirovo in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 20 troops, 20 motor vehicles, one artillery gun, and one electronic warfare station of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on power and transport infrastructure used in the interests of the AFU, production and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and ground robotic systems, military airfields, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 152 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
nine Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, a guided aerial bomb, and 2,256 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
