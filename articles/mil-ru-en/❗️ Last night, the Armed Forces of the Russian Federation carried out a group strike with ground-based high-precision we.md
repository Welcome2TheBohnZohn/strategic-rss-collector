# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a group strike with ground-based high-precision weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres

Published: 2026-09-29 07:25:19

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29684

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out a group strike with ground-based high-precision weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres, port, transport infrastructure facilities, and vessels operating in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets were hit:
📍
in
Kiev:
▫️
a locomotive shed equipped with a depot storing FP-5 Flamingo ground-based missiles and their components;
▫️
the Bimobile data processing centre, one of the main internet exchange points, ensuring the stability of information exchange, including intelligence data, in the interests of the AFU;
▫️
the Parkovy data centre, which processed public and commercial services' data, inter alia in the interests of the AFU;
📍
in
Kiev region,
the Belogorodka logistics depot, where fixed-wing UAVs were manufactured and stored;
📍
in
Odessa region:
▫️
the Usatovo power substation, providing electricity to the Ukrainian ports' infrastructure facilities;
▫️
a fixed-wing UAV manufacturing workshop in the settlement of Velikodolinskoye;
▫️
in the port of Izmail, port infrastructure facilities used to unload military cargo;
▫️
in the port of Odessa, a dry cargo ship, delivering cargo intended to support the AFU.
💥
In addition, a dry cargo ship delivering military cargo to the port of Odessa was hit at sea.
