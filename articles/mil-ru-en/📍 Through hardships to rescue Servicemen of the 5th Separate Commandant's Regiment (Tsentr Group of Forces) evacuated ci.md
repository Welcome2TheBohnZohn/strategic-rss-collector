# 📍 Through hardships to rescue Servicemen of the 5th Separate Commandant's Regiment (Tsentr Group of Forces) evacuated civilians from Belitskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).

Published: 2026-11-10 13:00:41

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30530

Through hardships to rescue
Servicemen of the 5th Separate Commandant's Regiment (Tsentr Group of Forces) evacuated civilians from Belitskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
People had been hiding in the basements for long time and waited for Russian servicemen
avoiding contact with militants.
📍
While making up an evacuation plan, the regiment's command took into account the current situation
and chose a way to use natural covers in the area like treelines and ravines.
🤝
The evacuation was successful
. People are now in a safe zone. There, they have been provided with the necessary assistance and accommodation.
❤️
Our warriors are the best. They risked their lives and once again proved that we leave no one behind.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
