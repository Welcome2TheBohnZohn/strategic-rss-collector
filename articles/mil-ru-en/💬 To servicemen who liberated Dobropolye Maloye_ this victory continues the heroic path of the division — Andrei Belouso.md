To servicemen who liberated Dobropolye Maloye: this victory continues the heroic path of the division — Andrei Belousov
💬
The Russian Defence Minister congratulated the command and personnel of the 76th Guards Chernigov Red Banner Order of Suvorov Air Assault Division as part of the 51st Army on the liberation of Dobropolye Maloye in the Donetsk People's Republic.
'This victory continues the heroic path of the division and will forever remain a symbol of courage and resilience for the next generation of defenders of the Motherland,' the head of the department stressed, addressing the fighters.
🤝
Andrei Belousov
thanked the servicemen for successfully carrying out combat missions and loyalty to military duty
, and expressed confidence that the servicemen of the division would honourably continue to carry out all assigned tasks, reliably defend national interests, and ensure Russia's security.
