Working together, thinking as one
📍
Combat training centre, Nizhny Novgorod region
✅
Unmanned systems teams, assault troops, and mobile fire groups practised joint actions based on experience gained from missions in the special operation zone. The organisation of combat training was personally inspected by Deputy Minister of Defence General of the Army Yunus-Bek Yevkurov.
📄
Key stages of a
tactical-special training exercise:
🎮
Drone teams
trained in conducting aerial reconnaissance, engaging targets with FPV drones, delivering ammunition, and evacuating wounded personnel using robotic platforms.
💥
Mobile firing groups
eliminated aerial targets using BMP-2M, Shilka SFU, small arms, and special interceptor drones.
▫️
Assault detachments
practised camouflaged and rapid entry to the enemy's positions on high-speed motor vehicles.
