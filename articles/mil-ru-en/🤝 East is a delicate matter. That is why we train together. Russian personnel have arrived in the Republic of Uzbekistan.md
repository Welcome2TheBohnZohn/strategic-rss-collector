# 🤝 East is a delicate matter. That is why we train together. Russian personnel have arrived in the Republic of Uzbekistan to take part in the Hamkorlik 2026 joint exercise.

Published: 2026-10-19 18:31:58

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30159

🤝
East is a delicate matter. That is why we train together.
📍
Russian personnel have arrived in the Republic of Uzbekistan to take part in the Hamkorlik 2026 joint exercise.
➡️
The units marched more than 300 kilometres through the mountainous terrain of Tajikistan and Uzbekistan before arriving at the Termez training ground in the Republic of Uzbekistan.
👉
There, Russian units began maintaining and preparing their equipment for the active phase of the exercise, which will take place from 20 to 27 October.
