🚩
As a result of active actions, the Tsentr Group's units liberated Veseloye Pole (Donetsk People's Republic).
🔥
Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of
two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Novoyavlenka, Maryevka, Starovarvarovka, Gornyak, and Roganskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Assault groups of the 51st Army have been advancing in north-eastern, central and southern parts of
the settlement of Dobropolye
(Donetsk People's Republic).
Over the past 24 hours,
more than 25 militants have been neutralised, and
72 buildings have been mopped up.
▪️
In total, up to 460 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, and one Msta-B artillery gun have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
📍
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one airmobile brigade, two air assault brigades, and one assault regiment of the AFU near Novosolenoye (Zaporozhye region), Vasilkovka, Fedorovskoye, Podgavrilovka, and Demurino (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 180 troops, one tank, one armoured fighting vehicle, and seven motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade have been hit close to Orekhov, Kirovo, Preobrazhenka, and Veselyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 20 troops, six motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
power facilities, manufacturing workshops, and storage areas of UAVs, ammunition and fuel depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.

six guided aerial bombs and 287 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
