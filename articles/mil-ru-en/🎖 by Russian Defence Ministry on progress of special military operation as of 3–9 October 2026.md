During the week, the Tsentr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
The losses were inflicted on formations of five mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, one jaeger brigade, one airborne brigade, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, two marine brigades, two national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade.
⏩
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), units of the 51st Army are developing an pincer movement
in the west and east of the city. All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement and perform personnel rotation were thwarted.
▪️
Since the beginning of the enemy's encirclement in Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), more than 520 militants, one armoured fighting vehicle, and six pickup trucks have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, more than 3,415 troops, 23 armoured fighting vehicles, 69 motor vehicles, six field artillery guns, and 13 electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
🚩
Over the past week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces have continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence, liberated Samoylovka and Golubkovo (Zaporozhye region).
💥
The Russian elements inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade.
▪️
The enemy lost in this direction over 1,845 troops, 14 armoured fighting vehicles, 67 motor vehicles, and seven field artillery guns.
📍
Last week, the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
The Group's units hit manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU.
▪️
Up to 245 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 127 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and seven electronic warfare and counter-fire stations were neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
31 guided aerial bombs, nine HIMARS MLRS projectiles, 17 Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 7,389 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
