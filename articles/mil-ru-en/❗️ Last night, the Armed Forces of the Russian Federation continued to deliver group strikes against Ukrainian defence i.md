Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to deliver group strikes against Ukrainian defence industry enterprises, logistics centres as well as ports used to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result,
the following targets were neutralised by unmanned aerial vehicles
:
📍
in Kiev region, a warehouse facility (Stellar Jet LLC) used to store manufactured Flamingo long-range cruise missiles and fuel for rocket boosters;
📍
in the port of Odessa,
six depots with military equipment
intended to be supplied to the AFU.
