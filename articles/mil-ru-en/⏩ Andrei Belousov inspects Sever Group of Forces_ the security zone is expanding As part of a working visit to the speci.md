Andrei Belousov inspects Sever Group of Forces: the
security zone is expanding
As part of a working visit to the special military operation zone, Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov visited the command post of one of the Sever Group's formations. During the visit, the head of the military department was informed by commanders on situation within their designated areas of responsibility.
⏩
Commander of the Sever Group of Forces Colonel General Yevgeny Nikiforov reported to the Minister of Defence on
successful actions aimed at creation of a security zone in the border areas of Sumy and Kharkov regions.
'In particular,
in Sumy direction, 10 settlements and more than 250 km² of territory are liberated.
We press on with offensive operations to liberate four more settlements,' the Commander of the Sever Group of Forces reported.
