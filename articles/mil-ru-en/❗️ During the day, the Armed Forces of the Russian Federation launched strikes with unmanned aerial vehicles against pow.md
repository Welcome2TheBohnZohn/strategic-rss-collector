During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched strikes
with unmanned aerial vehicles against power facilities, logistics and port infrastructure, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
▪️
a logistics centre in the village of Gorodnya in Chernigov region,
which stored and distributed military cargo to the AFU;
▪️
the Slobozhanskaya combined heat and power plant
in Kharkov region, which provides operation of Ukrainian defence industry enterprises;
▪️
a dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo to the AFU, as well as port infrastructure reassigned for the unloading of military cargo
in the port of Chernomorsk in Odessa region.
🔥
In addition,
a railway locomotive
used for the transport of goods to support the AFU
was hit close to the Ryadovoye railway station of Dnepropetrovsk region.
