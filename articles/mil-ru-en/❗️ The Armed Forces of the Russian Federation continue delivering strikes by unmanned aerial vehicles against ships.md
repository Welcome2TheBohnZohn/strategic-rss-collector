# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against ships

Published: 2026-11-06 14:35:21

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30470

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against ships
used to deliver military-purpose cargo to the AFU.
💥
Today, as a result of a strike in the Black Sea waters south-west of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military equipment and dual-use cargo to Ukraine
was hit.
