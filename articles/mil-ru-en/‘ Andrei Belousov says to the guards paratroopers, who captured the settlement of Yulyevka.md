Andrei Belousov says to the guards paratroopers, who captured the settlement of Yulyevka:
this victory continues the heroic path and glorious traditions of the Airborne Troops
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov congratulated the command and personnel of the 328th Guards Air Assault Regiment on the liberation of the settlement of Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
‘
The paratroopers, displaying courage and valor
in difficult battles with the enemy in Orekhov direction,
continue to build upon the glorious traditions of the heroes and soldiers of past years
,’ said the Russian Defence Minister.
🤝
Andrei Belousov thanked the servicemen for the successful accomplishment of combat tasks and their fidelity to military duty,
and also expressed confidence that the personnel of the unit will continue to serve Russia with honour and reliably ensure its security.
