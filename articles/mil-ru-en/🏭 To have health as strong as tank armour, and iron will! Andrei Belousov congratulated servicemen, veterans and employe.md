# 🏭 To have health as strong as tank armour, and iron will! Andrei Belousov congratulated servicemen, veterans and employees of defence enterprises on the Tankman day

Published: 2026-09-13 06:01:55

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29363

To have health as strong as tank armour, and iron will!
Andrei Belousov congratulated servicemen, veterans and employees of defence enterprises on the Tankman day
‘Tankmen have written many heroic pages into our military history, always demonstrating unparalleled courage, steadfastness and mutual support. These qualities, along with professionalism and a sense of responsibility for Russia's fate, enable them to operate successfully during the special military operation," noted the Defence Minister.
🏭
Andrei Belousov stressed that
scientists, designers, engineers and workers of tank-building plants,
who create formidable combat vehicles,
help tank crews reliably carry out combat missions.
Thanks to them,
tanks remain an effective weapon on the battlefield today and are not being retired.
▫️
The Defence Minister wished today's tank crews
to multiply the glorious traditions of their predecessors,
hone their military skills, and stand guard over Russia's sovereignty.
🤝
Thank
you for your courage, and for your faithful and heroic service!
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
