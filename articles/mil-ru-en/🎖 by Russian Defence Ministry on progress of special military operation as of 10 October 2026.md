💥
The Tsentr Group's units inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two national guard brigades near Starorayskoye, Roganskoye, Belozerskoye, Sergeyevka, and Veselaya Gora (Donetsk People's Republic).
▫️
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), the 51st Army's assault groups have advanced in the central and northern-western parts of the city. Marine units continued the offensive in the western part of the city and continued to destroy the enemy on the territory of the Dobropolskaya mining plant. Two enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
🔥
Over the past 24 hours, 82 buildings, including four workshops, have been liberated, more than 35 troops and two armoured fighting vehicles have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, Ukrainian armed formations sustained losses of up to 525 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, five artillery guns, and one electronic warfare station in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of one air assault brigade, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Ivanovka, Pokrovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Nikolskoye, Nikolay-Pole, Volnyanka, and Zalivnoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 275 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and seven motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Preobrazhenka and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 15 troops, 12 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
power industry facilities used in the interests of Ukraine's defence industry enterprises, production workshops of UAVs and their depots, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries in 151 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
two U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles and 725 fixed-wing UAVs.
