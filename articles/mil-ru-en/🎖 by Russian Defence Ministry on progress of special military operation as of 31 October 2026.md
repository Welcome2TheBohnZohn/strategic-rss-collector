↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
The fire damage was inflicted on manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, one assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Sergeyevka, Barvinovka, Vasilevskoye, Tersyanka, and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 190 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
They inflicted losses on units of one AFU mountain assault brigade and one territorial defence brigade near Orekhov, Yurkovka, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 20 Ukrainian troops and five motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, storage and launch sites of unmanned aerial vehicles, unmanned ground systems, ammunition depots, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries in 148 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
one guided aerial bomb, four Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, two Neptune long-range guided missiles, and 604 fixed-wing UAVs.
