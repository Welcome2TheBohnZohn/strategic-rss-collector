Published: 2026-09-09 16:47:33

the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to deliver group strikes
against military industrial facilities, Ukrainian seaports and ships, as well as logistics hubs used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result
of strikes employing high-precision air-based weapons and strike unmanned aerial vehicles, the following targets
were hit
:

▫️
the Delivery logistics centre
, used for storing and distributing military cargo;
▫️
the Nibulon logistics centre
, used for storing equipment intended to support the AFU;
▫️
a UAV storage depot
on the territory of Conveyrmash.
📍
in the port of Nikolayev
,
storage facilities with military equipment and fuel and lubricant tanks
intended to support the AFU were struck on the territory of the Nika Tera cargo terminal.

escorting ships into the port of Odessa was hit.
