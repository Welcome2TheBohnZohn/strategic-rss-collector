Neutralising mock enemy: Northern Fleet confirms readiness to any scenario
📍
Severomorsk, Murmansk region
🚁
The special exercise took place in two stages: the military personnel
prepared the airfield to receive aircraft,
and then
helicopters took off from the launch site to destroy the mock enemy.
⚡️
At the same time,
units exercised air and ground defence of the airfield and deployed an auxiliary command post.
📊
In total,
more than 750 servicemen, over 15 aircraft, and 16 units of special vehicles
were deployed during the exercise.
