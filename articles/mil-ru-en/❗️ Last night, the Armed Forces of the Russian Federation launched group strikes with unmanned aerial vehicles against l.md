Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched group strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres and ports of Ukraine, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
The following targets
were engaged:
📍
in Odessa region:
a logistics centre
in Velikaya Balka, which stored and distributed military cargo supplied to Ukraine from abroad;
📍
in the port of Chernomorsk,
a dry cargo ship and a Ro-Ro ship
delivering cargo to the AFU.
🔥
In addition,
strikes against railway infrastructure facilities
used to transport military cargo from European countries
have been continued
in western regions of Ukraine.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
