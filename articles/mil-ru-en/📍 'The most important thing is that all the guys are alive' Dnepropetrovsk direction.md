# 📍 'The most important thing is that all the guys are alive' Dnepropetrovsk direction

Published: 2026-11-17 16:45:31

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30656

'The most important thing is that all the guys are alive'
📍
Dnepropetrovsk direction
➡️
A mobile medical group of the 341st Separate Medical Battalion of the Tsentr Group of Forces operates
close to the front line. It is a high-tech field hub where wounded servicemen are provided with qualified medical care.
📌
A key advantage of this location is the ability to comply with the rule of the 'golden hour'.
This is time of high importance after the wound, when doctors' actions are most effective.
💬
Battalion commander Alfir Talipov emphasises that it is the cohesion and precise work of the medics on the front line that makes it possible to rescue the servicemen:
'At the following stages, medical equipment is improved, and manpower is developed. The experience is great. Sometimes, wounded patients arrive and don't need anything redone because everything was properly handled at the previous stage. We take care, give food and water, stabilise, and send'.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
