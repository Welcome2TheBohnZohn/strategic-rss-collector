The assault company's commander Ruslan Valeyev

of
Gruzskoye

As the officer noted,
the most difficult part wasn't to assault the settlement but to approach it:
‘There are a lot of open spaces like fields, burnt forest belts. We had to perform tricks of all kinds’.
Also, Ruslan said, that
realising the resistance is useless, the most of Ukrainian servicemen preferred to surrender
rather than retreat:
‘It's safer for them in our captivity. As they tell, it's better for them to surrender than to return to their units’.
