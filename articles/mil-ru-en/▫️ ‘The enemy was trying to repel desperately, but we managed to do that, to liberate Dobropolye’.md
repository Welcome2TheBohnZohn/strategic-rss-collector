# ▫️ ‘The enemy was trying to repel desperately, but we managed to do that, to liberate Dobropolye’

Published: 2026-10-21 18:58:02

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30203

▫️
‘The enemy was trying to repel desperately, but we managed to do that, to liberate Dobropolye’
💬
Servicemen of the 51st Combined Arms Army (the Tsentr Group of Forces)
revealed new details about the liberation of Dobropolye in the Donetsk People's Republic.
🤝
The success of the operation was the result of the Russian Army's coordinated actions:
🔥
The fire support of the 114th Brigade's artillery division
created conditions for assault groups to advance.
Artillerymen promptly suppressed the enemy's firing positions, covered the manoeuvres, and prohibited the AFU's units to regroup.
⚡️
The 80th Battalion's reconnaissance groups
laid the foundation for planning the entire operation.
They revealed the enemy's resistance points and escape routes. The continuous flow of reconnaissance data allowed the command to make decisions quickly.
🎮
The 56th Special Battalion actively
used UAVs to control the situation.
They were looking for routes, spotted ambushes, and adjusted assault detachments. This helped to cover our positions and maintain the offensive actions' high pace.
