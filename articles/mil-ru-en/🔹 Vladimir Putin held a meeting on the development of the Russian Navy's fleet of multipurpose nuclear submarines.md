# 🔹 Vladimir Putin held a meeting on the development of the Russian Navy's fleet of multipurpose nuclear submarines

Published: 2026-11-27 18:57:23

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30817

Vladimir Putin held a meeting on the development of the Russian Navy's fleet of multipurpose nuclear submarines
In today's environment, the Navy plays a crucial role in ensuring Russia's sovereignty and security and protecting our national interests. All its components, be it surface vessels and submarines or coastal units and infrastructure, must be ready to repel seaborne threats and possess significant firepower in order to effectively counter enemy naval groups and strike their strategic targets.
In addition, the President outlined the key objectives in developing the submarine force:
🔹
modernising and expanding production capacities as well as restoring and improving critical competencies and human resources;
🔹
building new submarines of various types and, crucially, improving their weapons, primarily by consistently enhancing the performance characteristics of long-range precision weaponry and anti-ship missiles;
🔹
using the funding allocated by the government for the development of the Navy rationally via focusing on priority areas and enlisting the cooperation of small and medium high-tech companies.
