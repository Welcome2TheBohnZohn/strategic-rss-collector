# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out group strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of the Ukrainian defence and fuel-energy industr

Published: 2026-09-23 06:15:44

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29563

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out group strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against enterprises of the Ukrainian defence and fuel-energy industries, logistics centres, and sea vessels operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
As a result of the strikes, the following targets were hit:
📍
in Kiev and Kiev region:
▫️
a depot storing materiel
for the production of unmanned aerial vehicles;
▫️
an industrial enterprise
(LLC Tucans) in Vishnevoye, which produced components for unmanned aerial vehicles;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
three logistics centres
in Odessa storing and distributing military and dual-use cargo to support the AFU;
▫️
a depot storing UAV components
in Mirnoye, Odessa region.
💥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo to the port of Odessa was destroyed at sea.

Russian Defence Ministry
