# . Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov summed up the results of the meeting of the CIS Council of Defence Ministers

Published: 2026-10-15 12:03:03

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30063

Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov summed up the results of the meeting of the CIS Council of Defence Ministers
.
'
Great attention was given to the organisation of operational and combat training of troops. This area of interaction was the main topic of our meeting,' said Andrei Belousov.
▫️
The head of the Russian military department noted that, within the framework of the meeting, the practice of planning combat training was discussed and priorities for this work in modern conditions were identified, and the ways and methods of employing unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as robotic systems, in modern combat operations were analysed.
'Directions have been identified for introducing the combat experience of the special military operation into the training of troops,' he added.
📈
The meeting considered
issues of enhancing the combat capability potential of troops
and conducting joint military exercises.
'Following the results of the meeting, decisions were signed aimed at further strengthening cooperation and enhancing the combat capabilities of the national armed forces,' said Andrei Belousov.
🤝
In accordance with the agenda of the meeting,
a set of issues was considered aimed at further enhancing the combat capabilities of the armies of the CIS states,
as well as developing interaction in the military sphere. The results and priorities of joint activities in military cooperation in the interests of Eurasian and regional security were analysed, taking into account the international situation.
'The Council approved the results and proposals for the development of joint projects in the field of military education and military sports, as well as the planning and organisation of events in the military sphere. The work plan of the Defence Ministers Council for 2027 has been approved,' noted the Russian Defence Minister.
✅
In his assessment, 'the meeting once again confirmed
the relevance and demand for developing cooperation within the format of the Council of Defence Ministers
to maintain stability within the Commonwealth'.
