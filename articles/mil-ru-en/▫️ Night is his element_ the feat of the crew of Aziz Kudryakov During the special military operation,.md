Night is his element: the feat of the crew of Aziz Kudryakov
▫️
During the special military operation,
his crew carried out 437 sorties,
most of them under cover of darkness. Kudryakov destroyed the enemy's tanks and SAM systems, demonstrating the highest professionalism.
🚁
The last manoeuvre:
defeating the enemy in Kursk border, the crew of the commander, Aziz Kudryakov, and the navigation officer, Mikhail Lazis, used all ammunition during the flight.
When they were on their way to the base, they saw an enemy drone rushing into the residential sector, and they decided to perform ram manoeuver.
▫️
The crew sacrificed itself to save hundreds of civilians from death.
▫️
By presidential decree,
the commander of the crew, Aziz Kudryakov, was awarded the title of Hero of the Russian Federation (postmortem).
The navigation officer Mikhail Lazis was awarded the Order for Courage (postmortem).
