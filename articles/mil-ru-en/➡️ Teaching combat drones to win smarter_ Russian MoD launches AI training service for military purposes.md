Teaching combat drones to win smarter:
Russian MoD launches AI training service for military purposes
The Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation has created a service providing developers of unmanned systems with access to objective control data from the combat employment of drones, aimed at training military-purpose neural networks.
➡️
The new service provides developers of unmanned systems with access to a specialised
storage facility offering prepared video footage showing the employment of FPV drones
, as well as other copters and fixed-wing UAVs.
'More than 40 organisations involved in training neural networks, including for integration into their own combat unmanned systems, are currently connected to the service,' noted Russian Deputy Defence Minister Dmitriy Shcherbinin, under whose leadership the project is being implemented.
✅
Specialists of the Russian Ministry of Defence have also
created a unified data recognition classifier for AI machine learning
, allowing developers to work with the dataset efficiently, in a structured manner and according to a common concept.
📄
The results of the work by military unmanned systems developers are uploaded back into the service
, where the information is checked for completeness and compliance with the classifier format, allowing the Russian Ministry of Defence to use this dataset to create and train its own neural networks.
📈
This approach makes it possible to enhance the
resilience and adaptability of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation to modern challenges
in the field of artificial intelligence.
🔒
The service is provided with information security
based on certified information protection tools using modern cryptography and authentication means.
