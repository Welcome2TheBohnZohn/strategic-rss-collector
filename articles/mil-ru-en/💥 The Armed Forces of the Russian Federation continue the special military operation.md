# 💥 The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-09-24 11:32:09

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29589

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
💥
Units of the Sever Group of Forces continue to tighten the encirclement and expand the external control zone in Kharkov region.
🚩
In the encirclement, as a result of active actions, Buzovo and Khizhnyakovo (Kharkov region)
have been taken under control. The combat actions are underway for the liberation of Rublenoye and Khripuny (Kharkov region). Strikes were delivered at enemy formations close to Varvarovka and Dolgenkoye (Kharkov region).
▪️
Over 40 servicemen and two armoured fighting vehicles have been neutralised during the day.
💥
Combat actions are underway
to expand the external control zone and to liberate Petropavlovka, Prikolotnoye, and Lozovaya (Kharkov region).
🔥
In addition, in Kharkov region, strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two territorial defence brigades close to Pitomnik, Izbitskoye, Berezhnoye, and Velikiy Burluk (Kharkov region).
💥
In Sumy region
, strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of an airomobile brigade, a mechanised brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Doroshovka, Grunovka, Ivolzhanskoye, Stepok (Sumy region), and the city of Sumy.
▪️
In total, up to 225 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, one self-propelled artillery system, and 30 motor vehicles have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Sever Group of Forces.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line
. Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Grushevka, Chervony Oskol (Kharkov region), Rubtsy, and Mayaki (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 230 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 16 motor vehicles, and two field artillery guns.
↗️
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Formations of three mechanised brigades, an airmobile brigade, a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade have been hit near Kramatorsk, Novoselovka, Belenkoye, Druzhkovka, and Slavyansk (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy has lost more than 175 troops, two tanks including a German-made Leopard tank, two Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, and 23 motor vehicles.
↗️
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation
. Russian troops engaged manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two national guard brigades close to Mezhevaya, Fedorovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Blagodat, Gornyak, Belozerskoye, Aleksandrovka, and Andreyevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
🚩
Near Dobropolye, the 51st Army units
expanded the outer ring of the encirclement and liberated Dobropolye (Maloye) of the Donetsk People's Republic.
💥
Russian troops repelled
the 4th and 15th National Guard brigades' attempts to break through the encirclement towards Svyatogorovka of the Donetsk People's Republic
▪️
More than 45 Ukrainian servicemen have been neutralised and 152 buildings have been mopped up.
▪️
In total, more than 375 troops, one tank, one armoured fighting vehicle, seven motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
