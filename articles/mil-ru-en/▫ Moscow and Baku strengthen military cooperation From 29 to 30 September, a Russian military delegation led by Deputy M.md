Moscow and Baku strengthen military cooperation
From 29 to 30 September,
a Russian military delegation led by Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Vasily Osmakov made a visit to the Republic of Azerbaijan.
▫
As part of the visit, the delegation took part in the opening ceremony of the ADEX 2026 international defence exhibition,
visited the Russian pavilion displaying military products; and talks were held between Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Vasily Osmakov and the Minister of Defence of the Republic of Azerbaijan Colonel General Zakir Hasanov.
▫️
During the meeting, the current state of bilateral military cooperation and the prospects for placing it on a comprehensive medium-term footing were discussed, and
agreements were reached on continuing the exchange of experience in the organisation of combat training, as well as in the fields of military education and military science.
▫️
The talks took place in a friendly and constructive atmosphere, and their results confirmed a mutual interest in deepening cooperation in the defence sphere
in accordance with the Declaration on Allied Interaction between the Russian Federation and the Republic of Azerbaijan dated 22 February 2022.
