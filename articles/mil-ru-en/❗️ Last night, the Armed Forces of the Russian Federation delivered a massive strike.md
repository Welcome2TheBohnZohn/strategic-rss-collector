# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike

Published: 2026-09-12 08:30:48

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29350

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike
against Ukrainian metals industry enterprises producing military-purpose products, in the cities of Zaporozhye, Dnepropetrovsk and Krivoy Rog, as well as against port infrastructure facilities in Odessa region through which cargo for the AFU is delivered, and maritime vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of strikes
employing high-precision air-, ground- and sea-based weapons and strike unmanned aerial vehicles, the following targets
were hit
:
📍
in Zaporozhye region:
▪️
the Zaporozhstal steel plant
(PJSC Zaporozhstal) in Zaporozhye, one of the main suppliers of sheet metal rolled stock for protective structures and armour for military hardware, as well as plates for body armour;
📍
in Dnepropetrovsk region:
▪️
a steel plant
in Krivoy Rog producing metallurgical products for the manufacture of weapons and military hardware;
▪️
the innovative electric smelting complex
of the Interpipe Steel metallurgical plant in Dnepropetrovsk, Ukraine's largest producer and supplier of rolled metal for the manufacture of military-purpose products;
▪️
the Severny and Novokrivorozhsky mining and processing plants
in Krivoy Rog, which carry out the extraction, beneficiation and agglomeration of iron ore for the production of metallurgical products used in military manufacturing;
📍
in the port of Chernomorsk in Odessa region:
▪️
port infrastructure facilities
used for the unloading and storage of military-purpose equipment,
two dry cargo ships
that delivered armaments and military hardware for the AFU, and
a sea tugboat
.
