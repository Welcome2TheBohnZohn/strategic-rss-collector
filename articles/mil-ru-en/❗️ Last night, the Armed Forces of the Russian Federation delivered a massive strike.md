# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike

Published: 2026-11-03 08:07:25

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30411

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike
by high-precision ground- and air-based weapons and strike unmanned aerial vehicles against power generation facilities of Ukraine in Vinnitsy, Dnepropetrovsk and Kharkov regions that supply electricity to defence industry enterprises and AFU facilities, as well as port infrastructure facilities in Odessa region used for military purposes.
📍
In Vinnitsy region, the following target was hit:
▫️
Ladyzhinskaya thermal power plant
, with a capacity of 1,800 MW.
📍
In Dnepropetrovsk region, the following targets were hit:
▫️
Krivorozhskaya thermal power plant
, with a capacity of 2,800 MW;
▫️
Pridneprovskaya thermal power plant,
with a capacity of 1,765 MW.
📍
In Kharkov region, the following targets were hit:
▫️
Kharkovskaya thermal power plant
, with a capacity of 86 MW;
▫️
Zmievskaya electrical substation
, with a capacity of 330 kV;
▫️
Losevo electrical substation
, with a capacity of 330 kV.
📍
In Odessa region, the following targets were hit:
▫️
at the port of Kiliya – port infrastructure facilities
used in the interests of the AFU;
▫️
at the port of Yuzhny and in the Black Sea waters – two dry cargo ships
delivering military equipment and dual-use cargo for the AFU.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
