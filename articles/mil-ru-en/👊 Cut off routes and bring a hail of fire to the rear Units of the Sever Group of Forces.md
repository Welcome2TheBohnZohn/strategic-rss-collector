Cut off routes and bring a hail of fire to the rear
Units of the Sever Group of Forces
continue to steadily tighten the noose around enemy units in Kharkov region.
The senior gunner of the Giatsint-S self-propelled gun with the call sign 'Koshchey' told how his crew is isolating the combat operations area and suppressing all AFU attempts to break through the encirclement.
'Our target is a cluster of enemy manpower. We assist our assault detachments, cut off the enemy's way out and rotation routes, as well as any opportunity to break through the encirclement in Kharkov direction. We can easily bring a hail of fire to the enemy's rear. In this way we destroy enemy logistics, transportation of hardware and personnel'.
