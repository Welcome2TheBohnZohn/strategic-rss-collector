🚩
As a result of resolute actions, the Tsentr Group of Forces
liberated Novotroitskoye, Petrovskoye,
and
Lidino
in the Donetsk People's Republic.
💥
Russian units hit formations
of four mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an airborne brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades.
▪️
In total, more than 2,740 troops, 37 armoured fighting vehicles, 76 motor vehicles, 11 field artillery guns, and 11 electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
🚩
Over the past week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces have continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence,
liberated Nikolskoye
in Zaporozhye region.
🔥
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade, three air assault brigades, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two marine brigades.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,695 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, 53 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
Last week, the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Up to 240 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 116 motor vehicles, five artillery guns, and nine electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, two U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, four Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, a Neptune long-range guided missile, and 5,695 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
