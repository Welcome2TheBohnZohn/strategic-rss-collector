# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to engage infrastructure facilities in Ukraine and vessels involved in supporting the AFU.

Published: 2026-10-03 07:00:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29793

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to engage infrastructure facilities in Ukraine and vessels involved in supporting the AFU.
💥
Last night, unmanned aerial vehicles
hit the following targets
:
▫️
in Kiev,
the Severny Road Bridge
across the Dnepr River. It facilitates deployment of troops and logistics in the delivery of military goods for the AFU;
▫️
in Kiev region,
the Gostomel logistics centre
, where materiel used to supply the AFU is stored.
💥
Moreover, in the north-western part of the Black Sea,
a dry cargo ship was hit
. The vessel was delivering dual-use goods to the port of Odessa intended to support the AFU.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
