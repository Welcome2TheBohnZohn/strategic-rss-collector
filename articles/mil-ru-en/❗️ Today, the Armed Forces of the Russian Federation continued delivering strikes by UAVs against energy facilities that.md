Today,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes
by UAVs against energy facilities that support the operation of defence industry enterprises and logistics centres of Ukraine, as well as port infrastructure and ships involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
🔥
As a result of the strike, the
PS-330 Novokievskaya electrical substation
in Khotov (Kiev region) and
the PS-330 Zhitomirskaya electrical substation
in Zhitomir were hit.
💥
In addition,
two dry cargo ships
delivering cargo to the ports of Odessa and Chernomorsk for the AFU were hit.
