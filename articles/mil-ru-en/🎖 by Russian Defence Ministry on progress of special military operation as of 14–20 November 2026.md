💥
During the week, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an airborne brigade, an air assault brigade, an assault brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, three marine brigades, and four national guard brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,375 troops, two tanks, 42 armoured fighting vehicles, 51 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and 16 electronic and counter-fire warfare stations.
🚩
Last week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depth of the enemy's defence. As a result of resolute actions,
the settlements of Tersyanka and Zalivnoye in Zaporozhnoye region were liberated.
💥
Russian troops inflicted damage on
manpower and military hardware of a mechanised brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade.
▪️
In total, more than 1,360 servicemen, 11 armoured fighting vehicles, 46 motor vehicles, and three artillery guns were neutralised in the Vostok Group's area of responsibility.
📍
Over the past week, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian troops delivered strikes at formations of two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, an assault brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade.
▪️
Up to 145 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 119 motor vehicles, and four electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
🎯
Air defence systems shot down
11 guided aerial bombs, 13 Flamingo cruise missiles, eight Neptune long-range cruise missiles, two U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 6,808 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
