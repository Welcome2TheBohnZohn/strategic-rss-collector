📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at formations of three mechanised brigades of the AFU, one marine brigade, one national guard brigade, and one special operations brigade near Shilovka, Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Yuryevka (Donetsk People's Republic), and Novotroitskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 460 troops, four armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
The Group's elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Mikhaylovskoye, Obshcheye, Voskresenka, Slavnoye (Zaporozhye region), Otrishki, Kolomiytsy, and Pavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 290 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, six motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
🚩
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions and liberated Novoandreyevka in Zaporozhye region.
💥
Strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the AFU and one territorial defence brigade near Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, Yurkovka, and Balabino (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, one artillery gun, 26 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on Ukrainian logistics centres, power and transport infrastructure, assembly and storage facilities of unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
four guided aerial bombs, four U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, two Neptune long-range guided missiles, and 1,025 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet destroyed one uncrewed surface vehicle of the Ukrainian Navy in the Black Sea waters.
