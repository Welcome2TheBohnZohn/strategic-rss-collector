Olkhovatka is under our control: in close-quarters combat assaultmen did all the best
Deputy company CO with the call sign 'Tark'
shared details about the liberation of
Olkhovatka
in Kharkov region.
'Tark' says that clear coordination from the air helped to advance:
The drone pilots adjust us from the air: where to go, where to bypass, where the enemy is, how to avoid him, how take a position'.
The CO also noted the superiority of our servicemen in close-quarters combat:
'As we mopped up one of the buildings, three of our fighters clashed with five of the enemy. In the course of the battle, three Ukrainian militants were neutralised, two of them escaped'.
