# ❗️ Throughout the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics and storage terminals used to support the AFU 

Published: 2026-09-24 16:03:49

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29594

❗️
Throughout the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics and storage terminals used to support the AFU as well as vessels involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following
targets in Odessa region were hit:
▫️
a logistics centre in Odessa, responsible for storing and distributing military supplies arriving in Ukraine from European states;
▫️
a storage facility for unmanned aerial vehicles in Novye Belyary of Odessa region.
💥
Moreover, a dry cargo ship transporting military equipment to the port of Odessa in order to support the AFU was hit while at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
