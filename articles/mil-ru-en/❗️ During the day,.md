# ❗️ During the day,

Published: 2026-08-27 16:35:22

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29075

❗️
During the day,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to deliver group strikes
against Ukrainian defence industry enterprises, logistics centres as well as ports and vessels that used to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result, the following targets
were hit
by strike unmanned aerial vehicles:
📍
In Kiev region:
▪️
an industrial enterprise in Velikaya Aleksandrovka (Ukrdefence Company LLC) producing the Lyut reconnaissance and unmanned ground vehicles and Khizhak remote-controlled ground modules;
▪️
a transport and logistics centre in Brovary (Raben Ukraine transport and logistics complex), used for the storage and distribution of components for UAVs of various types including fixed-wing drones.
📍
In Odessa region:
▪️
in the port of Izmail, a tanker unloading POLs delivered to Ukraine as well as two fuel storage tanks intended for the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▪️
in the port of Odessa, seven storage facilities containing military equipment intended to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
▪️
In addition, in the port of Odessa, an unmanned aerial vehicles workshop was hit.
