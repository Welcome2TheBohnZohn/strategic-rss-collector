↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, three air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Barvinovka, Vasilevskoye, Zalivnoye, Novorozovka, and Sofiyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 200 troops and five motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of two mechanised brigades of the AFU were hit near Kamyshevakha, Orekhov, and Preobrazhenka of Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 14 motor vehicles, and six electronic warfare stations of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on transport infrastructure facilities used by the AFU, production workshops, and storage areas of ground robotic systems and unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 153 areas.

972 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
