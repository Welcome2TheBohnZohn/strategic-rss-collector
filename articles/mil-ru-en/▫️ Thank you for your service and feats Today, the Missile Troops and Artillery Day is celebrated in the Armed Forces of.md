# ▫️ Thank you for your service and feats Today, the Missile Troops and Artillery Day is celebrated in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation.

Published: 2026-11-19 08:15:19

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30678

Thank you for your service and feats
▫️
📆
Today, the Missile Troops and Artillery Day is celebrated in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation.
💬
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov
congratulated the servicemen and veterans
, noting that artillerymen and missilemen have written many bright pages into the history of our great power.
‘Today, the personnel of the Missile Troops and Artillery selflessly perform their military duty, effectively carry out responsible tasks to deliver fire damage to the enemy, destroy its equipment and manpower, conduct counter-battery warfare, and make an invaluable contribution to strengthening the country's defence capability during the special military operation’.
▫️
Andrei Belousov expressed confidence that
‘in the future, the servicemen of the Missile Troops and Artillery will continue to raise their professional level, successfully master new models of weapons and equipment, and reliably ensure the sovereignty and strategic security of the state’.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
