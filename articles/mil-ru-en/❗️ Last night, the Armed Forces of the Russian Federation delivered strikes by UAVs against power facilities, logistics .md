Published: 2026-11-23 06:25:21

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30740

❗️
Last night,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
delivered strikes by UAVs against power facilities, logistics and port infrastructure, as well as ships used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit
:
▫️
a 330 kV electrical substation
in Dnepropetrovsk, which supports the operation of Ukraine's defence industry enterprises;
▫️
port infrastructure facilities
involved in the delivery of military equipment for the Ukrainian armed forces in the port of Izmail (Odessa region);
▫️
a dry cargo ship
, which delivered military-purpose cargo, was hit at sea south-west of Odessa.
🔥
In addition,
two railway locomotives
used for transporting armament and military hardware for the AFU were hit near the Dneprodzerzhinsk railway station in Dnepropetrovsk region.
