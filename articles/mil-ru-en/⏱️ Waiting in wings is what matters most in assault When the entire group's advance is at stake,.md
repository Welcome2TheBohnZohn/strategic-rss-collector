Waiting in wings is what matters most in assault
⏱️
When the entire group's advance is at stake,
it is necessary to act wisely.
During the liberation of
Mirovka
, the servicemen had to perform a ruse:
‘We moved in groups of three, the distance was great. Enemy drones were working along the way, somewhere we had to spend the night in forest belts. We reached assigned positions. There were two small strongholds.
As soon as night was coming, we mopped up and established control over them, so that other Russian assaultmen could advance.’

As a result of cohesive actions, the assault detachments established control over a powerful defensive area more than 10 square kilometres, including Mirovka itself.
