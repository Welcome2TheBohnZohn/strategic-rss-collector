Russia–Kazakhstan military cooperation dynamically develops — Andrei Belousov
Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov held a meeting with Minister of Defence of the Republic of Kazakhstan Aidos Myrzakhmetov as part of a working visit to Kazakhstan.
💬
During the meeting, the heads of defence departments of Russia and Kazakhstan
discussed topical issues of bilateral military cooperation.
'The Republic of Kazakhstan is our time-tested friend, good neighbour, and a CSTO ally. Russian-Kazakhstan military cooperation is dynamically developing. This is confirmed by the increase in the number of events, the emergence of new directions and fields of interaction,' the Russian Defence Minister said.
❗️
The Russian Defence Minister noted that in the course of bilateral military cooperation,
particular attention is paid to operational and combat training of troops. 'Military personnel training is carried out and we regularly hold joint consultations on a wide range of military activities,'
the Russian Defence Minister stated.
'I would like to express my gratitude for the assistance in resolving issues related to the functioning of the Russian training grounds. This makes it possible to develop weapons to ensure the security of our countries,' Andrei Belousov added.
➡️
The Russian Defence Minister noted that
a key priority of interaction is military-technical cooperation.
He also stressed that the
parties regularly hold talks on the coordination of defence policy.
🤝
In turn,
Aidos Myrzakhmetov stated that the Ministry of Defence of Kazakhstan gives particular importance to the development of relations with the Russian military department.
He congratulated the Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation and the Russian delegation on the holiday of the Day of National Unity.
✅
At the end of the meeting,
the plan for bilateral cooperation
between the Russian and Kazakhstan defence ministries for 2027
was approved.
