🚩
As a result of resolute actions, the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Novotroitskoye and Petrovskoye in the Donetsk People's Republic
.
💥
Russian troops
engaged manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Novoyavlenka, Roganskoye, Svyatogorovka, Belozerskoye, Veselaya Gora (Donetsk People's Republic), Demurino, and Tarasovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
⚡️
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic)
, the 51st Army's assault groups have advanced in the central and northern-western parts of the city. Marine units continued to mop up the western quarters of the city and the territory of the Doborpolskaya mine. An enemy's attempt to unblock the AFU surrendered units by a force of up to a platoon with the support of armoured vehicles was thwarted.
📊
Over the past 24 hours, 137 buildings
, including three production facilities, have been liberated. Up to 30 troops, two armoured vehicles, and one pickup truck have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 495 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, and four electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence
. Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an assault brigade, three air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Orestopol, Ivanovka, Velikomikhaylovka, Skotovatoye, Medichnoye in Dnepropetrovsk region, Volnyanka, and Mirolyubovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 230 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, seven motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions
. Russian element hit formations of a mechanised brigade, a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a territorial defence brigade close to Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, and Novoaleksandrovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 50 troops, 23 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on the transport infrastructure and energy facilities of Ukraine used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the production workshops of unmanned aerial vehicles and their launching sites as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 147 areas.
▫️

two guided aerial bombs and 909 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
