Battle
banner presented to Chelyabinsk Higher Tank Command School
🤝
Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation - Chief of the Main Military and Political Directorate of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, General of the Army Viktor Goremykin
presented the Battle banner
to the Chief of the Chelyabinsk Higher Tank Command School, which was re-established in 2026.
🚩
In addition,
the historical banners
of the Chelyabinsk Technician Tank School and the Higher Tank Command School given in 1944 and 1968
were presented
for School's exhibition.
❗️
The expansion of the higher military training institutions network
is carried out as a part of the task of military education system modernising,
which was assigned by the Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov during an expanded session of the Defence Ministry Board in December 2024.
💬
General of the Army Viktor Goremykin stressed
that the revival of
the Chelyabinsk Higher Tank Command School
is not just an opening of another one training institution, but the return of the officer's school and the special military training system.
The deputy head of the Russian Defence Ministry expressed confidence that the Battle banner of the restored Chelyabinsk Tank School will be a reliable bridge between the generations of cadets and officers, which will inspire professional skills and strengthen the comradeship.
