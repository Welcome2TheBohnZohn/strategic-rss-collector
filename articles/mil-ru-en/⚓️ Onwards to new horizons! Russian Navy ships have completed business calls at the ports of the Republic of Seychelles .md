# ⚓️ Onwards to new horizons! Russian Navy ships have completed business calls at the ports of the Republic of Seychelles and the Republic of Cameroon.

Published: 2026-11-06 13:42:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30469

Onwards to new horizons!
Russian Navy ships have completed business calls at the ports of the Republic of Seychelles and the Republic of Cameroon.
⚓️
The Pacific Fleet's frigate Marshal Shaposhnikov
completed her stay programme in the port of Victoria, Republic of Seychelles
, and went to sea.
▫️
The key event of the visit
was the first Russian-Seychellois naval anti-piracy exercise.
➡️
At the same time, the Baltic Fleet's training ship Perekop, carrying out long-range navigation tasks since the beginning of July,
completed her stay programme in the port of Douala, Republic of Cameroon.
▫️
Upon leaving port, the ship's crew
practised joint manoeuvring elements with the Cameroon Navy patrol boat P-108.
