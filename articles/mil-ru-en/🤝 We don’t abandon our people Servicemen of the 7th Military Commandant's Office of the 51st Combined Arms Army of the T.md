Published: 2026-10-15 18:32:31

We
don’t abandon our people
🤝
Servicemen of the 7th Military Commandant's Office of the 51st Combined Arms Army of the Tsentr Group
continue to evacuate civilians of Krasnoarmeysk and other liberated settlements of the DPR
and provide everything necessary to them.
One of those evacuated is an elderly resident of Krasnoarmeysk
, Aleksandr Priborenko. His wife was killed by a Ukrainian drone strike, and he himself was left without a home, but he managed to save a wounded Russian assault trooper with the call sign 'Bars'. The pensioner hid the barely conscious soldier in his cellar. Later, the assault trooper was evacuated by his comrades-in-arms, and Aleksander Vladimirovich decided to leave for Leningrad region. Servicemen of the commandant's office
accompanied him along the entire evacuation route,
protecting him from enemy drones.
