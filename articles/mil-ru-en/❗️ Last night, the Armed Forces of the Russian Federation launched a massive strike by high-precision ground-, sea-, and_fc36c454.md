Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation launched a massive strike by high-precision ground-, sea-, and air-based weapons, and unmanned aerial vehicles to engage Ukrainian defence industry enterprises
in the city of Kiev, and Kiev, Chernigov, Kirovograd, Dnepropetrovsk, Poltava, and Kharkov regions, as well as port and ship repair infrastructure in Odessa region, and sea vessels operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets were hit:
:
📍
in the city of Kiev and Kiev region:
▫️
an industrial enterprise (Smart Ammunition LLC)
in Kiev, which assembles and stores the FP-5 Flamingo Flamingo cruise missiles, FP-9 operational-tactical missiles, as well as their components;
▫️
a UAV assembly and storage site (PJSC Fanplit) in Kiev
were components for FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles were produced along with FP-1 and FP-2 UAVs;
▫️
PJSC Kyiv Cardboard and Paper Mill in Obukhov,
Kiev region, were FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles and their components were manufactured and stored;
📍
in Chernigov region:
▫️
an industrial enterprise (Plastmass-Priluki TH)
in Priluki, which manufactures and stores FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles components;
📍
in Kirovograd region:
▫️
a workshop of Oleksandriya Group of Industrial Enterprises
in Oleksandriya which produces components for long-range and medium-range unmanned aerial vehicles, including fixed-wing aerial vehicles;
📍
in Dnepropetrovsk region:
▫️
a rocket and space company (Production Association Yuzhny Machine-Building Plant named after A.M. Makarov, State Enterprise) in Dnepr,
which manufactures and stores FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles, as well as FP-1 and FP-2 long-range and medium-range unmanned aerial vehicles and supplies them to the AFU;
📍
in Poltava region:
▫️
a production enterprise (PJSC AvtoKrAZ)
in Kremenchug, which manufactures and stores special chassis for FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles;
▫️
JTI Ukraine JSC tobacco plant
in Kremenchug, where production of small arms ammunition intended to meet the needs of the AFU is organised;
📍
in Kharkov region:
▫️
a mechanical plant in Lozovaya,
which assembles and stores FP-5 Flamingo cruise missiles, as well as their components;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
PSC Danube Ship Repair
in the port of Izmail, which manufactures and stores uncrewed surface vehicles and their components;
📍
in the port of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship delivering military and dual-use cargo to support the AFU.
