Main efforts must be focused on learning combat experience!
Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Colonel General Valery Solodchuk congratulated cadets of the Military Academy of Logistical Support named after General of the Army A.V. Khrulyov on the start of new academic year.
Main efforts must be focused on learning combat experience, methods of conducting combat operations, analysing data of the use of new weapons and special military hardware,' noted the Deputy Minister of Defence.
📊
In 2026, more than 2,500 cadets entered the Military Academy of Logistical Support and its branches. It wasn't easy to choose the best ones: the competition grew by 10%, and in total about 6,000 people fought for the right to study here. Among those who managed to enter the Academy there are children of veterans and the participants of the special military operation.
