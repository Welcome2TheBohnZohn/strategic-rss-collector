Development of logistics support for CSTO troops discussed in Saint Petersburg
An international scientific and practical conference was held devoted to the development of logistic and technical support of the Troops (Collective Forces) of the Collective Security Treaty Organisation in the Military Academy of Logistical Support named after General of the Army A.V. Khrulyov of the Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation.
Key issues are:
🔹
development of the Collective Forces' logistic support system until 2040;
🔹
application of experience of modern armed conflicts;
🔹
active implementation of unmanned aerial vehicles and robotic systems to deliver cargoes to the front line.
💬
The main goal is to implement combat experience into the practice of joint exercising and employment of the Collective Forces.
Russian Deputy Defence Minister Colonel General Valery Solodchuk noted:
‘The joint logistics support system must be timely, modern, and advanced.’
❗️
The activities of the CSTO Defence Ministers Council Working Group
are aimed at further developing of the logistics support system and will contribute to enhancing the defence capability
of the armed forces of the CSTO member countries.
