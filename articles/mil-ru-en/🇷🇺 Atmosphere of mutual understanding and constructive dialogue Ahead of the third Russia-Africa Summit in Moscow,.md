Published: 2026-10-26 19:00:46

Atmosphere of mutual understanding and constructive dialogue
🇷🇺
🇨🇬
Ahead of the third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow,
Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation Vasily Osmakov held a working meeting
with Minister of National Defence of the Republic of the Congo Raymond-Zephyren Mbulu.
💬
During the talks, the sides discussed joint
bilateral cooperation projects in the defence sphere
and further plans for their practical implementation. Commitment to developing constructive dialogue and maintaining a high level of interdepartmental partnership was confirmed.
🤝
The meeting was held in an atmosphere of mutual understanding
and demonstrated traditionally friendly character of Russian-Congolese relations.
