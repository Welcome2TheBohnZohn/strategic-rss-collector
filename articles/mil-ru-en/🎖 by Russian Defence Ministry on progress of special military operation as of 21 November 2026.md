↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
The Group's units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, one assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Pokrovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Yasnaya Polyana, Trudovoye, Novotroitskoye, Zheltaya Krucha, Slavnoye, and Shchastlivoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 205 troops and eight motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
They have delivered strikes at formations of one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade and one assault regiment of the AFU near Balabino, Chervony Zhovten, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 20 troops and 13 motor vehicles have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
power and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, production workshops and depots of unmanned aerial vehicles and uncrewed surface vehicles, military airfields, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries in 148 areas, as well as the location of the meeting of the AFU's high-ranking representatives and representatives of the defence industry, including foreign ones.
