During his speech, Vladimir Putin congratulated Russian citizens on Reunification Day, marking the reunification of the Donetsk and Lugansk people's republics and the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions with the Russian Federation on September 30
Exactly four years ago, the Donetsk and Lugansk people's republics and the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions became full-fledged constituent entities of the Russian Federation, returning to the fold of Greater Russia. I am pleased to congratulate all citizens of our country on Unification Day. This holiday has come to embody our people’s unwavering aspiration to restore historical unity and the righteous struggle of the Russian people, the entire country, and the people of Donbass and Novorossiya for freedom and justice, the heroic deeds of the 2014 militias and the heroes of the special military operation who stood up to defend the Motherland and our people.
