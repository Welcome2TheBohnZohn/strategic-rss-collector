# ❗️ Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch UAV strikes against logistics centres and vessels

Published: 2026-10-13 15:09:16

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30017

❗️
Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to launch UAV strikes against logistics centres and vessels
involved in the delivery of military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strike,
a logistics depot of Mikulichi
in Kiev region was hit. It was used to store the AFU's attack drones.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military and dual-use cargo to the port of Nikolaev
was hit
in the Bug estuary.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
