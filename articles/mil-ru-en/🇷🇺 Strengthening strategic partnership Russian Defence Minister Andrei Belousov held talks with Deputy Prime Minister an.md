# 🇷🇺 Strengthening strategic partnership Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov held talks with Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence of Socialist Republic of Viet

Published: 2026-09-08 12:30:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29281

Strengthening
strategic partnership
🇷🇺
🇻🇳
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov held talks with Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of National Defence of Socialist Republic of Vietnam General of Army Phan Van Giang.
💬
The Russian Defence Minister noted
that the meeting is taking place as part of the State visit to Russia of Secretary General of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam, President of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam To Lam.
'The contacts between the defence ministries of the two countries have become regular and make an important contribution to the development of strategic relations between Russia and Vietnam.
We rely on a joint strategic partnership plan in the military sphere in our work,' said Andrei Belousov.
📆
The Russian Defence Minister also congratulated General of Army Phan Van Giang on the 81st anniversary of the independence of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
In turn, General of Army Phan Van Giang thanked Andrei Belousov for the opportunity to meet:
'We know that you have a very busy working schedule, and you have allocated time for this meeting'.
📈
He noted that during the official visit of the Vietnamese delegation in May of this year,
specific areas of development of bilateral military and military-technical cooperation were discussed
and are currently actively being implemented.
🤝
The talks took place in a constructive manner,
the sides discussed current issues of Russian-Vietnamese military and military-technical cooperation.
