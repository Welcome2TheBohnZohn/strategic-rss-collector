# ▫️ Security as common language The Directorate of the Chief of Engineer Troops of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation together with Laos partners organised

Published: 2026-09-14 12:41:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29399

Security as common language
▫️
The Directorate of the Chief of Engineer Troops of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation together with Laos partners organised
the humanitarian demining exercise
for the countries that are members of the ADMM-Plus in the first decade of September at the Nizhny Novgorod Guards Higher Military Engineering Command School.
🤝
By coordination of the Main Directorate for International Military Cooperation,
more than 100 servicemen
from Brunei, Cambodia, China, India, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Thailand, and Vietnam
took part in the event.
⏩
The servicemen
improved their skills of
humanitarian demining, combat coordination of engineering units, unexploded ordnance disposal, and the use of special hardware.
Modern ground robotic systems were actively used.
The exercise was highly appreciated by all member countries.
📆
The final session on the results of the three-year Russian-Lao chairmanship of the ADMM-Plus expert working group on humanitarian demining will take place in Laos in 2027.
