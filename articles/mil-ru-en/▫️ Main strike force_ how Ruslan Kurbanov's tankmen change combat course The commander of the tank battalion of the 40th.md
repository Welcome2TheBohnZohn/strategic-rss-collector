Main strike force: how Ruslan Kurbanov's tankmen change combat course
▫️
The commander of the tank battalion of the 40th Marine Brigade of the Vostok Group of Forces, lieutenant colonel Ruslan Kurbanov, together with his unit, celebrates a professional holiday on the front line. The officer is confident that
tank remain the main strike force on the battlefield.
‘Without armoured units, nothing can be done, especially breakthroughs in deep echeloned defence. Armoured troops proved that they are the main strike force of the Ground Forces,’ notes Ruslan Kurbanov.
🔥
The battalion experienced tens of the most difficult operations.
One day, the tankmen and the special forces servicemen were surrounded, but the tank crews refused to abandon their comrades. They took on a battle that lasted almost 23 hours, and already during the night they broke out of encirclement without losses, destroying a great deal of the enemy's hardware. Another time, precise tactics helped catch the enemy off guard: having formed eight columns led by tanks, the unit entered two settlements in just half an hour.
▫️
Today, Kurbanov's crews continue to perform tasks in the special military operation zone.
They celebrate in their usual environment: in combat positions, near their vehicles and reliable comrades.
