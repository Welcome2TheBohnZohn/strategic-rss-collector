# 📍 Andrei Belousov presents Gold Star medals to our heroes National Centre for State Defence Control of the Russian Federation

Published: 2026-08-28 07:30:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29081

Andrei Belousov presents Gold Star medals to our heroes
📍
National
Centre for State Defence Control of the Russian Federation
The Minister of Defence presented the Gold Star medals and personalised weapons to Russian servicemen, who were awarded the highest title of Russia, the Hero of the Russian Federation, for their courage and heroism in carrying out the tasks of the special military operation.
▫️
The Russian Defence Minister congratulated
the servicemen on their well-deserved awards, thanked them for their impeccable service and loyalty to their military duty.
💬
Andrei Belousov noted that the servicemen, sparing no effort, performed the task, demonstrated the highest skill and showed exceptional courage and heroism, and the awards presented today are recognition of outstanding contribution to national sovereignty and security.

Russian Defence Ministry
