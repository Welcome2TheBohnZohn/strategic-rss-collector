📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one airborne brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two marine brigades, and five national guard brigades close to Belozerskoye, Andreyevka, Novovodyanoye, Dobropolye, Novoyavlenka, and Gulevo (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU's losses amounted to more than 380 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one assault brigade, three air assault brigades, one assault regiment, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Kolomiytsy, Otrishki, Chaplino (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasinovka, Vasilkovoye, Volnyanka, Omelnik, Samoylovka, and Nikolskoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 290 troops and eight motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of one mechanised brigade, one mountain assault brigade and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Orekhov, Kamyshevakha, Zaporozhets, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, 11 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation inflicted damage on
the fuel, energy, and transport infrastructure facilities of Ukraine, used in the interests of the AFU, fuel and military materiel depots, assembly and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 147 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
258 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
