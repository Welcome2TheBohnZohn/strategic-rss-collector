Russia is one of the few countries capable of constructing nuclear submarines of such power, a world-class weapon of global importance. A fourth-generation, Yasen-class nuclear submarine, Perm is a multi-purpose missile vessel and the first submarine to carry Zircon hypersonic cruise missiles as standard armament.
