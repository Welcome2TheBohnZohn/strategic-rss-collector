We are developing cooperation:
Vasily Osmakov held a working meeting with Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Republic of Madagascar General Demosthene Pikulas.
💬
During the talks, the sides discussed joint bilateral defence cooperation projects and further plans for their implementation.
Commitment to developing a constructive dialogue
and maintaining a high level of interagency partnership
has been reaffirmed.
🤝
The meeting took place in a friendly spirit and confirmed mutual interest in strengthening Russia–Madagascar relations in the military field.
