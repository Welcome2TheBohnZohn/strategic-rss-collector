# 🤝 Worst part is left behind: Yuzhnaya Group's troops evacuate another 16 civilians from Konstantinovka

Published: 2026-11-26 10:00:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30791

Worst part is left behind:
Yuzhnaya Group's troops evacuate another 16 civilians from Konstantinovka
🤝
For a long time, Russian servicemen provided food and water for people who remained in the city
, waiting for the right moment to evacuate. As soon as the weather gave a chance, the servicemen organised an evacuation.
▫️
The enemy attempted to thwart the evacuation attacking people with UAVs.
But our lads risked their lives to get civilians to a safe area and help the wounded ones.
‘Thank God we did it, it's okay. Thank Russia's army for such lads,’ the rescued ones shared.
▫️
Now, all evacuees are already in the rear area, in warm and safe conditions, and Russian servicemen continue to save those who remain in the city.
