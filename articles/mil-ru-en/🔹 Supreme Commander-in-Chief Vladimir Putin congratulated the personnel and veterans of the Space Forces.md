# 🔹 Supreme Commander-in-Chief Vladimir Putin congratulated the personnel and veterans of the Space Forces.

Published: 2026-10-04 09:06:43

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29817

Supreme Commander-in-Chief Vladimir Putin
congratulated
the personnel and veterans of the Space Forces.
The message reads, in part:
'Thanks to the dedicated work and talent of several generations of outstanding designers, scientists, engineers, test specialists and military experts, our country has acquired a reliable space-based missile defence shield: an effective instrument for maintaining global strategic parity. Unique technologies have been developed, and a powerful scientific and industrial foundation has been established.
Today, the personnel and civilian staff of the Space Forces are carrying out critical tasks to ensure Russia’s security. They are doing extensive work to maintain communication and navigation systems and are promptly providing the Armed Forces and units participating in the special military operation with essential data. It is important to continue expanding the capabilities of our orbital satellite constellation and ground-based infrastructure, developing advanced technologies for cutting-edge weapons and equipment, and conducting promising studies.
I am confident that you will continue to serve the Fatherland with honour, make a significant contribution to strengthening the combat capability of the Army and Navy, and help advance the national space programme.'
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
