🚩
During the week, as a result of active offensive actions, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Maryevka, Gulevo, and Dobropolye (Maloye) in the Donetsk People's Republic.
💥
The losses were inflicted
on formations of four mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one assault brigade, one assault regiment, one unmanned systems brigade of the AFU, two marine brigades, four national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade.
💥
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), units of the 51st Army, developing an pincer movement in the west and east of the city, continue to tighten the encirclement ring
of the formations of the AFU 425th Assault Regiment, the 4th and 15th National Guard Brigades. All the enemy's attempts to break through the encirclement were thwarted.
▪️
Since the beginning of the enemy's encirclement in Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), more than 170 militants and two pickup trucks have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, more than 2,900 troops, 33 armoured fighting vehicles, 69 motor vehicles, 11 field artillery guns, and 16 electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the Tsentr Group's area of responsibility.
🚩
Over the past week, the Vostok Group of Forces continue to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence. As a result of decisive actions, the settlements of Zelenaya Dibrova and Svetlaya Dolina in Zaporozhye region have been liberated.
💥
Russian elements hit
manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one airmobile brigade, two assault brigades, four air assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade.
▪️
The enemy losses in this direction amounted to more than 1,900 troops, eight armoured fighting vehicles, 61 motor vehicles, and four field artillery guns.
📍
Last week, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade and one unmanned systems brigade of the AFU.
▪️
Up to 300 troops, 131 motor vehicles, 155-mm U.S.-made M777 howitzers, and nine electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
34 guided aerial bombs, 12 HIMARS MLRS projectiles, eight Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, and 6,011 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
🔥
The Black Sea Fleet eliminated four uncrewed surface vehicles of the AFU in the north-western part of the Black Sea.
