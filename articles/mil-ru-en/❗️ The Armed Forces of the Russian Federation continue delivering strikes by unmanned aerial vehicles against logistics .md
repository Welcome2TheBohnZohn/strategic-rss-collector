# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres and ships employed to deliver military-purpose cargo

Published: 2026-11-03 16:03:19

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30421

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue delivering strikes
by unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres and ships employed to deliver military-purpose cargo to the AFU.
💥
Today, as a result of a strike,
the Novaya Pochta logistics centre
in Berdichev (Zhitomir region), where military cargo supplied to the AFU by Western countries was stored and distributed,
was hit
.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military equipment and dual-use cargo to Ukraine
was hit in the Black Sea waters 24 km south of Odessa.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
