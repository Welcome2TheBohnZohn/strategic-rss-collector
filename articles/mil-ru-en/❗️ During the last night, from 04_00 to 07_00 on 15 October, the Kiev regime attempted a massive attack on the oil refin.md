During the last night, from 04:00 to 07:00 on 15 October,
the Kiev regime attempted a massive attack on the oil refinery in Ryazan.
💥
Air defence missile troops, fighter and army aviation, and mobile fire groups
intercepted and destroyed four Flamingo long-range cruise missiles and 69 fixed-wing UAVs.
▫️
As a result of UAV debris falling on the territory of the refinery, a fire broke out.
