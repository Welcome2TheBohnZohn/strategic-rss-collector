# ❗️ Full autonomy and coordination: during the Echelon 2026 exercise, combat service support units are practising actions taking into account the experience of the special military 

Published: 2026-09-30 18:45:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29727

Full autonomy and coordination: during the Echelon 2026 exercise, combat service support units are practising actions taking into account the experience of the special military operation.
❗️
Main objective: improving the coordination of command and control bodies and mastering the practical skills of technical and rear support units during joint operations.
'During the Echelon 2026 exercise, we demonstrate to our colleagues from CSTO countries how we perform combat service support tasks in real combat conditions. The main requirement for the rear area is that it must be fully dug in and camouflaged in the terrain. All premises must be of the bunker type, fully autonomous, equipped with all elements of living, laundry and bath services, and food supply for personnel,' said Sergei Spiridonov, Chief of Staff of the Combat Service Support Directorate of the Central Military District.
⚙️
Special attention during the exercise is paid to the deployment of field refueling points and the organisation of evacuation and repair of damaged equipment under the threat of enemy UAV impact.
🎮
In one of the areas, personnel practised tasks on delivering material resources to the line of contact using unmanned ground vehicles and UAVs, as well as the evacuation of the wounded and damaged equipment.
