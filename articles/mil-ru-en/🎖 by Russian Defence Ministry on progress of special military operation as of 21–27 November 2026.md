During the week, units of the Tsentr Group of Forces inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one airborne brigade, one jaeger brigade, one infantry brigade, one assault brigade, three assault regiments of the AFU, two marine brigades, and four national guard brigades.
▪️
The AFU losses in this direction amounted to more than 2,375 troops, 44 armoured fighting vehicles, 52 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and 10 electronic warfare stations.
🚩
Last week, units of the Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depth of the enemy's defence. As a result of resolute actions, the settlement of Barvinovka (Zaporozhye region) was liberated.
💥
Russian troops inflicted damage on
manpower and hardware of four air assault brigades, two assault brigades, two assault regiments of the AFU, and one marine brigade.
▪️
In total, more than 1,435 servicemen, four armoured fighting vehicles, 34 motor vehicles, and two artillery guns have been neutralised in the Vostok Group's area of responsibility.
↗️
Over the past week, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian troops delivered strikes at formations of three mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU.
▪️
Up to 145 troops, 100 motor vehicles, three field artillery guns, and six electronic warfare and counter-fire stations have been neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
17 guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, three Neptune long-range cruise missiles, and 5,422 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
