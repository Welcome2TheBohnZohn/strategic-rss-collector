🚩
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Mirovka in Zaporozhye region.
🔥
The Group inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, three assault regiments of the AFU, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade near Vasilkovskoye, Dibrova, Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Barvinovka, Voskresenka, and Volnyanka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 245 troops and eight motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Formations of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit near Orekhov, Kushugum, Kirovo, and Yurkovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, 27 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on energy and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, weapons, ammunition and military equipment depots, assembly plants, long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 158 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
1,226 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
