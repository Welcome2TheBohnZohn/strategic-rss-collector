# ✅ Course towards stability Deputy Defence Minister of the Russian Federation, Aleksei Krivoruchko, took part in the 13th Meeting of ASEAN Defence Ministers and Dialogue Partners.

Published: 2026-10-29 13:10:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30344

Course towards stability
Deputy Defence Minister of the Russian Federation, Aleksei Krivoruchko, took part in the 13th Meeting of ASEAN Defence Ministers and Dialogue Partners.
✅
In his address, the Russian Deputy Defence Minister highlighted
the importance of multilateral military cooperation in the Asia-Pacific region in maintaining its stability,
stressed the Russian Defence Ministry's contribution to the work of ADMM Plus, and presented Russian views on the evolution of the situation in the sphere of global and regional security.
🤝
Working meetings of the Deputy Head of the Russian Defence Ministry with the heads of foreign delegations participating in ADMM Plus were held
during the meeting, which was coordinated by the Main Directorate for International Military Cooperation of the Russian Defence Ministry.
