Situation requires preventive action — Andrei Belousov
Minister of Defence of Russian Federation Andrei Belousov welcomed by President of the Republic of Belarus Aleksandr Lukashenko during his working visit to Belarus
'The international situation continues to deteriorate. It remains extremely difficult.
Not only is it difficult, it is becoming more and more unpredictable. We can see what is happening in Europe, in the Middle East. And this calls for preventive action,' Andrei Belousov said.
💬
According to him,
this is precisely the main subject of discussion at a meeting with Minister of Defence of the Republic of Belarus
Lieutenant General Viktor Khrenin: 'What other actions, what other tasks we must set ourselves and resolve in the shortest possible time'.
🤝
President of the Republic of Belarus
Aleksandr Lukashenko thanked Andrei Belousov
for the joint grouping formed within the framework of the CSTO in the west direction.
