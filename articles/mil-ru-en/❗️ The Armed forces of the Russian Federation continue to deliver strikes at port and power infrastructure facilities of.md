# ❗️ The Armed forces of the Russian Federation continue to deliver strikes at port and power infrastructure facilities of Ukraine as well as vessels

Published: 2026-10-02 07:00:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29750

❗️
The Armed forces of the Russian Federation
continue to deliver strikes at port and power infrastructure facilities of Ukraine as well as vessels
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
Last night,
as a result of UAV strike, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
in Kiev,
a road-rail bridge across the Dnepr river
used by the enemy to deploy his troops and to deliver military cargo intended to support the AFU's groups in the territory of Donbass;
▫️
in Kiev region,
the Kievskaya electrical substation
(the settlement of Nalivaykovka), which ensures the transmission of electricity and its distribution to the defence industry facilities in Kiev and Kiev region;
▫️
in Odessa region,
production and industrial system
in the port of Izmail, which provides reception, storage, and transportation of military cargo and fuel for the needs of the AFU.
▫️
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military materiel and dual-use cargo for the AFU to the port of Odessa
was hit
in the Black Sea.
