📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one jaeger brigade, one unmanned systems brigade of the AFU, one marine brigade, three national guard brigades, and the Azov Special Operations Brigade close to Andreyevka, Novoroitskoye, Sergeyevka, Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), Maryevka, Novoandreyevka, Novopavlovka, and Kolona-Mezhevaya (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 425 troops, two Kazak armoured fighting vehicles, 11 motor vehicles, three electronic warfare stations, and one Israeli-made RADA RPS-42 counter-fire radar station.
↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued advancing into the depth of enemy defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two assault brigades, two air assault brigades, and two assault regiments of the AFU close to Samoylovka, Svetlaya Dolina, Barvinovka, Nikolskoye, Chervony Yar, Omelnik (Zaporozhye region), Podgavrilovka, and Pokrovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 315 troops, two armoured personnel carriers, and 10 motor vehicles.
💥
Units of the Dnepr Group of Forces inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU near Orekhov, Yulyevka, Grigorovka, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 40 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 19 motor vehicles, three electronic warfare stations, and one radar station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on the assembly facilities of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles and their storage areas, the fuel, power, transport and logistics infrastructure of Ukraine, ammunition depots used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 141 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
eight guided aerial bombs, three U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, one Neptune long-range cruise missile, and 1,185 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
⚓️
The Black Sea Fleet eliminated
six uncrewed surface vehicles of the Ukrainian Navy in the north-western part of the Black Sea.
