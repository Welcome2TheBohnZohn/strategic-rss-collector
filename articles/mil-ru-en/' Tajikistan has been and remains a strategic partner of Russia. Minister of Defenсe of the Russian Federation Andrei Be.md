# ' Tajikistan has been and remains a strategic partner of Russia. Minister of Defenсe of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov

Published: 2026-09-08 19:36:36

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29289

'
Tajikistan has been and remains a strategic partner of Russia.
'
🤝
Minister of Defenсe of the Russian Federation Andrei Belousov
took part in an official reception
marking the 35th anniversary of the independence of the Republic of Tajikistan.
'On behalf of the leadership of the Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation and on my own behalf, I sincerely congratulate you on the 35th anniversary of the state independence of the Republic of Tajikistan', Andrei Belousov said.
▫️
According to him, over these years
the republic has come a long way and has become a strong and independent state
that plays an important role in ensuring stability in the Central Asian region.
'Our peoples are bound by great heroic military past and a history of military feats. For us, the Republic of Tajikistan is not just a fraternal country. Victory in the Great Patriotic War forever defined us as comrades‑in‑arms,' he said.
▫️
The Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation noted that close military cooperation has been established between Russia and Tajikistan.
The Russian military base on the territory of the republic is in a state of readiness to combat external threats.
'We highly value Tajikistan’s contribution to strengthening regional and collective security.'﻿
💬
Andrei Belousov expressed confidence that the
allied relations between Russia and Tajikistan will continue to serve as a reliable foundation for peace and security in Central Asia.
'Tajikistan has been and remains a strategic partner of Russia,' he emphasised.
