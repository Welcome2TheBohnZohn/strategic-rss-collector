# ❗️ During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against Ukrainian ports and ships

Published: 2026-09-18 16:25:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29484

❗️
During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against Ukrainian ports and ships
used in the interests of the AFU.
🔥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit
:
▫️
in the port of Yuzhny (Odessa region) –
a dry cargo ship
that had delivered military-purpose cargo;
▫️
in the port of Odessa – a
tanker
that had delivered fuel and lubricants for the AFU.
💥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
carrying cargo intended to support the AFU to the port of Odessa was hit at sea crossing.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
