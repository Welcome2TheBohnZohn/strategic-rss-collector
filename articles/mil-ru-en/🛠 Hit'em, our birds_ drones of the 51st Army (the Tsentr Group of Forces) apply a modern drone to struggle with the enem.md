Published: 2026-10-08 10:31:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29918

Hit'em, our birds:
drones of the 51st Army (the Tsentr Group of Forces)
apply a modern drone to struggle with the enemy's ‘birds’
🛠
The main feature of the Sokol-I is a light weight, so it can be launched by hands without any launcher. The maximum altitude reaches 5,000 kilometres, and its maximum speed is up to 150 km/h.
💬
The UAV section leader with the call sign Vinni told
about the combat use of the drone
:
‘We take off as soon as any target appears, we begin to fly around searching it. We get locked on the target, in case if it's the enemy, we accompany and destroy it, let's say so. Also, we fly into the engine compartment from behind and explode, as well as we can land on or ram it and explode when we're already on the enemy. The most important thing is to see. If we see it, we destroy it anyway.’
🔹
