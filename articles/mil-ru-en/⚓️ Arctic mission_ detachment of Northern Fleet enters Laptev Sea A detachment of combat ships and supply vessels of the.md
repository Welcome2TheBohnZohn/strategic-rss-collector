Arctic mission: detachment of Northern Fleet enters Laptev Sea
A detachment of combat ships and supply vessels of the Northern Fleet, as part of the Arctic mission, passed through the Strait of Vilkitskiy, where it conducted ice reconnaissance on the route with the help of a Ka-29 helicopter and a UAV, and entered the Laptev Sea.
🗺
The detachment is now advancing on a given course and intensively maintains radio communication with ships on the Northern Sea Route to ensure their safety along the route.
🌊
There will be hard work:
in the Laptev Sea, sailors of the Northern Fleet will continue to monitor the ice situation. The monitoring of the situation here is an important task on which both the success of the mission and the defence of Russia's interests in the Arctic depend.
