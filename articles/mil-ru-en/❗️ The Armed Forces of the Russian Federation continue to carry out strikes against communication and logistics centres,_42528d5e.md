# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to carry out strikes against communication and logistics centres, as well as vessels

Published: 2026-09-27 16:02:11

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29655

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to carry out strikes against communication and logistics centres, as well as vessels
operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
Today, as a result of UAV strikes, the following targets were hit:
📍
in Kiev:
▫️
the Omega Telecom data processing centre
, which is one of the largest providers of high-speed internet services to the AFU, including storage of big number of databases;
▫️
the Kievstar data processing centre,
which is one of the largest providers of mobile communication and high-speed internet services for the AFU;
📍
in Odessa:
▫️
the Novaya Pochta logistics centre
used to store military cargo.
▫️
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military materiel and dual-use cargo to the port of Odessa to support the AFU
was hit at sea.

Russian Defence Ministry
