# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation inflicted UAV strikes on logistics centres, depots and Ukrainian seaports, as well as vessels

Published: 2026-09-21 06:30:28

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29533

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
inflicted
UAV
strikes on logistics centres, depots and Ukrainian seaports, as well as vessels
used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following objects were engaged:
📍
in Kiev region:
▫️
in the settlement of Schastlivoye,
the BDU Logistic centre
in which depots storing and distributing of military materiel were organised;
▫️
in the settlement of Boyarka,
a gas distribution station
providing gas supply to the defence industry facilities;
📍
in Odessa region:
▫️
in the port of Odessa,
a dry cargo ship
, delivering cargo intended to support the AFU;
▫️
in the port of Izmail, port infrastructure facilities used to unload and store military cargo.
▫️
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo intended to support the AFU to the port of Odessa was hit at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
