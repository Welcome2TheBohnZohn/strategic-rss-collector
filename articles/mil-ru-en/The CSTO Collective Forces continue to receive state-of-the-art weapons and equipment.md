The CSTO Collective Forces continue to receive state-of-the-art weapons and equipment
Much attention was traditionally paid to improving operational and combat readiness of the collective security system forces and their equipment. Several large-scale joint exercises were held in Belarus, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan and, of course, Russia. <...> Today, we will need to make some important decisions on the further development of the CSTO Collective Forces. This includes their air component and the establishment of mobile air defence units for joint military operations,
Vladimir Putin emphasised at an expanded-format meeting of the CSTO Security Council.
