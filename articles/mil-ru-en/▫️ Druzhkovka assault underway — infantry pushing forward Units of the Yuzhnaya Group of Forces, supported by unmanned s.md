Druzhkovka assault underway — infantry pushing forward
Units of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces, supported by unmanned systems teams,
have secured a foothold on the outskirts of Druzhkovka, and the infantry is already carrying out successful actions to storm the city
. This was reported by the deputy company CO of the Group's separate unmanned systems regiment with call sign 'Artist'.
The servicemen use a standard algorithm:
first they cut off the enemy's supply routes, depriving him of ammunition and provisions, and then they deploy the maximum number of strike and reconnaissance drones.
'Today, drones are the most deterrent and the most important factor enabling our valiant infantry to advance,' the serviceman says.
