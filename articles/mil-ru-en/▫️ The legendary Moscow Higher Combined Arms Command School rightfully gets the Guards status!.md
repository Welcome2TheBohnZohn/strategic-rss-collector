# ▫️ The legendary Moscow Higher Combined Arms Command School rightfully gets the Guards status!

Published: 2026-09-01 15:30:04

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29156

▫️
The legendary Moscow Higher Combined Arms Command School rightfully gets the Guards status!
Minister of Defence Andrei Belousov attached Ribbon of Saint George to the banner of Moscow Higher Combined Arms Command School. Since now, Russian legendary Higher School has officially turned into the Guards Higher School.
💬
Addressing to cadets, teachers, and veterans of the school,
Andrei Belousov noted that this title is
not just a status, but
more than a century of living history, heroism, courage and loyalty of thousands of his graduates, whom our country is proud of.
👉
The head of the Ministry of Defence congratulated the cadets and recalled that
‘the Guards status is not only a great honour, but also a serious responsibility’
📆
Moscow Higher Combined Arms Command School is rightfully called legendary. Its history is full of heroic pages.
At the beginning of October 1941, the cadets regiment took part in the fiercest battle for Moscow in Volokolamsk direction. For the heroism and courage shown in those battles, 59 cadets and 30 officers were awarded orders and medals. To conmemorate heroes, a memorial board was opened on the main building of the School.
📊
Over the entire history of the school, five Marshals and over 600 Generals studied here. Nearly 150 graduates awarded the title of Hero of the Soviet Union and the Russian Federation. The school itself was awarded four times with government awards.
▫️
Andrei Belousov
laid flowers at the monument to the "Kremlin Graduates"
and paid tribute to the memory of the servicemen who gave their lives during the military duty performance.
▫️
Today, the Moscow Guards Higher Combined Arms Command School
is one of the country's leading military educational institutions, that has a glorious history and glorious future.

Russian Defence Ministry
