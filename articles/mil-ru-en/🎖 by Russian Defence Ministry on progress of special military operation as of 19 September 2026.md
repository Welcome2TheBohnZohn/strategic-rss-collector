🚩
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation and liberated Maryevka and Gulevo (Donetsk People's Republic).
💥
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one special forces brigade, and two national guard brigades
have been hit
near Belozerskoye, Druzhkovka, Artema, Varvarovka, Starorayskoye and Novoyavlenka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 410 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, three self-propelled artillery systems, and 11 motor vehicles.
🚩
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Zelenaya Dibrova (Zaporozhye region).
💥
Strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades and two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Manvelovka, Malinovka, Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region), Petropavlovskoye, and Obshcheye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 280 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, 10 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, and Dimitrovo (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 55 troops, 18 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage on
ammunition, fuel, and military hardware depots, assembly workshops as well as storage facilities of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formation and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 936 fixed-wing UAVs.
