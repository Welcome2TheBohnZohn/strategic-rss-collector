work: precise blind strike on target at 800 metres
A Molniya-2 drone operator of the Tsentr Group of Forces
destroyed an AFU drone command post
in Dnepropetrovsk direction.
➡️
Russian reconnaissance units detected a take-off site and a dugout of the enemy. A Molniya strike UAV was launched to destroy the target.
800 metres away from the target, the enemy turned on the electronic warfare system and fully intercepted the video signal
: an image was lost on the screen.
🎯
The operator continued to control the drone relying on his memory and target data.
As the result the 'bird' reached precisely the dugout.
