The Vostok Group's units
continued to advance into the depth of the enemy's defence and
liberated the village of Timoshevka in Zaporozhye region.
💥
Russian troops
inflicted losses on
manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Kolomiytsy, Podgavrilovka, Dibrova (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novorozovka, Volnyanka, and Barvinovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 170 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, and two motor vehicles have been neutralised.
↗️
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Russian elements hit formations of a mechanised brigade of the AFU and a marine brigade near Kirovo, Orekhov, and Preobrazhenka in Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 16 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
launch sites of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 146 areas.

383 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
