Robots
bear the brunt
🛡
The Vostok Group's mobile firing groups, using ground robotic systems,
cover Russian units' positions and routes of movement
in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▫️
Joint operations of mobile firing groups and ground robots allow to distribute sectors of fire and provide mutual cover.
💬
The unit commander with the call sign Yudzhin said, that
the main purpose of the system is to protect a serviceman's life.
The robot takes concealed or open space position, while the operator is located in a safe dugout.
