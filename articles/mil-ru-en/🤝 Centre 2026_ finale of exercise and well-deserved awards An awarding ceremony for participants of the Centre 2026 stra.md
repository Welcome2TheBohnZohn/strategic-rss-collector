Centre 2026: finale of exercise and well-deserved awards
An awarding ceremony for participants of the Centre 2026 strategic command post exercise was held
at the Chebarkulskiy training ground. It was attended by Deputy Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation General of the Army Yunus-Bek Yevkurov and the command of the Central Military District.
🔹
Servicemen from the armed forces of all states that took part in the manoeuvres were awarded the departmental medal 'For Strengthening Military Cooperation'.
'Thank you very much to everyone - all tasks have been completed, the exercise's objectives achieved,' said Army General Yunus-Bek Yevkurov.
