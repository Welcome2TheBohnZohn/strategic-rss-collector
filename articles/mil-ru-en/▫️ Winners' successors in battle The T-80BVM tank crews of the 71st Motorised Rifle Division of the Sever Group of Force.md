The T-80BVM tank crews of the 71st Motorised Rifle Division of the Sever Group of Forces
continue to burn down the enemy's positions
in Kharkov region.

Every shot is precisely aimed at the target.
The tank commander with the call sign 'Grom' told what does it mean to wear the proud Guards title on the front line:
‘Every day, we try to confirm the title of the Guards by our combat work, moving forward, pushing the Nazis away. We are fighting to keep our civilian population's sleep tight, not to shame the honour of our heroic ancestors, who beat Nazism 80 years ago in the same way. And we are fighting in memory of those who posthumously received the title of Heroes of Russia, those who gave their lives to defend our borders, to destroy these neo-fascists’.
