# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against Ukrainian port infrastructure facilities

Published: 2026-10-16 07:34:51

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30079

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against Ukrainian port infrastructure facilities
as well as vessels operating in support of the AFU.
💥
In Odessa region,
the Bloom logistics centre
in Teplodar was hit. It stored military and dual-use cargo intended to supply the AFU.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship transporting military cargo
to the port of Odessa was hit while at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
