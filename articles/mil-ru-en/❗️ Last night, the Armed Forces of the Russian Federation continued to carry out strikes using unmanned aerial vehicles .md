Published: 2026-10-25 07:05:19

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics centres and facilities of Ukraine’s port infrastructure,
as well as vessels used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following facilities in Kharkov
were hit
:
▫️
the
Epicentr logistics centre,
where dual‑use products are stored to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▫️
a
distribution centre
that provides storage and distribution for military and dual‑use cargo for the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▫️
the
Vasishchevo logistics terminal
, used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine to store military equipment and dual‑use products.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
that was delivering supplies to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine to the port of Chernomorsk
was damaged at sea
.
