# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics infrastructure facilities used in support 

Published: 2026-11-01 07:32:55

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30382

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes using unmanned aerial vehicles against logistics infrastructure facilities used in support of the AFU as well as vessels involved in the transportation of military cargo
.
💥
The strikes hit railway traffic control facilities at Konotop railway station in Sumy region, which facilitate the delivery of weapons and military equipment to the AFU from the western regions of Ukraine.
💥
Moreover, 69 km south-west of Odessa, a dry cargo ship transporting cargo intended for the AFU was struck while at sea.
