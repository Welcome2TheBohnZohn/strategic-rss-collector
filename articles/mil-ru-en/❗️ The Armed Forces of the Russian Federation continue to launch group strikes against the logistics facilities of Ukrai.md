The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to launch group strikes
against the logistics facilities of Ukraine and maritime vessels operating in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
📍
During the day, strike drones
hit following targets in Kiev region:
▫️
DHL Logistik logistics centre
in Pogreb, where warehouses were used to store unmanned aerial vehicles of various types and components for them.
▫️
Zammler depot shopping and logistics centre
in Borispol, which is used to store and distribute military supplies supplied to the Armed Forces of Ukraine by Western countries.
📍
In Odessa region, strikes were delivered at a logistics centre
in Nerubayskoye, which was used to store and distribute military cargo delivered to the port of Odessa.
💥
In addition,
one dry cargo ship
with military materiel for the Armed Forces of Ukraine was hit in the port of Yuzhny.
