Mine density is high but we move forward
Servicemen of the Dnepr Group's engineer units are carrying out engineer reconnaissance and clearing roads to ensure the safety of routes leading to the settlement of Aleshki.
Roads adjacent to the city are being cleared of explosive objects and unexploded projectiles
to deliver humanitarian aid and organise the evacuation of civilians.
The majority of explosive objects and mines, mostly of Western production, are scattered or laid by enemy UAVs and are set to be non-removable.
In order to ensure the safety of civilians, military engineers destroy them on spot using external charges.
One of the Western munitions shown in the footage is a 60 mm HEF mine and it was found in the area of the city bypass road. It was designed to be used in NATO-standard mortars.
Military engineers note that such unexploded munitions pose a particular danger to the civilian population, as every slightest mechanical impact can lead to their detonation.
