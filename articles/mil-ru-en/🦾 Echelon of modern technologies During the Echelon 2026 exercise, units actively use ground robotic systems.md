Echelon

During the Echelon 2026 exercise, units actively use ground robotic systems.
⚙️
The units
practise delivering cargo to the line of contact
using ground robotic systems and UAVs,
as well as evacuating wounded servicemen and damaged vehicles.
The servicemen used the Bogomol-7 ground robotic system, which delivers cargo and evacuates ATVs.
🛠
The damaged vehicles modern assembly workshop operation was organised considering the special military operation experience:
hardware recovery and repair posts were deployed in underground fortifications.
▫️
The training area is protected from air attacks by observation posts and mobile firing groups.
