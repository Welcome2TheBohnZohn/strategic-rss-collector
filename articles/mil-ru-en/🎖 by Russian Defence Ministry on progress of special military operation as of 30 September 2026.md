🚩
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Yegorovka, Zaporozhye region.
💥
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of
an assault brigade, two air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade near Nikolskoye (Zaporozhye region), Chaplino, Malinovka, Demurino, and Kolomiytsy (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 280 troops and four motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted fire damage on two mechanised brigades, a mountain assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 35 Ukrainian troops and seven motor vehicles have been neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian groups of forces have inflicted fire damage on
transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, ammunition and electronic equipment depots, UAV storage areas, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 154 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems
shot down three guided aerial bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 768 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
