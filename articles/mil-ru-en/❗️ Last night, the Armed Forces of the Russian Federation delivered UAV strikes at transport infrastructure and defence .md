# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes at transport infrastructure and defence industry facilities, as well as vessels used in the interest

Published: 2026-11-27 06:57:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30803

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered UAV strikes at
transport infrastructure and defence industry facilities, as well as vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets were hit:
📍
the Termoplast industry enterprise involved in the production of attack UAVs in Letichev (Khmelnitsky region);
📍
the Mpka-Ukraine plant producing and supplying electrotechnical equipment for the AFU in Zaporozhye;
📍
a 330 kV power substation in Krivoy Rog (Dnepropetrovsk region) and a 330 kV electric substation in Zhitomir, that support the operation of Ukrainian defence industry facilities;
📍
a logistics centre storing components to produce solid missile fuel in the settlement of Belaya Tserkov (Kiev region);
📍
a transport infrastructure facility in Zaporozhye;
📍
four railway locomotives used to transport weapons and military hardware in Krivoy Rog, Pershotravensk, and Bogdanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region);
📍
a dry cargo ship delivering military cargo to Ukraine in the Black Sea waters south-west of Odessa.

Russian Defence Ministry
