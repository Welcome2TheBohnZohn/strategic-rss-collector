↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the AFU have been hit near Mayak, Tikhoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Veseloye, and Odarovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 195 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, one 155-mm Bogdana howitzer, and two motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
Formations of two AFU mechanised brigades were neutralised near Kirovo (Zaporozhye region) and Belozerka (Kherson region).
▪️
Up to 20 troops, 16 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage
on power and transport infrastructure used in the interests of the AFU, production and storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles and ground robotic systems,ammunition depots, military airfields, air surveillance radars, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.

four guided aerial bombs, one HIMARS-made MLRS projectile, and 819 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
