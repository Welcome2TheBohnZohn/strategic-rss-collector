↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
The Group's units inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the AFU, and a marine brigade close to Ivanovka, Fedorovskoye, Kolomiytsy, Pokrovskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Vasinovka, Volnyanka, and Novonikolaevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 200 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, and three motor vehicles.
💥
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Formations of a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the AFU were hit near Kirovo and Orekhov of Zaporozhye region.
▪️
Up to 40 troops, 19 motor vehicles, one counter-fire station, and three electronic warfare stations of the enemy were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
energy and transport infrastructure facilities used by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, depots and launch sites of long-range unmanned aerial vehicles, temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 159 areas.

1,072 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
