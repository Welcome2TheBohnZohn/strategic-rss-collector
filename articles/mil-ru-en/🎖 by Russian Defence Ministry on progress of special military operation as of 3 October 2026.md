↗️
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Russian units inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and two national guard brigades close to Svyatogorovka, Belozerskoye, Verovka, Blagodat, Druzhkovka, and Roganskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
📍
In Dobropolye (Donetsk People's Republic), assault groups of the 51st Army along with marine units, led an offensive in the north-western, eastern, and central parts of the city.
Units of the 76th Air Assault Division wiped out enemy position and seized a section of a treeline.
Over the past 24 hours, 70 buildings have been liberated and more than 20 militants of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been neutralised.
▪️
In total, up to 385 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, seven motor vehicles, one 155-mm Bogdana self-propelled artillery system, and three electronic warfare stations have been neutralised in the area of responsibility of the Tsentr Group of Forces.
📍
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence
. Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of an assault brigade, an air assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to Mirolyubovka, Kolomiytsy, Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), and Kirovo (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 240 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, and four motor vehicles.
↗️
The Dnepr Group of Forces' units took more advantageous lines and positions
. Formations of a mechanised brigade and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Orekhov, Veselyanka, and Kushugum (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
More than 35 troops and 12 motor vehicles were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on storage areas of unmanned aerial vehicles as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 149 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems
shot down six guided aerial bombs and 680 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
