Mastering Science of Victory. Keep going! Taking advantage of the innovations and experience of the special military operation to the maximum!
⏩
Andrei Belousov inspected the readiness of the Moscow Guards Higher Combined Arms Command School for the start of the new academic year. He examined the classrooms and checked the accommodation and recreation conditions of cadets.
📊
This year, more than 800 cadets have been enrolled in the first year, more than 100 of whom are participants of the special military operation.
💬
Andrei Belousov was reported that
the experience of combat operations during the military training is maximally implemented in the educational process.
🎮
The school provides training in promising military specializations, including ‘the use of Ground Forces units with unmanned aerial vehicles’. The training is conducted by experienced instructors, 90 per cent of whom took part in the special military operation.
🖥
New simulators are used as much as possible in the educational process.
One of them is the Hameleon-BPS. It allows to train future commanders to assess the situation and analyze intelligence data, make decisions and perform them in a single virtual platform. There are also 3D maps of the terrain, several types of UAVs, It also uses 3D maps of the area, several types of UAVs, including ground-based robotic systems.
➡️
Since September 1,
the training of cadets began at the pilot training cluster deployed at the school
with a system of information interaction and situational awareness of officials in the tactical management unit
"SVOD"
