↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depths of the enemy's defence.
Manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two air assault brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade have been hit close to Vasilkovka, Ivanovka, Otrishki (Dnepropetrovsk region), Nikolskoye, Obshcheye, and Trudovoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 340 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and six motor vehicles.
💥
The Dnepr Group's units inflicted losses
on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one unmanned systems brigade of the AFU close to Zapasnoye, Kirovo, Malokaterinovka (Zaporozhye region), Novodmitrovka (Kherson region), and the city of Kherson.
▪️
Up to 55 troops, 18 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on the logistics centres, fuel, power, and transport infrastructure of Ukraine, production and storage facilities of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 155 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
two guided aerial bombs, three U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, and 998 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
🔥
The Black Sea Fleet eliminated six uncrewed surface vehicles of the Ukrainian Navy in the Black Sea waters.
