# ❗️ Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against port infrastructure of Ukraine and ships

Published: 2026-10-09 16:16:00

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29943

❗️
Today, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued delivering strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against port infrastructure of Ukraine and ships
involved in the delivery of military cargo.
💥
As a result of the strikes, a
dry cargo ship
delivering dual-use cargo intended to support the AFU was hit in the port of Chernomorsk.
🔥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military-purpose cargo to the port of Odessa was hit at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
