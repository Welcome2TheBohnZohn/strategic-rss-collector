# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to deliver strikes at logistics and communication centres as well as vessels used to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.

Published: 2026-10-01 06:30:25

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29730

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to deliver strikes at logistics and communication centres as well as vessels used to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
Last night, unmanned aerial vehicles hit:
📍
in Kiev, a transport and logistics centre
(Rent-Logistik LTD). In its warehouse, a workshop producing uncrewed surface vehicles was set up;
📍
in Odessa and Odessa region:
▪️
the HOSTING-UA Data Centre,
through which reconnaissance and other service information were processed in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▪️
in the port of Chernomorsk,
a dry cargo ship
with military equipment and dual-use cargo for the Armed Forces of Ukraine was hit.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
