# ❗️ During the night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out group strikes against logistics centres

Published: 2026-09-13 07:10:02

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29368

❗️
During the night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out group strikes against logistics centres
through which military supplies are delivered and distributed, as well as
energy facilities
that support their operation.
💥
Air-based high-precision weapons and attack drones
hit
:
▪️
the Novaya Pochta logistics centre
used to store and distribute military equipment intended for the support of the Armed Forces of Ukraine in the settlement of Nerubayskoye (Odessa region).
▪️
the Usatovo power substation
in the city of Odessa and
the Novaya power substation
in the city of Nikolayev, which operate port and logistics infrastructure in Odessa and Nikolayev regions.
▪️
In addition,
railway infrastructure facilities
in western regions of Ukraine providing transport of military cargo from European countries were hit.

Russian Defence Ministry
