📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Russian elements inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, three marine brigades, the Azov Special Forces Brigade, and four national guard brigades close to Vodolazhskoye, Maryevka, Ivanovka, Novotroitskoye (Dnepropetrovsk region), Belozerskoye, Andreyevka, Novoandreyevka, Dobropolye, Verovka, Yuryevka, and Zavidovo-Kudashevo (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU's losses amounted to more than 420 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, one armoured fighting vehicle, 12 motor vehicles, and one artillery gun.
🔥
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, two air assault brigade, three assault regiments, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Orestopol, Krivorozhskoye, Kolomiytsy, Pavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Obshcheye, Golubkovo, Voskresenka, Svetlaya Dolina, and Slavnoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 285 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, nine motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
↗️
The Dnepr Group of Forces' units took more advantageous lines and positions.
Strikes were delivered at manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Orekhov, Balabino, Zaporozhets, Yulyevka, Zapasnoye, Yurkovka, and Preobrazhenka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 45 troops, 26 motor vehicles, two electronic warfare stations, and one radar station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation inflicted damage on
the logistics centres, fuel, power, and transport infrastructure facilities of Ukraine used by the AFU, assembly workshops and storage areas for unmanned aerial vehicles, ground robotic systems, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed forces and foreign mercenaries in 146 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs, two HIMARS-made MLRS projectiles, and 902 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
