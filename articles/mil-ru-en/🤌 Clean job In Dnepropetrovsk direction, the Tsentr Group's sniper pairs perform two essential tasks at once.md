In Dnepropetrovsk direction, the Tsentr Group's sniper pairs
perform two essential tasks at once:
protect assault groups from enemy drones and destroy camouflaged enemy observation posts.
💡
It is difficult to find such posts: the enemy hides well.
However, our snipers have come up with a clever trick:
they use the movements of enemy drones to spot the enemy's positions.
💬
The sniper company commander with the call sign 'Khudozhnik' said:
'We spot such enemy by drops from Baba Yaga. Goods are sent to them. We see these actions, move covertly to the position where these packages are delivered. We wait for the moment when he comes to a package to destroy him'.
