# ❗️ The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to strike communication and logistics centres, as well as ships used in the interests of the AFU.

Published: 2026-09-28 15:53:32

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29675

❗️
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to strike communication and logistics centres, as well as ships used in the interests of the AFU.
💥
Today, as a result of UAV strikes, the
Ukrtelecom data processing centre
on Antonovich Street in Kiev was hit. Ukrtelecom is one of the main companies providing high-speed internet services for the AFU, as well as storing large volumes of databases.
💥
In addition,
two dry cargo ships delivering
military-purpose cargo to the port of Odessa were hit at sea.
🔹
Russian Defence Ministry
