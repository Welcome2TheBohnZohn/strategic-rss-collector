# 👊 ‘We give no opportunity for enemy to advance,’ commander of artillery platoon with the call sign ‘Agro’ tells about neutralisation of enemy in Druzhkovka.

Published: 2026-11-27 15:45:24

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30810

👊
‘We give no opportunity for enemy to advance,’
commander of artillery platoon with the call sign ‘Agro’ tells about neutralisation of enemy in Druzhkovka.
‘We're working in Druzhkovka, right beyond Alekseyevo-Druzhkovka, we carry out fire assaults in order to make our assault groups advance,’ the serviceman said.
▫️
He told, how
Russian servicemen control all approaches and highways to the city
, disrupting the enemy's logistics.
❌
The serviceman also noted, that
the AFU has no more tube artillery left in this area.
The enemy uses only mortars shooting at a range of 1-2 kilometres.
