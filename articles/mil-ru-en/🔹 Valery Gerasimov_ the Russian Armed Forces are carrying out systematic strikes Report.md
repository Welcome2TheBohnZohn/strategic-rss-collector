Valery Gerasimov: the Russian Armed Forces are carrying out systematic strikes
Report
by Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation – First Deputy Minister of Defenсe of the Russian Federation
In response to attempts by the Kiev regime to disrupt Russian shipping in the Black Sea, the Russian Armed Forces are carrying out systematic strikes against Ukrainian port and logistics infrastructure and vessels. This has led to the suspension of operations at the main ports in the Black Sea.
In accordance with the plan of the General Staff, strikes are also being carried out against strategically important bridges, railway transport facilities, and data centres involved in meeting the operational requirements of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
As a result of the strikes, 76 logistics and storage centres, 203 port infrastructure facilities, and 93 vessels transporting military cargo to Ukraine have been hit.
Moreover, the Russian Armed Forces have begun carrying out massive strikes against Ukrainian energy facilities. A list of these facilities has been drawn up, along with the required allocation of assets to engage them.
