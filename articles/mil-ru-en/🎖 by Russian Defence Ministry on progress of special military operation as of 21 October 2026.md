The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence and liberated Chervony Yar (Zaporozhye region).
🔥
Strikes were delivered at
manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade close to Novonikolayevka, Tersyanka, Barvinovka, Volnyanka, Voskresenka, and Vasinovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 220 troops, five armoured fighting vehicles, and five motor vehicles.
🚩
As a result of decisive actions, units of the Dnepr Group of Forces liberated Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
💥
Formations of one mechanised brigade of the AFU and one marine brigade
have been hit
close to Orekhov, Kirovo, and Odarovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 24 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station have been neutralised.

transport and power infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, ammunition depots, production workshops and launch sites of long-range UAVs, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 142 areas.
