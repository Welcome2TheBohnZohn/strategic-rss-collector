# Vladimir Putin welcomed children of special military operation heroes into the Kremlin for tea

Published: 2026-11-04 18:41:29

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30442

Vladimir Putin welcomed children of special military operation heroes into the Kremlin for tea
On National Unity Day, the President took part in several official events, including a ceremony to lay flowers at the monument to Kuzma Minin and Dmitry Pozharsky in Red Square. The ceremony was attended by representatives of religious denominations and members of non-governmental and youth organisations, but the Russian leader also spoke with children of special military operation heroes who were in Red Square.
According to Presidential Press Secretary Dmitry Peskov, Vladimir Putin invited children whose fathers died as heroes while serving in the special military operation to a tea party at the Kremlin.
State Secretary — Deputy Minister of Defence and Chair of the Defenders of the Fatherland Foundation Anna Tsivileva attended the event as well.
