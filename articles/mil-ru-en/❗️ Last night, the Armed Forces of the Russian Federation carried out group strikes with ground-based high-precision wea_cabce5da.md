# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out group strikes with ground-based high-precision weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles against defence industry e

Published: 2026-10-13 07:34:05

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out group strikes with ground-based high-precision weapons and unmanned aerial vehicles against defence industry enterprises, logistics centres, railway and port infrastructure facilities of Ukraine, as well as vessels
delivering military cargo to Ukraine.
💥
As a result of the strikes, the following targets
were hit:
📍
in Kiev:
▪️
the Palp Mill Print production workshop,
where long-range missiles and unmanned aerial vehicles were assembled;
▪️
a locomotive shed of Darnitsa,
where maintenance and repair of the locomotive fleet were carried out; this fleet was used to transport military and dual-use cargo for the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
📍
in Nikolayev region:
▪️
in the settlement of Ternovka,
the Novaya Pochta logistics centre
used to store dual-use cargo to support the AFU;
▪️
in the settlement of Meshkovo-Pogorelovo,
a UAV depot.
⚓️
In addition, in Bugskoy Liman,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military and dual-use cargo to the port of Nikolayev was hit.

Russian Defence Ministry
