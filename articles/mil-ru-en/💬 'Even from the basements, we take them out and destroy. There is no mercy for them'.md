💬
Unmanned systems battalion serviceman with the call sign 'Bely'
told how our lads
liberated
Annovka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
▫️
The operation was performed out of central casting.
Experienced commanders led assault groups into the settlement during the night, and UAV operators actively covered their comrades from heavy Ukrainian copters.

Russian drones forced AFU servicemen to go out even from fortified basements.
