We take important decisions to ensure military security of our states
—
Andrei Belousov
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov summed up the results of the Russia-Belarus Defence Ministry Board Session.
'Today, at the Defence Ministry Board Session,
important decisions have been made to ensure the military security of our countries,
' Andrei Belousov said.
❗️
The Russian Defence Minister stressed that, during the session,
priority was given to the functioning of the regional grouping of troops (forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation, as well as the Unified Regional Air Defence System.
'At the same time,
joint operational and combat training of troops remains a priority direction of our interaction.
We will continue to actively introduce into the training process the experience gained during the special military operation. To practise new methods and tactics of combat operations,' he noted.
🤝
The Russian Defence Minister added that
adopted decisions will serve to strengthen cooperation
between the military departments of Russia and Belarus.
▫️
In his turn, Minister of Defence of the Republic of Belarus Lieutenant General Viktor Khrenin noted that the
agreements reached during the board session would contribute to strengthening the military security of the Union State
'and allow the systematic development of capabilities to respond to emerging challenges and threats'.
📑
At the end of the Russia-Belarus Defence Ministry Board Session,
the heads of delegations signed the final documents.
