# ❗️ During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes against Ukrainian ports and vessels used in the interests of the AFU.

Published: 2026-09-15 16:01:05

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29426

❗️
During the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes against Ukrainian ports and vessels used in the interests of the AFU.
As a result of UAV strikes, the following targets were hit:
▪️
in the port of Chernomorsk, a Ro‑Ro ship
that delivered cargo to support the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
▪️
in the port of Izmail,
a tanker
that delivered fuel and lubricants for the AFU.
▪️
in the Yuzhny port, a rolling stock
used to support the AFU at the moment of cargo loading.
▪️
In addition, strikes against railway infrastructure facilities in the western regions of Ukraine
which are used to transport military cargo from European countries
have been continued.
