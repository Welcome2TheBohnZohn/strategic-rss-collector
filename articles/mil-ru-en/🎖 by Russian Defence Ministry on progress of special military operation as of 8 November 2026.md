↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continued to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Manpower and materiel of two assault brigades, four air assault brigades, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and two marine brigades have been hit close to Malinovka, Kolomiytsy, Brovki (Dnepropetrovsk region), Zheltaya Krucha, Novoviktorovka, Novonikolayevka, and Kamyshevakha (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 185 troops and seven motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Formations of a mechanised brigade, a mountain assault brigade, and an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine have been hit near Grigorovskoye, Preobrazhenka, and Yurkovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, 14 motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces
have inflicted damage on ammunition depots, energy facilities, transport infrastructure, and logistics used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, workshops producing UAVs and UGVs and their storage areas as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 141 areas.

three guided aerial bombs and 538 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
