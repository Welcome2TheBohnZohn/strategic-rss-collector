# ❗️ From 19 to 25 September, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two mass strikes and seven group strikes, as a result of which fire damage was inflicted on:

Published: 2026-09-25 09:55:25

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29606

❗️
From 19 to 25 September,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out two mass strikes and seven group strikes, as a result of which fire damage was inflicted on:
▫️
military plants, metallurgical industry enterprises, along with fuel and power facilities of Ukraine that kept them running,
▫️
logistics and telecommunication centres,
▫️
transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU,
▫️
military airfields,
▫️
UAV production and storage areas,
▫️
ammunition, fuel, and military hardware depots,
▫️
temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed groups and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition,
the strikes disabled a number of facilities of the infrastructure of the Ukrainian ports
, which provided for the reception, storage and distribution of military cargo, as well as fuel and lubricants used in the interests of the AFU.
🔹
During the week, Russian troops hit 18 ships
, including 15 dry cargo ships and three tankers involved in supplying Western weapons, military hardware, and fuel to the Armed Forces of Ukraine.
