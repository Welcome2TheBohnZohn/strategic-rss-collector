'Even greeting were forbidden': why the command of the AFU warns against communicating with residents of Kramatorsk
💬
According to POW Vitaly Mosin, the
AFU command prohibited soldiers from making any contact with the people of Kramatorsk because of their extremely hostile attitude towards the Ukrainian army.
They attributed this to the fact that beneath the surface politeness of the local residents hides a profound hatred toward the AFU, along with a willingness to resort to radical measures.
'We got to Kramatorsk. A company CO warned us not to communicate the locals, enter their houses, greet them at all. Like, you'll talk to him, he'll smile in your face. You turn away, he'll stab you in the back. So, we were not allowed to you even to say hello to them.'
