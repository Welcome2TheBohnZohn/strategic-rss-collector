📍
The Tsentr Group's units improved the tactical situation.
Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and three national guard brigades have been hit near Lidino, Andreyevka, Belozerskoye, Dobropolye, Novovodyanoye, Gulevo, Novogrigorovka, and Sergeyevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 400 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 12 motor vehicles.
🚩
The Vostok Group of Forces advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence and liberated Rozovka in Zaporozhye region.
💥
Strikes were delivered
at manpower and hardware of two air assault brigades, an assault brigade, two assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a marine brigade close to the Velikomikhaylovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Novovladimirovka, Tavriyskoye, Obshcheye, Vasilkovoye, Omelnik, Samoylovka, Svetlaya Dolina, and Barvinovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The AFU lost more than 240 troops, one armoured fighting vehicle, and 11 motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line
. Formations of two mechanised brigades and one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine were hit near Yulyevka, Orekhov, and Grigorovka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 30 troops, nine motor vehicles, and one electronic warfare station were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troop artillery of the Russian groups of forces
have inflicted damage on the Ukrainian energy and transport infrastructures, ammunition and military equipment depots, assembly workshops as well as storage facilities of unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, and temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 152 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems
shot down eight guided aerial bombs and 956 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
