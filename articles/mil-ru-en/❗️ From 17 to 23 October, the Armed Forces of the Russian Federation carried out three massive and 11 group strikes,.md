# ❗️ From 17 to 23 October, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation carried out three massive and 11 group strikes,

Published: 2026-10-23 10:30:41

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30234

❗️
From 17 to 23 October, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
carried out three massive and 11 group strikes,
which hit enterprises of the defence industry, logistics centres, power and transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, medium- and long-range UAV manufacturing workshops and depots, military hardware depots, and temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries.
💥
In addition, the Ukrainian ports' infrastructure facilities, which were used to provide reception, storing and distribution of military cargo,
have been disabled.
💥
During the week,
14 dry cargo ships and container ships supplying weapons, military hardware, and dual-use cargo to the Armed Forces of Ukraine were hit.
▫️
The Sever Group's units have completed neutralisation of the encircled enemy
in the north-eastern part of Kharkov region and continue to eliminate the enemy's solo groups.
📍
During the week, units of the Sever Group of Forces improved the situation along the front line.
Manpower and hardware of four mechanised brigades, two motorised infantry brigades, two assault brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two territorial defence brigades, and one border detachment of the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine have been hit.
▪️
Over the past week, in the Sever Group's area of responsibility, the enemy has lost more than 1,505 troops, seven armoured fighting vehicles, 124 motor vehicles, two field artillery guns, and 10 electronic warfare stations.
🚩
Over the past week, the Zapad Group's units have taken more advantageous positions and, as a result of decisive actions, have established control over the settlements of Palamarevka and Kovalevka (Kharkov region).
💥
Strikes have been delivered at
formations of three mechanised brigades, two assault brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 1,560 troops, 19 armoured fighting vehicles, 130 motor vehicles, 12 field artillery guns, and seven electronic warfare stations in this direction.
🚩
Last week, the Yuzhnaya Group's units improved the situation along the front line and liberated Alekseyevo-Druzhkovka in the Donetsk People's Republic.
💥
Manpower and hardware of
four mechanised brigades, one motorised infantry brigade, one mountain assault brigade, two airmobile brigades, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade
have been hit.
▪️
In total, the enemy has lost more than 1,275 troops, 16 armoured fighting vehicles, 221 motor vehicles, 10 field artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations in the area of responsibility of the Yuzhnaya Group of Forces.
