↗️
The Vostok Group of Forces continue to advance into the depths of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of three air assault brigades, two assault brigades, and an assault regiment of the AFU near Dibrova, Podgavrilovka, Pokrovskoye of Dnepropetrovsk region, Vasinovka, Zheltaya Krucha, and Odarovka of Zaporozhye region.
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to over 200 troops, one armoured personnel carrier, and four motor vehicles.
📍
The Dnepr Group's units improved the situation along the front line.
The Group's units engaged formations of a mountain assault brigade, a coastal defence brigade, an assault regiment of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade near Volnoandreyevka, Kushugum, Natalovka of Zaporozhye region, and Antonovka of Kherson region.
▪️
Up to 30 troops and 19 motor vehicles of the AFU were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-Tactical Aviation, attack drones, missile troops, and artillery of the Russian Groups of Forces inflicted damage on
military airfields, energy, transport infrastructure facilities used in the interests of the AFU, unmanned aerial vehicles workshops, storage and launch sites, ammunition depots, and temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations in 149 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
three guided bombs, one U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectile, and 1,065 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
