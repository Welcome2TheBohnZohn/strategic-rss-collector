# The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.

Published: 2026-08-27 10:41:34

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29068

The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue the special military operation.
📍
The Sever Group of Forces improved its tactical situation
. Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of a mechanised brigade, an airmobile brigade of the AFU, and a territorial defence brigade close to Ulanovo and Sadki (Sumy region).
💥
In Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, two territorial defence brigades, and a national guard brigade close to Prikolotnoye, Berezhnoye, Petropavlovka, Liptsy, and Slatino (Kharkov region).
▪️
The AFU lost up to 245 troops, three armoured fighting vehicles, 14 motor vehicles, and one field artillery gun.
↗️
The Zapad Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and a territorial defence brigade near Proletarskoye (Kharkov region), Sidorovo, Mayaki, and Krestishche (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted to up to 210 troops, 20 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and four electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line
. Strikes were delivered at formations of two mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, an assault brigade, and a mountain assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine near Kramatorsk, Druzhkovka, Semenovka, and Nikolayevka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 190 troops, 30 motor vehicles, and three field artillery guns.
↗️
The Tsentr Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions
. Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, an airborne brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two national guard brigades have been hit near Ivanovka, Novopavlovka (Dnepropetrovsk region), Andreyevka, Dobropolye, and Belozerskoye (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 390 troops, one Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and three radars.
