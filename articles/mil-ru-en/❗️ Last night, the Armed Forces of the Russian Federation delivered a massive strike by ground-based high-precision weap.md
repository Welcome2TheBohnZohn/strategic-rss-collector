# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike by ground-based high-precision weapons and attack drones at a communication centre in Kiev regi

Published: 2026-09-30 07:30:23

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29711

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered a massive strike by ground-based high-precision weapons and attack drones at a communication centre in Kiev region, Ukraine's energy system facilities ensuring military production, and the port infrastructure of Izmail.
The following targets were engaged:
📍
in Kiev, the De Novo data processing centre,
one of the main Internet exchange points that ensures the processing, storage, and transmission of information in the interests of the AFU;
📍
in Kiev region:
▫️
the Kievskaya electrical substation
(the settlement of Nalivaykovka), which ensures the transmission of electricity and its distribution to the defence industry facilities in Kiev and Kiev region;
▫️
the Kievskaya hydropower station
(the settlement of Vyshgorod), which supports defence industry facilities in the northern part of Kiev region;
▫️
the Tripolskaya thermal power plant,
which provides power to defence industry facilities in the southern part of Kiev region;
▫️
a battery energy storage
station
in Gorenichi, used for energy accumulation for uninterrupted operation of military facilities in Kiev and Kiev region;
📍
in Odessa region – production and industrial facilities
in the port of Ishmael, which provides reception, storage, and transportation of military cargo and fuel for the needs of the AFU.
💥
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
delivering military materiel and dual-use cargo for the AFU to the port of Odessa
was hit
in the north-western part of the Black Sea.
