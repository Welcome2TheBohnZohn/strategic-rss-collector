Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to launch strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against energy facilities, port and railway infrastructure, as well as vessels
used in the interests of providing support to the AFU.
💥
As a result of the strike, the following targets
were hit:
▫️
a
power substation
in Velikiye Mezhirichi (Rovno region), which operates logistics centres used by the AFU to store military equipment;
▫️
a ferry terminal, a port control station, and a power substation i
n the Chernomorsk port of Odessa region, which ensure the unloading and storage of military cargo delivered to Ukraine;
▫️
a dry cargo ship
delivering military and dual-use goods to the AFU to the port of Odessa during a sea passage.
🔥
In addition,
two railway locomotives were hit
close to Dnepropetrovsk station, which are used to transport goods used to support the AFU.
