Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to launch strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against the facilities of Ukraine's port infrastructure, as well as ships operating in the interests of the AFU.
As a result of the strikes, the following targets were hit:
📍
in
Khmelnitsky region:
💥
The Grand Import logistics centre in the city of Khmelnitsky,
which is used to store military and dual-use cargo of the Armed Forces of Ukraine;
📍
in
Volyn region:
💥
the Vendor Trans Service LLC transport and logistics centre in Kovel,
used for storage and distribution of AFU cargo and materiel;
📍
in
Odessa region:
💥
three dry cargo vessels
unloading military and dual-use cargo used by the AFU, as well as port infrastructure facilities that support the operation of cargo terminals in the port of Reni.
💥
In addition,
one dry cargo ship
delivering military cargo to Ukraine was hit
on its way to the port of Odessa.
