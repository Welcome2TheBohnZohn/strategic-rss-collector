# ❗️ Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against port and transport infrastructures as well as logistics

Published: 2026-09-26 06:50:18

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29625

❗️
Last night, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continued to carry out strikes by unmanned aerial vehicles against port and transport infrastructures as well as logistics and communications centres
that supported the AFU.
💥
The strikes
hit
:
📍
in Kiev:
▫️
the Kosmonova data processing centre
, which provided mobile internet services and supported Starlink satellite communications systems in the interests of the AFU;
📍
in Odessa and Odessa region:
▫️
a port facility in Vilkovo
, through which weapons and ammunition from European states were delivered to Ukraine;
▫️
a road bridge in Mayaki
, which was used to transport military-purpose cargo to AFU units;
▫️
a Novaya Pochta logistics centre
, which stored military cargo for the AFU.
🔥
Furthermore,
a dry cargo ship
carrying military-purpose cargo was struck in the Black Sea.

Russian Defence Ministry
