Meeting the challenges of the times: we continue to strengthen CSTO's force capabilities
Russian Defence Minister Andrei Belousov took part in a joint meeting
of the Council of Ministers of Foreign Affairs, the Council of Ministers of Defence, and the Committee of Secretaries of Security Councils of the Collective Security Treaty Organisation.
📍
Moscow
💬
The officials discussed
▫️
the military and political situation in the CSTO collective security regions,
▫️
the state and prospects of the global and regional security sphere,
▫️
implementation of the priorities of the Russian Federation's chairmanship over the Organisation this year.
✅
The session took a number of joint decisions aimed
at strengthening the CSTO's force capabilities.
🤝
At the end of the joint meeting,
the participants reviewed the Organization’s activities during the intersessional period and planned events for the upcoming year.
