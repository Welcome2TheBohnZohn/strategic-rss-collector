🚩
As a result of active actions, units of the Sever Group of Forces
established control over Velikiy Prikol and Sadki
(Sumy region).
💥
Manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades and an airmobile brigade of the AFU
have been hit
near Barilovka, Kiyanitsa, Nikolskoye, Velikaya Rybitsa and Yunakovka (Sumy region).
🔥
In Kharkov region,
strikes were delivered at formations of four mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and two territorial defence brigades close to Prikolotnoye, Gogino, Slatino, Izbitskoye, Novoaleksandrovka, and Podsredneye in Kharkov region.
▪️
The AFU lost more than 230 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, 13 motor vehicles, and three electronic warfare stations.
📍
The Zapad Group of Forces took more advantageous lines and positions.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and two territorial defence brigades were hit close to Izyumskoye, Staroverovka, Grushevka, Peski-Radkovskiye, Maleevka, Prokopovka (Kharkov region), Svyatogorsk, and Karpovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy losses amounted more than 210 troops, a U.S.-made M1117 armoured personnel carrier, a Kazak armoured fighting vehicle, a Canadian-made Titan-S armoured vehicle, and 20 motor vehicles.
📍
The Yuzhnaya Group of Forces improved the tactical situation along the front line.
Formations of three mechanised brigades, a motorised infantry brigade, two airmobile brigades, a mountain assault brigade, an assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and a territorial defence brigade were hit near Klinovoye, Rayskoye, Slavyansk, Kramatorsk, Nikanorovka, Novoselovka, Druzhkovka and Kondratovka (Donetsk People's Republic).
▪️
The enemy lost up to 240 troops, a U.S.-made Stryker armoured personnel carrier, 26 motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and one Grad MLRS combat vehicle.
📍
The Tsentr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
Manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, a jaeger brigade, an assault brigade of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, a marine brigade, and three national guard brigades were hit near Belozerskoye, Andreyevka, Starorayskoye, Veselaya Gora, Dobropolye, Slavyanka, Fedorovka (Donetsk People's Republic), and Ivanovka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▫️
The AFU losses amounted to up to 375 troops, a Canadian-made Senator armoured fighting vehicle, seven motor vehicles, and three field artillery guns.
