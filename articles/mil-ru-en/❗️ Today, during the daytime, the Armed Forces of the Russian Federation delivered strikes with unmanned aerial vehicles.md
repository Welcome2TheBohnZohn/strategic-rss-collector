# ❗️ Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against energy facilities, port and railway infrastructure,

Published: 2026-11-13 17:02:01

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/30595

❗️
Today, during the daytime, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation delivered strikes with unmanned aerial vehicles against energy facilities, port and railway infrastructure, as well as maritime vessels involved in delivering military cargo to Ukraine.
▪️
As a result of the strike,
the PS-110 and PS-330 electrical substations
were hit in Shostka and Voronezh in Sumy region; these substations support the operation of defence industry enterprises producing unmanned aerial vehicles and their components, as well as logistics centres used for storing military property.
▪️
In addition,
a dry cargo ship
was hit at sea; it had been delivering military cargo to the port of Chernomorsk.
Two railway locomotives
were also hit in the area of the Serbka railway station in Odessa region; they were used for transporting cargo intended for the support of the Ukrainian Armed Forces.
