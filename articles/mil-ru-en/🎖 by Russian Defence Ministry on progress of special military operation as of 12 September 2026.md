🚩
As a result of the resolute actions, the Tsentr Group of Forces liberated Veseloye in the Donetsk People's Republic.
💥
The losses were inflicted
on manpower and hardware of three mechanised brigades, one assault regiment of the AFU, and four national guard brigades near Belozerskoye, Novovodyanoye, Andreyevka, Verovka (Donetsk People's Republic), and Maryevka (Dnepropetrovsk region).
▪️
The AFU losses amounted to more than 460 troops, two armoured fighting vehicles, nine motor vehicles, two artillery guns, and three electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Vostok Group's units advanced to the depth of the enemy's defence.
Russian troops inflicted losses on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade, one assault brigade, two air assault brigades, two assault regiments, one unmanned systems regiment of the AFU, and one marine brigade close to Avramovka, Otrishki (Dnepropetrovsk region), Obshcheye, Vasinovka, Barvinovka, Omelnik, and Trudovoye (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
The enemy lost more than 265 troops, an armoured fighting vehicle, eight motor vehicles, and one U.S.-made 155-mm M777 howitzer.
📍
The Dnepr Group of Forces' units took more advantageous lines and positions.
They inflicted damage on manpower and hardware of one mechanised brigade and one mountain assault brigade of the AFU close to Orekhov, Preobrazhenka, Kamyshevakha, and Yulyevka (Zaporozhye region).
▪️
Up to 50 troops, 24 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations were neutralised.
✈️
Operational-tactical aviation, unmanned aerial vehicles, missile troops, and artillery of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation have inflicted damage
on the logistics centres, fuel, power, and transport infrastructure of Ukraine, production facilities of unmanned aerial vehicles and unmanned ground vehicles, as well as temporary deployment areas of Ukrainian armed formations and foreign mercenaries in 155 areas.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
seven guided aerial bombs and 670 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
