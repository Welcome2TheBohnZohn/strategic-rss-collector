Diligence is mother of success: intensive combat training takes place at training grounds of Moscow Military District
The following exercises are trained:
▫️
Tactical medicine:
first aid, mutual aid, and rapid evacuation of wounded.
▫️
Firing:
intensive firing with weapons, including assault rifles and PKT machine guns, grenade throwing, as well as practical exercises on mortars and ATGMs operation, capturing and equipping positions.
▫️
The program includes
mandatory exercises on NBC protection and special trainings on dodging FPV-drones
in forest belt conditions.
