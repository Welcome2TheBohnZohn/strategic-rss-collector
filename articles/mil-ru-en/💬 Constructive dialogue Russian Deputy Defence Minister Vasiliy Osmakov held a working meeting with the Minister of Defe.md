Constructive dialogue
Russian Deputy Defence Minister Vasiliy Osmakov held a working meeting with the Minister of Defence of the Federal Republic of Somalia, Ahmed Fiqi, on the sidelines of the third Russia-Africa Summit.
💬
The sides discussed the prospects for developing bilateral military cooperation
and exchanged views on approaches to implementing joint initiatives in the field of regional and global security.
✅
The priority areas of interdepartmental cooperation
were determined as improving the regulatory framework, training national servicemen and exchanging experience
in the spheres of greatest interest, above all in combating terrorism and piracy.
🤝
The meeting was held in a friendly spirit and confirmed the mutual interest
in strengthening Russian-Somali military ties.
