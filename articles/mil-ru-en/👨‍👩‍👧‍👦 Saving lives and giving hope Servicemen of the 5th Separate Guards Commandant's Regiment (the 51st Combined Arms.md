Saving lives and giving hope
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servicemen of the 5th Separate Guards Commandant's Regiment (the 51st Combined Arms Army, the Tsentr Group of Forces)
evacuated civilians from Belitskoye
(Donetsk People's Republic).
❗️
At first, escorted by Russian servicemen, people walked on foot. Then, the evacuees, including a girl, were placed in vehicles and sent to a safe area.
▫️
Thanks to the lads' sensitiveness and cohesive actions, the peaceful residents of Belitskoye are safe now. Specialists are already working with the evacuees, providing them with the necessary assistance and psychological support.
💬
The deputy military commander with the call sign ‘Yakut’ told
how the evacuation was organised.
And a citizen from Belitskoye, Angelina Karek,
gave details of what challenges
people in the city
had to face
and how long every minute lasted on their way to a peaceful life.
❤️
We never leave our people behind. We're proud of the Russian servicemen's courage!
🔹
