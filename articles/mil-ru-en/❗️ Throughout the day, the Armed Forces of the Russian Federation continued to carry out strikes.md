# ❗️ Throughout the day, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes

Published: 2026-09-17 16:50:03

Source: https://t.me/mod_russia_en/29466

Throughout the day,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continued to carry out strikes
by unmanned aerial vehicles against Ukrainian ports as well as vessels used in support of the AFU.
🔥

a dry cargo ship
carrying military cargo was hit in the port of Chernomorsk, Odessa region.
💥

a dry cargo ship
carrying military equipment to the port of Izmail to support the Ukrainian Armed Forces was hit at sea.
