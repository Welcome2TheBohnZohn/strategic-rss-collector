🚩
The Vostok Group's units continued to advance to the depths of the enemy's defence and
liberated Zorevka (Zaporozhye region) during the day.
💥
The Russian troops inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of
two mechanised brigades, three air assault brigades, two assault brigades, three assault regiments of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, one marine brigade, and one territorial defence brigade.
▪️
The AFU losses in the area of responsibility of the Vostok Group of Forces amounted to more than 2,215 servicemen, 12 armoured fighting vehicles, 56 motor vehicles, and two electronic warfare stations.
↗️
The Dnepr Group of Forces improved the tactical situation.
They inflicted fire damage on manpower and hardware of two mechanised brigades, one mountain assault brigade, one unmanned systems brigade, one assault regiment of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, and one marine brigade.
▪️
During the week, more than 290 troops, six armoured fighting vehicles, 122 motor vehicles, and eight electronic warfare and counter-fire radar stations were neutralised.
▫️
Air defence systems shot down
48 guided aerial bombs, 14 U.S.-made HIMARS MLRS projectiles, four Flamingo long-range cruise missiles, two Neptune long-range cruise missiles, and 5,673 fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles.
💥
In the Black Sea waters, the Black Sea Fleet
eliminated 12 uncrewed surface vehicles of the Ukrainian Navy.
