中国海军将举办2026年西太海军论坛人道主义救援减灾研讨与桌面推演
The PLA Navy to Host the 2026 Western Pacific Naval Symposium HADR Workshop and Tabletop Exercise
11月26日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校发布消息。
陈曦：
12月上旬，中国海军将在南京举办2026年西太平洋海军论坛人道主义救援减灾研讨与桌面推演，邀请西太海军论坛成员国和观察员国代表参会。此次活动以“地震灾害背景下的多国海军人道主义联合救援减灾行动”为课题，重点演练指挥链整合、信息共享、行动协同等内容，并就人道主义救援减灾行动的规则遵循、组织指挥、行动样式等开展专题研讨，有助于进一步深化西太地区人道主义救援减灾务实合作，提升防范应对危机的协同能力。
Chen Xi:
In early December, the PLA Navy will host the 2026 Western Pacific Naval Symposium (WPNS) Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) Seminar and Tabletop Exercise in Nanjing. Representatives from member and observer states are invited to attend. Under the theme of "Multi-navy HADR in the Aftermath of an Earthquake", the exercise will focus on command chain integration, information sharing and operational coordination. Seminars will also be held on topics including rule compliance, command organization and operational patterns for HADR activities. This event will further deepen substantive cooperation on HADR in the Western Pacific region and enhance interoperability in crisis prevention and response.
