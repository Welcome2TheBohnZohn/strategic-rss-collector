# Russia’s Grand Mufti praises Pezeshkian’s UN Speech

Published: 2026-09-29 17:00:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278668/Russia-s-Grand-Mufti-praises-Pezeshkian-s-UN-Speech

Moscow, IRNA – Russia’s Grand Mufti reaffirmed the solidarity of Russian Muslims with Iran and praised President Masoud Pezeshkian’s speech at the United Nations General Assembly.
