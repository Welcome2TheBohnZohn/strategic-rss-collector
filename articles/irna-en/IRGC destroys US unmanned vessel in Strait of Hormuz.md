# IRGC destroys US unmanned vessel in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-09-10 19:00:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260629/IRGC-destroys-US-unmanned-vessel-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that it had struck and destroyed a US unmanned surface vessel at the entrance to the Strait of Hormuz.
