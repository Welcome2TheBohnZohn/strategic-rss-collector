# Iranian Navy chief says wartime blockade signals adversary’s failure

Published: 2026-11-28 06:10:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86334270/Iranian-Navy-chief-says-wartime-blockade-signals-adversary-s

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Navy commander, Rear Admiral Shahram Irani, says a maritime blockade imposed during the war signals that the opposing side has failed to achieve its objectives, adding that Iran will face no geographical limits in operations under directives issued by the country’s leader.
