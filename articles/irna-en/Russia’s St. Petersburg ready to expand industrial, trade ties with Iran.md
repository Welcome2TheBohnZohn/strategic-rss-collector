# Russia’s St. Petersburg ready to expand industrial, trade ties with Iran

Published: 2026-11-14 12:14:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321337/Russia-s-St-Petersburg-ready-to-expand-industrial-trade-ties

Tehran, IRNA – Officials from St. Petersburg have expressed readiness to expand industrial, scientific, and trade cooperation with Iran in various fields, Iran’s Mines and Mining Industries Development and Renovation Organization (IMIDRO) announces.
