# UN chief gravely concerned by renewed US-Iran exchange of fire: Spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-02 19:06:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253728/UN-chief-gravely-concerned-by-renewed-US-Iran-exchange-of-fire

New York, IRNA – UN spokesperson Stephane Dujarric said on Wednesday during a news conference that "I can tell the secretary-general is gravely concerned by the renewed exchange of fire overnight between the United States and the Islamic Republic of Iran. The secretary-general is deeply alarmed by reports of civilian casualties, including a strike that reportedly hit a wedding ceremony in Iran."
