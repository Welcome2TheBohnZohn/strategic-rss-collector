# Ghalibaf: Leader’s message is a guiding principle for all officials

Published: 2026-08-29 05:56:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248953/Ghalibaf-Leader-s-message-is-a-guiding-principle-for-all-officials

Tehran, IRNA — Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says the message of Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei on Government Week is a guiding principle for all Iranian officials.
