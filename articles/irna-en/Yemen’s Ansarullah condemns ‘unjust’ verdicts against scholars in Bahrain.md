# Yemen’s Ansarullah condemns ‘unjust’ verdicts against scholars in Bahrain

Published: 2026-11-18 06:57:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325306/Yemen-s-Ansarullah-condemns-unjust-verdicts-against-scholars

Tehran, IRNA – Yemen's Ansarullah movement has denounced the Bahraini government for its rulings against religious scholars in that country, describing the verdicts as unjust and aimed at bringing the Bahraini nation to its knees.
