# Pezeshkian stresses full coordination to preserve Iran’s economic stability

Published: 2026-08-26 10:21:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86246711/Pezeshkian-stresses-full-coordination-to-preserve-Iran-s-economic

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has stressed the need for full coordination among Iran’s economic institutions to maintain economic stability and contain inflationary expectations amid mounting external pressures.
