# Baqaei: Iran’s actions in Strait of Hormuz purely defensive

Published: 2026-09-07 10:05:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257351/Baqaei-Iran-s-actions-in-Strait-of-Hormuz-purely-defensive

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has said Iran’s actions in the Strait of Hormuz are quite defensive and are being carried out in response to the ongoing military aggression of the US and the Israeli regime.
