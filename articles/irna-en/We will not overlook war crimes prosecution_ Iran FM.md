# We will not overlook war crimes prosecution: Iran FM

Published: 2026-09-29 10:41:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86278242/We-will-not-overlook-war-crimes-prosecution-Iran-FM

In a meeting with the UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres on Monday, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi stressed Iran’s determination to defend its national sovereignty and territorial integrity with all its might, saying, “We do not overlook the prosecution and punishment of the commanders and perpetrators of war crimes.”
