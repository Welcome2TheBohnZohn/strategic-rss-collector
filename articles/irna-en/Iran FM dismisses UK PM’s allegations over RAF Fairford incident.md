# Iran FM dismisses UK PM’s allegations over RAF Fairford incident

Published: 2026-10-01 05:31:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279956/Iran-FM-dismisses-UK-PM-s-allegations-over-RAF-Fairford-incident

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Seyyed Abbas Araghchi has categorically rejected allegations by British Prime Minister Burnham that Iran was involved in an incident at the Royal Air Force (RAF) Fairford base.
