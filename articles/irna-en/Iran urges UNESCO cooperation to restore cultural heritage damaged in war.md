# Iran urges UNESCO cooperation to restore cultural heritage damaged in war

Published: 2026-09-30 17:06:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279757/Iran-urges-UNESCO-cooperation-to-restore-cultural-heritage-damaged

Tehran, IRNA – An official from Iran’s Ministry of Cultural Heritage and Tourism has called for strengthened international cooperation, particularly from UNESCO, to assess, document, and restore Iranian cultural heritage damaged in US attacks.
