# President Pezeshkian meets with Armenian PM

Published: 2026-11-11 14:53:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319359/President-Pezeshkian-meets-with-Armenian-PM

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian and Armenian Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan met on the sidelines of the 31st UN climate change conference (COP31) in Antalya, Turkiye, on Wednesday.
