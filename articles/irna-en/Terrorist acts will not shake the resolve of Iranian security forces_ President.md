# Terrorist acts will not shake the resolve of Iranian security forces: President

Published: 2026-10-10 14:21:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288715/Terrorist-acts-will-not-shake-the-resolve-of-Iranian-security

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says the security forces’ resolve will never be shaken by “blind terrorist acts, after a senior police official was assassinated in southeastern Sistan and Baluchestan Province.
