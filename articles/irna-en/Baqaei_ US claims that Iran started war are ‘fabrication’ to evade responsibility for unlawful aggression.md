# Baqaei: US claims that Iran started war are ‘fabrication’ to evade responsibility for unlawful aggression

Published: 2026-11-15 06:24:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322000/Baqaei-US-claims-that-Iran-started-war-are-fabrication-to

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has strongly reacted to US President Donald Trump’s claim that the ongoing war was started by Iran with its 1979 Islamic Revolution, saying that such a “deceptive narrative” is a fabrication intended to evade responsibility for the unlawful “war of choice” against Iran.
