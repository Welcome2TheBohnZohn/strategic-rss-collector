# Iranian envoy attends Caspian Sea Littoral States meeting

Published: 2026-10-08 08:51:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286756/Iranian-envoy-attends-Caspian-Sea-Littoral-States-meeting

Tehran, IRNA – Kazem Gharibabadi, Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs, has attended the meeting of the Working Group of Senior Officials of the Caspian Sea littoral states in Avaza, Turkmenistan.
