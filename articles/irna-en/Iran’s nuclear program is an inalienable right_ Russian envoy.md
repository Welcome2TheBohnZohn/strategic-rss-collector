# Iran’s nuclear program is an inalienable right: Russian envoy

Published: 2026-10-03 06:34:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281354/Iran-s-nuclear-program-is-an-inalienable-right-Russian-envoy

Tehran, IRNA – Russia’s Permanent Representative to International Organizations in Vienna Mikhail Ulyanov has rejected allegations by the US envoy to the UN over Iran’s nuclear program, saying that it is an inalienable right of Iran to have nuclear program.
