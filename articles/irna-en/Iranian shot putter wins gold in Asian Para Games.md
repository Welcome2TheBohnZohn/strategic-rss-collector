# Iranian shot putter wins gold in Asian Para Games

Published: 2026-10-20 14:51:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298554/Iranian-shot-putter-wins-gold-in-Asian-Para-Games

Nagoya, IRNA – Shot putter Yasin Khosravi from Iran has won the gold medal at the para-athletics competition at the 2026 Asian Para Games in Nagoya, Japan.
