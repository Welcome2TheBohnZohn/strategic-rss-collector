# Another violating tanker catches fire east of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-14 11:40:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321481/Another-violating-tanker-catches-fire-east-of-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Navy reported on Saturday that another offending oil tanker caught fire earlier in the day, after striking a naval mine in a prohibited area east of the Strait of Hormuz.
