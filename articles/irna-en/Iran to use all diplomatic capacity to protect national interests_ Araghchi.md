# Iran to use all diplomatic capacity to protect national interests: Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-05 13:53:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284092/Iran-to-use-all-diplomatic-capacity-to-protect-national-interests

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will use all its diplomatic capacity to safeguard national interests and strengthen the country’s position and security, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says.
