# Ghalibaf warns of threat of attacks on Iran’s dams

Published: 2026-11-17 06:12:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324054/Ghalibaf-warns-of-threat-of-attacks-on-Iran-s-dams

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has criticized a New York Times report on Iran’s dams, warning against attempts to justify attacks on civilian infrastructure.
