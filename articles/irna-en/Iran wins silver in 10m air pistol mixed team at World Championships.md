# Iran wins silver in 10m air pistol mixed team at World Championships

Published: 2026-11-07 06:40:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314360/Iran-wins-silver-in-10m-air-pistol-mixed-team-at-World-Championships

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Haniyeh Rostamiyan and Vahid Golkhandan have claimed the silver medal in the 10-meter air pistol mixed team event at the 2026 ISSF World Championship in Doha, Qatar.
