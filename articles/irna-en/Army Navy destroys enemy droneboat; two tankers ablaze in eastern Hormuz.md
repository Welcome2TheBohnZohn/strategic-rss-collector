# Army Navy destroys enemy droneboat; two tankers ablaze in eastern Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-30 13:20:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307528/Army-Navy-destroys-enemy-droneboat-two-tankers-ablaze-in-eastern

Tehran, IRNA – The Navy of the Islamic Republic of Iran has announced that an enemy Unmanned Surface Vehicle (USV) was destroyed and that two oil tankers struck mines in the eastern Strait of Hormuz.
