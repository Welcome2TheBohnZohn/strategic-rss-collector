# Gharibabadi: Europe was part of infrastructure of US aggression against Iran

Published: 2026-09-25 03:56:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273860/Gharibabadi-Europe-was-part-of-infrastructure-of-US-aggression

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs, Kazem Gharibabadi, has said NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte’s admission that some 5,000 sorties were flown from European bases in support of US military aggression against Iran proves that Europe formed part of the infrastructure of that aggression.
