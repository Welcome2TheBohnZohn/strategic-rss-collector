# Tehran denies Kuwaiti claims of attack by Iranian forces

Published: 2026-11-26 16:43:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333502/Iran-denies-Kuwaiti-claims-of-attack-by-Iranian-forces

Tehran, IRNA — The senior spokesperson for Iran’s Armed Forces has categorically denied claims by the Kuwaiti army that Iranian forces attacked a vital facility in Kuwait.
