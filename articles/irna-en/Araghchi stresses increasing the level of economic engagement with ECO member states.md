# Araghchi stresses increasing the level of economic engagement with ECO member states

Published: 2026-11-16 21:19:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323916/Araghchi-stresses-increasing-the-level-of-economic-engagement

Islamabad, IRNA — Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in a meeting with his Pakistani counterpart, Ishaq Dar, while expressing appreciation for Pakistan’s gracious hospitality and the successful hosting of the 30th meeting of the Council of Ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO), emphasized the strengthening of multilateral cooperation, the enhancement of economic and commercial engagement, and the expansion of coordination among member states.
