# Envoy: Sanctions constitute act of aggression under international law

Published: 2026-10-28 07:10:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305634/Envoy-Sanctions-constitute-act-of-aggression-under-international

New York, IRNA 2 Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations, Gholamhossein Darzi, has described sanctions as an example of economic terrorism, saying they constitute acts of aggression under international law.
