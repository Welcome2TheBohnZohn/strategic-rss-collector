# Chinese foreign minister says Beijing supports Iran’s sovereignty and security

Published: 2026-11-02 14:55:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310431/Chinese-foreign-minister-says-Beijing-supports-Iran-s-sovereignty

Tehran, IRNA – China supports Iran in safeguarding its national sovereignty and security and opposes a return to military confrontation in the Middle East, Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi said during a meeting with Ali Bagheri, deputy secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, in Beijing on Monday.
