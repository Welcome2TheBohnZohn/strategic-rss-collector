# Armed Forces strengthen diplomats’ hand at negotiating table: Commander

Published: 2026-11-03 14:13:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311439/Armed-Forces-strengthen-diplomats-hand-at-negotiating-table

Tehran, IRNA — Deputy Executive Coordinator of the Iranian Army Brigadier General Alireza Sheikh has emphasized the Armed Forces’ support for the country’s diplomacy, saying they strengthen diplomats’ bargaining power to ensure they negotiate from a position of strength.
