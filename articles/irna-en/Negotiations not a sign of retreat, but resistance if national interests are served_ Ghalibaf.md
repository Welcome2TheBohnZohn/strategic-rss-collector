# Negotiations not a sign of retreat, but resistance if national interests are served: Ghalibaf

Published: 2026-08-26 13:51:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86246955/Negotiations-not-a-sign-of-retreat-but-resistance-if-national

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says negotiations with adversaries become meaningful if they help advance national interests, realize the rights of the nation, eliminate oppression, and consolidate the achievements of the resistance.
