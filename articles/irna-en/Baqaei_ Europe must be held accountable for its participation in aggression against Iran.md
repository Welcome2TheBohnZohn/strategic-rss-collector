# Baqaei: Europe must be held accountable for its participation in aggression against Iran

Published: 2026-09-25 20:46:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274380/Baqaei-Europe-must-be-held-accountable-for-its-participation

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei In response to the NATO Secretary General's remarks regarding military assistance to the US and the Zionist regime for an act of aggression against Iran, has said that the European Union, its member states, and NATO members must be held accountable for their role and participation in the military aggression by the US and the Israeli regime against Iran.
