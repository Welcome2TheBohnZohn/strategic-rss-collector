# Iran seizes 50 border trespassers, 2 vessels in southern waters

Published: 2026-09-28 13:04:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277358/Iran-seizes-50-border-trespassers-2-vessels-in-southern-waters

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – The Border Guard Command of Hormozgan Province in southern Iran said that in separate operations, 50 border trespassers were arrested and 2 smuggleing vessels were seized in the waters of Minab and Qeshm.
