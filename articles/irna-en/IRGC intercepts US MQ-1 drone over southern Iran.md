# IRGC intercepts US MQ-1 drone over southern Iran

Published: 2026-11-06 14:36:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314072/IRGC-intercepts-US-MQ-1-drone-over-southern-Iran

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) has announced that another US MQ-1 reconnaissance-combat drone was intercepted and destroyed by a new advanced air defense system in the southern skies over Qeshm Island.
