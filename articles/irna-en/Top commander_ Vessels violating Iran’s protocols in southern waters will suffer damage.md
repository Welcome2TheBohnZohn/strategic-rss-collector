# Top commander: Vessels violating Iran’s protocols in southern waters will suffer damage

Published: 2026-10-26 21:10:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304339/Top-commander-Vessels-violating-Iran-s-protocols-in-southern

Tehran, IRNA – Iran's Armed Forces Chief of Staff Major General Ali Abdollahi has warned that any vessel violating Iranian security protocols in the southern waters “will put itself at risk and face serious damage.”
