# Leader’s message to elites, driving force behind Iran’s progress: Adviser

Published: 2026-10-27 18:21:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305329/Leader-s-message-to-elites-driving-force-behind-Iran-s-progress

Tehran, IRNA – The adviser to Iran’s Supreme Leader stated that the development of the “domain of eliteness,” highlighted in the Leader of the Islamic Revolution’s recent message, is the driving force behind the country’s indigenous progress.
