# Iran’s Armed Forces vow to defend nation to last drop of blood

Published: 2026-09-16 09:51:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265746/Iran-s-Armed-Forces-vow-to-defend-nation-to-last-drop-of-blood

Tehran, IRNA – The Senior Spokesperson of the Armed Forces, Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi, assures that the Armed Forces will guard the security, dignity, and authority of Iran to the last breath.
