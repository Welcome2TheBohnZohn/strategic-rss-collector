# Acting Defense Minister: Iran Changed Balance of Power in Region

Published: 2026-08-28 05:17:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248238/Acting-Defense-Minister-Iran-Changed-Balance-of-Power-in-Region

Tehran, IRNA – Iran's acting Defense Minister Brigadier General Majid Ibn Reza, affirmed on Thursday that his country has changed the balance of power in the region and proven that the era of sanctions, threats, and reaping the benefits from across the oceans is over.
