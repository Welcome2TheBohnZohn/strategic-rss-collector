# Iranian Navy spots oil tanker on fire in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-16 13:53:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294247/Iranian-Navy-spots-oil-tanker-on-fire-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian Navy surveillance teams have detected and identified a non-compliant oil tanker that had caught fire in the eastern Strait of Hormuz while attempting to pass through a prohibited area.
