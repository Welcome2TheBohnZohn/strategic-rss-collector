# Iran embassy in Indonesia highlights Pezeshkian’s UN message

Published: 2026-09-24 08:40:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86273286/Iran-embassy-in-Indonesia-highlights-Pezeshkian-s-UN-message

Iran’s embassy in Indonesia posted an image on social media on Thursday featuring excerpts from President Masoud Pezeshkian’s address to the United Nations General Assembly a day earlier, highlighting his messages that Iran is a victim of terrorism, that its power is for defense rather than aggression, and that Tehran remains ready for dialogue while rejecting the language of force.
