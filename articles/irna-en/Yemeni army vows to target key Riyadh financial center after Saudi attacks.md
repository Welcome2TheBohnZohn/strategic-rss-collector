# Yemeni army vows to target key Riyadh financial center after Saudi attacks

Published: 2026-11-20 15:20:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327596/Yemeni-army-vows-to-target-key-Riyadh-financial-center-after

Tehran, IRNA – The Yemeni Army announced on Friday that the King Abdullah Financial District in the city of Riyadh and Saudi Arabia’s Al-Shuqaiq power plant are among its legitimate targets.
