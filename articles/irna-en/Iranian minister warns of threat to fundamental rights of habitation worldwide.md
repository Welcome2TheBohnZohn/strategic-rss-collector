# Iranian minister warns of threat to fundamental rights of habitation worldwide

Published: 2026-10-12 09:23:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290477/Iranian-minister-warns-of-threat-to-fundamental-rights-of-habitation

Tehran, IRNA – Minister of Roads and Urban Development Farzaneh Sadegh has described the attacks on educational and residential areas during armed conflicts as a violation of fundamental human rights, as well as a threat to morality and humanity.
