# Iran’s Italy embassy warns against efforts to draw Europe into regional crisis following Kuwait attack

Published: 2026-11-27 06:39:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333718/Iran-s-Italy-embassy-warns-against-efforts-to-draw-Europe-into

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s embassy in Rome has rejected allegations that Iran was responsible for a drone attack on a military facility in Kuwait that killed two Italian nationals, warning against attempts to widen the regional conflict and draw Europe into it.
