# Enemy repeated Ba’athists’ miscalculation: Amoli Larijani

Published: 2026-09-22 11:13:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271269/Enemy-repeated-Ba-athists-miscalculation-Amoli-Larijani

Tehran, IRNA – Chairman of Iran’s Expediency Council Ayatollah Sadeq Amoli Larijani says the United States and Israeli regime repeated the same “miscalculation” made by former Iraqi dictator Saddam Hussein and his supporters by believing that military action against Iran would quickly achieve their objectives.
