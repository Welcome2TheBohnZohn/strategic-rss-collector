# Iran, Oman and Uzbekistan discuss maritime freedom and transit cooperation

Published: 2026-11-17 14:53:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324848/Iran-Oman-and-Uzbekistan-discuss-maritime-freedom-and-transit

Tehran, IRNA – The foreign ministers of Iran, Oman and Uzbekistan have discussed ways to strengthen transit cooperation linking Central Asia to regional waterways and stressed the importance of restoring freedom of navigation and ensuring safe maritime passage for international trade.
