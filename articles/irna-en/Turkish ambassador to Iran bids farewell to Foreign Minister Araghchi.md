# Turkish ambassador to Iran bids farewell to Foreign Minister Araghchi

Published: 2026-09-02 18:15:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253704/Turkish-ambassador-to-Iran-bids-farewell-to-Foreign-Minister

Tehran, IRNA – Upon the conclusion of his diplomatic mission in the Islamic Republic of Iran, the Turkish ambassador visited the Ministry of Foreign Affairs on Wednesday to meet with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and bid farewell to him.
