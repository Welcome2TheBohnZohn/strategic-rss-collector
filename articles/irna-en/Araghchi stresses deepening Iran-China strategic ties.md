# Araghchi stresses deepening Iran-China strategic ties

Published: 2026-08-26 10:09:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86246514/Araghchi-stresses-deepening-Iran-China-strategic-ties

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has stressed the need to further develop strategic relations between Iran and China and implement the two countries’ 25-year Comprehensive Cooperation Program.
