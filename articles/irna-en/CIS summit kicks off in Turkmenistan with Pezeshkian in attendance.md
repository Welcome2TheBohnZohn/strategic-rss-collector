# CIS summit kicks off in Turkmenistan with Pezeshkian in attendance

Published: 2026-10-09 09:03:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287483/CIS-summit-kicks-off-in-Turkmenistan-with-Pezeshkian-in-attendance

Moscow, IRNA – The summit of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) has started at Turkmenistan’s Avaza national tourism zone, with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian in attendance.
