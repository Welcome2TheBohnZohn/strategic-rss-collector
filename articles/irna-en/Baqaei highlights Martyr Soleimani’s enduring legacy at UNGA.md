# Baqaei highlights Martyr Soleimani’s enduring legacy at UNGA

Published: 2026-09-25 07:44:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273964/Baqaei-highlights-Martyr-Soleimani-s-enduring-legacy-at-UNGA

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has emphasized the enduring legacy of Iran’s martyred anti-terror commander, Lieutenant General Qassem Soleimani, while criticizing Israel’s presence at the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA).
