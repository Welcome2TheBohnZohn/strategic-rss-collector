# Iran voices against nuclear monopoly, calls for equal access to advanced technologies

Published: 2026-10-27 11:55:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305023/Iran-voices-against-nuclear-monopoly-calls-for-equal-access

Tehran, IRNA – Hossein Derakhshandeh, the deputy head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) has outlined four key priorities for equitable development of nuclear power globally, without any monopoly on the related technology.
