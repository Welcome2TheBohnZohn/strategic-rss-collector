# Iranian, Turkish foreign ministers meet on sidelines of OIC summit in Baghdad

Published: 2026-10-17 13:42:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295182/Iranian-Turkish-foreign-ministers-meet-on-sidelines-of-OIC-summit

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met his Turkish counterpart Hakan Fidan in Baghdad on Saturday on the sidelines of the 52nd Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC).
