# Pezeshkian extends condolences to Spain following railway tragedy

Published: 2026-10-30 15:55:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307594/Pezeshkian-extends-condolences-to-Spain-following-railway-tragedy

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has offered his condolences to the government and people of Spain over the railway disaster in Spain, leading to the loss of life of several people.
