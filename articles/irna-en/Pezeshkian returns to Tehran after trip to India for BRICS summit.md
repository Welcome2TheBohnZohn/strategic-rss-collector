# Pezeshkian returns to Tehran after trip to India for BRICS summit

Published: 2026-09-13 19:03:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263186/Pezeshkian-back-in-Tehran-after-trip-to-India-for-BRICS-summit

Tehran, IRNA - President Masoud Pezeshkian returned to Tehran on Sunday night after a three-day visit to India, where he attended and addressed the BRICS Leaders’ Summit, IRNA reported.
