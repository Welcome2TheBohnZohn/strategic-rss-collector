# President Pezeshkian congratulates Turkiye on Republic Day

Published: 2026-10-29 12:00:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306877/President-Pezeshkian-congratulates-Turkiye-on-Republic-Day

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has extended congratulations to his Turkish counterpart, Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, on the occasion of Turkiye’s national day, reaffirming Tehran’s readiness to expand cooperation with Ankara to advance mutual interests and strengthen regional stability and security.
