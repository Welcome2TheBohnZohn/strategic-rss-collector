# BRICS offers key platform to counter unilateralism, expand economic cooperation: President Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-09-11 09:27:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260927/BRICS-offers-key-platform-to-counter-unilateralism-expand-economic

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has described the 18th BRICS Summit in India as an important opportunity to expand economic, trade, industrial and scientific cooperation among member states while strengthening multilateralism in international relations.
