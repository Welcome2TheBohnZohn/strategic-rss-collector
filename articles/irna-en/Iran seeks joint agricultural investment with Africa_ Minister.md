# Iran seeks joint agricultural investment with Africa: Minister

Published: 2026-10-31 10:29:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308296/Iran-seeks-joint-agricultural-investment-with-Africa-Minister

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is seeking to expand agricultural cooperation with African countries through joint investment, technology transfer and production partnerships, Agriculture Minister Gholamreza Nouri Ghezeljeh has said.
