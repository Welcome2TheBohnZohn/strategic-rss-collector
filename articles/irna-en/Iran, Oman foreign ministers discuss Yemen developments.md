# Iran, Oman foreign ministers discuss Yemen developments

Published: 2026-10-14 05:26:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292179/Iran-Oman-foreign-ministers-discuss-Yemen-developments

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Omani counterpart, Sayyid Badr bin Hamad Al Busaidi, have held a phone conversation to review the latest regional developments, including the situation in Yemen.
