# Hormozgan exports $222 million in seafood despite US-Israeli attacks

Published: 2026-11-09 12:22:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86317028/Hormozgan-exports-222-million-in-seafood-despite-US-Israeli

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – The director-general of fisheries in Iran’s southern Hormozgan province said that, despite the critical conditions caused by the recent war, the province ranked first in the country in seafood exports, generating $222 million.
