# In photos: From Behbahan to Minab, Iran remembers martyred students

Published: 2026-10-28 07:36:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86305636/In-photos-From-Behbahan-to-Minab-Iran-remembers-martyred-students

The following photos show portraits of the 69 students killed in the Iraqi missile attack on Shahid Pirouz Middle School in Behbahan, southwestern Iran, on October 26, 1983, as well as the school’s destruction, funeral ceremonies, and scenes from the city at the time. The attack also killed four teachers and a school custodian.
