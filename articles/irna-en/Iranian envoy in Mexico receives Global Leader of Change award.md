# Iranian envoy in Mexico receives Global Leader of Change award

Published: 2026-10-03 08:40:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281631/Iranian-envoy-in-Mexico-receives-Global-Leader-of-Change-award

Mexico City, IRNA – Iranian Ambassador to Mexico Abolfazl Pasandideh has received the “Global Leader of Change” award in recognition of his diplomatic efforts to strengthen cooperation between the two nations.
