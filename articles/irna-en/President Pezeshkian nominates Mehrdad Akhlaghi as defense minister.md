# President Pezeshkian nominates Mehrdad Akhlaghi as defense minister

Published: 2026-10-05 15:06:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284155/President-Pezeshkian-nominates-Mehrdad-Akhlaghi-as-defense-minister

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has nominated Mehrdad Akhlaghi Ketabchi to serve as defense minister, submitting the nomination to Parliament for a confidence vote.
