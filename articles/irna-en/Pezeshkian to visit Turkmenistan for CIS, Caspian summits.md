# Pezeshkian to visit Turkmenistan for CIS, Caspian summits

Published: 2026-10-07 15:14:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286324/Pezeshkian-to-visit-Turkmenistan-for-CIS-Caspian-summits

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian will leave for Turkmenistan on October 8 to attend the Council of Heads of State of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) and the Environmental Summit of the Heads of Caspian Sea Littoral States.
