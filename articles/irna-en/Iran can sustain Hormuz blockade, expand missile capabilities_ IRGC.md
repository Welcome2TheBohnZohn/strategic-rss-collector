# Iran can sustain Hormuz blockade, expand missile capabilities: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-26 10:17:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333233/Iran-can-sustain-Hormuz-blockade-expand-missile-capabilities

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has sufficient firepower to keep the Strait of Hormuz closed and is continuing to improve its missile capabilities, the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) spokesperson Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi says.
