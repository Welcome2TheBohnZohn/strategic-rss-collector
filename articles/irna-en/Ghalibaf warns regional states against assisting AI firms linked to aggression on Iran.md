# Ghalibaf warns regional states against assisting AI firms linked to aggression on Iran

Published: 2026-10-18 18:53:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296491/Ghalibaf-warns-regional-states-against-assisting-AI-firms-linked

Tehran, IRNA - Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has warned regional governments against providing resources to artificial intelligence and data companies linked to military aggression against Iran, vowing a forceful response to anyone who assists them.
