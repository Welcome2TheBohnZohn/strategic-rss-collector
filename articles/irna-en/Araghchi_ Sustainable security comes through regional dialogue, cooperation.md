# Araghchi: Sustainable security comes through regional dialogue, cooperation

Published: 2026-09-19 12:10:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268179/Araghchi-Sustainable-security-comes-through-regional-dialogue

Tehran, IRNA - Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has emphasized that sustainable security can only be achieved through dialogue, confidence-building, cooperation, and mutual respect among regional countries, while rejecting the presence and intervention of foreign military powers.
