# Iranian, Chinese parliamentary delegations meet on sidelines of BRICS forum

Published: 2026-10-26 09:30:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303726/Iranian-Chinese-parliamentary-delegations-meet-on-sidelines

Tehran, IRNA – The heads of the Iranian and Chinese parliamentary delegations have met on the sidelines of the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum in New Delhhi, India, stressing the need to strengthen bilateral ties.
