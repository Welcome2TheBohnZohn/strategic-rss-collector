# Iran rebuilds 1,288 schools damaged in recent aggression: Education minister

Published: 2026-11-07 08:14:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314589/Iran-rebuilds-1-288-schools-damaged-in-recent-aggression-Education

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has rebuilt 1,288 schools damaged in recent attacks and returned them to service with the help of charitable donors, Education Minister Alireza Kazemi says, warning that the educational losses caused by school closures cannot be measured or compensated.
