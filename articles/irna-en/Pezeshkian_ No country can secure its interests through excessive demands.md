# Pezeshkian: No country can secure its interests through excessive demands

Published: 2026-08-27 15:25:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247912/Pezeshkian-No-country-can-secure-its-interests-through-excessive

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has emphasized, “No country can secure its interests through excessive demands,” adding that conditions should be created for both countries to benefit from their shared interests through interaction.
