# Iran appreciates Russia and China for their principled stances and for vetoing US anti-Iran resolution

Published: 2026-09-17 18:20:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266946/Iran-appreciates-Russia-and-China-for-their-principled-stances

New York, IRNA – The Permanent Mission of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations expressed appreciation to Russia and China for their negative votes—which blocked the US-sponsored anti-Iran resolution—and to Pakistan and Somalia for their abstentions, as well as for the principled and valuable stances taken by these nations.
