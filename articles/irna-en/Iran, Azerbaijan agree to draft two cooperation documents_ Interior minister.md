# Iran, Azerbaijan agree to draft two cooperation documents: Interior minister

Published: 2026-10-12 18:42:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290991/Iran-Azerbaijan-agree-to-draft-two-cooperation-documents-Interior

Tehran, IRNA - Interior Minister Eskandar Momeni announced an agreement to draft two cooperation documents between Iran and the Republic of Azerbaijan, saying his meeting with Azerbaijani President Ilham Aliyev focused on mechanisms for implementing agreements reached between the two countries.
