# IRGC denies reports of attack on nuclear facilities in Isfahan

Published: 2026-09-05 14:36:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255742/IRGC-denies-reports-of-attack-on-nuclear-facilities-in-Isfahan

Isfahan, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) in Isfahan province has denied reports of US-Israeli attacks against nuclear facilities in the central province.
