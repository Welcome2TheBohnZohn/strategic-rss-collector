# In photos: Iranian Army ground force team A wins Commander’s Cup Polo Tournament

Published: 2026-10-24 08:18:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86301519/In-photos-Iranian-Army-ground-force-team-A-wins-Commander-s

Iran’s National Polo League’s 12th-week matches, held on Thursday, October 22, 2026, under the title “Commander’s Cup” in memory of martyred Major General Abdolrahim Mousavi, concluded with the Iranian Army ground force team A taking the title.
