# Leader issues message on anniversary of Operation Zulfaqari in Abadan

Published: 2026-10-31 17:40:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308615/Leader-issues-message-on-anniversary-of-Operation-Zolfaghari

Tehran, IRNA — Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has issued a message marking the anniversary of Operation Zulfaqari—the popular epic that drove the Ba'athist aggressors off Abadan Island— and highlighted the factors behind the Iranian triumph during the Sacred Defense (1980-88 Iran-Iraq War).
