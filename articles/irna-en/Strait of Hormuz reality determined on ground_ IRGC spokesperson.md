# Strait of Hormuz reality determined on ground: IRGC spokesperson

Published: 2026-10-29 10:30:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306814/Strait-of-Hormuz-reality-determined-on-ground-IRGC-spokesperson

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), has said that developments in the Strait of Hormuz are determined in the air, on land and at sea, not on social media or in the US’s media war rooms.
