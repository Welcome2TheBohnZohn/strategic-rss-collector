# Iraq PUK leader meets Iran FM in Tehran

Published: 2026-09-15 10:20:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264661/Iraq-PUK-leader-meets-Iran-FM-in-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA — The leader of the Patriotic Union of Kurdistan (PUK), Bafel Talabani, has met Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi at the foreign ministry in Tehran.
