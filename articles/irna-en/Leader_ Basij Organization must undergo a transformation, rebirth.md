# Leader: Basij Organization must undergo a transformation, rebirth

Published: 2026-11-26 11:32:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333294/Leader-Basij-Organization-must-undergo-a-transformation-rebirth

Tehran, IRNA — Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has called for the necessity of the Basij Organization to undergo a transformation and experience rebirth, likening the self-sacrificing spirit of the Iranian nation during the recent US-Israeli-imposed war to the Basij volunteer forces.
