# Ansarullah condemns Saudi airstrike on boat off Yemen coast that killed 19 civilians

Published: 2026-11-20 05:54:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327210/Ansarullah-condemns-Saudi-airstrike-on-boat-off-Yemen-coast-that

Tehran, IRNA – Yemen’s Ansarullah has condemned a Saudi attack on a boat carrying more than 25 displaced people en route from Ras al-Ara to Hays, which left 19 people dead and four others injured.
