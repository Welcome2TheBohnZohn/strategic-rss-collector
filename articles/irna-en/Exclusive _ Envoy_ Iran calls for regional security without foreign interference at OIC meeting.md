# Exclusive | Envoy: Iran calls for regional security without foreign interference at OIC meeting

Published: 2026-10-17 08:33:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294795/Exclusive-Envoy-Iran-calls-for-regional-security-without-foreign

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will emphasize regional security arrangements free from foreign interference, opposition to Israeli military acts of aggression, and stronger economic cooperation among Islamic countries at the 52nd session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC), Iran’s ambassador to Iraq, Mohammad Kazem Al-e Sadegh, told IRNA.
