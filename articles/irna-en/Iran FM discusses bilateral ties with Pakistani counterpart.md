# Iran FM discusses bilateral ties with Pakistani counterpart

Published: 2026-09-19 09:57:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267977/Iran-FM-discusses-bilateral-ties-with-Pakistani-counterpart

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Minister, Abbas Araghchi has held a phone conversation with his Pakistani counterpart, Mohammad Ishaq Dar, to review bilateral relations and regional developments.
