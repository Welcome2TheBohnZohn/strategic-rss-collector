# Iranian women’s para taekwondo team wins first-ever world title

Published: 2026-11-22 15:20:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329510/Iranian-women-s-para-taekwondo-team-wins-first-ever-world-title

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s women’s para taekwondo team has clinched its maiden world title at the 2026 World Para Taekwondo Championships in Muju, South Korea.
