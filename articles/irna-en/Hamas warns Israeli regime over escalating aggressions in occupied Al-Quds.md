# Hamas warns Israeli regime over escalating aggressions in occupied Al-Quds

Published: 2026-09-08 13:14:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258649/Hamas-warns-Israeli-regime-over-escalating-aggressions-in-occupied

Tehran, IRNA – The Palestinian Islamic Resistance Movement (Hamas) warns of the consequences of the Israeli regime’s decision to launch a wide-scale military operation in the northeast of occupied Al-Quds, saying the move was part of the regime’s ongoing aggression against Palestinians.
