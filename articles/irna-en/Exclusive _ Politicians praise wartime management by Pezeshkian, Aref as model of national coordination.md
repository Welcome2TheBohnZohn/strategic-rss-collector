# Exclusive | Politicians praise wartime management by Pezeshkian, Aref as model of national coordination

Published: 2026-08-27 06:23:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247401/Exclusive-Politicians-praise-wartime-management-by-Pezeshkian

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian officials and political figures have praised President Masoud Pezeshkian and First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref for their management of the country during wartime, describing the administration’s handling of economic, social and political pressures as a major test of national governance.
