# VP: Iran documents war environmental damage for global legal pursuit

Published: 2026-10-11 12:15:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289528/VP-Iran-documents-war-environmental-damage-for-global-legal

Tehran, IRNA - Vice President and Head of the Department of Environment Shina Ansari has said that documented reports on the damage inflicted on the country’s environment—including the release of pollutants, harm to biodiversity, and destruction of natural and wetland habitats—have been prepared and will be used at international environmental summits for legal pursuit.
