# Iran, Kazakhstan discuss ways to expand cooperation in sports, tourism

Published: 2026-09-15 16:44:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265127/Iran-Kazakhstan-discuss-ways-to-expand-cooperation-in-sports

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Kazakhstan, Ali-Akbar Jokar, and Kazakhstan’s Minister of Tourism and Sports, Yerbol Myrzabossynov, have discussed ways to enhance bilateral cooperation in sports and tourism.
