# Iranian deputy FM, Lebanese Shia council vice president discuss regional developments

Published: 2026-10-14 18:40:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293117/Iranian-deputy-FM-Lebanese-Shia-council-vice-president-discuss

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Saeed Khatibzadeh and Vice President of Lebanon’s Supreme Islamic Shiite Council Sheikh Ali al-Khatib have discussed the latest developments in Lebanon and the region during a meeting in Beirut.
