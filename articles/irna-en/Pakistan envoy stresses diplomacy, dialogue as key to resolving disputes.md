# Pakistan envoy stresses diplomacy, dialogue as key to resolving disputes

Published: 2026-09-07 16:10:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257697/Pakistan-envoy-stresses-diplomacy-dialogue-as-key-to-resolving

Tehran, IRNA – Pakistan’s Ambassador to the Islamic Republic of Iran, Imran Ahmed Siddiqui, has emphasized that Islamabad strongly believes diplomacy, dialogue, and confidence-building are the most effective means for resolving disputes.
