# In photos: President attends National Children’s Day ceremony in Tehran

Published: 2026-10-12 05:47:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86290054/In-photos-President-attends-National-Children-s-Day-ceremony

A ceremony to mark National Children’s Day was held at the Institute for the Intellectual Development of Children and Young Adults in Tehran on Sunday, October 11, 2026. President Masoud Pezeshkian, Minister of Education Alireza Kazemi, and several other officials attended the celebrations with hundreds of cheering, joyful kids.
