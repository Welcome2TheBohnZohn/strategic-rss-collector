# Iran’s Air Force, civil defense body pledge to boost protection of critical infrastructure

Published: 2026-11-22 14:53:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329480/Iran-s-Air-Force-civil-defense-body-pledge-to-boost-protection

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s passive defense organization and the country’s Army Air Force have agreed to expand cooperation to protect critical infrastructure and strengthen defense capabilities, according to a statement by the Army’s public relations department.
