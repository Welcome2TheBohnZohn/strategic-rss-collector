# Iran: All states are obliged to refrain from implementing unilateral US sanctions

Published: 2026-08-28 12:23:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248560/Iran-All-states-are-obliged-to-refrain-from-implementing-unilateral

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs has strongly condemned the US sanctions policies and declared that all states are obligated to refrain from implementing the unlawful sanctions, which violate the United Nations Charter.
