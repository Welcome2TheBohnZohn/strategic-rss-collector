# Israel seeks to drag US into another war with chemical weapons allegations: Iran FM

Published: 2026-11-26 07:04:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332990/Israel-seeks-to-drag-US-into-another-war-with-chemical-weapons

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has condemned Israeli allegations that Iran is pursuing chemical weapons, describing them as an attempt to mislead the United States and draw it into another war.
