# Iran’s envoy submits credentials to South Korean president

Published: 2026-10-12 10:44:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290506/Iran-s-envoy-submits-credentials-to-South-Korean-president

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s new Ambassador to South Korea, Ahmad Erfanian, has submitted his credentials to Lee Jae Myung, the President of South Korea, at a ceremony at the South Korean Presidential Palace.
