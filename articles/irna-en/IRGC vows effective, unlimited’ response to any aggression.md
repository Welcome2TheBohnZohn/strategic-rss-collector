# IRGC vows effective, unlimited’ response to any aggression

Published: 2026-11-03 19:51:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311744/IRGC-vows-effective-unlimited-response-to-any-aggression

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has warned that any new US-Israeli attack on Iran will be met with a broad and effective response, dismissing threats and psychological warfare against the country as baseless.
