# Americans want end to wars, focus on their own issues: Pezeshkian’s deputy

Published: 2026-11-05 09:47:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313232/Americans-want-end-to-wars-focus-on-their-own-issues-Pezeshkian-s

Rasht, IRNA – Mohammad Jafar Ghaempanah, deputy to the president for executive affairs, says the outcome of the US elections reflected voters’ demand for an end to wars and threats and greater attention to domestic issues and people’s livelihoods.
