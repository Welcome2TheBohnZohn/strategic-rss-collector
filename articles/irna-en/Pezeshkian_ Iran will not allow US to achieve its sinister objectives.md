# Pezeshkian: Iran will not allow US to achieve its sinister objectives

Published: 2026-10-15 10:12:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293436/Pezeshkian-Iran-will-not-allow-US-to-achieve-its-sinister-objectives

Tabriz, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran will not allow the United States to achieve its sinister goals by disrupting the country’s supply routes and creating public discontent.
