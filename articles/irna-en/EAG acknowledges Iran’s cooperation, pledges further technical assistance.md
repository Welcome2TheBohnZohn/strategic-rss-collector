# EAG acknowledges Iran’s cooperation, pledges further technical assistance

Published: 2026-11-23 12:40:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330299/EAG-acknowledges-Iran-s-cooperation-pledges-further-technical

Tehran, IRNA – The Eurasian Group on Combating Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism (EAG) has acknowledged Iran’s progress in implementing the FATF action plan and pledged to continue providing technical assistance to the country.
