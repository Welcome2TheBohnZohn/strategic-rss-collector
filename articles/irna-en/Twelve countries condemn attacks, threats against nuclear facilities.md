# Twelve countries condemn attacks, threats against nuclear facilities

Published: 2026-10-24 06:04:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301408/Twelve-countries-condemn-attacks-threats-against-nuclear-facilities

New York, IRNA – Twelve countries, including Iran, Russia, China, and Pakistan, have condemned any attack or threat of attack against nuclear facilities dedicated to peaceful purposes and operating under International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) safeguards.
