# Terrorist team dismantled in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-09-12 13:02:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261932/Terrorist-team-dismantled-in-southeastern-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Force Quds Headquarters has announced the elimination of a terrorist team in the southeastern Iranian province of Sistan and Baluchestan.
