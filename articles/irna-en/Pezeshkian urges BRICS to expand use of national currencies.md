# Pezeshkian urges BRICS to expand use of national currencies

Published: 2026-09-11 14:15:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261081/Pezeshkian-urges-BRICS-to-expand-use-of-national-currencies

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for expanding the use of national currencies in intra-BRICS trade, describing it as one of the bloc’s most essential priorities.
