# VP Aref warns of ‘asymmetric’, ‘multilayered’ response to US

Published: 2026-09-03 14:32:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254222/VP-Aref-warns-of-asymmetric-multilayered-response-to-US

Tehran, IRNA — First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref says recent US crimes have changed Iran’s security and defense doctrine, warning that Tehran’s future response will be “asymmetric” and “multilayered.”
