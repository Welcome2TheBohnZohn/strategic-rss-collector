# Iraqi Kurdistan’s president meets Iranian delegation in Erbil

Published: 2026-11-09 05:35:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316408/Iraqi-Kurdistan-s-president-meets-Iranian-delegation-in-Erbil

Tehran, IRNA – President of Iraq’s Kurdistan Region Nechirvan Barzani has met with an Iranian delegation, with both sides stressing the need to strengthen and expand bilateral relations across various sectors.
