# Leader: Iran won’t return to era that enemy longs for

Published: 2026-09-28 12:04:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277306/Leader-Iran-won-t-return-to-era-that-enemy-longs-for

Tehran, IRNA – Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei, the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, said, “I, as a servant of the dear people of Iran, declare that those bygone days to which our enemy longs to return have passed and will never be repeated.”
