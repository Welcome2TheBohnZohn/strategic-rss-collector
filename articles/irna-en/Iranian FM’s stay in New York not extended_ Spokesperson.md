# Iranian FM’s stay in New York not extended: Spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-28 16:55:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277535/Iranian-FM-s-stay-in-New-York-not-extended-Spokesperson

New York, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Iranian ministry of foreign affairs said that the stay of Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi in New York has not been extended.
