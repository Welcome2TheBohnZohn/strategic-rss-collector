# Iran sharply reacts to remarks by German FM

Published: 2026-11-04 22:19:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312964/Iran-sharply-reacts-to-remarks-by-German-FM

Tehran, IRNA – In response to remarks by the German foreign minister justifying the military aggression by the US and the Israeli regime against Iran, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has said that by endorsing the aggression of the US and Israel against Iran, the German foreign minister has reduced himself to the level of an apologist for the most heinous war crimes.
