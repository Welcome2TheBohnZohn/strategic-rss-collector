# Iran, Tajikistan presidents discuss ways to strengthen bilateral cooperation

Published: 2026-08-31 14:01:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251214/Iran-Tajikistan-presidents-discuss-ways-to-strengthen-bilateral

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian and his Tajik counterpart, Emomali Rahmon, have discussed the current status of bilateral relations and mutual cooperation and explored ways to strengthen joint interaction.
