# Iran says inflationary pressures eased in three months to September

Published: 2026-10-23 10:32:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300977/Iran-says-inflationary-pressures-eased-in-three-months-to-September

Tehran, IRNA – The governor of the Central Bank of Iran (CBI) says inflationary pressures that accelerated sharply after the 40-day war had eased in the three months to September.
