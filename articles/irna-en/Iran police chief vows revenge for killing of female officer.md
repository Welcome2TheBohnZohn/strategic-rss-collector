# Iran police chief vows revenge for killing of female officer

Published: 2026-10-10 11:15:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288553/Iran-police-chief-vows-revenge-for-killing-of-female-officer

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s police chief, Brigadier General Ahmadreza Radan, has vowed revenge for the killing of Nosrat Eftekhari, deputy for cultural and social affairs of the police command in Sistan and Baluchestan Province, in a terrorist attack near Zahedan.
