# Pezeshkian inaugurates 18,910 municipal, rural projects worth over $640m

Published: 2026-08-29 13:03:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249537/Pezeshkian-inaugurates-18-910-municipal-rural-projects-worth

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has inaugurated 18,910 municipal and rural development projects across the country, with a total investment exceeding 640 million dollars.
