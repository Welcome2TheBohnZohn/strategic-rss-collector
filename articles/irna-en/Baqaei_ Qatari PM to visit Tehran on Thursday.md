# Baqaei: Qatari PM to visit Tehran on Thursday

Published: 2026-08-26 20:13:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247266/Baqaei-Qatari-PM-to-visit-Tehran-on-Thursday

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei confirmed on Wednesday that Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman bin Jassim al-Thani will travel to Tehran on Thursday.
