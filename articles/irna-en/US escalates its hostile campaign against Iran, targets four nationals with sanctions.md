# US escalates its hostile campaign against Iran, targets four nationals with sanctions

Published: 2026-11-21 07:09:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327896/US-escalates-its-hostile-campaign-against-Iran-targets-four

New York, IRNA — The US Treasury Department has imposed new sanctions on four Iranian nationals involved in collecting oil revenues, continuing Washington’s economic pressure on Iran following its failure to achieve its objectives through war of aggression.
