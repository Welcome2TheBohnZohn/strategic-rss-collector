# Iran condemns UK’s ‘baseless’ claims over Fairford air base incident

Published: 2026-10-12 19:33:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291010/Iran-condemns-UK-s-baseless-claims-over-Fairford-air-base-incident

Tehran, IRNA - The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has strongly condemned “baseless” claims by British officials blaming an alleged security incident at the Fairford air base on Iran, calling it an “ugly conspiracy” against the country.
