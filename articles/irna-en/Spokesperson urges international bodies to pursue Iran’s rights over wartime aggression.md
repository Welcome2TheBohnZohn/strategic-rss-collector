# Spokesperson urges international bodies to pursue Iran’s rights over wartime aggression

Published: 2026-11-07 10:01:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314781/Spokesperson-urges-international-bodies-to-pursue-Iran-s-rights

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian government spokesperson, Fatemeh Mohajerani, has called on international organizations to take an active role in securing the rights of Iranians, saying attacks on the country during two wars imposed by the US and the Israeli regime amounted to acts of terrorism rather than conventional military operations.
