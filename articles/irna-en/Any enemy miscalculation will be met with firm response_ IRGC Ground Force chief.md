# Any enemy miscalculation will be met with firm response: IRGC Ground Force chief

Published: 2026-09-17 07:40:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266504/Any-enemy-miscalculation-will-be-met-with-firm-response-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Mohammad Karami, commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Force, has warned that any miscalculation by the enemy will be met with a firm response.
