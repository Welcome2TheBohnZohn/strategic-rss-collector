# In photos: Minab martyrs commemorated in Pakistan

Published: 2026-10-07 09:27:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86285948/In-photos-Minab-martyrs-commemorated-in-Pakistan

A commemorative ceremony honoring the martyrs of the Shajareh Tayyebeh Primary School in Minab and Lamerd, along with Sacred Defense Week, was held in Karachi, Pakistan, hosted by Iran’s Consulate General on October 7, 2026.
