# Qalibaf: Bessent on the brink as oil reserves fall and bond yields surge

Published: 2026-09-03 20:08:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254422/Qalibaf-Bessent-on-the-brink-as-oil-reserves-fall-and-bond-yields

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has warned US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent that rapidly declining strategic oil reserves, rising US Treasury yields and surging oil futures prices have placed him and the US President Donald Trump administration in a difficult position.
