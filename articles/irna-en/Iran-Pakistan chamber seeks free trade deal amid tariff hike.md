# Iran-Pakistan chamber seeks free trade deal amid tariff hike

Published: 2026-09-07 12:14:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257551/Iran-Pakistan-chamber-seeks-free-trade-deal-amid-tariff-hike

Tehran, IRNA – The head of the Iran-Pakistan Joint Chamber says the chamber is negotiating to remove trade barriers following Pakistan’s recent increase in tariffs on some Iranian exports.
