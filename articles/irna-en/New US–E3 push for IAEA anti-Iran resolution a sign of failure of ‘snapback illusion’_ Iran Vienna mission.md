# New US–E3 push for IAEA anti-Iran resolution a sign of failure of ‘snapback illusion’: Iran Vienna mission

Published: 2026-09-05 06:38:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255121/New-US-E3-push-for-IAEA-anti-Iran-resolution-a-sign-of-failure

London, IRNA — Iran’s permanent mission to the United Nations and other international organizations in Vienna says a new attempt by the US and the three European countries of Germany, Britain, and France to refer Iran’s nuclear issue to the UN Security Council clearly shows “the complete failure of the snapback illusion” they have already pursued.
