# Oman says regional meeting on Strait of Hormuz postponed

Published: 2026-09-13 19:39:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263196/Oman-postpones-regional-meeting-on-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA - Omani Foreign Minister Badr al-Busaidi has announced that a regional meeting on the Strait of Hormuz, which had been scheduled for Monday in Salalah, has been postponed.
