# Parliamentary commission approves mandatory use of full ‘Persian Gulf’ name

Published: 2026-09-20 16:20:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269356/Parliamentary-commission-approves-mandatory-use-of-full-Persian

Tehran, IRNA – The Parliament’s National Security and Foreign Policy Commission has approved a provision requiring the full name “Persian Gulf” to be used in all negotiations, treaties, agreements, publications and relevant documents, as well as communications involving vessels and fleets.
