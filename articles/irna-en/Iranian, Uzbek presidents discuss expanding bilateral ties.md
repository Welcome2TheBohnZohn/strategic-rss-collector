# Iranian, Uzbek presidents discuss expanding bilateral ties

Published: 2026-09-29 17:51:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278706/Iranian-Uzbek-presidents-discuss-expanding-bilateral-ties

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Uzbek President Shavkat Mirziyoyev held a phone call Tuesday, stressing the need to use existing capacities to deepen friendly and brotherly relations and expand cooperation across various fields.
