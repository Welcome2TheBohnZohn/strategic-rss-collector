# Stop your crocodile tears, Araghchi tells French counterpart

Published: 2026-09-19 07:17:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267751/Stop-your-crocodile-tears-Araghchi-tells-French-counterpart

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister, Abbas Araghchi, has hit back at recent human rights allegations by French Foreign Minister, Jean-Noel Barrot, criticizing Paris for its silence over the killing of Iranian schoolchildren by the United States.
