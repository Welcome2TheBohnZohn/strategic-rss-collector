# Iran’s UN Mission: Accusations regarding dams and dirty bomb are disinformation campaign

Published: 2026-11-17 22:10:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325186/Iran-s-UN-Mission-Accusations-regarding-dams-and-dirty-bomb

New York, IRNA – The Permanent Mission of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations dismissed The New York Times’ report regarding Iran’s dams, as well as CNN’s fabricated narrative concerning allegations of a dirty bomb, as baseless—characterizing them as part of a campaign of disinformation and the dissemination of falsehoods.
