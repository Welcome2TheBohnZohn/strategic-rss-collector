# Araghchi calls for active OIC role in tackling Islamic world’s challenges

Published: 2026-10-19 06:40:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296709/Araghchi-calls-for-active-OIC-role-in-tackling-Islamic-world-s

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has called on the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) to take a more active role in addressing challenges facing the Islamic world and resolving differences.
