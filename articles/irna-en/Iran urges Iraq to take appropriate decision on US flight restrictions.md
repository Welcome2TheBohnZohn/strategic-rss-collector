# Iran urges Iraq to take appropriate decision on US flight restrictions

Published: 2026-10-02 05:52:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280636/Iran-urges-Iraq-to-take-appropriate-decision-on-US-flight-restrictions

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Foreign Ministry has condemned the US government’s illegal and inhumane actions and moves aimed at disrupting Iran’s trade and air transportation ties with other countries, stressing that Iraq should take appropriate decisions in response to US efforts to restrict flights.
