# US military interventions, unilateral sanctions threaten global energy security: Iran

Published: 2026-10-26 12:45:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303933/US-military-interventions-unilateral-sanctions-threaten-global

Moscow, IRNA – Acting Oil Minister Hamid Bovard says US military interventions, hostile actions in several nations, along with unilateral sanctions, have negatively impacted investment, security, and predictability of global energy markets.
