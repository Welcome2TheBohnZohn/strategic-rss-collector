# Massacre of Minab schoolchildren a disgrace for false advocates of human rights: Envoy

Published: 2026-11-20 16:26:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327635/Massacre-of-Minab-schoolchildren-a-disgrace-for-false-advocates

Moscow, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Russia, in a message marking World Children’s Day (November 20), described the martyrdom of the children of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab as proof of disgrace for the false claimants of human rights.
