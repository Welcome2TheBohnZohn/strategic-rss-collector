# Baqaei: Tehran meeting to focus on endogenous security

Published: 2026-10-26 08:48:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303695/Baqaei-Tehran-meeting-to-focus-on-endogenous-security

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says that Tehran will host senior officials from Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Turkey later on Monday, with regional security topping the agenda of the talks. He says that the discussions could pave the way for measures to ensure security in West Asia through regional efforts.
