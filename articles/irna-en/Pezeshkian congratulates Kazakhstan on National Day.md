# Pezeshkian congratulates Kazakhstan on National Day

Published: 2026-10-25 07:11:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302427/Pezeshkian-congratulates-Kazakhstan-on-National-Day

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated Kazakhstan on its National Day, highlighting the shared capacities and responsibilities of Iran and Kazakhstan in promoting regional peace, stability and security.
