# Iran holds conference to advance justice for EB patients

Published: 2026-10-25 11:06:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86302879/Iran-holds-conference-to-advance-justice-for-EB-patients

A conference titled A Strong Iran: Epic of Authority and Victory of Justice for Epidermolysis Bullosa Patients was held at the Imam Khomeini Judicial Complex on October 25, 2026, attended by the Tehran Province chief justice, Judiciary spokesperson, head of the EB Patients’ House, a lawyer representing the patients, and around 50 patients and their family members.
