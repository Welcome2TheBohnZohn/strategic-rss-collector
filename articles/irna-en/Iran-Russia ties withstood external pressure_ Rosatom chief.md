# Iran-Russia ties withstood external pressure: Rosatom chief

Published: 2026-11-22 06:37:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328879/Iran-Russia-ties-withstood-external-pressure-Rosatom-chief

Moscow, IRNA – Rosatom Director General, Alexey Likhachev, says relations between Iran and Russia, as well as the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant project, remain strong in the face of any external pressure and were proven during the US-Israeli attacks on Iran.
