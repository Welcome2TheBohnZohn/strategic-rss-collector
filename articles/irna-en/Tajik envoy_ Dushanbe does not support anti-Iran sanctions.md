# Tajik envoy: Dushanbe does not support anti-Iran sanctions

Published: 2026-09-01 11:20:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251954/Tajik-envoy-Dushanbe-does-not-support-anti-Iran-sanctions

Tehran, IRNA – Ambassador of Tajikistan in Iran Zahidi Nizomiddin Shamsiddinzoda has stressed his country’s opposition to sanctions against Iran.
