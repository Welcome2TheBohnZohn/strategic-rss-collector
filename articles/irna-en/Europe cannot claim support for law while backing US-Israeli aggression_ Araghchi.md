# Europe cannot claim support for law while backing US-Israeli aggression: Araghchi

Published: 2026-09-23 06:47:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272021/Europe-cannot-claim-support-for-law-while-backing-US-Israeli

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says European countries cannot claim to support the rule of law and human rights while remaining indifferent to, or supporting, the US-Israeli military aggression against Iran.
