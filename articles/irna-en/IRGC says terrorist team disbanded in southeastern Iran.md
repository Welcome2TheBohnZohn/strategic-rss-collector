# IRGC says terrorist team disbanded in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-09-05 10:53:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255525/IRGC-says-terrorist-team-disbanded-in-southeastern-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The Quds Headquarters of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Forces has announced the destruction of a terrorist team in Iran’s southeastern Sistan and Baluchestan Province.
