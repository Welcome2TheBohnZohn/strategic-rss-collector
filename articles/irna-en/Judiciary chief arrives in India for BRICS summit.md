# Judiciary chief arrives in India for BRICS summit

Published: 2026-09-03 12:12:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254172/Judiciary-chief-arrives-in-India-for-BRICS-summit

Tehran, IRNA – Judiciary Chief Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei, heading a high‑level judicial and legal delegation, has arrived in New Delhi, India, to attend the BRICS meeting of judicial leaders.
