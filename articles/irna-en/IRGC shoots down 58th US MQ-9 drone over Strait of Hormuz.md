# IRGC shoots down 58th US MQ-9 drone over Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-21 05:49:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327810/IRGC-shoots-down-58th-US-MQ-9-drone-over-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced that it shot down the 58th MQ-9 drone operated by the US military.
