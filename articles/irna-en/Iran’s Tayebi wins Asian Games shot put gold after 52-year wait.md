# Iran’s Tayebi wins Asian Games shot put gold after 52-year wait

Published: 2026-09-26 16:11:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275364/Iran-s-Tayebi-wins-Asian-Games-shot-put-gold-after-52-year-wait

Nagoya, IRNA – Iranian shot putter Mohammadreza Tayebi won the men’s gold medal at the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, Japan, with a throw of 20.81 meters, breaking the Games record and ending a 52-year wait for Iran in the event.
