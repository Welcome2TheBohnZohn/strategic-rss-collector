# IRGC: New weapons will be deployed if nature of conflict changes

Published: 2026-09-29 19:32:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278745/IRGC-New-weapons-will-be-deployed-if-nature-of-conflict-changes

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), says IRGC is continuously developing and mass-producing new weapons to strengthen its defensive capabilities, IRGC spokesman Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi said, adding that Iran would deploy new weapons if the nature of any future conflict changed.
