# Iranian, Indian judicial officials discuss cooperation on BRICS summit sidelines

Published: 2026-09-05 10:30:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255496/Iranian-Indian-judicial-officials-discuss-cooperation-on-BRICS

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian and Indian judicial officials have held discussions on expanding bilateral and multilateral legal cooperation on the sidelines of a BRICS meeting of chief justices and senior judicial officials in New Delhi, with stronger judicial ties, arbitration and mediation emerging as key areas of focus.
