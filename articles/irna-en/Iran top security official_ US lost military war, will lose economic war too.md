# Iran top security official: US lost military war, will lose economic war too

Published: 2026-10-06 14:21:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285209/Iran-top-security-official-US-lost-military-war-will-lose-economic

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council Mohsen Rezaei, addressing the United States, emphasized: “You lost the military war. You will fail in the economic war too.”
