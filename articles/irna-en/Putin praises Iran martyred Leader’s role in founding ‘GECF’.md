# Putin praises Iran martyred Leader’s role in founding ‘GECF’

Published: 2026-10-27 15:23:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305219/Putin-praises-Iran-martyred-Leader-s-role-in-founding-GECF

Moscow, IRNA – In his remarks at the 8th Summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF), Russian President Vladimir Putin emphasized that Iran’s martyred Leader, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, played a significant role in the establishment of GECF.
