# IRGC destroys US surveillance balloon control system in Erbil

Published: 2026-09-02 05:54:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252749/IRGC-destroys-US-surveillance-balloon-control-system-in-Erbil

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) announces that it destroyed a US surveillance balloon control system at an American base in Erbil, northern Iraq, following a hybrid missile and drone attack.
