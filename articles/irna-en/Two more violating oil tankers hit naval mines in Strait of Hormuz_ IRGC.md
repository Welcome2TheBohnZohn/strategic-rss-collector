# Two more violating oil tankers hit naval mines in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-11 12:27:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319077/Two-more-violating-oil-tankers-hit-naval-mines-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that two more violating oil tankers have caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting naval mines.
