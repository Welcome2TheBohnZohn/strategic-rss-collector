# Iran’s Red Crescent rescued 7,250 people from rubble during two wars: Director

Published: 2026-10-10 11:32:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288528/Iran-s-Red-Crescent-rescued-7-250-people-from-rubble-during-two

Tehran, IRNA — The Iranian Red Crescent Society rescued 7,250 people from beneath the rubble during the 12-day war and the Ramadan war, says Pir Hossein Kolivand, the president of the IRCS.
