# President Pezeshkian meets Azerbaijani counterpart on CIS summit sidelines

Published: 2026-10-09 11:41:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287626/President-Pezeshkian-meets-Azerbaijani-counterpart-on-CIS-summit
