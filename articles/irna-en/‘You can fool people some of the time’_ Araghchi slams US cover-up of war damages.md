# ‘You can fool people some of the time’: Araghchi slams US cover-up of war damages

Published: 2026-10-08 07:04:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286680/You-can-fool-people-some-of-the-time-Araghchi-slams-US-cover-up

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, citing a new report by the US Congressional Research Service about damage to American military aircraft during the war with Iran, reacted to CENTCOM's concealment regarding the extent of these damages.
