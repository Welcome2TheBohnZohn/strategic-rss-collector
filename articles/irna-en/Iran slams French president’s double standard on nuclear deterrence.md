# Iran slams French president’s double standard on nuclear deterrence

Published: 2026-10-09 15:55:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287738/Iran-slams-French-president-s-double-standard-on-nuclear-deterrence

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson has criticized the French president over a blatant double standard on nuclear deterrence, questioning why the same logic that France views as guaranteeing its security and freedom suddenly turns into a “threat” when it comes to Iran.
