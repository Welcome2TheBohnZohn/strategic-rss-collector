# Iran ambassador to Ivory Coast meets FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-12 12:10:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290705/Iran-ambassador-to-Ivory-Coast-meets-FM-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Ambassador to Ivory Coast, Seyyed Gholamreza Mir Mohammad Meigouni, met with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi to present a report on the embassy’s latest measures and plans.
