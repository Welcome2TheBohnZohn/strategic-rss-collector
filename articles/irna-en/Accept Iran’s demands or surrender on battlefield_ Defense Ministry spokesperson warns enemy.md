# Accept Iran’s demands or surrender on battlefield: Defense Ministry spokesperson warns enemy

Published: 2026-11-11 13:11:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319236/Accept-Iran-s-demands-or-surrender-on-battlefield-Defense-Ministry

Tehran, IRNA – Defense Ministry spokesperson Reza Talaei-Nik says Iran’s defense capabilities have been strengthened, leaving the enemy with two choices: accept Iran’s legitimate demands or surrender on the battlefield.
