# Iran calls for expansion of regional mining cooperation

Published: 2026-10-28 10:59:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305926/Iran-calls-for-expansion-of-regional-mining-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – The head of Iran Mining House has stressed the need to expand economic ties with regional countries, saying that boosting domestic mineral production and processing would help increase exports and strengthen Iran’s presence in regional markets.
