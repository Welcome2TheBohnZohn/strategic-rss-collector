# Iran’s Kanoon urges UNICEF to pursue justice for war-hit children

Published: 2026-11-21 06:51:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327936/Iran-s-Kanoon-urges-UNICEF-to-pursue-justice-for-war-hit-children

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Institute for the Intellectual Development of Children and Young Adults (IIDCYA), better known as Kanoon, has called on the United Nations International Children’s Emergency Fund (UNICEF) to publicly condemn attacks that killed or injured Iranian children and pursue accountability for violations of their rights.
