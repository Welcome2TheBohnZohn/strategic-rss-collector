# Iraq and Iran agree on 40 daily flights by Iranian airlines to and from Najaf

Published: 2026-10-02 18:33:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281141/Iraq-and-Iran-agree-on-40-daily-flights-by-Iranian-airlines-to

Tehran, IRNA – The Iraqi Prime Minister’s Office on Friday announced an agreement under which Iranian airlines, excluding Mahan Air, will operate 40 flights per day from and to Najaf International Airport.
