# Iran crowned U17 Beach Wrestling World Champions

Published: 2026-08-31 11:04:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251023/Iran-crowned-U17-Beach-Wrestling-World-Champions

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s youth beach wrestling team has claimed the championship title at the Beach Wrestling World Championships in Baku, Azerbaijan, after earning six medals across two days of competition.
