# Qatar says efforts to bridge differences between Iran, US continue

Published: 2026-11-03 16:17:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311522/Qatar-says-efforts-to-bridge-differences-between-Iran-US-continue

Tehran, IRNA – Qatari Foreign Ministry spokesperson Majed al-Ansari says efforts to bridge differences between Tehran and Washington are ongoing, with both sides reviewing proposals and regional countries coordinating to achieve a diplomatic solution.
