# US appeasers will learn too late that submission offers no salvation: Tehran

Published: 2026-09-24 09:55:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86273365/US-appeasers-will-learn-too-late-that-submission-offers-no-salvation

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says that countries seeking refuge under US power would eventually learn that turning away from reality and sacrificing their dignity offers no protection, in a pointed message to Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney over his response to a US threat to “annihilate” Iran.
