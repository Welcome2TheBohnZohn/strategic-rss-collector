# Pezeshkian urges int’l support for countries facing environmental costs of war, sanctions

Published: 2026-10-16 09:22:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294009/Pezeshkian-urges-int-l-support-for-countries-facing-environmental

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has reaffirmed the country’s commitment to biodiversity conservation, urging the international community to ensure that countries facing sanctions or military aggression do not bear the environmental costs alone.
