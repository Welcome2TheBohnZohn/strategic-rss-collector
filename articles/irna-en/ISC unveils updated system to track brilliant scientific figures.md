# ISC unveils updated system to track brilliant scientific figures

Published: 2026-09-15 16:32:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265113/ISC-unveils-updated-system-to-track-brilliant-scientific-figures

Shiraz, IRNA – The updated version of the “System for Tracking Researchers’ and Scientists’ Honors” of the ‘Islamic World Science & Technology Monitoring and Citation Institute’ (ISC) was unveiled to identify outstanding scientific, technological, and innovation figures in Iran and Islamic countries.
