# Araghchi stresses continuation of dialogue and cooperation with neighbors

Published: 2026-10-06 21:52:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285443/Araghchi-stresses-continuation-of-dialogue-and-cooperation-with

Tehran, IRNA – In a message regarding the visit of the Armenian Foreign Minister to Tehran, the Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, Abbas Araghchi, has emphasized that continued dialogue and cooperation with neighbors is the path prioritized by the Islamic Republic of Iran for building a region that is more secure, stable, and prosperous.
