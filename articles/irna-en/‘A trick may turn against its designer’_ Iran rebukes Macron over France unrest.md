# ‘A trick may turn against its designer’: Iran rebukes Macron over France unrest

Published: 2026-10-18 18:10:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296467/A-trick-may-turn-against-its-designer-Iran-rebukes-Macron

Tehran, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has sharply criticized French President Emmanuel Macron over his handling of unrest in France, accusing him of applying a double standard between protests in Iran and those in his own country.
