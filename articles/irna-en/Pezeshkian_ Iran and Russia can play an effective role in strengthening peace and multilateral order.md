# Pezeshkian: Iran and Russia can play an effective role in strengthening peace and multilateral order

Published: 2026-10-19 17:04:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297527/President-Pezeshkian-meets-Russian-FM-in-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian, emphasizing the need to accelerate the implementation of agreements and understandings between Tehran and Moscow, termed the strengthening of bilateral cooperation in the fields of energy, transportation, trade, and power plant infrastructure as a priority for the two nations' relations.
