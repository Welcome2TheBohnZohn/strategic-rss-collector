# Yemen warns Saudi attacks will not go unpunished

Published: 2026-09-07 16:50:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257794/Yemen-warns-Saudi-attacks-will-not-go-unpunished

Tehran, IRNA – Yemen’s armed forces spokesperson Brigadier General Yahya Saree says that continued Saudi attacks on Yemen would not go unanswered, following Saudi airstrikes on several areas in the country.
