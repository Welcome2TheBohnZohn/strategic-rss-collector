# In photos: Iran honors Red Crescent’s first responders

Published: 2026-10-14 06:16:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86292242/In-photos-Iran-honors-Red-Crescent-s-first-responders

Tehran held a ceremony on October 13, 2026, to honor members of the Iranian Red Crescent Society (IRCS) for their rescue and relief efforts during the US–Israeli-imposed war on Iran. The event was attended by IRCS President Pirhossein Kolivand, along with senior officials and members of the society.
