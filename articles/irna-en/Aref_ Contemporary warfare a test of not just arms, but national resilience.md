# Aref: Contemporary warfare a test of not just arms, but national resilience

Published: 2026-11-22 17:44:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329585/Aref-Contemporary-warfare-a-test-of-not-just-arms-but-national

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref has emphasized the need for strengthening the people-centered approach to deal with the enemies, arguing that today’s war is not merely limited to an exchange of fire, but also a test of willpower, strategic calculations, and national resilience.
