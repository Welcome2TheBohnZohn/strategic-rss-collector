# Tehran hosts funeral procession for 110 unidentified Iranian soldiers

Published: 2026-11-14 10:03:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86321344/Tehran-hosts-funeral-procession-for-110-unidentified-Iranian

A funeral procession for 110 unidentified Iranian martyrs who were killed in action during the 1980–1988 Iran-Iraq War was held in Tehran on November 13, 2026, coinciding with the anniversary of the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatemeh Zahra (PBUH). The procession began outside the University of Tehran and proceeded toward the martyrs’ memorial site.
