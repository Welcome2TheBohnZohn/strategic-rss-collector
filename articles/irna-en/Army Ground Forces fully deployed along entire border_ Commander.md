# Army Ground Forces fully deployed along entire border: Commander

Published: 2026-10-15 08:54:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293381/Army-Ground-Forces-fully-deployed-along-entire-border-Commander

Tehran, IRNA – The commander of the Iranian Army’s Ground Forces says the forces are strongly deployed along the country’s entire border, from the northwest to the Makran coast, making any enemy plan for a ground offensive an “unattainable dream.”
