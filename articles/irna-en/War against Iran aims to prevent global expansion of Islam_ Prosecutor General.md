# War against Iran aims to prevent global expansion of Islam: Prosecutor General

Published: 2026-11-22 13:01:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329407/War-against-Iran-aims-to-prevent-global-expansion-of-Islam-Prosecutor

Orumiyeh, IRNA – Prosecutor General Mohammad Movahedi-Azad says the war waged by the US, the Israeli regime, and global hegemonic powers against Iran is aimed at preventing the global expansion of Islam’s influence.
