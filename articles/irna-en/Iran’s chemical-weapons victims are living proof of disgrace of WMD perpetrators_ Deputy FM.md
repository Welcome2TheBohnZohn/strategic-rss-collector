# Iran’s chemical-weapons victims are living proof of disgrace of WMD perpetrators: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-11-24 10:54:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331286/Iran-s-chemical-weapons-victims-are-living-proof-of-disgrace

Tehran, IRNA — Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has criticized the countries that supplied chemical weapons for deviating from accountability, saying the Iranian victims are living proof of the disgrace of those who falsely claim to be against such weapons.
