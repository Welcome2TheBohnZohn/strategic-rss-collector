# Armed Forces pledge stronger response to new aggression against Iran

Published: 2026-09-21 09:42:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270042/Armed-Forces-pledge-stronger-response-to-new-aggression-against

Tehran, IRNA – The General Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces and Khatam al-Anbya Central Headquarters have issued a statement, pledging a strong response to any new act of aggression against Iran that will deal a heavy blow to aggressors, particularly the United States and Israel.
