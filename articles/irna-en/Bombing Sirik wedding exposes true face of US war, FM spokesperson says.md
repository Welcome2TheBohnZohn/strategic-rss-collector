# Bombing Sirik wedding exposes true face of US war, FM spokesperson says

Published: 2026-09-02 12:50:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253421/Bombing-Sirik-wedding-exposes-true-face-of-US-war-FM-spokesperson

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says the true face of the US-Israeli war against Iran can be seen in the bombing of a wedding in Sirik, not in AI-generated videos portraying US officials as triumphant heroes.
