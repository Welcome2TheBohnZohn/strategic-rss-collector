# Hamas says US-led Board of Peace is biased against Palestinians

Published: 2026-09-05 13:43:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255617/Hamas-says-US-led-Board-of-Peace-is-biased-against-Palestinians

Tehran, IRNA – A spokesperson for Hamas, Hazem Qassem, has criticized an article published by the Board of Peace on its official X account, describing it as biased and unfair toward the Palestinian people and their resistance.
