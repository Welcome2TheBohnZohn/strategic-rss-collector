# Iran warns IAEA confidentiality breaches enabled nuclear sabotage

Published: 2026-09-08 16:36:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258834/Iran-warns-IAEA-confidentiality-breaches-enabled-nuclear-sabotage

London, IRNA – Iran says that breaches of confidentiality at the International Atomic Energy Agency had previously exposed sensitive information that was later used to facilitate sabotage against safeguarded nuclear facilities.
