# Leader: Signs of police force’s significant impact in recent conflicts have become more evident

Published: 2026-10-08 18:13:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287230/Leader-Signs-of-police-force-s-significant-impact-in-recent

Tehran, IRNA – Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei, the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, said, “In recent years, the significant role played by police force—spanning levels from command and staff echelons to local police stations and outposts—in ensuring security has become increasingly prominent; the impact of this role became even more evident during the second and third imposed wars.”
