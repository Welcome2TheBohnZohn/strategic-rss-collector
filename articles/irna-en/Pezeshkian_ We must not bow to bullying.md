# Pezeshkian: We must not bow to bullying

Published: 2026-10-10 08:16:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288278/Pezeshkian-We-must-not-bow-to-bullying

Karaj, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian stresses the need to advance scientific knowledge, improve productivity, and make greater use of modern technology, saying Iran must not bow to bullying or unfair treatment.
