# Iran to boost deterrence with domestic capabilities: Commander

Published: 2026-10-27 15:30:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305226/Iran-to-boost-deterrence-with-domestic-capabilities-Commander

Tehran, IRNA – The commander of Iran’s Khatam al-Anbiya Joint Air Defense Headquarters said the country’s air defense will continue to advance and strengthen its deterrence power, relying on domestic capabilities.
