# Iran condemns Colombia’s ‘baseless’ accusations against its armed forces

Published: 2026-09-25 14:36:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274242/Iran-condemns-Colombia-s-baseless-accusations-against-its-armed

Tehran, IRNA — Iran has strongly condemned the baseless claims made by the Colombian government against the Islamic Republic and part of Iran’s official armed forces.
