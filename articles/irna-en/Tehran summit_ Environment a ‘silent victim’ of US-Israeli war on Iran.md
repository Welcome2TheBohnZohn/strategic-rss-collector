# Tehran summit: Environment a ‘silent victim’ of US-Israeli war on Iran

Published: 2026-11-20 08:11:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327293/Tehran-summit-Environment-a-silent-victim-of-US-Israeli-war

Tehran, IRNA - War against people and war against nature are “expressions of the same state of mind,” American economist Jeffrey Sachs told a Tehran seminar, as Iran hosted an annual event marking the International Day for Preventing the Exploitation of the Environment in War and Armed Conflict.
