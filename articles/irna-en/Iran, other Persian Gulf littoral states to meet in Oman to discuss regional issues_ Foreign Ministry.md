# Iran, other Persian Gulf littoral states to meet in Oman to discuss regional issues: Foreign Ministry

Published: 2026-09-11 14:53:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261089/Iran-other-Persian-Gulf-littoral-states-to-meet-in-Oman-to-discuss

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says Iran and other Persian Gulf littoral states will sit down next week to discuss regional issues, including a Tehran–Muscat deal to manage shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.
