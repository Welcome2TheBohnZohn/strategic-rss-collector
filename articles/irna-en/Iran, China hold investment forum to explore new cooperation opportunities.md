# Iran, China hold investment forum to explore new cooperation opportunities

Published: 2026-11-08 14:46:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316081/Iran-China-hold-investment-forum-to-explore-new-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Iran and China have held an investment and trade forum in Shanghai to explore new opportunities for cooperation and expand bilateral trade and investment ties, bringing together economic and diplomatic officials, major Iranian companies and Chinese business representatives.
