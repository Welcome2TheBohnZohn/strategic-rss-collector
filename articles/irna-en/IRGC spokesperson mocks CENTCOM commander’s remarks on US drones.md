# IRGC spokesperson mocks CENTCOM commander’s remarks on US drones

Published: 2026-11-07 06:03:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314379/IRGC-spokesperson-mocks-CENTCOM-commander-s-remarks-on-US-drones

Tehran, IRNA – Spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi has reacted to remarks by the commander of the US Central Command (CENTCOM) about the transformation of multimillion-dollar American MQ drones into kamikaze unmanned aerial vehicles.
