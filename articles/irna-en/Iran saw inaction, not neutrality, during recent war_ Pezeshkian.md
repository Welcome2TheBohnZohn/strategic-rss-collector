# Iran saw inaction, not neutrality, during recent war: Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-09-24 06:14:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273123/Iran-saw-inaction-not-neutrality-during-recent-war-Pezeshkian

New York, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian told European Council President Antonio Costa on Wednesday that Iran had seen inaction rather than neutrality from some countries during the recent war, calling on European states to reconsider their approach and maintain greater independence.
