# Negotiations are another battlefield; enemy lacks capacity for new war: IRGC spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-22 08:46:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271016/Negotiations-are-another-battlefield-enemy-lacks-capacity-for

Tehran, IRNA — The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says Iran is fully prepared for war but can also pursue negotiations if national interests require, stressing that talks can constitute “another battlefield.”
