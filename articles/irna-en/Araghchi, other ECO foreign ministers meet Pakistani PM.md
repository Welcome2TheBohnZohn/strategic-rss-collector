# Araghchi, other ECO foreign ministers meet Pakistani PM

Published: 2026-11-16 15:48:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323743/Araghchi-other-ECO-foreign-ministers-meet-Pakistani-PM

Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif held a joint meeting with the heads of delegations attending the 30th ECO Council of Ministers, including Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi.
