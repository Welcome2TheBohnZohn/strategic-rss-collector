# Iran able enough to target US warships, says Acting Defense Minister

Published: 2026-09-08 06:16:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258021/Iran-able-enough-to-target-US-warships-says-Acting-Defense-Minister

Tehran, IRNA — Acting Defense Minister Brigadier General Seyyed Majid Ibn al-Reza says the country is fully capable of targeting US warships involved in enforcing economic blockades, stressing that this capability exists and its results will become evident.
