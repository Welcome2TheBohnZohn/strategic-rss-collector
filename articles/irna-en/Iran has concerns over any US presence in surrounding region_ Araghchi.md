# Iran has concerns over any US presence in surrounding region: Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-16 12:09:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294187/Iran-has-concerns-over-any-US-presence-in-surrounding-region

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Seyyed Abbas Araghchi has reiterated that Tehran “has concerns and sensitivities regarding any US presence in the surrounding region.”
