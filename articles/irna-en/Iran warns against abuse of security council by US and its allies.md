# Iran warns against abuse of security council by US and its allies

Published: 2026-10-01 19:05:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280512/Iran-warns-against-abuse-of-security-council-by-US-and-its-allies

New York, IRNA – Gholamhossein Darzi, Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations, emphasized that the Security Council must uphold the UN Charter and international law while refraining from any politicization or abuse of its procedures; anf also warned that the United States and its allies have repeatedly exploited the Council’s procedures and powers to advance short-sighted political agendas.
