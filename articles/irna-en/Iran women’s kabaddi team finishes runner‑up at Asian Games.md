# Iran women’s kabaddi team finishes runner‑up at Asian Games

Published: 2026-09-26 10:23:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274969/Iran-women-s-kabaddi-team-finishes-runner-up-at-Asian-Games

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s national women’s kabaddi team has delivered a strong performance and finished as runner‑up at the 2026 Asian Games kabaddi final in Nagoya.
