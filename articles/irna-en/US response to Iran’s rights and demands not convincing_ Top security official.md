# US response to Iran’s rights and demands not convincing: Top security official

Published: 2026-11-03 16:08:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311620/US-response-to-Iran-s-rights-and-demands-not-convincing-Top

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Mohsen Rezaei dismissed the US response to Iran’s demands as “unconvincing”, saying that Washington must explicitly address and comply with “Iran’s seven conditions.”
