# In photos: Sugarcane plantations covering farms in southwest Iran

Published: 2026-09-16 09:16:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86265539/In-photos-Sugarcane-plantations-covering-farms-in-southwest

Sugarcane plantations cover about 16,500 hectares of farmland in Khuzestan Province. Planting began in mid‑August and continues until late September, and due to the province’s intense heat, the work is done in the early hours of the day.
