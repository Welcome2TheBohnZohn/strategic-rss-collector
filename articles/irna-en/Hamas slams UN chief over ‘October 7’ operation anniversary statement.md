# Hamas slams UN chief over ‘October 7’ operation anniversary statement

Published: 2026-10-06 13:17:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285155/Hamas-slams-UN-chief-over-October-7-operation-anniversary-statement

Tehran, IRNA – Hamas has strongly condemned a statement by UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on the third anniversary of the Israeli aggression on the Gaza Strip, saying it contains baseless claims and ignores historical facts.
