# Iran has no intention of transferring enriched uranium abroad: Diplomat

Published: 2026-11-24 08:41:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331069/Iran-has-no-intention-of-transferring-enriched-uranium-abroad

Moscow, IRNA — Mojtaba Ferdowsipoor, head of the Iranian Interests Section in Cairo, has rejected the speculations that the Islamic Republic will transfer its enriched uranium abroad, stressing that the stockpile will remain in the country and under the supervision of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA).
