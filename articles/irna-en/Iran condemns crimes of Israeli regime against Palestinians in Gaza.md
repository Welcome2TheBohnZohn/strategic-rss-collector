# Iran condemns crimes of Israeli regime against Palestinians in Gaza

Published: 2026-11-19 20:03:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327105/Iran-condemns-crimes-of-Israeli-regime-against-Palestinians-in

Tehran, IRNA — The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran strongly condemned the crimes committed by the Zionist regime against the oppressed Palestinian people—particularly in Gaza—and emphasized the urgent need for effective action by the international community to halt the genocide and punish the Zionist perpetrators.
