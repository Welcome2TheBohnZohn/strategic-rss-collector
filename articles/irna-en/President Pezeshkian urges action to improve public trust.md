# President Pezeshkian urges action to improve public trust

Published: 2026-10-07 07:51:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285718/President-Pezeshkian-urges-action-to-improve-public-trust

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for greater focus on solving people’s problems, saying social trust would not have deteriorated to its level if officials had worked to resolve difficulties instead of criticizing others.
