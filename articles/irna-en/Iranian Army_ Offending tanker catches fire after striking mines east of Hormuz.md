# Iranian Army: Offending tanker catches fire after striking mines east of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-31 19:01:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308650/Iranian-Army-Offending-tanker-catches-fire-after-striking-mines

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Army Navy says another oil tanker caught fire after striking naval mines while sailing through an unauthorized and unsafe area east of the Strait of Hormuz.
