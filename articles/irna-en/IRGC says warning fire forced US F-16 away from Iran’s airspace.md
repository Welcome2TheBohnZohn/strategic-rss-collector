# IRGC says warning fire forced US F-16 away from Iran’s airspace

Published: 2026-11-27 09:22:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333823/IRGC-says-warning-fire-forced-US-F-16-away-from-Iran-s-airspace

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced that a US F-16 fighter jet changed course and moved away from Iranian airspace after warning fire from an air defense system operated by the IRGC Aerospace Force.
