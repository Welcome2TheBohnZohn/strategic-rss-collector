# Araghchi pursues Caspian Sea vessel attack in Ukraine FM meeting

Published: 2026-09-24 14:56:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273603/Araghchi-pursues-Caspian-Sea-vessel-attack-in-Ukraine-FM-meeting

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has met with his Ukrainian counterpart to address a Ukrainian attack on an Iranian vessel in the Caspian Sea, as well as bilateral relations and broader security developments across Europe and West Asia.
