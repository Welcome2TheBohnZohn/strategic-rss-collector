# Ghalibaf slams US ‘dirty bomb’ allegations as fresh anti-Iran war propaganda

Published: 2026-11-17 17:53:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325040/Ghalibaf-slams-US-dirty-bomb-allegations-as-fresh-anti-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has dismissed US media reports alleging an Iranian plot to target senior Trump administration officials with a so-called dirty bomb or chemical weapon.
