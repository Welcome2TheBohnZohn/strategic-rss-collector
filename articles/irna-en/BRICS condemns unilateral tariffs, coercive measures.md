# BRICS condemns unilateral tariffs, coercive measures

Published: 2026-09-12 14:57:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262034/BRICS-condemns-unilateral-tariffs-coercive-measures

Tehran, IRNA – The BRICS countries have voiced serious concern over the rise of unilateral tariffs and non-tariff measures that distort trade and violate World Trade Organization rules, while condemning unilateral coercive measures that violate international law.
