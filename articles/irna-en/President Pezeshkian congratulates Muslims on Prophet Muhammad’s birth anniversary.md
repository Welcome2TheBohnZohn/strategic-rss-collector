# President Pezeshkian congratulates Muslims on Prophet Muhammad’s birth anniversary

Published: 2026-08-29 19:20:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249826/President-Pezeshkian-congratulates-Muslims-on-Prophet-Muhammad-s

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated the leaders and people of Muslim countries on the anniversary of the birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), emphasizing the great Prophet’s message of peace, security and unity for humanity.
