# Iran-Uzbekistan trade reaches $280 million in five months

Published: 2026-09-27 10:45:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276139/Iran-Uzbekistan-trade-reaches-280-million-in-five-months

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s trade with Uzbekistan reached about $280 million in the first five months of the current Iranian year that started on March 21, with agricultural exports, technology cooperation and joint investment identified as key areas for expanding bilateral economic ties, an Iranian official has said.
