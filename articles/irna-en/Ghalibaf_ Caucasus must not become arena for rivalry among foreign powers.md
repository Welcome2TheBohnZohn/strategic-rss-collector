# Ghalibaf: Caucasus must not become arena for rivalry among foreign powers

Published: 2026-10-05 16:49:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284230/Ghalibaf-Caucasus-must-not-become-arena-for-rivalry-among-foreign

Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has said the Caucasus region must not become an arena for geopolitical rivalry among foreign powers.
