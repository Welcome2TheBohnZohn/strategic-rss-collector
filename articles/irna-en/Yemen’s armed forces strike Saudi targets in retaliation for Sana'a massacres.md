# Yemen’s armed forces strike Saudi targets in retaliation for Sana'a massacres

Published: 2026-10-25 19:18:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303232/Yemen-s-armed-forces-strike-Saudi-targets-in-retaliation-for

Tehran, IRNA - Yemen’s armed forces said Sunday they carried out three military operations in retaliation for massacres committed by Saudi Arabia and its allied forces a day earlier in Sana'a and in provinces in the country’s southwest.
