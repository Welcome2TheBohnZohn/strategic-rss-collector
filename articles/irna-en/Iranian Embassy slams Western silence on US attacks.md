# Iranian Embassy slams Western silence on US attacks

Published: 2026-09-02 15:29:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253517/Iranian-Embassy-slams-Western-silence-on-US-attacks

London, IRNA – The Iranian Embassy in Germany has warned that Western silence over US attacks amounts to abandoning the values of democracy and human rights that the West has long claimed to uphold.
