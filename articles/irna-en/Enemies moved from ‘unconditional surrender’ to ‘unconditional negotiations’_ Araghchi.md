# Enemies moved from ‘unconditional surrender’ to ‘unconditional negotiations’: Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-23 16:09:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330488/Enemies-moved-from-unconditional-surrender-to-unconditional

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the US and Israeli regime failed to achieve their objectives in the two recent wars against Iran, shifting from demands for “unconditional surrender” to “unconditional ceasefire” in the 12-day war and “unconditional negotiations” in the 40-day war.
