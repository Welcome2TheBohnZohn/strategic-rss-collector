# Iran criticizes France over double standard on protests

Published: 2026-10-06 13:04:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285128/Iran-criticizes-France-over-double-standard-on-protests

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized France for a double standard in its approach to protests in Iran and France, saying human rights and the right to demonstrate should be upheld universally.
