# Iranian Navy identifies oil tanker that caught fire in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-13 16:23:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291981/Iranian-Navy-identifies-oil-tanker-that-caught-fire-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Navy says it has identified a non-compliant oil tanker that sustained damage and caught fire in the eastern Strait of Hormuz after switching off its tracking systems and attempting to cross a prohibited area.
