# First VP hails Iran’s growing role in regional blocs ahead of ECO summit

Published: 2026-10-31 12:20:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308389/First-VP-hails-Iran-s-growing-role-in-regional-blocs-ahead-of

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref, referring to the country’s presence in regional blocs and unions, says Iran has been maintaining serious and effective participation in bodies such as the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) and the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU).
