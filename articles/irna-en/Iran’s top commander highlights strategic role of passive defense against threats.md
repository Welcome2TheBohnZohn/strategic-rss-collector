# Iran’s top commander highlights strategic role of passive defense against threats

Published: 2026-10-31 14:07:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308463/Iran-s-top-commander-highlights-strategic-role-of-passive-defense

Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has highlighted the strategic role of passive defense in safeguarding the country’s military capabilities and ensuring operational continuity amid evolving security threats.
