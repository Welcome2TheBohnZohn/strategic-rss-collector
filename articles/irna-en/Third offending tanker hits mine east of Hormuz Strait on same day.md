# Third offending tanker hits mine east of Hormuz Strait on same day

Published: 2026-11-17 15:34:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324933/Third-offending-tanker-hits-mine-east-of-Hormuz-Strait-on-same

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Navy stated that a third offending oil tanker struck a naval mine and caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, November 17.
