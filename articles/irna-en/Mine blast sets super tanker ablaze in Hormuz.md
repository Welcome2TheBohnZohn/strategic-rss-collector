# Mine blast sets super tanker ablaze in Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-30 14:15:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307539/Mine-blast-sets-super-tanker-ablaze-in-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced the explosion of a super tanker after it struck a sea mine on an unauthorized route in the Strait of Hormuz.
