# Iran: Calls for unconditional IAEA Safeguards compliance meaningless

Published: 2026-09-07 20:49:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257904/Iran-Calls-for-unconditional-IAEA-Safeguards-compliance-meaningless

London, IRNA – Iran’s mission to the United Nations in Vienna says calls on Tehran to unconditionally comply with International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) Safeguards are “meaningless” under the current “hazardous” circumstances.
