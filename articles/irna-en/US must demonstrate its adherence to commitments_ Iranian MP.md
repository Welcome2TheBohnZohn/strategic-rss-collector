# US must demonstrate its adherence to commitments: Iranian MP

Published: 2026-10-29 15:59:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307037/US-must-demonstrate-its-adherence-to-commitments-Iranian-MP

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Republic will no longer accept an agreement bearing the signature of an American president unless and until the United States demonstrates its adherence to the commitments reached, says Ebrahim Azizi, a lawmaker and the head of the Iranian Parliament’s National Security and Foreign Policy Committee.
