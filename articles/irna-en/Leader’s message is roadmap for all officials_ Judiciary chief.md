# Leader’s message is roadmap for all officials: Judiciary chief

Published: 2026-08-29 06:58:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248920/Leader-s-message-is-roadmap-for-all-officials-Judiciary-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Judiciary Chief Gholamhossein Mohseni‑Ejei says the message of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei, on the occasion of Government Week serves as a roadmap for all officials and servants of the nation.
