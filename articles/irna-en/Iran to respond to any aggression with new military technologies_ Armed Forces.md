# Iran to respond to any aggression with new military technologies: Armed Forces

Published: 2026-09-27 07:08:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275787/Iran-to-respond-to-any-aggression-with-new-military-technologies

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi, senior spokesperson of Iran’s Armed Forces, says Iran’s missiles and drones have become more advanced and powerful, warning that the country has “surprises” for its enemies and will respond to any possible aggression with new military technologies.
