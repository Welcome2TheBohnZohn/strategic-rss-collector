# Iran warns US, regional states of ‘painful’ attacks if they act against Tehran

Published: 2026-09-20 14:10:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269254/Iran-warns-US-regional-states-of-painful-attacks-if-they-act

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s central military command has warned the United States and its regional allies that any new action against Iran would trigger “continuous, effective and painful” attacks targeting US bases and interests across the region.
