# Pezeshkian congratulates National Day of Persian Poetry and Literature

Published: 2026-09-19 07:48:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267783/Pezeshkian-congratulates-National-Day-of-Persian-Poetry-and-Literature

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated the National Day of Persian Poetry and Literature, praising the well-known Iranian poet Shahriar’s role in linking tradition with modern life.
