# Islamabad will always support Tehran, says Pakistan’s Parliament speaker

Published: 2026-11-16 17:55:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323751/Islamabad-will-always-support-Tehran-says-Pakistan-s-Parliament

Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistan’s National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq has called relations with Iran strong and unparalleled, urged deeper economic cooperation, and said Pakistan will always support Iran.
