# Pezeshkian: Sustainable Persian Gulf security depends on regional responsibility

Published: 2026-09-30 10:36:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279374/Pezeshkian-Sustainable-Persian-Gulf-security-depends-on-regional

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian stresses the special importance of the Strait of Hormuz to regional and global security and economy, emphasizing a responsible approach based on preserving stability and the interests of regional countries.
