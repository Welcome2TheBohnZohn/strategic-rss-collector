# Muscat meeting to focus on new maritime arrangements in Strait of Hormuz: Araghchi

Published: 2026-09-13 13:44:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262964/Muscat-meeting-to-focus-on-new-maritime-arrangements-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the upcoming regional meeting in Muscat will focus exclusively on establishing new maritime arrangements in the Strait of Hormuz.
