# Iran ready to deliver response beyond enemy’s expectations: Speaker Ghalibaf

Published: 2026-10-25 09:37:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302709/Iran-ready-to-deliver-response-beyond-enemy-s-expectations-Speaker

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is prepared to deliver a response beyond its adversaries’ imagination to any attack on its territorial integrity, Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said, adding that threats of a wider war or assassination would not force the Iranian nation to abandon its rights and interests.
