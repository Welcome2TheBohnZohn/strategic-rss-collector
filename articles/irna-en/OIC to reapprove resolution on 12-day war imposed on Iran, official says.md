# OIC to reapprove resolution on 12-day war imposed on Iran, official says

Published: 2026-10-17 17:08:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295327/OIC-to-reapprove-resolution-on-12-day-war-imposed-on-Iran-official

Tehran, IRNA – The Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) is set to reapprove a resolution adopted at an emergency meeting following last year’s 12-day US-Israeli war imposed on Iran, Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said on Saturday.
