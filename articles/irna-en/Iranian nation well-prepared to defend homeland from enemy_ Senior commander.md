# Iranian nation well-prepared to defend homeland from enemy: Senior commander

Published: 2026-09-28 09:12:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277001/Iranian-nation-well-prepared-to-defend-homeland-from-enemy-Senior

Tehran, IRNA – Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari, deputy chief of the Iranian Army for Coordination, has described all the people of the country as soldiers, emphasizing that their combat readiness to defend the Iran sends a clear message to enemies.
