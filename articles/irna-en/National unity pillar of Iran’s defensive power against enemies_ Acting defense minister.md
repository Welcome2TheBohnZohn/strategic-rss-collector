# National unity pillar of Iran’s defensive power against enemies: Acting defense minister

Published: 2026-08-29 14:39:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249574/National-unity-pillar-of-Iran-s-defensive-power-against-enemies

TEHRAN, Iran — Iran’s Acting Defense Minister Brigadier General Seyyed Majid Ibn al-Reza says the recently imposed wars demonstrated the unity of the Iranian people, adding that the enemy will face a united Iran should they attack the nation.
