# Iran to follow reciprocal approach towards United States: Administration spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-01 11:13:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252162/Iran-to-follow-reciprocal-approach-towards-United-States-Administration

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Administration spokesperson Fatemeh Mohajerani, referring to anti-Iran policies of the United States, says Tehran will follow a reciprocal approach to Washington.
