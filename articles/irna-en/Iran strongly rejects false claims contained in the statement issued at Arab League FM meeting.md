# Iran strongly rejects false claims contained in the statement issued at Arab League FM meeting

Published: 2026-09-09 23:13:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260039/Iran-strongly-rejects-false-claims-contained-in-the-statement

Tehran, IRNA — In a statement, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran rejected and condemned all the false claims and baseless accusations leveled against Iran in the final communiqué of the 166th session of the Arab League Council of Foreign Ministers.
