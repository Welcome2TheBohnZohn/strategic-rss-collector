# Countries that supplied Saddam with chemical weapons now accuse Iran of developing them: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-11-21 16:25:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328560/Countries-that-supplied-Saddam-with-chemical-weapons-now-accuse

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has criticized the double standards of countries that helped Saddam Hussein’s regime acquire chemical weapons during the war against Iran, but now accuse Tehran of seeking to develop such weapons.
