# Six French nationals sentenced to life in prison in Iraq for Daesh membership

Published: 2026-11-18 15:25:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326043/Six-French-nationals-sentenced-to-life-in-prison-in-Iraq-for

Tehran, IRNA – An Iraqi criminal court has sentenced six French nationals to life imprisonment for membership in the Daesh terrorist group, bringing the total number of French citizens convicted in the case to 16, according to local media.
