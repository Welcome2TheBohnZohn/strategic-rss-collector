# IRGC targets US Marines camp in Jordan

Published: 2026-09-02 06:29:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252767/IRGC-targets-US-Marines-camp-in-Jordan

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says it has carried out a ballistic missile strike on a military camp housing US Marines in Jordan in response to the recent US aggression against Iranian soil.
