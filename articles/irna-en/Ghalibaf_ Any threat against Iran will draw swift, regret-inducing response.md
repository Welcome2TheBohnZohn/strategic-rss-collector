# Ghalibaf: Any threat against Iran will draw swift, regret-inducing response

Published: 2026-09-29 08:05:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278036/Ghalibaf-Any-threat-against-Iran-will-draw-swift-regret-inducing

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has warned that any threat or adventurism against the Islamic Republic will be met with a “swift and regret-inducing” response, saying the Iranian nation will not retreat in the face of threats, rhetoric, or economic and air blockades.
