# Leader congratulates U23 freestyle wrestlers on world title

Published: 2026-10-19 08:35:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297005/Leader-congratulates-U23-freestyle-wrestlers-on-world-title

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has congratulated Iran’s under-23 freestyle wrestling team on winning the world championship in the United States, praising the athletes’ achievement and the endeavours behind it.
