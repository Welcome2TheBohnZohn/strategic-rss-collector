# Iran, Cuba discuss ways to expand health cooperation

Published: 2026-09-15 13:10:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264995/Iran-Cuba-discuss-ways-to-expand-health-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – The director general for international cooperation at Iran’s Ministry of Health and Cuba’s ambassador to Tehran discussed ways to expand bilateral cooperation in the health, pharmaceutical, and medical equipment sectors.
