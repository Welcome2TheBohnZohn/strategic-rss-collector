# Iran eases pilgrim visas for holy cities

Published: 2026-09-08 20:45:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258951/Iran-eases-pilgrim-visas-for-holy-cities

Qom, IRNA – Iranian foreign ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei announced that the ministry has adopted policies for the fast and low-cost visas issuance to facilitate pilgrimage to Iran’s holy sites for foreign visitors.
