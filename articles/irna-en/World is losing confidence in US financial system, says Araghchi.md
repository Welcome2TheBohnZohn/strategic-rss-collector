# World is losing confidence in US financial system, says Araghchi

Published: 2026-09-11 14:21:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261082/World-is-losing-confidence-in-US-financial-system-says-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has taken a jab at the US Treasury Secretary for his rhetoric about launching an economic war on Iran, saying Scott Bessent should look at his own failure to break the fever in the bond market amid eroding trust in the American financial system.
