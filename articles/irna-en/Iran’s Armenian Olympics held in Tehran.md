# Iran’s Armenian Olympics held in Tehran

Published: 2026-09-12 10:40:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86261806/Iran-s-Armenian-Olympics-held-in-Tehran

The 57th Armenian Olympics of Iran kicked off on September 9, 2026 at the Ararat Stadium in Tehran, with the presence of Minister of Culture Reza Salehi Amiri, and Armenian representatives in Parliament, among other attendees. A classic car exhibition was also held.
