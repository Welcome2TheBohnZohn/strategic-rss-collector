# Public commemorations for martyred Leader will continue until Eid al-Fitr: VP

Published: 2026-11-25 12:26:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332466/Public-commemorations-for-martyred-Leader-will-continue-until

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad-Reza Aref has announced that Iran will hold public commemorative events to mark the first anniversary of the martyrdom of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, until Eid al-Fitr.
