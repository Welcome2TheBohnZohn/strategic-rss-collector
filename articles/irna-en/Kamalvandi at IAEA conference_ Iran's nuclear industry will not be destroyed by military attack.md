# Kamalvandi at IAEA conference: Iran's nuclear industry will not be destroyed by military attack

Published: 2026-09-15 17:51:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265194/Kamalvandi-at-IAEA-conference-Iran-s-nuclear-industry-will-not

London, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran emphasized at the IAEA’s 70th General Conference that military attacks, sabotage, and assassinations cannot destroy Iran’s deeply rooted nuclear industry, and that the country will not relinquish its nuclear rights.
