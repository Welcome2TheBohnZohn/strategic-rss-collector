# Security chief: US response to Iran’s 7 conditions ‘insufficient’

Published: 2026-10-11 16:55:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289861/Security-chief-US-response-to-Iran-s-7-conditions-insufficient

Tehran, IRNA - Major General Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council, said Sunday that the Americans have responded to the seven conditions Iran has put forward for ending the war and opening the Strait of Hormuz, but that Tehran considers their response insufficient.
