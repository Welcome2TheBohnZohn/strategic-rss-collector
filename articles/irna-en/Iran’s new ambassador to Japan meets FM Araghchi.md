# Iran’s new ambassador to Japan meets FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-22 14:51:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329492/Iran-s-new-ambassador-to-Japan-meets-FM-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s newly appointed ambassador to Japan met and held talks with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi before departing on his diplomatic mission to the East Asian nation.
