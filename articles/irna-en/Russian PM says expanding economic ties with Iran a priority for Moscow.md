# Russian PM says expanding economic ties with Iran a priority for Moscow

Published: 2026-10-19 15:32:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297449/Russian-PM-says-expanding-economic-ties-with-Iran-a-priority

Tehran, IRNA – Russian Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin said expanding trade and economic cooperation with Iran is a priority for Moscow during a meeting with First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref on the sidelines of a Shanghai Cooperation Organization meeting in Dushanbe, Tajikistan, on Monday.
