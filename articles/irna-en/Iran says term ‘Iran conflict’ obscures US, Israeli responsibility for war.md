# Iran says term ‘Iran conflict’ obscures US, Israeli responsibility for war

Published: 2026-10-23 05:57:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300785/Iran-says-term-Iran-conflict-obscures-US-Israeli-responsibility

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmail Baqaei has criticized the use of the term “Iran conflict” to describe the US-Israeli-imposed war against Iran, saying the wording obscured responsibility for initiating the war and distorted the facts.
