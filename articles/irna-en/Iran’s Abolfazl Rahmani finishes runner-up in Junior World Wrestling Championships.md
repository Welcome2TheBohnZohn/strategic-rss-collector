# Iran’s Abolfazl Rahmani finishes runner-up in Junior World Wrestling Championships

Published: 2026-08-26 12:57:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86246719/Iran-s-Abolfazl-Rahmani-finishes-runner-up-in-Junior-World-Wrestling

Sari, IRNA – Abolfazl Rahmani, a wrestler from Mazandaran province in northern Iran, secured a silver medal in the 92kg category at the U20 World Wrestling Championships.
