# Ayatollah Khamenei’s martyrdom strengthened national unity: VP Aref

Published: 2026-11-14 16:05:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321650/Ayatollah-Khamenei-s-martyrdom-strengthened-national-unity-VP

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad-Reza Aref says the martyrdom of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, has strengthened national unity and resolve to continue his path.
