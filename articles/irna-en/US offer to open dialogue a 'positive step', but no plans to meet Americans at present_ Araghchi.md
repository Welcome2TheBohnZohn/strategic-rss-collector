# US offer to open dialogue a 'positive step', but no plans to meet Americans at present: Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-31 15:11:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308513/US-offer-to-open-dialogue-a-positive-step-but-no-plans-to

Tehran, IRNA –Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Iran has thoroughly reviewed a message sent by US Vice President JD Vance, stressing, however, that there is no need to meet with the Americans at present.
