# Iran says US cannot sow regional chaos and then play victim

Published: 2026-10-21 14:49:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299705/Iran-says-US-cannot-sow-regional-chaos-and-then-play-victim

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says the United States cannot fuel regional instability and then demand that the international community condemn Iran, holding Washington and Israel responsible for the consequences of their military aggression.
