# Two more violating oil tankers catch fire in Strait of Hormuz: Iran Army

Published: 2026-11-22 16:43:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329579/Two-more-violating-oil-tankers-catch-fire-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Iranian Army Navy has announced that two more oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines as they were entering a prohibited zone east of the Strait of Hormuz.
