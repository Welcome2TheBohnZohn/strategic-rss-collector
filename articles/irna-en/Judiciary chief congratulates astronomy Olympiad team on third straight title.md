# Judiciary chief congratulates astronomy Olympiad team on third straight title

Published: 2026-10-04 18:58:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283246/Judiciary-chief-congratulates-astronomy-Olympiad-team-on-third

Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s judiciary chief congratulated the national astronomy and astrophysics Olympiad team on winning five gold medals and the championship for the third consecutive year at the international Olympiad in Vietnam.
