# IRGC commander: Iran’s Armed Forces hold operational control of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-21 06:04:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298925/IRGC-commander-Iran-s-Armed-Forces-hold-operational-control

Khomein, IRNA – Iranian Armed Forces control the battlefield in the Strait of Hormuz, which will continue for as long as the Islamic Republic desires, says Major General Mostafa Izadi, Deputy Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC).
