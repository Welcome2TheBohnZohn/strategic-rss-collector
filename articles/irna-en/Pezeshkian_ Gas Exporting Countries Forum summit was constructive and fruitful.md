# Pezeshkian: Gas Exporting Countries Forum summit was constructive and fruitful

Published: 2026-10-27 21:51:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305393/Pezeshkian-Gas-Exporting-Countries-Forum-summit-was-constructive

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkianprior to his departure from Russia, Masoud Pezeshkian, President of the Islamic Republic of Iran, commented on his two-day visit to the country, stating: "During the 8th Summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF), extensive and in-depth discussions and interactions took place. We reached positive understandings, and the summit proved to be both constructive and fruitful."
