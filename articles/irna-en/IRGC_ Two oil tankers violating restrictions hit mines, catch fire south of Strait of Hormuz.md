# IRGC: Two oil tankers violating restrictions hit mines, catch fire south of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-15 05:39:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321966/IRGC-Two-oil-tankers-violating-restrictions-hit-mines-catch

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced that two oil tankers violating restrictions have hit naval mines south of the Strait of Hormuz and are on fire.
