# President Pezeshkian leaves Tehran for Ashgabat for CIS, Caspian summits

Published: 2026-10-08 14:21:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287108/President-Pezeshkian-leaves-Tehran-for-Ashgabat-for-CIS-Caspian

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has departed for Turkmenistan to attend the Council of Heads of State of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) and the Environmental Summit of the Heads of Caspian Sea Littoral States.
