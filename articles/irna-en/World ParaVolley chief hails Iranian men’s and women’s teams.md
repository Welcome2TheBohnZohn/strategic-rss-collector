# World ParaVolley chief hails Iranian men’s and women’s teams

Published: 2026-10-23 15:01:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301129/World-ParaVolley-chief-hails-Iranian-men-s-and-women-s-teams

Nagoya, IRNA – The Head of World ParaVolley congratulated the Iranian national men’s and women’s sitting volleyball teams on winning gold and silver medals at the 5th Asian Para Games Aichi-Nagoya 2026.
