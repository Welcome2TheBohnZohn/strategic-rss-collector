# Armenian envoy stresses expansion of Iran–Armenia rail, trade ties

Published: 2026-09-20 05:42:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268546/Armenian-envoy-stresses-expansion-of-Iran-Armenia-rail-trade

Tehran, IRNA — Armenian Ambassador to Iran Grigor Hakobyan has stressed the expansion of railway and trade cooperation between Tehran and Yerevan, saying linking the two countries’ rail networks would give Armenia access to southern international waters and diversify Iran’s routes to Europe.
