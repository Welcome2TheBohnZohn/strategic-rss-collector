# Iran wins Greco-Roman wrestling World Championship title

Published: 2026-10-26 14:29:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304105/Iran-wins-Greco-Roman-wrestling-World-Championship-title

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s national Greco-Roman wrestling team won the early overall title at the World Championships in Kazakhstan, with three gold, two silver and two bronze medals.
