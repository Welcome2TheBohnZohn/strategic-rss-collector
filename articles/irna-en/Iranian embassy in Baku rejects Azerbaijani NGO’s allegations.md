# Iranian embassy in Baku rejects Azerbaijani NGO’s allegations

Published: 2026-10-14 14:11:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292942/Iranian-embassy-in-Baku-rejects-Azerbaijani-NGO-s-allegations

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s embassy in Baku has rejected allegations by an Azerbaijani non-governmental organization linking Iran to issues surrounding school dress codes in Azerbaijan, urging the need to avoid unnecessary tensions in friendly relations between the two neighbors.
