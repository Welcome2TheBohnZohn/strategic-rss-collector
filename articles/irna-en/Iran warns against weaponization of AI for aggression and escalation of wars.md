# Iran warns against weaponization of AI for aggression and escalation of wars

Published: 2026-11-25 06:38:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331860/Iran-warns-against-weaponization-of-AI-for-aggression-and-escalation

New York, IRNA — Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations in New York, Gholamhossein Darzi, has warned that the weaponization of artificial intelligence for aggression and escalation of conflicts raises serious concerns, citing the US and Israeli regime’s wars against Iran in June 2025 and February 2026.
