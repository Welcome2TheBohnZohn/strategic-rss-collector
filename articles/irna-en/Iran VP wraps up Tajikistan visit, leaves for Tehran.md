# Iran VP wraps up Tajikistan visit, leaves for Tehran

Published: 2026-10-20 14:31:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298562/Iran-VP-wraps-up-Tajikistan-visit-leaves-for-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref wrapped up his two-day visit to Tajikistan, where he attended the SCO prime ministers’ summit, and left Dushanbe for Tehran on Tuesday evening.
