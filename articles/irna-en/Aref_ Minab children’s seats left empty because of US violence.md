# Aref: Minab children’s seats left empty because of US violence

Published: 2026-09-23 07:30:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272160/Aref-Minab-children-s-seats-left-empty-because-of-US-violence

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref stated that the students of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab ought to have been in their classroom today, yet the brutality and violence and the arrogance and violence of the United States have left their seats empty among the Iranian students.
