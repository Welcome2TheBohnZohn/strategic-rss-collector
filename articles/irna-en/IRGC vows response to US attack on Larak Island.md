# IRGC vows response to US attack on Larak Island

Published: 2026-08-30 20:40:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86250409/IRGC-vows-response-to-US-attack-on-Larak-Island

Tehran, IRNA - The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says a US attack on the Persian Gulf island of Larak left several Iranian military personnel and civilians martyred and wounded, and vowed to respond to the act of aggression.
