# Iran demands end to selective IAEA statute implementation

Published: 2026-10-23 12:11:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301031/Iran-demands-end-to-selective-IAEA-statute-implementation

London, IRNA – On the 70th anniversary of the approval of the IAEA statute, Iran’s permanent mission to the UN in Vienna issued a statement calling for an end to the selective and politically motivated implementation of the Agency’s obligations.
