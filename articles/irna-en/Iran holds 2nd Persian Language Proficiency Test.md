# Iran holds 2nd Persian Language Proficiency Test

Published: 2026-10-30 12:40:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307514/Iran-holds-2nd-Persian-Language-Proficiency-Test

Tehran, IRNA – The second session of the Persian Language Proficiency Test (Parsik) has been held at four domestic and international examination centers, says a deputy of the minister of science, research, and technology.
