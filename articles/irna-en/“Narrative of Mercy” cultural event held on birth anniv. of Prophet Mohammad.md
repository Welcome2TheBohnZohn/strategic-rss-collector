# “Narrative of Mercy” cultural event held on birth anniv. of Prophet Mohammad

Published: 2026-08-30 07:28:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86250013/Narrative-of-Mercy-cultural-event-held-on-birth-anniv-of-Prophet

Commemorating the birth anniversary of the Prophet Mohammad (PBUH), a cultural and artistic event titled “Narrative of Mercy” was held at Tehran’s Vahdat Hall on Saturday (August 29), featuring a performance of the symphony “The Soul of the Word” (Jam-e-Jahan Nama) by Iran’s National Music Orchestra. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, Culture Minister Abbas Salehi, and a number of foreign ambassadors attended the event.
