# Iran says international silence on US attacks is ‘not neutrality’

Published: 2026-09-02 14:06:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86253520/Iran-says-international-silence-on-US-attacks-is-not-neutrality

Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmail Baqaei says that governments and international organizations that remain silent over US attacks on civilians, or seek to justify them, should not regard their silence as neutrality.
