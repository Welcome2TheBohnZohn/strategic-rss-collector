# US expected to end aggression, blockade if committed to diplomacy: Iran

Published: 2026-11-14 05:29:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320910/US-expected-to-end-aggression-blockade-if-committed-to-diplomacy

New York, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador and chargé d’affaires to the United Nations says that if Washington is genuinely committed to a diplomatic solution, it must immediately and unconditionally end its aggression against Iran, lift the maritime blockade and other unlawful unilateral measures, and provide credible guarantees against renewed attacks.
