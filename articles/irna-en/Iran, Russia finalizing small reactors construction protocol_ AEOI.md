# Iran, Russia finalizing small reactors construction protocol: AEOI

Published: 2026-10-28 14:38:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306261/Iran-Russia-finalizing-small-reactors-construction-protocol

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) has announced that cooperation with Russia to build small modular reactors is underway, and a protocol with Moscow will be signed to accelerate the construction process.
