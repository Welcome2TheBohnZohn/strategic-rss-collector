# Iran, Oman FMs hold talks in New York

Published: 2026-09-26 06:45:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274583/Iran-Oman-FMs-hold-talks-in-New-York

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Omani counterpart, Sayyid Badr al-Busaidi, have held talks on the margins of the 81st UN General Assembly session in New York.
