# Iran beats Maldives in Asian Games beach volleyball opener

Published: 2026-09-20 08:48:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268784/Iran-beats-Maldives-in-Asian-Games-beach-volleyball-opener

Nagoya, IRNA– Iran’s beach volleyball team has claimed its first win at the Asian Games in Nagoya, defeating Maldives 2–0 in straight sets at Mizuho Park Stadium.
