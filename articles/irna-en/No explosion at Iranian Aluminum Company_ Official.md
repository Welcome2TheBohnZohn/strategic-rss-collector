# No explosion at Iranian Aluminum Company: Official

Published: 2026-10-21 07:21:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298891/No-explosion-at-Iranian-Aluminum-Company-Official

Arak, IRNA – The governor of Arak, central Iran, says a technical fault in the electrical system and voltage fluctuations caused an incident at Iranian Aluminum Company (IRALCO), with no explosion or sabotage involved.
