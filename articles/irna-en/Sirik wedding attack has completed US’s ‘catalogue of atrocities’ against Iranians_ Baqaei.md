# Sirik wedding attack has completed US’s ‘catalogue of atrocities’ against Iranians: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-02 07:02:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252866/Sirik-wedding-attack-has-completed-US-s-catalogue-of-atrocities

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says “the United States’ catalogue of atrocities” against the Iranian nation is now complete following the attack on a wedding in the Kuhestak Port of the city of Sirik in southern Iran.
