# Iran ready for joint probe into Kuwait attack: Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-27 05:41:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333676/Iran-ready-for-joint-probe-into-Kuwait-attack-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has firmly rejected allegations that Iran was involved in a drone attack in Kuwait and expressed Tehran’s readiness to conduct a joint investigation with Kuwait and Italy to establish the facts.
