# Iran holds UN Security Council responsible for Israeli attacks on Lebanon

Published: 2026-09-09 15:07:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259845/Iran-holds-UN-Security-Council-responsible-for-Israeli-attacks

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry has condemned Israel’s continued military attacks on Lebanon, saying the UN Security Council bore responsibility for failing to act against Israeli aggression.
