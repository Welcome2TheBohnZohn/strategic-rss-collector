# Iran launches 10 natural gas industry projects with $350m investment

Published: 2026-11-28 07:52:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86334496/Iran-launches-10-natural-gas-industry-projects-with-350m-investment

Tehran, IRNA — Iran has commissioned 10 natural gas industry projects under five development plans, backed by $350 million in investment, to increase daily production capacity by 32 million cubic meters of sweet gas.
