# Tehran meeting offers opportunity to advance regional peace, security: Foreign Minister Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-28 07:46:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305695/Tehran-meeting-offers-opportunity-to-advance-regional-peace

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says a quadrilateral meeting in Tehran provided an important opportunity to advance dialogue on regional peace and security, stressing that sustained cooperation would require confidence-building, mutual respect for sovereignty and practical commitments.
