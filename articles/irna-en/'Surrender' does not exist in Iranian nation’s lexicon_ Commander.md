# 'Surrender' does not exist in Iranian nation’s lexicon: Commander

Published: 2026-10-29 11:43:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306865/Surrender-does-not-exist-in-Iranian-nation-s-lexicon-Commander

Tehran, IRNA — The enemies of the Islamic Republic failed to achieve their goals in their recent war of aggression, emphasizing that “surrender” has no place in the lexicon of the Iranian nation, says Deputy Chief of the Iranian Army for Coordination Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari.
