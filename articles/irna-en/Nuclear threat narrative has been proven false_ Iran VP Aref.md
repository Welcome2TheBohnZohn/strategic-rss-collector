# Nuclear threat narrative has been proven false: Iran VP Aref

Published: 2026-09-05 09:55:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255348/Nuclear-threat-narrative-has-been-proven-false-Iran-VP-Aref

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref says that the world has realized that the alleged nuclear threat used against Iran was nothing but a false narrative, stressing that the Islamic Republic remains committed to using advanced technologies to serve national and regional needs.
