# Bahrain’s allegations against Iran ‘a cynical attempt’ to distort facts: Iran UN envoy

Published: 2026-11-24 05:47:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330813/Bahrain-s-allegations-against-Iran-a-cynical-attempt-to-distort

New York, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations in New York, Gholamhossein Darzi, has rejected Bahrain’s “baseless allegations” against Iran, saying the United States uses military bases across the region, including in Bahrain, to carry out its ongoing acts of aggression against Iran.
