# Iran no longer needs to import gasoline: President

Published: 2026-10-18 12:38:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296178/Iran-no-longer-needs-to-import-gasoline-President

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran’s conservation and consumption management helped prevent shortages in the fuel sector, despite high energy consumption and difficulties securing fuel needs amid the US economic war.
