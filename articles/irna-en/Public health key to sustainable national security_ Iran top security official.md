# Public health key to sustainable national security: Iran top security official

Published: 2026-10-10 05:51:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287991/Public-health-key-to-sustainable-national-security-Iran-top

Hamedan, IRNA – Iran’s Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, Major General Mohsen Rezaei, has described public health and trust as foundations of sustainable national security, praising healthcare volunteers for their efforts to improve people’s quality of life.
