# Iran to equip 12,000 schools with solar power plants

Published: 2026-11-05 08:25:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313129/Iran-to-equip-12-000-schools-with-solar-power-plants

Sanandaj, IRNA – Iran plans to equip 12,000 schools nationwide with solar power plants in the first phase of a renewable energy project, with the required equipment already dispatched to the provinces, an official says.
