# Exclusive | Media reports on consultation with Qatari mediator ‘baseless speculations’: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-29 07:11:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277935/Exclusive-Media-reports-on-consultation-with-Qatari-mediator

New York, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has dismissed as ‘baseless speculations’ some media reports regarding Iran’s diplomatic consultation with the Qatari mediator in New York.
