# Iran's fight against aggressive enemies will continue until complete victory: Deputy speaker

Published: 2026-09-10 15:23:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260550/Iran-s-fight-against-aggressive-enemies-will-continue-until-complete

Karaj, IRNA – Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad says Iran remains determined to fight the foreign aggressors until complete victory, noting that the enemies do not yet know the Armed Forces and the people of the Islamic Republic.
