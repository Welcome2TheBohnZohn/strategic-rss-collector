# Yemeni forces repel Saudi-backed advance in al-Jawf

Published: 2026-10-04 14:05:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283076/Yemeni-forces-repel-Saudi-backed-advance-in-al-Jawf

Tehran, IRNA – Yemen’s armed forces repelled an attempt by Saudi-backed forces to advance toward their positions in eastern al-Jawf province, destroying military equipment and killing or wounding dozens of mercenaries, the armed forces spokesperson said on Sunday.
