# Iran’s Army shoots down US Lucas drone over port city of Bandar Abbas

Published: 2026-10-25 08:22:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302670/Iran-s-Army-shoots-down-US-Lucas-drone-over-port-city-of-Bandar

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army air defense force has shot down a US Lucas drone over the southern port city of Bandar Abbas, according to the Army.
