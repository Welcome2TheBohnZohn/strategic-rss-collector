# In photos: Persepolis defeats Sanat Naft Abadan in Iran Pro League

Published: 2026-10-10 09:33:21

The football teams of Persepolis and Sanat Naft Abadan faced each other at Shohada-ye Shahr-e Qods Stadium on October 9, 2026, on Day 8 of the Iran Pro League. The match ended in a 3–1 victory for Persepolis.
