# Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until US accepts its terms: Iran

Published: 2026-11-07 06:37:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314322/Strait-of-Hormuz-will-stay-closed-until-US-accepts-its-terms

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Armed Forces have control over the Strait of Hormuz, which will remain closed until the United States accepts Tehran’s conditions, Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia has said.
