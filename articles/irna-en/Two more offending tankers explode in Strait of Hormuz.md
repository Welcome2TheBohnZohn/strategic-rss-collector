# Two more offending tankers explode in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-10 12:39:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318130/Two-more-offending-tankers-explode-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Navy announced on Tuesday that two more offending oil tankers have exploded in an unauthorized route south of the Strait of Hormuz and have been left burning.
