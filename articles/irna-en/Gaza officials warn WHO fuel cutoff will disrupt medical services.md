# Gaza officials warn WHO fuel cutoff will disrupt medical services

Published: 2026-09-15 08:50:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264593/Gaza-officials-warn-WHO-fuel-cutoff-will-disrupt-medical-services

Tehran, IRNA – Director of Hospitals at Gaza’s Health Ministry Mohammed Zaqout has warned that the World Health Organization’s decision to halt fuel supplies to hospitals in the Gaza Strip in the coming days will seriously affect patients’ health and the provision of medical services.
