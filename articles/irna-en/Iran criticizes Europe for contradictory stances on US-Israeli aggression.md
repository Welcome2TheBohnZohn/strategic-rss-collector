# Iran criticizes Europe for contradictory stances on US-Israeli aggression

Published: 2026-10-25 05:47:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302353/Iran-criticizes-Europe-for-contradictory-stances-on-US-Israeli

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized Germany and its European partners for their irresponsible and contradictory stances on the US-Israeli military aggression against Iran.
