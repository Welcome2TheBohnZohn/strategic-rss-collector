# Iran says Israel must be held accountable for genocide, war crimes

Published: 2026-10-08 07:15:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286712/Iran-says-Israel-must-be-held-accountable-for-genocide-war-crimes

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s representative to the Sixth Committee of the UN General Assembly has strongly condemned US-Israeli crimes against the peoples of Iran and Palestine, saying Israel must be held accountable for genocide, war crimes, crimes against humanity, and terrorist acts.
