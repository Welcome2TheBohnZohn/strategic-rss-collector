# Pezeshkian’s UN speech prevented false narratives against Iran, says Iranology Foundation chief

Published: 2026-09-26 16:30:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275373/Pezeshkian-s-UN-speech-prevented-false-narratives-against-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The head of the Iranology Foundation, Ali Akbar Salehi, has emphasized that Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian’s speech at the United Nations not only conveyed Iran’s legitimate position but also prevented false narratives against the country.
