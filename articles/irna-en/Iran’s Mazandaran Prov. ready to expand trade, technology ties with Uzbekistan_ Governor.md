# Iran’s Mazandaran Prov. ready to expand trade, technology ties with Uzbekistan: Governor

Published: 2026-10-21 10:17:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299415/Iran-s-Mazandaran-Prov-ready-to-expand-trade-technology-ties

Sari, IRNA – Iran’s northern Mazandaran Province is ready to expand trade with Uzbekistan, transfer technical expertise and pursue joint economic projects, the provincial governor has said.
