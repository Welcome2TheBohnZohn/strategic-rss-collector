# In photos: Memorial ceremony for prominent Iranian industrialist Mohsen Khalili Iraqi

Published: 2026-10-19 07:42:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86296729/In-photos-Memorial-ceremony-for-prominent-Iranian-industrialist

Industrialists and entrepreneurs attend a memorial ceremony for Mohsen Khalili Iraqi, a veteran figure in Iran’s industrial sector, at the Iran Chamber of Commerce in Tehran on October 18, 2026. Khalili devoted nearly seven decades to industrial activities and improving the business environment, advocating a stronger role for the private sector in economic policymaking and the development of Iran’s industry.
