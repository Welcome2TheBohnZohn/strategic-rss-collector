# Pezeshkian meets Bangladesh PM in New York

Published: 2026-09-23 19:19:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272952/Pezeshkian-meets-Bangladesh-PM-in-New-York

New York, IRNA – On the sidelines of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly, President Masoud Pezeshkian met and held talks with Bangladesh Prime Minister Tarique Rahman Wednesday.
