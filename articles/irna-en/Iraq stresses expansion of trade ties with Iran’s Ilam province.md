# Iraq stresses expansion of trade ties with Iran’s Ilam province

Published: 2026-09-07 18:16:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257432/Iraq-stresses-expansion-of-trade-ties-with-Iran-s-Ilam-province

Ilam, IRNA – The Consul General of Iraq in Ilam province Saadoun al‑Saedi, says Ilam’s geographical and cultural closeness to Iraq gives a strong potential to expand commercial and tourism links with the country’s border provinces.
