# Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until Iran’s demands are met: IRGC advisor

Published: 2026-10-07 16:11:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286279/Strait-of-Hormuz-will-stay-closed-until-Iran-s-demands-are-met

Tehran, IRNA – A senior advisor to the commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says the Strait of Hormuz is ​closed, and the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran ​have full control over it, adding that this situation will continue until Iran’s legitimate demands are met.
