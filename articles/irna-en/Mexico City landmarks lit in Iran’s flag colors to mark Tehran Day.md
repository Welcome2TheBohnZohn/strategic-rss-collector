# Mexico City landmarks lit in Iran’s flag colors to mark Tehran Day

Published: 2026-10-07 09:04:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285885/Mexico-City-landmarks-lit-in-Iran-s-flag-colors-to-mark-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA – In a symbolic gesture marking Tehran Day, several of Mexico City’s most iconic landmarks, including the Zócalo, the Angel of Independence, and the Cuahtémoc Monument, were illuminated in the colors of Iran’s flag.
