# Leader: Nurses are angels of hope

Published: 2026-10-16 11:10:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294145/Leader-Nurses-are-angels-of-hope

Tehran, IRNA — Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei, the Supreme Leader of the Islamic Revolution, highlighted the reviving, hope-inspiring role of nurses and their significant impact on society’s physical and mental health and security.
