# Foreign flights resume at Imam Khomeini Airport

Published: 2026-10-08 10:32:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286907/Foreign-flights-resume-at-Imam-Khomeini-Airport

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign flights have resumed at Imam Khomeini International Airport after a 40-day interruption, with Iraqi Airways operating its first direct Najaf-Tehran service on Thursday, marking a step toward the gradual restoration of international air traffic.
