# Iran police force represents professional, people-backed security: IRGC Commander

Published: 2026-10-03 10:10:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281738/Iran-police-force-represents-professional-people-backed-security

Tehran, IRNA – Major General Ahmad Vahidi, Chief Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), has described Iran’s Law Enforcement Command (FARAJA) as a powerful, professional and intelligent police force whose security role is supported by the public.
