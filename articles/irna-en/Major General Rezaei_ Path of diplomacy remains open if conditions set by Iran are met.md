# Major General Rezaei: Path of diplomacy remains open if conditions set by Iran are met

Published: 2026-10-31 22:37:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308690/Major-General-Rezaei-Path-of-diplomacy-remains-open-if-conditions

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei has stressed that the path of diplomacy remains open, provided that Iran’s conditions are met.
