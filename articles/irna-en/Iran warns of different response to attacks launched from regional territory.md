# Iran warns of different response to attacks launched from regional territory

Published: 2026-10-05 14:53:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86284110/Iran-warns-of-different-response-to-attacks-launched-from-regional

Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has stressed that the region has had bitter experiences with the US military presence and its interventions. “We must all bear in mind that we are permanent neighbors,” he said, urging regional countries, particularly those that provided their facilities for Israeli attacks on Iran, to reconsider such actions. Baqaei warned that any action originating from the territory of regional countries would be met with a different kind of response.
