# Iran’s security indicators have improved, Armed Forces chief says

Published: 2026-10-10 16:32:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288758/Iran-s-security-indicators-have-improved-Armed-Forces-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s security indicators have improved despite two wars over the past year, Major General Ali Abdollahi, chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces, said, praising the country’s police force for maintaining security despite attacks on its facilities.
