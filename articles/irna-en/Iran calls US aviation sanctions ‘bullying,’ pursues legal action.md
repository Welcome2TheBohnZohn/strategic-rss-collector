# Iran calls US aviation sanctions ‘bullying,’ pursues legal action

Published: 2026-09-29 12:36:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278447/Iran-calls-US-aviation-sanctions-bullying-pursues-legal-action

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s government spokesperson called US sanctions on Iranian aviation companies “act of bullying,” saying Tehran has filed a complaint with international bodies, including ICAO, and is seriously pursuing the legal case.
