# Iran vows stronger defense in response to threats

Published: 2026-10-21 09:49:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299332/Iran-vows-stronger-defense-in-response-to-threats

Tehran, IRNA – Minister of Defense and Armed Forces Logistics, Mehrdad Akhlaghi, stresses that Iran’s response to repeated threats is focused on strengthening its preparedness and defensive capabilities rather than engaging in a war of words.
