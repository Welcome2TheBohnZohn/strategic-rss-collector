# Iran stresses implementation of agreements with Azerbaijan

Published: 2026-10-13 11:35:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291528/Iran-stresses-implementation-of-agreements-with-Azerbaijan

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Interior Minister Eskandar Momeni has emphasized Tehran’s focus on strengthening relations with Azerbaijan, implementing bilateral agreements, and expanding cooperation between the two countries.
