# UN spokesperson affirms sanctions hurt ordinary people

Published: 2026-09-30 08:02:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278982/UN-spokesperson-affirms-sanctions-hurt-ordinary-people

New York, IRNA – United Nations Secretary-General spokesperson Stephane Dujarric says that the UN chief has repeatedly expressed concern over the growing use of unilateral sanctions, stressing that such measures ultimately harm ordinary people.
