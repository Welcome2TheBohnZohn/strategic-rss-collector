# Germany lacked courage to condemn attacks on Iran: Foreign Ministry spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-16 16:37:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266181/Germany-lacked-courage-to-condemn-attacks-on-Iran-Foreign-Ministry

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei says Germany has failed to show “even the minimum moral courage” to condemn US-Israeli war of aggression against Iran.
