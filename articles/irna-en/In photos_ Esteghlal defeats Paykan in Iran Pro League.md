# In photos: Esteghlal defeats Paykan in Iran Pro League

Published: 2026-09-13 06:37:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86262307/In-photos-Esteghlal-defeats-Paykan-in-Iran-Pro-League

The football teams of Esteghlal and Paykan faced each other at Shohada-ye Shahr-e Qods Stadium, southern Tehran, on September 10, 2026, on Matchday 7 of the Iran Pro League. The match ended in a 1–0 victory for Esteghlal.
