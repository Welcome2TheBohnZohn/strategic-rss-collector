# Pezeshkian urges stronger UN role in addressing international crises

Published: 2026-09-01 17:01:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252485/Pezeshkian-urges-stronger-UN-role-in-addressing-international

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for a more effective and efficient role for the United Nations in dealing with international developments and crises, saying international institutions should not allow powerful states to act with impunity.
