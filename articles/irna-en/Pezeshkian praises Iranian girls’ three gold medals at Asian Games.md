# Pezeshkian praises Iranian girls’ three gold medals at Asian Games

Published: 2026-10-02 15:40:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281075/Pezeshkian-praises-Iranian-girls-three-gold-medals-at-Asian

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has praised Iranian girls’ success in winning three gold medals at the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, Japan, stressing, “Iranian women can stand atop the world’s peaks.”
