# Senior commander: Iran will give immediate, firm, and powerful response to any level of threat

Published: 2026-11-09 19:46:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86317318/Senior-commander-Iran-will-give-immediate-firm-and-powerful

Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Iranian Army’s Ground Forces Brigadier General Ali Jahanshahi saying that the Army Ground Force is prepared to counter any type of threat, added that this force is ready to deliver an immediate, firm, and powerful response—with greater intensity than ever before—to any level of threat.
