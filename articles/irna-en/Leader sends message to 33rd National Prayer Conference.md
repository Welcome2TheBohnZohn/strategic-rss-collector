# Leader sends message to 33rd National Prayer Conference

Published: 2026-10-01 05:56:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279964/Leader-sends-message-to-33rd-National-Prayer-Conference

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has issued a message to the 33rd National Prayer Conference, stressing the importance of establishing and promoting prayer as a prerequisite for building an Islamic society.
