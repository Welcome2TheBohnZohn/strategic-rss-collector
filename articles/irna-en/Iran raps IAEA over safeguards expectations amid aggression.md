# Iran raps IAEA over safeguards expectations amid aggression

Published: 2026-09-09 20:54:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260011/Iran-raps-IAEA-over-safeguards-expectations-amid-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s permanent mission to the United Nations and other international organizations in Vienna has criticized the IAEA’s expectation of normal safeguards implementation amid continued US and Israeli threats and aggression.
