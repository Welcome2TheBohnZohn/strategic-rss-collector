# ‘False flags’ used to justify war against Iran, says FM spokesperson

Published: 2026-10-07 15:56:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286323/False-flags-used-to-justify-war-against-Iran-says-FM-spokesperson

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized those behind the war against Iran for resorting to “false flags” to manufacture incidents, assign blame, and justify an unlawful war of aggression.
