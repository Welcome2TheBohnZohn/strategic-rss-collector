# IRGC Navy: Two violating oil tankers stopped in south of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-30 12:33:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307507/IRGC-Navy-Two-violating-oil-tankers-stopped-in-south-of-Strait

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that two violating oil tankers, one in the eastern part and the other in the western part of the Strait of Hormuz, which intended to pass through an unauthorized route, suffered accidents and were stopped in the southern waters of the Strait of Hormuz.
