# Araghchi arrives in Islamabad to attend ECO summit

Published: 2026-11-15 19:16:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322845/Araghchi-arrives-in-Islamabad-to-attend-ECO-summit

Islamabad, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi arrived in Islamabad on Sunday at the head of a political-economic delegation to attend the 30th meeting of foreign ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) member states.
