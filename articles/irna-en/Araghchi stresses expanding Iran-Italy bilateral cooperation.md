# Araghchi stresses expanding Iran-Italy bilateral cooperation

Published: 2026-10-21 16:07:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299771/Araghchi-stresses-expanding-Iran-Italy-bilateral-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi stressed the need to strengthen bilateral cooperation during a meeting with Ali Akbar Rezaei, Iran’s newly appointed Ambassador to Italy.
