# IRNA chief calls for expanded media cooperation with Cyprus

Published: 2026-11-08 16:33:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316175/IRNA-chief-calls-for-expanded-media-cooperation-with-Cyprus

Tehran, IRNA – Managing Director of the Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA), Hossein Jaberi-Ansari, has emphasized the need to explore opportunities for cooperation between IRNA and Cypriot media outlets and pave the way for expanding media relations.
