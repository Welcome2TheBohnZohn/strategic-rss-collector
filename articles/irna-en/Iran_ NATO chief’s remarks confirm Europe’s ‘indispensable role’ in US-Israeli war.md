# Iran: NATO chief’s remarks confirm Europe’s ‘indispensable role’ in US-Israeli war

Published: 2026-09-27 06:29:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275744/Iran-NATO-chief-s-remarks-confirm-Europe-s-indispensable-role

New York, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Deputy Permanent Representative to the United Nations in New York, Gholamhossein Darzi, says NATO chief’s acknowledgment that thousands of planes took off from European territory to launch attacks against Iran demonstrates “the indispensable role” Europe played in facilitating US aggression and war crimes against Iran.
