# Attacks on Iran undermined international legal order: VP

Published: 2026-10-20 09:04:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298055/Attacks-on-Iran-undermined-international-legal-order-VP

Tehran, IRNA — Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref says that the 40-day military aggression against Iran was not merely an attack on the country, but a direct assault on the foundations of the international legal order and the principle of sovereign equality of states.
