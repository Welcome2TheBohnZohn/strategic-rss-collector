# Pezeshkian says 5% cut in electricity consumption saved Iran up to $10 billion

Published: 2026-11-22 15:33:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329529/Pezeshkian-says-5-cut-in-electricity-consumption-saved-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has reduced electricity consumption by 5% compared with last year, saving between $5 billion and $10 billion, President Masoud Pezeshkian said on Sunday.
