# Iranian people prevented a repeat of history foreign occupation: Top security official

Published: 2026-09-16 16:31:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266101/Iranian-people-prevented-a-repeat-of-history-foreign-occupation

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei says that the Iranian people have prevented a history of foreign occupation and intervention from being repeated during 200 days of conflict.
