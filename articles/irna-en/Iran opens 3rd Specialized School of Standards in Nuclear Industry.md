# Iran opens 3rd Specialized School of Standards in Nuclear Industry

Published: 2026-10-17 12:03:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295060/Iran-opens-3rd-Specialized-School-of-Standards-in-Nuclear-Industry

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has inaugurated its Third Specialized School of Standards in the Nuclear Industry, with Mohammad Eslami, Head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI), in attendance.
