# Pezeshkian calls for deeper Iran-Azerbaijan ties, implementation of agreements

Published: 2026-09-29 17:45:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278700/Pezeshkian-calls-for-deeper-Iran-Azerbaijan-ties-implementation

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for stronger ties with Azerbaijan, stressing that shared borders should not become an obstacle to expanding relations and urging faster development of road, air and rail links between the two neighbors.
