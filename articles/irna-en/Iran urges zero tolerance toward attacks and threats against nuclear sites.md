# Iran urges zero tolerance toward attacks and threats against nuclear sites

Published: 2026-09-18 12:44:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267359/Iran-urges-zero-tolerance-toward-attacks-and-threats-against

London, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador and permanent representative to international organizations in Vienna called for confrontation and zero tolerance toward any attacks on or threats against nuclear facilities under IAEA safeguards.
