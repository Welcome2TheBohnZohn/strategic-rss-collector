# Liquefied gas carrier explodes after striking naval mine in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-11-13 08:28:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320447/Liquefied-gas-carrier-explodes-after-striking-naval-mine-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that a liquefied gas carrier, which ignored multiple warnings and attempted to navigate an unauthorized and dangerous route, struck a naval mine and exploded in the eastern Strait of Hormuz.
