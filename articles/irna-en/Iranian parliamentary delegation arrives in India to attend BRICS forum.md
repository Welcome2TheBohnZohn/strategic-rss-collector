# Iranian parliamentary delegation arrives in India to attend BRICS forum

Published: 2026-10-25 13:47:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303027/Iranian-parliamentary-delegation-arrives-in-India-to-attend-BRICS

New Delhi, IRNA – An Iranian parliamentary delegation has arrived in the Indian capital to participate in the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum, set to begin tomorrow.
