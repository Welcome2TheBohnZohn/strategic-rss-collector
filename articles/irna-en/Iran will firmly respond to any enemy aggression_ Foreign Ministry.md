# Iran will firmly respond to any enemy aggression: Foreign Ministry

Published: 2026-09-09 12:20:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259670/Iran-will-firmly-respond-to-any-enemy-aggression-Foreign-Ministry

Tehran, IRNA – Iran's Foreign Ministry has issued yet another strong condemnation of US attacks on several oil tankers, warning that the Iranian Armed Forces will firmly and decisively respond to enemy aggression by exercising their inherent right to self-defense.
