# Iran stands against an enemy with top military technology: Senior commander

Published: 2026-09-05 12:22:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255583/Iran-stands-against-an-enemy-with-top-military-technology-Senior

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Coordinator of the Islamic Republic of Iran Army Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari says Iran stands against an enemy possessing the world’s full range of equipment and technology, describing modern warfare as a battle of willpower and endurance.
