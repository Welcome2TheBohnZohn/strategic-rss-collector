# Envoy: Iran faced military campaign, intensified economic pressure and incitement of unrest last year

Published: 2026-11-19 04:59:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326353/Envoy-Iran-faced-military-campaign-intensified-economic-pressure

New York, IRNA — Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations, Gholamhossein Darzi, says over last year, the Islamic Republic of Iran has faced a coordinated campaign involving military attacks, attempts to incite domestic unrest and exploit it for specific objectives, the imposition of unilateral coercive measures, and intensified economic pressure.
