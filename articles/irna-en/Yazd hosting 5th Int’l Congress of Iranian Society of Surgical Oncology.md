# Yazd hosting 5th Int’l Congress of Iranian Society of Surgical Oncology

Published: 2026-10-28 08:29:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305817/Yazd-hosting-5th-Int-l-Congress-of-Iranian-Society-of-Surgical

Yazd, IRNA – The 5th International Congress of the Iranian Society of Surgical Oncology (ICISSO) opened in Yazd on Wednesday, bringing together surgeons, researchers and specialists to discuss advances in cancer diagnosis and treatment.
