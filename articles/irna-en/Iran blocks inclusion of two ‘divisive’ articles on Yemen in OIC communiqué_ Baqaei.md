# Iran blocks inclusion of two ‘divisive’ articles on Yemen in OIC communiqué: Baqaei

Published: 2026-10-20 08:29:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298085/Iran-blocks-inclusion-of-two-divisive-articles-on-Yemen-in

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says Iran prevented the inclusion of two divisive paragraphs on Yemen in the final communiqué of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) foreign ministers’ meeting in Baghdad, Iraq.
