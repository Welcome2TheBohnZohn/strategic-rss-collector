# Pezeshkian calls for strengthening Iran–KRG relations

Published: 2026-11-12 12:30:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319993/Pezeshkian-calls-for-strengthening-Iran-KRG-relations

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian, highlighting the deep historical, cultural, and ethnic ties between the people of Iran and the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, has called for the strengthening of bilateral relations between the two sides across all sectors.
