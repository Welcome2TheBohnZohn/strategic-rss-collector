# Iran: International engagement with Afghan officials is essential

Published: 2026-09-16 19:12:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266214/Iran-International-engagement-with-Afghan-officials-is-essential

New York, IRNA – Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations Gholamhossein Darzi stating that Tehran is closely monitoring developments in Afghanistan, pointed out that the continued international engagement with Afghan officials remains essential for advancing stability and addressing the challenges facing Afghanistan.
