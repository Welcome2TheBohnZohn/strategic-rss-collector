# Access to medicine, food, water must not be affected by political disputes: VP

Published: 2026-10-20 07:41:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297979/Access-to-medicine-food-water-must-not-be-affected-by-political

Tehran, IRNA — Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref has called on the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) member states to ensure that access to medicine, water, food, and healthcare services is not affected by political disputes and economic measures.
