# Iran ready for diplomacy and war, deputy foreign minister says

Published: 2026-10-12 14:14:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290798/Iran-ready-for-diplomacy-and-war-deputy-foreign-minister-says

Tehran, IRNA – Tehran is finalizing its response to US considerations on a proposal Iran submitted during a UN General Assembly in New York and remains prepared for both diplomacy and military confrontation, Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi has said.
