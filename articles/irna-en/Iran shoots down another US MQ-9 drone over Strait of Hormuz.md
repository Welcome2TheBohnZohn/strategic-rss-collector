# Iran shoots down another US MQ-9 drone over Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-18 11:54:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325873/Iran-shoots-down-another-US-MQ-9-drone-over-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army Air Defense Force has announced the interception and destruction of one more US MQ-9 drone, in the skies over east of the Strait of Hormuz.
