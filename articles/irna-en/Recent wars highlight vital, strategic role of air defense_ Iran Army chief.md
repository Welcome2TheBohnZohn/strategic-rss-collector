# Recent wars highlight vital, strategic role of air defense: Iran Army chief

Published: 2026-09-01 10:56:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251962/Recent-wars-highlight-vital-strategic-role-of-air-defense-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Army Commander Major General Amir Hatami has described air defense as a symbol of Iran’s defensive power and self-reliance, saying recent wars have once again demonstrated its vital and strategic importance in securing the country and protecting its airspace.
