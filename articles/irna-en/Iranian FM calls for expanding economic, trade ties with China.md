# Iranian FM calls for expanding economic, trade ties with China

Published: 2026-09-30 14:16:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279625/Iranian-FM-calls-for-expanding-economic-trade-ties-with-China

Tehran, IRNA – Iran Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi, in a meeting with the country’s consul general in the Chinese city of Guangzhou, stressed expanding economic and trade relations with China.
