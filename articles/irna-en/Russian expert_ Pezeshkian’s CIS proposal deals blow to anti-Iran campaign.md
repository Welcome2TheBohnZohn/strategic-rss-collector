# Russian expert: Pezeshkian’s CIS proposal deals blow to anti-Iran campaign

Published: 2026-10-14 12:31:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292721/Russian-expert-Pezeshkian-s-CIS-proposal-deals-blow-to-anti-Iran

Moscow, IRNA – A Russian lecturer and expert says Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian’s attendance at the CIS heads-of-state summit is regarded as a landmark event for both Iran and the member states, and the proposal he presented there struck a blow to the US-Israeli campaign against Iran.
