# Speaker: Iran will defeat enemies in economic war

Published: 2026-08-27 10:16:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247702/Speaker-Iran-will-defeat-enemies-in-economic-war

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says the country is facing an all-out economic war, expressing confidence that the government, backed by the people, the private sector and all branches of the Islamic system, will defeat the enemy in this arena.
