# Putin expresses solidarity with Iran while praising people’s resistance

Published: 2026-09-01 16:39:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252451/Putin-expresses-solidarity-with-Iran-while-praising-people-s

Tehran, IRNA – Russian President Vladimir Putin has expressed solidarity with the Iranian people and praised their courage and resistance as he met with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on Tuesday.
