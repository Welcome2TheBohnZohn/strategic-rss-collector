# Iraq opposes politicization of pilgrimage affairs: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-27 11:35:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305010/Iraq-opposes-politicization-of-pilgrimage-affairs-Ambassador

Tehran, IRNA – Iraq’s ambassador to Iran has said that the Iraqi government insists that pilgrimage affairs between the two neighboring countries must not be politicized or face any interruption.
