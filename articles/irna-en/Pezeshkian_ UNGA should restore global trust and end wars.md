# Pezeshkian: UNGA should restore global trust and end wars

Published: 2026-09-22 08:52:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271078/Pezeshkian-UNGA-should-restore-global-trust-and-end-wars

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has expressed hope that a UN General Assembly meeting convened under the banner of trust can restore confidence worldwide and work toward ending war and bloodshed.
