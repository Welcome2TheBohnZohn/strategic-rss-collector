# Bagheri: Iran and China share similar views on many international and regional issues

Published: 2026-11-02 22:04:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310672/Bagheri-Iran-and-China-share-similar-views-on-many-international

Tehran, IRNA – Referring to his consultations with the Chinese Foreign Minister, the Deputy for International Affairs of the Supreme National Security Council Ali Bagheri Kani stated that the Islamic Republic of Iran and the People's Republic of China share common views on many international and regional issues.
