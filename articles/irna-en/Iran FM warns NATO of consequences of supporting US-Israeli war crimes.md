# Iran FM warns NATO of consequences of supporting US-Israeli war crimes

Published: 2026-09-23 16:05:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272817/Iran-FM-warns-NATO-of-consequences-of-supporting-US-Israeli-war

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, referring to the NATO chief’s claim of providing support for the US-Israeli aggression against Iran, has warned European officials about the consequences of facilitating and backing war crimes.
