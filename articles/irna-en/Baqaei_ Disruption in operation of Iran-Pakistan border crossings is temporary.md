# Baqaei: Disruption in operation of Iran-Pakistan border crossings is temporary

Published: 2026-10-17 19:57:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295416/Baqaei-Disruption-in-operation-of-Iran-Pakistan-border-crossings

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei announced a temporary suspension of cross-border movement between Iran and Pakistan due to technical issues, saying that the two countries are in consultations to resolve the problem and restore normal operations at the border crossings.
