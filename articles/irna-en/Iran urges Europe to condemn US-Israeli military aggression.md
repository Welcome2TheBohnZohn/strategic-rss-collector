# Iran urges Europe to condemn US-Israeli military aggression

Published: 2026-10-22 06:34:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300077/Iran-urges-Europe-to-condemn-US-Israeli-military-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has urged European countries to condemn the military aggression against Iran, stressing that Tehran will firmly defend its national sovereignty and territorial integrity against any threat or attack.
