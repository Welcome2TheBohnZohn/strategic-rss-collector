# In photos: Students renew pledge to martyred Leader

Published: 2026-11-03 10:43:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86311204/In-photos-Students-renew-pledge-to-martyred-Leader

The following images show a group of students gathered at the site of the martyrdom of Iran’s martyred Islamic Revolution Leader, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, on November 2, 2026, marking the anniversary of their meeting with him. The gathering was attended by Education Minister Alireza Kazemi, along with senior officials.
