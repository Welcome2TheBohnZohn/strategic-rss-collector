# Minab school bombing targeted foundations of morality, humanity: Judiciary spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-22 11:28:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271337/Minab-school-bombing-targeted-foundations-of-morality-humanity

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – Judiciary spokesperson Ali Kazemi has condemned the US-Israeli bombing of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab, southern Iran, describing it as more than a military crime and saying it targeted the foundations of morality and humanity in the international community.
