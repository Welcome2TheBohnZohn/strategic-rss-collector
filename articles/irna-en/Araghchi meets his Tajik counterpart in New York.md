# Araghchi meets his Tajik counterpart in New York

Published: 2026-09-23 18:39:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272933/Araghchi-meets-his-Tajik-counterpart-in-New-York

New York, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met and held talks with his Tajik counterpart, Sirojiddin Muhriddin, on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.
