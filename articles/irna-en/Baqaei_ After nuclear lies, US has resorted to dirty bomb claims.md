# Baqaei: After nuclear lies, US has resorted to dirty bomb claims

Published: 2026-11-23 10:47:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86330251/Baqaei-After-nuclear-lies-US-has-resorted-to-dirty-bomb-claims

Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei, referring to US claims that Iran might use a “dirty bomb,” has said that the allegations were an attempt to evade accountability before public opinion for launching an illegal and unjust war against Iran.
