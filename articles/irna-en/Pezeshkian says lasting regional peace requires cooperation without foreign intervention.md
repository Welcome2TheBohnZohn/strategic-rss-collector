# Pezeshkian says lasting regional peace requires cooperation without foreign intervention

Tehran, IRNA – Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says lasting peace and security in the region could only be achieved with cooperation and solidarity among regional countries without interference by extra-regional powers.
