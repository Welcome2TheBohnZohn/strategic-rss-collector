# Fourth intruding American MQ-1 drone shot down over Hormuz: IRGC

Published: 2026-09-15 09:35:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264716/Fourth-intruding-American-MQ-1-drone-shot-down-over-Hormuz-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has shot down one more advanced MQ-1 drone over the Strait of Hormuz, bringing the number of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) of the US enemy intercepted this week to four.
