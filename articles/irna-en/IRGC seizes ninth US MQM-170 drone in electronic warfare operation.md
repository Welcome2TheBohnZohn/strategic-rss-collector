# IRGC seizes ninth US MQM-170 drone in electronic warfare operation

Published: 2026-11-17 07:04:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324097/IRGC-seizes-ninth-US-MQM-170-drone-in-electronic-warfare-operation

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has seized another US MQM-170 drone, bringing the number of such aircraft captured by Iranian forces to nine.
