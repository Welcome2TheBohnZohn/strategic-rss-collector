# Ghalibaf: US ‘arsenal’ of interventions, Treasury falsehoods will fail to curb oil prices

Published: 2026-09-10 19:46:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260658/Ghalibaf-US-arsenal-of-interventions-Treasury-falsehoods

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Qalibaf has mocked Washington’s efforts to curb rising oil prices, and said the US measures, including market interventions and the release of strategic reserves, have failed to stop the surge in crude prices.
