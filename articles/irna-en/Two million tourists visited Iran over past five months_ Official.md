# Two million tourists visited Iran over past five months: Official

Published: 2026-09-09 09:37:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259444/Two-million-tourists-visited-Iran-over-past-five-months-Official

Qom, IRNA — Two million foreign tourists have visited Iran between March and August 2026, says Deputy Minister of Cultural Heritage, Tourism, and Handicrafts Anoushiravan Mohseni‑Bandpei.
