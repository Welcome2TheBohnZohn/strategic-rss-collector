# Repercussions of US-Israeli war extend beyond Iranian borders: IRGC spokesperson

Published: 2026-10-24 14:33:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302057/Repercussions-of-US-Israeli-war-extend-beyond-Iranian-borders

Qazvin, IRNA – Spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Brigadier General Hossein Mohebi, referring to the fallout of the US-Israeli war of aggression, says the confrontation could have repercussions extending beyond Iran’s borders, affecting other nations as well.
