# Iran ready to counter any potential threat: Air Defense Force commander

Published: 2026-09-01 13:34:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252329/Iran-ready-to-counter-any-potential-threat-Air-Defense-Force

Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Iranian Army’s Air Defense Force emphasized that the country is fully prepared to counter any potential threat, and that the country's defense systems and requirements are continuously being updated and upgraded.
