# Bahrain’s crackdown on clerics will ‘deepen gap between state and society’: Velayati

Published: 2026-11-20 12:13:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327494/Bahrain-s-crackdown-on-clerics-will-deepen-gap-between-state

Tehran, IRNA – Ali-Akbar Velayati, a senior adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, criticized the Bahraini authorities over their pressure on clerics and the crackdown on civil demands, emphasizing that such behavior “will deepen the gap between the state and society.”
