# Iran reiterates full sovereignty over three Persian Gulf islands, rejects UAE’s outlandish claims

Published: 2026-09-29 07:22:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277992/Iran-reiterates-full-sovereignty-over-three-Persian-Gulf-islands

New York, IRNA – A counsellor at Iran’s Permanent Mission to the United Nations has harshly reacted to the UAE representative’s claims, emphasizing that the Islamic Republic’s sovereignty over the three islands in the Persian Gulf is non-negotiable.
