# Iran stresses international transit facilitation at Geneva transport meeting

Published: 2026-09-23 14:20:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272716/Iran-stresses-international-transit-facilitation-at-Geneva-transport

London, IRNA – Iran has stressed the need to harmonize border and customs procedures to reduce the time and cost of international transit at a United Nations transport meeting in Geneva.
