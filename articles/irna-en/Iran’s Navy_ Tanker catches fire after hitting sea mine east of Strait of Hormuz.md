# Iran’s Navy: Tanker catches fire after hitting sea mine east of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-07 11:39:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314896/Iran-s-Navy-Tanker-catches-fire-after-hitting-sea-mine-east

Tehran, IRNA – A tanker has caught fire after hitting a sea mine, east of the Strait of Hormuz while sailing through a prohibited and unsafe zone, the Iranian Army's Navy says.
