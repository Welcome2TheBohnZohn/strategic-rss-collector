# Iran secures world title, 8 medals at International Earth Science Olympiad

Published: 2026-08-27 15:33:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247966/Iran-secures-world-title-8-medals-at-International-Earth-Science

London, IRNA — The Iranian national team had delivered a great performance at the 19th International Earth Science Olympiad (IESO) 2026 in, Turin, Italy, with two silver and six bronze medals.
