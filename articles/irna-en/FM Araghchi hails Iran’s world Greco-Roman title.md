# FM Araghchi hails Iran’s world Greco-Roman title

Published: 2026-10-27 13:25:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305122/FM-Araghchi-hails-Iran-s-world-Greco-Roman-title

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has congratulated the national Greco-Roman wrestling team on winning the world championship, wishing them “conquering higher peaks of glory.”
