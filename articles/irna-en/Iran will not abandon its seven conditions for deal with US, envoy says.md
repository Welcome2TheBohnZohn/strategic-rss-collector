# Iran will not abandon its seven conditions for deal with US, envoy says

Published: 2026-10-31 16:27:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308576/Iran-will-not-abandon-its-seven-conditions-for-deal-with-US

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will not back down from its seven conditions for an agreement with the United States, including an end to all wars in the region, its ambassador to Egypt, Mojtaba Ferdowsipour, said on Saturday, stressing that Tehran has no trust in Washington.
