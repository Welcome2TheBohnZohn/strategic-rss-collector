# IRGC Navy chief praises 200 nights of nationwide demonstrations

Published: 2026-09-15 20:04:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265251/IRGC-Navy-chief-praises-200-nights-of-nationwide-demonstrations

Tehran, IRNA – The commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has praised the Iranian public for 200 consecutive nights of nationwide demonstrations in support of the armed forces.
