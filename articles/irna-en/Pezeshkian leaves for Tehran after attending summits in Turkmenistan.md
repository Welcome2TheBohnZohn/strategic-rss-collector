# Pezeshkian leaves for Tehran after attending summits in Turkmenistan

Published: 2026-10-09 15:02:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287726/Pezeshkian-leaves-for-Tehran-after-attending-summits-in-Turkmenistan

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has left Turkmenistan for Tehran after attending and delivering speeches at two summits that had brought several regional heads of state and representatives together to deliberate on issues of common interest.
