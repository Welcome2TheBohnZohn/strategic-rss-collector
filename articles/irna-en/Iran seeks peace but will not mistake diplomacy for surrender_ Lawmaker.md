# Iran seeks peace but will not mistake diplomacy for surrender: Lawmaker

Published: 2026-10-27 09:42:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304849/Iran-seeks-peace-but-will-not-mistake-diplomacy-for-surrender

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is committed to peace and stability but will not equate diplomacy with surrender or abandon its legitimate rights, Ahmad Naderi, head of Iran’s parliamentary delegation to the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum, said in New Delhi on Tuesday.
