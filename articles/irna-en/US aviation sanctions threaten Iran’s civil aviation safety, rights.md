# US aviation sanctions threaten Iran’s civil aviation safety, rights

Published: 2026-09-30 07:09:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279005/US-aviation-sanctions-threaten-Iran-s-civil-aviation-safety

Tehran, IRNA – US sanctions on Iran’s aviation sector have raised concerns over flight safety and the rights of people who rely on air travel for family visits, medical treatment, education and other essential needs, and should not pay the price for Washington’s bullying.
