# Araghchi meets senior Algerian diplomat in Baghdad

Published: 2026-10-17 16:19:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295309/Araghchi-meets-senior-Algerian-diplomat-in-Baghdad

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has held talks with the Secretary of State to Algeria's Minister of Foreign Affairs and in-charge of the National Community Abroad, in the Iraqi capital, Baghdad.
