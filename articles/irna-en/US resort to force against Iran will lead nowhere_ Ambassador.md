# US resort to force against Iran will lead nowhere: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-16 15:14:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294284/US-resort-to-force-against-Iran-will-lead-nowhere-Ambassador

Moscow, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador to Russia has stressed that the US resort to force against Iran will yield no results and will only further complicate the situation in the region.
