# Baghdad hosts 52nd OIC foreign ministers’ meeting with Iran FM in attendance

Published: 2026-10-17 09:36:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294931/Baghdad-hosts-52nd-OIC-foreign-ministers-meeting-with-Iran-FM

Tehran, IRNA – The 52nd session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) opened in Baghdad on Saturday, with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in attendance.
