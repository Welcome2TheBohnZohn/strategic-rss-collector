# Iran condemns Colombia’s move to open embassy in occupied al-Quds

Published: 2026-10-16 07:55:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293949/Iran-condemns-Colombia-s-action-to-open-embassy-in-occupied-al-Quds

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Foreign Ministry has strongly condemned Colombia’s action to open an embassy in occupied al-Quds as contrary to the UN Charter and international law.
