# Araghchi: US response to Iran’s proposals to be conveyed through Qatar

Published: 2026-09-29 05:46:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277742/Araghchi-US-response-to-Iran-s-proposals-to-be-conveyed-through

New York, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Tehran has held another round of discussions with Qatari mediators on ideas that will be presented to the United States, with Washington’s final response to be conveyed to Iran through the mediators.
