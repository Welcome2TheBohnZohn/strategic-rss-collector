# Defense Minister: Iran’s combat power to be boosted for unrestricted response anywhere

Published: 2026-11-01 12:47:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309273/Defense-Minister-Iran-s-combat-power-to-be-boosted-for-unrestricted

Tehran, IRNA - Defense Minister Mehrdad Akhlaghi has vowed to resolutely pursue strengthening smart combat readiness and to fulfill the Leader’s strategy of a swift, effective, and proportionate response to any threat, anywhere, without geographic limitations.
