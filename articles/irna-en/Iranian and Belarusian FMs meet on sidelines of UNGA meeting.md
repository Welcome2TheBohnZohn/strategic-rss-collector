# Iranian and Belarusian FMs meet on sidelines of UNGA meeting

Published: 2026-09-22 17:01:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271642/Iranian-and-Belarusian-FMs-meet-on-sidelines-of-UNGA-meeting

New York, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi and Belarusian Foreign Minister Maksim Ryzhenkov met and held talks on the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly meeting in New York.
