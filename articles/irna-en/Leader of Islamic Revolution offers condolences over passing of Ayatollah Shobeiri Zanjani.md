# Leader of Islamic Revolution offers condolences over passing of Ayatollah Shobeiri Zanjani

Published: 2026-09-22 07:09:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270878/Leader-of-Islamic-Revolution-offers-condolences-over-passing

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei has issued a message of condolence over the passing of prominent Shia cleric and source of emulation Ayatollah Seyyed Mousa Shobeiri Zanjani.
