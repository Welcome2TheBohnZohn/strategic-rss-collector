# Araghchi marks Nasrallah’s martyrdom anniversary, says killing leaders cannot ease ideals

Published: 2026-09-27 19:03:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276533/Araghchi-marks-Nasrallah-s-martyrdom-anniversary-says-killing

Tehran, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has marked the second anniversary of the martyrdom of Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, the late secretary-general of Lebanon’s Hezbollah, saying his legacy of resistance and dignity will endure.
