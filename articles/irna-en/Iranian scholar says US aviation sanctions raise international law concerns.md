# Iranian scholar says US aviation sanctions raise international law concerns

Published: 2026-09-29 08:37:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278074/Iranian-scholar-says-US-aviation-sanctions-raise-international

Tehran, IRNA — An Iranian academic has said that secondary sanctions targeting Iran’s aviation sector could violate fundamental principles of international law and undermine countries’ obligations under international agreements.
