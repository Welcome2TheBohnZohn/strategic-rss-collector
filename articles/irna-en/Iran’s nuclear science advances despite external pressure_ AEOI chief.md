# Iran’s nuclear science advances despite external pressure: AEOI chief

Published: 2026-11-26 10:32:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333250/Iran-s-nuclear-science-advances-despite-external-pressure-AEOI

Rasht, IRNA – Head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran Mohammad Eslami says the country’s scientific progress has drawn opposition from its adversaries, stressing that the Islamic Republic will continue developing its technological capabilities without seeking permission from foreign powers.
