# Pakistan’s FM and Iranian ambassador discuss expansion of ties and regional developments

Published: 2026-11-04 20:19:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312930/Pakistan-s-FM-and-Iranian-ambassador-discuss-expansion-of-ties

Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistan’s deputy prime minister and minister of foreign affairs, in a meeting with the ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran in Islamabad, exchanged views on the latest regional developments and the expansion of bilateral relations.
