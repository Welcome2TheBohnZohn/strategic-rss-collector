# Iran’s cultural, civilizational heritage strengthened national unity during war: Baqaei

Published: 2026-11-23 13:56:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330390/Iran-s-cultural-civilizational-heritage-strengthened-national

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says Iran’s civilizational, cultural and religious heritage played a key role in strengthening national unity and resilience during the 40-day war with the United States and the Israeli regime.
