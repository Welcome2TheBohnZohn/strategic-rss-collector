# Iranian embassy rejects speculation linking Iran to RAF Fairford arrests

Published: 2026-09-28 14:15:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277399/Iranian-embassy-rejects-speculation-linking-Iran-to-RAF-Fairford

London, IRNA – The Iranian Embassy in London has strongly rejected speculation by some British individuals and media outlets seeking to link Iran to the arrest of five suspects near the US-used RAF Fairford airbase.
