# Baqaei equates Rubio's ‘Men with Swords’ remark with Hitler, Daesh terrorists

Published: 2026-11-17 06:57:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324153/Baqaei-equates-Rubio-s-Men-with-Swords-remark-with-Hitler

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has censured US Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s recent remarks directed at the International Criminal Court, proclaiming “Stop quoting laws to men with swords” as America is the sword.
