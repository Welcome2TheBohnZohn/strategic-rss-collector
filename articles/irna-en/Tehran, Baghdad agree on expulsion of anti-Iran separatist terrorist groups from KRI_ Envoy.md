# Tehran, Baghdad agree on expulsion of anti-Iran separatist terrorist groups from KRI: Envoy

Published: 2026-10-20 10:44:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298169/Tehran-Baghdad-agree-on-expulsion-of-anti-Iran-separatist-terrorist

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Iraq says Tehran and Baghdad have agreed on the expulsion of anti-Iran separatist terrorist elements from the Kurdistan Region of Iraq or handing them over to Iran.
