# VP: World must not forget Minab incident

Published: 2026-10-11 09:24:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289337/VP-World-must-not-forget-Minab-incident

Tehran, IRNA – Vice President for Women and Family Affairs Zahra Behrouz-Azar says that just as the global community has preserved the memory of Hiroshima, the US attack on Minab must neither be forgotten nor obscured by distorted accounts and revised terminology.
