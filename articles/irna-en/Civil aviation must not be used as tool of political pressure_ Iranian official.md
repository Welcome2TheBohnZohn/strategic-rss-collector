# Civil aviation must not be used as tool of political pressure: Iranian official

Published: 2026-09-12 09:52:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261624/Civil-aviation-must-not-be-used-as-tool-of-political-pressure

Tehran, IRNA — The head of Iran’s Civil Aviation Organization has condemned the United States for imposing broad and unilateral sanctions on the country’s civil aviation sector, warning that the continuation of such measures could harm regional and international aviation.
