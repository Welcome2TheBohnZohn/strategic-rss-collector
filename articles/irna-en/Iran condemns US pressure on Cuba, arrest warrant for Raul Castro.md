# Iran condemns US pressure on Cuba, arrest warrant for Raul Castro

Published: 2026-10-21 05:45:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298906/Iran-condemns-US-pressure-on-Cuba-arrest-warrant-for-Raul-Castro

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Foreign Ministry has condemned US interventionist actions against Cuba, including sanctions and efforts to intimidate Cuban officials, describing an arrest warrant for former Cuban President Raul Castro as illegal and criminal.
