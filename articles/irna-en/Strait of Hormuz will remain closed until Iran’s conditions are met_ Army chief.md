# Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until Iran’s conditions are met: Army chief

Published: 2026-11-24 09:53:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331202/Strait-of-Hormuz-will-remain-closed-until-Iran-s-conditions-are

Tehran, IRNA — Chief Commander of the Islamic Republic of Iran, Army Major General Amir Hatami, has reiterated Iran’s resolve to safeguard national interests, saying the acceptance of seven conditions put forward to the United States is the only way to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
