# Iran ready to help resolve Saudi-Yemen issues: Baqaei

Published: 2026-10-17 08:10:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294651/Iran-ready-to-help-resolve-Saudi-Yemen-issues-Baqaei

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says Iran is well-prepared to help resolve issues between Saudi Arabia and Yemen through peaceful means, stressing that the Islamic Republic remains steadfast in its principled position on the Yemen issue.
