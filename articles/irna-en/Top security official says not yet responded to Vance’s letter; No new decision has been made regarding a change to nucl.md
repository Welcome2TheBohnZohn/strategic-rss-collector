# Top security official says not yet responded to Vance’s letter; No new decision has been made regarding a change to nuclear doctrine

Published: 2026-10-31 20:28:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308655/Top-security-official-says-not-yet-responded-to-Vance-s-letter

Tehran, IRNA — Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei has confirmed that he has not responded to a letter by US Vice President JD Vance yet, and there is no change in nuclear doctrine of the Islamic Republic of Iran.
