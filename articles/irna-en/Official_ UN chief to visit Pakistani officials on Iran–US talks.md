# Official: UN chief to visit Pakistani officials on Iran–US talks

Published: 2026-10-06 06:16:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284523/Official-UN-chief-to-visit-Pakistani-officials-on-Iran-US-talks

New York, IRNA — Deputy Spokesman for the UN Secretary-General, Farhan Haq, has confirmed that Secretary-General António Guterres is currently in Pakistan, where he is expected to discuss the ongoing diplomatic efforts and mediation between Tehran and Washington with Pakistani officials.
