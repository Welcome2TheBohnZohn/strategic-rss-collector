# Leader tells Iranian youth: We are bound by a covenant to steer Iran to ‘summits of progress’

Published: 2026-10-28 15:54:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306315/Leader-tells-Iranian-youth-We-are-bound-by-a-covenant-to-steer

Tehran, IRNA –Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has described the determination, resolve, and sense of responsibility toward building a bright future as the connecting thread of a covenant between the leadership and the Iranian youth.
