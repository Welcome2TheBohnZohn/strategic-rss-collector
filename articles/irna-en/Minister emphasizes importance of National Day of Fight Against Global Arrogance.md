# Minister emphasizes importance of National Day of Fight Against Global Arrogance

Published: 2026-10-26 08:57:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303731/Minister-emphasizes-importance-of-Fighting-Against-Global-Arrogance

Tehran, IRNA – Minister of Education Alireza Kazemi says the ongoing circumstances in the face of the continued hostile enemy hostilities have increased the Iranian nation’s responsibilities during this year’s commemorations of the 1979 US Embassy takeover, known as the National Day of the Fight against Global Arrogance.
