# Official urges seminary students, clerics to make efforts to reinforce culture of resistance

Published: 2026-10-13 09:46:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291512/Official-urges-seminary-students-clerics-to-make-efforts-to

Qom, IRNA — Head of Iran’s seminaries Ayatollah Alireza A’rafi asked students and clerics across the country to use the upcoming opportunities to strengthen the culture of martyrdom and resistance in society.
