# Iran summons rotating president of European Union

Published: 2026-10-19 17:59:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297566/Iran-summons-rotating-president-of-European-Union

Tehran, IRNA – In response to the inclusion of baseless allegations against Iran in the statement issued at the European Union summit, Aidan Cronin, the Ambassador of Ireland—which currently holds the rotating presidency of the EU—was summoned to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs by Alireza Yousefi, Assistant to the Foreign Minister and Director-General for Western Europe.
