# Leader orders swift, decisive response to any new aggression, with no geographical limits

Published: 2026-10-31 10:54:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308323/Leader-orders-swift-decisive-response-to-any-new-aggression

Tehran, IRNA — Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has ordered senior Iranian military commanders to respond swiftly and forcefully to any new aggression against the country, without geographical restrictions.
