# Pro-Palestinian protesters rally in Liverpool ahead of Labour Party conference

Published: 2026-09-26 14:59:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275294/Pro-Palestinian-protesters-rally-in-Liverpool-ahead-of-Labour

London, IRNA – Pro-Palestinian supporters rallied in Liverpool on Saturday ahead of the annual conference of Britain’s ruling Labour Party, calling on the government to end military support for Israel and halt arms sales to the regime.
