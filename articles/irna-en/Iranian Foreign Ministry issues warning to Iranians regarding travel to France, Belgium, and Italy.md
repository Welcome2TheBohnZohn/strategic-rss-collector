# Iranian Foreign Ministry issues warning to Iranians regarding travel to France, Belgium, and Italy

Published: 2026-10-19 20:14:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297620/Iranian-Foreign-Ministry-issues-warning-to-Iranians-regarding

Tehran, IRNA – In light of intensifying unrest and disruptions to public order in France, Belgium, and Italy, the Public Relations Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran advises Iranian citizens to refrain from making non-essential trips to these countries or being present in areas affected by the unrest.
