# Araghchi: Development must not be held hostage to coercion, discrimination

Published: 2026-09-26 08:05:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274695/Araghchi-Development-must-not-be-held-hostage-to-coercion-discrimination

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says development must never be held hostage to coercion, discrimination, political agendas, or unilateralism, stressing that sustainable development is impossible without peace and respect for international law.
