# Iran ready to join three initiatives at COP17 on biodiversity conservation

Published: 2026-10-31 10:12:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308168/Iran-ready-to-join-three-initiatives-at-COP17-on-biodiversity

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has expressed readiness to join three initiatives launched at the proposal of the presidency of the 17th Conference of the Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD COP 17), which focus on biodiversity monitoring, mountain and freshwater ecosystems, and areas supporting conservation.
