# Minab atrocity must remain lasting symbol of US brutality and Iranian resilience: Baqaei

Published: 2026-11-10 14:23:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318151/Minab-atrocity-must-remain-lasting-symbol-of-US-brutality-and

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has stressed the need for a comprehensive legal and international follow-up on the US atrocity in Minab, saying the incident must become a lasting symbol of the aggressors’ brutality and the Iranian nation’s resilience in historical memory and global public opinion.
