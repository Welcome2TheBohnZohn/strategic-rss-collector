# Yemen says it downed Saudi Typhoon fighter

Published: 2026-10-15 08:03:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293303/Yemen-says-it-downed-Saudi-Typhoon-fighter

Tehran, IRNA – Yemen’s armed forces have downed a Saudi Typhoon fighter jet over the northern province of Sa’ada, military spokesperson Yahya Saree has said, as Sana’a reported a sharp escalation in Saudi-led coalition attacks.
