# Leader’s adviser praises nurses’ role during imposed wars

Published: 2026-10-15 10:29:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293522/Leader-s-adviser-praises-nurses-role-during-imposed-wars

Tehran, IRNA – Ali Akbar Velayati, a senior adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, has congratulated nurses on Nurses’ Day, praising their historic contributions and dedication, particularly during the imposed wars.
