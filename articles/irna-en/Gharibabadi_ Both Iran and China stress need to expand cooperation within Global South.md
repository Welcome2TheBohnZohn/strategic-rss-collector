# Gharibabadi: Both Iran and China stress need to expand cooperation within Global South

Published: 2026-10-21 20:22:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299906/Gharibabadi-Both-Iran-and-China-stress-need-to-expand-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Kazem Gharibabadi, Deputy for Legal and International Affairs at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, emphasized in a message that both Iran and China underscore the need for expanding cooperation within the Global South.
