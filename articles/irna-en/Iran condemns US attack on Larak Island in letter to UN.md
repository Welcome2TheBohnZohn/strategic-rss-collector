# Iran condemns US attack on Larak Island in letter to UN

Published: 2026-09-01 10:08:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251637/Iran-condemns-US-attack-on-Larak-Island-in-letter-to-UN

New York, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador and deputy permanent representative to the United Nations, Gholamhossein Darzi, has strongly condemned the latest US aggression against Larak Island in a letter to the UN secretary-general and Security Council president.
