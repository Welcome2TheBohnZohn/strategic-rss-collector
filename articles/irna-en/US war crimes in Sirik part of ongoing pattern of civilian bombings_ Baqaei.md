# US war crimes in Sirik part of ongoing pattern of civilian bombings: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-02 15:32:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253587/US-war-crimes-in-Sirik-part-of-ongoing-pattern-of-civilian-bombings

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has deplored the US Central Command (CENTCOM) for denying American bombing of civilians on Sirik Island, saying that even the US propaganda machine has failed to refute this horrifying reality of its military.
