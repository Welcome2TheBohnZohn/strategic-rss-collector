# IRGC advisor warns of decisive response to any hostile action against Iran

Published: 2026-10-18 16:44:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296369/IRGC-advisor-warns-of-decisive-response-to-any-hostile-action

Sari, IRNA – A senior advisor to the commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has warned that any hostile action against Iran would be met with a decisive response, saying the country’s adversaries are now forced to respect the Iranian nation despite their military capabilities.
