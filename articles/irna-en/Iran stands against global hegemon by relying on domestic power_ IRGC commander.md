# Iran stands against global hegemon by relying on domestic power: IRGC commander

Published: 2026-08-29 13:31:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249546/Iran-stands-against-global-hegemon-by-relying-on-domestic-power

Isfahan, IRNA – A senior advisor to the commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says Iran has successfully resisted the joint American-Zionist war of aggression despite having only a fraction of the defense budgets of its enemies.
