# Akhlaghi becomes Iran’s new defense minister

Published: 2026-10-18 08:44:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295908/Akhlaghi-becomes-Iran-s-new-defense-minister

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s parliament has approved Mehrdad Akhlaghi Ketabchi as the country’s new minister of defense and armed forces logistics, endorsing his appointment by President Masoud Pezeshkian.
