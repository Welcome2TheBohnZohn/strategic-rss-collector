# China firmly opposes illegal unilateral US sanctions against Iran

Published: 2026-09-23 13:31:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272674/China-firmly-opposes-illegal-unilateral-US-sanctions-against

Tehran, IRNA – Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun has emphasized that China firmly opposes illegal unilateral sanctions against Iran, amid US threats to sanction countries allowing Iranian commercial flights to land.
