# Iranian and Brazilian FMs call for continued diplomatic efforts to reduce regional tensions

Published: 2026-10-19 20:27:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297626/Iranian-and-Brazilian-FMs-call-for-continued-diplomatic-efforts

Tehran, IRNA – The Foreign Ministers of the Islamic Republic of Iran and the Federative Republic of Brazil have emphasized the importance of continuing diplomatic consultations and political efforts aimed at reducing tensions and establishing lasting stability and security in the region.
