# Security synergy main focus of SCO anti-terrorism drill: Top IRGC cmdr.

Published: 2026-10-07 11:15:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286059/Security-synergy-main-focus-of-SCO-anti-terrorism-drill-Top

Tehran, IRNA – The deputy commander for operations of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Forces says the main focus of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization’s (SCO) joint anti-terrorism exercise is security synergy among member states and confronting extremist activities and violent groups.
