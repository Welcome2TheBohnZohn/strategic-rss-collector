# Rubio’s remarks on ancient Iran and Greece raise concerns over political use of history: Diplomat

Published: 2026-10-12 10:18:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290352/Rubio-s-remarks-on-ancient-Iran-and-Greece-raise-concerns-over

Tehran, IRNA – An Iranian Foreign Ministry official has warned that US Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s remarks on the ancient wars between Iran and Greece risk using history to advance a political narrative of civilizational confrontation, raising concerns over an attempt to turn Iran’s historical legacy into an instrument of contemporary rivalry.
