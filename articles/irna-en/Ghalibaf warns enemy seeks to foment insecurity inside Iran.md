# Ghalibaf warns enemy seeks to foment insecurity inside Iran

Published: 2026-10-07 13:34:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286163/Ghalibaf-warns-enemy-seeks-to-foment-insecurity-inside-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has warned that the enemy has shifted its focus toward fomenting insecurity and violent acts inside Iran after failing to harm the country through military action.
