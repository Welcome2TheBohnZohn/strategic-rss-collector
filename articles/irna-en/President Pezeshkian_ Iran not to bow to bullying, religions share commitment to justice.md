# President Pezeshkian: Iran not to bow to bullying, religions share commitment to justice

Published: 2026-09-11 19:24:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261189/President-Pezeshkian-Iran-not-to-bow-to-bullying-religions

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has said that all religions are rooted in justice and opposition to oppression, stressing that Iran will not bow to bullying or surrender its independence.
