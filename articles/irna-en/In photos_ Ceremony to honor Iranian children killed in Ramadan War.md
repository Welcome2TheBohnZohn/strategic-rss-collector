# In photos: Ceremony to honor Iranian children killed in Ramadan War

Published: 2026-11-23 06:13:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86329793/In-photos-Ceremony-to-honor-Iranian-children-killed-in-Ramadan

Government officials and families of child and teenage victims of the Ramadan War attend a ceremony marking Children's Day at the Martyrs and Veterans Affairs Foundation in Tehran on Sunday, November 22, 2026. The event was attended by Martyr’s Foundation President Ahmad Mousavi, administration spokesperson Fatemeh Mohajerani, Deputy to the President for Women and Family Affairs Zahra Behrouz-Azar, and Deputy to the President for Legal Affairs Hojjatoleslam Majid Ansari.
