# Pezeshkian to deliver Iran’s wartime message at UN General Assembly

Published: 2026-09-23 12:48:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272532/Pezeshkian-to-deliver-Iran-s-wartime-message-at-UN-General-Assembly

New York, IRNA –President Masoud Pezeshkian is set to address this year's United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) at a time when Iran continues to face a war of aggression launched by the United States and Israel seven months ago, a conflict whose wider regional ramifications have made it a major focus of international attention.
