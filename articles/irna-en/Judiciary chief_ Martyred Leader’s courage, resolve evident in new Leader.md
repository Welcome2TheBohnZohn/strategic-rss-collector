Tehran, IRNA — Judiciary Chief Gholamhossein Mohseni Eje’i says the courage and firmness of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution are fully evident in the current Leader, Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei.
