# Pezeshkian: Iran–India cooperation serves their interests, regional peace

Published: 2026-10-07 08:57:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285892/Pezeshkian-Iran-India-cooperation-serves-their-interests-regional

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says that expanding cooperation between Iran and India would not only serve the interests of the two countries but also contribute to regional peace and security.
