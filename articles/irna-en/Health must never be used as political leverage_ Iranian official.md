# Health must never be used as political leverage: Iranian official

Published: 2026-10-15 09:34:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293429/Health-must-never-be-used-as-political-leverage-Iranian-official

Tehran, IRNA – Ali Jafarian, a senior advisor to Iran’s health minister, has called for health to be kept separate from political disputes, stressing that it serves as a bridge for peace and stability and is part of the shared future of all nations.
