# Iran says US is angry because it failed to force Tehran to surrender

Published: 2026-08-26 18:56:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247142/Iran-says-US-is-angry-because-it-failed-to-force-Tehran-to-surrender

Rasht, IRNA – The United States is angry because it has failed to force Iran to surrender, Vice President for Executive Affairs Mohammad Jafar Ghaempanah said on Wednesday, noting that Tehran’s resistance had prompted Washington to shift from threats to calls for negotiations.
