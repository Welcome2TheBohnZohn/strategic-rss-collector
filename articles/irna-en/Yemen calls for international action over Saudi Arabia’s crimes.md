# Yemen calls for international action over Saudi Arabia’s crimes

Published: 2026-09-19 15:18:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268267/Yemen-calls-for-international-action-over-Saudi-Arabia-s-crimes

Tehran, IRNA – Yemen’s Foreign Ministry called on the international community to fulfill its responsibilities in response to “criminal” actions by Saudi Arabia in Yemen, al-Masirah television reported on Saturday.
