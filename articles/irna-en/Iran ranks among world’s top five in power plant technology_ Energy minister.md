# Iran ranks among world’s top five in power plant technology: Energy minister

Published: 2026-08-27 10:21:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247694/Iran-ranks-among-world-s-top-five-in-power-plant-technology

Bojnurd, IRNA – Iran ranks among the world’s top five countries in power plant technology, with a significant share of domestically manufactured power equipment exported to international markets, Energy Minister Abbas Aliabadi has said.
