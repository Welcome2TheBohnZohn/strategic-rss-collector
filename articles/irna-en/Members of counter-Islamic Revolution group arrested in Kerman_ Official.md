# Members of counter-Islamic Revolution group arrested in Kerman: Official

Published: 2026-10-15 07:58:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293332/Members-of-organized-counter-revolutionary-team-arrested-in-Kerman

Kerman, IRNA – The prosecutor of Kerman Province in southern Iran says members of an organized team linked to counter-Islamic Revolution groups have been arrested by the Intelligence Unit of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC).
