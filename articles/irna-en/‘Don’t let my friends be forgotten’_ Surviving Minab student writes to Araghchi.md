# ‘Don’t let my friends be forgotten’: Surviving Minab student writes to Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-11 17:43:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289886/Don-t-let-my-friends-be-forgotten-Surviving-Minab-student

Tehran, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Sunday he has received a letter from a surviving student of the Shajareh Tayyebah School in Minab, which came under a deadly US missile attack late in February, and published its text in an Instagram message.
