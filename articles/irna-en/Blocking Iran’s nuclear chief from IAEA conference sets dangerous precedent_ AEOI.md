# Blocking Iran’s nuclear chief from IAEA conference sets dangerous precedent: AEOI

Published: 2026-09-14 16:04:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264082/Blocking-Iran-s-nuclear-chief-from-IAEA-conference-sets-dangerous

London, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI), Behrouz Kamalvandi, has described preventing AEOI chief Mohammad Eslami from attending the IAEA General Conference as a dangerous precedent for all countries, stressing that the agency and its member states must stand up to US bullying and political pressure.
