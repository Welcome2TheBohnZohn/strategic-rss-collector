# Enemy suffered heavy blows in war against Iran: Army chief

Published: 2026-09-27 09:43:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276031/Enemy-suffered-heavy-blows-in-war-against-Iran-Army-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Chief Commander of Iran’s Army Major General Abdolrahim Mousavi says the war has entered a sensitive and decisive phase, stressing that despite Iran’s victory and the heavy blows inflicted on the aggressor, the country must remain prepared for new aggression.
