# Iran’s road minister proposes new transit routes, infrastructure cooperation with Kazakhstan

Published: 2026-10-17 07:30:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294687/Iran-s-road-minister-proposes-new-transit-routes-infrastructure

Astana, IRNA — Iranian Minister of Roads and Urban Development Farzaneh Sadegh has proposed expanding regional transit routes, strengthening railway connectivity and boosting infrastructure cooperation with Kazakhstan to facilitate trade and improve logistics links across the region.
