# Memorial service for Grand Ayatollah Vahid Khorasani organized by Leader

Published: 2026-11-19 18:40:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327064/Memorial-service-for-Grand-Ayatollah-Vahid-Khorasani-by-Leader

Tehran, IRNA — A memorial and commemoration ceremony for Grand Ayatollah Hossein Vahid Khorasani was held at the Razavi Holy Shrine on the first night following the burial of this esteemed religious authority, organized by Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei.
