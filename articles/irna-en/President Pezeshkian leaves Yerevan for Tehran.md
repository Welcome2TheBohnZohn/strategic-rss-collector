# President Pezeshkian leaves Yerevan for Tehran

Published: 2026-10-16 13:38:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294243/President-Pezeshkian-leaves-Yerevan-for-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has left Yerevan for Tehran after participating in the 17th meeting of the Conference of the Parties (COP 17) to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD).
