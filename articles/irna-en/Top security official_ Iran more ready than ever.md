# Top security official: Iran more ready than ever

Published: 2026-09-19 18:33:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268390/Top-security-official-Iran-more-ready-than-before

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei says that the Iranian Armed Forces are more prepared than before if the US intends to attack the Islamic Republic.
