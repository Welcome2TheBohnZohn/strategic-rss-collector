# Iranian envoy derides European concerns over economic fallout of US-Israeli aggression

Published: 2026-11-01 14:19:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309407/Iranian-envoy-derides-European-concerns-over-economic-fallout

London – Iran’s ambassador to Germany has criticized Western governments for remaining silent over the US-Israeli military aggression against Iran, saying they are now expressing concern about the economic consequences of the same aggression for their own countries.
