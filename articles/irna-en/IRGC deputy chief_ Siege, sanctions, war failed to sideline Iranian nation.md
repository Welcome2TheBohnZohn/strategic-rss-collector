# IRGC deputy chief: Siege, sanctions, war failed to sideline Iranian nation

Published: 2026-09-08 09:58:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258395/IRGC-deputy-chief-Siege-sanctions-war-failed-to-sideline-Iranian

Birjand, IRNA – Deputy Commander-in-Chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Major General Mostafa Izadi has said that the enemy sought to push the Iranian people out of the field through pressure, sanctions, economic hardship, and war, but the nation stood firm and remained on the scene.
