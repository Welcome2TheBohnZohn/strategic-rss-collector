# Severity of Iran Armed Forces’ actions will increase tenfold, if needed: Military spokesperson

Published: 2026-11-12 14:41:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320053/Severity-of-Iran-Armed-Forces-actions-will-increase-tenfold

Tehran, IRNA — Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi, a senior spokesperson for Iran’s Armed Forces, says the country’s level of deterrence has increased compared to the past, enabling it to decisively deal with any new aggression in response to anti-Iran threats and rhetoric from the enemy.
