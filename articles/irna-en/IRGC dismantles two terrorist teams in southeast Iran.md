# IRGC dismantles two terrorist teams in southeast Iran

Published: 2026-10-27 17:46:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305261/IRGC-dismantles-two-terrorist-teams-in-southeast-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has dismantled two terrorist teams and arrested their members in the southeastern cities of Saravan and Iranshahr, foiling planned attacks, the IRGC Ground Force’s Quds Headquarters said on Tuesday.
