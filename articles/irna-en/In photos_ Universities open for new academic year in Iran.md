# In photos: Universities open for new academic year in Iran

Published: 2026-10-12 09:01:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86290361/In-photos-Universities-open-for-new-academic-year-in-Iran

The opening ceremony for universities for the new academic year took place at Shahid Beheshti University in Tehran on October 12, 2026, attended by President Masoud Pezeshkian, Science Minister Hossein Simaei Sarraf, Health Minister Mohammad-Reza Zafarghandi, university presidents, and students.
