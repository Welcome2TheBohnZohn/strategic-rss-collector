# US strike in Kermanshah claims lives of four IRGC Aerospace forces

Published: 2026-09-02 08:47:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253070/US-strike-in-Kermanshah-claims-lives-of-four-IRGC-Aerospace-forces

Kermanshah, IRNA — Four IRGC aerospace personnel have been killed in Kermanshah during a US attack, according to a statement from the provincial IRGC public relations office.
