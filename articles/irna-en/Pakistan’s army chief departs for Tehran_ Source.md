# Pakistan’s army chief departs for Tehran: Source

Published: 2026-10-20 08:22:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298078/Pakistan-s-army-chief-departs-for-Tehran-Source

Tehran, IRNA — Chief of Army Staff of Pakistan, Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, has departed for Tehran to meet with high-ranking Iranian officials, an informed diplomatic source told IRNA.
