# Almond Festival in northwest Iran

Published: 2026-09-12 10:07:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86261767/Almond-Festival-in-northwest-Iran

The city of Khosrowshah, in Iran’s East Azarbaijan Province, hosted its second edition of Almon Festival on Wednesday evening (September 9). People and local officials from across the province, including the provincial capital, Tabriz, were in attendance.
