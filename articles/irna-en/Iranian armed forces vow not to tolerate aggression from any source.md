# Iranian armed forces vow not to tolerate aggression from any source

Published: 2026-08-31 19:43:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251441/Iranian-armed-forces-vow-not-to-tolerate-aggression-from-any

Tehran, IRNA – The General Staff of Iran’s Armed Forces and Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters have warned that the Islamic Republic will not tolerate aggression from any source.
