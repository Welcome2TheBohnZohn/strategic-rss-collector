# US sanctions will not stop Iran’s aviation industry: Official

Published: 2026-09-09 10:44:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259552/US-sanctions-will-not-stop-Iran-s-aviation-industry-Official

Tehran, IRNA — The head of Iran’s Civil Aviation Organization says new US sanctions against Iranian airlines are a continuation of a failed policy, stressing that they may raise costs but cannot halt the country’s flight operations.
