# Hamas terms Nasser Medical Complex bombardment as full-fledged war crime

Published: 2026-11-12 07:05:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319703/Hamas-terms-Nasser-Medical-Complex-bombardment-as-full-fledged

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Resistance Movement of Palestine (Hamas), condemning the bombing of the “Nasser Medical Complex” in Khan Younis, southern Gaza Strip, which resulted in the martyrdom of six Palestinian citizens and the injury of 20 others, held the Zionist regime and the Board of Peace responsible for this crime.
