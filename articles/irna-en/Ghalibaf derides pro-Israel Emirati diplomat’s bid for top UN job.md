# Ghalibaf derides pro-Israel Emirati diplomat’s bid for top UN job

Published: 2026-10-25 15:40:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303099/Ghalibaf-derides-pro-Israel-Emirati-diplomat-s-bid-for-top-UN

Tehran, IRNA – Iran's Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has ridiculed Lana Nusseibeh, a senior diplomat from the United Arab Emirates, for entering the race to become the next United Nations secretary-general.
