# Leader: Shortcomings of past population policies must be remedied

Published: 2026-11-24 08:17:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331070/Leader-Shortcomings-of-past-population-policies-must-be-remedied

Tehran, IRNA — Leader of the Islamic Revolution Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has stressed the key role of population growth in building a stronger Iran, emphasizing that shortcomings resulting from some past population policies must be addressed.
