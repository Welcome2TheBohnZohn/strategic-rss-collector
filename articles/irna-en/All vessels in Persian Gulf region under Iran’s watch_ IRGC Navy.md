# All vessels in Persian Gulf region under Iran’s watch: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-09-16 09:26:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265699/All-vessels-in-Persian-Gulf-region-under-Iran-s-watch-IRGC-Navy

Sari, IRNA – No vessel can move through the Persian Gulf, the Strait of Hormuz, or the Sea of Oman without being monitored by Iran, a senior official of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says, stressing that the force has the capability to strike any vessel if ordered.
