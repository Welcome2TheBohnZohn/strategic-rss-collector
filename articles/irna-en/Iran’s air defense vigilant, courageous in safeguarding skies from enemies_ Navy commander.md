# Iran’s air defense vigilant, courageous in safeguarding skies from enemies: Navy commander

Published: 2026-09-01 10:41:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251956/Iran-s-air-defense-vigilant-courageous-in-safeguarding-skies

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Navy Commander, Rear Admiral Shahram Irani, says the country’s Air Defense Force stood against the aggressive American-Zionist enemy and defended the skies with vigilance and courage, relying on domestic know-how and capabilities.
