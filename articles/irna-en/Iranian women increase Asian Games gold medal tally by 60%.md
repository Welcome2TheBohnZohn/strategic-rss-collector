# Iranian women increase Asian Games gold medal tally by 60%

Published: 2026-10-03 08:00:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281507/Iranian-women-increase-Asian-Games-gold-medal-tally-by-60

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian women athletes increased the country’s Asian Games gold medal tally by 60 percent at the Aichi-Nagoya 2026 Asian Games, Vice President for Women and Family Affairs Zahra Behrouz-Azar says.
