# Trump suffering from ‘schizophrenia’, needs treatment: Iran deputy FM

Published: 2026-08-28 11:44:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248540/Trump-suffering-from-schizophrenia-needs-treatment-Iran-deputy
