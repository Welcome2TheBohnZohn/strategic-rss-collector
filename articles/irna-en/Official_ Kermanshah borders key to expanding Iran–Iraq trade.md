# Official: Kermanshah borders key to expanding Iran–Iraq trade

Published: 2026-09-16 09:21:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265615/Official-Kermanshah-borders-key-to-expanding-Iran-Iraq-trade

Kermanshah, IRNA — Iraq’s consul general in Kermanshah has said the existence of five official borders and one border market in Kermanshah is a very important capacity for developing trade between the two countries.
