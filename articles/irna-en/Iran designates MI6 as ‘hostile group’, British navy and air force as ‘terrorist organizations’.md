# Iran designates MI6 as ‘hostile group’, British navy and air force as ‘terrorist organizations’

Published: 2026-11-01 06:31:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308838/Iran-designates-MI6-as-hostile-group-British-navy-and-air

Tehran, IRNA — Iran has designated Britain’s spying service, MI6, as a “hostile group” and declared the Royal Navy and Royal Air Force “terrorist organizations” in a retaliatory move against London’s malicious actions against the Islamic Republic.
