# Iranian Foreign Ministry urges Europe to resist unlawful US pressure

Published: 2026-09-01 14:36:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252312/Iranian-Foreign-Ministry-urges-Europe-to-resist-unlawful-US-pressure

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei urges European countries to avoid “absolute obedience to unlawful US orders,” warning that aligning with Washington at Iran’s expense would not earn Europe greater respect or strengthen its position.
