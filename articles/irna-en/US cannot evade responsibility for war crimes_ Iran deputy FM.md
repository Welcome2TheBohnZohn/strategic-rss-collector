# US cannot evade responsibility for war crimes: Iran deputy FM

Published: 2026-09-18 12:27:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267350/US-cannot-evade-responsibility-for-war-crimes-Iran-deputy-FM

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s deputy foreign minister for legal affairs said everyone says that the US brutal attacks on Iran’s ‘Minab’ and ‘Lamerd’ cities were blatant war crimes, and Washington cannot evade its responsibility for these crimes.
