# Israeli regime intensifies attacks across Gaza Strip

Published: 2026-09-04 06:27:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254511/Israeli-regime-intensifies-attacks-across-Gaza-Strip

Tehran, IRNA – The Israeli regime has intensified attacks across the Gaza Strip, with heavy gunfire, artillery shelling and airstrikes reported in Gaza City and central Gaza, according to the Palestinian media.
