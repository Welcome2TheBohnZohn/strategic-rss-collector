# Legal diplomacy must deliver tangible results in protecting national interests: Iran official

Published: 2026-10-29 09:20:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306693/Legal-diplomacy-must-deliver-tangible-results-in-protecting-national

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has called for a results-oriented approach to legal and multilateral diplomacy, stressing that international engagement must produce tangible outcomes in defending Iran’s national interests and securing the rights of its people.
