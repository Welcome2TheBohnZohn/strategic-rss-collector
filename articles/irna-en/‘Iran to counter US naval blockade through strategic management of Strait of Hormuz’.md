# ‘Iran to counter US naval blockade through strategic management of Strait of Hormuz’

Published: 2026-10-12 11:28:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290513/Iran-to-counter-US-naval-blockade-through-strategic-management

Tehran, IRNA – Major General Yahya Rahim Safavi, head of the Shaheed Hajj Qassem Soleimani Research Institute of Sacred Defense Sciences and Teachings, says Iran’s main strategy for the Strait of Hormuz was its intelligent management in response to the US naval blockade against the country.
