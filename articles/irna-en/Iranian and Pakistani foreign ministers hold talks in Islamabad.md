# Iranian and Pakistani foreign ministers hold talks in Islamabad

Published: 2026-11-16 14:26:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323679/Iranian-and-Pakistani-foreign-ministers-hold-talks-in-Islamabad

Islamabad, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Pakistani counterpart, Senator Mohammad Ishaq Dar, held bilateral talks in Islamabad on Monday on the sidelines of the 30th meeting of foreign ministers of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO).
