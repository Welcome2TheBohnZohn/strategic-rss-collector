# Police officer martyred in terrorist attack in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-10-14 11:01:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292678/Police-officer-martyred-in-terrorist-attack-in-southeastern-Iran

Zahedan, IRNA – A police officer has been martyred in a terrorist attack on a police vehicle in Mehrestan County, Sistan and Baluchestan Province, southeastern Iran.
