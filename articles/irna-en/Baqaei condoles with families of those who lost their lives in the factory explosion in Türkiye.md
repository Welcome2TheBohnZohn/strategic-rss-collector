# Baqaei condoles with families of those who lost their lives in the factory explosion in Türkiye

Published: 2026-11-11 20:05:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319525/Baqaei-condoles-with-families-of-those-who-lost-their-lives-in

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei, in a message while referring to an explosion at an iron and steel manufacturing plant in the province of Izmir, western Turkiye, extended condolences and sympathy of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the families of the victims, as well as to the government and people of the friendly neighboring country.
