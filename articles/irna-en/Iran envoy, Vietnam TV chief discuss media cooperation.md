# Iran envoy, Vietnam TV chief discuss media cooperation

Published: 2026-10-16 16:27:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294329/Iran-envoy-Vietnam-TV-chief-discuss-media-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador to Hanoi and the head of Vietnam Television exchanged views on expanding cooperation between the radio and television organizations of the two countries.
