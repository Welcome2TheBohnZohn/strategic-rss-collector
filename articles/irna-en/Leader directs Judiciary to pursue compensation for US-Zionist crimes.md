# Leader directs Judiciary to pursue compensation for US-Zionist crimes

Published: 2026-10-24 17:08:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302156/Leader-directs-Judiciary-to-pursue-compensation-for-US-Zionist

Tehran, IRNA - Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has directed the Judiciary to seriously pursue the rights of the Iranian nation and compensation for material and moral damages resulting from the American-Zionist crimes.
