# Iran to pursue national interests through diplomacy or battlefield: AEOI spox.

Published: 2026-10-11 12:10:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289565/Iran-to-pursue-national-interests-through-diplomacy-or-battlefield

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will pursue its national interests through diplomacy if possible, but will use force if necessary, spokesperson for the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) Behrouz Kamalvandi has said.
