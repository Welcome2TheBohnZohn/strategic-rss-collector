# Hundreds of thousands in Netherlands march against Gaza genocide

Published: 2026-11-09 05:55:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316432/Hundreds-of-thousands-in-Netherlands-march-against-Gaza-genocide

Tehran, IRNA – News reports indicated that more than 200,000 people in the Netherlands took part in a large-scale protest in Amsterdam on Sunday, denouncing the genocide in Gaza and demanding swift government action against the Israeli regime.
