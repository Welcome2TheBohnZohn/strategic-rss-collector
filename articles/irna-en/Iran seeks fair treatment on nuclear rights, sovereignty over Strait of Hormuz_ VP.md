# Iran seeks fair treatment on nuclear rights, sovereignty over Strait of Hormuz: VP

Published: 2026-11-14 13:11:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321499/Iran-seeks-fair-treatment-on-nuclear-rights-sovereignty-over

Tehran, IRNA – Vice President for Legal Affairs Majid Ansari has emphasized that Iran seeks fair treatment and respect for its nuclear rights, territorial sovereignty, and authority over the Strait of Hormuz.
