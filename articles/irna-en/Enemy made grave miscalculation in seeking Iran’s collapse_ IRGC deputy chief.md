# Enemy made grave miscalculation in seeking Iran’s collapse: IRGC deputy chief

Published: 2026-10-07 06:38:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285644/Enemy-made-grave-miscalculation-in-seeking-Iran-s-collapse-IRGC

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – The enemy made a grave miscalculation by believing that military attacks, sanctions, unrest, and assassination of Iranian officials could create a power vacuum and lead to the collapse of the country within a week, Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Political Deputy Brigadier General Yadollah Javani says.
