# Iran and Iraq aim to increase tourist exchanges to 10 million, minister says

Published: 2026-10-21 16:27:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299780/Iran-and-Iraq-aim-to-increase-tourist-exchanges-to-10-million

Ahvaz, IRNA – Iran and Iraq have the potential to increase annual tourist exchanges to 10 million people, Tourism Minister Reza Salehi-Amiri said on Wednesday, stressing the need for new planning and stronger cross-border infrastructure to achieve the target.
