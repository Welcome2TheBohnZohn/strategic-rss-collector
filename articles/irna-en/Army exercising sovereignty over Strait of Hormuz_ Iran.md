# Army exercising sovereignty over Strait of Hormuz: Iran

Published: 2026-11-22 05:30:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328803/Army-exercising-sovereignty-over-Strait-of-Hormuz-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Army spokesperson, Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia, says that Iranian forces are maintaining control over the Strait of Hormuz and exercising the country’s sovereignty over the strategic waterway.
