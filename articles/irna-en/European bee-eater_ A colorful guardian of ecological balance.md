# European bee-eater: A colorful guardian of ecological balance

Published: 2026-10-10 10:16:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86288467/European-bee-eater-A-colorful-guardian-of-ecological-balance

The European bee-eater is one of Iran’s most colorful and remarkable birds. It feeds on a wide variety of flying insects such as bees, wasps, dragonflies, butterflies, beetles, grasshoppers, and flies. By naturally controlling insect populations, the bird plays an important role in maintaining ecological balance, making it a natural form of pest control.
