# Muslims in Russia celebrate birth anniversary of Prophet Mohammad

Published: 2026-08-30 06:35:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249951/Muslims-in-Russia-celebrate-birth-anniversary-of-Prophet-Mohammad

Moscow, IRNA – Sunni and Shia Muslims across Russia have commemorated the birth anniversary of the Prophet Mohammad (PBUH), a key Islamic occasion marking unity among Muslim nations.
