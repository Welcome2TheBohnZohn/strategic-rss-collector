# Strait of Hormuz gives Iran strategic leverage in global affairs: Official

Published: 2026-11-08 08:16:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315606/Strait-of-Hormuz-gives-Iran-strategic-leverage-in-global-affairs

Tehran, IRNA — The Strait of Hormuz has become an important instrument of influence for Iran, with developments there directly linked to the country’s financial security, the secretary of the Expediency Council’s secretariat says.
