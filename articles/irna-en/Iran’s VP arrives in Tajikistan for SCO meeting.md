# Iran’s VP arrives in Tajikistan for SCO meeting

Published: 2026-10-19 09:47:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297115/Iran-s-VP-arrives-in-Tajikistan-for-SCO-meeting

Tehran, IRNA — Iran's First Vice President, Mohammad-Reza Aref, has arrived in the capital of Tajikistan to attend the meeting of the Heads of Government of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) member states.
