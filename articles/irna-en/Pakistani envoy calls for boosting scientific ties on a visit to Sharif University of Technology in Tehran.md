# Pakistani envoy calls for boosting scientific ties on a visit to Sharif University of Technology in Tehran

Published: 2026-09-15 10:17:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264788/Pakistani-envoy-calls-for-boosting-scientific-ties-on-a-visit

Tehran, IRNA — Pakistan’s Ambassador to Iran, while visiting Sharif University of Technology in Tehran, has emphasized expanding scientific and technological collaboration between the two countries’ higher academic institutions.
