# In photos: Iran’s Kurdistan honors child and teenage martyrs

Published: 2026-11-03 10:36:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86311252/In-photos-Iran-s-Kurdistan-honors-child-and-teenage-martyrs

The following images show a ceremony honoring 306 child and teenage martyrs from the western province of Kurdistan, which took place in Sanandaj, the capital of the province, on November 3, 2026. The event also paid tribute to the children killed in the Shajareh Tayyebeh school attack in Minab and Palestinian children martyred in Gaza.
