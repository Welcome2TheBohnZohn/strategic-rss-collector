# Iran and Russia discuss opportunities for oil exploration cooperation in southern Caspian Sea

Published: 2026-10-10 16:54:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288804/Iran-and-Russia-discuss-opportunities-for-oil-exploration-cooperation

Sari, IRNA – In a meeting with the ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Russia, the CEO of the Caspian Oil Company highlighted the potential for expanding exploration activities and developing hydrocarbon structures in the southern sector of the Caspian Sea.
