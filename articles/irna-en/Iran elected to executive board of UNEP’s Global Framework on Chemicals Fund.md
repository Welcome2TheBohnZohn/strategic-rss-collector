# Iran elected to executive board of UNEP’s Global Framework on Chemicals Fund

Published: 2026-11-25 12:37:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332473/Iran-elected-to-executive-board-of-UNEP-s-Global-Framework-on

London, IRNA – Iran has been elected to the Executive Board of the Special Program Trust Fund of the Global Framework on Chemicals (GFC) under the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) for a three-year term, representing the Asia-Pacific regional group.
