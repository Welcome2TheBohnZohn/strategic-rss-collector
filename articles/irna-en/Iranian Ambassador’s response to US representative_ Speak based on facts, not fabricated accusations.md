# Iranian Ambassador’s response to US representative: Speak based on facts, not fabricated accusations

Published: 2026-10-27 19:55:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305366/Iranian-Ambassador-s-response-to-US-representative-Speak-based

New York, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations Gholamhossein Darzi speaking at the end of the UN Security Council meeting on Iran, in response to the claims made by US representative Mike Waltz, [the Iranian representative] displayed images of those martyred in acts of aggression by the United States and the Zionist regime against Iran, stating: "Let us speak based on facts, not on fabricated accusations."
