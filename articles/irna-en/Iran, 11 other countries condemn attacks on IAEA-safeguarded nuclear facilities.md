# Iran, 11 other countries condemn attacks on IAEA-safeguarded nuclear facilities

Published: 2026-10-24 13:45:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302038/Iran-11-other-countries-condemn-attacks-on-IAEA-safeguarded

Tehran, IRNA - Iran, together with 11 other countries, issued a joint statement at the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly First Committee, calling for a complete prohibition on all forms of attack and threats against nuclear facilities under International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) safeguards.
