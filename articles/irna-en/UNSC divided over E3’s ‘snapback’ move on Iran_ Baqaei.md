# UNSC divided over E3’s ‘snapback’ move on Iran: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-14 10:25:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263745/UNSC-divided-over-E3-s-snapback-move-on-Iran-Baqaei

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei, referring to Europe’s push for the so-called ‘snapback’ sanctions mechanism against Iran, says UN Security Council members have not reached a consensus yet.
