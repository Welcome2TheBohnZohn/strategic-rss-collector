# Iran envoy slams Europe’s stance on US, Israeli aggression

Published: 2026-11-06 16:43:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314145/Iran-envoy-slams-Europe-s-stance-on-US-Israeli-aggression

London, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Germany has criticized European countries’ positions regarding the US and the Israeli regime’s aggression against Iran, urging them to take responsibility for their own wrong decisions and respect the “great Iran” instead of making baseless demands.
