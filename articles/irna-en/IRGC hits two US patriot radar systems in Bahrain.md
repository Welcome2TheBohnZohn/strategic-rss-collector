# IRGC hits two US patriot radar systems in Bahrain

Published: 2026-11-22 06:05:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328844/IRGC-hits-two-US-patriot-radar-systems-in-Bahrain

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says its Aerospace Force has struck two US Patriot air defense radars at military bases in Bahrain in a combined missile and drone operation launched in response to continued US aggression and violations of Iranian airspace.
