# Justice must never be biased or shaped by political considerations: Iran Judiciary Chief

Published: 2026-09-05 07:48:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255223/Justice-must-never-be-biased-or-shaped-by-political-considerations

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Judiciary Chief, Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Eje’i, has called for a fair international legal order, stressing that justice must not be selective, subordinate to political considerations, or determined by a country’s power and influence.
