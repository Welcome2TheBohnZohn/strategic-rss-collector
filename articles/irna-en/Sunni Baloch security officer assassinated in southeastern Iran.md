# Sunni Baloch security officer assassinated in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-11-18 15:54:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326115/Sunni-Baloch-security-officer-assassinated-in-southeastern-Iran

Zahedan, IRNA – A Sunni Baloch man, tasked with maintaining security in an area in southeastern Sistan and Baluchestan Province, has been assassinated in an armed attack.
