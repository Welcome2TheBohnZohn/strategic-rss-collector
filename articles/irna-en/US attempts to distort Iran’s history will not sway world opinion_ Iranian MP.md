# US attempts to distort Iran’s history will not sway world opinion: Iranian MP

Published: 2026-10-16 10:24:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294046/US-attempts-to-distort-Iran-s-history-will-not-sway-world-opinion

Mashhad, IRNA – Member of the Cultural Commission of Iran’s Parliament, Hassanali Akhlaghi-Amiri, says that the US Secretary of State’s distortion of Iran’s history would not affect global public opinion or the world’s view of Iran.
