# Land blockade of Iran is impossible: Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-23 15:51:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330512/Land-blockade-of-Iran-is-impossible-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says a land blockade of Iran is impossible given the country’s size and extensive ties with neighboring countries, stressing the need to strengthen national resilience to make sanctions ineffective.
