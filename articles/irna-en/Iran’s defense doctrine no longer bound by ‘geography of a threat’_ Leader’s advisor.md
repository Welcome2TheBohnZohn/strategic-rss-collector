# Iran’s defense doctrine no longer bound by ‘geography of a threat’: Leader’s advisor

Published: 2026-11-01 19:33:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309609/Iran-s-defense-doctrine-no-longer-bound-by-geography-of-a-threat

Tehran, IRNA - Mohammad Mokhber, senior advisor and aide to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, said in a message that Iran’s defense doctrine is rapidly evolving and that the geography of a threat no longer determines the geography of the response.
