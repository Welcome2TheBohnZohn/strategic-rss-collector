# Effective environmental programs depend on regional security and stability: Environment Department chief

Published: 2026-11-12 09:18:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319827/Effective-environmental-programs-depend-on-regional-security

Tehran, IRNA – Head of the Department of Environment Shina Ansari says implementing effective environmental programs depends on security and stability in the region, highlighting the environmental consequences of recent wars against Iran.
