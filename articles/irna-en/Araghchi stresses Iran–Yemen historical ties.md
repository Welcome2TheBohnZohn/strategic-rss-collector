# Araghchi stresses Iran–Yemen historical ties

Published: 2026-10-15 14:27:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293675/Araghchi-stresses-Iran-Yemen-historical-ties

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has emphasized the importance of the historical ties between Iran and Yemen, noting that the blockade of Yemen has caused severe suffering for its people.
