# Oil tanker hits mine in south of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-27 12:44:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333992/Oil-tanker-hits-mine-in-south-of-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — An oil tanker struck a naval mine and exploded north of Jazirat Umm al Ghanam (Ghanam Island) in the Strait of Hormuz, the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) Navy says.
