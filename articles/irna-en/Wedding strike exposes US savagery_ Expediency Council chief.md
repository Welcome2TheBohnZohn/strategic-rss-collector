# Wedding strike exposes US savagery: Expediency Council chief

Published: 2026-09-03 10:16:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254031/Wedding-strike-exposes-US-savagery-Expediency-Council-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Head of Iran’s Expediency Council Ayatollah Sadegh Amoli Larijani has condemned the US strike in Sirik, Hormozgan Province, calling the killing of women and children a war crime that exposes Washington’s hypocrisy on human rights.
