# ‘War crime’: Iran sends formal letter to UN over deadly US attack on wedding

Published: 2026-09-03 05:56:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253837/War-crime-Iran-sends-formal-letter-to-UN-over-deadly-US-attack

New York, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador and deputy permanent representative to the United Nations, Gholamhossein Darzi, has strongly condemned a deadly US strike on a home in Hormozgan during a wedding, calling it a war crime.
