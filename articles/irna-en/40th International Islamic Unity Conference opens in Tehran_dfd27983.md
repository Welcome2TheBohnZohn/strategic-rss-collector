# 40th International Islamic Unity Conference opens in Tehran

Published: 2026-08-27 10:04:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86247685/40th-International-Islamic-Unity-Conference-opens-in-Tehran

The 40th International Islamic Unity Conference opened on Thursday (August 27) in Iran’s capital, Tehran. President Masoud Pezeshkian addressed the conference that focuses on promoting the ideas and vision of the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei.
