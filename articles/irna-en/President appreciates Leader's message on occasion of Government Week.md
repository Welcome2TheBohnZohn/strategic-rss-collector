# President appreciates Leader's message on occasion of Government Week

Published: 2026-08-28 19:41:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248783/President-appreciates-Leader-s-message-on-occasion-of-Government

Tehran, IRNA – In a message acknowledging the communication from Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei, on the occasion of Government Week, Masoud Pezeshkian, the President of the Islamic Republic of Iran, emphasized that we assure the great nation of Iran and the cherished Leadership that, in complete unity and cohesion with the other branches of government, we will safeguard the country's authority and dignity as we pursue a path of flourishing and progress.
