# President Pezeshkian: Regional diplomacy must help contain crises, conflicts

Published: 2026-10-10 05:36:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287990/President-Pezeshkian-Regional-diplomacy-must-help-contain-crises

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian says regional diplomacy should go beyond holding meetings and serve as a tool for managing crises, maintaining dialogue, and preventing conflicts from escalating.
