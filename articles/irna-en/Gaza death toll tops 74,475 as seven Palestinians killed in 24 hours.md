# Gaza death toll tops 74,475 as seven Palestinians killed in 24 hours

Published: 2026-10-18 11:03:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296107/Gaza-death-toll-tops-74-475-as-seven-Palestinians-killed-in-24

Tehran, IRNA — Seven Palestinians have been killed and 25 others wounded in Israeli attacks on the Gaza Strip over the past 24 hours, bringing the overall death toll since October 2023 to 74,475.
