# Pezeshkian: Iran supports any agreement that strengthens unity among Islamic countries

Published: 2026-10-26 14:38:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304102/Pezeshkian-Iran-supports-any-agreement-that-strengthens-unity

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran supports any understanding that strengthens unity and cohesion among Islamic countries, as well as regional security and development.
