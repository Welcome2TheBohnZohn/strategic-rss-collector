# Iran attends International United Cultures Forum in Russia

Published: 2026-09-25 12:06:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274150/Iran-attends-International-United-Cultures-Forum-in-Russia

Saint Petersburg, IRNA — The 12th International United Cultures Forum has opened in St. Petersburg with ministers and cultural figures from 70 countries, including Iran’s Culture and Islamic Guidance Minister Seyyed Abbas Salehi.
