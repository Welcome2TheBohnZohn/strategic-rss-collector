# Pezeshkian: Iran, Russia and other nations will seek to realize multilateralism

Published: 2026-10-26 20:47:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304321/Pezeshkian-Iran-Russia-and-other-nations-will-seek-to-realize

Moscow, IRNA – Referring to the fact that the Islamic Republic of Iran and the Russian Federation enjoy good relations, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has stressed that the challenge ahead is US unilateralism, which seeks to advance its agenda through bullying; however, together with Russia and other nations, Iran is striving to realize multilateralism globally.
