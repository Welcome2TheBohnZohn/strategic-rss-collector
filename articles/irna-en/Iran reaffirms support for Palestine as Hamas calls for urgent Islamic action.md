# Iran reaffirms support for Palestine as Hamas calls for urgent Islamic action

Published: 2026-09-02 16:37:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253620/Iran-reaffirms-support-for-Palestine-as-Hamas-calls-for-urgent

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi reaffirms Iran’s “principled and firm” support for the Palestinian cause and the establishment of an independent Palestinian state, as Hamas calls for serious and immediate action by Islamic countries against Israeli aggression and crimes against Palestinians.
