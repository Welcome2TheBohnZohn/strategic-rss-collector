# Strategies to strengthen cooperation between ECO and WHO reviewed in Tehran

Published: 2026-10-10 18:49:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288861/Strategies-to-strengthen-cooperation-between-ECO-and-WHO-reviewed

Tehran, IRNA – The Secretary-General of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) met with the new World Health Organization (WHO) Representative to the Islamic Republic of Iran and discussed ways to enhance cooperation between ECO member states and the WHO.
