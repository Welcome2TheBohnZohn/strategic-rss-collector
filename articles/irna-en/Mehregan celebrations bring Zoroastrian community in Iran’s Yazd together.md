# Mehregan celebrations bring Zoroastrian community in Iran’s Yazd together

Published: 2026-10-03 07:41:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281445/Mehregan-celebrations-bring-Zoroastrian-community-in-Iran-s-Yazd

Yazd, IRNA – Zoroastrians in Yazd Province gathered Friday to mark Mehregan, an ancient Iranian festival, with ceremonies held in Yazd city, Taft and Sharifabad in Ardakan, as well as in Tehran, Isfahan, Shiraz and Kerman provinces.
