# Pezeshkian vows to implement Leader’s directive to end ‘neither war nor peace’ situation

Published: 2026-10-28 12:41:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306120/Pezeshkian-vows-to-implement-Leader-s-directive-to-end-neither

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has described the recent directive of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution to the Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council to end the current state of “neither war nor peace” as an important direction for the country’s future horizon.
