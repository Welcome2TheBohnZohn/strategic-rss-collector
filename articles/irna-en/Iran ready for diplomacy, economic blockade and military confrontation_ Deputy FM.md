# Iran ready for diplomacy, economic blockade and military confrontation: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-11-15 19:53:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322858/Iran-ready-for-diplomacy-economic-blockade-and-military-confrontation

Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s deputy foreign minister for legal and international affairs, Kazem Gharibabadi, says the Islamic Republic is ready for three possible scenarios in dealing with the United States: diplomacy, economic blockade, and military aggression.
