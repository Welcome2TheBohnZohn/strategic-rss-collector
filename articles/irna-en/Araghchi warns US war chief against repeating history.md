# Araghchi warns US war chief against repeating history

Published: 2026-11-16 06:03:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323006/Araghchi-warns-US-war-chief-against-repeating-history

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has warned US Secretary of War Pete Hegseth against repeating historical mistakes, saying that those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it.
