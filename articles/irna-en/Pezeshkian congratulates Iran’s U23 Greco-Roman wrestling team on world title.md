# Pezeshkian congratulates Iran’s U23 Greco-Roman wrestling team on world title

Published: 2026-10-15 14:07:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293637/Pezeshkian-congratulates-Iran-s-U23-Greco-Roman-wrestling-team

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated the Greco-Roman wrestling team on winning the championship title at the 2026 U23 World Greco-Roman Wrestling Championships held in Las Vegas, USA.
