# IRGC spokesperson: War with Iran or with the American people?

Published: 2026-10-04 14:07:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283050/IRGC-spokesperson-War-with-Iran-or-with-the-American-people

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has underscored the high costs of deploying new US forces, questioning whether the US government is waging war on Iran or on the American people.
