# US, Britain lack legitimacy to comment on human rights: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-11-03 10:48:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311248/US-Britain-lack-legitimacy-to-comment-on-human-rights-Deputy

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi says the United States and Britain lack the legitimacy to comment on human rights because of their widespread violations of the rights of their own citizens and other nations.
