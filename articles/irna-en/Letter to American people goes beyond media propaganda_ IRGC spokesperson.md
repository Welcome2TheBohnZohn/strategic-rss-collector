# Letter to American people goes beyond media propaganda: IRGC spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-29 08:28:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277995/Letter-to-American-people-goes-beyond-media-propaganda-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), says the force has prepared a detailed letter to the American people to explain Iran’s positions in a sincere and logical manner.
