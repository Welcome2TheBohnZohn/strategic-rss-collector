# In photos: St. George commemoration ceremony at Tehran’s Saint George Church

Published: 2026-09-28 08:17:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86276976/In-photos-St-George-commemoration-ceremony-at-Tehran-s-Saint

Members of Iran’s Armenian Christian community attend a commemoration ceremony for St. George, accompanied by a Mass, at Saint George Church of Tehran. The ceremony was held over Thursday and Friday evenings and Saturday afternoon, with the participation of representatives of Tehran and northern Iran’s Armenian Christians.
