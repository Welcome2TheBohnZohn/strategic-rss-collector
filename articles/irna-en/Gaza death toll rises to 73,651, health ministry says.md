# Gaza death toll rises to 73,651, health ministry says

Published: 2026-09-05 17:05:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255817/Gaza-death-toll-rises-to-73-651-health-ministry-says

The death toll from Israeli attacks on the Gaza Strip has risen to 73,651, with 174,575 people wounded, Gaza’s health ministry said on Saturday.
