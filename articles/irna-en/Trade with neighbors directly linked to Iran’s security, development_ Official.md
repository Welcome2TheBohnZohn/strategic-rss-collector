# Trade with neighbors directly linked to Iran’s security, development: Official

Published: 2026-09-09 14:36:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259832/Trade-with-neighbors-directly-linked-to-Iran-s-security-development

Kermanshah, IRNA – Expanding trade with neighbors, including Iraq, is linked to Iran’s security and development and requires centralized decision-making and flexibility in dealing with the neighboring country’s regulations, the head of the Iran-Iraq Joint Chamber of Commerce said.
