# Iran warns against unilateral measures in global economy

Published: 2026-10-13 08:20:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291316/Iran-warns-against-unilateral-measures-in-global-economy

Tehran, IRNA — The deputy for international relations of the Central Bank of Iran warns that unilateral measures can deepen economic fragmentation, increase risks, and fuel uncertainty in the global economy.
