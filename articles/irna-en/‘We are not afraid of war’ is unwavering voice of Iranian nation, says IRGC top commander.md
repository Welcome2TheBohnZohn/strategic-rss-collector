# ‘We are not afraid of war’ is unwavering voice of Iranian nation, says IRGC top commander

Published: 2026-09-24 15:46:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273612/We-are-not-afraid-of-war-is-unwavering-voice-of-Iranian-nation

Tehran, IRNA — Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Major General Ahmad Vahidi, has emphasized, “We are not afraid of war,” reflecting the unwavering voice of the Iranian nation, heard around the world from the UN podium.
