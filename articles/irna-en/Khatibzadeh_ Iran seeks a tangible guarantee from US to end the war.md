# Khatibzadeh: Iran seeks a tangible guarantee from US to end the war

Published: 2026-10-01 19:36:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280523/Khatibzadeh-Iran-seeks-a-tangible-guarantee-from-the-US-to-end

Moscow, IRNA — Head of the Center for Political and International Studies at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Saeed Khatibzadeh stating that Tehran’s policy is to resolve issues diplomatically—despite US breaches of commitment and acts of betrayal—described Iran’s seven-day initiative as being in line with this approach, adding that Iran seeks concrete US guarantees to end the war.
