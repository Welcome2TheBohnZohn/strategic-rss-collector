# IRGC: Fourth offending tanker catches fire in Strait of Hormuz after defying Iranian warnings

Published: 2026-10-30 21:16:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307702/IRGC-Fourth-offending-tanker-catches-fire-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The IRGC Navy announced that the fourth oil tanker caught fire after attempting to pass through the Strait of Hormuz via an unauthorized route.
