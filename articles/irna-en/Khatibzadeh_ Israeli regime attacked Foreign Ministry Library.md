# Khatibzadeh: Israeli regime attacked Foreign Ministry Library

Published: 2026-10-25 07:49:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302569/Khatibzadeh-Israeli-regime-attacked-Foreign-Ministry-Library

Tehran, IRNA — The Zionist regime’s attack on the Foreign Ministry Library, comparable to the Mongol invasions, halted activity at the century-old institution, says Saeed Khatibzadeh, Head of the Center for Political and International Studies at the Foreign Ministry.
