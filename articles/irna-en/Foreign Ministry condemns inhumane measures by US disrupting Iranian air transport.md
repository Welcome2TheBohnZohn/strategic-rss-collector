# Foreign Ministry condemns inhumane measures by US disrupting Iranian air transport

Published: 2026-10-01 20:47:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280545/Foreign-Ministry-condemns-inhumane-measures-by-US-disrupting

Tehran, IRNA – The Foreign Ministry in a statement condemned the US government's illegal and inhumane actions and maneuvers aimed at disrupting Iran's trade relations and air transport with other countries.
