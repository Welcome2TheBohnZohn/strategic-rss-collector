# Akhlaghi is a well-deserved nominee for defense minister: Armed Forces chief

Published: 2026-10-17 15:35:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295267/Akhlaghi-is-a-well-deserved-nominee-for-defense-minister-Armed

Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of Iran’s Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has hailed the nomination of Mehrdad Akhlaqi as the new minister of defense, arguing that he is a fitting choice for the post, given his experience within the country’s defense industry.
