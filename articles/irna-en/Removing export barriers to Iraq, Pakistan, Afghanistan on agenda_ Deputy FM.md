# Removing export barriers to Iraq, Pakistan, Afghanistan on agenda: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-10-24 09:40:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301776/Removing-export-barriers-to-Iraq-Pakistan-Afghanistan-on-agenda

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is reviewing regulations governing exporters’ obligations to address disruptions in transportation and logistics, with proposals also under consideration to remove barriers to exports to neighboring Iraq, Pakistan, and Afghanistan, a senior Foreign Ministry official says.
