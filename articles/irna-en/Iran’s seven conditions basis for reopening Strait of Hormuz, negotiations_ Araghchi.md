# Iran’s seven conditions basis for reopening Strait of Hormuz, negotiations: Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-21 12:14:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328309/Iran-s-seven-conditions-basis-for-reopening-Strait-of-Hormuz

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding is no longer on the table; rather, Iran’s proposal, also known as 'Seven Conditions' combining US commitments under the MoU, is the basis for the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and negotiations.
