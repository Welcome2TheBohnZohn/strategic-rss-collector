# Iran files complaint over German lawmaker’s remarks at IPU Assembly

Published: 2026-10-08 11:43:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286951/Iran-files-complaint-over-German-lawmaker-s-remarks-at-IPU-Assembly

Tehran, IRNA – Manouchehr Mottaki, Head of the Executive Council of the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU), has protested remarks by a German parliamentarian at the IPU Assembly in Tanzania, saying they violated the assembly’s rules.
