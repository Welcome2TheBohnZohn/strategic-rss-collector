# Araghchi meets new Iranian ambassador to Uganda

Published: 2026-10-21 15:48:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299743/Araghchi-meets-new-Iranian-ambassador-to-Uganda

Tehran, IRNA – Reza Nourian, Iran's newly appointed ambassador to Uganda, has met with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi prior to his departure for his diplomatic mission in the African country.
