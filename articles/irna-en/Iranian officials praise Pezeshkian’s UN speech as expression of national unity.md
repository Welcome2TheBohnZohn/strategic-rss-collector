# Iranian officials praise Pezeshkian’s UN speech as expression of national unity

Published: 2026-09-24 10:32:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273395/Iranian-officials-praise-Pezeshkian-s-UN-speech-as-expression

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian officials have praised President Masoud Pezeshkian’s address to the United Nations General Assembly, describing it as a reflection of national unity, resistance and Iran’s determination to defend its sovereignty.
