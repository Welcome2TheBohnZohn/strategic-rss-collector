# Iran calls on ITU to impartially assess damage to its communications infrastructure

Published: 2026-11-13 10:34:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320576/Iran-calls-on-ITU-to-impartially-assess-damage-to-its-communications

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Minister of Information and Communications Technology (ICT), Sattar Hashemi, has called for an impartial and comprehensive assessment of damage to the country’s communications and broadcasting infrastructure as well as technical recommendations for its reconstruction by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU).
