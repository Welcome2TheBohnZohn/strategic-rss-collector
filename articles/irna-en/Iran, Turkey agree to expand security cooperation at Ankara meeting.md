# Iran, Turkey agree to expand security cooperation at Ankara meeting

Published: 2026-11-27 09:58:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333862/Iran-Turkey-agree-to-expand-security-cooperation-at-Ankara-meeting

Ankara, IRNA – Iran and Turkey have agreed to strengthen operational cooperation and information sharing on border security, counterterrorism and efforts to combat drug and arms smuggling during their eighth joint security meeting in Ankara.
