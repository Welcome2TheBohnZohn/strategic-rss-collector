# ICC leaders criticize efforts to erode international legal order

Published: 2026-09-04 07:22:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254547/ICC-leaders-criticize-efforts-to-erode-international-legal-order

Tehran, IRNA – The International Criminal Court’s current and former presidents have warned that efforts to isolate the court threaten the international legal order amid US pressure on ICC members.
