# Pezeshkian arrives in New Delhi to attend 18th BRICS Summit

Published: 2026-09-11 09:28:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260926/Pezeshkian-arrives-in-New-Delhi-to-attend-18th-BRICS-Summit

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian arrived in Indian capital of New Delhi on Friday to attend the 18th BRICS Summit and was received by India’s Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Shantanu Thakur upon his arrival.
