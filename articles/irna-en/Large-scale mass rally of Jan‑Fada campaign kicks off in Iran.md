# Large-scale mass rally of Jan‑Fada campaign kicks off in Iran

Published: 2026-09-18 07:29:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267119/Large-scale-mass-rally-of-Jan-Fada-campaign-kicks-off-in-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Hundreds of thousands of Iranians have poured into the streets of Tehran as the large-scale mass rally of the Jan-Fada, the national campaign titled Jan‑Fada aimed at defending the homeland, has started.
