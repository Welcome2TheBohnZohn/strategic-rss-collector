# Iran repairs more than 40,000 homes damaged in recent war

Published: 2026-11-25 13:20:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332550/Iran-repairs-more-than-40-000-homes-damaged-in-recent-war

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has repaired more than 40,000 residential units damaged during the recent US-Israeli-imposed war, with nearly 20,000 volunteer groups taking part in the reconstruction effort, says the head of the county’s Basij Organization.
