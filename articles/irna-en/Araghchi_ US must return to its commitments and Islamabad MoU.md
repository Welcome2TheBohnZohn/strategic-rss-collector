# Araghchi: US must return to its commitments and Islamabad MoU

Published: 2026-08-31 20:59:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251472/Araghchi-US-must-return-to-its-commitments-and-Islamabad-MoU

Tehran, IRNA – Referring to his meetings and discussions on the first day of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization summit—and noting that in all these meetings he emphasized the Iranian nation's resolve to secure its rights and also discussed the aggressive actions of the United States and their consequences—Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran Abbas Araghchi said that the United States must return to its commitments and the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding.
