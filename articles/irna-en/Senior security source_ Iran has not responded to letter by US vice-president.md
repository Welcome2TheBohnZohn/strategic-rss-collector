# Senior security source: Iran has not responded to letter by US vice-president

Published: 2026-10-29 20:37:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307156/Senior-security-source-Iran-has-not-responded-to-letter-by-US

Tehran, IRNA – Referring to a letter from the US Vice President JD Vance, a senior security source said that Iran has not yet responded to the message, and that media speculations regarding its content are inaccurate.
