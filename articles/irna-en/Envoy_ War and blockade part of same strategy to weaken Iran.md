# Envoy: War and blockade part of same strategy to weaken Iran

Published: 2026-09-09 22:04:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260023/Envoy-War-and-blockade-part-of-same-strategy-to-weaken-Iran

London, IRNA – Ali Bahreini, head of the Permanent Mission of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations Office and other International Organizations in Geneva, has censured the United States over unilateral sanctions and warfare against the Islamic Republic, saying aggression and blockade are part of a broader strategy to undermine the independence of Iran.
