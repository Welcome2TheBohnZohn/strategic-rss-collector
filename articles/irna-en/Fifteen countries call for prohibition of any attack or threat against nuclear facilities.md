# Fifteen countries call for prohibition of any attack or threat against nuclear facilities

Published: 2026-09-18 10:39:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267234/Fifteen-countries-call-for-prohibition-of-any-attack-or-threat

London, IRNA – Fifteen countries, including Iran, China and Russia, have stressed that peaceful, safeguarded nuclear facilities must never be subjected to any attack or threat of attack.
