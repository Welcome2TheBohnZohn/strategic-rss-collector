# Unity of Islamic world cornerstone of Iran's foreign policy: Araghchi

Published: 2026-08-29 16:11:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249683/Unity-of-Islamic-world-cornerstone-of-Iran-s-foreign-policy

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Iran will keep pursuing the issue of unity and empathy among Muslims in all circumstances based on the teachings of the late founder of the Islamic Republic and the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution.
