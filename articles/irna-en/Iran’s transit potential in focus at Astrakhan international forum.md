# Iran’s transit potential in focus at Astrakhan international forum

Published: 2026-11-27 13:43:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86334023/Iran-s-transit-potential-in-focus-at-Astrakhan-international

Moscow, IRNA — Iran’s unique transit capacities were highlighted at the 4th International North-South Transport Corridor Forum in Astrakhan, where regional officials underscored the need to develop the key route and expand cooperation with Tehran.
