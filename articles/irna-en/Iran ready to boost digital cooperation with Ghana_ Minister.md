# Iran ready to boost digital cooperation with Ghana: Minister

Published: 2026-11-10 16:02:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318310/Iran-ready-to-boost-digital-cooperation-with-Ghana-Minister

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s minister of information and communications technology said that the country is ready to expand bilateral cooperation with Ghana in the fields of Information and Communication Technology (ICT), AI, digital government, and the communication infrastructure.
