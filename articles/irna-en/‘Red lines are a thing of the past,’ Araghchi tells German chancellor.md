# ‘Red lines are a thing of the past,’ Araghchi tells German chancellor

Published: 2026-10-31 06:23:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307891/Red-lines-are-a-thing-of-the-past-Araghchi-tells-German-chancellor

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has reacted to remarks by German Chancellor Friedrich Merz, who called for a swift end to the US-Israeli war on Iran amid rising energy prices caused by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.
