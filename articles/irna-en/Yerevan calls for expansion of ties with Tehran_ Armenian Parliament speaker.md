# Yerevan calls for expansion of ties with Tehran: Armenian Parliament speaker

Published: 2026-09-24 15:37:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273532/Yerevan-calls-for-expansion-of-ties-with-Tehran-Armenian-Parliament
