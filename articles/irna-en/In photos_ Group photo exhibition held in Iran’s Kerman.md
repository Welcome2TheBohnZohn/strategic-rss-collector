# In photos: Group photo exhibition held in Iran’s Kerman

Published: 2026-09-16 12:09:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86265690/In-photos-Group-photo-exhibition-held-in-Iran-s-Kerman

The Hamnegar group photo exhibition opened in Kerman Province, southeast Iran, on September 15, 2026, with the presence of the provincial culture chief and a number of artists and photography enthusiasts. The exhibition will continue until September 20.
