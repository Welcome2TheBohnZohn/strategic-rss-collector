# Iran pursues justice for Minab school attack victims at national, international levels

Published: 2026-10-09 05:58:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287354/Iran-pursues-justice-for-Minab-school-attack-victims-at-national

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – Deputy Justice Minister for Human Rights and International Affairs, Askar Jalalian, announces that efforts are being made to seek justice for the victims of the Shajareh Tayyebeh school attack in Minab at both national and international levels.
