# Former football player Hamid Alidoosti passes away

Published: 2026-11-02 10:49:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86310129/Former-football-player-Hamid-Alidoosti-passes-away

Hamid Alidoosti, a former Iran national football team player and one of the country’s standout forwards, has died at 70 following years in football and a struggle with illness.
