Published: 2026-11-15 19:00:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322836/Wreckage-of-advanced-Orbiter-4-drone-found-in-western-Iran-Army

Tehran, IRNA - The wreckage of an advanced Orbiter 4 surveillance drone has been discovered in the western Iranian highlands, the Iranian Army said Sunday evening.
