# Baqaei reprimands White House spokesperson for baseless anti-Iran accusations

Published: 2026-09-19 16:11:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268308/Baqaei-reprimands-White-House-spokesperson-for-baseless-anti-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has delivered a scathing rebuke to the White House press secretary for her anti-Iran rhetoric, saying the baseless accusations against the Islamic Republic expose the intensity of crimes committed by the United States itself.
