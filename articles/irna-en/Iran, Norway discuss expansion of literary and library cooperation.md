# Iran, Norway discuss expansion of literary and library cooperation

Published: 2026-08-29 10:27:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249267/Iran-Norway-discuss-expansion-of-literary-and-library-cooperation

London, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Oslo Alireza Jahangiri has met Ase Wetas, Director General of the National Library of Norway, to discuss expanding literary and library cooperation, exchanging resources, and promoting authors from both countries.
