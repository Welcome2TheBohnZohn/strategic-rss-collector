# Araghchi meets with Libya’s top diplomat in Baghdad

Published: 2026-10-17 12:02:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295073/Araghchi-meets-with-Libya-s-top-diplomat-in-Baghdad

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has met with Libya's Acting Foreign Minister Taher al-Baour in Baghdad to discuss bilateral relations and regional developments.
