# Iran’s air defense capability significantly enhanced: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-19 14:06:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326891/Iran-s-air-defense-capability-significantly-enhanced-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA — The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) says that a significant upgrade in the country’s air defense power has been made, stating that previous defensive gaps have been fully addressed.
