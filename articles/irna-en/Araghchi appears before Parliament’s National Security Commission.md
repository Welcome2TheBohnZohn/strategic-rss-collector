# Araghchi appears before Parliament’s National Security Commission

Published: 2026-09-07 18:30:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257845/Araghchi-appears-before-Parliament-s-National-Security-Commission

Tehran, IRNA – The Chairman of the Parliament's National Security and Foreign Policy Commission announced Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi's attendance at the commission, and said that the meeting addressed issues such as measures taken regarding the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding and interactions between Iran and Oman, particularly concerning the Strait of Hormuz.
