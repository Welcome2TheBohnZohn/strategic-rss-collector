# Iranian air defense shoots down two drones over Persian Gulf

Published: 2026-11-25 05:15:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331851/Iranian-air-defense-shoots-down-two-drones-over-Persian-Gulf

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army says its air defense force has destroyed two drones, identified as Lucas and MQ-9, over the Persian Gulf.
