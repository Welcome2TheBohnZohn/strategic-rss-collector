# Iran’s response to any new aggression will be swift and decisive: Army spokesperson

Published: 2026-11-21 16:03:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328552/Iran-s-response-to-any-new-aggression-will-be-swift-and-decisive

Tehran, IRNA – Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia says Iran’s Armed Forces are stronger than before and will respond firmly and immediately to any new aggression.
