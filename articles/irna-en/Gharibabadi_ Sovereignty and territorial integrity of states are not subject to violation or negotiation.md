# Gharibabadi: Sovereignty and territorial integrity of states are not subject to violation or negotiation

Published: 2026-09-25 21:00:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274384/Gharibabadi-Sovereignty-and-territorial-integrity-of-states

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi, noting that the credibility of international law hinges on the equality of states before the law, added: The Islamic Republic of Iran believes that if international law is to truly safeguard peace, justice, and human dignity, it cannot be a set of selective rules—rigorously enforced against some states while disregarded for others due to political considerations and power dynamics.
