# Gharibabadi stresses US responsibility for nuclear test victims

Published: 2026-10-01 16:20:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280449/Gharibabadi-stresses-US-responsibility-for-nuclear-test-victims

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has criticized the United States over the consequences of its nuclear tests, stressing Washington’s responsibility toward the victims.
