# No US vessels in Persian Gulf, Hormuz, or northern Indian Ocean: IRGC adviser

Published: 2026-10-04 19:53:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283268/No-US-vessels-in-Persian-Gulf-Hormuz-or-northern-Indian-Ocean

Tehran, IRNA - A senior IRGC adviser says that for the first time, no US vessels are present in the Persian Gulf, the Strait of Hormuz, the Sea of Oman, or even the northern Indian Ocean.
