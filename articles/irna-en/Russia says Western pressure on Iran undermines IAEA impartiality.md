# Russia says Western pressure on Iran undermines IAEA impartiality

Published: 2026-11-15 16:42:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322767/Russia-says-Western-pressure-on-Iran-undermines-IAEA-impartiality

London, IRNA – Russia has criticized Western countries for exploiting the International Atomic Energy Agency’s (IAEA) safeguards system to exert political pressure on Iran, warning that their actions are undermining the agency’s impartiality and escalating tensions over Tehran’s peaceful nuclear program.
