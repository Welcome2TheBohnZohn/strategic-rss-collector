# IRGC, Army chiefs meet, emphasize strong defense against enemies

Published: 2026-09-12 12:21:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261920/IRGC-Army-chiefs-meet-emphasize-strong-defense-against-enemies

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Commander-in-Chief Major General Ahmad Vahidi and Iran’s Army Commander Major General Amir Hatami have met to assess the latest battlefield situation, emphasizing strong defense against the enemies orchestrating malicious objectives against the country.
