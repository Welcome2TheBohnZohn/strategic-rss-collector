# Pakistan reaffirms Iran’s right to peaceful nuclear energy

Published: 2026-09-10 09:32:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260257/Pakistan-reaffirms-Iran-s-right-to-peaceful-nuclear-energy

Islamabad, IRNA — Pakistan has reaffirmed that Iran, as a signatory to the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT), has a legitimate right to develop and use nuclear energy for peaceful purposes.
