# Foreign Ministry stresses development of Mazandaran citrus exports to neighboring countries

Published: 2026-09-29 15:10:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278578/Foreign-Ministry-stresses-development-of-Mazandaran-citrus-exports

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry has stressed the need to expand exports of citrus products from northern Mazandaran province to neighboring countries, focusing on the province’s production and export potential.
