# IRGC spokesperson says US faces shortfall in strategic weapons after war

Published: 2026-08-26 15:42:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247016/IRGC-spokesperson-says-US-faces-shortfall-in-strategic-weapons

Tehran, IRNA – The United States has suffered shortages in strategic weapons following its recent war against Iran, and replenishing some of its depleted stocks could take years, a spokesman for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says.
