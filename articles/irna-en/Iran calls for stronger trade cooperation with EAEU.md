# Iran calls for stronger trade cooperation with EAEU

Published: 2026-10-14 16:37:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293059/Iran-calls-for-stronger-trade-cooperation-with-EAEU

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s minister of industry, mine and trade called for removing trade barriers and facilitating direct engagement between companies and the private sector of the member states of the Iran-EAEU free trade agreement, aimed at stronger trade cooperation.
