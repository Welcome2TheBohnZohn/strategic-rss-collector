# Ghalibaf: US carrot-and-stick approach will not work against Iran

Published: 2026-10-29 19:33:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307146/Ghalibaf-US-carrot-and-stick-approach-will-not-work-against

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says the Islamic Republic’s conditions on possible negotiations with the United States and ending the war have been clearly and repeatedly articulated, stressing that the “carrot-and-stick” approach will not work against Tehran.
