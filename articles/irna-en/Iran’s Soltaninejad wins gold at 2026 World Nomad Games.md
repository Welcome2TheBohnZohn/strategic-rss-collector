# Iran’s Soltaninejad wins gold at 2026 World Nomad Games

Published: 2026-09-04 08:27:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254598/Iran-s-Soltaninejad-wins-gold-at-2026-World-Nomad-Games

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian athlete, Sajjad Soltaninejad, has won the gold medal at the 2026 World Nomad Games in Kyrgyzstan after three days of intense competition.
