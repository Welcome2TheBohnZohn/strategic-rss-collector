# Iranian, Russian, Azerbaijani foreign ministers hold brief talks ahead of CIS summit

Published: 2026-10-09 09:14:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287500/Iranian-Russian-Azerbaijani-foreign-ministers-hold-brief-talks

Moscow, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Russian and Azerbaijani counterparts, Sergey Lavrov and Jeyhun Bayramov, have engaged in a brief conversation ahead of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) summit in Turkmenistan.
