# Hormuz Strait to remain closed until Iran’s seven conditions are met: Ghalibaf

Published: 2026-10-04 08:08:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282520/Hormuz-Strait-to-remain-closed-until-Iran-s-seven-conditions

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will not reopen the Strait of Hormuz until seven conditions outlined in the Islamabad memorandum are fulfilled, Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has said, adding that Tehran does not base its national security policy on statements or tweets by US officials.
