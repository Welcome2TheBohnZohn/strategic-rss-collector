# President Pezeshkian says Iran will not bow to US, Israeli bullying

Published: 2026-11-26 07:43:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333044/President-Pezeshkian-says-Iran-will-not-bow-to-US-Israeli-bullying

Mashhad, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iranians will stand firm in defending their country and will not bow to pressure from the United States and the Israeli regime, stressing that national unity is essential to overcoming external challenges.
