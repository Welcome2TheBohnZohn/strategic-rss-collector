# In photos: Gohar Zamin beat Daem Panah in Iran futsal league

Published: 2026-11-16 06:07:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86322933/In-photos-Gohar-Zamin-beat-Daem-Panah-in-Iran-futsal-league

Futsal teams Daem Panah and Gohar Zamin faced off in the 11th week of Iran’s Futsal Super League in Mashhad on November 15, 2026. The match ended 3-2 in favor of Gohar Zamin Sirjan.
