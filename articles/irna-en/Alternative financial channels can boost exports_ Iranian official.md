# Alternative financial channels can boost exports: Iranian official

Published: 2026-09-09 10:40:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259292/Alternative-financial-channels-can-boost-exports-Iranian-official

Tehran, IRNA – Chairman of the Iran-Vietnam Joint Chamber of Commerce Mostafa Mousavi has said alternative financial mechanisms, including barter trade, can help boost exports and facilitate the return of export revenues amid restrictions on financial transfers.
