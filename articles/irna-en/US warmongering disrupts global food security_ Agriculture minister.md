# US warmongering disrupts global food security: Agriculture minister

Published: 2026-10-20 10:33:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298263/US-warmongering-disrupts-global-food-security-Agriculture-minister

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s agriculture minister has warned that US unilateralism and warmongering are disrupting global food security and the transportation of food and agricultural products, calling on international organizations to ensure everyone’s right to access food.
