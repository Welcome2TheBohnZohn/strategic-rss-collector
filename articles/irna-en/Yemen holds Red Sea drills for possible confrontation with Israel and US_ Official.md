# Yemen holds Red Sea drills for possible confrontation with Israel and US: Official

Published: 2026-09-03 06:45:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253863/Yemen-holds-Red-Sea-drills-for-possible-confrontation-with-Israel

Tehran, IRNA – A senior Yemeni official says Yemen’s armed forces are conducting new military drills in the Red Sea to prepare for a possible confrontation with Israel and the United States.
