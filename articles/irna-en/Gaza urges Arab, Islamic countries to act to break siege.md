# Gaza urges Arab, Islamic countries to act to break siege

Published: 2026-10-20 15:25:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298605/Gaza-urges-Arab-Islamic-countries-to-act-to-break-siege

Tehran, IRNA – The Government Media Office in the Gaza Strip has called on Arab and Islamic countries to intervene urgently to break the siege imposed on the territory, warning that cuts to aid could have catastrophic consequences for Palestinians.
