# IRGC: Offending supertanker is burning in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-10 17:19:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288817/IRGC-Offending-supertanker-is-burning-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Navy of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) announced that a rogue supertanker carrying crude oil exploded after striking a naval mine while exiting the Strait of Hormuz via an unauthorized route.
