# Iran judiciary ready to facilitate legal cases for Iranian, Indian traders

Published: 2026-09-05 06:19:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255103/Iran-judiciary-ready-to-facilitate-legal-cases-for-Iranian-Indian

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Judiciary is ready to facilitate and accelerate the handling of judicial and legal cases involving Iranian and Indian traders and economic actors engaged in joint business activities, Judiciary Chief Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Eje’i said.
