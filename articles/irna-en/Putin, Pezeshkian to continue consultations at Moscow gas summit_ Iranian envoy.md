# Putin, Pezeshkian to continue consultations at Moscow gas summit: Iranian envoy

Published: 2026-10-20 06:10:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297831/Putin-Pezeshkian-to-continue-consultations-at-Moscow-gas-summit

Moscow, IRNA — Iranian ambassador to Russia says consultations between President Masoud Pezeshkian and his Russian counterpart, Vladimir Putin, will continue on the sidelines of the upcoming summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF) in Moscow, as Tehran and Moscow intensify high-level diplomatic engagement.
