# Iran’s astronomy Olympiad triumph highlights youth scientific talent: Leader’s aide

Published: 2026-10-05 09:22:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283693/Iran-s-astronomy-Olympiad-triumph-highlights-youth-scientific

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian students’ third consecutive world title in astronomy and astrophysics, marked by five gold medals, demonstrates the country’s potential among its youth, adviser and aide to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution Mohammad Mokhber said in a congratulatory message.
