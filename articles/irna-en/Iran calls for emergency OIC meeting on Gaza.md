# Iran calls for emergency OIC meeting on Gaza

Published: 2026-11-19 15:47:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326995/Iran-calls-for-emergency-OIC-meeting-on-Gaza

Tehran, IRNA — The Iranian foreign minister has called for an emergency meeting of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) foreign ministers to address the lifting of the blockade on Gaza.
