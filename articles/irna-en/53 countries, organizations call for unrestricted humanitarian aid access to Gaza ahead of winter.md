# 53 countries, organizations call for unrestricted humanitarian aid access to Gaza ahead of winter

Published: 2026-11-13 06:31:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320364/53-countries-organizations-call-for-unrestricted-humanitarian

Tehran, IRNA – A coalition of 53 countries and organizations has called for all crossings into Gaza to be opened, allowing humanitarian and medical aid—including shelter supplies, fuel, and medicines—to enter without restrictions as winter approaches.
