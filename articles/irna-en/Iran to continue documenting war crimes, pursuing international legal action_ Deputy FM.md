# Iran to continue documenting war crimes, pursuing international legal action: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-10-05 08:08:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283610/Iran-to-continue-documenting-war-crimes-pursuing-international

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs, Kazem Gharibabadi, has emphasized the need to continue documenting crimes and damages resulting from military aggression by the United States and the Israeli regime and reporting them to international organizations.
