# Top security official: Anyone who confronts Iran, will have to pay price for it

Published: 2026-11-04 17:44:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312862/Top-security-official-Anyone-who-confronts-Iran-will-have-to

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) Mohsen Rezaei, warned that anyone who confronts Iran will pay the price, both on the battlefield and at their own doorstep.
