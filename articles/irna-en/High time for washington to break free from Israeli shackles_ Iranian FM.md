# High time for washington to break free from Israeli shackles: Iranian FM

Published: 2026-09-22 10:10:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86271211/High-time-for-washington-to-break-free-from-Israeli-shackles

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said, “It is time for Washington to break free from these shackles,” responding to an article in Israel Hayom arguing that US policy should require Washington to bear a cost for any perceived disrespect or attack against Israel.
