# Iran condemns terrorist attack in Pakistan

Published: 2026-09-19 05:46:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267595/Iran-condemns-terrorist-attack-in-Pakistan

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has strongly condemned a terrorist attack near a mosque in Kohat, Pakistan’s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, during Friday prayers, which killed and injured dozens of people.
