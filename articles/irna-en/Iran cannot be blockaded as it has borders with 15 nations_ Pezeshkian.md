# Iran cannot be blockaded as it has borders with 15 nations: Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-10-08 15:04:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287130/Iran-cannot-be-blockaded-as-it-has-borders-with-15-nations-Pezeshkian

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian, referring to the US economic war, says the enemy cannot easily isolate the Islamic Republic, given that the country shares borders with 15 nations, allowing it to continue trade, political, and security exchanges through various routes.
