# Iran, Pakistan to expand tourism cooperation

Published: 2026-10-03 10:52:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281776/Iran-Pakistan-to-expand-tourism-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Minister of Cultural Heritage, Tourism and Handicrafts, Reza Salehi Amiri has proposed drawing up a five-year roadmap to expand cultural and tourism cooperation with Pakistan.
