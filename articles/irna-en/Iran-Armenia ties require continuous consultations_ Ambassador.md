# Iran-Armenia ties require continuous consultations: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-10 12:41:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288483/Iran-Armenia-ties-require-continuous-consultations-Ambassador

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador to Armenia Khalil Shirgholami has highlighted the need for continuous consultations between Tehran and Yerevan to map out future cooperation and expand bilateral relations, describing ties between the two countries as strategic.
