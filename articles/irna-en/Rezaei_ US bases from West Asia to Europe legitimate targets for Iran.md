# Rezaei: US bases from West Asia to Europe legitimate targets for Iran

Published: 2026-11-12 06:16:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319655/Rezaei-US-bases-from-West-Asia-to-Europe-legitimate-targets

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei has warned that all US military bases involved in an aggression against Iran would become legitimate targets for Iranian attacks if Washington launches another war against the country.
