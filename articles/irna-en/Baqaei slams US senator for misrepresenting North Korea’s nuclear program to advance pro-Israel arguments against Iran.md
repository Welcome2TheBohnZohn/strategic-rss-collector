# Baqaei slams US senator for misrepresenting North Korea’s nuclear program to advance pro-Israel arguments against Iran

Published: 2026-11-26 06:01:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332961/Baqaei-slams-US-senator-for-misrepresenting-North-Korea-s-nuclear

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized US Senator John Cornyn for attributing remarks about North Korea’s nuclear program to Iran, criticizing him for echoing pro-Israel lobbying arguments.
