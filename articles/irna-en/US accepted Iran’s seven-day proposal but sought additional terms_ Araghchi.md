# US accepted Iran’s seven-day proposal but sought additional terms: Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-21 06:33:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298957/US-accepted-Iran-s-seven-day-proposal-but-sought-additional-terms

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the United States has agreed to the conditions outlined in Iran’s seven-day initiative but has requested the addition of several clauses.
