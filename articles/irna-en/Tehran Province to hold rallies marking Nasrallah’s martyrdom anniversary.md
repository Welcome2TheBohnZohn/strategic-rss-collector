# Tehran Province to hold rallies marking Nasrallah’s martyrdom anniversary

Published: 2026-09-27 10:48:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276152/Tehran-Province-to-hold-rallies-marking-Nasrallah-s-martyrdom

Varamin, IRNA – People in cities and towns across Tehran Province will hold evening rallies on Sunday to commemorate the second anniversary of the martyrdom of Hezbollah Secretary-General Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, an Iranian official says.
