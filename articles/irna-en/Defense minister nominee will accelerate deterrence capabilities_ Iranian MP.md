# Defense minister nominee will accelerate deterrence capabilities: Iranian MP

Published: 2026-10-10 12:36:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288609/Defense-minister-nominee-will-accelerate-deterrence-capabilities

Tehran, IRNA –Salar Velayatmadar, chairman of the Iranian Parliament's Defense Committee, has backed the nomination of Mehrdad Akhlaqi as the new minister of defense, emphasizing that his appointment could accelerate the pace of advancements and the country's deterrence capabilities.
