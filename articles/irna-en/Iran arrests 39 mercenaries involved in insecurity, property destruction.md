# Iran arrests 39 mercenaries involved in insecurity, property destruction

Published: 2026-10-19 12:21:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297322/Iran-arrests-39-mercenaries-involved-in-insecurity-property

Tehran, IRNA - The Ministry of Intelligence has announced that 39 key figures and individuals involved in creating insecurity and destroying public and private property in Qazvin Province have been identified and arrested.
