# Defense minister says supplying Armed Forces a priority

Published: 2026-10-19 15:12:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297450/Defense-minister-says-supplying-Armed-Forces-a-priority

Tehran, IRNA - Minister of Defense and Armed Forces Logistics Mehrdad Akhlaghi Ketabchi has vowed that the ministry under his watch will continue to supply the needs of the Iranian Armed Forces.
