# Iran condemns US strikes on civilians as war crimes, reaffirms its right to self-defense

Published: 2026-09-02 14:24:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253521/Iran-condemns-US-strikes-on-civilians-as-war-crimes-reaffirms

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry has condemned US attacks on civilian areas and infrastructure across several provinces as “brutal war crimes” and reaffirmed the Islamic Republic’s right to retaliate in self-defense.
