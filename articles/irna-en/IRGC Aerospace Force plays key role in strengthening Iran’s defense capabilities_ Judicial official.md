# IRGC Aerospace Force plays key role in strengthening Iran’s defense capabilities: Judicial official

Published: 2026-11-08 13:18:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316025/IRGC-Aerospace-Force-plays-key-role-in-strengthening-Iran-s-defense

Tehran, IRNA – A senior Iranian judicial official has emphasized the strategic position of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Aerospace Force in developing advanced defense technologies and turning indigenous capabilities into a foundation for national deterrence and security.
