# Iran’s top security official meets with Putin, delivers Leader’s message

Published: 2026-11-05 15:18:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313516/Iran-s-top-secretary-official-meets-with-Putin-in-Kremlin

Tehran, IRNA – Major General Mohsen Rezaei, Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC), has met with the Russian president and delivered a message from Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei.
