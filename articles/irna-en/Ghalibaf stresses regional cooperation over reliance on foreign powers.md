# Ghalibaf stresses regional cooperation over reliance on foreign powers

Published: 2026-08-29 16:07:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249641/Ghalibaf-stresses-regional-cooperation-over-reliance-on-foreign

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has stressed the need for Muslim countries to rely on regional cooperation and their own capabilities rather than extra-regional powers.
