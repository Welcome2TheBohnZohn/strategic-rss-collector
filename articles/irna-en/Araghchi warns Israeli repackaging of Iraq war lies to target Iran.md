# Araghchi warns Israeli repackaging of Iraq war lies to target Iran

Published: 2026-11-17 20:00:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325128/Araghchi-warns-Israeli-repackaging-of-Iraq-war-lies-to-target

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says media campaigns targeting Iran have intensified in recent days, warning that the Israeli regime and its lobbyists are seeking to reproduce the propaganda tactics used to justify the US-led invasion of Iraq in the early 2000s.
