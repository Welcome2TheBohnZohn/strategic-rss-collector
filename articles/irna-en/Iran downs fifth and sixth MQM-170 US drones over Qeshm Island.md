# Iran downs fifth and sixth MQM-170 US drones over Qeshm Island

Published: 2026-11-05 20:22:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313659/Iran-downs-fifth-and-sixth-MQM-170-US-drones-over-Qeshm-Island

Tehran, IRNA – The Public Relations office of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) in a statement announced the downing of fifth and sixth American reconnaissance drones—MQM-170—over the island of Qeshm.
