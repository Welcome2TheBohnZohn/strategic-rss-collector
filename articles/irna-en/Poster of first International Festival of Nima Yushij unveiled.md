# Poster of first International Festival of Nima Yushij unveiled

Published: 2026-10-04 06:17:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282367/Poster-of-1st-International-Festival-of-Nima-Yushij-unveiled

Sari, IRNA – The poster for the International Festival of Nima Youshij, dedicated to cultural, artistic, and literary studies, has been unveiled ahead of the event, set to take place November 17–19 at the University of Mazandaran in northern Iran.
