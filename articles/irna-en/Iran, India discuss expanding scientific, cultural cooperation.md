# Iran, India discuss expanding scientific, cultural cooperation

Published: 2026-11-18 05:53:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325275/Iran-India-discuss-expanding-scientific-cultural-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Head of the Iranology Foundation Ali Akbar Salehi has stressed the need for stronger ties between Iran and India, saying that expanding scientific, cultural, and tourism cooperation requires greater attention and joint planning.
