# Iran, Russia, China reject US-E3 draft resolution against Tehran at IAEA BoG as 
‘baseless’

Published: 2026-09-09 16:08:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259885/Iran-Russia-China-reject-US-E3-draft-resolution-against-Tehran

London, IRNA – Iran, Russia, and China have jointly rejected an anti-Iran draft resolution presented by the United States and the E3 — Germany, France, and Britain — at the IAEA Board of Governors meeting, calling the move legally baseless.
