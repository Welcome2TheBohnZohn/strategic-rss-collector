# Pezeshkian: Iran ready to expand all-out ties with Thailand

Published: 2026-10-06 11:03:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284970/Pezeshkian-Iran-ready-to-expand-all-out-ties-with-Thailand

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has said the Islamic Republic of Iran is ready to expand its relations with Thailand in various fields, including economic, scientific, and technological areas.
