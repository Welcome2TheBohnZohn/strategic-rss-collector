# Two more offending oil tankers hit naval mines in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-26 12:21:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333327/Two-more-offending-oil-tankers-hit-naval-mines-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has reported explosions involving two more offending oil tankers traveling along unauthorized and unsafe routes in the Strait of Hormuz.
