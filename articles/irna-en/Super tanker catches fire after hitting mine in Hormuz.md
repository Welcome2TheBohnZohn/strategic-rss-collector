# Super tanker catches fire after hitting mine in Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-20 20:06:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298772/Super-tanker-catches-fire-after-hitting-mine-in-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy announced that a supertanker carrying more than two million barrels of crude oil exploded and caught fire after striking a naval mine south of the Strait of Hormuz.
