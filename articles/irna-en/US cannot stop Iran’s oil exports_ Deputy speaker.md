# US cannot stop Iran’s oil exports: Deputy speaker

Published: 2026-10-01 10:28:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280185/US-cannot-stop-Iran-s-oil-exports-Deputy-speaker

Sari, IRNA – Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad says the United States will never be able to bring Iran’s oil exports to zero, stressing that Washington miscalculated Iran’s military capabilities and national unity.
