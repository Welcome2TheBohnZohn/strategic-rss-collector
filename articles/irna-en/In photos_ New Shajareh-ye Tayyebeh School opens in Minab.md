# In photos: New Shajareh-ye Tayyebeh School opens in Minab

Published: 2026-09-23 10:20:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86272492/In-photos-New-Shajareh-ye-Tayyebeh-School-opens-in-Minab

A new Shajareh-ye Tayyebeh School was inaugurated in Minab, Hormozgan Province, on September 23, 2026, marking the start of the 2026–27 school year with a ceremony honoring the memory of 168 student martyrs. The event was attended by Vice President Hossein Afshin, Tehran Mayor Alireza Zakani, Deputy Education Minister Rezvan Hakimzadeh, and families of martyrs and survivors.
