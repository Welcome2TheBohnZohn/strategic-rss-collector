# Iran-Pakistan border trade serves interests of both brotherly nations: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-25 13:10:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302954/Iran-Pakistan-border-trade-serves-interests-of-both-brotherly

Islamabad, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Pakistan, Reza Amiri-Moghaddam, has described border trade between the two neighboring countries as an integral part of high-level bilateral agreements that serves the interests of both nations.
