# US strike on Qeshm causes no damage to civilian areas or infrastructure

Published: 2026-11-09 05:30:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316406/US-strike-on-Qeshm-causes-no-damage-to-civilian-areas-or-infrastructure

Qeshm, IRNA – A US military strike on locations on Qeshm Island has caused no damage to residential or commercial areas or essential infrastructure, says Hormozgan Province’s deputy governor for political, security and social affairs.
