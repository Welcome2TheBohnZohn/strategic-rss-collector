# Iraq to resume Iran flights from Najaf airport within 24 hours

Published: 2026-09-28 14:42:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277438/Iraq-to-resume-Iran-flights-from-Najaf-airport-within-24-hours

Tehran, IRNA – Iraq will resume direct flights to Iran from Najaf International Airport within 24 hours, Iraq’s ambassador to Tehran Yasser al-Hajjaj said on Monday, adding that Baghdad had not ordered a halt to air services between the two neighboring countries.
