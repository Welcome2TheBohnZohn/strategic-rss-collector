# Commander: Iranians’ resistance disrupted enemy’s calculations

Published: 2026-09-21 10:18:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270080/Commander-Iranians-resistance-disrupted-enemy-s-calculations

Bandar Abbas, IRNA - The commander of Iran Navy Southern Fleet says that resistance of the Iranian nation during the recent war disrupted the enemy’s calculations and prevented the realization of its strategic objectives.
