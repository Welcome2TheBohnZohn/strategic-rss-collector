# Top security official: Trump foolishly continues the war, but Iran is fully ready for self-defense

Published: 2026-09-20 07:19:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268682/Top-security-official-Trump-foolishly-continues-war-but-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Mohsen Rezaei says US President Donald Trump waged a foolish war on Iran and is foolishly continuing it. However, Iran stands ready to give a stronger and more painful response should the US resume military aggression, as it did during the 40-day conflict that began earlier this year.
