# Russia rejects claims of evacuating staff, nuclear plant specialists from Iran

Published: 2026-10-24 12:13:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301924/Russia-rejects-claims-of-evacuating-staff-nuclear-plant-specialists

Tehran, IRNA – The Embassy of the Russian Federation in Iran, in a statement, has vehemently denied reports that it is preparing to evacuate diplomatic staff and specialists working at the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant.
