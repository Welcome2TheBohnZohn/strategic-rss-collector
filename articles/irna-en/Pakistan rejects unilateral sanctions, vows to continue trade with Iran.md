# Pakistan rejects unilateral sanctions, vows to continue trade with Iran

Published: 2026-08-27 09:41:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247657/Pakistan-rejects-unilateral-sanctions-vows-to-continue-trade

Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistan will continue bilateral trade with Iran in accordance with international law and is not bound by unilateral sanctions imposed on Tehran, Pakistani Foreign Ministry spokesperson Tahir Andrabi has said.
