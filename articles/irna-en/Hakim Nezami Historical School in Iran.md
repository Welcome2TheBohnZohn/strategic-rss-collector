# Hakim Nezami Historical School in Iran

Published: 2026-10-07 08:05:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86285812/Hakim-Nezami-Historical-School-in-Iran

Built in 1908 in Iran’s northern city of Astara, Hakim Nezami School has been transformed into a specialized museum showcasing the region’s history, culture, and educational heritage. The historic school once welcomed renowned figures, including Nima Yooshij, widely regarded as the father of modern Persian poetry.
