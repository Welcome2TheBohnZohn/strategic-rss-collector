# Iran army chief hails police endeavors to maintain security

Published: 2026-10-04 05:58:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282314/Iran-army-chief-hails-police-endeavors-to-maintain-security

Tehran, IRNA – Army Chief General Amir Hatami has praised the round-the-clock efforts of police personnel, saying that sustainable security and the protection of public peace are indebted to the strong presence and sacrifices of polices forces.
