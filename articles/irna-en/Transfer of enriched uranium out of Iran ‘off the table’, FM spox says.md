# Transfer of enriched uranium out of Iran ‘off the table’, FM spox says

Published: 2026-11-09 15:59:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86317115/Transfer-of-enriched-uranium-out-of-Iran-off-the-table-FM

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has categorically rejected transferring enriched uranium out of Iran, stressing that the option was “off the table” and asserting that there is no justification for such a move.
