# Ghalibaf: Live with it, era of US F-35s being hunted down has begun

Published: 2026-09-18 19:37:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267490/Ghalibaf-Live-with-it-era-of-US-F-35s-being-hunted-down-has

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says the era in which US F-35 and F-15 fighter jets are being “hunted down” has already begun as reports point to mounting pressure and damage to the US military, some seven months into Washington's war on Iran.
