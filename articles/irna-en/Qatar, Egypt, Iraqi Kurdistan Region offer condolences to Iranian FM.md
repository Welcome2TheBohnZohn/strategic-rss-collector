# Qatar, Egypt, Iraqi Kurdistan Region offer condolences to Iranian FM

Published: 2026-10-02 09:17:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280770/Qatar-Egypt-Iraqi-Kurdistan-Region-offer-condolences-to-Iranian

Tehran, IRNA – Qatar’s prime minister and foreign minister, Egypt’s foreign minister, and Kurdistan Region president have offered condolences to Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi over the demise of his sister.
