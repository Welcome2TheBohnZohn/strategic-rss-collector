# Iranian Embassy in London rejects security and espionage allegations against Tehran

Published: 2026-11-12 22:14:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320289/Iranian-Embassy-in-London-rejects-security-and-espionage-allegations

Tehran, IRNA — The Embassy of the Islamic Republic of Iran in Britain in a statement categorically rejected security and espionage allegations against Tehran.
