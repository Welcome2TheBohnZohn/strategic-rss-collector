# Pezeshkian emphasizes continued regional consultations in phone call with Sultan of Oman

Published: 2026-10-11 11:41:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289587/Pezeshkian-emphasizes-continued-regional-consultations-in-phone

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian, in a telephone conversation with the Sultan of Oman, has emphasized the importance of bilateral cooperation and constructive consultations aimed at consolidating regional peace and stability.
