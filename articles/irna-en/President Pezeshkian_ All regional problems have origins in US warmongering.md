# President Pezeshkian: All regional problems have origins in US warmongering

Published: 2026-10-20 17:38:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298685/President-Pezeshkian-meets-Pakistani-Army-Chief

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian told Pakistani Army chief Field Marshal Asim Munir Tuesday that the tensions in the region are all result of US warmongering policies.
