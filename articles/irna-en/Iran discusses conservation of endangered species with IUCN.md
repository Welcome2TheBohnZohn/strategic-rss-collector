# Iran discusses conservation of endangered species with IUCN

Published: 2026-10-24 06:28:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301427/Iran-discusses-conservation-of-endangered-species-with-IUCN

Tehran, IRNA – An Iranian delegation attending the 17th Conference of the Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity (COP17) has held talks with officials of the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) on protecting endangered species and expanding joint cooperation.
