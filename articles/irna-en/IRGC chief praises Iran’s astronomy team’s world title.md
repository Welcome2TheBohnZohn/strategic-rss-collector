# IRGC chief praises Iran’s astronomy team’s world title

Published: 2026-10-04 15:13:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283123/IRGC-chief-praises-Iran-s-astronomy-team-s-world-title

Tehran, IRNA – Chief Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Major General Ahmad Vahidi has congratulated the Iranian national astronomy and astrophysics Olympiad team for winning five gold medals and the world title in Hanoi, Vietnam.
