# President Pezeshkian urges ambassadors to prioritize diplomacy, implementation of agreements

Published: 2026-11-03 19:31:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311732/President-Pezeshkian-urges-ambassadors-to-prioritize-diplomacy

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has called on Iran’s newly appointed ambassadors to pursue diplomatic engagement, strengthen bilateral relations and ensure the implementation of agreements with their host countries.
