# Baqaei: US claim regarding Iran’s development of chemical and biological weapons is a big lie

Published: 2026-11-19 20:59:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327143/Baqaei-US-claim-regarding-Iran-s-development-of-chemical-and

Tehran, IRNA — In response to a new claim by US officials regarding the "existence of signs pointing to the development of chemical and biological weapons" by Iran, the Foreign Ministry spokesperson wrote: After concocting the "big lie" about an "Iranian nuclear threat," US officials are now claiming that there are signs Iran is developing biological or chemical weapons!
