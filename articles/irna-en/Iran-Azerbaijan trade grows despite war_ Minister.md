# Iran-Azerbaijan trade grows despite war: Minister

Published: 2026-09-29 05:36:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277784/Iran-Azerbaijan-trade-grows-despite-war-Minister

Tehran, IRNA – Trade between Iran and the Republic of Azerbaijan increased over the past six months despite the challenges posed by the US-Israeli war on Iran, Minister of Roads and Urban Development Farzaneh Sadeq has said, stressing the need to expand infrastructure and remove obstacles to sustain the growth.
