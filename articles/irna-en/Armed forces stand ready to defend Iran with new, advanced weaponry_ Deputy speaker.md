# Armed forces stand ready to defend Iran with new, advanced weaponry: Deputy speaker

Published: 2026-11-08 16:31:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316162/Armed-forces-stand-ready-to-defend-Iran-with-new-advanced-weaponry

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad, referring to the country's proactive deterrence, says the Iranian Armed Forces are fully prepared to confront any enemy threats or hostile acts, with new and more advanced weapons.
