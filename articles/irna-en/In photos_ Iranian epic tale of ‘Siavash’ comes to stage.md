# In photos: Iranian epic tale of ‘Siavash’ comes to stage

Published: 2026-10-31 05:18:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86307792/In-photos-Iranian-epic-tale-of-Siavash-comes-to-stage

The concert-theater production “Siavash,” based on an adaptation of the story of Siavash, a legendary Iranian prince whose tale is narrated in the Shahnameh (The Book of Kings), a work by Ferdowsi, a 9th- and 10th-century Iranian poet, was staged in Tehran on October 29, 2026.
