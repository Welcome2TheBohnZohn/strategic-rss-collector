# Oman meeting called off at Saudi Arabia’s request: Tehran

Published: 2026-09-14 09:30:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263603/Oman-meeting-canceled-at-Saudi-Arabia-s-request-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says a planned meeting of the foreign ministers of regional countries in Oman was canceled at Saudi Arabia’s request, adding that Iran and Oman have finalized an understanding on maritime routes and will decide with Muscat how to announce it.
