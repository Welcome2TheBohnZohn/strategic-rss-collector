# Pezeshkian stresses strengthening Iran–Algeria ties

Published: 2026-11-01 09:36:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309014/Pezeshkian-stresses-strengthening-Iran-Algeria-ties

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian, in a message, congratulated the 72nd anniversary of the start of the liberation revolution and Algeria’s National Day to the President, government, and people of that country, and emphasized the expansion of relations between Iran and Algeria.
