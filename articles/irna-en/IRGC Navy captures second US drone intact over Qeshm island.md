# IRGC Navy captures second US drone intact over Qeshm island

Published: 2026-10-30 21:37:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307704/IRGC-Navy-captures-second-US-drone-intact-over-Qeshm-island

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has captured the second US reconnaissance and decoy drone intact west of the Strait of Hormuz after taking control of its guidance and control systems.
