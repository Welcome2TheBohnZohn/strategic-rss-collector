# Foreign Minister Araghchi leaves Islamabad

Published: 2026-11-16 18:00:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323837/Foreign-Minister-Araghchi-leaves-Islamabad-for-Tehran

Islamabad, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has departed Islamabad following the conclusion of the ECO meeting and meetings with high-ranking Pakistani officials.
