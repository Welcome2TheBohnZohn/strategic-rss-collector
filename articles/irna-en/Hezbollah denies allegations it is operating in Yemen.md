# Hezbollah denies allegations it is operating in Yemen

Published: 2026-11-04 13:28:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312676/Hezbollah-denies-allegations-it-is-operating-in-Yemen

Tehran, IRNA - Hezbollah has strongly denied allegations by the interior minister of the so-called Yemeni Transitional Council that the movement has experts in Yemen involved in managing military operations and developing military capabilities of the Yemeni forces.
