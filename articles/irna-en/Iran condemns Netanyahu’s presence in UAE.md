# Iran condemns Netanyahu’s presence in UAE

Published: 2026-09-29 21:43:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278782/Iran-condemns-Netanyahu-s-presence-in-UAE

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry has condemned Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s visit to the United Arab Emirates, warning regional countries of what it called the “very dangerous consequences” of Israel’s presence in the region.
