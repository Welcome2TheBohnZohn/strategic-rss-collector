# No relocation can shield US bases from Iran’s Armed Forces: Top commander

Published: 2026-11-24 16:14:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331601/No-relocation-can-shield-US-bases-from-Iran-s-Armed-Forces-Top

Tehran, IRNA – The head of the Chiefs of Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces says the relocation of US military bases in the region will not affect Iran’s resolve to counter threats, warning that all such bases will remain within range of Iranian forces if necessary.
