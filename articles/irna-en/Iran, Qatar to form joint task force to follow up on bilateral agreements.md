# Iran, Qatar to form joint task force to follow up on bilateral agreements

Published: 2026-10-06 15:47:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285266/Iran-Qatar-to-form-joint-task-force-to-follow-up-on-bilateral

Tehran, IRNA – Interior Minister Eskandar Momeni says Iran and Qatar would form a joint task force between their interior ministries to follow up on decisions and agreements reached during recent meetings.
