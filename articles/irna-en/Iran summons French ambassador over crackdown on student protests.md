# Iran summons French ambassador over crackdown on student protests

Published: 2026-10-06 16:23:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285284/Iran-summons-French-ambassador-over-crackdown-on-student-protests

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Foreign Ministry has summoned France’s ambassador to Tehran over the French security forces’ handling of student protests, calling for respect for the protesters’ fundamental rights and peaceful assembly.
