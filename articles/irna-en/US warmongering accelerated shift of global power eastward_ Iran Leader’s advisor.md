# US warmongering accelerated shift of global power eastward: Iran Leader’s advisor

Published: 2026-09-10 08:23:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260205/US-warmongering-accelerated-shift-of-global-power-eastward-Iran

Tehran, IRNA — Major General Yahya Rahim Safavi, a senior military adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, says US warmongering accelerated the shift of global power and economic influence toward the East.
