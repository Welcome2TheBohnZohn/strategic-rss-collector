# Iran’s Qom holds 2nd anniversary memorial for late Nasrallah

Published: 2026-10-03 06:01:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281341/Iran-s-Qom-holds-2nd-anniversary-memorial-for-late-Nasrallah

Qom, IRNA – The ceremony marking the second anniversary of the martyrdom of Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, the former Secretary-General of the Lebanese Hezbollah, was held with a large presence of people from all walks of life in Qom.
