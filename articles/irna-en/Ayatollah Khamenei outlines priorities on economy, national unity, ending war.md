# Ayatollah Khamenei outlines priorities on economy, national unity, ending war

Published: 2026-10-29 07:59:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306627/Iran-Leader-outlines-priorities-on-economy-national-unity-ending

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei emphasized economic resilience, public welfare, national unity, sustainable security and the protection of Iran’s national interests as key priorities amid the war with the United States and the Israeli regime.
