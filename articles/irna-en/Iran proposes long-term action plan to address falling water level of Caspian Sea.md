# Iran proposes long-term action plan to address falling water level of Caspian Sea

Published: 2026-10-09 12:16:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287637/Iran-proposes-long-term-action-plan-to-address-falling-water

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has proposed the formulation and implementation of a long-term regional program to address the shrinking water level of the world’s largest lake.
