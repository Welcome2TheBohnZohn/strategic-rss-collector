# Pezeshkian to meet Putin in Turkmenistan

Published: 2026-10-06 18:13:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285375/Pezeshkian-to-meet-Putin-in-Turkmenistan

Moscow, IRNA – An assistant to the Russian President, Yuri Ushakov, said Tuesday that President Vladimir Putin will meet with his Iranian counterpart on the sidelines of the meeting of Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) in Turkmenistan.
