# President: All legal rights of Iranian nation must be recognized

Published: 2026-08-27 15:07:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247948/President-All-legal-rights-of-Iranian-nation-must-be-recognized

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian in a meeting in Tehran on Thursday with visiting Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman bin Jassim Al Thani emphasized the need for recognizing all the rights of the Iranian nation within the framework of international law, saying that the Islamic Republic of Iran demands nothing beyond its legal rights and will not yield to pressure or coercion.
