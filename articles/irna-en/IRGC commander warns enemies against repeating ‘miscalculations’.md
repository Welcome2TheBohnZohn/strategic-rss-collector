# IRGC commander warns enemies against repeating ‘miscalculations’

Published: 2026-11-04 15:34:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312691/IRGC-commander-warns-enemies-against-repeating-miscalculations

Tehran, IRNA – The commander of the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) Ground Force has warned that any further miscalculations by Iran’s adversaries would accelerate their expulsion from the region and contribute to the destruction of Israel.
