# Diplomat: Russia enjoys close interaction with Iran

Published: 2026-10-07 08:01:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285712/Diplomat-Russia-enjoys-close-interaction-with-Iran

Bandar Anzali, IRNA – The Consul General of the Russian Federation in Rasht, Sergey Vladimirovich Azarov, says that Moscow and Tehran enjoy constructive interaction in various fields and are seeking to further expand mutual ties.
