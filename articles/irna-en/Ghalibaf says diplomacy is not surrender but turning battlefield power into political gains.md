# Ghalibaf says diplomacy is not surrender but turning battlefield power into political gains

Published: 2026-09-22 15:11:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271543/Ghalibaf-says-diplomacy-is-not-surrender-but-turning-battlefield

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says diplomacy should not be viewed as surrender but as a means of converting power on the battlefield into political gains.
