# General Mohebbi: Ridiculous claim of IRGC attack on cooling systems in France is Israeli scenario

Published: 2026-11-14 20:08:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321812/General-Mohebbi-Rdiculous-claim-of-attack-on-cooling-systems

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, in a message, emphasized that the ridiculous claim of an attack on cooling systems in France is a scenario by the Zionists.
