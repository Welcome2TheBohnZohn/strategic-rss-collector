# Criminal cases opened over US crimes in several areas of Iran: Prosecutor General

Published: 2026-09-05 17:04:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255794/Criminal-cases-opened-over-US-crimes-in-several-areas-of-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Prosecutor General Mohammad Movahhedi Azad says that criminal cases have been opened regarding US crimes in various parts of Iran, and the evidence gathered in these cases is currently being reviewed.
