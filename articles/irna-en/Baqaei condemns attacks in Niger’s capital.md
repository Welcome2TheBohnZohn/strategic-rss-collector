# Baqaei condemns attacks in Niger’s capital

Published: 2026-09-03 15:38:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254283/Baqaei-condemns-attacks-in-Niger-s-capital

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has condemned the Niamey attacks, reaffirming Iran’s support for Niger’s sovereignty and stability.
