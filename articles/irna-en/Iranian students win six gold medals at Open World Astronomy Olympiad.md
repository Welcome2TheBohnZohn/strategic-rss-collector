# Iranian students win six gold medals at Open World Astronomy Olympiad

Published: 2026-11-14 10:01:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321364/Iranian-students-win-six-gold-medals-at-Open-World-Astronomy

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian students have won six gold medals at the 2026 Open World Astronomy Olympiad (OWAO) held in Russia’s Sirius Federal Territory.
