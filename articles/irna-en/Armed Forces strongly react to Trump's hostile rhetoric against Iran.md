# Armed Forces strongly react to Trump's hostile rhetoric against Iran

Published: 2026-09-22 21:44:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271787/Armed-Forces-strongly-react-to-Trump-s-hostile-rhetoric-against

Tehran, IRNA — The General Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces and Khatam al-Anbya Central Headquarters announced that the hostile statements and repeated threats against Iran by the US president—accompanied by the abuse of the United Nations podium to legitimize aggression, the incitement of insecurity, and global banditry—serve merely as a tool for domestic propaganda rather than being grounded in the realities on the ground.
