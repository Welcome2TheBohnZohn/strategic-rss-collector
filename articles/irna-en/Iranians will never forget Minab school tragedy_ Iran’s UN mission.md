# Iranians will never forget Minab school tragedy: Iran’s UN mission

Published: 2026-11-21 05:40:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327823/Iranians-will-never-forget-Minab-school-tragedy-Iran-s-UN-mission

New York, IRNA — The Permanent Mission of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the United Nations has stressed that Tehran will never forget the killing of 168 students and teachers in a missile attack on a school in the southern Iranian city of Minab, calling for the suffering of the victims to be heard and their memory preserved.
