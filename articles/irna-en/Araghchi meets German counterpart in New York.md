# Araghchi meets German counterpart in New York

Published: 2026-09-23 18:56:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272944/Araghchi-meets-German-counterpart-in-New-York

New York, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul held talks on Wednesday on the sidelines of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York.
