# Iran’s oil minister resigns, president names caretaker

Published: 2026-10-04 17:57:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283228/Iran-s-oil-minister-resigns-president-names-caretaker

Tehran, IRNA - Iranian Minister of Petroleum Mohsen Paknejad has resigned, and President Masoud Pezeshkian has appointed Hamid Bovard as caretaker head of the Oil Ministry, the president’s office said.
