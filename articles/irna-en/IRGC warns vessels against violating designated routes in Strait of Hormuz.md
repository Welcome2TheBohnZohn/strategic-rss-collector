# IRGC warns vessels against violating designated routes in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-09 15:59:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287769/IRGC-warns-vessels-against-violating-designated-routes-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has renewed its warning to vessels defying the shipping guidelines and attempting to pass through a restricted zone south of the Strait of Hormuz.
