# President Pezeshkian urges unity, focus on public needs amid economic war

Published: 2026-10-15 07:08:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293265/President-Pezeshkian-urges-unity-focus-on-public-needs-amid

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian has urged government officials to avoid divisive disputes and mobilize all available resources to address people’s problems, saying national unity could turn economic hardship into an opportunity to tackle long-standing issues.
