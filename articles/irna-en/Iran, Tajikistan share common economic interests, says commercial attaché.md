# Iran, Tajikistan share common economic interests, says commercial attaché

Published: 2026-09-19 14:18:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268196/Iran-Tajikistan-share-common-economic-interests-says-commercial

Tehran, IRNA – The commercial attaché of the Islamic Republic of Iran in Tajikistan, emphasizing deepening economic relations and securing common interests of Tehran and Dushanbe, has identified key potential areas of cooperation between the two nations.
