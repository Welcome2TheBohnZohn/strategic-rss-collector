# Iran, Nicaragua FMs hold talks on UNGA sidelines

Published: 2026-09-27 05:59:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275688/Iran-Nicaragua-FMs-hold-talks-on-UNGA-sidelines

New York, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has met with his Nicaraguan counterpart Valdrack Ludwing Jaentschke Whitaker on the sidelines of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York.
