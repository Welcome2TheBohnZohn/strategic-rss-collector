# Iran urges regional countries to prevent US use of their territory for attacks

Published: 2026-11-19 06:54:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326409/Iran-urges-regional-countries-to-prevent-US-use-of-their-territory

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry has defended the Armed Forces’ response to the US attacks as an exercise of the country’s inherent right to self-defense, urging regional countries to prevent Washington from using their territory and military bases for operations against Iran.
