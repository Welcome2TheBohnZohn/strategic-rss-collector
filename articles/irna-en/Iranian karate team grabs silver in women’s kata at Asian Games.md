# Iranian karate team grabs silver in women’s kata at Asian Games

Published: 2026-09-22 08:49:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271061/Iranian-karate-team-grabs-silver-in-women-s-kata-at-Asian-Games

Nagoya, IRNA — The Iranian women's national karate kata team has settled for the silver medal after losing to Vietnam in the final at the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, Japan.
