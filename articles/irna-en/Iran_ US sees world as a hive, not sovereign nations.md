# Iran: US sees world as a hive, not sovereign nations

Published: 2026-09-18 14:14:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267380/Iran-US-sees-world-as-a-hive-not-sovereign-nations

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesman says Washington still assumes nations lack memory and critical thought, treating the world as a ‘Pluribus’ hive where minds switch off, eyes lock onto Washington’s lips, and its narrative is swallowed whole.
