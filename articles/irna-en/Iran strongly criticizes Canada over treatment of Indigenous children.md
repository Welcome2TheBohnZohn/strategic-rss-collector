# Iran strongly criticizes Canada over treatment of Indigenous children

Published: 2026-10-02 12:46:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280966/Iran-strongly-criticizes-Canada-over-treatment-of-Indigenous

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesman has stressed that reconciliation over Canada’s Indigenous children cannot be reduced to a commemorative day or political rhetoric, saying serious questions about residential schools remain unanswered.
