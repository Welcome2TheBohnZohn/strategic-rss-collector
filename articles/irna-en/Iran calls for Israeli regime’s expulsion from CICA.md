# Iran calls for Israeli regime’s expulsion from CICA

Published: 2026-10-15 16:29:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293728/Iran-calls-for-Israeli-regime-s-expulsion-from-CICA

Tehran, IRNA — Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs, Kazem Gharibabadi, has described the Israeli regime as the main source of insecurity and instability in Asia, particularly West Asia, calling for its crimes to be condemned and for it to be expelled from the CICA mechanism.
