# Historic Army-IRGC solidarity must be documented for future generations: Spokesperson Mohebi

Published: 2026-09-15 08:19:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264559/Historic-Army-IRGC-solidarity-must-be-documented-for-future-generations

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), emphasizes that the profound solidarity between Iran’s Army and the IRGC must be preserved and documented for future generations.
