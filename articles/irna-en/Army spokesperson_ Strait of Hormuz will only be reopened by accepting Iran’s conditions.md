# Army spokesperson: Strait of Hormuz will only be reopened by accepting Iran’s conditions

Published: 2026-11-16 21:54:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323922/Army-spokesperson-Strait-of-Hormuz-will-only-be-reopened-by

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – Iranian Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia, while emphasizing the complete dominance of the armed forces over the Strait of Hormuz, says that the reopening of this strategic waterway is only possible with the acceptance of the Islamic Republic of Iran’s conditions by the United States, and any renewed aggression by the enemy will be met with an immediate, crushing response that goes beyond the geographical borders of the region.
