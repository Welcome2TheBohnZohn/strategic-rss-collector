# Army spokesperson: Strait of Hormuz will only be reopened by accepting Iran’s conditions

Published: 2026-11-16 21:54:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323922/Army-spokesperson-Strait-of-Hormuz-will-only-be-reopened-by

Bandar Abbas, IRNA — An Iranian Army spokesperson asserted the Armed Forces’ complete dominance over the Strait of Hormuz, saying the reopening of this strategic waterway depends on the US accepting Iran’s conditions. He added that any renewed aggression by the enemy would be met with an immediate, overwhelming response that extends beyond the region’s geographical borders.
