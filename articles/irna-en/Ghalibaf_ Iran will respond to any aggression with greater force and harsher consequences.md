# Ghalibaf: Iran will respond to any aggression with greater force and harsher consequences

Published: 2026-09-06 10:30:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86256429/Ghalibaf-Iran-will-respond-to-any-aggression-with-greater-force

Tehran, IRNA — Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has warned that any aggression against Iran’s interests and security will receive a faster, heavier, and more painful response.
