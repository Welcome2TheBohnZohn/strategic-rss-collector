# Over $2.3 bn in goods exported from Iran’ Bushehr Province: Official

Published: 2026-09-13 06:34:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262324/Over-2-3-bn-in-goods-exported-from-Iran-Bushehr-Province-Official

Bushehr, IRNA — The head of the provincial customs office of Bushehr says 2.364  billion dollars worth of goods have been exported from the province’s customs offices in the first five months of this Iranian calendar year (March 20 – August 20).
