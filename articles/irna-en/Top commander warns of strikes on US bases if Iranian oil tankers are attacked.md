# Top commander warns of strikes on US bases if Iranian oil tankers are attacked

Published: 2026-09-08 21:02:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258958/Top-commander-warns-of-strikes-on-US-bases-if-Iranian-oil-tankers

Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has warned the United States that any attack on Iranian oil tankers would trigger strikes on US bases across the region.
