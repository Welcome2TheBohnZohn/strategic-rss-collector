# Iranian Foreign Ministry: Center linked to French embassy closed after many warnings

Published: 2026-09-21 19:56:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270561/Iranian-Foreign-Ministry-Center-linked-to-French-embassy-closed

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian foreign ministry has strongly rejected as untrue claims made by the French Foreign Ministry regarding the closure of the language center affiliated with the French Embassy.
