# IRGC commander: Iranian Armed Forces disrupted enemy calculations

Published: 2026-11-09 20:04:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86317326/IRGC-commander-Pride-inspiring-operations-of-Iranian-Armed-Forces

Tehran, IRNA – The commander of the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) Ground Force says that the pride-inspiring operations of the Iranian Armed Forces, especially the IRGC’s Aerospace Force, during the second and third imposed wars, disrupted the excessive demands and arrogant calculations of the American-Israeli enemy.
