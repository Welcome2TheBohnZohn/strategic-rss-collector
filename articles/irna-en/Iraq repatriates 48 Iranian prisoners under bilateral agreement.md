# Iraq repatriates 48 Iranian prisoners under bilateral agreement

Published: 2026-09-15 12:55:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264937/Iraq-repatriates-48-Iranian-prisoners-under-bilateral-agreement

Tehran, IRNA – The Director-General of Consular Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran, Mojtaba Shasti Karimi, says Iraq has repatriated 48 Iranian prisoners under a bilateral agreement on the transfer of sentenced prisoners.
