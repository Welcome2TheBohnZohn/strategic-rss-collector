# Iranian athletes scoop medals at World Kurash Championships

Published: 2026-11-08 05:20:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315347/Iranian-athletes-scoop-medals-at-World-Kurash-Championships

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian kurash fighters have won six bronze medals on the opening day of the 15th World Kurash Championships in Astana, Kazakhstan.
