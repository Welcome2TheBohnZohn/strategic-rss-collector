# Leader’s sincere response to a Letter from a ten-year-old Student

Published: 2026-11-02 17:15:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310543/Leader-s-sincere-response-to-a-Letter-from-a-ten-year-old-Student

Tehran, IRNA – Following a sincere and affectionate letter sent by a ten-year-old student from Tehran to the Leader of the Revolution; in response to the student’s expression of affection, Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei wrote a letter to the student and also presented a gift in return.
