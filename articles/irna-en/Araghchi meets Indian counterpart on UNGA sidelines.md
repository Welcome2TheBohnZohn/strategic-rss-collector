# Araghchi meets Indian counterpart on UNGA sidelines

Published: 2026-09-26 08:12:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274681/Araghchi-meets-Indian-counterpart-on-UNGA-sidelines

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchgi and his Indian counterpart, Subrahmanyam Jaishankar, have held talks on the sidelines of the 81st UN General Assembly session in New York.
