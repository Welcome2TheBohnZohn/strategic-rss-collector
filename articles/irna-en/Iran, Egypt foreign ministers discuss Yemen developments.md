# Iran, Egypt foreign ministers discuss Yemen developments

Published: 2026-10-13 11:27:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291673/Iran-Egypt-foreign-ministers-discuss-Yemen-developments

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Egyptian counterpart, Badr Abdelatty, have discussed regional developments, including the situation in Yemen, and diplomatic efforts to reduce tensions.
