# Iran’s U23 wrestling triumph in US reflects national strength: Parliament speaker

Published: 2026-10-19 07:49:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296902/Iran-s-U23-wrestling-triumph-in-US-reflects-national-strength

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has described Iran’s dominant victory at the U23 World Wrestling Championships in the United States as a demonstration of the nation’s strength and determination.
