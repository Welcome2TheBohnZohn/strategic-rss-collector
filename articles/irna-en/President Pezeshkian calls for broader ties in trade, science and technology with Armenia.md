# President Pezeshkian calls for broader ties in trade, science and technology with Armenia

Published: 2026-10-05 18:37:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284288/President-Pezeshkian-calls-for-broader-ties-in-trade-science

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian said Iran and Armenia should further expand their longstanding friendly relations, describing Armenians living in Iran as respected and honest people and an important asset in strengthening ties between the two countries.
