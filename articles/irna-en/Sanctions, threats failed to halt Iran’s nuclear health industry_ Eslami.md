# Sanctions, threats failed to halt Iran’s nuclear health industry: Eslami

Published: 2026-10-08 08:48:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286729/Sanctions-threats-failed-to-halt-Iran-s-nuclear-health-industry

Tehran, IRNA – Head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) Mohammad Eslami said sanctions, threats and attacks against the country’s nuclear industry have failed to halt its progress in the field of health, stressing that Iran has maintained its advances at the frontiers of science.
