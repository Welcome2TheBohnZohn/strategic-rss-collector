# Baqaei recalls Minab school martyrs on first day of new school year

Published: 2026-09-23 14:18:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272723/Baqaei-recalls-Minab-school-martyrs-on-first-day-of-new-school

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has marked the first day of the new school year by honoring the memory of the Minab school students massacared in a US-Israeli attack, saying the angels of Minab will forever remain in the hearts of Iranians.
