# IRGC destroyed US early-warning radar in Bahrain

Published: 2026-11-13 05:28:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320332/IRGC-destroyed-US-early-warning-radar-in-Bahrain

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced that its Aerospace Force targeted and destroyed a US military early-warning radar located on the island of Bahrain.
