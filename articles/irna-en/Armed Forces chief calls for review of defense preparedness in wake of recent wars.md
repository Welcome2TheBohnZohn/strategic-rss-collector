# Armed Forces chief calls for review of defense preparedness in wake of recent wars

Published: 2026-10-19 16:30:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297486/Armed-Forces-chief-calls-for-review-of-defense-preparedness-in

Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of Iran's Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has called for a review of defense preparedness in light of recent wars, urging the Armed Forces to enhance its defense capabilities based on lessons learned and in line with the hybrid, technological, and intelligence-driven nature of future wars.
