# Iran will continue path of resistance, avenging martyred Leader: FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-08-29 05:59:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248952/Iran-will-continue-path-of-resistance-avenging-martyred-Leader

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the movement to avenge the martyrdom of the Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei will continue, stressing that the country will resist aggression and pressure by relying on independence, domestic capabilities and national dignity.
