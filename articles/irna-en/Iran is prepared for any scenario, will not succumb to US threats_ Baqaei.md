# Iran is prepared for any scenario, will not succumb to US threats: Baqaei

Published: 2026-10-25 17:04:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303173/Iran-is-prepared-for-any-scenario-will-not-succumb-to-US-threats

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has reiterated Iran’s resolve to defend itself against any aggression, saying that while the country keeps the door open to diplomacy, it is fully prepared to confront any scenario.
