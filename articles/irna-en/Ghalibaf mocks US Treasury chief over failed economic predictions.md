# Ghalibaf mocks US Treasury chief over failed economic predictions

Published: 2026-10-14 19:18:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293134/Ghalibaf-mocks-US-Treasury-chief-over-failed-economic-predictions

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has mocked US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent over his repeated deadlines for Iran’s economic collapse, saying the missed predictions could earn him a Nobel Prize in Physics for proving that time could be irrelevant.
