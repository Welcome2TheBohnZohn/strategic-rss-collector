# President Pezeshkian departs Moscow for Tehran

Published: 2026-10-27 20:23:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305375/President-Pezeshkian-departs-Moscow-for-Tehran

Moscow, IRNA – Masoud Pezeshkian, the President of the Islamic Republic of Iran, departed Moscow for Tehran a few moments ago, following his participation in and address to the 8th Summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF).
