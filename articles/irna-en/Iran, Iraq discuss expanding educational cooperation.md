# Iran, Iraq discuss expanding educational cooperation

Published: 2026-10-05 15:12:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284164/Iran-Iraq-discuss-expanding-educational-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Iran and Iraq have the potential to expand cooperation in education, Education Minister Alireza Kazemi said on Monday during a meeting with Iraqi Ambassador to Tehran Yasser Abdul Zahra Al-Hajjaj.
