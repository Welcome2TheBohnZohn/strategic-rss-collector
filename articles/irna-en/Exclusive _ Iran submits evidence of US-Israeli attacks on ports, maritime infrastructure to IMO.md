# Exclusive | Iran submits evidence of US-Israeli attacks on ports, maritime infrastructure to IMO

Published: 2026-11-01 10:42:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309209/Exclusive-Iran-submits-evidence-of-US-Israeli-attacks-on-ports

London, IRNA — Iran has submitted documents and photographic evidence of US and Israeli attacks on the country’s ports, civilian vessels, and maritime infrastructure to the International Maritime Organization (IMO), warning that the damage poses serious risks to maritime safety across the Persian Gulf, the Strait of Hormuz, and the Sea of Oman.
