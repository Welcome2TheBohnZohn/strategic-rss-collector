# Iran: Armed forces have full control over Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-09-08 15:57:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258764/Iran-Armed-forces-have-full-control-over-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Deputy Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) reiterated that Iran’s armed forces maintain full command and control over the Strait of Hormuz.
