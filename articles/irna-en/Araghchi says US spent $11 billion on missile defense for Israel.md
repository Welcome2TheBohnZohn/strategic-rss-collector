# Araghchi says US spent $11 billion on missile defense for Israel

Published: 2026-10-23 07:18:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300832/Araghchi-says-US-spent-11-billion-on-missile-defense-for-Israel

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the United States has spent $11 billion in taxpayer money on missile defense for Israel over the past three years.
