# IRGC deputy commander warns of decisive response to any aggression against Iran

Published: 2026-11-13 07:56:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320436/IRGC-deputy-commander-warns-of-decisive-response-to-any-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – The Deputy Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has warned that Iran’s Armed Forces will deliver an immediate, effective, and decisive response to any aggression, stressing that they face no geographical limits in confronting aggressors.
