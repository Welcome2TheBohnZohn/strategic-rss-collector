# Iran steps up international efforts to lift flight restrictions

Published: 2026-09-28 10:49:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277161/Iran-steps-up-international-efforts-to-lift-flight-restrictions

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Civil Aviation Organization is pursuing diplomatic and legal measures to restore suspended flight routes, including a formal complaint filed with the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO), the Iranian organization’s head Abuzar Shiroudi says.
