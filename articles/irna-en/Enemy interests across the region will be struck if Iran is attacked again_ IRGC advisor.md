# Enemy interests across the region will be struck if Iran is attacked again: IRGC advisor

Published: 2026-10-11 15:23:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289769/Enemy-interests-across-the-region-will-be-struck-if-Iran-is-attacked

Birjand, IRNA – A senior advisor to the commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has warned that Iran would target the interests and infrastructure of its adversaries in the region if attacks against the country are repeated.
