# Pezeshkian warns against return of unilateralism, urges BRICS to defend multilateralism

Published: 2026-09-12 13:01:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261956/Pezeshkian-warns-against-return-of-unilateralism-urges-BRICS

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, addressing the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi, has once again called for stronger multilateralism and global justice, warning that the return of unilateralism would weaken the very foundation of global governance.
