# Indonesia backs Iran, calls for end to war

Published: 2026-10-26 08:31:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303546/Indonesia-backs-Iran-calls-for-end-to-war

Tehran, IRNA — Indonesia supports Iran amid the ongoing war and calls for an end to hostilities, says Rolliansyah (Roy) Soemirat, Indonesian Ambassador to Iran.
