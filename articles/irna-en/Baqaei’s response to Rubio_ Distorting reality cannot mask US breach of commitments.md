# Baqaei’s response to Rubio: Distorting reality cannot mask US breach of commitments

Published: 2026-10-07 21:40:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286529/Baqaei-s-response-to-Rubio-Distorting-reality-cannot-mask-US

Tehran, IRNA – In response to the US Secretary of State's claim that Iran has squandered the opportunity for an agreement, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei stressed that distorting historical facts cannot erase the reality of the US's repeated breaches of commitment from the public mind.
