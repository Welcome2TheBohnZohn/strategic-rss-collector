# Leader says Anvari fulfilled his duty to Islamic Revolution through ‘Road of War’ novel

Published: 2026-11-11 10:29:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319075/Leader-says-Anvari-fulfilled-his-duty-to-Islamic-Revolution-through

Tehran, IRNA — Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has said that Iranian writer Mansour Anvari fulfilled his duty to the Islamic Revolution through his literary works, particularly his 12-volume novel “Jade-ye Jang” (Road of War).
