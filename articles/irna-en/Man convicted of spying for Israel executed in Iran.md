# Man convicted of spying for Israel executed in Iran

Published: 2026-09-19 08:57:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267915/Man-convicted-of-spying-for-Israel-executed-in-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – An individual convicted of espionage and intelligence cooperation with Israel has been executed in Iran after providing sensitive information about military and missile sites in Isfahan Province to Israel’s Mossad, the Judiciary’s media center reports.
