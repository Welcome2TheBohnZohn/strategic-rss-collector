# Vietnam, Iran’s Kashan discuss trade ties

Published: 2026-11-01 09:48:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309049/Vietnam-Iran-s-Kashan-discuss-trade-ties

Kashan, IRNA – Vietnam’s ambassador to Iran has highlighted the tourism, industrial, and economic potential of Kashan, a historic city in central Iran, saying it offers opportunities for cooperation between Vietnam and Iran.
