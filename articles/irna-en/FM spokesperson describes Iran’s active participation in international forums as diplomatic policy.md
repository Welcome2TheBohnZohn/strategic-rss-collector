# FM spokesperson describes Iran’s active participation in international forums as diplomatic policy

Published: 2026-10-15 13:12:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293627/FM-spokesperson-describes-Iran-s-active-participation-in-international

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has referred to Iranian officials’ participation in three diplomatic events, emphasizing that active participation in international forums to elaborate the country’s positions is part of Iran’s diplomatic policy.
