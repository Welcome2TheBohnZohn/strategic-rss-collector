# Iran, Russia Chief Justices meet on sidelines of BRICS summit

Published: 2026-09-04 14:21:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254825/Iran-Russia-Chief-Justices-meet-on-sidelines-of-BRICS-summit

Moscow, IRNA — In a meeting with Iran’s Judiciary Chief, Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei, the head of Russia’s Supreme Court praised the Iranian nation’s resilience and courage in the face of aggression and reaffirmed Moscow’s support for Tehran.
