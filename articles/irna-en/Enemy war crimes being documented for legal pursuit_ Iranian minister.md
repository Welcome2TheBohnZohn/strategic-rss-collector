# Enemy war crimes being documented for legal pursuit: Iranian minister

Published: 2026-09-08 14:48:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258754/Enemy-war-crimes-being-documented-for-legal-pursuit-Iranian

Hamedan, IRNA – Minister of Justice Amin Hossein Rahimi says the crimes committed during the US-Israeli imposed war on Iran, and the material damage are being documented for lawsuits as well as, judicial and legal follow-up at international forums.
