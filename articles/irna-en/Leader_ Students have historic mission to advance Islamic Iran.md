# Leader: Students have historic mission to advance Islamic Iran

Published: 2026-09-22 16:16:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271609/Leader-Students-have-historic-mission-to-advance-Islamic-Iran

Tehran, IRNA — Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei, the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, in a message on Tuesday, said the students and scholars have a historic mission to elevate Islamic Iran by acquiring knowledge, piety, hope, ethics, wisdom and capability while overcoming the boundaries of knowledge and advancing scientific progress.
