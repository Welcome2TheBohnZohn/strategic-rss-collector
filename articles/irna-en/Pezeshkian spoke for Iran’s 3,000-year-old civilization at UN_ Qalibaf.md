# Pezeshkian spoke for Iran’s 3,000-year-old civilization at UN: Qalibaf

Published: 2026-09-23 19:47:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272964/Pezeshkian-spoke-for-Iran-s-3-000-year-old-civilization-at-UN

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has lauded President Masoud Pezeshkian’s address to the UN General Assembly, saying the speech reflected Iran’s national strength and its 3,000-year-old civilization.
