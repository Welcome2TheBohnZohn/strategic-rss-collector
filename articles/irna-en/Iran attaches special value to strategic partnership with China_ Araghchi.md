# Iran attaches special value to strategic partnership with China: Araghchi

Published: 2026-09-17 06:43:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266410/Iran-attaches-special-value-to-strategic-partnership-with-China

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Iran attaches special importance and value to its strategic partnership with China, stressing that decisions made today will affect the region’s future strategic balance.
