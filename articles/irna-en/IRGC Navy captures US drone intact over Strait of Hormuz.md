# IRGC Navy captures US drone intact over Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-29 10:23:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306801/IRGC-Navy-captures-US-drone-intact-over-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has captured a US reconnaissance and decoy drone intact west of the Strait of Hormuz after taking control of its guidance and control systems.
