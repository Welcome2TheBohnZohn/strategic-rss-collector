# Iran adopts faster response, expanded operational reach under new directives: Army spokesperson

Published: 2026-11-09 10:29:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316892/Iran-adopts-faster-response-expanded-operational-reach-under

Tehran, IRNA — Iran's Army spokesperson, Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia, says the latest directives issued by Commander-in-Chief Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei emphasize rapid responses, operational effectiveness, and the removal of geographic limits, adding that these principles now form the basis for the Armed Forces’ actions.
