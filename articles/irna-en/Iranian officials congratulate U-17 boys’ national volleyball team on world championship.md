# Iranian officials congratulate U-17 boys’ national volleyball team on world championship

Published: 2026-08-30 07:24:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249952/Iranian-officials-congratulate-U-17-boys-national-volleyball

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian and Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, in separate messages, have congratulated Iran’s Under-17 Boys’ National Volleyball Team on winning the world championship.
