# Pezeshkian says Iran is ready for talks, but rejects coercion and bullying

Published: 2026-09-26 12:08:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275074/Pezeshkian-says-Iran-is-ready-for-talks-but-rejects-coercion

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran engages in dialogue to resolve issues but will never accept bullying or the use of force, and will never allow anyone to force it into submission.
