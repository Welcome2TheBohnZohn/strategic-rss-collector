# Iran will take all necessary measures to break US blockade: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-11-21 16:47:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328605/Iran-will-take-all-necessary-measures-to-break-US-blockade-Deputy

Tehran, IRNA – Iran considers the US economic blockade imposed on the country an act of war and will take all necessary measures to break it, Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi said on Saturday, stressing that such restrictions are unacceptable to the Islamic Republic.
