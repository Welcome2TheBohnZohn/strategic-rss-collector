# Araghchi meets Pakistan’s Army chief to discuss regional security

Published: 2026-11-16 16:05:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323759/Araghchi-meets-Pakistan-s-Army-chief-to-discuss-regional-security

Islamabad, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met with Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, Chief of Army Staff of Pakistan, as part of his bilateral meetings on the sidelines of the ECO Council of Foreign Ministers summit held in Islamabad.
