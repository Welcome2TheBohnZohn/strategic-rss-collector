# IRGC Spokesperson: Yemen’s victories rewards of years of resistance

Published: 2026-09-12 09:32:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261681/IRGC-Spokesperson-Yemen-s-victories-rewards-of-years-of-resistance

Tehran, IRNA — The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, says the Yemeni Ansarullah’s victories are the fruit of years of steadfastness in the toughest arenas.
