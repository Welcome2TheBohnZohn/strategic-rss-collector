# Russian official calls for stronger Tehran-Moscow ties to protect shared interests

Published: 2026-11-01 17:10:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309494/Russian-official-calls-for-stronger-Tehran-Moscow-ties-to-protect

Ahvaz, IRNA – The head of the Russian-Iranian Council for Trade Relations, Rajab Safarov, has stressed the need to expand bilateral cooperation, saying closer ties in economic, political, and security fields would strengthen the two countries’ positions and protect their shared interests.
