# President Pezeshkian hails Iran-Turkmenistan ties, hopes for closer cooperation

Published: 2026-09-27 07:59:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275898/President-Pezeshkian-hails-Iran-Turkmenistan-ties-hopes-for

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated Turkmenistan’s national leader and president on their country’s Independence Day, expressing hope that bilateral relations will further expand in line with the interests of both nations.
