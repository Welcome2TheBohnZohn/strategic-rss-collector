# IRGC Navy reports blast in third tanker in Hormuz Strait

Published: 2026-11-10 19:54:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318445/IRGC-Navy-reports-blast-in-third-tanker-in-Hormuz-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that a third oil tanker attempting to cross an unauthorized route through the Strait of Hormuz detonated after hitting a naval mine on Tuesday afternoon.
