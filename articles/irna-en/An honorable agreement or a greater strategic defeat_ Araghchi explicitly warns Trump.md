# An honorable agreement or a greater strategic defeat: Araghchi explicitly warns Trump

Published: 2026-10-21 14:11:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299652/An-honorable-agreement-or-a-greater-strategic-defeat-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, referring to the US president's repeated threats, has warned Donald Trump that his delusion regarding Iran’s military and nation would result in nothing, but hundreds of billions of dollars in losses for the American people and yet another severe strategic defeat.
