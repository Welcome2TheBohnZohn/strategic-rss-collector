# Iran deems new UK sanctions illegal and contrary to shared interests of two countries

Published: 2026-09-08 21:48:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258974/Iran-deems-new-UK-sanctions-illegal-and-contrary-to-shared-interests

London, IRNA – Expressing deep regret over the British government's decision to impose fresh sanctions on the Iranian people, the Embassy of the Islamic Republic of Iran in London condemned the move as illegal and contrary to the two nations' shared interests, warning that London's alignment with the US policy of economic terrorism would further heighten the wall of mistrust between the two countries.
