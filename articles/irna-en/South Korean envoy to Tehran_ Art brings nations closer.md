# South Korean envoy to Tehran: Art brings nations closer

Published: 2026-11-07 10:23:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314832/South-Korean-envoy-to-Tehran-Art-brings-nations-closer

Tehran, IRNA – South Korean Ambassador to Iran, Kim Junpyo, says that art transcends borders and brings nations closer, describing it as a common language that fosters friendship and cultural understanding.
