# Iran says US bid to block its IAEA conference committee membership fails

Published: 2026-09-15 16:04:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265112/Iran-says-US-bid-to-block-its-IAEA-conference-committee-membership

Tehran, IRNA – Iran says that a US effort to prevent it from joining a committee of the International Atomic Energy Agency’s General Conference had failed, with Tehran securing significantly more votes in a subsequent ballot.
