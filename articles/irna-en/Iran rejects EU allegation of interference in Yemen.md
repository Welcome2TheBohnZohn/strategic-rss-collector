# Iran rejects EU allegation of interference in Yemen

Published: 2026-11-05 13:56:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313450/Iran-rejects-EU-allegation-of-interference-in-Yemen

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for Iran’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs has strongly rejected the claim made by the EU’s foreign policy chief regarding Iran’s interference in the Saudi–Yemen conflict.
