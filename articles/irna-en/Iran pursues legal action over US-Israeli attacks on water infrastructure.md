# Iran pursues legal action over US-Israeli attacks on water infrastructure

Published: 2026-11-15 14:04:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322621/Iran-pursues-legal-action-over-US-Israeli-attacks-on-water-infrastructure

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has formally submitted reports documenting attacks on its water supply facilities and damage to more than 410 points across the country’s drinking water network to international bodies, the head of the National Water and Wastewater Engineering Company said on Sunday.
