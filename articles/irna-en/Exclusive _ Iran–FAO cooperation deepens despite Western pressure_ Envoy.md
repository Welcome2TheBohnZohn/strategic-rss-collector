# Exclusive | Iran–FAO cooperation deepens despite Western pressure: Envoy

Published: 2026-10-10 09:44:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288302/Exclusive-Iran-FAO-cooperation-deepens-despite-Western-pressure

London, IRNA — Iran’s cooperation with the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) continues to expand despite Western sanctions and political pressure, with the organization ready to help strengthen the country’s food security, Iran’s permanent representative to the agency says.
