# Envoy says IAEA BoG resolution not effective

Published: 2026-09-12 08:44:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261510/Envoy-says-IAEA-BoG-resolution-not-effective

London, IRNA — Iran’s permanent ambassador to the United Nations and other international organizations in Vienna, Reza Najafi, has criticized Western nations for politicizing the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) Board of Governors and stressed that there wouldn’t be any actual effect from this resolution.
