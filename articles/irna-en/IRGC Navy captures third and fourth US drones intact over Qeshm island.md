# IRGC Navy captures third and fourth US drones intact over Qeshm island

Published: 2026-11-02 21:48:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310669/IRGC-Navy-captures-third-and-fourth-US-drones-intact-over-Qeshm

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has captured the fourth US reconnaissance and decoy drone intact west of the Strait of Hormuz after taking control of its guidance and control systems.
