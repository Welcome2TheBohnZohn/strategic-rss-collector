# Iran, Pakistan explore ways to expand mining cooperation

Published: 2026-10-13 12:26:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291740/Iran-Pakistan-explore-ways-to-expand-mining-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – The Deputy Minister of Industry, Mining, and Trade, who also serves as the Head of Iran’s Trade Promotion Organization (TPO), has met with the Managing Director of the Pakistan Mineral Development Corporation to discuss ways to expand trade and economic relations between the two countries and utilize existing capacities to enhance cooperation in the mining sector.
