# Pakistani PM to visit Russia, Iran to invite presidents to SCO summit in Islamabad: Reports

Published: 2026-11-22 19:03:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329654/Pakistani-PM-to-visit-Russia-Iran-to-invite-presidents-to-SCO

Islamabad, IRNA - Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif will make key visits to Russia and Iran, where he will invite the presidents of both countries to attend the upcoming Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit of heads of state in Islamabad, according to reports.
