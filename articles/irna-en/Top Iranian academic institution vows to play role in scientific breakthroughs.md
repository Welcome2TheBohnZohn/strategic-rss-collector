# Top Iranian academic institution vows to play role in scientific breakthroughs

Published: 2026-10-27 18:15:09

Tehran, IRNA – Following the Leader of the Islamic Revolution’s call to push the boundaries of scientific knowledge, the head of Iran’s Academic Center for Education, Culture and Research (ACECR) stressed the institution’s readiness to contribute to the country's scientific surge and achieve Iran's scientific authority.
