# IRGC official warns of more surprises if US aggression continues

Published: 2026-09-09 10:29:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259503/IRGC-official-warns-of-more-surprises-if-US-aggression-continues

Tehran, IRNA – Brigadier General Ramezan Sharif, advisor to the chief commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), has said if the enemy continues its aggression and miscalculations against Iran, it should expect more unprecedented Iranian responses that will again alter the region’s strategic dynamics in the country’s favor.
