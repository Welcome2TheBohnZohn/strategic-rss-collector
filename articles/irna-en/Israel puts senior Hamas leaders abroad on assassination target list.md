# Israel puts senior Hamas leaders abroad on assassination target list

Published: 2026-09-29 16:20:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278623/Israel-puts-senior-Hamas-leaders-abroad-on-assassination-target

Tehran, IRNA – Israel has placed senior Hamas leaders living outside the Gaza Strip on a list of assassination targets, Israeli minister in charge of military affairs said, according to Al Arabiya.
