# Iran warns aggressors against underestimating its strength

Published: 2026-10-23 08:24:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300887/Iran-warns-aggressors-against-underestimating-its-strength

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmail Baqaei has warned Iran’s adversaries against underestimating the country’s defensive capabilities, invoking the writings of an 11th-century Persian historian to argue that any expectation of an easy victory would end in defeat and humiliation.
