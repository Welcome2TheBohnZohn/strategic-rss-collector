# Iran’s nuclear policy unchanged; deterrence debate natural after attacks on nuclear sites: Iran

Published: 2026-09-14 10:33:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263740/Iran-s-nuclear-policy-unchanged-deterrence-debate-natural-after

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s nuclear policy remains clear, and discussions about nuclear deterrence are not unusual after repeated attacks and acts of sabotage targeting the country’s nuclear facilities, Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei says, rejecting claims that Tehran has changed its nuclear doctrine or is considering withdrawal from the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT).
