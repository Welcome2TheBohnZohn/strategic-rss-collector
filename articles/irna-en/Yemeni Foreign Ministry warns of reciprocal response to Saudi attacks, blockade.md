# Yemeni Foreign Ministry warns of reciprocal response to Saudi attacks, blockade

Published: 2026-10-03 16:08:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282055/Yemeni-Foreign-Ministry-warns-of-reciprocal-response-to-Saudi

Tehran, IRNA – The Yemeni Foreign Ministry, responding to the fresh Saudi aggression against the country, has emphasized the need for continuing resistance and retaliatory strikes aimed at lifting the illegal blockade on Yemen.
