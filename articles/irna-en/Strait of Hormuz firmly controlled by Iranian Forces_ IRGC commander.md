# Strait of Hormuz firmly controlled by Iranian Forces: IRGC commander

Published: 2026-09-08 10:48:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258370/Strait-of-Hormuz-firmly-controlled-by-Iranian-Forces-IRGC-commander

Birjand, IRNA – Major General Mostafa Izadi, Deputy Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), says IRGC firmly controls the Strait of Hormuz and Iran’s Armed Forces remain fully prepared in southern regions.
