# Iran: US and Israeli regime bombed IAEA Statute and nuclear safety rules

Published: 2026-09-07 19:35:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257875/Iran-US-and-Israeli-regime-bombed-IAEA-Statute-and-nuclear-safety

London, IRNA – At the meeting of the IAEA Board of Governors, the Islamic Republic of Iran condemned the aggression by the United States and the Israeli regime against the country's peaceful, safeguards-subjected nuclear facilities, emphasizing that through these attacks, the aggressors bombed the UN Charter, the IAEA Statute, and fundamental nuclear safety rules.
