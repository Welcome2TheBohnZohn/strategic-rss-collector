# Bus crash in western Iran kills 12, injures 16

Published: 2026-11-15 06:05:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321950/Bus-crash-in-western-Iran-kills-12-injures-16

Ilam, IRNA – Twelve people were killed, and sixteen others were injured when a bus carrying oil company employees overturned in Ilam Province, western Iran, the governor of Abdanan County says.
