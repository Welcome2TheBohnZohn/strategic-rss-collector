# Iran’s people seek peace but will not bow to coercion: President Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-11-03 19:22:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311728/Iran-s-people-seek-peace-but-will-not-bow-to-coercion-President

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has reaffirmed Tehran’s commitment to peaceful engagement with the international community, stressing that the country will defend its rights and interests under international law and will not accept coercion or externally imposed demands.
