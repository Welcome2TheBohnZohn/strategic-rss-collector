# Iran’s missile development will not be halted by threats or sanctions: IRGC chief

Published: 2026-11-12 07:02:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319708/Iran-s-missile-development-will-not-be-halted-by-threats-or-sanctions

Tehran, IRNA – Commander-in-Chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Major General Ahmad Vahidi says Iran’s efforts to strengthen its military capabilities will continue despite threats and sanctions, stressing that the path pioneered by the late missile commander Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam cannot be stopped.
