# US-destroyed Minab school to be fully equipped with smart technology: Iran

Published: 2026-09-23 08:32:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272269/US-destroyed-Minab-school-to-be-fully-equipped-with-smart-technology

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy President for Science, Technology, and Knowledge-Based Economy Hossein Afshin says Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab, Hormozgan Province, will be fully equipped with smart technology.
