# Iran’s new envoy to Italy submits copy of his credentials

Published: 2026-10-31 09:59:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308219/Iran-s-new-envoy-to-Italy-submits-copy-of-his-credentials

London, IRNA — Ali Akbar Rezaei, the new Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Italy, has submitted a copy of his credentials to Bruno Antonio Pasquino, the Head of the Diplomatic Protocol of the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
