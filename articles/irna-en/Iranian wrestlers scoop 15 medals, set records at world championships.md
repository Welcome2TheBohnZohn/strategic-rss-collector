# Iranian wrestlers scoop 15 medals, set records at world championships

Published: 2026-11-02 06:09:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309727/Iranian-wrestlers-scoop-15-medals-set-records-at-world-championships

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s freestyle and Greco-Roman teams won 15 medals, including six gold, four silver and five bronze, at the 2026 World Wrestling Championships in Kazakhstan. Iran was the only nation to reach the team podium in both disciplines.
