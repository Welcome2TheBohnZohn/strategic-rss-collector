# Leader congratulates Iran’s U23 Greco-Roman wrestling team on world title

Published: 2026-10-17 13:08:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295168/Leader-congratulates-Iran-s-U23-Greco-Roman-wrestling-team-on

Tehran, IRNA – The Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei, has congratulated the Greco-Roman wrestling team on winning the championship title at the 2026 U23 World Greco-Roman Wrestling Championships held in Las Vegas, USA.
