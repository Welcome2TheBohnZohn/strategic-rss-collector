# Yemeni army launches drone attack on Saudi-affiliated militia positions in Taiz

Published: 2026-09-05 20:24:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255902/Yemeni-army-launches-drone-attack-on-Saudi-affiliated-militia

Tehran, IRNA – The Yemeni armed forces launched several drones at positions held by Saudi-backed militias whole at the same time, an explosion was heard in the city of Taiz.
