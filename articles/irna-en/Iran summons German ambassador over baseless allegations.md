# Iran summons German ambassador over baseless allegations

Published: 2026-10-19 18:46:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297588/Iran-summons-German-ambassador-over-baseless-allegations

Tehran, IRNA – The German ambassador to Tehran was summoned to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs due to the repeated, baseless accusations made by German officials; during the meeting, the Islamic Republic of Iran’s strong protest regarding the contemptible approach taken by certain German officials toward Iran was conveyed to him.
