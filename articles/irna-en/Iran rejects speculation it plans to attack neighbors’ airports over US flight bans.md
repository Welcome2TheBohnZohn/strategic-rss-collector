# Iran rejects speculation it plans to attack neighbors’ airports over US flight bans

Published: 2026-09-26 14:52:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275301/Iran-rejects-speculation-it-plans-to-attack-neighbors-airports

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) has denied claims that the country would respond militarily to recent aviation restrictions, saying negotiations with the relevant countries to lift the unlawful restrictions are being seriously pursued and followed up.
