# Iranian Red Crescent Society seeks status of missing pilots in Qatar

Published: 2026-09-14 11:48:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263858/Iranian-Red-Crescent-Society-seeks-status-of-missing-pilots-in

Tehran, IRNA — President of the Iranian Red Crescent Society (IRCS) Pir Hossein Kolivand, in a phone call, has urged the Qatar Red Crescent Society to use its channels to follow up on the status of three missing Iranian pilots.
