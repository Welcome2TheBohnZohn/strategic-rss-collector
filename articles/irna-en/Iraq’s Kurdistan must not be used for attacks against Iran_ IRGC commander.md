# Iraq’s Kurdistan must not be used for attacks against Iran: IRGC commander

Published: 2026-08-26 16:37:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247084/Iraq-s-Kurdistan-must-not-be-used-for-attacks-against-Iran-IRGC

Urmia, IRNA – The commander of Hamza Sayyid al-Shuhada Base of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Forces says Iran has no problem with its neighbors, including Iraq's semi-autonomous Kurdistan Region, but does not want their soil to be used for any aggression against the country.
