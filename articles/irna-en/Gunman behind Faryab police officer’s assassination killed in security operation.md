# Gunman behind Faryab police officer’s assassination killed in security operation

Published: 2026-10-10 16:30:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288788/Gunman-behind-Faryab-police-officer-s-assassination-killed-in

Faryab, IRNA – The governor of Faryab County, Kerman Province, Abolhassan Hosseini, has announced that the gunman who assassinated police officer Major Mehdi Jamshidi was killed in a security operation after intelligence work in his hideout.
