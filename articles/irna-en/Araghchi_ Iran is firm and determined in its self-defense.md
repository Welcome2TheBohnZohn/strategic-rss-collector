# Araghchi: Iran is firm and determined in its self-defense

Published: 2026-10-07 20:33:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286507/Araghchi-Iran-is-firm-and-determined-in-its-self-defense

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said the foreign ministry presented Iran's conditions and considerations to the mediators in the form of a precise and operational plan, and it became clear to everyone that the Islamic Republic of Iran is steadfast and determined in its self-defense.
