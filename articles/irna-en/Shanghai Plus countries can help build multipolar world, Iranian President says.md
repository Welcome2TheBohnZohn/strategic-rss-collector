# Shanghai Plus countries can help build multipolar world, Iranian President says

Published: 2026-09-01 12:13:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252204/Shanghai-Plus-countries-can-help-build-multipolar-world-Iranian

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has told member states, observers and partners of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization under the Shanghai Plus framework that they can play an important role in building a multipolar world based on multilateralism, sovereign equality and the rule of law.
