# Iran captures second US underwater drone in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-09-27 11:53:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276227/Iran-captures-second-US-underwater-drone-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced the seizure of a second unmanned underwater vehicle (UUV), belonging to "the US terrorist military" in the Strait of Hormuz.
