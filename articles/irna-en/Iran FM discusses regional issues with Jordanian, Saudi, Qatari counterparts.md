# Iran FM discusses regional issues with Jordanian, Saudi, Qatari counterparts

Published: 2026-11-05 14:13:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313468/Iran-FM-discusses-regional-issues-with-Jordanian-Saudi-Qatari
