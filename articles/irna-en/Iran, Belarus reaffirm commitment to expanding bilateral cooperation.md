# Iran, Belarus reaffirm commitment to expanding bilateral cooperation

Published: 2026-09-10 10:42:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260348/Iran-Belarus-reaffirm-commitment-to-expanding-bilateral-cooperation

Moscow, IRNA — Iran’s newly appointed Ambassador to Belarus, Mohsen Omid-Zamani, has presented a copy of his credentials to Belarusian Foreign Minister Maxim Ryzhenkov, reaffirming the two countries’ commitment to strengthening bilateral ties.
