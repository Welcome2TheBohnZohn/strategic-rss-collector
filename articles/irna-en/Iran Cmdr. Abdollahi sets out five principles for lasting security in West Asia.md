# Iran Cmdr. Abdollahi sets out five principles for lasting security in West Asia

Published: 2026-11-03 10:47:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311218/Iran-Cmdr-Abdollahi-sets-out-five-principles-for-lasting-security

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Armed Forces Chief of Staff Major General Ali Abdollahi has outlined five principles for peace and security in West Asia, including an end to conflicts, sanctions relief, and new regional security arrangements.
