# Finalists of Rasul Medal selected ahead of winners’ announcement in November

Published: 2026-09-30 10:22:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279341/Finalists-of-Rasul-Medal-selected-ahead-of-winners-announcement

Tehran, IRNA — The shortlist for the second edition of the Rasul Medal has been announced, with 24 finalists selected in six scientific fields ahead of the winners’ official announcement in November.
