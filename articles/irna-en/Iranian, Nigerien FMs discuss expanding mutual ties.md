# Iranian, Nigerien FMs discuss expanding mutual ties

Published: 2026-09-29 07:28:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277958/Iranian-Nigerien-FMs-discuss-expanding-mutual-ties

Tehran, IRNA –Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and his Nigerien counterpart, Bakary Yaou Sangare, have met on the sidelines of the 81st UN General Assembly, discussing bilateral relations and international developments.
