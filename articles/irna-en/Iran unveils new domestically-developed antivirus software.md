# Iran unveils new domestically-developed antivirus software

Published: 2026-09-07 08:37:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257215/Iran-unveils-new-domestically-developed-antivirus-software

Tehran, IRNA – Simultaneously with ELECOMP 2026, the country's largest exhibition for electronics, computer technologies, and e-commerce, Iran unveiled its second homegrown antivirus software, dubbed Ayyza, using artificial intelligence and machine learning to detect an unknown malware and unidentified cyber threats.
