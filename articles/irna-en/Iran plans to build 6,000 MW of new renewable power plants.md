# Iran plans to build 6,000 MW of new renewable power plants

Published: 2026-10-28 12:59:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306168/Iran-plans-to-build-6-000-MW-of-new-renewable-power-plants

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s deputy minister of energy says the ministry plans to construct 6,000 megawatts (MW) of new renewable power plants across the country.
