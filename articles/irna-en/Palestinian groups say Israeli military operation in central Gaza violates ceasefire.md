# Palestinian groups say Israeli military operation in central Gaza violates ceasefire

Published: 2026-11-18 16:24:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326151/Palestinian-groups-say-Israeli-military-operation-in-central

Tehran, IRNA – Palestinian Islamic Jihad and the Mujahideen Movement have condemned a new Israeli military operation in central Gaza, saying it violated the ceasefire agreement and risked further escalation.
