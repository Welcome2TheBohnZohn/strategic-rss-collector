# 'No to War' chants fill streets outside US Embassy in London

Published: 2026-11-07 16:39:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315150/No-to-War-chants-fill-streets-outside-US-Embassy-in-London

London, IRNA – Anti-war activists, civil society campaigners and dozens of Iranians living in Britain gathered outside the US Embassy in London on Saturday to protest against Washington’s military threats and hostile policies towards Iran.
