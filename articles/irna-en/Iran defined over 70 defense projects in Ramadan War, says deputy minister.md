# Iran defined over 70 defense projects in Ramadan War, says deputy minister

Published: 2026-11-18 10:39:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325720/Iran-defined-over-70-defense-projects-in-Ramadan-War-says-deputy

Tehran, IRNA — The deputy defense minister says that his ministry defined more than 70 projects across various fields during the third imposed war, focusing on translating battlefield developments and requirements into technology and operational capability.
