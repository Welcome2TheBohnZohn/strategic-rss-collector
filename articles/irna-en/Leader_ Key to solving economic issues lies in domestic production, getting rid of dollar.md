# Leader: Key to solving economic issues lies in domestic production, getting rid of dollar

Published: 2026-08-28 18:28:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248750/Leader-Key-to-solving-economic-issues-lies-in-domestic-production

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojataba Hosseini Khamenei hailed the administration of President Masoud Pezeshkian for handling the people’s needs amid the US-Israeli aggression and blockade, urging increased domestic production and eliminating the US dollar from transactions.
