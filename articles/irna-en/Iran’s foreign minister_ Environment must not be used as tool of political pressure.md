# Iran’s foreign minister: Environment must not be used as tool of political pressure

Published: 2026-10-11 10:17:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289468/Iran-s-foreign-minister-Environment-must-not-be-used-as-tool

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the environment is a shared heritage of humanity and must not be used as a tool of political pressure or sacrificed to short-term economic and military interests, stressing the importance of international law in protecting the environment during armed conflicts.
