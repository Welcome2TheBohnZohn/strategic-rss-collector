# Tehran will not bow to Washington’s dictates, Foreign Ministry spokesperson says

Published: 2026-09-01 13:40:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252330/Tehran-will-not-bow-to-Washington-s-dictates-Foreign-Ministry

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has stressed that Tehran bases its decisions on national interests and will not bow to Washington’s dictates or attempts to impose its excessive demands.
