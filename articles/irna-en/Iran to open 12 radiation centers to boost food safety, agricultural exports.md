# Iran to open 12 radiation centers to boost food safety, agricultural exports

Published: 2026-11-26 08:59:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333142/Iran-to-open-12-radiation-centers-to-boost-food-safety-agricultural

Rasht, IRNA – Iran plans to open 12 radiation-processing centers by the end of the current Iranian year (March 20, 2026), an Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) official has said as a new facility began operations in Gilan Province, northern Iran.
