# Aref: US cannot take any new action

Published: 2026-09-01 19:24:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252589/Aref-US-cannot-take-any-new-action

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref said what we are witnessing today is primarily a psychological operation aimed at influencing society and the market; the government has a plan to counter the economic war, the situation is being well managed, and conditions will improve in the future.
