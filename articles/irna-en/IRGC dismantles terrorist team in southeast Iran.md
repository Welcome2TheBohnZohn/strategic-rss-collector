# IRGC dismantles terrorist team in southeast Iran

Published: 2026-10-01 09:49:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280140/IRGC-dismantles-terrorist-team-in-southeast-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has dismantled a terrorist team in Iran’s southeastern province of Sistan and Baluchestan.
