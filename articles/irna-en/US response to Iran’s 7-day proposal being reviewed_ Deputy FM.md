# US response to Iran’s 7-day proposal being reviewed: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-10-04 14:52:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283103/US-response-to-Iran-s-7-day-proposal-being-reviewed-Deputy-FM
