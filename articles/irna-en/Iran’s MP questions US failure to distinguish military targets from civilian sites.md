# Iran’s MP questions US failure to distinguish military targets from civilian sites

Published: 2026-09-02 09:10:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253083/Iran-s-MP-questions-US-failure-to-distinguish-military-targets

Tehran, IRNA – Hasan Ghashghavi, spokesperson for the Iranian Parliament’s National Security and Foreign Policy Commission, has questioned how the United States can fail to distinguish between military targets and civilian sites, following a US attack on a wedding ceremony in Sirik, Hormuzgan Province.
