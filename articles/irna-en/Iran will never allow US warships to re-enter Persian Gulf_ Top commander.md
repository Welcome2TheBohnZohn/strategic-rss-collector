# Iran will never allow US warships to re-enter Persian Gulf: Top commander

Published: 2026-10-24 08:10:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301529/Iran-will-never-allow-US-warships-to-re-enter-Persian-Gulf-Top

Tehran, IRNA – Major General Ali Abdollahi, Chief of the General Staff of Iran’s Armed Forces, has vowed that Iran will never allow US warships to re-enter the Persian Gulf, saying American forces would not dare approach the region’s waters.
