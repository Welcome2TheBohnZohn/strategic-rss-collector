# Iran–China friendship rooted in historical, civilizational ties, ambassador says

Published: 2026-10-31 07:22:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307919/Iran-China-friendship-rooted-in-historical-civilizational-ties

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador to China has described the friendship between Tehran and Beijing as extending beyond conventional political and economic relations, saying that historical and civilizational bonds between the two nations provide a solid foundation for closer ties.
