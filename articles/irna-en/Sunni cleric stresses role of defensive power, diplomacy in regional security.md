# Sunni cleric stresses role of defensive power, diplomacy in regional security

Published: 2026-09-26 14:15:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275192/Sunni-cleric-stresses-role-of-defensive-power-diplomacy-in-regional

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – A prominent Sunni cleric in Hormozgan Province in southern Iran, Sheikh Hossein Bazmandegan, has stressed that lasting regional security depends on a combination of defensive capabilities, national cohesion, political prudence, and active diplomacy.
