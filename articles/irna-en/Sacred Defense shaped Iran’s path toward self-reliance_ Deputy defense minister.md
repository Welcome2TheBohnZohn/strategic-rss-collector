# Sacred Defense shaped Iran’s path toward self-reliance: Deputy defense minister

Published: 2026-09-21 11:55:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270193/Sacred-Defense-shaped-Iran-s-path-toward-self-reliance-Deputy

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Acting Defense Minister Brigadier General Majid Ibn al-Reza has said the eight-year Sacred Defense was more than a historical period of resistance, describing it as a school of experience, self-confidence, sacrifice and responsibility that shaped the country’s subsequent defense path.
