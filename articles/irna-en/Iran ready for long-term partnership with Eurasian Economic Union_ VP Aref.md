# Iran ready for long-term partnership with Eurasian Economic Union: VP Aref

Published: 2026-10-03 07:22:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281356/Iran-ready-for-long-term-partnership-with-Eurasian-Economic-Union

Moscow, IRNA – Iran is ready to play an active, constructive and responsible role alongside member states of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU), First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref said in a message to the EAEU Intergovernmental Council’s meeting.
