# In photos: Funeral procession for Ayatollah Shobeiri Zanjani in Iran’s Qom

Published: 2026-09-23 10:28:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86272513/In-photos-Funeral-procession-for-Ayatollah-Shobeiri-Zanjani

The funeral procession for Ayatollah Seyyed Mousa Shobeiri Zanjani was held in Qom on Wednesday, September 23, 2026, with the participation of large crowds of people, scholars, and his students. His remains were laid to rest near the shrine of Hazrat Fatima Masoumeh (SA).
