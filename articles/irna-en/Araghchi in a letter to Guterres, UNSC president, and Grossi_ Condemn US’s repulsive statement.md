# Araghchi in a letter to Guterres, UNSC president, and Grossi: Condemn US’s repulsive statement

Published: 2026-11-10 21:33:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318470/Araghchi-in-a-letter-to-Guterres-UNSC-president-and-Grossi

New York, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, following an announcement by the US Department of State offering a reward of up to $20 million for information regarding Iran’s nuclear program, in a letter addressed to the UN secretary-general, the president of the Security Council, and the Director General of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), has for a clear response and the condemnation of such announcement.
