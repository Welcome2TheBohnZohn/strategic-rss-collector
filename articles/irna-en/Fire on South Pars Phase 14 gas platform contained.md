# Fire on South Pars Phase 14 gas platform contained

Published: 2026-11-10 21:52:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318479/Fire-on-South-Pars-Phase-14-gas-platform-contained

Bushehr, IRNA – A fire on one of the gas platforms in Phase 14 of the South Pars Field was contained thanks to the timely intervention of operational and safety personnel, and all staff on the platform were evacuated safely.
