# Leader’s aide highlights strategic role of Iranian oil industry in nation’s power equation

Published: 2026-09-27 15:03:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276371/Leader-s-aide-highlights-strategic-role-of-Iranian-oil-industry

Tehran, IRNA – A senior military advisor to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, says Iran's power equation is determined not only in the public arena, but also in the energy sector, emphasizing that the oil industry constitutes a part of national strength and security.
