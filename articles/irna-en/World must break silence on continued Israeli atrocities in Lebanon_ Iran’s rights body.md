# World must break silence on continued Israeli atrocities in Lebanon: Iran’s rights body

Published: 2026-09-13 14:50:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263014/World-must-break-silence-on-continued-Israeli-atrocities-in-Lebanon

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s High Council for Human Rights (HCHR) has strongly condemned the Israeli regime's continued airstrikes and shelling of residential areas and crimes against the people of Lebanon.
