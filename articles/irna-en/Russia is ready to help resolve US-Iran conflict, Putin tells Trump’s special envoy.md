# Russia is ready to help resolve US-Iran conflict, Putin tells Trump’s special envoy

Published: 2026-10-21 12:09:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299537/Russia-is-ready-to-help-resolve-US-Iran-conflict-Putin-tells

Moscow, IRNA – Russian President Vladimir Putin, in a meeting with Donald Trump’s special envoy, has expressed Moscow's willingness to assist in resolving the conflict between Iran and the United States.
