# Iran tightens security measures in Strait of Hormuz: Army

Published: 2026-11-25 10:02:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332270/Iran-tightens-security-measures-in-Strait-of-Hormuz-Army

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia says the military had stepped up surveillance and control of the Strait of Hormuz, warning that vessels must coordinate their passage with the Iranian Armed Forces to ensure safe transit.
