# Gharibabadi heads to Baku for CICA summit

Published: 2026-10-14 12:33:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292873/Gharibabadi-heads-to-Baku-for-CICA-summit

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs, Kazem Gharibabadi, has departed for Baku to attend the Conference on Interaction and Confidence-Building Measures in Asia (CICA) summit.
