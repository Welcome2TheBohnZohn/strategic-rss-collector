# Pezeshkian: Universities bear key responsibility for progress, solving problems

Published: 2026-09-24 12:09:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273455/Pezeshkian-Universities-bear-key-responsibility-for-progress

Tehran, IRNA – Congratulating the start of the Iranian academic year, President Masoud Pezeshkian has said universities, along with other executive bodies, bear a major responsibility for national progress, reconstruction, and solving the country’s problems.
