# Iran calls for stronger regional transport and rail cooperation

Published: 2026-10-15 15:05:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293683/Iran-calls-for-stronger-regional-transport-and-rail-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Minister of Roads and Urban Development, Farzaneh Sadegh, has emphasized the need to protect civilian infrastructure, maintain regional transit, remove border barriers, and enhance rail cooperation.
