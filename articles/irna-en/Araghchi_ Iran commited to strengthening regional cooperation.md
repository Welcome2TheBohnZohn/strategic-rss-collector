# Araghchi: Iran commited to strengthening regional cooperation

Published: 2026-09-25 19:01:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274332/Araghchi-Iran-commited-to-strengthening-regional-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said that Iran is committed to strengthening regional cooperation and solidarity, and believes that ECO can play a more active role in advancing economic and trade cooperation, regional connectivity, and sustainable progress among member states.
