# Araghchi returns to Tehran after talks with Turkmen officials

Published: 2026-11-02 16:11:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310503/Araghchi-returns-to-Tehran-after-talks-with-Turkmen-officials

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi returned to Tehran on Monday after a one-day visit to Ashgabat, where he held diplomatic talks with senior Turkmen officials on bilateral relations and ways to expand cooperation.
