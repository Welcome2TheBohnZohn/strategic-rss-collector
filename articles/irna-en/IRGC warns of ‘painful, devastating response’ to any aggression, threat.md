# IRGC warns of ‘painful, devastating response’ to any aggression, threat

Published: 2026-10-02 14:02:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281010/IRGC-warns-of-painful-devastating-response-to-any-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) has warned, “The era of cost-free threats is over; any aggression will be met with a painful and devastating response.”
