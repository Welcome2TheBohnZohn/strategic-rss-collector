# Iran’s Parliament to pursue US war crimes in Lamerd through legal channels: MP

Published: 2026-08-26 12:52:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86246865/Iran-s-Parliament-to-pursue-US-war-crimes-in-Lamerd-through-legal

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Parliament, in cooperation with the Judiciary, will pursue the legal and international cases arising from US war crimes in Lamerd, alongside the US attack in Minab, Iranian Parliament Deputy Speaker Ali Nikzad says.
