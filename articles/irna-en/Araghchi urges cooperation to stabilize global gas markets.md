# Araghchi urges cooperation to stabilize global gas markets

Published: 2026-10-26 07:38:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303576/Araghchi-urges-cooperation-to-stabilize-global-gas-markets

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has emphasized that constructive dialogue, responsible conduct and mutual fulfilment of commitments can ease tensions, ensure safe shipping and help stabilize global energy markets, arguing that the outcome depends on the United States’ political choices.
