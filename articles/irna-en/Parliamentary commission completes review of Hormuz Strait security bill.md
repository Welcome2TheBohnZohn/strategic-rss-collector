# Parliamentary commission completes review of Hormuz Strait security bill

Published: 2026-09-27 16:04:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276399/Parliamentary-commission-completes-review-of-Hormuz-Strait-security

Tehran, IRNA – The Parliament’s National Security and Foreign Policy Commission has completed its review of a strategic bill aimed at securing the Strait of Hormuz and ensuring sustainable development in the waterway and the Persian Gulf, the commission’s spokesperson said on Sunday.
