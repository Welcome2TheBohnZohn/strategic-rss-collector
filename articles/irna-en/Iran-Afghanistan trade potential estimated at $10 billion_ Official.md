# Iran-Afghanistan trade potential estimated at $10 billion: Official

Published: 2026-11-04 12:16:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312534/Iran-Afghanistan-trade-potential-estimated-at-10-billion-Official

Mashhad, IRNA – Iran and Afghanistan have the potential to expand their bilateral trade to around $10 billion, although actual economic exchanges are already considerably higher than official figures suggest, the head of the Iran-Afghanistan Joint Chamber of Commerce said.
