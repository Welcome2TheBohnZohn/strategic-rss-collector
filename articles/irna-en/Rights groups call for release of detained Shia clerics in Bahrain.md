# Rights groups call for release of detained Shia clerics in Bahrain

Published: 2026-11-18 16:27:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326078/Rights-groups-call-for-release-of-detained-Shia-clerics-in-Bahrain

Tehran, IRNA – Twenty human rights organizations have called for the release of detainees and the dropping of charges related to peaceful religious activities in Bahrain, expressing concern over the mass trial of Shia clerics and allegations of torture and forced confessions.
