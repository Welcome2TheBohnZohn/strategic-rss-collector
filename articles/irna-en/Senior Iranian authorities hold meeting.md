# Senior Iranian authorities hold meeting

Published: 2026-10-11 12:43:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86289649/Senior-Iranian-authorities-hold-meeting

The heads of Iran’s three branches of government met on October 11, 2026, at a session hosted by President Pezeshkian, with Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf and Judiciary Chief Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei, to review the country’s latest developments and discuss key ongoing issues.
