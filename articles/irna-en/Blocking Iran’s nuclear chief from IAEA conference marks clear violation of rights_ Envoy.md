# Blocking Iran’s nuclear chief from IAEA conference marks clear violation of rights: Envoy

Published: 2026-09-14 11:44:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263805/Blocking-Iran-s-nuclear-chief-from-IAEA-conference-marks-clear

London, IRNA — Iran’s permanent representative to international organizations in Vienna, Reza Najafi, has described preventing the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) chief, Mohammad Eslami, from attending the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) General Conference as a clear violation of Iran’s rights.
