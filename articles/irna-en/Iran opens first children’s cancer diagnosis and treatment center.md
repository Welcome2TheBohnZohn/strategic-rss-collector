# Iran opens first children’s cancer diagnosis and treatment center

Published: 2026-10-08 09:36:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286861/Iran-opens-first-children-s-cancer-diagnosis-and-treatment-center

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has inaugurated its first university-based center for the diagnosis and treatment of childhood cancer at Hakim Children’s Hospital in Tehran, with Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref and Health Minister Mohammad-Reza Zafarghandi in attendance.
