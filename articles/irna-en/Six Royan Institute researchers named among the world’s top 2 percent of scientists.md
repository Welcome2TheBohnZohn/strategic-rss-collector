# Six Royan Institute researchers named among the world’s top 2 percent of scientists

Published: 2026-10-13 08:45:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291487/Six-Royan-Institute-researchers-named-among-the-world-s-top-2

In the latest international assessment of researchers’ scientific performance for 2025, six researchers from Royan Institute, affiliated with the Academic Center for Education, Culture and Research (ACECR), have been included in the list of the World’s Top 2% Scientists.
