# Iran’s strength lies in steadfastness, resilience of people: Baqaei

Published: 2026-10-29 16:44:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307063/Iran-s-strength-lies-in-steadfastness-resilience-of-people

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei, referring to Iran’s prudence and intelligence in confrontation with its enemies, says the country’s current authority is the result of steadfastness, intelligence, and the active presence of the people.
