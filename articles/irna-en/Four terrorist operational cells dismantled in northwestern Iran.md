# Four terrorist operational cells dismantled in northwestern Iran

Published: 2026-11-23 11:46:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330264/Four-terrorist-operational-cells-dismantled-in-northwestern-Iran

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence has announced the dismantling of four more terrorist-separatist cells and the arrest of over two dozen operatives in a coordinated anti-terror operation in West Azerbaijan Province.
