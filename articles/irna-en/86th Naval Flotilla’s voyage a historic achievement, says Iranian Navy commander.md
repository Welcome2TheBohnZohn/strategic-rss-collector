# 86th Naval Flotilla’s voyage a historic achievement, says Iranian Navy commander

Published: 2026-11-01 13:58:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309355/86th-Naval-Flotilla-s-voyage-a-historic-achievement-says-Iranian

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Navy commander Rear Admiral Shahram Irani has described the 86th Naval Flotilla’s voyage as a historic achievement, saying it was the result of months of planning.
