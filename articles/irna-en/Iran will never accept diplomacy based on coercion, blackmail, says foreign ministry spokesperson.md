# Iran will never accept diplomacy based on coercion, blackmail, says foreign ministry spokesperson

Published: 2026-11-03 14:18:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311501/Iran-will-never-accept-diplomacy-based-on-coercion-blackmail

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will never accept diplomacy based on coercion, blackmail or the imposition of unilateral demands, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said, stressing that Tehran regards diplomacy, alongside defensive capabilities, as an essential instrument for safeguarding its national interests.
