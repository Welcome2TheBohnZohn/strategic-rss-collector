# Iran: Zionist regime poses gravest threat to Lebanon’s independence, stability

Published: 2026-11-25 14:57:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332624/Iran-Zionist-regime-poses-gravest-threat-to-Lebanon-s-independence

Tehran, IRNA – Iran's Foreign Ministry has strongly condemned the Israeli aggression against Lebanon, stating that the attacks—coinciding with the country's Independence Day— show the Zionist regime poses the greatest threat to its independence, territorial integrity, and stability.
