Published: 2026-09-20 07:18:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268694/Ghalibaf-calls-Sacred-Defense-Week-tested-experience-for-safeguarding

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says Sacred Defense Week is not merely a recollection of a historical event but a tested experience for safeguarding Iran’s independence and national dignity against enemy aggression.
