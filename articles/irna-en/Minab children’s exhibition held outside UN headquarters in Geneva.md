# Minab children’s exhibition held outside UN headquarters in Geneva

Published: 2026-09-21 13:50:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270367/Minab-children-s-exhibition-held-outside-UN-headquarters-in-Geneva

London, IRNA – An exhibition highlighting the impact of a deadly late February US attack on a school in Minab, southern Iran, has been held outside the United Nations headquarters in Geneva.
