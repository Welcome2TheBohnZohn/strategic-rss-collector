# Iran says all-front ceasefire essential to ending regional war

Published: 2026-10-17 07:38:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294695/Iran-says-all-front-ceasefire-essential-to-ending-regional-war

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Deputy Foreign Minister Saeed Khatibzadeh says ending the war on all fronts is a key condition for peace, stressing that continued fighting in any part of the region would mean the war persists across the region.
