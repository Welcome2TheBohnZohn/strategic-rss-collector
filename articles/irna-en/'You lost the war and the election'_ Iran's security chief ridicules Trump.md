# 'You lost the war and the election': Iran's security chief ridicules Trump

Published: 2026-11-07 16:31:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315151/You-lost-the-war-and-the-election-Iran-s-security-chief-ridicules

Tehran, IRNA – Major General Mohsen Rezaei, Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC), has derided US President Donald Trump for launching an unlawful war, reminding him of the consequences of his aggressive policy, and warning him against any new military mischief.
