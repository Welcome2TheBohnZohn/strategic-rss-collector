# IRGC urges media to preserve Iran’s social cohesion

Published: 2026-09-09 10:47:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259564/IRGC-urges-media-to-preserve-Iran-s-social-cohesion

Arak, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has urged Iranian media outlets to preserve the social solidarity and public cohesion that emerged during the recent conflicts against the country.
