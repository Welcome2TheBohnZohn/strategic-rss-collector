# Pezeshkian: Iran’s defense and missile capabilities owe much to universities and schools

Published: 2026-10-17 09:08:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294840/Pezeshkian-Iran-s-defense-and-missile-capabilities-owe-much

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran’s defense and missile capabilities are indebted to the role played by the country’s schools and universities, stressing the need to invest in younger generations to strengthen national security.
