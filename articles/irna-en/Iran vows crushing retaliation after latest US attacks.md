# Iran vows crushing retaliation after latest US attacks

Published: 2026-09-01 19:06:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252580/Iran-vows-crushing-retaliation-after-latest-US-attacks

Tehran, IRNA – Iran's top military command has vowed to deliver "crushing and devastating" blows to the United States following a new wave of American airstrikes targeting military positions in the southern provinces.
