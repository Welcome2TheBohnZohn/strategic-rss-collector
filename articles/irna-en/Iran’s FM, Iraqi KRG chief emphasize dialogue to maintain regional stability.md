# Iran’s FM, Iraqi KRG chief emphasize dialogue to maintain regional stability

Published: 2026-11-12 16:12:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320128/Iran-s-FM-Iraqi-KRG-chief-emphasize-dialogue-to-maintain-regional

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and Iraqi Kurdistan Region President Nechirvan Barzani have exchanged views on the current regional and international developments, emphasizing the need for dialogue to maintain regional stability.
