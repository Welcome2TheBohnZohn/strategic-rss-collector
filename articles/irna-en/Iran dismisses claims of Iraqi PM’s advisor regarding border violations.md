# Iran dismisses claims of Iraqi PM’s advisor regarding border violations

Published: 2026-10-24 16:04:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302074/Iran-dismisses-claims-of-Iraqi-PM-s-advisor-regarding-border

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Embassy in Baghdad has refuted allegations leveled by an aide to Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi regarding the Al-Shib border crossing between the two countries.
