# Two more offending oil tankers catch fire east of Strait of Hormuz: Iran Army

Published: 2026-11-12 16:30:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320137/Two-more-offending-oil-tankers-catch-fire-east-of-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Iranian Army Navy has announced that two more oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines as they were entering a prohibited zone, east of the Strait of Hormuz.
