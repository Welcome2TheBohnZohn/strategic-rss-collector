# Russia seeks to learn from Iran’s wartime emergency response experience

Published: 2026-09-19 16:55:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268331/Russia-seeks-to-learn-from-Iran-s-wartime-emergency-response

Moscow, IRNA – Russia is interested in sending a delegation to Tehran to learn from Iran’s experience in protecting infrastructure and providing emergency services during wartime, Russian Emergency Situations Minister General Alexander Kurenkov said on Saturday.
