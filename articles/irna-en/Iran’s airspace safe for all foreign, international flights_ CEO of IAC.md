# Iran’s airspace safe for all foreign, international flights: CEO of IAC

Published: 2026-09-29 14:36:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278501/Iran-s-airspace-safe-for-all-foreign-international-flights

Zahedan, IRNA – The deputy minister of roads and urban development and CEO of the Iran Airport and Air Navigation Company (IAC), Morteza Dehqan, has emphasized that Iranian airspace is safe for all foreign and international flights, with all international standards and regulations fully observed.
