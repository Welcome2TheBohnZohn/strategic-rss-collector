# Araghchi: Europe must confront US aggression

Published: 2026-09-24 21:32:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273795/Araghchi-Europe-must-confront-US-aggression

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi continued an intensive diplomatic activity on the sidelines of the 81st United Nations General Assembly, holding talks with his counterpart from Spain, and placing Washington’s war of aggression, the insecurity it has manufactured in West Asia, and the Zionist regime’s ongoing occupation and genocide at the center of his discussions.
