# Iran: Trump’s annihilation threats made at UNGA are ‘grave and alarming’

Published: 2026-09-28 07:46:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276919/Iran-Trump-s-annihilation-threats-made-at-UNGA-are-grave-and

New York, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations in New York, Gholamhossein Darzi, says the latest threats made by US President Donald Trump to “annihilate” Iran are grave and alarming because they were issued from the podium of the United Nations.
