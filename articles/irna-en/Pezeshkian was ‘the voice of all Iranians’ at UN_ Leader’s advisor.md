# Pezeshkian was ‘the voice of all Iranians’ at UN: Leader’s advisor

Published: 2026-09-23 16:08:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272810/Pezeshkian-was-the-voice-of-all-Iranians-at-the-UN-Official

Tehran, IRNA – A senior advisor to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution has praised President Masoud Pezeshkian’s firm and well‑reasoned stance at the UN General Assembly, saying he has been a worthy representative and the voice of all Iranians.
