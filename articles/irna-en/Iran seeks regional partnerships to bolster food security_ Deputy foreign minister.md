# Iran seeks regional partnerships to bolster food security: Deputy foreign minister

Published: 2026-11-02 11:33:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310135/Iran-seeks-regional-partnerships-to-bolster-food-security-Deputy

Tehran, IRNA — Iran is ready to expand scientific, trade, and investment cooperation in agriculture with countries in the region and beyond, Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi says, describing agricultural diplomacy as a means of securing sustainable food supplies.
