# Pezeshkian: UN speech reflected collective wisdom of Reformist, Principlist parties

Published: 2026-09-30 11:25:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279388/Pezeshkian-UN-speech-reflected-collective-wisdom-of-Reformist

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian says his address to the UN General Assembly was the product of the collective wisdom of both Reformist and Principlist political parties, noting that he spoke firmly in defense of the Iranian people’s rights.
