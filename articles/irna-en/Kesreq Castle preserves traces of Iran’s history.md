# Kesreq Castle preserves traces of Iran’s history

Published: 2026-10-18 08:49:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295654/Kesreq-Castle-preserves-traces-of-Iran-s-history

Bojnurd, IRNA – Kesreq Esfarayen Castle, located in North Khorasan Province, northeastern Iran, dates back to the 5th century AH (11th century AD) and is among the historical sites preserving traces of the region’s medieval Islamic history.
