# Tehran, Baku stress expansion of infrastructure cooperation

Published: 2026-09-06 08:42:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86256212/Tehran-Baku-stress-expansion-of-infrastructure-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA — Minister of Roads and Urban Development Farzaneh Sadegh and Azerbaijan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Chairman of the Joint Economic Commission Shahin Mustafayev have discussed ways to expand economic and transit cooperation.
