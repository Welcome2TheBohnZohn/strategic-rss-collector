# IRGC commander condemns US ‘terror attack’ on wedding ceremony

Published: 2026-09-03 13:19:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254205/IRGC-commander-condemns-US-terror-attack-on-wedding-ceremony

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Commander-in-Chief Major General Ahmad Vahidi has condemned the US attack on a wedding in Kuhestak as a terrorist act, saying it reflects the enemies’ hostility and inability to break the Iranian people’s resolve.
