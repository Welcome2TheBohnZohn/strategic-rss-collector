# Six Iranian referees present at Nagoya canoeing events

Published: 2026-09-16 12:07:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265565/Six-Iranian-referees-present-at-Nagoya-canoeing-events

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Canoeing Federation has announced that it has six representatives in the officials and referees’ section of the canoe sprint and slalom competitions at the 20th Asian Games Aichi–Nagoya 2026.
