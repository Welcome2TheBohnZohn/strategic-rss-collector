# New US attack on Iran will have far-reaching consequences: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-10-13 13:59:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291855/New-US-attack-on-Iran-will-have-far-reaching-consequences-Deputy

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs, Kazem Gharibabadi, has warned that a renewed US military attack against Iran would have consequences extending beyond the Middle East.
