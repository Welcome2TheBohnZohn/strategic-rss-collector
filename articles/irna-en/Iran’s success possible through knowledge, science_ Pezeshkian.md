# Iran’s success possible through knowledge, science: Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-11-17 14:56:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86324865/Iran-s-success-possible-through-knowledge-science-Pezeshkian

President Masoud Pezeshkian has stressed that Iran’s dignity and progress depend on scientific knowledge and practical skills, and that science must be paired with a commitment to solving the problems of the people and the country.
