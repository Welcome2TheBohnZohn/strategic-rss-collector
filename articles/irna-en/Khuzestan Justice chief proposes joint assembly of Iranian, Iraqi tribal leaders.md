# Khuzestan Justice chief proposes joint assembly of Iranian, Iraqi tribal leaders

Published: 2026-11-08 15:41:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316120/Khuzestan-Justice-chief-proposes-joint-assembly-of-Iranian-Iraqi

Ahvaz, IRNA – The head of the Justice Department in Khuzestan Province has proposed the establishment of a joint assembly composed of tribal leaders from both Khuzestan and Iraq.
