# Araghchi stresses need for a responsible response from Islamic countries regarding violation of Iran's sovereignty

Published: 2026-09-07 21:04:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257907/Araghchi-stresses-need-for-a-responsible-response-from-Islamic

Tehran, IRNA – In a telephone conversation with the new Secretary-General of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi emphasized the need for Islamic countries to respond responsibly to the violation of Iran's sovereignty and territorial integrity, and to counter the use of force and aggression.
