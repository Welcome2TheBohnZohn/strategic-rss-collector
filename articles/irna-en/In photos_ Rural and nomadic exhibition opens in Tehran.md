# In photos: Rural and nomadic exhibition opens in Tehran

Published: 2026-10-10 10:47:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86288422/In-photos-Rural-and-nomadic-exhibition-opens-in-Tehran

The 6th International Exhibition of Rural and Nomadic Capabilities is taking place at the Tehran International Exhibition Centre from October 8 to 11, 2026. More than 400 booths have been set up to showcase the capacities of Iran’s provinces, while 50 booths are dedicated to startups and technology.
