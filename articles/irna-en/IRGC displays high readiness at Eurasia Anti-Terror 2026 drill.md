# IRGC displays high readiness at Eurasia Anti-Terror 2026 drill

Published: 2026-10-13 16:29:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291919/IRGC-displays-high-readiness-at-Eurasia-Anti-Terror-2026-drill
