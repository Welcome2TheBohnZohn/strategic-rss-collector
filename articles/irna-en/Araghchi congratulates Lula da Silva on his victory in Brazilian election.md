# Araghchi congratulates Lula da Silva on his victory in Brazilian election

Published: 2026-10-27 21:18:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305385/Araghchi-congratulates-Lula-da-Silva-on-his-victory-in-Brazilian

Tehran, IRNA – Abbas Araghchi, the Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, in a telephone conversation on Tuesday evening with Brazilian Foreign Minister Mauro Vieira, congratulated the incumbent president, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, on his victory in Brazil’s 2026 presidential election.
