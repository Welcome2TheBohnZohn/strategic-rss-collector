# Iran, Oman, Uzbekistan FMs discuss North-South Corridor capacities

Published: 2026-11-17 17:35:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325050/Iran-Oman-Uzbekistan-FMs-discuss-North-South-Corridor-capacities

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign ministers of Iran, Oman, and Uzbekistan held a trilateral meeting in Muscat on Tuesday, November 17, to discuss and exchange views on the capacities of the North-South Corridor.
