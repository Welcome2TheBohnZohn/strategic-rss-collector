# Minab school massacre symbolizes oppression inflicted on Iran: Minister

Published: 2026-09-14 14:18:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263958/Minab-school-massacre-symbolizes-oppression-inflicted-on-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Minister of Culture and Islamic Guidance Abbas Salehi has described the Minab school tragedy as a symbol of oppression inflicted on Iran, saying its international resonance could help deter future unjust wars.
