# Support for multilateralism, expanded trade, fight against unilateralism outcomes of BRICS summit: President

Published: 2026-09-13 16:12:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263097/Support-for-multilateralism-expanded-trade-fight-against-unilateralism

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian, reporting on his visit to India to attend the 18th BRICS Leaders’ Summit, has expressed hope that the grouping of 11 countries spanning four continents would work together to promote multilateralism and prevent the United States from realizing its sinister goals in the region and the world.
