# Araghchi urges expansion of ties with Iraq, Kazakhstan

Published: 2026-09-20 16:25:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269382/Araghchi-urges-expansion-of-ties-with-Iraq-Kazakhstan

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, in separate meetings with Iran’s new ambassador to Kazakhstan and new consul general in the Iraqi city of Karbala, urged for utilizing existing capacities to expand cooperation with the countries.
