# IRGC navy reports explosions on two tankers in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-08 06:06:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315378/IRGC-navy-reports-explosions-on-two-tankers-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has reported that two oil tankers caught fire following explosions in the Strait of Hormuz early Sunday.
