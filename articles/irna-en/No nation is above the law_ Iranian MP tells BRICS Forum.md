# No nation is above the law: Iranian MP tells BRICS Forum

Published: 2026-10-26 12:48:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304014/No-nation-is-above-the-law-Iranian-MP-tells-BRICS-Forum

Tehran, IRNA – The head of Iran's parliamentary delegation to the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum said the global order must be based on the rule of law, stressing that no country should consider itself above the law.
