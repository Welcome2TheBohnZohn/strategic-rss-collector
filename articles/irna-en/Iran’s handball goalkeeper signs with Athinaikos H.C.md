# Iran’s handball goalkeeper signs with Athinaikos H.C.

Published: 2026-09-27 07:24:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275790/Iran-s-handball-goalkeeper-signs-with-Athinaikos-H-C

Tehran, IRNA – Zahra Afshari, a promising 19-year-old goalkeeper for Iran’s national handball program, has signed a one-year contract with the Greek club Athinaikos H.C.
