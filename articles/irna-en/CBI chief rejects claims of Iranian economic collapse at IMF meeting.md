# CBI chief rejects claims of Iranian economic collapse at IMF meeting

Published: 2026-10-14 10:45:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292677/CBI-chief-rejects-claims-of-Iranian-economic-collapse-at-IMF

Tehran, IRNA – Central Bank of Iran Governor Abdolnaser Hemmati has dismissed claims that Iran’s economy is on the verge of collapse, saying it has shown considerable resilience despite the war waged by the United States and the Israeli regime as well as Washington’s maximum sanctions and economic pressure.
