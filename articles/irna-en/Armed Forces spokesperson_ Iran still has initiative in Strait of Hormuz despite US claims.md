# Armed Forces spokesperson: Iran still has initiative in Strait of Hormuz despite US claims

Published: 2026-09-19 22:21:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268447/Armed-Forces-spokesperson-Iran-still-has-initiative-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Armed Forces spokesperson Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi has strongly rejected the latest US claims about the Strait of Hormuz, saying the initiative in the strategic waterway is firmly in Iran’s hands.
