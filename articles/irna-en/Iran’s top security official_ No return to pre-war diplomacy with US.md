# Iran’s top security official: No return to pre-war diplomacy with US

Published: 2026-09-23 19:37:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272960/Iran-s-top-security-official-No-return-to-pre-war-diplomacy

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) Mohsen Rezaei has reiterated that Iran will not return to its pre-war diplomatic approach towardd the United States, calling on Washington to “take action” and meet Iran’s conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz.
