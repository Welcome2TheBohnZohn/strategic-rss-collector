# Iran Minister of Science departs for Riyadh

Published: 2026-09-15 10:02:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264741/Iran-Minister-of-Science-departs-for-Riyadh

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Minister of Science, Research and Technology, Hossein Simaei Saraf, has left Tehran for Riyadh at the head of a delegation to attend UNESCO’s artificial intelligence summit in the Saudi capital.
