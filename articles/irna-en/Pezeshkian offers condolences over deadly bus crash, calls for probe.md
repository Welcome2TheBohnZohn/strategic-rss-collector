# Pezeshkian offers condolences over deadly bus crash, calls for probe

Tehran, IRNA –President Masoud Pezeshkian has expressed condolences to the families of the victims of a bus crash in the western Iranian province of Ilam Province, that killed a dozen people and injured several more.
