# Senior MP: Iran’s steadfastness leaves US in strategic dead end

Published: 2026-11-14 06:03:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320961/Senior-MP-Iran-s-steadfastness-leaves-US-in-strategic-dead-end

Gorgan, IRNA — The chairman of the National Security and Foreign Policy Commission of Iran’s Parliament says Iran’s resistance across multiple fronts has driven the US into a strategic dead end, adding that should the enemy persist in its miscalculations, Iran’s response will be more regret-inducing than ever before.
