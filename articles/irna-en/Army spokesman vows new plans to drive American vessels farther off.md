# Army spokesman vows new plans to drive American vessels farther off

Published: 2026-10-16 14:23:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294199/Army-spokesman-vows-new-plans-to-drive-American-vessels-farther

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Army spokesman, commenting on the pullback of American vessels to over 500 kilometers from Iran’s coastline, says that new operational plans are underway and will soon compel US vessels to withdraw even farther from the region.
