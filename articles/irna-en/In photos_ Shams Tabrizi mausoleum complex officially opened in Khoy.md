# In photos: Shams Tabrizi mausoleum complex officially opened in Khoy

Published: 2026-10-09 07:44:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86287436/In-photos-Shams-Tabrizi-mausoleum-complex-officially-opened

The mausoleum complex of Shams Tabrizi was officially inaugurated on Wednesday evening, October 7, 2026, in the northwestern County of Khoy. The event was attended by Reza Salehi-Amiri, the Minister of Cultural Heritage, Tourism, and Handicrafts, along with several Iranian and foreign officials.
