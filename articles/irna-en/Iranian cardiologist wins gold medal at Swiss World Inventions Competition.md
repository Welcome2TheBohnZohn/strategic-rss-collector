# Iranian cardiologist wins gold medal at Swiss World Inventions Competition

Published: 2026-08-31 11:02:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251040/Iranian-cardiologist-wins-gold-medal-at-Swiss-World-Inventions

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian cardiologist and professor Azin Alizadeh-Asl has won a gold medal at the World Inventions Competition in Switzerland for an innovation aimed at improving transesophageal echocardiography images, the Ministry of Health says.
