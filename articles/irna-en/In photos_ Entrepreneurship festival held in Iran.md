# In photos: Entrepreneurship festival held in Iran

Published: 2026-09-06 07:14:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86256134/In-photos-Entrepreneurship-festival-held-in-Iran

The second edition of the Alavi Empowerment and Entrepreneurship Festival, along with a ceremony celebrating the launch of 150,000 employment projects, was held on September 5, 2026, at the Imam Khomeini Camp Complex in Lavasan, Tehran Province. Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref was in attendance.
