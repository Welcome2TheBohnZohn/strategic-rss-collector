# President Pezeshkian, Qatari Emir meet in Moscow, emphasize expanding energy cooperation

Published: 2026-10-27 10:02:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304898/President-Pezeshkian-Qatari-Emir-meet-in-Moscow-emphasize-expanding

Moscow, IRNA – Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian and Qatar’s Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani have met in Moscow to discuss ways to expand bilateral relations and strengthen joint cooperation in the energy sector.
