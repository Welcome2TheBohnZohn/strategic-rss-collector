# Iran warns of consequences of US, Israeli ‘unlawful threats’

Published: 2026-10-20 06:18:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297854/Iran-warns-of-consequences-of-US-Israeli-unlawful-threats

New York, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations in New York, Gholamhossein Darzi, has warned the UN secretary-general and the Security Council about US and Israeli threats of further aggression against Iran.
