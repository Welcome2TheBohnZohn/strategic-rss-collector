# Top commander: Iran and resistance fighters will deliver devastating response to any threat

Published: 2026-09-28 19:47:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277611/Top-commander-Iran-and-resistance-fighters-will-deliver-devastating

New York, IRNA – Chief of Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi in a message has warned that Iran's Armed Forces, alongside the fighters and combatants of the Islamic Resistance, stand fully prepared to deliver a decisive and devastating response to any threat against the Islamic Ummah.
