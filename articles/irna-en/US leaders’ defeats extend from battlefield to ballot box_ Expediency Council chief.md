# US leaders’ defeats extend from battlefield to ballot box: Expediency Council chief

Published: 2026-11-04 16:18:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312766/US-leaders-defeats-extend-from-battlefield-to-ballot-box-Expediency

Tehran, IRNA – Chairman of Iran’s Expediency Council Ayatollah Sadeq Amoli-Larijani has described the defeat of US President Donald Trump and Republicans in the midterm elections as humiliating, saying the series of defeats suffered by US leaders has extended from the battlefield to the ballot box.
