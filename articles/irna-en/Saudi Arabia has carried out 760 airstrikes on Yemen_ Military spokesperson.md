# Saudi Arabia has carried out 760 airstrikes on Yemen: Military spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-20 18:31:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269442/Saudi-Arabia-has-carried-out-760-airstrikes-on-Yemen-Military

Tehran, IRNA - Saudi Arabia has carried out 760 airstrikes on Yemen since it intensified its attacks against the country, the spokesman for the Yemeni armed forces said Sunday night.
