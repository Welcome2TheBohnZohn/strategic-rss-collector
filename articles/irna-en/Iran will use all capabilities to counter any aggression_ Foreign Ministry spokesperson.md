# Iran will use all capabilities to counter any aggression: Foreign Ministry spokesperson

Published: 2026-10-13 15:47:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291957/Iran-will-use-all-capabilities-to-counter-any-aggression-Foreign

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is determined to use all its capabilities to repel any aggression against the country, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said, stressing that the country’s operational experience in “three rounds of heroic defense” had strengthened its readiness to confront threats.
