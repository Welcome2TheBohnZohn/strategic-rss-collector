# Six more Palestinians killed in Gaza; death toll rises to 74,243

Published: 2026-10-05 12:09:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284018/Six-more-Palestinians-killed-in-Gaza-death-toll-rises-to-74-243

Tehran, IRNA – The total death toll in Gaza has risen to 74,243 since October 7, 2023, as six more Palestinians were martyred in the Zionist regime’s aggression over the past 24 hours, while 26 others were wounded.
