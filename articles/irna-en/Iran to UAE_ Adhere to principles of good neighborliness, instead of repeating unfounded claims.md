# Iran to UAE: Adhere to principles of good neighborliness, instead of repeating unfounded claims

Published: 2026-09-29 10:46:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278324/Iran-to-UAE-Adhere-to-principles-of-good-neighborliness-instead

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has categorically rejected statements made by the United Arab Emirates at the United Nations General Assembly regarding three Iranian islands in the Persian Gulf.
