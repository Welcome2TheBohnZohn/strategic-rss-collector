# Araghchi calls for balanced OIC resolution on Yemen

Published: 2026-10-18 10:15:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296062/Araghchi-calls-for-balanced-OIC-resolution-on-Yemen

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Tehran may openly object to a proposed resolution on Yemen at the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) if the final text fails to reflect fairness and balance, adding that Iran’s position will depend on the wording ultimately adopted.
