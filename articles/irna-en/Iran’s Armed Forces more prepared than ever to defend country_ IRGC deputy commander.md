# Iran’s Armed Forces more prepared than ever to defend country: IRGC deputy commander

Published: 2026-11-17 17:37:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324956/Iran-s-Armed-Forces-more-prepared-than-ever-to-defend-country

Sanandaj, IRNA – The Deputy Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has stressed Iran’s heightened defense readiness, saying the country’s Armed Forces, including the Army, IRGC, and Basij, are more prepared than ever to defend the country and safeguard national security.
