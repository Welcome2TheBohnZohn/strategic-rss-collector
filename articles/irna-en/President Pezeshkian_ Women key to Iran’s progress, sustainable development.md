# President Pezeshkian: Women key to Iran’s progress, sustainable development

Published: 2026-10-25 08:22:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302598/President-Pezeshkian-Women-key-to-Iran-s-progress-sustainable

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has described women as essential to Iran’s progress, saying sustainable development requires recognizing their capabilities and ensuring equal opportunities for effective participation across society.
