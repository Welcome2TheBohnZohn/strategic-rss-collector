# Iranian Army spokesperson: US facing strategic isolation

Published: 2026-10-15 06:39:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293244/Iranian-Army-spokesperson-US-facing-strategic-isolation

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akrami-Nia says the United States launched an illegal war against Iran with the aim of destroying Iranian-Islamic civilization, but has now found itself strategically isolated for the first time since World War II, with even its closest allies refusing to come to its aid.
