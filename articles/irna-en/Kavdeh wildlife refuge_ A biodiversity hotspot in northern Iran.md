# Kavdeh wildlife refuge: A biodiversity hotspot in northern Iran

Published: 2026-10-31 05:45:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86307831/Kavdeh-wildlife-refuge-A-biodiversity-hotspot-in-northern-Iran

Spanning approximately 74,000 hectares in Firuzkuh County, Kavdeh wildlife refuge is home to a wide variety of wildlife and birds of prey. Located at the ecological boundary between the Hyrcanian forests and the Irano-Turanian vegetation zone, the refuge supports diverse animal and plant species, highlighting the importance of conservation efforts to protect its natural habitats.
