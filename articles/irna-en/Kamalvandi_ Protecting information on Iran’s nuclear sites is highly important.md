# Kamalvandi: Protecting information on Iran’s nuclear sites is highly important

Published: 2026-09-16 11:27:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265956/Kamalvandi-Protecting-information-on-Iran-s-nuclear-sites-is

London, IRNA – Spokesperson for the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) Behrouz Kamalvandi has stressed the importance of protecting information about Iranian nuclear facilities, including damaged sites, citing the possibility of further attacks and the International Atomic Energy Agency’s (IAEA) failure to safeguard such information.
