# In photos: President Pezeshkian returns home from New York

Published: 2026-09-27 05:48:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86275678/In-photos-President-Pezeshkian-returns-home-from-New-York

President Masoud Pezeshkian returned to Tehran on Saturday evening following his address to the 81st UN General Assembly, touching down at Mehrabad Airport, where he was formally received by the Head of the Supreme Leader’s Office.
