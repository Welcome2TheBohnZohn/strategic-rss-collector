# Enemy spy drone downed off Iran’s southern coast: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-21 15:45:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328562/Enemy-spy-drone-downed-off-Iran-s-southern-coast-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA –The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced the interception and destruction of another enemy spy drone off the coast of Bushehr in the northern Persian Gulf.
