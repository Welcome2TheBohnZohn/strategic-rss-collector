# Araghchi calls for regional security framework free from foreign interference

Published: 2026-11-07 10:21:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314680/Araghchi-calls-for-regional-security-framework-free-from-foreign

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has stressed the need for regional security arrangements free from the involvement of extra-regional powers, raising the issue in phone talks with 12 regional counterparts over the past 48 hours.
