# Araghchi: US waged war on Iran on Israel’s behalf

Published: 2026-09-08 21:23:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258967/Araghchi-US-waged-war-on-Iran-on-Israel-s-behalf

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi mocked US policy towards Iran, saying Washington waged war on Tehran on behalf of Israel and resorted to further sanctions after both military action and sanctions failed to achieve their goals.
