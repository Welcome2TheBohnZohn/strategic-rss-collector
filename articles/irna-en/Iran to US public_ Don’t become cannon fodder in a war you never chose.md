# Iran to US public: Don’t become cannon fodder in a war you never chose

Published: 2026-09-05 05:47:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255058/Iran-to-US-public-Don-t-become-cannon-fodder-in-a-war-you-never

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has called on the American people to refuse to be recruited into the war against Iran and not become “cannon fodder” in a war they never chose, and that was instead waged by Israel.
