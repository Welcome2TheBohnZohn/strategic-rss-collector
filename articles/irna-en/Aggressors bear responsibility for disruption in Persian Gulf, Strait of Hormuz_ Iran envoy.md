# Aggressors bear responsibility for disruption in Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz: Iran envoy

Published: 2026-10-30 05:23:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307217/Aggressors-bear-responsibility-for-disruption-in-Persian-Gulf

New York, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations in New York Gholamhossein Darzi has said aggressors bear direct responsibility for any disruption, obstruction or interference with maritime navigation in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz.
