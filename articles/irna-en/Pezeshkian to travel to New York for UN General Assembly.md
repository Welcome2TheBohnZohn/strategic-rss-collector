# Pezeshkian to travel to New York for UN General Assembly

Published: 2026-09-21 09:12:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269961/Pezeshkian-to-travel-to-New-York-for-UN-General-Assembly

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian will travel to New York on Tuesday to attend the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly, the director-general of the Presidential Office’s Public Relations Department says.
