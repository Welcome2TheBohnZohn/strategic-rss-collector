# PGSA: Iran acts against illegal transit of ships in Persian Gulf

Published: 2026-11-11 11:50:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319184/PGSA-Iran-acts-against-illegal-transit-of-ships-in-Persian-Gulf

Tehran, IRNA – The Persian Gulf Strait Authority has announced that strict legal enforcement action has been taken against vessels attempting unauthorized transit using forged documentation.
