# IRGC downs MQ-1 drone over Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-29 06:27:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306575/IRGC-downs-MQ-1-drone-near-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced the interception and destruction of an American MQ-1 drone over the eastern Strait of Hormuz.
