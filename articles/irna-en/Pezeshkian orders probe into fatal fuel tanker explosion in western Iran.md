# Pezeshkian orders probe into fatal fuel tanker explosion in western Iran

Published: 2026-09-06 10:59:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86256553/Pezeshkian-orders-probe-into-fatal-fuel-tanker-explosion-in-western

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has ordered an immediate investigation into the causes of a fuel tanker explosion that killed 11 people and injured seven others on the Sanandaj-Hamedan road in western Iran.
