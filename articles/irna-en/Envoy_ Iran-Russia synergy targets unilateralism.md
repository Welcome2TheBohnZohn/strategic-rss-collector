# Envoy: Iran-Russia synergy targets unilateralism

Published: 2026-10-10 05:29:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287973/Envoy-Iran-Russia-synergy-targets-unilateralism

Moscow, IRNA — Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to the Russian Federation, Kazem Jalali, says that the goal of Tehran-Moscow synergy is to stand against unilateralism and contribute to the formation of a multipolar and just world.
