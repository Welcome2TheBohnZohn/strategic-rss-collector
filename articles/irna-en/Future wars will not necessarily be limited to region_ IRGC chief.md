# Future wars will not necessarily be limited to region: IRGC chief

Published: 2026-11-24 16:08:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331544/Future-wars-will-not-necessarily-be-limited-to-region-IRGC-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Commander-in-Chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Major General Ahmad Vahidi has emphasized that future wars will not necessarily be confined to the region, adding that Iran will continue to develop its missile capabilities based on its requirements.
