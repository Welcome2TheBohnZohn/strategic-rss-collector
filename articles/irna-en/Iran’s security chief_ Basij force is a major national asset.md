# Iran’s security chief: Basij force is a major national asset

Published: 2026-11-27 07:54:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333766/Iran-s-security-chief-Basij-force-is-a-major-national-asset

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) Major General Mohsen Rezaei has described Iran’s Basij force as a major national asset, saying its roots in the country’s history, culture, faith and public participation give it a distinction few nations possess.
