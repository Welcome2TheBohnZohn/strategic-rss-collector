# Iran emerged more united, stronger from recent war: Senior lawmaker

Published: 2026-09-15 08:47:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264606/Iran-emerged-more-united-stronger-from-recent-war-Senior-lawmaker

Mashhad, IRNA – Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad says the recent war failed to weaken or divide Iran; instead, it strengthened national unity and preserved the country’s defense and strategic capabilities.
