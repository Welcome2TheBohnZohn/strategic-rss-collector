# EAEU’s highest honor medal awarded to Iran’s industry minister

Published: 2026-10-15 11:02:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293541/EAEU-s-highest-honor-medal-awarded-to-Iran-s-industry-minister

Tehran, IRNA – The Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) has awarded its highest honor medal to Iran’s Minister of Industry, Mine and Trade Mohammad Atabak, in recognition of his efforts to expand economic cooperation and strengthen trade ties between Iran and the bloc’s member states.
