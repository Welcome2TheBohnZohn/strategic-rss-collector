# Iran: Strait of Hormuz remains closed, requires coordination for passage

Published: 2026-10-15 08:24:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293369/Iran-Strait-of-Hormuz-remains-closed-requires-coordination

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy chief of the Iranian Army for coordination Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari says the Strait of Hormuz remains closed and that any passage through the strategic waterway must be coordinated with Iranian forces who maintain full control and surveillance over the area.
