# Iranian girl secures country’s first quota for 2028 Olympics

Published: 2026-11-05 15:24:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313510/Iranian-girl-secures-country-s-first-quota-for-2028-Olympics

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian female athlete Hanieh Rostamian has secured the country’s first quota for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games in the women’s 10m air pistol competition at the World Championships in Doha.
