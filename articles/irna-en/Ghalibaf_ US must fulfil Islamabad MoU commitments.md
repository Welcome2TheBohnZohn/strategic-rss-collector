# Ghalibaf: US must fulfil Islamabad MoU commitments

Published: 2026-08-27 16:21:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248006/Ghalibaf-US-must-fulfil-Islamabad-MoU-commitments

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf reiterates that the US attempt for the economic siege of Iran will fail and that it is the US that must fulfill its commitments under Islamabad MoU.
