# Another offending tanker catches fire after striking mines east of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-02 14:29:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310422/Another-offending-tanker-catches-fire-after-striking-mines-east

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Army Navy says another non-compliant oil tanker caught fire on Monday morning local time, after striking mines while sailing through an unauthorized and unsafe route east of the Strait of Hormuz.
