# US targets two fishing boats off Qeshm; two crew members missing

Published: 2026-10-31 10:15:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308287/US-targets-two-fishing-boats-off-Qeshm-two-crew-members-missing

Bandar Abbas, IRNA – An attack by the US military on two fishing boats in waters off Qeshm Island, southern Iran, has left two crew members missing, Hormozgan Province Deputy Governor Ahmad Nafisi says.
