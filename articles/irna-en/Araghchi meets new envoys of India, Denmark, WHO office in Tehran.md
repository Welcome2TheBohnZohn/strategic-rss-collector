# Araghchi meets new envoys of India, Denmark, WHO office in Tehran

Published: 2026-09-09 07:44:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259289/Araghchi-meets-new-envoys-of-India-Denmark-WHO-office-in-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA – The new ambassadors of India and Denmark, along with the new representative of the World Health Organization (WHO) office in Iran, have met with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi at the beginning of their missions.
