# Iran expresses sympathy with Nepal over deadly floods, landslide

Published: 2026-08-27 11:43:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247764/Iran-expresses-sympathy-with-Nepal-over-deadly-floods-landslide

Tehran, IRNA – Following the deadly floods and landslides in Nepal leaving heavy casualties, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has expressed sympathy with the government and people of Nepal.
