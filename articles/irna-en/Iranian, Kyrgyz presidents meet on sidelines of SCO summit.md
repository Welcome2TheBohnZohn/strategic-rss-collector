# Iranian, Kyrgyz presidents meet on sidelines of SCO summit

Published: 2026-08-31 13:18:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251207/Iranian-Kyrgyz-presidents-meet-on-sidelines-of-SCO-summit

TEHRAN, Iran — Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Kyrgyz President Sadyr Japarov meet on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization and Shanghai Plus summits in Kyrgyzstan to discuss the latest state of bilateral relations and ways to strengthen cooperation.
