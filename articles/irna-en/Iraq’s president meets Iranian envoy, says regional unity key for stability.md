# Iraq’s president meets Iranian envoy, says regional unity key for stability

Published: 2026-11-15 17:58:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322811/Iraq-s-president-meets-Iranian-envoy-says-regional-unity-key

Tehran, IRNA - Iraqi President Nizar al-Amidi has described regional stability as a vital priority requiring coordinated efforts and unified stances among the peoples of the region, his office said.
