# Iran demonstrated strategic resilience despite US attacks, says Judiciary chief

Published: 2026-09-04 09:44:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254637/Iran-demonstrated-strategic-resilience-despite-US-attacks-says

Tehran, IRNA – Judiciary Chief Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei says that Iran has faced illegal attacks by the United States during the current year but has demonstrated its strategic resilience and stability through the strong presence of its people and their comprehensive support for the country and its officials.
