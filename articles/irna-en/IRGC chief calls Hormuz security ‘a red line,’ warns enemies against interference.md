# IRGC chief calls Hormuz security ‘a red line,’ warns enemies against interference

Published: 2026-10-08 16:16:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287189/IRGC-chief-calls-Hormuz-security-a-red-line-warns-enemies

Tehran, IRNA - The commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) warns that the security of the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz is “a red line” for the Islamic Republic, and that no extra-regional power can threaten, dominate, or interfere in the strategic waterway.
