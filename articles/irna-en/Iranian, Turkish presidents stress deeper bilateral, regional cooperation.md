# Iranian, Turkish presidents stress deeper bilateral, regional cooperation

Published: 2026-11-12 10:29:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319898/Iranian-Turkish-presidents-stress-deeper-bilateral-regional

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian and his Turkish counterpart, Recep Tayyip Erdogan, have underscored the need to deepen bilateral ties and expand regional cooperation, particularly in the political, economic, and trade sectors.
