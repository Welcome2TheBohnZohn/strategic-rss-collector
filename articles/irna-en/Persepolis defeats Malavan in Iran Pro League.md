# Persepolis defeats Malavan in Iran Pro League

Published: 2026-08-30 06:57:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86249998/Persepolis-defeats-Malavan-in-Iran-Pro-League

The football teams of Tehran’s Persepolis and Bandar Anzali’s Malavan faced each other at Shohada-ye Shahr-e Qods Stadium on August 29, 2026, on Matchday 4 of the Iran Pro League. The match ended in a 3–0 victory for Persepolis.
