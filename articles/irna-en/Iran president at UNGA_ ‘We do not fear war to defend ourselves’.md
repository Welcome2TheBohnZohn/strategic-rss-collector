# Iran president at UNGA: ‘We do not fear war to defend ourselves’

Published: 2026-09-23 14:14:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272731/President-Pezeshkian-begins-his-address-at-UNGA-meeting-in-New

Tehran, IRNA –President Masoud Pezeshkian told the 81st UN General Assembly on Wednesday that war had been “imposed” on the Islamic Republic, declaring that Iran would never stop defending itself, nor would it shy away from negotiations to achieve peace.
