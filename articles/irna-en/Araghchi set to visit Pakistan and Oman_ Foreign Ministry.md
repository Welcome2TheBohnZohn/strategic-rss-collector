# Araghchi set to visit Pakistan and Oman: Foreign Ministry

Published: 2026-11-15 13:00:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322588/Araghchi-set-to-visit-Pakistan-and-Oman-Foreign-Ministry

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi will embark on a two-nation official trip to Pakistan and Oman, the Foreign Ministry spokesperson announced on Sunday afternoon.
