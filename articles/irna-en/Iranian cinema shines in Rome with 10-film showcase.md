# Iranian cinema shines in Rome with 10-film showcase

Published: 2026-10-04 05:10:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282315/Iranian-cinema-shines-in-Rome-with-10-film-showcase

Vienna, IRNA – Iran’s cinema took the spotlight in Rome as 10 feature, short and documentary films were screened for audiences during a three-day program attended by filmmakers and cinema enthusiasts.
