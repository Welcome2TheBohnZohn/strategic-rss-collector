# Iranian Army downs advanced US spy drone over Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-09-20 18:16:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269431/Iranian-Army-downs-advanced-US-spy-drone-over-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA - The Iranian Army has intercepted an advanced Orbiter reconnaissance drone over the Strait of Hormuz, the latest in a series of American unmanned aircraft brought down by the country's armed forces during the war.
