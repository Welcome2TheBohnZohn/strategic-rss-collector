# Ghalibaf reacts to record gas prices in US

Published: 2026-09-13 18:30:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263172/Ghalibaf-reacts-to-record-gas-prices-in-US

Tehran, IRNA - Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has shared a clip from the popular American animated series The Simpsons in response to a Bloomberg report that fuel prices had reached $9.99 at a gas station in the United States.
