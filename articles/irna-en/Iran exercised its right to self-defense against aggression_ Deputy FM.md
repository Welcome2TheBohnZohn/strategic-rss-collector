# Iran exercised its right to self-defense against aggression: Deputy FM

Published: 2026-11-23 13:21:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330398/Iran-exercised-its-right-to-self-defense-against-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has emphasized that the Iranian nation exercised its natural right to self-defense against aggression to its territories and national interest.
