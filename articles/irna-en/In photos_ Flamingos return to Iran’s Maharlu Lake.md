# In photos: Flamingos return to Iran’s Maharlu Lake

Published: 2026-11-14 10:26:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86321272/In-photos-Flamingos-return-to-Iran-s-Maharlu-Lake

Flamingos have returned to Maharlu Lake in Fars Province, southern Iran, with the arrival of autumn and cooler temperatures, transforming the seasonal wetland into a spectacular wildlife habitat.
