# Sanctions on any SCO member, threaten entire bloc: Iran

Published: 2026-09-01 14:51:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252404/Sanctions-on-any-SCO-member-threaten-entire-bloc-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Iran foreign ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei reiterated that sanctions imposed on any member of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) constitute pressure on all other members.
