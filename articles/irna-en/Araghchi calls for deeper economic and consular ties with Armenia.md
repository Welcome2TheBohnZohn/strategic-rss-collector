# Araghchi calls for deeper economic and consular ties with Armenia

Published: 2026-09-09 16:27:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259890/Araghchi-calls-for-deeper-economic-and-consular-ties-with-Armenia

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has stressed the need to deepen economic and consular relations with Armenia as part of Tehran’s policy of good neighborliness.
