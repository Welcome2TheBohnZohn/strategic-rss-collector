# Iran moves closer to launching Shahid Soleimani satellite constellation: Minister

Published: 2026-10-04 11:02:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282897/Iran-moves-closer-to-launching-Shahid-Soleimani-satellite-constellation

Tehran, IRNA — Iran is preparing to take a major step toward developing satellite constellations, with three satellites set to be unveiled and launched as part of the country’s expanding space program, says the country’s minister of information and communications technology.
