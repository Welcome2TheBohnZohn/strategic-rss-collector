# Gas Exporting Countries Forum summit kicks off in Moscow

Published: 2026-10-27 12:09:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305054/Gas-Exporting-Countries-Forum-summit-kicks-off-in-Moscow

Moscow, IRNA – The 8th Summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF) opened on Tuesday, October 27, in Moscow, with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian in attendance.
