# IRGC chief highlights nationwide gatherings key to final victory in war

Published: 2026-09-16 10:40:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265820/IRGC-chief-highlights-nationwide-gatherings-key-to-final-victory

Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Major General Ahmad Vahidi, says maintaining an informed and vigilant presence across critical national spheres is the only path to final victory in a complex and modern imposed war.
