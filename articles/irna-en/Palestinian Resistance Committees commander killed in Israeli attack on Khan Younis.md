# Palestinian Resistance Committees commander killed in Israeli attack on Khan Younis

Published: 2026-10-21 15:56:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299749/Palestinian-Resistance-Committees-commander-killed-in-Israeli

Tehran, IRNA – Ramadan Mohammad Diab, a senior commander of the Palestinian Resistance Committees, has been killed in an Israeli attack on Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip, the group said.
