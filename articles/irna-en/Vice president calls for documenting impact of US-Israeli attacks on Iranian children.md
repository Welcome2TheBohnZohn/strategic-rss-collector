# Vice president calls for documenting impact of US-Israeli attacks on Iranian children

Published: 2026-11-07 09:37:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314720/Vice-president-calls-for-documenting-impact-of-US-Israeli-attacks

Tehran, IRNA — The vice president for women and family affairs has called for documenting the human and educational consequences of US-Israeli attacks on Iranian children, stressing the need to pursue justice for victims and hold those responsible accountable.
