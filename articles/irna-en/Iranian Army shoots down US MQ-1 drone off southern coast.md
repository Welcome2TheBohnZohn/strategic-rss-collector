# Iranian Army shoots down US MQ-1 drone off southern coast

Published: 2026-11-13 19:30:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320807/Iranian-Army-shoots-down-US-MQ-1-drone-off-southern-coast

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army Navy says it has shot down a US MQ-1 drone using an advanced air defense system near the coastal area of Kuhestak in the country’s southern Hormozgan Province.
