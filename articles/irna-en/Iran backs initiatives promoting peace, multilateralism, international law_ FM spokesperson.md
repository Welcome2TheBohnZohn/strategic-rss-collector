# Iran backs initiatives promoting peace, multilateralism, international law: FM spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-01 16:54:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252378/Iran-backs-initiatives-promoting-peace-multilateralism-international

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says Tehran supports any initiative that promotes international peace and security, multilateralism, the UN Charter and international law.
