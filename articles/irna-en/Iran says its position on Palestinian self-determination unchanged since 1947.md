# Iran says its position on Palestinian self-determination unchanged since 1947

Published: 2026-11-03 16:21:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311619/Iran-says-its-position-on-Palestinian-self-determination-unchanged

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s principled position on the Palestinian people’s right to self-determination has remained unchanged since 1947, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said on Tuesday.
