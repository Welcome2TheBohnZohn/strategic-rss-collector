# New ‘surprises’ await enemy on battlefield in possible future war: Defense Ministry spokesperson

Published: 2026-08-28 09:27:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248357/New-surprises-await-enemy-on-battlefield-in-possible-future

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Ministry of Defense and Armed Forces Logistics, Brigadier General Reza Talaei‑Nik, says that Iran has prepared new weapons, equipment and technical and tactical capabilities for a possible future war, stressing that the “surprises” will be revealed on the battlefield.
