# Foreign Ministry summons German ambassador over Berlin’s anti-Iran positions

Published: 2026-09-17 16:46:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266907/Foreign-Ministry-summons-German-ambassador-over-Berlin-s-anti-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs has summoned the German ambassador in Tehran to protest the positions of senior German officials against Iran and the interventionist actions of the German embassy.
