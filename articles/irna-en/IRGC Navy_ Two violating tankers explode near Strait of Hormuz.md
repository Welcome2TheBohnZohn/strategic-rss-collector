# IRGC Navy: Two violating tankers explode near Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-28 06:07:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86334293/IRGC-Navy-Two-violating-tankers-explode-near-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says two tankers exploded and caught fire near the entrance to Khasab Port and east of the Strait of Hormuz after attempting to cross the strategic waterway through an unauthorized route.
