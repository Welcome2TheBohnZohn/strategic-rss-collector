# Minister calls for promoting equity in Iran’s public education

Published: 2026-09-22 10:07:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271153/Minister-calls-for-promoting-equity-in-Iran-s-public-education

Tehran, IRNA – Education Minister Alireza Kazemi, emphasizing educational equity, says that creating equal learning opportunities and standardizing educational, developmental, and sports facilities remain among the country’s core policies.
