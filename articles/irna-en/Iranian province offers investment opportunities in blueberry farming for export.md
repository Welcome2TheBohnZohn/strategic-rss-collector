# Iranian province offers investment opportunities in blueberry farming for export

Published: 2026-10-27 07:12:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304618/Iranian-province-offers-investment-opportunities-in-blueberry

Yasouj, IRNA — Investment in blueberry cultivation in Kohgiluyeh and Boyer-Ahmad Province offers promising opportunities for income generation, job creation and non-oil exports, with local agricultural authorities highlighting the region’s climatic diversity and the crop’s long-term profitability.
