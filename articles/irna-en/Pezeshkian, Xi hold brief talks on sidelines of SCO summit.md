# Pezeshkian, Xi hold brief talks on sidelines of SCO summit

Published: 2026-09-01 10:11:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252077/Pezeshkian-Xi-hold-brief-talks-on-sidelines-of-SCO-summit

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian and his Chinese counterpart Xi Jinping have held a brief meeting on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan.
