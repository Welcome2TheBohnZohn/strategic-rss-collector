# Iran calls for regional security cooperation in Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-05 13:40:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284075/Iran-calls-for-regional-security-cooperation-in-Persian-Gulf

Tehran, IRNA – The head of Iran’s parliamentary delegation to Tanzania has emphasized that lasting security in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz should be ensured through the participation of coastal states, dialogue, confidence-building, and shared responsibility.
