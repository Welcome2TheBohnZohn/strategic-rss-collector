# IRGC: Two oil tankers, tugboat catch fire in Strait of Hormuz; two LUCAS drones seized

Published: 2026-11-05 05:41:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313001/IRGC-Two-oil-tankers-tugboat-catch-fire-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that two oil tankers and a tugboat caught fire in separate incidents in the Strait of Hormuz after they attempted to use an unauthorized route, while its electronic warfare unit captured two LUCAS drones.
