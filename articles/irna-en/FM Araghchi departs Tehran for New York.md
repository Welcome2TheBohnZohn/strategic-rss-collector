# FM Araghchi departs Tehran for New York

Published: 2026-09-20 18:47:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269449/FM-Araghchi-departs-Tehran-for-New-York

Tehran, IRNA - Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi left Tehran for New York on Sunday evening to attend the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly.
