# Iran to pursue war-related environmental damage in international forums: VP

Published: 2026-11-10 16:31:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318259/Iran-to-pursue-war-related-environmental-damage-in-international

Tehran, IRNA – Vice President and Head of the Department of Environment Shina Ansari has called for documenting and pursuing legal action in international forums over environmental damage caused by recent wars.
