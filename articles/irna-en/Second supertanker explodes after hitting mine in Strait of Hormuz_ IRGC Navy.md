# Second supertanker explodes after hitting mine in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-10-17 13:41:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295178/Second-supertanker-explodes-after-hitting-mine-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has reported an explosion aboard a second supertanker following a collision with a naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz.
