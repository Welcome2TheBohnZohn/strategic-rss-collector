# Iran, Afghanistan to expand transit cooperation via Chabahar: Envoy

Published: 2026-09-26 10:45:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275045/Iran-Afghanistan-to-expand-transit-cooperation-via-Chabahar

Tehran, IRNA – Iran and Afghanistan are broadening transport and transit cooperation, particularly through the use of Chabahar Port, says Iran’s acting ambassador in Kabul, Alireza Bikdeli.
