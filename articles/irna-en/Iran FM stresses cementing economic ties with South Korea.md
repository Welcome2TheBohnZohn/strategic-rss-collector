# Iran FM stresses cementing economic ties with South Korea

Published: 2026-09-15 19:06:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265223/Iran-FM-stresses-cementing-economic-ties-with-South-Korea

Tehran, IRNA – Iran foreign minister, during a meeting with the country’s new ambassador to South Korea, emphasized the need to strengthen bilateral relations, particularly in the economic, commercial, and academic fields.
