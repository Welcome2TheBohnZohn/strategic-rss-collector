# Zahedan Book Fair opens with cultural, literary programs

Published: 2026-10-05 07:54:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86283618/Zahedan-Book-Fair-opens-with-cultural-literary-programs

The Zahedan Book Fair opened on the evening of October 4, 2026, bringing together publishers, cultural officials, and residents as part of a program featuring books alongside cultural and artistic activities.
