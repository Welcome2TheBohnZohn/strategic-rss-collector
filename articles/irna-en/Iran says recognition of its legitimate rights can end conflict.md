# Iran says recognition of its legitimate rights can end conflict

Published: 2026-09-30 10:04:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86279260/Iran-says-recognition-of-its-legitimate-rights-can-end-conflict

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has consistently called for an end to conflict and the establishment of peace, but will continue to defend its rights, security and national interests, Spokesperson for the Ministry of Defense and Armed Forces Logistics Brigadier General Reza Talaei-Nik said.
