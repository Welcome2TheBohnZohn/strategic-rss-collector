# Yemen has inflicted $2 trillion in damages on Saudi Arabia: Ex-US ambassador

Published: 2026-10-26 10:19:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86303875/Yemen-has-inflicted-2-trillion-in-damages-on-Saudi-Arabia-Ex-US

Chas Freeman, former US Ambassador to Saudi Arabia, says estimates of the damage inflicted upon Saudi Arabia by Yemen’s Ansarallah have reached two trillion dollars, warning that this is a war that Saudi Arabia cannot win.
