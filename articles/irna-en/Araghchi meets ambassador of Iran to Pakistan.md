# Araghchi meets ambassador of Iran to Pakistan

Published: 2026-09-09 20:29:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260004/Araghchi-meets-ambassador-of-Iran-to-Pakistan

Tehran, IRNA – Reza Amiri-Moghaddam, the Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Pakistan, met and held talks with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi this evening (Wednesday).
