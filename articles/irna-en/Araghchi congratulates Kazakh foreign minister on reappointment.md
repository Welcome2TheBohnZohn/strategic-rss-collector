# Araghchi congratulates Kazakh foreign minister on reappointment

Published: 2026-09-11 10:32:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260974/Araghchi-congratulates-Kazakh-foreign-minister-on-reappointment

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has congratulated Mukhtar Tleuberdi on his reappointment as Kazakhstan’s foreign minister, three years after he left the post.
