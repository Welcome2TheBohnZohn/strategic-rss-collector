# US ‘war of choice’ shattered illusion of absolute power: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-03 15:19:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254268/US-war-of-choice-shattered-illusion-of-absolute-power-Baqaei

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says the US “war of choice” was an unlawful and unprovoked aggression aimed at collapsing the country, but instead shattered the illusion of Washington’s absolute power and moral standing.
