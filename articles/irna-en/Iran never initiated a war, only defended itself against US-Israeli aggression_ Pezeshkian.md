# Iran never initiated a war, only defended itself against US-Israeli aggression: Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-09-13 13:22:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262942/Iran-never-initiated-a-war-only-defended-itself-against-US-Israeli

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian, referring to the recently imposed war on the country, says Iran did not start the conflict, but was forced to defend itself against the aggression launched by the United States and the Israeli regime under false pretexts.
