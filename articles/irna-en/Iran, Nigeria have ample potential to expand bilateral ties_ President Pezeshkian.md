# Iran, Nigeria have ample potential to expand bilateral ties: President Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-11-03 18:28:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311703/Iran-Nigeria-have-ample-potential-to-expand-bilateral-ties

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for stronger economic, political and cultural cooperation between Tehran and Abuja, saying the two countries have considerable untapped potential to deepen their relations.
