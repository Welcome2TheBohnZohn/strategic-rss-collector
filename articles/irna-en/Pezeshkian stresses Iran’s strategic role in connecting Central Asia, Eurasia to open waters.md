# Pezeshkian stresses Iran’s strategic role in connecting Central Asia, Eurasia to open waters

Published: 2026-10-09 10:09:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287546/Pezeshkian-stresses-Iran-s-strategic-role-in-connecting-Central

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has described Iran’s participation as the first invited special guest at the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) summit as an opportunity to enhance cooperation, highlighting Iran’s strategic role in linking Central Asia and Eurasia to open waters.
