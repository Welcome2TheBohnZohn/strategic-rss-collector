# Official vows to seriously pursue removing obstacles to boosting Kermanshah’s trade

Published: 2026-09-09 08:59:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259395/Official-vows-to-seriously-pursue-removing-obstacles-to-boosting

Kermanshah, IRNA – The head of the office in charge of Iran–Iraq economic development says the body is seriously pursuing the resolution of obstacles hindering the prosperity of Kermanshah’s trade with Iraq.
