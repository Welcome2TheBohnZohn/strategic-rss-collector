# Commander: ‘Triangle of power’ enabled Iran to overcome US-Israeli war

Published: 2026-09-07 14:08:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257678/Commander-Triangle-of-power-enabled-Iran-to-overcome-US-Israeli

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Armed Forces, leadership and people formed a “triangle of power” that enabled Iran to overcome its adversaries’ plans during two US-Israeli-imposed wars against the country, Deputy Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces Brigadier General Kioumars Heidari said on Monday.
