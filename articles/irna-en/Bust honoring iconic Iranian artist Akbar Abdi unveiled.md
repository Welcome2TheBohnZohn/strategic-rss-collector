# Bust honoring iconic Iranian artist Akbar Abdi unveiled

Published: 2026-09-06 07:38:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86256146/Bust-honoring-iconic-Iranian-artist-Akbar-Abdi-unveiled

Tehran, IRNA – A memorial ceremony marking the 40th day since the death of acclaimed Iranian actor Akbar Abdi was held in the presence of fellow artists, friends, colleagues and family members, culminating in the unveiling of a bust honoring him.
