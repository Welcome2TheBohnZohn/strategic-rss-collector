# Baqaei: European countries that placed their facilities at disposal of aggressor must be held accountable

Published: 2026-10-05 21:40:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284341/Baqaei-European-countries-that-placed-their-facilities-at-disposal

Tehran, IRNA – Referring to the explicit demands made of European capitals during yesterday’s meeting between the Foreign Minister and foreign ambassadors and heads of diplomatic missions, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said that very single European country that placed its territory or facilities at the disposal of the aggressors must be held accountable for its actions; for this constitutes a clear instance of participation and complicity in the crime of international aggression and the use of force against an independent state and UN member.
