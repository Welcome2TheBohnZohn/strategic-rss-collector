# Iranian Army targets US Patriot systems at Kuwait’s Ali Al Salem base

Published: 2026-11-18 06:10:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325322/Iranian-Army-targets-US-Patriot-systems-at-Kuwait-s-Ali-Al-Salem

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Army has targeted US Patriot air defense systems and drone and missile launch platforms at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait with combat drones, according to a statement issued by the Army’s Public Relations Office.
