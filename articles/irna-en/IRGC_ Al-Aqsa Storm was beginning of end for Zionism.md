# IRGC: Al-Aqsa Storm was beginning of end for Zionism

Published: 2026-10-06 20:10:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285411/IRGC-Al-Aqsa-Storm-was-beginning-of-end-for-Zionism

Tehran, IRNA – In a statement commemorating the 7th of October—marking the third anniversary of the historic and strategic Operation Al-Aqsa Storm—the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) described the operation as the beginning of the end for Zionism and the shattering of the myth of the Zionist army's invincibility, as well as a future-shaping event that accelerates the definitive decline of the racist, occupying regime of the Holy Quds.
