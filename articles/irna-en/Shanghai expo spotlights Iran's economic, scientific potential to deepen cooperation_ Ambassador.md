# Shanghai expo spotlights Iran's economic, scientific potential to deepen cooperation: Ambassador

Published: 2026-11-08 12:46:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316002/Shanghai-expo-spotlights-Iran-s-economic-scientific-potential

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to China, referring to the potential to establish joint value chains, has emphasized the need for the expansion of economic, scientific and technological cooperation, as well as joint investments, between Tehran and Beijing.
