# Iran’s National Post Company wins Asia-Pacific Postal Union Innovation Award

Published: 2026-09-02 10:01:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253206/Iran-s-National-Post-Company-wins-Asia-Pacific-Postal-Union-Innovation

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s National Post Company has won the Innovation Award at the fourth Postal Business Forum of the Asia-Pacific Postal Union (APPU) in Thailand for innovative services supporting the Tehran Virtual Book Fair.
