# US aggression puts livelihoods of Iranian fishermen in danger: Official

Published: 2026-11-02 13:14:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310331/US-aggression-puts-livelihoods-of-Iranian-fishermen-in-danger

Tehran, IRNA – The CEO of the Qeshm Fishermen’s Cooperative says US military aggression against Iran has threatened fishermen’s livelihoods, destroyed fishing boats and vessels, and killed several fishermen.
