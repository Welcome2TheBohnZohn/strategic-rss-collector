# Iran reaches Men's Volleyball Asian Championship final after beating Australia

Published: 2026-09-12 10:14:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261786/Iran-reaches-Men-s-Volleyball-Asian-Championship-final-after

Tehran, IRNA – Iran reached the final of the AVC Men's Volleyball Asian Championship after defeating Australia 3-1 in the semifinal in Fukuoka, Japan, on Saturday.
