# Enemy seeks to disrupt armed forces’ cohesion: Iranian commander

Published: 2026-10-23 13:21:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86301057/Enemy-seeks-to-disrupt-armed-forces-cohesion-Iranian-commander

Tehran, IRNA – The commander of the Iranian Army’s Ground Forces stressed the need for comprehensive readiness in the current critical conditions, stating that alongside military threats, the enemy seeks to undermine the resolve and disrupt the cohesion of the armed forces.
