# Iran’s Armed Forces operating at highest level of coordination, says commander

Published: 2026-11-07 13:40:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314943/Iran-s-Armed-Forces-operating-at-highest-level-of-coordination

Astara, IRNA – The commander of the Northern Fleet and the Fourth Naval Zone of Imam Reza (AS) says coordination and cooperation among Iran’s Armed Forces are currently at their highest level, highlighting the strong synergy in their various branches.
