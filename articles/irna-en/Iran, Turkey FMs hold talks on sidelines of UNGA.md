# Iran, Turkey FMs hold talks on sidelines of UNGA

Published: 2026-09-26 09:11:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274709/Iran-Turkey-FMs-hold-talks-on-sidelines-of-UNGA

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchgi and his Turkish counterpart, Hakan Fidan, have held talks on the sidelines of the 81st UN General Assembly in New York.
