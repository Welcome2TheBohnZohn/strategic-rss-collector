# Economy minister seeks Islamic cooperation to counter US economic pressure

Published: 2026-11-02 14:09:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310386/Economy-minister-seeks-Islamic-cooperation-to-counter-US-economic

Tehran, IRNA – Economy Minister Ali Madanizadeh says he plans to hold bilateral talks with counterparts from other Islamic countries to strengthen economic cooperation and counter US efforts to intensify economic pressure on Tehran.
