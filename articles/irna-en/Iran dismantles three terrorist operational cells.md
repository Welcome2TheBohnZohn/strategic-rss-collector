# Iran dismantles three terrorist operational cells

Published: 2026-09-20 12:22:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269194/Iran-dismantles-three-terrorist-operational-cells

Tehran, IRNA – Several elements affiliated with terrorist groups acting as mercenaries for the American-Zionist enemy have been killed or arrested in three provinces across Iran. They were planning assassination operations and sabotage of vital infrastructure.
