# Iranian, Lebanese FM hold talks on sidelines of OIC summit in Baghdad

Published: 2026-10-17 15:08:13

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Seyyed Abbas Araghchi has met with his Lebanese counterpart Youssef Rajji on the sidelines of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC)’s Council of Foreign Ministers’ meeting in Baghdad, the capital of Iraq.
