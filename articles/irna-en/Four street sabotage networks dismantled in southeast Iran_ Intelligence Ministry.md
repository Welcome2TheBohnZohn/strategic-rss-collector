# Four street sabotage networks dismantled in southeast Iran: Intelligence Ministry

Published: 2026-10-03 10:55:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281820/Four-street-sabotage-networks-dismantled-in-southeast-Iran-Intelligence

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Intelligence Ministry says four organized street sabotage networks comprising 31 people had been identified and dismantled in Sirjan, Kerman Province.
