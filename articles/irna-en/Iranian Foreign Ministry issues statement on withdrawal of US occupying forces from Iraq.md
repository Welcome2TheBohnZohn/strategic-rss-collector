# Iranian Foreign Ministry issues statement on withdrawal of US occupying forces from Iraq

Published: 2026-10-03 21:57:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282237/Iranian-Foreign-Ministry-issues-statement-on-withdrawal-of-US

Tehran, IRNA – In a statement regarding the withdrawal of the US occupying forces from Iraq, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran emphasized the necessity of holding the US government accountable for the crimes committed and the damages inflicted upon the people of Iraq and other nations during the occupation.
