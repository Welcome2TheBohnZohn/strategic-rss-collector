# Iran president leaves for Moscow to attend GECF summit

Published: 2026-10-26 16:44:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304216/Iran-president-leaves-for-Moscow-to-attend-GECF-summit

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian departed for Moscow on Monday evening to attend and address the eighth summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF), with talks on bilateral cooperation also on his agenda.
