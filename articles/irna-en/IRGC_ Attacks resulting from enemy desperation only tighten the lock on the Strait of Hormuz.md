Tehran, IRNA – In a statement emphasizing that such attacks—born of the enemy's desperation—only serve to tighten the lock on the Strait of Hormuz, the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) declared that its forces have initiated a response that will make the aggressors regret their actions.
