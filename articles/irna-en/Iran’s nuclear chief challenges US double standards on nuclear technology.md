# Iran’s nuclear chief challenges US double standards on nuclear technology

Published: 2026-10-11 09:45:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289415/Iran-s-nuclear-chief-challenges-US-double-standards-on-nuclear

Tehran, IRNA – Director of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) Mohammad Eslami has criticized Washington’s double standards on nuclear technology, questioning why the United States prioritizes expanding its own nuclear capabilities while seeking to deny Iran access to the same technology.
