# No breakthrough yet in consultations with Qatari mediator over Hormuz plan: Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-01 14:24:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309431/No-breakthrough-yet-in-consultations-with-Qatari-mediator-over

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, referring to the talks between an Iranian delegation and the Qatari mediators in Doha, says although consultations over a “two-day plan” on reopening the Strait of Hormuz continue, but a definitive outcome has not been reached yet.
