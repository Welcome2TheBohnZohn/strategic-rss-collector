# General Rezaei: Any evil act against Iran will bring affliction on US to be recorded in history

Published: 2026-08-27 21:20:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248152/General-Rezaei-Any-evil-act-against-Iran-will-bring-affliction

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) Major General Mohsen Rezaei has warned the United States that any new military aggression against the Islamic Republic would draw a forceful response against American military and economic interests, while stressing that Washington must take concrete steps before Tehran reopens the Strait of Hormuz.
