# St. Petersburg university seeks to establish Persian language department

Published: 2026-09-27 07:57:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275855/St-Petersburg-university-seeks-to-establish-Persian-language

St. Petersburg, IRNA – The rector of Saint Petersburg State Institute of Culture has expressed the university’s interest in establishing a Persian language department, as Iranian Culture Minister Abbas Salehi visited the institution during his trip to Russia.
