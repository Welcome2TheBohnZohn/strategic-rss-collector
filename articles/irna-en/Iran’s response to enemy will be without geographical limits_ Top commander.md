# Iran’s response to enemy will be without geographical limits: Top commander

Published: 2026-10-31 16:41:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308578/Iran-s-response-to-enemy-will-be-without-geographical-limits

Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has reiterated his pledge of a firm and decisive response to US or Israeli aggression, saying Iran is fully prepared to strike enemy targets promptly, powerfully, intensely and without geographical limitations.
