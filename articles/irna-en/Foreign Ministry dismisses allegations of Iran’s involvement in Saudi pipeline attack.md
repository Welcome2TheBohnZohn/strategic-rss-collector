# Foreign Ministry dismisses allegations of Iran’s involvement in Saudi pipeline attack

Published: 2026-09-14 10:07:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263752/Foreign-Ministry-dismisses-allegations-of-Iran-s-involvement

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has dismissed US allegations of Iran’s involvement in a recent attack from Iraqi soil that struck Saudi Arabia’s East-West oil pipeline, saying that Washington is making false claims to deepen disputes between regional countries and complicate the situation.
