# Iran has not started the war but will respond powerfully to any attack: Pezeshkian

Published: 2026-09-25 14:16:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274215/Iran-has-not-started-the-war-but-will-respond-powerfully-to-any

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says that Iran has not started the war and will not do so, stressing that the country has faced an imposed war and will defend itself against any attack.
