# Iran’s active participation in GECF in line with strengthening multilateralism: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-24 16:54:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302148/Iran-s-active-participation-in-GECF-in-line-with-strengthening

Moscow, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Russia says the Islamic Republic’s active participation in the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF) represents the country’s energy diplomacy, interconnecting regional networks, and strengthening multilateralism.
