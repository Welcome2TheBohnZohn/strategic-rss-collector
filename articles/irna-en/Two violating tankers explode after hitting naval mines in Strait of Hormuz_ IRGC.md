# Two violating tankers explode after hitting naval mines in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-15 14:56:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322680/Two-violating-tankers-explode-after-hitting-naval-mines-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that two oil tankers attempting to pass through unauthorized routes in the Strait of Hormuz exploded after striking naval mines, and one of them sank in the strategic waterway.
