# Iran’s new strategy will shatter enemy’s foundations: Top security official

Published: 2026-09-02 14:05:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253480/Iran-s-new-strategy-will-shatter-enemy-s-foundations-Top-security

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei has renewed his threat to the United States over its ongoing military aggression and economic war on the country, warning that the Islamic Republic's new approach would ultimately lead to the enemy's defeat.
