# Pezeshkian: Iran welcomes expansion of ties with Cyprus in all fields

Published: 2026-11-03 17:34:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311674/Pezeshkian-Iran-welcomes-expansion-of-ties-with-Cyprus-in-all

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has expressed Tehran’s readiness to expand relations and cooperation with Cyprus across all areas of mutual interest, calling for joint efforts to bring bilateral ties in line with their longstanding historical background and existing potential.
