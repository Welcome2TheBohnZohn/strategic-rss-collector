# Iran ready for more devastating response to any new US adventurism: Rezaei

Published: 2026-10-18 10:01:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295950/Iran-ready-for-more-devastating-response-to-any-new-US-adventurism

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, Major General Mohsen Rezaei, says that Iran’s Armed Forces have prepared a plan for any new US–Israeli adventurism that would be more devastating than the 12-day and 40-day wars.
