# Iran’s deterrence strategy adapts to evolving threats: Defense Ministry

Published: 2026-11-18 10:40:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325597/Iran-s-deterrence-strategy-adapts-to-evolving-threats-Defense

Tehran, IRNA – Defense Ministry spokesperson Brigadier General Reza Talaei-Nik says Iran’s deterrence strategy remains dynamic and active in response to the changing nature and patterns of warfare, noting that recent hybrid warfare differs significantly from previous conflicts.
