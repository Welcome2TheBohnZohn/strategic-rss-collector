# Pezeshkian: Expansion of comprehensive cooperation with Tajikistan on Iran’s foreign policy agenda

Published: 2026-09-07 07:59:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257199/Pezeshkian-Expansion-of-comprehensive-cooperation-with-Tajikistan

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated Tajikistan on its Independence Day, saying Tehran has made the expansion of comprehensive cooperation with Dushanbe a foreign policy priority.
