# Hezbollah chief offers condolences on passing of Ayatollah Vahid Khorasani

Published: 2026-11-18 11:57:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325834/Hezbollah-chief-offers-condolences-on-passing-of-Ayatollah-Vahid

Tehran, IRNA - The Secretary-General of Lebanon's Hezbollah, while offering condolences on the passing of Ayatollah Vahid Khorasani, emphasized that throughout his life he firmly defended the path of truth.
