# Hamas says silence of some Palestinian factions over Gaza crisis amounts to complicity in siege

Published: 2026-11-08 15:06:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316125/Hamas-says-silence-of-some-Palestinian-factions-over-Gaza-crisis

Tehran, IRNA – Hamas spokesperson Hazem Qassem says that the silence of some Palestinian parties over the humanitarian catastrophe in the Gaza Strip amounted to complicity in the crime of besieging the territory.
