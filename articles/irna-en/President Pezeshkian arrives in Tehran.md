# President Pezeshkian arrives in Tehran

Published: 2026-10-09 16:52:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287799/President-Pezeshkian-arrives-in-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has arrived in Tehran after attending and delivering speeches at two summits that had brought several regional heads of state and representatives together to deliberate on issues of common interest.
