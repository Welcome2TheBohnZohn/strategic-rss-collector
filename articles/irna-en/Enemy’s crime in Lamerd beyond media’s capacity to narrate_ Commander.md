# Enemy’s crime in Lamerd beyond media’s capacity to narrate: Commander

Published: 2026-10-13 14:03:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291870/Enemy-s-crime-in-Lamerd-beyond-media-s-capacity-to-narrate-Commander
