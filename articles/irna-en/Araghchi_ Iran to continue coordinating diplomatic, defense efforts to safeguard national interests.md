# Araghchi: Iran to continue coordinating diplomatic, defense efforts to safeguard national interests

Published: 2026-09-08 16:29:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258790/Araghchi-Iran-to-continue-coordinating-diplomatic-defense-efforts

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says coordination between the country’s diplomatic and defense efforts will continue to safeguard national interests.
