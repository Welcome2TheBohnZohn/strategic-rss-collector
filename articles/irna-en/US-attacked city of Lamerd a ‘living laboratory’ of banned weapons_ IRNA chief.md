# US-attacked city of Lamerd a ‘living laboratory’ of banned weapons: IRNA chief

Published: 2026-10-13 12:20:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291784/US-attacked-city-of-Lamerd-a-living-laboratory-of-banned-weapons

Shiraz, IRNA – The Head of the Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA) has described the southern city of Lamerd in Fars province as a “living laboratory of internationally banned weapons.”
