# Exclusive | Baqaei: Regional meetings must address root causes of insecurity in Persian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-24 20:01:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302216/Exclusive-Baqaei-Regional-meetings-must-addres-root-causes

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei, emphasizing the need to examine the root causes of insecurity in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz within regional forums, urged European countries—in order to demonstrate the seriousness of their commitment to diplomacy—to adhere to international law and abandon coercive approaches and illegal sanctions against Iran.
