# ‘Iran Cultural Week’ to be held across Russia

Published: 2026-09-22 13:37:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271488/Iran-Cultural-Week-to-be-held-across-Russia

Tehran, IRNA – Cultural Week of the Islamic Republic of Iran will be held in four major cities across Russia from September 24 to 26, including 13 cultural, artistic and scientific programs.
