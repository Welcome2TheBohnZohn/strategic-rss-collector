# Tehran, Tashkent agree to expand long-term agricultural cooperation: Uzbekistan

Published: 2026-09-22 11:35:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271262/Tehran-Tashkent-agree-to-expand-long-term-agricultural-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Uzbekistan and Iran have agreed to expand long-term cooperation in agriculture, pursue joint projects and strengthen ties between economic and business representatives, Uzbekistan’s Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources announced.
