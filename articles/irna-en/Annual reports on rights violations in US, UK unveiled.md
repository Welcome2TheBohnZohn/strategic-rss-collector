# Annual reports on rights violations in US, UK unveiled

Published: 2026-11-04 05:54:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86311896/Annual-reports-on-rights-violations-in-US-UK-unveiled

The annual reports on human rights violations in the United States and Britain were unveiled during a ceremony in Tehran on November 3, 2026. The ceremony was attended by Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi and Hamid Ahmadi, director general for human rights at Iran’s Foreign Ministry.
