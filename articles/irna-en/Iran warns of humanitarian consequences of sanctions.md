# Iran warns of humanitarian consequences of sanctions

Published: 2026-10-19 09:50:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297045/Iran-warns-of-humanitarian-consequences-of-sanctions

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s High Council for Human Rights has condemned economic sanctions and restrictions that disrupt people’s access to medicines and medical treatment, calling on the international community to take immediate action to protect Iranian patients and vulnerable groups.
