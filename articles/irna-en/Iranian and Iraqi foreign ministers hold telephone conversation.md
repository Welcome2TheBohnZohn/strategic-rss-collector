# Iranian and Iraqi foreign ministers hold telephone conversation

Published: 2026-09-12 20:52:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262190/Iranian-and-Iraqi-foreign-ministers-hold-telephone-conversation

Tehran, IRNA — Abbas Araghchi, Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, and Fuad Hussein, Foreign Minister of the Republic of Iraq, held a telephone conversation to discuss and exchange views on bilateral issues and regional developments.
