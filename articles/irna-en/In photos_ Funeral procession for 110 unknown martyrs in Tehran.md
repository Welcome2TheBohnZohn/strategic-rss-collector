# In photos: Funeral procession for 110 unknown martyrs in Tehran

Published: 2026-11-13 09:12:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86320487/In-photos-Funeral-procession-for-110-unknown-martyrs-in-Tehran

The funeral procession for 110 unknown martyrs of the Sacred Defense (a war imposed on Iran by the Iraqi Ba’ath regime from 1980 to 1988) kicked off in Tehran on February 13, 2026. This event coincided with the martyrdom anniversary of Hazrat Fatimah Zahra (SA) and was attended by Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian.
