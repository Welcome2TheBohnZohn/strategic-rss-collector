# IRGC spokesperson: US army should account for losses to its expensive equipment

Published: 2026-11-17 21:24:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325168/IRGC-spokesperson-US-army-should-account-for-losses-to-its-expensive

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, in a message, emphasized that instead of showcasing military superiority, the US army should account for the successive losses of its expensive equipment.
