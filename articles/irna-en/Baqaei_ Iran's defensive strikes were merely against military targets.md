# Baqaei: Iran's defensive strikes were merely against military targets

Published: 2026-09-04 00:15:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254471/Baqaei-Iran-s-defensive-strikes-were-merely-against-military

Tehran, IRNA – In a statement, the Spokesperson for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic of Iran emphasized that—unlike the United States, which has made attacking civilian targets a standard pattern of its illegal wars—Iran’s defensive strikes were directed exclusively at military targets, noting that the official report from Qatar confirms this fact.
