# Iraqi political leader calls for dialogue to end regional war

Published: 2026-10-18 09:21:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295955/Iraqi-political-leader-calls-for-dialogue-to-end-regional-war

Tehran, IRNA – Ammar al-Hakim, leader of Iraq’s National Wisdom Movement, called for an end to regional tensions and war through dialogue and mutual understanding.
