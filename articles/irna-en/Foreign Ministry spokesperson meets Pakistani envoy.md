# Foreign Ministry spokesperson meets Pakistani envoy

Published: 2026-10-08 05:21:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286593/Foreign-Ministry-spokesperson-meets-Pakistani-envoy

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has met with Omran Ahmed Siddiqui, the newly appointed Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan to Iran here in Tehran.
