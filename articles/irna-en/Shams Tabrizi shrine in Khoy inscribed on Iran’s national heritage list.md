# Shams Tabrizi shrine in Khoy inscribed on Iran’s national heritage list

Published: 2026-10-07 05:12:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285516/Shams-Tabrizi-shrine-in-Khoy-inscribed-on-Iran-s-national-heritage

Urmia, IRNA – The resting place of Shams Tabrizi in the northwestern County of Khoy has been officially inscribed on Iran’s national heritage list ahead of its official opening, the Ministry of Cultural Heritage, Tourism and Handicrafts has announced.
