# Ex-president Khatami calls Ayatollah Vahid Khorasani’s passing major loss

Published: 2026-11-18 10:34:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325761/Ex-president-Khatami-calls-Ayatollah-Vahid-Khorasani-s-passing

Tehran, IRNA — Former Iranian president Mohammad Khatami has described the passing of senior cleric Grand Ayatollah Hossein Vahid Khorasani as a major loss for the Muslim world and Shia Islam, particularly seminaries.
