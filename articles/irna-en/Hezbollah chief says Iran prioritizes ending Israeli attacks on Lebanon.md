# Hezbollah chief says Iran prioritizes ending Israeli attacks on Lebanon

Published: 2026-10-29 06:09:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306544/Hezbollah-chief-says-Iran-prioritizes-ending-Israeli-attacks

Tehran, IRNA – Hezbollah Secretary-General Sheikh Naim Qassem says Iran has made ending Israeli attacks on Lebanon and securing an Israeli withdrawal from Lebanese territory a priority, describing the Islamic Republic’s position as a major sacrifice appreciated by the Lebanese movement.
