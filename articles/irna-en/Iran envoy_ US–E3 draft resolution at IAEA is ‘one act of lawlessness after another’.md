# Iran envoy: US–E3 draft resolution at IAEA is ‘one act of lawlessness after another’

Published: 2026-09-08 08:25:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258134/Iran-envoy-US-E3-draft-resolution-at-IAEA-is-one-act-of-lawlessness

London, IRNA — Iran’s Ambassador to Germany, Majid Neeli, has described the latest move by the United States and the three European countries of Germany, France, and Britain to refer Iran’s nuclear issue to the UN Security Council as another act of “lawlessness,” recalling a similar move by the three European countries last year.
