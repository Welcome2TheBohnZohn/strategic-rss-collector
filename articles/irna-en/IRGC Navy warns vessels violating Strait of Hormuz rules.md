# IRGC Navy warns vessels violating Strait of Hormuz rules

Published: 2026-10-17 08:38:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294797/IRGC-Navy-warns-vessels-violating-Strait-of-Hormuz-rules

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says that ships violating Iranian regulations in the Strait of Hormuz will ultimately “burn in the fire of trusting the terrorist US military.”
