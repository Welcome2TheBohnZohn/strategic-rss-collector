# Army carries out drone attacks on US bases in UAE, Bahrain

Published: 2026-09-02 07:18:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252926/Army-carries-out-drone-attacks-on-US-bases-in-UAE-Bahrain

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Army says it has launched drone attacks on US bases in Bahrain and the UAE in response to the latest US aggression including its attack on a wedding in southern Iran.
