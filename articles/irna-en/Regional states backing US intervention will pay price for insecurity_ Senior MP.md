# Regional states backing US intervention will pay price for insecurity: Senior MP

Published: 2026-11-21 12:51:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328272/Regional-states-backing-US-intervention-will-pay-price-for-insecurity

Hamedan, IRNA - Deputy Parliament Speaker Hamidreza Haji Babaei says regional countries that have laid the groundwork for US intervention and warmongering must bear the consequences of insecurity in the region.
