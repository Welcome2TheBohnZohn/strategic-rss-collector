# Iran condemns airstrikes on Yemen, calls for end to attacks and blockade

Published: 2026-11-08 16:24:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316160/Iran-condemns-airstrikes-on-Yemen-calls-for-end-to-attacks-and

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Foreign Ministry has strongly condemned airstrikes targeting civilian infrastructure and residential areas in Yemen, calling for an immediate halt to military attacks and the lifting of the blockade on the country.
