# Iran, Russia reaffirm commitment to strategic cooperation

Published: 2026-11-02 15:33:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310474/Iran-Russia-reaffirm-commitment-to-strategic-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Mohsen Rezaei and Russian President Vladimir Putin have reaffirmed their commitment to continuing strategic cooperation in line with a roadmap established by the two countries’ top leaders.
