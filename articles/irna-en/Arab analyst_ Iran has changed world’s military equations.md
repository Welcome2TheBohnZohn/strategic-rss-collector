# Arab analyst: Iran has changed world’s military equations

Published: 2026-09-20 11:53:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269060/Arab-analyst-Iran-has-changed-world-s-military-equations

Tehran, IRNA – Prominent Arab analyst Abdel Bari Atwan has said that the Islamic Republic of Iran has changed the world’s military equations, citing the country’s major success in striking US military bases with ballistic missiles, as well as chasing and hunting US F-35 and F-15 stealth aircraft.
