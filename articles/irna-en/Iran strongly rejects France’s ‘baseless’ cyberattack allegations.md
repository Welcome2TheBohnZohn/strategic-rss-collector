# Iran strongly rejects France’s ‘baseless’ cyberattack allegations

Published: 2026-11-10 15:42:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318293/Iran-strongly-rejects-France-s-baseless-cyberattack-allegations

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry has categorically rejected baseless and malicious allegations by the French government that Iran was involved in alleged cyber operations in France, strongly condemning Paris’s discredited policy of making accusations against Iran.
