# Armed Forces commanders warn of decisive response to any aggression

Published: 2026-09-22 16:19:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271554/Armed-Forces-commanders-warn-of-decisive-response-to-any-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – Senior commanders of Iran’s Armed Forces have emphasized that the country’s security is a red line, noting that any aggressor would face a forceful, decisive, and appropriate response.
