# Tasnim News Agency’s reporter freed after 450 days in Israeli detention

Published: 2026-10-30 16:04:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307604/Tasnim-News-Agency-s-reporter-freed-after-450-days-in-Israeli

Tehran, IRNA — Farah Abu Ayyash, a Tasnim News Agency correspondent in the West Bank, returned to her family on Thursday night after spending 450 days in the prisons of the Israeli regime.
