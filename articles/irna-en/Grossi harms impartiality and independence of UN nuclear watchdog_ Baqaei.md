# Grossi harms impartiality and independence of UN nuclear watchdog: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-11 13:33:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261060/Grossi-harms-impartiality-and-independence-of-UN-nuclear-watchdog

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has criticized the head of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) for his continued politically motivated and biased stance towards Iran’s peaceful nuclear program, warning that Rafael Grossi is squandering the credibility of the Agency.
