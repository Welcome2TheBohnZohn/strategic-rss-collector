# Foreign Minister Araghchi arrives in New York to attend the UN General Assembly

Published: 2026-09-21 20:28:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270576/Foreign-Minister-Araghchi-arrives-in-New-York-to-attend-the-UN

New York, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi arrived in New York on Monday evening (local time) to attend the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly.
