# Iran, China, Russia set conditions for resumption of IAEA verification activities

Published: 2026-11-19 06:09:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326387/Iran-China-Russia-set-conditions-for-resumption-of-IAEA-verification

London, IRNA – Iran, China and Russia say that a complete end to hostile acts, the removal of military threats and credible guarantees against further attacks are prerequisites for the full resumption of the International Atomic Energy Agency’s (IAEA) verification activities in Iran.
