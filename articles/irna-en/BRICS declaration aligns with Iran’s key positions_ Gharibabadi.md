# BRICS declaration aligns with Iran’s key positions: Gharibabadi

Published: 2026-09-12 16:28:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262089/BRICS-declaration-aligns-with-Iran-s-key-positions-Gharibabadi

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Foreign Minister for Legal and International Affairs Kazem Gharibabadi has emphasized that BRICS’ New Delhi Declaration aligns with Iran’s positions on key issues, including opposition to unilateral sanctions, condemnation of attacks on peaceful nuclear facilities, support for Iran’s World Trade Organization (WTO) membership bid, and backing for Palestinian rights.
