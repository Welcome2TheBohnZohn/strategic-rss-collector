# Pezeshkian: Iran, India can expand economic ties by countering sanctions

Published: 2026-08-31 09:51:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86250914/Pezeshkian-Iran-India-can-expand-economic-ties-by-countering

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran and India have extensive potential to expand cooperation in political, economic, scientific, and cultural fields, stressing that stronger bilateral cooperation and efforts to neutralize the effects of sanctions can help further advance economic relations between the two countries.
