# Iran says BRICS and SCO members are working to counter US unilateralism

Published: 2026-09-14 09:36:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263673/Iran-says-BRICS-and-SCO-members-are-working-to-counter-US-unilateralism

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says members of the BRICS and Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) use every opportunity to demonstrate their opposition to the US unilateral approach and to seek solutions to counter its consequences.
