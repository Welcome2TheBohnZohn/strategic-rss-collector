# Iran won’t accept unrestricted inspections under threat: Envoy

Published: 2026-11-19 13:06:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326861/Iran-won-t-accept-unrestricted-inspections-under-threat-Envoy

London, IRNA — Iran’s Ambassador and Permanent Representative to International Organizations in Vienna stresses that Tehran will not submit to unrestricted inspections under continued military threats, stating that any request from the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) will be decided based on security considerations.
