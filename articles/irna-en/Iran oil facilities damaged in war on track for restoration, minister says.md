# Iran oil facilities damaged in war on track for restoration, minister says

Published: 2026-09-16 15:07:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266083/Iran-oil-facilities-damaged-in-war-on-track-for-restoration

Tehran, IRNA – Oil Minister Mohsen Paknejad says that the reconstruction of oil industry facilities damaged during the US-Israeli-imposed war was progressing well, with part of the lost capacity expected to return by the end of the current calendar year to late March.
