# President Pezeshkian congratulates Jafari, Beiranvand on AFC honors

Published: 2026-11-21 05:27:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327817/President-Pezeshkian-congratulates-Jafari-Beiranvand-on-AFC

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated Marzieh Jafari on her second consecutive title as Asia’s best women’s football coach, as well as Alireza Beiranvand on his selection as AFC Player of the Year.
