# Six drones targeted US air defense radar at Bahrain helicopter base: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-15 05:45:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321974/Six-drones-targeted-US-air-defense-radar-at-Bahrain-helicopter

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has announced that six drones struck a US air defense radar installation at the Riffa helicopter base in Bahrain during the second phase of Iran’s response to US military aggression.
