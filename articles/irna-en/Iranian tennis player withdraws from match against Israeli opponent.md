# Iranian tennis player withdraws from match against Israeli opponent

Published: 2026-11-27 14:42:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86334063/Iranian-tennis-player-withdraws-from-match-against-Israeli-opponent

Tehran, IRNA — Ali Yazdani, an Iranian tennis player, withdrew from the men’s doubles quarterfinals of the ITF World Tennis Tour in Antalya, Türkiye, to avoid playing an opponent from the Israeli regime.
