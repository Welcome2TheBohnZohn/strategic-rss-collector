# Iran’s taekwondo star wins gold at 2026 Asian Games

Published: 2026-10-03 10:18:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86281706/Iran-s-taekwondo-star-wins-gold-at-2026-Asian-Games

Nagoya, IRNA – Iran’s Arian Salimi has decisively won the gold medal in the men’s +80kg taekwondo event at the 2026 Asian Games.
