# Iran, Oman agree to open temporary shipping corridor through Strait of Hormuz: Top security official

Published: 2026-08-28 06:15:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248254/Iran-Oman-agree-to-open-temporary-shipping-corridor-through

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council Major General Mohsen Rezaei says Iran and Oman have agreed to open a temporary corridor through the Strait of Hormuz for ships to enter and exit.
