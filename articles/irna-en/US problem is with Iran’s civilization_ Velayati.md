# US problem is with Iran’s civilization: Velayati

Published: 2026-10-10 09:26:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288259/US-problem-is-with-Iran-s-civilization-Velayati

Tehran, IRNA — Ali Akbar Velayati, a senior adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution on international affairs, has responded to remarks by US Secretary of State Marco Rubio, saying that Washington’s problem is not with the Islamic Revolution but with Iran’s strong and independent civilization.
