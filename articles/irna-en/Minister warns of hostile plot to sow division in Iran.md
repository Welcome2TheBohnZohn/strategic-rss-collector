# Minister warns of hostile plot to sow division in Iran

Published: 2026-08-27 12:31:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247781/Minister-warns-of-hostile-plot-to-sow-division-in-Iran

Hamedan, IRNA – Minister of Justice Amin Hossein Rahimi has warned that attempts to sow division and weaken national unity pose a greater threat to Iran than external pressure, stressing the country will not yield to threats.
