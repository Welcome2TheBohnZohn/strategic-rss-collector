# Four oil tankers trying to break the ban hit mines; IRGC shoots down 56th MQ-9 drone

Published: 2026-11-04 06:16:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311879/Four-oil-tankers-trying-to-break-the-ban-hit-mines-IRGC-shoots

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Public Relations has announced that four violating oil tankers were destroyed after mines, two other violating vessels were halted, and the 56th MQ-9 drone was shot down.
