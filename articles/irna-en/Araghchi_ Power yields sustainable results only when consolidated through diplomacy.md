# Araghchi: Power yields sustainable results only when consolidated through diplomacy

Published: 2026-09-19 21:30:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268444/Araghchi-Power-yields-sustainable-results-only-when-consolidated

Tehran, IRNA – In the new preface to the sixth edition of his book titled 'The Power of Negotiation', Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi writes, diplomacy and power are not in opposition to one another; rather, they move in the same direction and in tandem.
