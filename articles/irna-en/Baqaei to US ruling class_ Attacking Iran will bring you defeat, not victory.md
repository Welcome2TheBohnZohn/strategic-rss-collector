# Baqaei to US ruling class: Attacking Iran will bring you defeat, not victory

Published: 2026-11-04 13:23:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312660/Baqaei-to-US-ruling-class-Attacking-Iran-will-bring-you-defeat
