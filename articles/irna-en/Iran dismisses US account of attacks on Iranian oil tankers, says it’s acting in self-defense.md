# Iran dismisses US account of attacks on Iranian oil tankers, says it’s acting in self-defense

Published: 2026-09-07 11:04:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257492/Iran-dismisses-US-account-of-attacks-on-Iranian-oil-tankers

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has dismissed the US narrative about its attacks on Iranian oil tankers in the Persian Gulf, saying Iran is acting in self-defense in the face of a maritime blockade and economic war imposed by Washington.
