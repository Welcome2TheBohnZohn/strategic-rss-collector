# Iran Greco-Roman wrestlers win four medals on opening day of world championships

Published: 2026-10-25 17:19:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303189/Iran-Greco-Roman-wrestlers-win-four-medals-on-opening-day-of

Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s Greco-Roman team claimed one gold, two silver and one bronze medal across the first four weight classes at the World Wrestling Championships in Astana on Sunday.
