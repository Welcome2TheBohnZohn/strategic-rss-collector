# Ship carrying 2,500 containers hits mine, sinks south of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-14 10:35:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292706/Ship-carrying-2-500-containers-hits-mine-sinks-south-of-Strait

Tehran, IRNA — A container ship carrying more than 2,500 containers has sunk in waters south of the Strait of Hormuz after hitting a naval mine and catching fire, the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says.
