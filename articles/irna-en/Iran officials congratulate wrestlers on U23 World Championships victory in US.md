# Iran officials congratulate wrestlers on U23 World Championships victory in US

Published: 2026-10-15 10:41:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293480/Iran-officials-congratulate-wrestlers-on-U23-World-Championships

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian officials, in separate messages, have congratulated the country’s Greco-Roman wrestling team on winning the U23 World Championships in the United States, praising the athletes’ determination and international achievement.
