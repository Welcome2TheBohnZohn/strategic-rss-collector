# Fourth violating oil tanker explodes after hitting mine east of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-24 13:32:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331453/Fourth-violating-oil-tanker-explodes-after-hitting-mine-east

Tehran, IRNA – A fourth oil tanker violating designated maritime routes in the Strait of Hormuz exploded and caught fire after striking a naval mine early on Tuesday, the IRGC Navy announced.
