# Baqaei: US visa ban on Iranian president’s media team is an attempt to hide the truth

Published: 2026-09-21 21:30:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270589/Baqaei-US-visa-ban-on-Iranian-president-s-media-team-is-an-attempt

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says the Trump administration’s denial of visas to members of President Masoud Pezeshkian’s media team ahead of this week’s United Nations General Assembly is not an exercise in managing access; it is an attempt to conceal the truth and push a campaign of misinformation.
