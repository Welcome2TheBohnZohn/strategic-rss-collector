# IRGC shoots down 19th US MQ-1 drone

Published: 2026-11-21 13:30:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328446/IRGC-shoots-down-19th-US-MQ-1-drone

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) has shot down a US MQ-1 drone over the southern Iranian island of Sirik, marking the 19th aircraft of its type destroyed, the IRGC said on Saturday.
