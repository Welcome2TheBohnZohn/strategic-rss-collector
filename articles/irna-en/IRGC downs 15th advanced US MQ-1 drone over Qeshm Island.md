# IRGC downs 15th advanced US MQ-1 drone over Qeshm Island

Published: 2026-11-04 19:56:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312921/IRGC-downs-15th-advanced-US-MQ-1-drone-over-Qeshm-Island

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) said it has intercepted and destroyed a US MQ-1 drone over Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, marking the 15th aircraft of its kind brought down by Iranian air defenses.
