# Iran’s enemy failed to achieve its objectives: Army commander

Published: 2026-08-29 10:29:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249370/Iran-s-enemy-failed-to-achieve-its-objectives-Army-commander

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Army Commander Major General Amir Hatami says the enemy failed to achieve any of its objectives during the recent imposed wars, despite attempts to destroy Iran’s missile and drone capabilities.
