# Iran president to visit Moscow for GECF summit

Published: 2026-10-26 09:25:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303771/Iran-president-to-visit-Moscow-for-GECF-summit

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian will travel to Moscow on Monday to attend and address the eighth summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF), with talks on bilateral cooperation also on his agenda.
