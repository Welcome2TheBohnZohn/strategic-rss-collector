# US, Israeli regime are sworn enemies of Muslim unity: Iran Leader

Published: 2026-08-30 06:09:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249966/US-Israeli-regime-are-sworn-enemies-of-Muslim-unity-Iran-Leader

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has described the United States and the Israeli regime as “sworn enemies” of unity among Muslims, stressing the need for greater solidarity and cooperation across the Islamic world.
