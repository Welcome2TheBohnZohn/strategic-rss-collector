# Hamas urges international pressure on Israel to honor Gaza commitments

Published: 2026-11-07 14:19:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315043/Hamas-urges-international-pressure-on-Israel-to-honor-Gaza-commitments

Tehran, IRNA – The Palestinian Islamic Resistance Movement (Hamas), has called on international mediators and the Arab League to take urgent action to ensure Israel fulfils its commitments, concerning Gaza, particularly by allowing humanitarian aid into the Palestinian territory.
