# Any assessment of Hormuz crisis ‘incomplete’ without addressing root causes: Iran’s UN envoy

Published: 2026-09-14 16:14:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86264119/Any-assessment-of-Hormuz-crisis-incomplete-without-addressing

London, IRNA - Iran’s permanent representative to the United Nations Office in Geneva has warned that any assessment of the economic and developmental consequences of the Strait of Hormuz crisis would be incomplete without addressing its root causes, which he attributed directly to US and Israeli military aggression.
