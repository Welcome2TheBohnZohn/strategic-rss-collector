# Eleven offending oil tankers catch fire in Strait of Hormuz in nine days: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-11-02 13:53:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310371/Eleven-offending-oil-tankers-catch-fire-in-Strait-of-Hormuz-in

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says 11 offending oil tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz within nine days, from October 25 to November 2.
