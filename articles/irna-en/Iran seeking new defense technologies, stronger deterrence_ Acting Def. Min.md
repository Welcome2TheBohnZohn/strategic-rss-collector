# Iran seeking new defense technologies, stronger deterrence: Acting Def. Min.

Published: 2026-09-17 13:06:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266783/Iran-seeking-new-defense-technologies-stronger-deterrence-Acting

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s acting defense minister has stated that today, his ministry is seeking to create new technologies and enhance deterrence power through utilizing the full capacity of the country’s human resources, particularly specialized women.
