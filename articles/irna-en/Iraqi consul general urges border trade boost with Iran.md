# Iraqi consul general urges border trade boost with Iran

Published: 2026-09-07 14:04:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257685/Iraqi-consul-general-urges-border-trade-boost-with-Iran

Ilam, IRNA –Iraq's consul general in Kermanshah met with the governor of Ilam Province to discuss ways to expand border cooperation and trade between the border provinces of the two neighboring countries.
