# Araghchi: International community must hold US and Israeli regime accountable for their illegal actions

Published: 2026-09-28 20:39:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277628/Araghchi-International-community-must-hold-US-and-Israeli-regime

New York, IRNA – The Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran attributed the prevailing insecurity in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz to the interventionist and illegal actions of the United States and the Zionist regime, saying that the international community must hold them accountable for all the resulting humanitarian, security, and economic consequences.
