# Iranian parliament deputy speaker condemns US attack on wedding ceremony in Sirik

Published: 2026-09-02 11:07:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253281/Iranian-parliament-deputy-speaker-condemns-US-attack-on-wedding

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad has condemned a US attack on a wedding ceremony in Kuhestak, Sirik County, describing it as a continuation of the attacks on Minab and Lamerd on February 28, 2026, and saying Washington is seeking to disrupt everyday life in Iran.
