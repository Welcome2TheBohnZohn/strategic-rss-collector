# Iran’s vice president calls on regional rulers for rationality, unity

Published: 2026-09-11 11:31:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86261007/Iran-s-vice-president-calls-on-regional-rulers-for-rationality

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s first vice president hailed the Yemeni nation’s recent victories and called on regional rulers to embrace rationality, overcome dependency, and unite under the flag of Islam.
