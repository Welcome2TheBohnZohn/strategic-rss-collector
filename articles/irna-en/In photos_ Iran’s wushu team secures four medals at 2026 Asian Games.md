# In photos: Iran’s wushu team secures four medals at 2026 Asian Games

Published: 2026-09-23 07:46:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86272180/In-photos-Iran-s-wushu-team-secures-four-medals-at-2026-Asian

Iran’s wushu team secured four medals on the third day of the Nagoya 2026 Asian Games after four Iranian sanda fighters advanced to the semifinals, while Helia Asadian narrowly missed out on a bronze medal in the taolu competition, finishing fourth by just 0.003 points.
