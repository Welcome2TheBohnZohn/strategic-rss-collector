# In photos: Iran celebrates ancient Mehregan Festival

Published: 2026-10-10 07:40:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86288159/In-photos-Iran-celebrates-ancient-Mehregan-Festival

The ancient Iranian festival of Mehregan was celebrated at the historic Karaftu Cave in Divandarreh County, Kurdistan Province, on October 9, 2026, bringing together artists and enthusiasts of Iran’s cultural heritage to honor longstanding traditions centered on friendship, fidelity, solidarity and gratitude for nature’s blessings.
