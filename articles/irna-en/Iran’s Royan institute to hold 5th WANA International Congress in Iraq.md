# Iran’s Royan institute to hold 5th WANA International Congress in Iraq

Published: 2026-11-25 07:06:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331991/Iran-s-Royan-institute-to-hold-5th-WANA-International-Congress

Tehran, IRNA – Royan Institute, affiliated with the Academic Center for Education, Culture and Research (ACECR), will hold the 5th International Congress of the West Asia and North Africa (WANA) Scientific Network in Karbala, Iraq, on December 17–18, 2026.
