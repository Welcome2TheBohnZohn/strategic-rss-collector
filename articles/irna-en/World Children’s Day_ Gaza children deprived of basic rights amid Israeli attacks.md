# World Children’s Day: Gaza children deprived of basic rights amid Israeli attacks

Published: 2026-11-20 09:45:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327326/World-Children-s-Day-Gaza-children-deprived-of-basic-rights

Tehran, IRNA – Thousands of children in Gaza continue to suffer from Israeli attacks, hunger, displacement, and deprivation of medical care and education, as the world marks World Children’s Day and the anniversary of the adoption of the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child.
