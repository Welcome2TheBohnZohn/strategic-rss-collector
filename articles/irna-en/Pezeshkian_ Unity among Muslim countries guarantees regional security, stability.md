# Pezeshkian: Unity among Muslim countries guarantees regional security, stability

Published: 2026-11-03 17:14:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311666/Pezeshkian-Unity-among-Muslim-countries-guarantees-regional

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has described unity among Muslim nations and stronger regional cooperation as strategic necessities for safeguarding security and stability, saying solidarity would prevent the Israeli regime from pursuing its objectives in the region.
