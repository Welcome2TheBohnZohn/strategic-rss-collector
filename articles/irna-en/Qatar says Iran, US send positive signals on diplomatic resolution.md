# Qatar says Iran, US send positive signals on diplomatic resolution

Published: 2026-11-10 12:42:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318116/Qatar-says-Iran-US-send-positive-signals-on-diplomatic-resolution

Tehran, IRNA – Qatar’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs says it has received positive signals from Iran and the United States regarding a diplomatic resolution to their differences, stressing that Doha will continue its mediation efforts to reach a comprehensive agreement and ensure regional security and stability.
