# Iran’s US chemical weapons compliance concerns remain unresolved: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-07 09:38:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285951/Iran-s-US-chemical-weapons-compliance-concerns-remain-unresolved

London, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador and permanent representative to the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW) has said Tehran’s concerns over US non-compliance with the Chemical Weapons Convention and its calls for clarification have remained unanswered for more than two decades.
