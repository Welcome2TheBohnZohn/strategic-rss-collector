# Two more tankers hit naval mines in Strait of Hormuz, says IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-11-16 06:16:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322979/Two-more-tankers-hit-naval-mines-in-Strait-of-Hormuz-says-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has reported a massive explosion and intense fire involving two more oil tankers after they hit naval mines, while attempting to transit via prohibited and unsafe routes in the Strait of Hormuz.
