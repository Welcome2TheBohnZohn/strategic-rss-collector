# Pakistan PM: Aggression against Iran main cause of regional crisis; diplomacy only solution

Published: 2026-09-01 11:09:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252011/Pakistan-PM-Aggression-against-Iran-main-cause-of-regional-crisis

Islamabad, IRNA – Pakistan’s Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has said that the violation of Iran’s sovereignty and territorial integrity earlier this year triggered a major regional crisis, stressing that diplomacy remains the only sustainable way to resolve the conflict.
