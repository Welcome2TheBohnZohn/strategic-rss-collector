# Iran, Russia call for expanded judicial cooperation

Published: 2026-10-01 12:26:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280297/Iran-Russia-call-for-expanded-judicial-cooperation

Moscow, IRNA – Prosecutor General Mohammad Movahedi-Azad and his Russian counterpart, Aleksandr Vladimirovich Gutsan, have emphasized the need to expand judicial cooperation and strengthen bilateral and multilateral legal ties between the two countries.
