# Araghchi meets Greek counterpart in New York

Published: 2026-09-22 18:16:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271693/Araghchi-meets-Greek-counterpart-in-New-York

New York, IRNA — Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in a meeting with his Greek counterpart, Giorgos Gerapetritis, on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly meeting in New York discussed bilateral ties and international developments.
