# IRGC Navy Commander: Do not be deceived by US; Any suspicious move will be targeted

Published: 2026-09-05 20:39:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255906/IRGC-Navy-Commander-Do-not-be-deceived-by-US-Any-suspicious

Tehran, IRNA – In a statement emphasizing that "do not be deceived by the US" and warning that "any suspicious move will be targeted," the Commander of the IRGC Navy declared that the act of aggression by the US terrorist military—in attacking three oil tankers belonging to the Islamic Republic of Iran—will be met with a firm response.
