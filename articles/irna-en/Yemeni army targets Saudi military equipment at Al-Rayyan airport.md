# Yemeni army targets Saudi military equipment at Al-Rayyan airport

Published: 2026-10-21 14:59:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299719/Yemeni-army-targets-Saudi-military-equipment-at-Al-Rayyan-airport

Tehran, IRNA – The Yemeni armed forces announced on Wednesday, October 21, that it had targeted military equipment transferred by Saudi Arabia to Yemen’s Al-Rayyan Airport via military transport aircraft.
