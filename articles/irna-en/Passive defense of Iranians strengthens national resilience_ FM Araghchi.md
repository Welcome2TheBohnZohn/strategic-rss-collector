# Passive defense of Iranians strengthens national resilience: FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-26 10:23:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303859/Passive-defense-of-Iranians-strengthens-national-resilience

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has highlighted the role of people-oriented passive defense in strengthening the Iranian nation's resilience and steadfastness during the two recent US-Israeli-imposed wars.
