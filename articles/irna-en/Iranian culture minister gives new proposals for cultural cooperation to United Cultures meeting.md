# Iranian culture minister gives new proposals for cultural cooperation to United Cultures meeting

Published: 2026-09-25 19:25:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86274344/Iranian-culture-minister-gives-new-proposals-for-cultural-cooperation

Moscow, IRNA –Speaking at the 12th International Forum of United Cultures, the Minister of Culture and Islamic Guidance outlined the Islamic Republic of Iran’s proposals for strengthening bilateral and multilateral cultural cooperation; highlighting certain aspects of the crimes committed by aggressors against the country, he emphasized that the Iranian nation's steadfastness in the face of recent events is undoubtedly a manifestation of the country's civilizational resilience.
