# Pezeshkian: Iran will not surrender to military aggression

Published: 2026-11-02 16:26:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310446/Pezeshkian-Iran-will-not-surrender-to-military-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says Iran will not surrender to military aggression, stressing that the country will firmly resist its enemies while strengthening public resilience and addressing people’s concerns.
