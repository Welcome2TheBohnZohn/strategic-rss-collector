# Araghchi’s message of appreciation to President Pezeshkian: Diplomacy is at forefront of defending Iran

Published: 2026-08-27 16:59:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248043/Araghchi-s-message-of-appreciation-to-President-Pezeshkian-Diplomacy

Tehran, IRNA – Following the selection of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as the "Outstanding Agency" in the "Yavar-e Fath" (Supporter of Victory) category of the 21st Shahid Rajaei Festival, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi issued a message to President Masoud Pezeshkian expressing appreciation for the support and confidence shown by him and the government toward the Ministry.
