# Iran should use shifting global order to mitigate sanctions impact: MP

Published: 2026-09-16 09:00:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265624/Iran-should-use-shifting-global-order-to-mitigate-sanctions-impact

Tehran, IRNA – A member of the Iranian Parliament’s National Security and Foreign Policy Commission has called for leveraging emerging opportunities in the changing global order, saying expanded cooperation with independent countries can help Iran mitigate the impact of sanctions.
