# Greeks protest Rubio visit, US military presence

Published: 2026-10-07 08:23:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285815/Greeks-protest-Rubio-visit-US-military-presence

Tehran, IRNA – Demonstrators in Athens and Thessaloniki have staged anti-war rallies to protest US policies and the expansion of military cooperation between Greece and the United States as US Secretary of State Marco Rubio visited the country.
