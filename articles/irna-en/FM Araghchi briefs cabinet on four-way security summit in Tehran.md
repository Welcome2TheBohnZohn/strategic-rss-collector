# FM Araghchi briefs cabinet on four-way security summit in Tehran

Published: 2026-10-28 14:34:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306180/FM-Araghchi-briefs-cabinet-on-four-way-security-summit-in-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA –Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has presented a report on the latest diplomatic developments and consultations on regional and international issues, especially the recent quadrilateral summit between Iran, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Turkiye.
