# Iran warns South Korea over military presence in Persian Gulf

Published: 2026-09-07 08:41:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257207/Iran-warns-South-Korea-over-military-presence-in-Persian-Gulf

Tehran, IRNA — Iran has warned South Korea against any military participation in the Persian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz, saying such involvement would amount to direct support for an aggressor and could have serious consequences.
