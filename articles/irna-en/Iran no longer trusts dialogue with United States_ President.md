# Iran no longer trusts dialogue with United States: President

Published: 2026-09-26 12:28:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275158/Iran-no-longer-trusts-dialogue-with-United-States-President

Tehran, IRNA –President Masoud Pezeshkian has said Iran no longer trusts negotiations with Washington, pointing to repeated US breaches of the diplomatic process, attacks and sanctions imposed after every round of talks with Tehran.
