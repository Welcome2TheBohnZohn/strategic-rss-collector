# Mexican artist honors Minab school victims with ‘Butterflies of Minab’

Published: 2026-10-21 07:41:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298974/Mexican-artist-honors-Minab-school-victims-with-Butterflies

Tehran, IRNA – Mexican artist María Schietekat has honored the children killed in a US airstrike on Shajareh Tayyebeh Elementary School in Iran’s southern city of Minab by creating an artwork titled “Butterflies of Minab” and presenting it to Iran’s ambassador to Mexico.
