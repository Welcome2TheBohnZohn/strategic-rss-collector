# Trump’s new threat will not break Iranian nation’s resolve: Ambassador

Published: 2026-10-20 07:04:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297887/Trump-s-new-threat-will-not-break-Iranian-nation-s-resolve-Ambassador

Moscow, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to the Russian Federation, Kazem Jalali, referring to the US President Donald Trump’s threat of a renewed aggression against the Islamic Republic — specifically the potential use of the so-called “mother of all bombs” — says such threats will not break the resolve of the Iranian nation.
