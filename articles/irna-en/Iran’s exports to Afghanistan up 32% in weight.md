# Iran’s exports to Afghanistan up 32% in weight

Published: 2026-11-22 05:47:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328794/Iran-s-exports-to-Afghanistan-up-32-in-weight

Taybad, IRNA – The weight of goods exported to Afghanistan through the Dogharoun border crossing in northeastern Iran increased by 32% in the first seven months of the current Iranian calendar year (March 21-October 22) compared to the same period last year, a local customs official says.
