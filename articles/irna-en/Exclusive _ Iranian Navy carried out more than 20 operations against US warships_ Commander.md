# Exclusive | Iranian Navy carried out more than 20 operations against US warships: Commander

Published: 2026-10-29 09:52:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306716/Exclusive-Iranian-Navy-carried-out-more-than-20-operations

Tehran, IRNA – Naval forces of the Iranian Army carried out more than 20 operations against US warships during the 40-day conflict that began on February 28, while approximately 300 additional operations targeted American interests in various locations, including through missile and drone strikes, the coordinating deputy of the Islamic Republic of Iran Army Navy has said.
