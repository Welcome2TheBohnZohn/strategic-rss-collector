# Offending tanker hits mines, catches fire east of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-05 13:47:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313445/Offending-tanker-hits-mines-catches-fire-east-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Navy says that in the early hours of Thursday, an offending oil tanker caught fire after hitting a naval mine while passing through an unsafe area east of the Strait of Hormuz.
