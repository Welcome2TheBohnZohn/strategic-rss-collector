# Iran strongly rejects membership of US to IAEA BoG

Published: 2026-09-17 15:18:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266841/Iran-strongly-rejects-membership-of-US-to-IAEA-BoG

London, IRNA — Iran has strongly rejected the US designation to the IAEA Board of Governors as incompatible with the letter and spirit of the Statute, as well as the Agency’s founding principles.
