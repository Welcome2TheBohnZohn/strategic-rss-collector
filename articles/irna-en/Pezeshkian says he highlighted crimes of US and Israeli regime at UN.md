# Pezeshkian says he highlighted crimes of US and Israeli regime at UN

Published: 2026-09-26 19:52:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275492/Pezeshkian-says-he-highlighted-crimes-of-US-and-Israeli-regime

Tehran, IRNA – Elaborating on his meetings and sessions at the 81st UN General Assembly, President Masoud Pezeshkian emphasized: "In my speech and all my meetings, I highlighted the plight of the Iranian people and the crimes committed by the Americans and the Zionists."
