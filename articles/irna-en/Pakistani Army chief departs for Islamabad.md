# Pakistani Army chief departs for Islamabad

Published: 2026-10-20 19:18:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298747/Pakistani-Army-chief-departs-for-Islamabad

Tehran, IRNA – Field Marshal Asim Munir, the Commander of the Pakistan Army, and his accompanying delegation departed Tehran on Tuesday evening, following consultations with senior Iranian officials.
