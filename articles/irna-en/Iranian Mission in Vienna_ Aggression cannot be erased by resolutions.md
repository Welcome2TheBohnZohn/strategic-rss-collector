# Iranian Mission in Vienna: Aggression cannot be erased by resolutions

Published: 2026-11-18 19:49:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326246/Iran-s-Mission-in-Vienna-Aggression-Cannot-Be-Erased-by-Resolutions

London, IRNA – Iranian Permanent Mission to International Organizations in Vienna criticized the legal and technical foundations of the International Atomic Energy Agency's reports on Iran, calling on the IAEA Board of Governors to address the consequences of military attacks against the country's nuclear facilities.
