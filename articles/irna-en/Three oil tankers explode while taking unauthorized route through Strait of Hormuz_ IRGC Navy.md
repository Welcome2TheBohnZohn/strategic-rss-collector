# Three oil tankers explode while taking unauthorized route through Strait of Hormuz: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-10-29 09:45:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306780/Three-oil-tankers-explode-while-taking-unauthorized-route-through

Tehran, IRNA – Three oil tankers exploded and caught fire after striking sea mines while attempting to navigate an unauthorized route south of the Strait of Hormuz, the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced.
