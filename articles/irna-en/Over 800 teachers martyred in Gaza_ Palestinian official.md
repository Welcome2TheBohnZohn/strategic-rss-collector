# Over 800 teachers martyred in Gaza: Palestinian official

Published: 2026-11-01 06:39:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308800/Over-800-teachers-martyred-in-Gaza-Palestinian-official

Tehran, IRNA – More than 800 teachers have been martyred in the Gaza Strip since October 2023, a Palestinian education official says, describing the targeting of teachers as an attack on the future of Palestinian generations and their right to education and life.
