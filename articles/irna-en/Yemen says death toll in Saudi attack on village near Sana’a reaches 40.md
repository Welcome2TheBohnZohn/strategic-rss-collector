# Yemen says death toll in Saudi attack on village near Sana’a reaches 40

Published: 2026-11-09 06:22:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86316500/Yemen-says-death-toll-in-Saudi-attack-on-village-near-Sana-a

Tehran, IRNA — The death toll from Saudi airstrikes on a village near Yemen’s capital has reached 40, with another 40 people wounded, according to the Health Ministry in Sana’a.
