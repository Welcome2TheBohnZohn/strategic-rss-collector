# 159 Iranian prisoners repatriated from Iraq: Iranian judicial official

Published: 2026-09-20 11:57:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269146/159-Iranian-prisoners-repatriated-from-Iraq-Iranian-judicial

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Justice Ministry has announced the repatriation of 159 Iranian prisoners from Iraq following joint efforts with the Judiciary and the Foreign Ministry.
