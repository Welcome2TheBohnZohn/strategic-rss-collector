# Araghchi warns Merz: ‘Red lines are a thing of the past’

Published: 2026-10-31 11:35:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86308341/Araghchi-warns-Merz-Red-lines-are-a-thing-of-the-past

Responding to remarks by German Chancellor Friedrich Merz regarding the cost of rising energy prices — which has exceeded 100 billion euros for European companies and consumers over the past eight months — Iran’s foreign minister said, “Red lines are a thing of the past.”
