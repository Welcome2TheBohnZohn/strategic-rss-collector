# Iran warns Bahrain, regional states against attacks from their territory

Published: 2026-10-06 06:09:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284434/Iran-warns-Bahrain-regional-states-against-hosting-attacks

New York, IRNA – Tehran has warned Bahrain and other regional states against allowing their territory or airspace to be used in attacks on Iran, stressing that it will respond if they allow such attacks again.
