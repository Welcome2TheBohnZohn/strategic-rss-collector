# Iran’s Yousofi breaks world record, wins clean and jerk gold at World Championships

Published: 2026-11-08 10:04:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315772/Iran-s-Yousofi-breaks-world-record-wins-clean-and-jerk-gold

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian weightlifter Alireza Yousefi won the clean and jerk gold medal with a world-record lift and secured silver overall in the super-heavyweight category at the 2026 World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo, China.
