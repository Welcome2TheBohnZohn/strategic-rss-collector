# Ghalibaf says era of unipolar order is over

Published: 2026-09-18 07:45:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86267141/Ghalibaf-says-era-of-unipolar-order-is-over

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says that the era of a unipolar world order has come to an end, praising recent vetoes by China and Russia at the UN Security Council.
