# Baqaei: Canada’s support for US actions against Iran ‘a display of submission to intimidation’

Published: 2026-09-08 06:40:07

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258086/Baqaei-Canada-s-support-for-US-actions-against-Iran-a-display

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has strongly criticized Canada for supporting the United States’ economic warfare against Iran, describing Ottawa’s position as “submission to intimidation”, which will not protect it from aggression by its “bullying neighbor.”
