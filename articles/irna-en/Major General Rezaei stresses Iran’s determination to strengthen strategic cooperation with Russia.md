# Major General Rezaei stresses Iran’s determination to strengthen strategic cooperation with Russia

Published: 2026-10-19 21:24:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297637/Major-General-Rezaei-stresses-Iran-s-determination-to-strengthen

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, Major General Mohsen Rezaei, in a meeting with Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov in Tehran, has emphasized the Islamic Republic of Iran’s determination to strengthen strategic cooperation between Tehran and Moscow.
