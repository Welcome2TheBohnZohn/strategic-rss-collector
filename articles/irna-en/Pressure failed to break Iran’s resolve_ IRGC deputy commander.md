# Pressure failed to break Iran’s resolve: IRGC deputy commander

Published: 2026-10-30 08:50:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86307324/Pressure-failed-to-break-Iran-s-resolve-IRGC-deputy-commander

Gorgan, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) deputy commander-in-chief has said hostile efforts to weaken Iran through war, sanctions and unrest have failed to undermine the Iranian people’s resolve.
