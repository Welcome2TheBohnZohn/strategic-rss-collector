# Iran says ready to help draft global treaty on plastic pollution

Published: 2026-10-28 10:02:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305978/Iran-says-ready-to-help-draft-global-treaty-on-plastic-pollution

Tehran, IRNA — Iran has declared its readiness to contribute effectively to drafting a global treaty to combat plastic pollution, stressing that the agreement must respect countries’ national circumstances, development rights, and capacities.
