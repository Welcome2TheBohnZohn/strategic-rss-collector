# Pezeshkian opens 246 tourism projects worth 350 trillion rials

Published: 2026-09-27 14:10:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276358/Pezeshkian-opens-246-tourism-projects-worth-350-trillion-rials

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian on Sunday inaugurated 246 tourism projects with a total investment of 350 trillion rials and expected to create 3,200 jobs.
