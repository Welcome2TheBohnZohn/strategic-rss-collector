# UK expands unilateral sanctions against Iran

Published: 2026-09-08 13:08:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258619/UK-expands-unilateral-sanctions-against-Iran

London, IRNA – The UK government, maintaining its hostile and unconstructive stance toward Iran, has issued new regulations to expand unilateral sanctions on the financial, commercial, and aviation sectors.
