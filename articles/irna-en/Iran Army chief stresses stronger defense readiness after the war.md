# Iran Army chief stresses stronger defense readiness after the war

Published: 2026-09-02 16:40:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253640/Iran-Army-chief-stresses-stronger-defense-readiness-after-the

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Armed Forces must strengthen their readiness and continue building defensive capabilities following the recent US-Israeli war against the country, Army Chief Major General Amir Hatami said during a meeting with Acting Defense Minister Brigadier General Majid Ibn-e-Reza.
