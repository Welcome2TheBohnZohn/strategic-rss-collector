# Iran Chamber chief says joint committee with Uruguay in the works

Published: 2026-11-10 14:27:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86318222/Iran-Chamber-chief-says-joint-committee-with-Uruguay-in-the-works

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is pursuing the establishment of a joint committee of commerce with Uruguay to strengthen bilateral trade and investment ties, the president of the Iran Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture said on Tuesday.
