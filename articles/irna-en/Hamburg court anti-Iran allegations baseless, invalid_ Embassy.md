# Hamburg court anti-Iran allegations baseless, invalid: Embassy

Published: 2026-10-16 16:16:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294313/Hamburg-court-anti-Iran-allegations-baseless-invalid-Embassy

London, IRNA — Iran’s Embassy in Germany has dismissed a Hamburg court ruling based on security-related and baseless claims against Tehran as invalid, describing it as part of the West’s double standards and political posturing against Iran.
