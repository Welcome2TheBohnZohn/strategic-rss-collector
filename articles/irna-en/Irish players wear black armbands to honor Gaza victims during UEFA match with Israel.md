# Irish players wear black armbands to honor Gaza victims during UEFA match with Israel

Published: 2026-09-28 10:39:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277206/Irish-players-wear-black-armbands-to-honor-Gaza-victims-during

Tehran, IRNA — Republic of Ireland football players, facing the Israeli team in a UEFA Nations League match on Sunday, bowed their heads during Israel’s national anthem and wore black armbands in solidarity with Palestinians in Gaza.
