# Araghchi: ECO convergence must translate into practical action for common interests

Published: 2026-11-16 06:35:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323060/Araghchi-ECO-convergence-must-translate-into-practical-action

Islamabad, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has underscored the importance of deepening convergence and cooperation among member states of the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO), urging them to translate shared commitments into concrete actions that serve their collective interests.
