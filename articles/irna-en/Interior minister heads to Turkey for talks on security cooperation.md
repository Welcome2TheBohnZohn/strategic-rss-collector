# Interior minister heads to Turkey for talks on security cooperation

Published: 2026-09-15 14:11:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265043/Interior-minister-heads-to-Turkey-for-talks-on-security-cooperation

Tehran, IRNA – Interior Minister Eskandar Momeni left for Ankara on Tuesday for a two-day visit to discuss bilateral ties, particularly police cooperation, counter-terrorism efforts and border security, Interior Ministry said.
