# Iranian envoys to Thailand, Vatican and South Africa report to Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-05 17:34:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86284242/Iranian-envoys-to-Thailand-Vatican-and-South-Africa-report-to

Tehran, IRNA – The ambassadors of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Thailand, the Vatican, and South Africa have met with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi to discuss the latest status of relations between the Islamic Republic of Iran and their respective countries of assignment.
