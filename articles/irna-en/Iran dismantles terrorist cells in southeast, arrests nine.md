# Iran dismantles terrorist cells in southeast, arrests nine

Published: 2026-09-07 10:45:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86257439/Iran-dismantles-terrorist-cells-in-southeast-arrests-nine

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence has announced that three operational cells belonging to a Takfiri terrorist group had been disbanded in the country’s southeast, with nine terrorists arrested and three others killed during clashes with security forces.
