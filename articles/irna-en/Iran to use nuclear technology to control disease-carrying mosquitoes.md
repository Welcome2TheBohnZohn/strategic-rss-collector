# Iran to use nuclear technology to control disease-carrying mosquitoes

Published: 2026-10-11 12:01:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289551/Iran-to-use-nuclear-technology-to-control-disease-carrying-mosquitoes

Tehran, IRNA – The Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) and the Ministry of Health have signed an agreement to use nuclear technology to control disease-carrying mosquitoes, including the Aedes aegypti species.
