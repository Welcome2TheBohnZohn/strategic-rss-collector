# Iran criticizes Grossi’s double standard in condemning attack on nuclear facilities

Published: 2026-11-09 20:49:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86317340/Iran-criticizes-Grossi-s-double-standard-in-condemning-attack

Tehran, IRNA —  The Iranian delegation in Vienna has criticized the double standard employed by the Director General of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) Rafael Grossi regarding the condemnation of attacks on nuclear facilities, saying that when Iranian facilities were bombed, Grossi deemed issuing a condemnation to be outside the scope of his mandate.
