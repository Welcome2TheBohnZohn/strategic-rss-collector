# Terrorist killed in clash with police in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-10-21 09:27:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299344/Terrorist-killed-in-clash-with-police-in-southeastern-Iran

Zahedan, IRNA – Police have killed a terrorist during a shootout in Khash County, southeastern Iran, while two others fled and remain at large, according to Sistan and Baluchestan Police Information Center.
