# IRNA chief visits sites of deadly US-Israeli airstrikes on Iran’s Lamerd

Published: 2026-10-13 08:30:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291444/IRNA-chief-visits-sites-of-deadly-US-Israeli-airstrikes-on-Iran-s

Shiraz, IRNA – Hossein Jaberi-Ansari, the CEO of the Islamic Republic News Agency, who has traveled to Iran’s southern Fars Province, has visited the sites and areas in the city of Lamerd that were damaged in the recent US-Israeli bombing campaign.
