# Iran dismantles terrorist cell, arrests arms smugglers

Published: 2026-11-11 10:31:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319013/Iran-dismantles-terrorist-cell-arrests-arms-smugglers

Tehran, IRNA – The Ministry of Intelligence has announced that five separatist terrorists and three arms smugglers have been identified and arrested in operations in Kermanshah, Alborz, and Tehran provinces.
