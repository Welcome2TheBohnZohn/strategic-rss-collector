# President Pezeshkian congratulates Iranian Greco-Roman wrestling team on world championship victory

Published: 2026-10-27 03:18:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304379/President-Pezeshkian-congratulates-Iranian-Greco-Roman-wrestling

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated the Iranian Greco-Roman wrestling team on its commanding early victory at the 2026 World Wrestling Championships in Astana, Kazakhstan, describing the achievement as a source of national pride.
