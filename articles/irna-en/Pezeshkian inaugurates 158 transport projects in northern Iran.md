# Pezeshkian inaugurates 158 transport projects in northern Iran

Published: 2026-08-26 15:45:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247009/Pezeshkian-inaugurates-158-transport-projects-in-northern-Iran

NOSHAHR, Iran — President Masoud Pezeshkian inaugurates 158 projects of the Ministry of Roads and Urban Development in Mazandaran province worth about 23 trillion tomans, during an online ceremony marking Government Week.
