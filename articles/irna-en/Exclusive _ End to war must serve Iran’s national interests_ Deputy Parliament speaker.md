# Exclusive | End to war must serve Iran’s national interests: Deputy Parliament speaker

Published: 2026-08-28 07:40:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248303/Exclusive-End-to-war-must-serve-Iran-s-national-interests

Tehran, IRNA – Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad has dismissed the US claim of an economic blockade on Iran as a “psychological operation,” stressing that any decision to end the war must be based on national security and interests, not pressure or war fatigue.
