# In photos: Int’l congresses on stem cells and biomedicine held in Iran

Published: 2026-09-06 07:58:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86256121/In-photos-Int-l-congresses-on-stem-cells-and-biomedicine-held

The “27th Reproductive Biomedicine” and the “22nd Congress on Stem Cell Biology and Technology,” organized by the Royan Institute, were held in Tehran on September 2, 2026. The head of the institution, Abdolhossein Shahverdi, along with professors, researchers, and students in the field, attended the international twin congress.
