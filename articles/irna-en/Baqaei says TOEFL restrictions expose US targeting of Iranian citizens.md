# Baqaei says TOEFL restrictions expose US targeting of Iranian citizens

Published: 2026-09-01 14:58:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252412/Baqaei-says-TOEFL-restrictions-expose-US-targeting-of-Iranian

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says US restrictions on international educational services, including the TOEFL test for Iranian users, show that Washington is targeting Iranian citizens in all areas.
