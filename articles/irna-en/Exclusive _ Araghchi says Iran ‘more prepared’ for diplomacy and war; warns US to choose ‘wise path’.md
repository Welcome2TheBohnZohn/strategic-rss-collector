# Exclusive | Araghchi says Iran ‘more prepared’ for diplomacy and war; warns US to choose ‘wise path’

Published: 2026-11-02 14:45:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86310439/Exclusive-Araghchi-says-Iran-more-prepared-for-diplomacy

Ashgabat, IRNA - Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Iran is prepared for both diplomatic and military scenarios “more than ever before,” stating that the ball is now in Washington’s court to choose between a “wise path” of negotiation or repeating the “mistake of military aggression.”
