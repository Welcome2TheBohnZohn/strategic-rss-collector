# Pezeshkian’s GECF summit participation to boost Iran’s role in forum: envoy

Published: 2026-10-27 08:56:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304791/Pezeshkian-s-GECF-summit-participation-to-boost-Iran-s-role-in

Moscow, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to Russia, Kazem Jalali, says President Masoud Pezeshkian’s participation in Tuesday’s summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF) in Russia is important for advancing Iran’s role in the forum and expanding cooperation with Moscow, particularly in energy and transport.
