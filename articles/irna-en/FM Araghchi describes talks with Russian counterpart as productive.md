# FM Araghchi describes talks with Russian counterpart as productive

Published: 2026-10-19 15:26:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297436/FM-Araghchi-describes-talks-with-Russian-counterpart-as-productive

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says talks with his Russian counterpart Sergey Lavrov in Tehran were intensive and productive, focusing on bilateral relations as well as key regional and international developments.
