# Iran’s nuclear industry showcases export potential at Shiraz Expo 2026

Published: 2026-10-14 06:07:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292210/Iran-s-nuclear-industry-showcases-export-potential-at-Shiraz

Shiraz, IRNA – The Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) has displayed its products and technological capabilities at Shiraz Expo 2026, highlighting potential export opportunities in healthcare, agriculture, nuclear medicine and radiation applications, including markets in the Persian Gulf littoral states.
