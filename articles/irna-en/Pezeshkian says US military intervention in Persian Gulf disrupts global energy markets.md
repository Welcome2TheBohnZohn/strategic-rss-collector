# Pezeshkian says US military intervention in Persian Gulf disrupts global energy markets

Published: 2026-10-27 15:00:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305178/Pezeshkian-says-US-military-intervention-in-Persian-Gulf-disrupts

Moscow, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian says that US military intervention in the Persian Gulf is disrupting global energy pricing, supply and transit, warning that its consequences extended far beyond regional countries and threatened international energy security and economic growth.
