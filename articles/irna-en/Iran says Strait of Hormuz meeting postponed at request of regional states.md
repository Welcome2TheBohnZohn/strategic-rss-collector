# Iran says Strait of Hormuz meeting postponed at request of regional states

Published: 2026-09-13 19:47:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86263200/Iran-says-Strait-of-Hormuz-meeting-postponed-at-request-of-regional

Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s Foreign Ministry confirmed Sunday that a planned meeting of foreign ministers from Persian Gulf coastal states has been postponed at the request of some regional countries.
