# President Pezeshkian underlines expansion of ties with Brazil

Published: 2026-10-26 07:29:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86303561/President-Pezeshkian-underlines-expansion-of-ties-with-Brazil

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has congratulated the victory of Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva in Brazil’s presidential election and stressed the expansion of relations between Iran and Brazil.
