# Two more violating supertankers catch fire in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-11-19 11:06:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326732/Two-more-violating-supertankers-catch-fire-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that two supertankers violating maritime regulations have caught fire and exploded after striking sea mines, after attempting to navigate an unauthorized route through the Strait of Hormuz.
