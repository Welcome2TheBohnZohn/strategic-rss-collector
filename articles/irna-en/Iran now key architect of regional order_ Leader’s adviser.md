# Iran now key architect of regional order: Leader’s adviser

Published: 2026-11-06 10:28:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313897/Iran-now-key-architect-of-regional-order-Leader-s-adviser

Tehran, IRNA – Ali-Akbar Velayati, a senior adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, says the geopolitical Tsunami sweeping West Asia has engulfed the Israeli and US elections, stressing that Iran has now emerged as one of the main architects of the regional order.
