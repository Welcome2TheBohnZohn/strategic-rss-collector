# Qaempanah: Regional countries should take charge of own security

Published: 2026-09-26 16:55:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86275392/Qaempanah-Regional-countries-should-take-charge-of-own-security

Tehran, IRNA – Mohammad Jafar Qaempanah, Vice President for Executive Affairs, emphasized that the US presence in the region is not constructive, and said that the region's destiny must be shaped and developed by the countries of the region themselves.
