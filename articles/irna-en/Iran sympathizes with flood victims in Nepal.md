# Iran sympathizes with flood victims in Nepal

Published: 2026-08-28 09:08:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248392/Iran-sympathizes-with-flood-victims-in-Nepal

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has expressed sympathy with the government and people of Nepal following the floods and landslides in that country.
