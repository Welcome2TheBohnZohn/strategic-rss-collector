# Unseen photos of Iran’s participation at 1990 Beijing Asian Games

Published: 2026-09-30 10:30:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86279371/Unseen-photos-of-Iran-s-participation-at-1990-Beijing-Asian-Games

The 1990 Beijing Asian Games, the 11th edition of the Asian Games, was held in Beijing in October 1990, and Iran’s delegation finished in fifth place with 18 medals, four gold, six silver, and eight bronze.
