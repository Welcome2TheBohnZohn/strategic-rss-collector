# In photos: National Iranian Soldier Festival held in Tehran

Published: 2026-09-27 09:35:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86276033/In-photos-National-Iranian-Soldier-Festival-held-in-Tehran

The National Iranian Soldier Festival, named in honor of the late Chairman of the Chief of Staff of Iran’s Armed Forces, Major General Abdolrahim Mousavi, was held on September 27, at Imam Ali Officers’ Academy. Major General Amir Hatami, Commander-in-Chief of the Iranian Army, was in attendance.
