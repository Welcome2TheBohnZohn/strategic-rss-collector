# Iran moves to establish joint free trade zone with Turkey in Khoy

Published: 2026-08-30 08:26:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86250041/Iran-moves-to-establish-joint-free-trade-zone-with-Turkey-in

Khoy, IRNA – Iran has completed the necessary legal and administrative steps to establish a joint free trade zone with Turkey in Khoy, northwestern Iran, and is now awaiting further action from the Turkish side to advance and finalize the project, an Iranian official has said.
