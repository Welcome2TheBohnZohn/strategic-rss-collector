# Iran will not wait once signs of enemy attack are confirmed: Top commander

Published: 2026-10-31 15:03:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86308521/Iran-will-not-wait-once-signs-of-enemy-attack-are-confirmed

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Armed Forces will not wait for an enemy attack to respond once signs of hostile activities are confirmed, Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi said on Saturday.
