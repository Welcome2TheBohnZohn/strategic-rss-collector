# Baqaei: Attending UNGA not a privilege, a legal right

Published: 2026-09-19 20:39:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86268432/Baqaei-Attending-UNGA-not-a-privilege-a-legal-right

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says that Iran will not abondon its right to be present in the UN General Assembly, stressing that attending the UN meeting is not a prividlege given by Washington.
