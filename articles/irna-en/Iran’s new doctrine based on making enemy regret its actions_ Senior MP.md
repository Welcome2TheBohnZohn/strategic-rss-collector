# Iran’s new doctrine based on making enemy regret its actions: Senior MP

Published: 2026-11-06 15:28:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314095/Iran-s-new-doctrine-based-on-making-enemy-regret-its-actions

Tehran, IRNA — A senior Iranian parliament member has warned that if war resumes against the country, the Islamic Republic will not hesitate to use all its offensive and defensive capabilities without any reservations, stating, “This is the order the Armed Forces have received.”
