# Army air defense destroys US enemy MQ-9 drone

Published: 2026-11-07 07:04:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86314355/Army-air-defense-destroys-US-enemy-MQ-9-drone

Tehran, IRNA — The Army of the Islamic Republic of Iran reports that it has intercepted and destroyed an MQ-9 drone belonging to the aggressive US enemy over Qeshm in the Strait of Hormuz.
