# Iran armed forces fully prepared to crush any aggression: IRGC deputy

Published: 2026-10-21 13:49:05

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299630/Iran-armed-forces-fully-prepared-to-crush-any-aggression-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – The deputy for political affairs of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) stressed that Iran’s armed forces are fully prepared to confront and crush any potential aggression by the enemies.
