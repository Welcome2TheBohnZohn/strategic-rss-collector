# Araghchi says Iran’s defense capabilities and diplomacy will safeguard national independence

Published: 2026-10-18 16:00:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296374/Araghchi-says-Iran-s-defense-capabilities-and-diplomacy-will

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has stressed that Iran’s defense capabilities and diplomatic efforts will work toward safeguarding national independence, sovereignty and interests, emphasizing continued coordination between the foreign and defense ministries.
