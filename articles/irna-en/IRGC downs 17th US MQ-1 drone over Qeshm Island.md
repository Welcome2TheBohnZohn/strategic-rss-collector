# IRGC downs 17th US MQ-1 drone over Qeshm Island

Published: 2026-11-14 13:08:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86321541/IRGC-downs-17th-US-MQ-1-drone-over-Qeshm-Island

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) says it has shot down a US MQ-1 drone over Qeshm Island in the Persian Gulf, marking the 17th such drone it has downed.
