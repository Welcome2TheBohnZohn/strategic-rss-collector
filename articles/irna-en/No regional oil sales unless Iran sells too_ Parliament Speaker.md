# No regional oil sales unless Iran sells too: Parliament Speaker

Published: 2026-08-28 13:07:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86248570/No-regional-oil-sales-unless-Iran-sells-too-Parliament-Speaker

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s parliament speaker has firmly responded to recent remarks by US CENTCOM Commander Brad Cooper, reiterating Tehran’s earlier stance that no one would sell oil in the region if Iran doesn’t.
