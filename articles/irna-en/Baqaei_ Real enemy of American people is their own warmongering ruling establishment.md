# Baqaei: Real enemy of American people is their own warmongering ruling establishment

Published: 2026-10-10 23:02:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288923/Baghaei-Real-enemy-of-American-people-is-their-own-warmongering

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei while referring to a claim by US President Donald Trump regarding a potential Iranian attack on the cities of Los Angeles and Santiago—and his description of the cost as very negligible—said that to frighten their people, they fabricate an imaginary enemy in the sky in order to overshadow the real threat on the ground.
