# Navy commander: Hormuz security depends on Iran’s will and readiness

Published: 2026-10-08 09:20:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286839/Navy-commander-Hormuz-security-depends-on-Iran-s-will-and-readiness

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Navy commander Rear Admiral Shahram Irani has stressed the strategic importance of the Strait of Hormuz, saying Hormuz security rests on the will, vigilance and readiness of Iran’s defenders.
