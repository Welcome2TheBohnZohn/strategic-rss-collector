# Russian foreign minister to visit Tehran on Monday: Iranian envoy

Published: 2026-10-18 15:00:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296325/Russian-foreign-minister-to-visit-Tehran-on-Monday-Iranian-envoy

Moscow, IRNA – Iranian Ambassador to Moscow, Kazem Jalali, has announced that Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov will visit Tehran on Monday as part of ongoing high-level consultations between the two countries.
