# US brutality drives world toward nuclear deterrence: Iran Leader’s adviser

Published: 2026-11-03 12:48:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311438/US-brutality-drives-world-toward-nuclear-deterrence-Iran-Leader-s

Tehran, IRNA – An adviser to Iran’s Leader, referring to the growing number of nuclear warheads by countries possessing nuclear weapons, stated that US brutality has driven the world toward nuclear deterrence.
