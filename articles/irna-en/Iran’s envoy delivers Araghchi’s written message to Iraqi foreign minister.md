# Iran’s envoy delivers Araghchi’s written message to Iraqi foreign minister

Published: 2026-08-30 16:52:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86250337/Iran-s-envoy-delivers-Araghchi-s-written-message-to-Iraqi-foreign

Tehran, IRNA - Iran's Ambassador to Iraq, Mohammad Kazem Al-e Sadegh, has delivered a written message from Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi to his Iraqi counterpart, Fuad Hussein.
