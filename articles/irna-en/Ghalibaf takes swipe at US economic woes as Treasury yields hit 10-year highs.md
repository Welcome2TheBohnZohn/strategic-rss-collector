# Ghalibaf takes swipe at US economic woes as Treasury yields hit 10-year highs

Published: 2026-09-24 18:37:12

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86273736/Ghalibaf-takes-swipe-at-US-economic-woes-as-Treasury-yields-hit

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has taken aim at rising US borrowing costs and fuel-price pressures referring to the 10-year US Treasury yield reaching 5.1 percent.
