# Pezeshkian sends National Day greetings to Saudi leaders

Published: 2026-09-23 12:34:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272548/Pezeshkian-sends-National-Day-greetings-to-Saudi-leaders

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has extended congratulations to Saudi King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud and Crown Prince and Prime Minister Mohammed bin Salman on the occasion of Saudi Arabia’s National Day.
