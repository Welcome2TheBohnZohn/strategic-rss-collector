# Senior MP: American formula won’t bring regional security

Published: 2026-10-27 10:16:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304877/Senior-MP-American-formula-won-t-bring-regional-security

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian Parliament Deputy Speaker Hamidreza Haji Babai stressed that an American formula cannot bring security to the region, stating that regional security is ensured by the region’s own countries.
