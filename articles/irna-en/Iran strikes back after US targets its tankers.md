# Iran strikes back after US targets its tankers

Published: 2026-09-06 06:00:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86256016/Iran-strikes-back-after-US-targets-its-tankers

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has responded to the aggression of the US military in attacking three Iranian tankers by firing three missiles from Sirik toward the Strait of Hormuz.
