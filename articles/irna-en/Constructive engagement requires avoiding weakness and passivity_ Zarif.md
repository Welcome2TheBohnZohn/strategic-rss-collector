# Constructive engagement requires avoiding weakness and passivity: Zarif

Published: 2026-10-10 16:29:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288784/Constructive-engagement-requires-avoiding-weakness-and-passivity

Tehran, IRNA – Former foreign minister, Mohammad Javad Zarif, emphasizing the importance of national self-confidence in foreign policy, says Iran needs to engage in dialogue with the global power structure on an equal footing, avoiding any weakness and passivity.
