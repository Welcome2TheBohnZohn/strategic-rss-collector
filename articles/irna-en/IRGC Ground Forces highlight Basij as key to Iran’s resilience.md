# IRGC Ground Forces highlight Basij as key to Iran’s resilience

Published: 2026-11-23 06:22:08

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329766/IRGC-Ground-Forces-highlight-Basij-as-key-to-Iran-s-resilience

Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Forces Brigadier General Mohammad Karami describes the Basij Organization as a source of national strength, saying its volunteer forces serve wherever the country’s interests require.
