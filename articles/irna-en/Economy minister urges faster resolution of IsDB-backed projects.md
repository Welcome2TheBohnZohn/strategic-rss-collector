# Economy minister urges faster resolution of IsDB-backed projects

Published: 2026-10-16 15:39:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294290/Economy-minister-urges-faster-resolution-of-IsDB-backed-projects

Tehran, IRNA — Minister of Economic Affairs and Finance Ali Madanizadeh has stressed expediting the resolution of approved projects, removing executive obstacles, and utilizing the financing capacities of the Islamic Development Bank to complete the country’s priority projects.
