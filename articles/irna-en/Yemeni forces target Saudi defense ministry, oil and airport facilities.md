# Yemeni forces target Saudi defense ministry, oil and airport facilities

Published: 2026-10-22 13:22:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300432/Yemeni-forces-target-Saudi-defense-ministry-oil-and-airport

Tehran, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Yemeni Armed Forces said that over the past 48 hours, four major targets in Saudi Arabia including ministry of defense building in Riyadh, Aramco facility in Yanbu, and King Fahd and Abha airports were attacked with ballistic missiles and drones.
