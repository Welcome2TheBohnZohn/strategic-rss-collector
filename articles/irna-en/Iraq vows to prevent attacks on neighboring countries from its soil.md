# Iraq vows to prevent attacks on neighboring countries from its soil

Published: 2026-09-13 06:13:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262316/Iraq-vows-to-prevent-attacks-on-neighboring-countries-from-its

Tehran, IRNA — Iraq will not allow its territory to be used as a launching pad for attacks against neighboring countries or to undermine regional security, spokesman for the commander-in-chief of the Iraqi Armed Forces, Sabah Al-Numan, says.
