# Cambodian PM condemns aggression against Iran; stresses start of a new phase in bilateral cooperation

Published: 2026-09-10 18:18:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260612/Cambodian-PM-condemns-aggression-against-Iran-stresses-start

Tehran, IRNA — In a meeting with the ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran, the Prime Minister of Cambodia condemned the recent US-Israeli aggression against Iran and the violation of international principles and values; and described the arrival of the new Iranian ambassador in Phnom Penh as the start of a significant new phase in cooperation between the two nations while stressing the expansion of bilateral relations.
