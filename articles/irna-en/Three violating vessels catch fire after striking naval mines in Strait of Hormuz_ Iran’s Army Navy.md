# Three violating vessels catch fire after striking naval mines in Strait of Hormuz: Iran’s Army Navy

Published: 2026-11-19 05:21:47

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326369/Three-violating-vessels-catch-fire-after-striking-naval-mines

Tehran, IRNA – The Iranian Army Navy has announced that three oil tankers caught fire in the eastern part of the Strait of Hormuz after violating maritime regulations.
