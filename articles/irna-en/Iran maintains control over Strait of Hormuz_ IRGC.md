# Iran maintains control over Strait of Hormuz: IRGC

Published: 2026-08-27 16:33:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247997/Iran-maintains-control-over-Strait-of-Hormuz-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) says Iran maintains control over the Strait of Hormuz and is moving beyond a reactive posture toward greater strategic initiative in shaping the course and cost of war and diplomacy.
