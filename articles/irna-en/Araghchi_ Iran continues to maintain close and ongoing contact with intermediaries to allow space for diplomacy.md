# Araghchi: Iran continues to maintain close and ongoing contact with intermediaries to allow space for diplomacy

Published: 2026-10-28 19:01:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306427/Araghchi-Iran-continues-to-maintain-close-and-ongoing-contact

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says that, in order to provide an opportunity for diplomacy, Iran will continue its close and ongoing communication with intermediaries.
