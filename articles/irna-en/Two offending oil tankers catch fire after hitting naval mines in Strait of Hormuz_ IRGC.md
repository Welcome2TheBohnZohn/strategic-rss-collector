# Two offending oil tankers catch fire after hitting naval mines in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-14 05:48:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320921/Two-offending-oil-tankers-catch-fire-after-hitting-naval-mines

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced that two more violating oil tankers have caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting naval mines.
