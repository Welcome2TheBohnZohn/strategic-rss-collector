# VP Aref praises Iran's team for winning international astronomy and astrophysics Olympiad

Published: 2026-10-04 16:07:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283157/VP-Aref-praises-Iran-s-team-for-winning-international-astronomy

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref, applauding Iranian students for winning the International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics, says the success of the national team heralds the emergence of a creative and self-confident generation.
