# Iran offers condolences to Pakistan after 14 newborns killed in Islamabad hospital fire

Published: 2026-08-26 19:39:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247242/Iran-offers-condolences-to-Pakistan-after-14-newborns-killed

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has expressed condolences to Pakistan over the deaths of at least 14 newborn babies in a fire at a hospital in the capital, Islamabad.
