# Iran and Poland hold third round of political consultations

Published: 2026-10-21 21:07:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86299931/Iran-and-Poland-hold-third-round-of-political-consultations

Tehran, IRNA – The third round of political consultations between the Islamic Republic of Iran and the Republic of Poland, at the level of deputy foreign ministers, was held in Tehran on Wednesday.
