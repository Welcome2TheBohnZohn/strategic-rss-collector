# Army chief vows decisive response to any aggression

Published: 2026-09-21 13:03:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270252/Army-chief-vows-decisive-response-to-any-aggression

Tehran, IRNA - The Commander-in-Chief of the Army Major General Amir Hatami has said any aggression against the security and territorial integrity of Iran will face a decisive and regret-inducing response.
