# Baqaei declines New York Times interview invitation in protest to baseless claims about Iran

Published: 2026-11-19 05:42:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326383/Baqaei-declines-New-York-Times-interview-invitation-in-protest

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has declined an invitation for interview by The New York Times in protest to the unprofessional approach by the American newspaper.
