# Ghalibaf: No regional peace without end to Israeli war on Lebanon

Published: 2026-11-16 19:00:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86323871/Ghalibaf-No-regional-peace-without-end-to-Israeli-war-on-Lebanon

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Parliament speaker has underlined the need for an end to Israel’s war on Lebanon and the restoration of the Arab country's sovereignty over its entire territory as a prerequisite for peace in West Asia.
