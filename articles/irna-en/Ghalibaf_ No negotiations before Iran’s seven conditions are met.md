# Ghalibaf: No negotiations before Iran’s seven conditions are met

Published: 2026-11-24 10:39:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86331268/Ghalibaf-No-negotiations-before-Iran-s-seven-conditions-are

Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has said, in response to recent rhetoric and threats by the US president and treasury secretary, that no action and negotiations will take place until Iran’s seven conditions are met.
