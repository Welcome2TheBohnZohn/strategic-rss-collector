# Iran’s FM discusses regional developments with Turkish, Pakistani officials

Published: 2026-09-17 06:42:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266460/Iran-s-FM-discusses-regional-developments-with-Turkish-Pakistani

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has held separate telephone conversations with his Turkish counterpart Hakan Fidan and Pakistan’s Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir to discuss the latest regional developments and issues of mutual interest.
