# Imam Khamenei turned Islamic unity into practical movement: Lebanese scholar

Published: 2026-08-29 16:54:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249691/Imam-Khamenei-turned-Islamic-unity-into-practical-movement-Lebanese

TEHRAN, Iran — Lebanese scholar Sayyed Ali Fadlallah, a former Friday prayer leader of Beirut, says that the martyred Leader of the Islamic Revolution, Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, went beyond theoretical discussions of Islamic unity and turned it into a practical program for the Muslim community.
