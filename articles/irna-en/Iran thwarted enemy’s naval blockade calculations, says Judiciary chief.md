# Iran thwarted enemy’s naval blockade calculations, says Judiciary chief

Published: 2026-09-27 15:42:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276342/Iran-thwarted-enemy-s-naval-blockade-calculations-says-Judiciary

Tehran, IRNA – Judiciary Chief Gholamhossein Mohseni-Ejei says the enemy had assumed that a naval blockade would prevent essential goods from reaching Iran, but Iran thwarted the calculation and took the initiative.
