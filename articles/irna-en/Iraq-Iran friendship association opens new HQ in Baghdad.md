# Iraq-Iran friendship association opens new HQ in Baghdad

Published: 2026-11-22 06:00:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328819/Iraq-Iran-friendship-association-opens-new-HQ-in-Baghdad

Tehran, IRNA — The Iran-Iraq friendship association’s new headquarters has opened in Baghdad in the presence of Iran’s Ambassador Mohammad Kazem Al-e Sadeq, aiming to strengthen cooperation between the two countries.
