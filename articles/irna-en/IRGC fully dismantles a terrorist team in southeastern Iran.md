# IRGC fully dismantles a terrorist team in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-10-06 11:36:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285073/IRGC-fully-dismantles-a-terrorist-team-in-southeastern-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) identified and completely dismantled a terrorist team involved in the assassination of two Sunni clerics in Sistan and Baluchestan province.
