# Iran warns US of more severe blows to warships if aggression continues

Published: 2026-09-05 19:11:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255880/Iran-warns-US-of-more-severe-blows-to-warships-if-aggression

Tehran, IRNA – Spokesperson for the Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters has warned the “terrorist” US military that the armed forces will deal more “severe and extensive” blows to American warships if Washington continues acts of aggression in the region.
