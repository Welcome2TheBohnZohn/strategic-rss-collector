# Saddam’s fate awaits Trump and Netanyahu: IRGC commander

Published: 2026-09-20 16:22:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269342/Saddam-s-fate-awaits-Trump-and-Netanyahu-IRGC-commander

Tehran, IRNA – The advisor to the chief commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), hailing the resilience of the Iranian people and Armed Forces against foreign aggressors, says US President Donald Trump, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, and their supporters will face the same fate as former Iraqi dictator Saddam Hussein.
