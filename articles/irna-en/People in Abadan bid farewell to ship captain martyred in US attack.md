# People in Abadan bid farewell to ship captain martyred in US attack

Published: 2026-11-01 18:42:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309590/People-in-Abadan-bid-farewell-to-ship-captain-martyred-in-US

Abadan, IRNA - A farewell ceremony for the body of Nasser Ebrahimi-Nejadan, the captain of a vessel who was martyred in an attack by the American enemy in the open waters of the Persian Gulf, was held in Abadan on Sunday evening.
