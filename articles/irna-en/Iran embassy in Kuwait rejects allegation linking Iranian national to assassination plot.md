# Iran embassy in Kuwait rejects allegation linking Iranian national to assassination plot

Published: 2026-10-28 11:30:20

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86306018/Iran-embassy-in-Kuwait-rejects-allegation-linking-Iranian-national

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s embassy in Kuwait has firmly denied allegations that an Iranian national was involved in a plot to assassinate public figures or threaten Kuwait’s national security, saying the individual had not been in the country for the past 10 years.
