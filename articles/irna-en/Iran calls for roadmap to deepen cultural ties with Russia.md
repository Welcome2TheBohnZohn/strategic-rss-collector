# Iran calls for roadmap to deepen cultural ties with Russia

Published: 2026-09-28 08:22:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276986/Iran-calls-for-roadmap-to-deepen-cultural-ties-with-Russia

Moscow, IRNA – Iranian Culture Minister Abbas Saleh Shariati has called for a clear roadmap and sustained initiatives to deepen cultural relations with Russia, describing artists as cultural ambassadors of the Iranian nation.
