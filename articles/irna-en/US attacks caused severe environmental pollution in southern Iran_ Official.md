# US attacks caused severe environmental pollution in southern Iran: Official

Published: 2026-10-11 10:30:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289318/US-attacks-caused-severe-environmental-pollution-in-southern

Tehran, IRNA — Head of Iran’s Department of Environment Shina Ansari says attacks on fuel storage tanks and facilities in Assaluyeh, a Persian Gulf port city in southern Iran, caused massive air pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.
