# Aref hails executive-judiciary cooperation on essential goods, market oversight

Published: 2026-10-27 13:38:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86305137/Aref-hails-executive-judiciary-cooperation-on-essential-goods

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s executive and judiciary branches are strengthening coordination on the supply of essential goods and market oversight, First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref said on Tuesday, stressing the importance of joint efforts to address public concerns and resolve economic problems.
