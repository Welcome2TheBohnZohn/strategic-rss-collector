# Rezaei: Enriched uranium will not exit Iran by any means

Published: 2026-10-20 18:56:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298732/Rezaei-Enriched-uranium-will-not-exit-Iran-by-any-means

Tehran, IRNA – Secretary of Supreme National Security (SNSC) Major General Mohsen Rezaei told Pakistani mediator Field Marshal Asim Munir on Tuesday evening that even a particle of enriched uranium will not exit Iran at all.
