# Supertanker explodes after hitting mine in ‘unauthorized’ Hormuz route: IRGC Navy

Published: 2026-10-11 18:16:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289902/Supertanker-explodes-after-hitting-mine-in-unauthorized-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA - The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC)’s Navy announced Sunday that a violating supertanker carrying crude oil suffered a powerful explosion after striking a naval mine as it was attempting to exit the Strait of Hormuz through an unauthorized route.
