# Iran’s Caspian ports hold untapped potential to boost trade: Chamber chief

Published: 2026-09-02 14:01:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253524/Iran-s-Caspian-ports-hold-untapped-potential-to-boost-trade

Rasht, IRNA – The head of Iran’s Chamber of Commerce, Samad Hasanzadeh, says the country’s northern ports remain underutilized and could play a significant role in importing essential goods under current conditions.
