# Need to pivot a wedding to a wake? Outsource to the US: Iran’s London Embassy

Published: 2026-09-03 16:53:52

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86254317/Need-to-pivot-a-wedding-to-a-wake-Outsource-to-the-US-Iran-s

London, IRNA — The Iranian Embassy in London has written that the attack on a wedding ceremony in Iran’s Kuhestak is the most recent item on the long list of deadly attacks by the US and its allies on weddings in Iraq, Yemen, and Afghanistan, saying that if one wishes to turn a wedding into a funeral, they should “leave it to the US terrorist military.”
