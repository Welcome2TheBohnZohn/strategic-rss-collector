# Leader lauds late artist Kourosh Zarei’s tireless efforts to promote performing arts

Published: 2026-11-11 10:39:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86319068/Leader-lauds-late-artist-Kourosh-Zarei-s-tireless-efforts-to

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei has issued a message of condolence on the passing of Iranian pro-Islamic Revolution artist Kourosh Zarei, praising his tireless efforts to promote the performing arts.
