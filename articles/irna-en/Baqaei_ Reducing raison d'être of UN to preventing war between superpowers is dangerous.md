# Baqaei: Reducing raison d'être of UN to preventing war between superpowers is dangerous

Published: 2026-09-02 20:29:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253766/Baqaei-Reducing-raison-d-%C3%AAtre-of-UN-to-preventing-war-between

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says that reducing the raison d'être of the United Nations to “preventing war between superpowers” is a misleading and dangerous interpretation of the spirit of the Charter and the fundamental principle prohibiting the use of force in relations among all states (Article 2, Paragraph 4).
