# In photos: Steel Symposium 1405 opens in Isfahan

Published: 2026-10-28 07:51:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86305736/In-photos-Steel-Symposium-1405-opens-in-Isfahan

The opening ceremony of the 28th edition of the Steel Symposium 1405 was held at the Isfahan International Exhibition Center on October 27, 2026. The event brought together more than 180 companies active in the steel industry and related sectors to showcase production capabilities and promote professional cooperation.
