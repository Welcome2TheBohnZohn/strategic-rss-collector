# IRGC: Assassination of Mamosta Nezhati shows enemy’s attempt to create division among Muslims

Published: 2026-09-09 10:51:45

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259576/IRGC-Assassination-of-Mamosta-Nezhati-shows-enemy-s-attempt

Urmia, IRNA – The Public Relations Department of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) in West Azarbaijan Province has described the assassination of Sunni Kurdish cleric Mamosta Mohammad Nezhati by Kurdish separatist groups as the enemy’s attempt to create division among Muslims.
