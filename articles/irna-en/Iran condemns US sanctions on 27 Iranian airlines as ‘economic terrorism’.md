# Iran condemns US sanctions on 27 Iranian airlines as ‘economic terrorism’

Published: 2026-09-10 06:43:39

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260146/Iran-condemns-US-sanctions-on-27-Iranian-airlines-as-economic

New York, IRNA — The Islamic Republic has condemned new US sanctions targeting 27 Iranian airlines and aviation-related entities, describing the measures as “economic terrorism” and “ collective punishment” that threaten civilian aviation safety and restrict the movement of millions of people.
