# Iranian, Brazilian presidents emphasize strengthening bilateral cooperation

Published: 2026-11-12 14:34:33

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320065/Iranian-Brazilian-presidents-emphasize-strengthening-bilateral

Tehran, IRNA — Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian and his Brazilian counterpart, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, have emphasized the necessity of leveraging the extensive potential of the two nations to expand cooperation across various sectors.
