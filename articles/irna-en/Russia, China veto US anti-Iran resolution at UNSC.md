# Russia, China veto US anti-Iran resolution at UNSC

Published: 2026-09-17 15:40:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266867/Russia-China-veto-US-anti-Iran-resolution-at-UNSC

New York, IRNA – Russia and China have vetoed an anti-Iran US draft resolution at the United Nations Security Council aimed at extending the mandate of the Expert Panel of the 1737 Committee.
