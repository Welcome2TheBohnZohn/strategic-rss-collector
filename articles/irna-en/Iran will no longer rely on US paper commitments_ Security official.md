# Iran will no longer rely on US paper commitments: Security official

Published: 2026-11-06 11:54:13

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86313995/Iran-will-no-longer-rely-on-US-paper-commitments-Security-official

Tehran, IRNA – The deputy secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) stressed that the US has consistently violated commitments over the past year and a half, emphasizing that Iran has the right not to rely on American’s paper commitments before entering a new phase of diplomacy.
