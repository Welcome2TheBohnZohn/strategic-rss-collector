# Milan rally commemorates Iranian, Palestinian children killed in war

Published: 2026-10-26 15:09:27

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304077/Milan-rally-commemorates-Iranian-Palestinian-children-killed

Tehran, IRNA – Artists and demonstrators in Milan, Italy, have commemorated children killed in Iranian southern city of Minab and Gaza during events marking the 82nd anniversary of the Gorla school massacre in World War Two.
