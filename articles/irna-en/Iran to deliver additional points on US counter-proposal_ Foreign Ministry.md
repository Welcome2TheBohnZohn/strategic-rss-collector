# Iran to deliver additional points on US counter-proposal: Foreign Ministry

Published: 2026-10-04 09:28:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282713/Iran-to-deliver-additional-points-on-US-counter-proposal-Foreign

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says Iran will convey its additional points on a US counter-proposal to an earlier Iranian plan for ending the war after conducting the necessary assessments.
