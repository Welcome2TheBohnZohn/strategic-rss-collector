# 40th International Islamic Unity Conference opens in Tehran

Published: 2026-08-27 07:06:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247498/40th-International-Islamic-Unity-Conference-opens-in-Tehran

Tehran, IRNA – The 40th International Islamic Unity Conference has opened in the Iranian capital, Tehran, with the participation of President Masoud Pezeshkian and around 600 Iranian and foreign guests from 40 countries.
