# US bases in region suffered serious damage: Iran Army spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-29 16:24:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278618/US-bases-in-region-suffered-serious-damage-Iran-Army-spokesperson

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akrami-Nia says US bases in the region have suffered serious damage, noting that the damage has affected the support and supply operations of American forces and created challenges for Washington.
