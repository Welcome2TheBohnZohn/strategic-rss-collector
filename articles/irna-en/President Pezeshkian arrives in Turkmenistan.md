# President Pezeshkian arrives in Turkmenistan

Published: 2026-10-08 14:55:09

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287128/President-Pezeshkian-arrives-in-Turkmenistan

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has arrived in Turkmenistan to attend the Council of Heads of State of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) and the Environmental Summit of the Heads of Caspian Sea Littoral States.
