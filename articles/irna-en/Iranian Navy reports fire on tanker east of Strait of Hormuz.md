# Iranian Navy reports fire on tanker east of Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-16 05:57:04

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322942/Iranian-Navy-reports-fire-on-tanker-east-of-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Republic of Iran Navy has announced that a vessel violating maritime regulations caught fire after striking a naval mine east of the Strait of Hormuz.
