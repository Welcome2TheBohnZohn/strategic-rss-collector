# US aviation sanctions directly threaten human safety, health, and lives: HCHR

Published: 2026-09-28 12:57:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277347/US-aviation-sanctions-directly-threaten-human-safety-health

Tehran, IRNA – The High Council for Human Rights of the Islamic Republic of Iran (HCHR) has emphasized that US sanctions targeting the country’s aviation sector are not merely economic or commercial measures; rather, they directly affect human safety, health, and lives.
