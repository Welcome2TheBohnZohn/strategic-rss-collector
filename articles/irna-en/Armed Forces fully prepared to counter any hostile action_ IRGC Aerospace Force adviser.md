# Armed Forces fully prepared to counter any hostile action: IRGC Aerospace Force adviser

Published: 2026-11-20 10:34:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327401/Armed-Forces-fully-prepared-to-counter-any-hostile-action-IRGC

Tehran, IRNA – A senior advisor to the commander of the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps (IRGC) Aerospace Force says Iran faces no restrictions on deploying its missile capabilities to new geographic areas if ordered, stressing that the country’s Armed Forces are fully prepared to respond decisively to any hostile action.
