# Pezeshkian and Putin stress strengthening strategic ties between Iran and Russia

Published: 2026-10-07 15:40:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86286337/Pezeshkian-and-Putin-stress-strengthening-strategic-ties-between

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian and Russian President Vladimir Putin stressed the need to maintain and strengthen bilateral and strategic cooperation between Tehran and Moscow during a phone call on Wednesday.
