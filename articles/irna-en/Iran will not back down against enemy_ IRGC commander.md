# Iran will not back down against enemy: IRGC commander

Published: 2026-09-23 10:09:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86272453/Iran-will-not-back-down-against-enemy-IRGC-commander

Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Ground Force of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Brigadier General Mohammad Karami, says Iran will not back down in the face of its enemies, stressing that the future belongs to the Iranian people.
