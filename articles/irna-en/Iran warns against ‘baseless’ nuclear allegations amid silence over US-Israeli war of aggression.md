# Iran warns against ‘baseless’ nuclear allegations amid silence over US-Israeli war of aggression

Published: 2026-11-17 07:43:43

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324272/Iran-warns-against-baseless-nuclear-allegations-amid-silence

New York, IRNA — Iran’s Ambassador and Chargé d’Affaires to the United Nations, Gholamhossein Darzi, has expressed deep concern over baseless allegations leveled by certain countries against Iran’s peaceful nuclear program, while maintaining silence over the illegal and unjustified US-Israeli war.
