# Iran, Pakistan close to finalizing free trade agreement: Official

Published: 2026-11-21 12:40:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86328362/Iran-Pakistan-close-to-finalizing-free-trade-agreement-Official

Tehran, IRNA – The deputy director of the East Asia and Subcontinent Office at the Iran Trade Promotion Organization says Iran’s trade with Pakistan is ongoing, and the two countries’ free trade agreement is in its final stages and is expected to be finalized by the end of this year.
