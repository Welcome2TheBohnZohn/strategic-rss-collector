# Army chief says Iran’s real strength lies in people’s faith, resolve

Published: 2026-09-16 13:12:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266044/Army-chief-says-Iran-s-real-strength-lies-in-people-s-faith

Tehran, IRNA – Army chief Major General Amir Hatami says that Iran’s real strength is rooted in the faith, unity and resolve of its people, alongside the defensive and deterrent capabilities of its armed forces.
