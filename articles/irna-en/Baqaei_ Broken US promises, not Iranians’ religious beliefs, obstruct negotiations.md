# Baqaei: Broken US promises, not Iranians’ religious beliefs, obstruct negotiations

Published: 2026-10-22 08:53:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86300182/Baqaei-Broken-US-promises-not-Iranians-religious-beliefs

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei has rejected US claims that Iranian religious beliefs are an obstacle to negotiations, saying the impasse stems from Washington’s negotiating approach and failure to honor its commitments.
