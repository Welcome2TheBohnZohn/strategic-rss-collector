# Interior Minister: Iran-Pakistan borders to operate around clock

Published: 2026-09-21 21:15:28

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86270584/Interior-Minister-Iran-Pakistan-borders-to-operate-around-clock

Tehran, IRNA – Emphasizing that relations between Iran and Pakistan are strategic and deep, Iranian Interior Minister Eskandar Momeni said that following bilateral agreements, the two countries' existing border crossings will operate around the clock, and a new crossing will be opened at Kuhak.
