# Any enemy mischief will be met with crushing response: IRGC commander

Published: 2026-10-14 14:59:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292945/Any-enemy-mischief-will-be-met-with-crushing-response-IRGC-commander

Gorgan, IRNA – Brigadier General Yadollah Javani, deputy for political affairs of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), underscoring the full combat readiness of the Iranian Armed Forces to counter potential threats, says any miscalculation by the enemy will prompt a crushing response.
