# Baqaei: British government cannot conceal its destructive role in insecurity in West Asia by deflecting blame

Published: 2026-10-15 19:10:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293793/Baqaei-British-government-cannot-conceal-its-destructive-role

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei in a message emphasized that the British government cannot conceal its destructive role in the current instability in West Asia by deflecting blame and distorting the reality.
