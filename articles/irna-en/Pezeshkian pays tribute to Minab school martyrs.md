# Pezeshkian pays tribute to Minab school martyrs

Published: 2026-09-21 09:36:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269895/Pezeshkian-pays-tribute-to-Minab-school-martyrs

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has honored the memory of the Minab school martyrs during the ceremony marking the start of the new school year in Tehran.
