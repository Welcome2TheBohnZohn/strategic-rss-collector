# US-Israel-linked terrorist team dismantled in southeastern Iran

Published: 2026-08-29 13:32:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249578/US-Israel-linked-terrorist-team-dismantled-in-southeastern-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Ground Force Quds Headquarters has announced the elimination of a terrorist team affiliated with the spy agencies of the Israeli regime and the United States in the southeastern Iranian province of Sistan and Baluchestan.
