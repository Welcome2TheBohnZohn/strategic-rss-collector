# Russia slams US, Israeli aggression against Iran’s nuclear sites as illegal

Published: 2026-11-17 13:47:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324824/Russia-slams-US-Israeli-aggression-against-Iran-s-nuclear-sites

London, IRNA – Russian envoy to Vienna-based international organizations slammed the US and Israeli aggression against Iran’s safeguarded nuclear facilities as illegal, calling for collective diplomatic efforts to ease tensions and ensure security and stability in the Middle East.
