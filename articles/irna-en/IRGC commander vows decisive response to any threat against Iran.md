# IRGC commander vows decisive response to any threat against Iran

Published: 2026-11-23 17:05:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330567/IRGC-commander-vows-decisive-response-to-any-threat-against-Iran

Tehran, IRNA – Commander of the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), Major General Ahmad Vahidi, says the elite force will deliver a decisive response to any threat or aggression by the enemy within the geography of the threat.
