# Iran dismantles two US-Israeli-backed terrorist cells in Kurdistan Province

Published: 2026-11-17 13:41:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324792/Iran-dismantles-two-US-Israeli-backed-terrorist-cells-in-Kurdistan

Tehran, IRNA – The Ministry of Intelligence has announced the dismantling of two armed terrorist-separatist cells in the western province of Kurdistan, with nine members arrested and a large cache of weapons and communication equipment seized, foiling planned terrorist operations and efforts to undermine security in the province.
