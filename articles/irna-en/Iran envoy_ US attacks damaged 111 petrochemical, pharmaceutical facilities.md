# Iran envoy: US attacks damaged 111 petrochemical, pharmaceutical facilities

Published: 2026-10-07 06:15:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86285592/Iran-envoy-US-attacks-damaged-111-petrochemical-pharmaceutical

London, IRNA – Iran’s ambassador to the Netherlands and Representative to the Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW) has said that US military attacks on Iran’s chemical industries damaged 111 petrochemical and pharmaceutical facilities, disrupting supply chains and affecting the implementation of the Chemical Weapons Convention.
