# Minab school massacre leaves an eternal wound on Iran: FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-08-30 06:17:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86249928/Minab-school-massacre-leaves-an-eternal-wound-on-Iran-FM-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has described the Minab school massacre as an eternal wound on Iran, urging people and contemporary art enthusiasts to visit an exhibition featuring paintings inspired by the incident.
