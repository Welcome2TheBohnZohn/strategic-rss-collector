# Iran rejects US claims, says Ansarallah makes its own decisions

Published: 2026-09-10 05:52:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86260094/Iran-rejects-US-claims-says-Ansarallah-makes-its-own-decisions

Tehran, IRNA — Iran has rejected US Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s allegations of Iranian interference in Yemen, stressing that the Ansarallah movement is an independent Yemeni actor that makes its own decisions and does not operate as a proxy for any country.
