# Iran stresses effective implementation of global treaty on genetic resources and traditional knowledge

Published: 2026-09-17 17:15:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266928/Iran-stresses-effective-implementation-of-global-treaty-on-genetic

London, IRNA — The Deputy Permanent Representative of the Islamic Republic of Iran in Geneva described the global treaty on genetic resources and traditional knowledge as a significant step toward establishing a more transparent and balanced international intellectual property system, and emphasized the necessity of its effective implementation.
