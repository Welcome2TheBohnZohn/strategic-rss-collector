# Pezeshkian hails Iran’s 52-medal haul at Asian Games in Nagoya

Published: 2026-10-04 17:43:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283225/Pezeshkian-hails-Iran-s-52-medal-haul-at-Asian-Games-in-Nagoya

Tehran, IRNA - President Masoud Pezeshkian congratulated the country’s sports delegation on its performance at the Asian Games in Nagoya, describing the success as a valuable display of the will, hope, and capability of Iranian youth.
