# ‘Islamic Unity’ foundation of countering arrogance, Zionism: Turkish scholar

Published: 2026-08-31 12:54:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86251185/Islamic-Unity-foundation-of-countering-arrogance-Zionism

Tehran, IRNA – A Turkish Islamic scholar believes that Islamic unity, based on the vision of Martyr Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei, is a Quranic necessity and the true foundation for confronting arrogance and Zionism.
