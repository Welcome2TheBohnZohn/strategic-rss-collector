# Iran says global refugee support ‘insufficient’ as it hosts millions under sanctions

Published: 2026-10-14 12:39:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292882/Iran-says-global-refugee-support-insufficient-as-it-hosts-millions

Tehran, IRNA - Iran’s refugee affairs chief criticized international support for hosting refugees as insufficient, stressing that the country has continued providing public services to millions of refugees and migrants despite sanctions and attacks.
