# Mexico’s ambassador welcomes joint trade cooperation committee with Iran

Published: 2026-09-06 13:11:02

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86256598/Mexico-s-ambassador-welcomes-joint-trade-cooperation-committee

Tehran, IRNA – Mexico’s ambassador to Tehran, Guillermo Puente Ordorica, has welcomed the formation of a joint Iran-Mexico committee to expand trade relations, identifying health tourism, the automotive industry and auto parts manufacturing as potential areas of cooperation.
