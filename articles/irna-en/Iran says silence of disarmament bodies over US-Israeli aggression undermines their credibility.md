# Iran says silence of disarmament bodies over US-Israeli aggression undermines their credibility

Published: 2026-11-26 06:41:30

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333011/Iran-says-silence-of-disarmament-bodies-over-US-Israeli-aggression

London, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador and Permanent Representative to the United Nations and other international organizations in Austria, Reza Najafi, has criticized international disarmament and non-proliferation bodies for their silence over the US-Israeli war of aggression against Iran, stressing that their inaction has seriously undermined the credibility and effectiveness of these organizations.
