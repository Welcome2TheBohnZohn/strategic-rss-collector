# Seoul has no plans to join war or deploy troops to region: South Korean FM tells Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-26 12:57:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333364/Seoul-has-no-plans-to-join-war-or-deploy-troops-to-region-South

Tehran, IRNA — South Korean Foreign Minister Cho Hyun, in a telephone conversation with his Iranian counterpart, Abbas Araghchi, has said that his country does not plan to deploy troops or participate in war in the region.
