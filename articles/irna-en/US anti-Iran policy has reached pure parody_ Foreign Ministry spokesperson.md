# US anti-Iran policy has reached pure parody: Foreign Ministry spokesperson

Published: 2026-08-27 06:49:21

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247461/US-anti-Iran-policy-has-reached-pure-parody-Foreign-Ministry
