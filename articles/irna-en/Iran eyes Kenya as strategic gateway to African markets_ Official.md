# Iran eyes Kenya as strategic gateway to African markets: Official

Published: 2026-10-14 09:34:24

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292594/Iran-eyes-Kenya-as-strategic-gateway-to-African-markets-Official

Tehran, IRNA – President of Iran Chamber of Commerce, Industries, Mines and Agriculture Samad Hassanzadeh has said Iran sees Kenya as a strategic and two-way partner, and can play a role as Kenya’s gateway to Central Asia and Eurasia.
