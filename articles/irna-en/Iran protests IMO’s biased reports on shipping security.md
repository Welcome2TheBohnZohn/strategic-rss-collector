# Iran protests IMO’s biased reports on shipping security

Published: 2026-11-04 14:38:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86312673/Iran-protests-IMO-s-biased-reports-on-shipping-security

London, IRNA – The Islamic Republic of Iran has criticized the International Maritime Organization (IMO) biased reports on the shipping crisis, stressing that seafarer protection should not be selective and that the difficult conditions facing Iranian seafarers must also be addressed.
