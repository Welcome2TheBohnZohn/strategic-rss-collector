# Enemy keeps long distance out of fear of Iran’s Navy: Commander

Published: 2026-11-27 12:18:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333982/Enemy-keeps-long-distance-out-of-fear-of-Iran-s-Navy-Commander

Mashhad, IRNA — The enemies of the Islamic Republic claim Iran’s Navy lacks the capability to fight, yet they have deployed their forces miles away from Iranian forces out of fear, says Deputy Chief of the Iranian Army for Coordination Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari.
