# Iran beat Japan to win fourth straight Asian Games volleyball gold

Published: 2026-10-03 13:42:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282001/Iran-beat-Japan-to-win-fourth-straight-Asian-Games-volleyball

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s men’s volleyball team beat hosts Japan 3-1 in the final of the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya on Saturday to win the gold medal for a fourth consecutive edition.
