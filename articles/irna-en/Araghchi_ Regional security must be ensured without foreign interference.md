# Araghchi: Regional security must be ensured without foreign interference

Published: 2026-10-25 09:26:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302752/Araghchi-Regional-security-must-be-ensured-without-foreign-interference

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Iran seeks peaceful coexistence with its neighbors, stressing that peace and security in West Asia must be ensured by regional countries themselves, without foreign interference.
