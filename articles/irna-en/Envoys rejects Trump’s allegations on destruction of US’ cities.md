# Envoys rejects Trump’s allegations on destruction of US’ cities

Published: 2026-10-14 06:23:40

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86292297/Envoys-rejects-Trump-s-allegations-on-destruction-of-US-cities

Moscow, IRNA – Iran’s Ambassador to Russia, Kazem Jalali, said Donald Trump’s allegations about the possible destruction of American cities are completely unfounded, unrealistic, and amount to nothing more than campaign rhetoric.
