# Pezeshkian: Iran will not bow to pressure or intimidation

Published: 2026-09-13 05:32:00

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86262276/Pezeshkian-Iran-will-not-bow-to-pressure-or-intimidation

Tehran, IRNA — President Masoud Pezeshkian says that the Iranian people will not be forced into submission through pressure or intimidation, declaring that Iran will not surrender.
