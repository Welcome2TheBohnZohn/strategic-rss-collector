# Sanaa: Massacre of Yemeni people will not deter them from reclaiming their rights

Published: 2026-10-24 18:00:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86302174/Sanaa-Massacre-of-Yemeni-people-will-not-deter-them-from-reclaiming

Tehran, IRNA – In a statement referring to Saudi attacks against the people of Yemen, the Foreign Ministry of the Yemeni government in Sana'a emphasized that these killings will never deter them from reclaiming their rights.
