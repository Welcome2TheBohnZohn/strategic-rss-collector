# Armed Forces chief stresses domestic capabilities to strengthen electronic, cyber warfare

Published: 2026-09-01 13:10:56

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86252288/Armed-Forces-chief-stresses-domestic-capabilities-to-strengthen

Tehran, IRNA – Chief of Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces Major General Ali Abdollahi has stressed the need to rely on domestic capabilities and the country’s elite youth to boost air defense’s electronic and cyber warfare capabilities.
