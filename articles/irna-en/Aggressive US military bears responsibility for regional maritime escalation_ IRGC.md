# Aggressive US military bears responsibility for regional maritime escalation: IRGC

Published: 2026-10-09 14:39:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287702/Aggressive-US-military-bears-responsibility-for-regional-maritime

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says the US military must be blamed for the escalation of tensions in the regional shipping routes, announcing a new fire incident on a tanker attempting to pass through an illegal route south of the Strait of Hormuz.
