# Iran sends largest trade delegation in decade to Russia

Published: 2026-09-20 11:55:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269115/Iran-sends-largest-trade-delegation-in-decade-to-Russia

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has sent its largest trade delegation in the past decade to Russia, comprising about 30 government officials and 180 representatives of chambers of commerce, traders, and economic activists, an official at the Trade Promotion Organization of Iran (TPO) says.
