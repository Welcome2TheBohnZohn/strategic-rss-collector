# President Pezeshkian highlights unity as key to building stronger Iran

Published: 2026-10-11 11:38:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289489/President-Pezeshkian-highlights-unity-as-key-to-building-stronger

Tehran, IRNA – President Masoud Pezeshkian has called on Iranians to support one another and set aside divisions, saying national solidarity would help the country grow stronger across scientific, social and humanitarian fields.
