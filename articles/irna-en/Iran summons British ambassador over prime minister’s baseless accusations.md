# Iran summons British ambassador over prime minister’s baseless accusations

Published: 2026-10-01 14:48:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86280403/Iran-summons-British-ambassador-over-prime-minister-s-baseless

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Foreign Ministry has summoned the British ambassador in Tehran following “baseless” accusations by the British prime minister regarding Iran’s involvement behind the attack at the US Air Force base in Fairford.
