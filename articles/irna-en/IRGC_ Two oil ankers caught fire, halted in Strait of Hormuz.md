# IRGC: Two oil ankers caught fire, halted in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-10-20 11:48:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298387/IRGC-Two-tanker-caught-fire-halted-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – Two non-compliant tankers caught fire and were halted after hitting mines laid in the southern Strait of Hormuz while attempting to pass through prohibited area, The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps said on Tuesday.
