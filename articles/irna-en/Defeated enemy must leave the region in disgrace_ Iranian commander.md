# Defeated enemy must leave the region in disgrace: Iranian commander

Published: 2026-09-20 12:32:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86269165/Defeated-enemy-must-leave-the-region-in-disgrace-Iranian-commander

Tehran, IRNA – Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari, deputy chief of the Iranian Army for Coordination, referring to the US-Israeli imposed war, says the enemies must leave the region after facing disgrace and humiliation at the hands of the Iranian Armed Forces.
