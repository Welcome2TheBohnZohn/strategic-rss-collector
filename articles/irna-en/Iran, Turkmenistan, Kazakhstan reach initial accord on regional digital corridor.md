# Iran, Turkmenistan, Kazakhstan reach initial accord on regional digital corridor

Published: 2026-11-10 13:58:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318204/Iran-Turkmenistan-Kazakhstan-reach-initial-accord-on-regional

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s minister of information and communications technology announced an initial trilateral agreement with Turkmenistan and Kazakhstan to establish a digital corridor to connect Central Asia to the Persian Gulf and neighboring countries.
