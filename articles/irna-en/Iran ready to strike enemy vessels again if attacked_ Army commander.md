# Iran ready to strike enemy vessels again if attacked: Army commander

Published: 2026-10-11 13:21:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289688/Iran-ready-to-strike-enemy-vessels-again-if-attacked-Army-commander

Tehran, IRNA – Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari, deputy chief of the Iranian Army for Coordination, says Iran had inflicted damage on enemy vessels and would strike them again if further action were taken against the country.
