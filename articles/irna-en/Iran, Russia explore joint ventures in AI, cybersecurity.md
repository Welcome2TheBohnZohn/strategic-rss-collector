# Iran, Russia explore joint ventures in AI, cybersecurity

Published: 2026-10-11 05:40:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86289037/Iran-Russia-explore-joint-ventures-in-AI-cybersecurity

Moscow, IRNA – Iranian and Russian technology companies have examined joint investment, technology transfer and market expansion in advanced technologies, with artificial intelligence and cybersecurity emerging as key areas for deeper cooperation at a bilateral forum in Moscow.
