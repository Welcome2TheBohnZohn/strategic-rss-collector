# Iran ready for decisive response to any new aggression, says Army spokesperson

Published: 2026-09-27 13:09:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86276288/Iran-ready-for-decisive-response-to-any-new-aggression-says

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akrami-Nia has emphasized the Armed Forces’ readiness to confront any potential aggression, noting that if the enemy launches another attack, the Armed Forces will deliver a more powerful, extensive, and decisive response.
