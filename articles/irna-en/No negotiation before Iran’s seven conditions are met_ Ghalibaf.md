# No negotiation before Iran’s seven conditions are met: Ghalibaf

Published: 2026-11-24 07:29:51

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330981/No-negotiation-before-Iran-s-seven-conditions-are-met-Ghalibaf

Tehran, IRNA — Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says no negotiations will take place until seven conditions set by Iran are met, stressing that the country is fully prepared to respond decisively to any hostile moves.
