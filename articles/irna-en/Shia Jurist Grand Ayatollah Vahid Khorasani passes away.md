# Shia Jurist Grand Ayatollah Vahid Khorasani passes away

Published: 2026-11-17 05:22:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86324009/Shia-Jurist-Grand-Ayatollah-Vahid-Khorasani-passes-away

Tehran, IRNA – Grand Ayatollah Hossein Vahid Khorasani, a leading Shia source of emulation and prominent Islamic jurist, has passed away, his office announced on Tuesday.
