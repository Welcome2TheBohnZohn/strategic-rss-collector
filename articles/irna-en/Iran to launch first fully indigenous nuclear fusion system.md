# Iran to launch first fully indigenous nuclear fusion system

Published: 2026-11-15 15:38:50

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322711/Iran-to-launch-first-fully-indigenous-nuclear-fusion-system

Tehran, IRNA – Iran is preparing to bring its first domestically developed nuclear fusion system into operation, with the project highlighting the country’s advances in cutting-edge nuclear technology, the head of the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) said on Sunday.
