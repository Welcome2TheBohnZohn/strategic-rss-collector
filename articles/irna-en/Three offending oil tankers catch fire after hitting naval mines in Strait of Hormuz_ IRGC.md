# Three offending oil tankers catch fire after hitting naval mines in Strait of Hormuz: IRGC

Published: 2026-11-08 11:31:42

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315927/Three-offending-oil-tankers-catch-fire-after-hitting-naval-mines

Tehran, IRNA – The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy says one more tanker caught fire after an explosion, bringing to three such incidents on vessels attempting to pass through unauthorized routes in the Strait of Hormuz, since early hours of Sunday.
