# Tehran orders Swedish diplomat to leave Iran within 48 hours

Published: 2026-09-17 10:33:06

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266666/Tehran-orders-Swedish-diplomat-to-leave-Iran-within-48-hours

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has rejected Sweden’s allegations against the Islamic Republic and ordered a Swedish diplomat in Tehran to leave the country within 48 hours in response to Stockholm’s decision to prevent an Iranian diplomat from continuing his duties in Sweden.
