# US miscalculated Iran’s resilience in war, Basij chief says

Published: 2026-10-18 16:02:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86296330/US-miscalculated-Iran-s-resilience-in-war-Basij-chief-says

Tehran, IRNA – Hossein Taeb, head of Iran’s Basij Organization, said the United States had accepted defeat in the military confrontation with Iran after miscalculating the country’s resilience and the ability of its armed forces to sustain the fight.
