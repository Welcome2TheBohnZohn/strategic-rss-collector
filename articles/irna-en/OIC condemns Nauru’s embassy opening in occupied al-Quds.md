# OIC condemns Nauru’s embassy opening in occupied al-Quds

Published: 2026-09-09 15:36:10

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259869/OIC-condemns-Nauru-s-embassy-opening-in-occupied-al-Quds

Tehran, IRNA – The Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) strongly condemned the Republic of Nauru’s decision to open its embassy in occupied Jerusalem al-Quds.
