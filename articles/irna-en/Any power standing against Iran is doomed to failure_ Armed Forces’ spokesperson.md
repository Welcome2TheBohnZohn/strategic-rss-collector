# Any power standing against Iran is doomed to failure: Armed Forces’ spokesperson

Published: 2026-11-08 10:11:26

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86315818/Any-power-standing-against-Iran-is-doomed-to-failure-Armed-Forces

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian Armed Forces spokesperson Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi says any power that stands against Iran’s strong resolve is doomed to fail and lose credibility in the eyes of world public opinion.
