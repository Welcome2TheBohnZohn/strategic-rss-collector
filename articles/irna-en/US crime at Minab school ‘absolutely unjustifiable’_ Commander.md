# US crime at Minab school ‘absolutely unjustifiable’: Commander

Published: 2026-11-19 11:10:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86326782/US-crime-at-Minab-school-absolutely-unjustifiable-Commander

Bandar Abbas, IRNA — The head of the Chiefs of Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces has described the catastrophe at the Minab school as clear evidence of a US crime, stressing that the horrific act committed by the US military against the children of the school is “absolutely unjustifiable.”
