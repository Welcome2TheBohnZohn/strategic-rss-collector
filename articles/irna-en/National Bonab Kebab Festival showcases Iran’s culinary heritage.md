# National Bonab Kebab Festival showcases Iran’s culinary heritage

Published: 2026-09-13 06:01:55

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86262286/National-Bonab-Kebab-Festival-showcases-Iran-s-culinary-heritage

The ninth National Bonab Kebab Festival opened in Bonab City, East Azerbaijan Province, drawing officials, residents, and tourists. Running through September 15, the event features traditional Bonab kebab alongside cultural and artistic programs and showcases local products, highlighting the city’s growing reputation as a destination for culinary tourism.
