# Iran’s new consul general in Kazan meets Araghchi to outline agenda

Published: 2026-09-02 16:06:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253627/Iran-s-new-consul-general-in-Kazan-meets-Araghchi-to-outline

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s newly appointed Consul General in Kazan, Russia, Mohammad Basraei, met with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi ahead of departing for his post to present his proposed agenda for advancing bilateral ties.
