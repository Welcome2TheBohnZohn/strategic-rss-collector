# Army chief hails Basij as nation's shield, formidable barrier against foes

Published: 2026-11-23 06:44:17

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86329777/Army-chief-hails-Basij-as-nation-s-shield-formidable-barrier

Tehran, IRNA — Chief Commander of the Islamic Republic of Iran, Army Major General Amir Hatami, has hailed the role the Basij— an organization of volunteer forces—played during critical situations in the country, including the recent US-Israeli war of aggression.
