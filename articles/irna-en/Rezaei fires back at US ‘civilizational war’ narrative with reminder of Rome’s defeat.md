# Rezaei fires back at US ‘civilizational war’ narrative with reminder of Rome’s defeat

Published: 2026-10-09 18:49:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86287846/Rezaei-fires-back-at-US-civilizational-war-narrative-with-reminder

Tehran, IRNA – Major General Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council (SNSC), has invoked the ancient defeat of Rome by Sasanian king Shapur I to counter an attempt by US officials to frame the aggression against Iran as a civilizational war.
