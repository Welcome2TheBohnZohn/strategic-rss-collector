# Senior MP: Strike on US warships represents mere fraction of Iran’s military prowess

Published: 2026-09-08 09:15:19

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258329/MP-Strike-on-US-warships-represents-mere-fraction-of-Iran-s

Tehran, IRNA — Iranian Deputy Parliament Speaker Ali Nikzad says that the strikes on US warships and the obliteration of their commanders’ headquarters represent only a small portion of Iran’s full military capacity.
