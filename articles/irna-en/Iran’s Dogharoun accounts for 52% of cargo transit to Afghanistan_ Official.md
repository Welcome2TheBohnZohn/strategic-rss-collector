# Iran’s Dogharoun accounts for 52% of cargo transit to Afghanistan: Official

Published: 2026-10-18 07:18:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295734/Iran-s-Dogharoun-accounts-for-52-of-cargo-transit-to-Afghanistan

Taybad, IRNA – The Dogharoun border terminal accounts for 52 percent of cargo transit to Afghanistan through four border crossings in Iran’s northeastern Khorasan Razavi Province, according to a local official.
