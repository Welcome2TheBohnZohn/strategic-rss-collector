# Iran warns of consequences of promoting civilizational confrontation

Published: 2026-10-12 10:48:37

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86290453/Iran-warns-of-consequences-of-promoting-civilizational-confrontation

Tehran, IRNA — Acting Defense Minister Brigadier General Majid Ibn al-Reza warns that those who deliberately promote civilizational confrontation must bear responsibility for its dangerous consequences, saying they should not assume that its repercussions will never affect them.
