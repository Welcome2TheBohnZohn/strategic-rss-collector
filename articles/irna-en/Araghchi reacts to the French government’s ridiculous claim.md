# Araghchi reacts to the French government’s ridiculous claim

Published: 2026-11-10 18:39:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86318401/Araghchi-reacts-to-the-French-government-s-ridiculous-claim

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, In response to the French government's ridiculous claim regarding a cyberattack on refrigerators in that country, wrote in a post on his X account that the French government is shifting the blame onto others to cover up its own inadequacies.
