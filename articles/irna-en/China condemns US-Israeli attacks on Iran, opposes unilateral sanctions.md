# China condemns US-Israeli attacks on Iran, opposes unilateral sanctions

Published: 2026-10-19 16:20:53

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86297507/China-condemns-US-Israeli-attacks-on-Iran-opposes-unilateral

Tehran, IRNA – Chinese Premier Li Qiang condemned the US-Israeli-imposed war against Iran during a meeting with First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref on Monday, reaffirming Beijing’s opposition to unilateral US sanctions and its support for Iran’s legitimate rights.
