# Third tanker hits naval mine in eastern Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-17 21:47:48

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86325178/Third-tanker-hits-naval-mine-in-eastern-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s IRGC Navy says a third tanker violating regulations set in place by Tehran has struck a naval mine and caught fire in the eastern part of the Strait of Hormuz.
