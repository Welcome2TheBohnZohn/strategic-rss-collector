# Iran on par with developed nations in nuclear science: AEOI chief

Published: 2026-10-13 12:24:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86291602/Iran-on-par-with-developed-nations-in-nuclear-science-AEOI-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Iran has reached the level of developed countries in nuclear science and technology, producing domestically the equipment and systems needed by national industries, Vice President and Atomic Energy Organization of Iran (AEOI) Chief Mohammad Eslami said Tuesday.
