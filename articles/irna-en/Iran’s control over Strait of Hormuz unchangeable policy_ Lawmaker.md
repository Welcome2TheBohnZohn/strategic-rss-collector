# Iran’s control over Strait of Hormuz unchangeable policy: Lawmaker

Published: 2026-10-04 16:09:31

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86283124/Iran-s-control-over-Strait-of-Hormuz-unchangeable-policy-Lawmaker

Tehran, IRNA — Alaeddin Boroujerdi, a senior member of the Parliament’s National Security and Foreign Policy Commission, says Tehran will continue to maintain firm control over the Strait of Hormuz, calling it an unchangeable policy of the Islamic Republic.
