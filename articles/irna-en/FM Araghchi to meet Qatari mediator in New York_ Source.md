# FM Araghchi to meet Qatari mediator in New York: Source

Published: 2026-09-28 16:11:14

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277496/FM-Araghchi-to-meet-Qatari-mediator-in-New-York-Source

New York, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi is scheduled to meet with the Qatari mediator in New York later today to discuss the latest developments regarding the exchange of messages between Tehran and Washington, according to an informed source.
