# Aref and Araghchi hail Iran’s Asian Games success, volleyball title

Published: 2026-10-03 16:16:57

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282103/Aref-and-Araghchi-hail-Iran-s-Asian-Games-success-volleyball

Tehran, IRNA – First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref congratulated Iranian athletes on winning 52 medals at the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, while Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi praised the national volleyball team for retaining its Asian Games title.
