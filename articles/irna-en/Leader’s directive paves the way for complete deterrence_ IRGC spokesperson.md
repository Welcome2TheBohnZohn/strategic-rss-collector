# Leader’s directive paves the way for complete deterrence: IRGC spokesperson

Published: 2026-11-01 13:16:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86309361/Leader-s-directive-paves-the-way-for-complete-deterrence-IRGC

Kerman, IRNA – The spokesperson for the Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC), referring to a recent order from the Leader of the Islamic Revolution to respond swiftly and forcefully to any new aggression, says the removal of geographical limits in targeting enemies paves the way for complete deterrence.
