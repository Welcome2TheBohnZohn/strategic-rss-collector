# Iran vows to prosecute perpetrators of Minab, Lamerd crimes

Published: 2026-11-03 12:01:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86311390/Iran-vows-to-prosecute-perpetrators-of-Minab-Lamerd-crimes

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s minister of justice stressed that the crimes committed by the enemies in the deadly attack on a primary school in the city of Minab and a stadium in the city of Lamerd will be legally pursued until the perpetrators are held accountable.
