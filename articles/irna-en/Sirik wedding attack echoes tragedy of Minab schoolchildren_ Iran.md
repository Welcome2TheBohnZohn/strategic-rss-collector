# Sirik wedding attack echoes tragedy of Minab schoolchildren: Iran

Published: 2026-09-05 08:31:35

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86255296/Sirik-wedding-attack-echoes-tragedy-of-Minab-schoolchildren

Tehran, IRNA — Iran’s administration spokesperson, Fatemeh Mohajerani, says the recent US attack on the Sirik district in southern Hormozgan Province cannot be viewed separately from the earlier killing of schoolchildren in Minab, stressing that the United States, as the aggressor and initiator of the military action, should be held accountable for the tragedies.
