# Shenzhen summit proved US no longer able to forge alliance against Iran: Leader’s adviser

Published: 2026-11-20 13:33:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86327535/Shenzhen-summit-proved-US-no-longer-able-to-forge-alliance-against

Tehran – IRNA – Mohammad Mokhber, a senior adviser to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution, says the recent trilateral summit of Chinese, Russian, and US leaders in Shenzhen conveyed a clear message to the region that “the United States can no longer forge an alliance against Iran.”
