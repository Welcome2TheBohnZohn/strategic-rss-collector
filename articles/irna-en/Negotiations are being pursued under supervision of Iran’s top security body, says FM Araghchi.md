# Negotiations are being pursued under supervision of Iran’s top security body, says FM Araghchi

Published: 2026-11-26 17:01:58

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333497/Negotiations-are-being-pursued-under-supervision-of-Iran-s-top

Tehran, IRNA — Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, referring to the ongoing efforts by mediators to advance the diplomatic process between Iran and the United States, says the negotiation mechanism is being pursued under the supervision of the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC).
