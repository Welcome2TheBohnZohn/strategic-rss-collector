# Mourning flag raised over holy shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh (SA)

Published: 2026-09-22 08:55:49

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86270997/Mourning-flag-raised-over-holy-shrine-of-Hazrat-Masoumeh-SA

A ceremony to replace the flag on the dome of the holy shrine of Hazrat Fatemeh Masoumeh (SA) was held in the city of Qom on September 20, 2026, coinciding with the approaching anniversary of her martyrdom. A large number of pilgrims and shrine keepers attended the event.
