# Iran’s envoy: US’ new threats show desperation, confusion

Published: 2026-10-16 15:03:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86294257/Iran-s-envoy-US-new-threats-show-desperation-confusion

London, IRNA — Iran’s ambassador to Berlin has pointed to a German newspaper report questioning whether the ‘mother of all bombs’ would even work against Iran, calling the US threat to use such a weapon a sign that warmongers are desperate and confused in the face of Iranian resilience.
