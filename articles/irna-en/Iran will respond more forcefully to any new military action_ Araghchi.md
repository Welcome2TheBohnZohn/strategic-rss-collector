# Iran will respond more forcefully to any new military action: Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-04 08:57:34

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86282653/Iran-will-respond-more-forcefully-to-any-new-military-action

Tehran, IRNA – Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Iran remains firm and serious in both defending itself and pursuing diplomacy, warning that the country will respond more forcefully than before if it faces renewed military action.
