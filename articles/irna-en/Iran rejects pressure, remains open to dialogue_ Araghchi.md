# Iran rejects pressure, remains open to dialogue: Araghchi

Published: 2026-10-17 13:00:54

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86295149/Iran-rejects-pressure-remains-open-to-dialogue-Araghchi

Tehran, IRNA – Iran will not surrender its sovereign rights under pressure but remains ready for dialogue based on mutual respect and credible guarantees, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said on Saturday.
