# Committee formed to mark martyrdom anniversary of Iran’s martyred Leader

Published: 2026-11-13 11:33:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86320640/Committee-formed-to-mark-martyrdom-anniversary-of-Iran-s-martyred

Tehran, IRNA — Following a directive from Seyed Mohsen Khamenei, head of the Office for the Preservation and Publication of the Works of the Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali Khamenei, a committee was formed to commemorate the martyrdom anniversary of Iran’s martyred Leader.
