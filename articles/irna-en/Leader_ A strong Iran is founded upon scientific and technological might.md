# Leader: A strong Iran is founded upon scientific and technological might

Published: 2026-10-26 18:11:16

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86304252/Leader-A-strong-Iran-is-founded-upon-scientific-and-technological

Tehran, IRNA – Leader of the Islamic Revolution Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei, in a message addressed to elites and medalists in scientific and athletic competitions on the global stage, highlighted the unparalleled role of these young, high-achieving individuals—who bring honor to the nation in science and sports—in fostering hope, progress, and vitality within the country.
