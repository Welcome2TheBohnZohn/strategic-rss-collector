# Iran conveyed conditions to US in Qatar-mediated New York meeting: Baqaei

Published: 2026-09-23 06:00:03

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86271938/Iran-conveyed-conditions-to-US-in-Qatar-mediated-New-York-meeting

New York, IRNA – Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei says a meeting between Iranian and American representatives in New York was held through Qatari mediation, during which Iran clearly conveyed its conditions for the resumption of diplomacy to the US side.
