# Official: Iran attends Eurasian ombudsmen gathering in Moscow

Published: 2026-11-15 14:54:01

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86322450/Official-Iran-attends-Eurasian-ombudsmen-gathering-in-Moscow

Moscow, IRNA – The Head of Iran’s General Inspection Office has arrived in Moscow to participate in the 11th Eurasian Ombudsmen Union and the 10th International Scientific and Practical Ombudsman Conference, and to meet Russian judicial officials.
