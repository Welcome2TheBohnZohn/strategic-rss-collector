# Iran criticizes IAEA over safeguards expectations amid US, Israeli threats

Published: 2026-09-09 16:25:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86259865/Iran-criticizes-IAEA-over-safeguards-expectations-amid-US-Israeli

London, IRNA — Iran’s permanent mission to the United Nations and other international organizations in Vienna has criticized the IAEA’s expectation of normal safeguards implementation amid continued US and Israeli threats and aggression.
