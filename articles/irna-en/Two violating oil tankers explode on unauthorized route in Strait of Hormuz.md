# Two violating oil tankers explode on unauthorized route in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-25 06:05:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86331897/Two-violating-oil-tankers-explode-on-unauthorized-route-in-Strait

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy Public Relations announced that two violating oil tankers exploded on the unauthorized route of the Strait of Hormuz.
