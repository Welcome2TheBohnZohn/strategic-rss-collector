# Judiciary chief heads to India for BRICS summit

Published: 2026-09-03 07:48:59

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86253908/Judiciary-chief-heads-to-India-for-BRICS-summit

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Judiciary Chief, Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei, says his trip to India for the BRICS summit aims to advance issues related to international trade and convey the voice of the Iranian people to the world.
