# Iranian judoka wins gold at SCO Member States Open Cup in Russia

Published: 2026-11-15 15:36:11

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86322714/Iranian-judoka-wins-gold-at-SCO-Member-States-Open-Cup-in-Russia

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian judoka Elyas Parhizgar has won a gold medal at the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) Member States Open Cup, underway in Russia.
