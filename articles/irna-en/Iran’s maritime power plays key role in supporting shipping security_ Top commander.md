# Iran’s maritime power plays key role in supporting shipping security: Top commander

Published: 2026-09-30 09:42:44

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86278922/Iran-s-maritime-power-plays-key-role-in-supporting-shipping-security

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Navy commander Rear Admiral Shahram Irani has described Iran’s maritime power as a key factor in safeguarding national interests, regional security and the safety of shipping routes.
