# Hollywood-style Shows cannot mask US war crimes: Iran’s London embassy

Published: 2026-09-17 12:50:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86266768/Hollywood-style-Shows-cannot-mask-US-war-crimes-Iran-s-London

London, IRNA — The Iranian Embassy in London has posted a tragic video of the burial scene of the ‘Minab’ school children martyred in a US missile attack, slamming the US war crimes as so blatant that they cannot be concealed by “Hollywood-style movies produced by the US Secretary of War.”
