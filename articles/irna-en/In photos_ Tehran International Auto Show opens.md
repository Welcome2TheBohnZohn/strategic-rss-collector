# In photos: Tehran International Auto Show opens

Published: 2026-09-15 08:32:23

Source: https://en.irna.ir/photo/86264472/In-photos-Tehran-International-Auto-Show-opens

The 7th Tehran International Auto Show opened on Sunday, September 13, 2026, bringing together the latest automotive products, technologies and industry developments at Shahr-e Aftab International Exhibition Center. The event will run through September 17.
