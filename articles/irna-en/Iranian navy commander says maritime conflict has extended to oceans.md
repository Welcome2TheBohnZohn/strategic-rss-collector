# Iranian navy commander says maritime conflict has extended to oceans

Published: 2026-11-27 10:27:38

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86333900/Iranian-navy-commander-says-maritime-conflict-has-extended-to

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Navy Commander Rear Admiral Shahram Irani says the conflict at sea has expanded into the oceans, noting that Iranian forces have prevented adversaries from entering the country’s territorial and nearby waters.
