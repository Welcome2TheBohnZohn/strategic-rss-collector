# War proved US cannot be legitimate superpower: Army

Published: 2026-09-16 10:47:22

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86265890/War-proved-US-cannot-be-legitimate-superpower-Army

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akrami-Nia says the US-Israeli war on Iran demonstrated that the United States cannot appear as a legitimate superpower, arguing that the world is moving toward a “post-American” order.
