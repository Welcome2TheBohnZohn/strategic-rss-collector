# Iran prepared to withstand naval blockade: VP Aref

Published: 2026-11-25 08:04:41

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86332088/Iran-prepared-to-withstand-naval-blockade-VP-Aref

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s First Vice President Mohammad Reza Aref says that the country is not concerned about a US-imposed naval blockade, pointing to a significant increase in the unloading and clearance of essential goods through its northern and other non-southern borders.
