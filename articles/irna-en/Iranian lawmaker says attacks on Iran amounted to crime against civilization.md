# Iranian lawmaker says attacks on Iran amounted to crime against civilization

Published: 2026-09-28 14:13:18

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86277401/Iranian-lawmaker-says-attacks-on-Iran-amounted-to-crime-against

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian lawmaker Ebrahim Rezaei says that the US-Israeli attacks on Iran went beyond a military assault, describing them as an overt crime against the region’s civilization, culture and history.
