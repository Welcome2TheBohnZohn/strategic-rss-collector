# Araghchi urges UN to react to unlawful US sanctions

Published: 2026-08-26 19:04:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86247222/Araghchi-urges-UN-to-react-to-unlawful-US-sanctions

Tehran, IRNA – Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi published on Wednesday a letter to United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres in which he urged the organization and the UN Security Council to respond to the United States' latest economic offensive against his country.
