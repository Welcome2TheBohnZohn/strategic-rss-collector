# Speaker Ghalibaf vows justice for slain police official in southeast Iran

Published: 2026-10-10 10:31:36

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86288456/Speaker-Ghalibaf-vows-justice-for-slain-police-official-in-southeast

Tehran, IRNA – Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has vowed that those responsible for the assassination of Nosrat Eftekhari, a senior police official in Sistan-Baluchestan Province, will face justice, praising her efforts to address local communities’ problems and strengthen social cohesion.
