# Iranian ambassador meets with Tajikistan’s minister of transport; emphasizes expansion of cooperation

Published: 2026-10-15 18:27:15

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86293781/Iranian-ambassador-meets-with-Tajikistan-s-minister-of-transport

Tehran, IRNA — The Iranian ambassador to Tajikistan and the Tajik Minister of Transport met and discussed ongoing cooperation in the fields of transport and transit, as well as ways to further develop and expand this collaboration.
