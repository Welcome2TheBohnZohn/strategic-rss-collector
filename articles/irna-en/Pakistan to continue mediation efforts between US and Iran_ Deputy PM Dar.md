# Pakistan to continue mediation efforts between US and Iran: Deputy PM Dar

Published: 2026-10-20 13:28:29

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86298491/Pakistan-to-continue-mediation-efforts-between-US-and-Iran-Deputy
