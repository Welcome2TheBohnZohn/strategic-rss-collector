# Three more offending tankers hit mines in Strait of Hormuz

Published: 2026-11-24 05:34:46

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86330775/Three-more-offending-tankers-hit-mines-in-Strait-of-Hormuz

Tehran, IRNA — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy has announced three separate fire incidents involving oil tankers as they were trying to pass through unsafe routes in the Strait of Hormuz.
