# Naval forces fully prepared to defend Iran: Navy chief

Published: 2026-08-30 12:07:25

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86250210/Naval-forces-fully-prepared-to-defend-Iran-Navy-chief

Tehran, IRNA – Iran’s Navy Commander, Rear Admiral Shahram Irani, has warned that the enemy has miscalculated by underestimating the resolve of the Iranian people and the Armed Forces, stressing that Navy forces remain strong and are ready to defend the country.
