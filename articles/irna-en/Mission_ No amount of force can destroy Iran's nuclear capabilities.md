# Mission: No amount of force can destroy Iran's nuclear capabilities

Published: 2026-09-08 19:05:32

Source: https://en.irna.ir/news/86258906/Mission-No-amount-of-force-can-destroy-Iran-s-nuclear-capabilities

London, IRNA – The Islamic Republic of Iran emphasized at the IAEA Board of Governors meeting that its nuclear capabilities are rooted in knowledge, determination, and human resources of a proud nation and no amount of force can change it.
