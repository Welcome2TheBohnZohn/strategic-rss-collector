# 🗓 On October 5, Moscow hosted the eighth meeting of the Moscow Format Consultations on Afghanistan

Published: 2026-10-05 15:31:30

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32576

🗓
On October 5, Moscow
hosted the eighth meeting
of
the Moscow Format Consultations on Afghanistan
, attended by special representatives and senior officials from
Russia, Afghanistan, India, Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, China, Pakistan, Tajikistan
and
Uzbekistan
.
Afghanistan’s delegation was led by Foreign Minister
Amir Khan Muttaqi
. Delegations from
Belarus
and
Egypt
attended as guests.
👉
Sergey Lavrov’s remarks at the eighth meeting of the Moscow Format Consultations on Afghanistan
***
The Moscow Format Consultations
provide an authoritative multilateral platform for countries in the region
to coordinate practical approaches to issues related to Afghanistan.
During the meeting, participants reaffirmed that
there is no alternative to developing cooperation with Kabul on an equal and mutually beneficial basis
and supporting Afghanistan’s integration into the global economy.
Participants once again called on the international community
to provide regular humanitarian assistance to the Afghan people
, stressing that such aid
must not be politicised
.
🤝
On the sidelines of the event,
Sergey Lavrov
held separate talks
with Foreign Minister of Afghanistan
Amir Khan Muttaqi
.
The Ministers discussed
prospects for developing bilateral cooperation
and working together in international and regional forums.
