Remarks
by Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at a meeting with participants in the Dialogue for the Future programme organised by
the Alexander Gorchakov Public Diplomacy Fund
(Moscow, November 26, 2026)
Key points
:
•
The old order
that has prevailed for 500 years – dominated first by Europe and later by the “collective West” –
is inevitably giving way to new centres of growth
. This is not the result of revolutions, but
a natural historical process
driven by the laws of socioeconomic development.
• We have pursued a consistent policy.
We have never tried to impose anything on anyone.
We have always advocated, firstly,
collective consideration of problems
, whether at the
#UN
, the
#OSCE
or any other forum, and, secondly,
honouring the agreements reached
. Words must match deeds.
•
We were deceived time and again in the early years following the dissolution of the Soviet Union
, when Russia was assuming an independent role on the international stage and reaching agreements with the Americans and Europeans.
The NATO-Russia Council
was established, and an extensive framework of relations between Russia and the EU was developed. Yet all of this was brought down overnight simply because it was decided that
Russia had no right to defend its legitimate interests.
• Had it not been for Ukraine, another pretext would have been found to evade commitments to equal and mutually beneficial cooperation.
☝️
All the West ever wanted was to dictate its terms.
• Since President
Donald Trump
took office, the US has demonstrated
a degree of political maturity
that has long been absent in Europe. Despite the remaining differences, the Americans are
open to dialogue
and, moreover, are interested in maintaining diplomatic and other contacts.
• No two countries on Earth can have interests that coincide 100% on every issue, especially when it comes to major powers such as the US and Russia. This was true both during the Cold War and in the era of détente.
Dialogue, however, is essential under all circumstances.
To their credit, the Americans understand this and are
engaging in dialogue
on Ukraine, recognising
the need to eliminate the root causes of the crisis
, namely the creation of threats to Russia’s security right on our borders.
•
We are a people accustomed to facing all manner of trials.
Our people have endured them in the past, are enduring them now and will undoubtedly continue to do so in the future. At the same time,
we remain open to dialogue with everyone
, but it must be a dialogue between decent people, conducted in a manner befitting decent people.
