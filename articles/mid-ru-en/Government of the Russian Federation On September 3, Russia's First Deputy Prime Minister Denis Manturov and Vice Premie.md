Government of the Russian Federation
:
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
On September 3, Russia's First Deputy Prime Minister Denis Manturov and Vice Premier of the State Council of China Ding Xuexiang
held a meeting
of the Russia-China Intergovernmental Commission on Investment Cooperation on the sidelines of
the 11th Eastern Economic Forum
.
In the first half of 2026,
Russia-China trade grew by 22%
and
exceeded $135 billion
.
💬
Russia's First Deputy Prime Minister
Denis Manturov
:
Expanding investment cooperation helps lay a solid foundation for further sustainable growth in trade.
Creating comfortable and predictable conditions for the continued expansion of investment cooperation remains our priority
.
Joint Russia-China initiatives supported by the Commission include
around 90 projects worth approximately $240 billion
. More than half are already at the implementation stage.
Joint projects
are being carried out in metallurgy, chemicals and forestry, mechanical engineering, agriculture, transport, trade and logistics.
☝️
The Commission’s key areas of work in the near term include
:
• continuous monitoring and targeted support for projects;
• raising business awareness of investment opportunities;
• developing new mechanisms and instruments to attract financing for investment projects.
