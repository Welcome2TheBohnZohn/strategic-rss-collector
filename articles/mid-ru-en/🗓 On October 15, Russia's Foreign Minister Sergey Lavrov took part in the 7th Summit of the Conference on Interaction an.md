# 🗓 On October 15, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov took part in the 7th Summit of the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia

Published: 2026-10-16 12:34:54

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32791

🗓
On October 15, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
took part
in
the 7th Summit of the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia
(
#CICA
) in Baku.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks at the Summit's Plenary Session
Discussions focused on
prospects for CICA’s development
and its adaptation to the changing international environment.
The Russian Side emphasised
the importance of integrating CICA harmoniously into the emerging architecture of cooperation across Eurasia
, with a view to establishing a continent-wide framework of equal and indivisible security and seamless cross-border socioeconomic connectivity.
🤝
Sergey Lavrov reaffirmed
Russia’s unwavering commitment to deepening practical cooperation within CICA
,
including in the areas it coordinates
: international information security and support for small and medium-sized enterprises.
Other priorities
include fostering contacts between volunteer organisations and advancing mechanisms for practical industrial cooperation.
Our country’s contribution to
strengthening transport and logistics links across the CICA region
was also highlighted.
Russia welcomed
the extension of
Azerbaijan’s CICA chairmanship
for 2027-2028
and supported
Laos
joining the Conference as a full member.
