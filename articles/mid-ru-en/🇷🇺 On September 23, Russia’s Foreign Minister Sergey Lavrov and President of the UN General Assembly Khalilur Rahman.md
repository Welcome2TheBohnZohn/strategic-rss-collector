On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and President of the UN General Assembly Khalilur Rahman
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Sides discussed issues on
the Session's agenda
in detail.
It was emphasised that the UN General Assembly remains
the central and most representative forum
for dialogue among states. In this context, they stressed
the need to improve the atmosphere and restore mutually respectful dialogue within this body
.
Sergey Lavrov wished Khalilur Rahman
success
in his important international role.
Russia's Foreign Minister expressed hope that the General Assembly’s work during the current Session would be conducted
professionally and free from politicisation
, with a view to
collectively finding responses to global challenges and threats
.
