On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Cuba Bruno Rodriguez
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
In their traditionally open and trusting manner, the Ministers held
a constructive exchange of views on current issues of bilateral cooperation
, discussed selected regional matters and a
greed to further coordinate their positions in international forums
, above all at the UN.
Sergey Lavrov reaffirmed Russia’s
unwavering support for Havana’s just demand for an immediate and complete end to
the US trade, economic, financial and, more recently, energy blockade
. He stressed
the need to remove Cuba
, a friend of Russia, from
the notorious US list of so-called state sponsors of terrorism
and
condemned
the extraterritorial nature of the unilateral restrictive measures imposed on the Island of Freedom.
The Ministers reaffirmed their commitment to continued dialogue and cooperation to strengthen the Russia-Cuba strategic partnership
, promote global and regional security, and build a more just
#MultipolarWorld
order based on respect for the sovereignty of states and their right to choose their own development paths.
