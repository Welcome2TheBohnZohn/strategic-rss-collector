# 🎙 Opening remarks by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi, Chairman of the Abu Dhabi Executive Council Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed

🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi, Chairman of the Abu Dhabi Executive Council Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan on the sidelines of
the XVIII BRICS Summit
(New Delhi, September 12, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Your Highness, I am very glad
to see you again
.
At the outset of our meeting, I would like to note that this year – in December, I believe –
we mark 55 years since the establishment of diplomatic relations between our countries
.
Much has changed over this time, both in the world and in our relations.
The UAE has become one of our closest partners in the region
.
📈
This goes beyond the economy, where things are going well: our trade is growing
– it is already substantial and continues to grow. It also extends to political cooperation and security matters.
Of course, the region is going through
a very difficult period
, but
we remain in
regular contact
with the UAE leadership
, with your father, President of the UAE
Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan
. I ask you to convey my very best wishes to him.
Our officials visit each other, meet and discuss various issues.
He and I discuss the most important and pressing matters
by telephone
. I am confident that, despite all the difficulties we face today, the situation will stabilise.
🤝
Our relations will endure and grow stronger – I have no doubt about that.
Once again, I am very glad to see you and to discuss our bilateral relations. And, of course,
it is very important for us to hear your assessment of events unfolding in the region right now
, as well as your views on how they may develop.
