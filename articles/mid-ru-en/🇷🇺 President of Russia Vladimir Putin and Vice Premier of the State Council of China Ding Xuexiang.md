# 🇷🇺 President of Russia Vladimir Putin and Vice Premier of the State Council of China Ding Xuexiang

Published: 2026-09-02 15:00:00

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31803

🇷🇺
🇨🇳
President of Russia Vladimir Putin and Vice Premier of the State Council of China Ding Xuexiang
held a meeting
on the sidelines of
the Eastern Economic Forum
(Vladivostok, September 2, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am pleased to meet with you again, this time here in
Vladivostok
. Last year,
we met at the St Petersburg International Economic Forum
, and tomorrow we will take part together in
the Plenary Session of the Eastern Economic Forum
.
We are grateful to the Chinese Side for
the traditionally high level of its delegations
at these two major discussion platforms.
Just the day before yesterday, as you know, in Bishke we held
an exceptionally useful, substantive and cordial meeting
with my friend,
President Xi Jinping
, before jointly taking part in
events marking the 25th anniversary of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
.
The high intensity of Russian-Chinese contacts, including at the highest level, confirms the unprecedented nature of our bilateral ties
. Their foundations were laid by
the Treaty of Good-Neighbourliness and Friendly Cooperation
of July 16, 2001, whose 25th anniversary we are celebrating this year.
At the present stage,
our comprehensive partnership and strategic cooperation are developing dynamically
across the board on the principles of equality, mutual support on key issues, mutual understanding and respect for each other’s interests.
📈
Over the past three years,
bilateral trade has consistently exceeded $200 billion, reaching almost $240 billion in 2025
and providing a solid material foundation for our cooperation. We expect a new record this year.
Mutual investment is growing, while cooperation in the flagship energy sector continues to deepen.
Russia is a leading supplier of our traditional commodities to the Chinese market.
Moscow is also a key partner for Beijing in the field of peaceful nuclear energy.
🤝
I am confident that your joint work with colleagues to identify the best ways to further develop our economic cooperation
will be productive
and
will contribute to strengthening the friendly relations between Russia and China
.
