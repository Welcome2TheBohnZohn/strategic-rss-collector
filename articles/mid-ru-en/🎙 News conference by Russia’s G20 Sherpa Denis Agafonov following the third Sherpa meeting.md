News conference by Russia’s G20 Sherpa
Denis Agafonov
following the
third Sherpa meeting
on preparations for the G20 Summit
(Washington, D.C., October 2, 2026)
Key points:
• A series of events, expert meetings and ministerial industry dialogues were held ahead of the G20 Summit in Miami scheduled for December 2026.
• Largely based on the agreements reached during the industry dialogue by the United States, a draft declaration has been developed that will likely be called the Doral Declaration, as custom has it.
• Our meeting focused mainly on the work on this declaration. The discussion helped identify both converging views and areas of disagreement.
• Before the summit convenes, both the presiding party and the sherpas are to find ways to reach consensus and agree on the matters that the country leaders could approve in December in Miami.
• We support the United States’ ambition to produce a finalised and approved document.
• I believe that right now, we are moving in the right direction towards a final declaration that will reflect the actual key economic problems and potential solutions.
#Agenda
• We share the United States’ view that the Group of Twenty shall return to its origins and focus on macroeconomic issues. Our positions are close on many industry-specific topics as well as on energy. However, there are certain matters that should certainly be considered in a slightly different light.
#Geopolitics
• Of course, the geopolitical context occasionally arises in the discussion and it currently has a broad geography, but overall, the dialogue is well-balanced and non-confrontational.
#RussiaG20
• On Russia’s Side, the heads of federal executive bodies at the ministerial level participated in almost all the events concerning the four priorities, which reaffirms our attention and our understanding of the importance of the G20 format.
• The business aspect of Russia-US cooperation within the Group of Twenty can be described as currently being on pause.
• The decision on Russian President Vladimir Putin’s participation in this year’s G20 Summit in Miami will be made closer to the event.
• Russia intends to participate in the G20 events in Britain in 2027.
#SouthAfrica
• The decision [not to invite South Africa to the G20 Summit] is without authority. We insist on South Africa’s immediate reinstatement.

the recent BRICS summit

#BRICS
countries on solidarity with South Africa.
• We hope that common sense will prevail and South Africa will participate in further events.
