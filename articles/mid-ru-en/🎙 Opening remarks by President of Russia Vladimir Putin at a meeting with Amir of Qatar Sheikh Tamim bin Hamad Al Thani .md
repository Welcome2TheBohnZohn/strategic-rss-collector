Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with Amir of Qatar Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani on the sidelines of
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
(Moscow, October 27, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Russia and Qatar are among the world’s largest producers and exporters of natural gas.
Much depends on our effective cooperation and coordination
, including the stability of the global energy market.
Your Highness, we value your personal attention and contribution to advancing
friendly and mutually beneficial relations between Russia and Qatar
.
🤝
Bilateral cooperation continues to develop steadily, and we have established a substantive political dialogue.
The Russian-Qatari Intergovernmental Commission is actively working, co-chaired on the Russian Side by First Deputy Prime Minister
Denis Manturov
.
A range of measures is being implemented to diversify and increase bilateral trade.
Although modest in absolute terms, it continues to grow,
having risen by 10%.
Investment cooperation is expanding.
The Qatar Investment Authority
holds stakes in several Russian companies, including
Rosneft
and
VTB
. Partnerships with
the Russian Direct Investment Fund
are also developing.
Humanitarian contacts are growing.
Days of Russian Culture
took place in Doha in February this year. A Qatari delegation participated in
the United Cultures Forum in St Petersburg
. Tourist exchanges are also increasing.
I would also highlight our closer coordination on the international stage
, given Qatar’s important mediation role in resolving various crises, including in the Persian Gulf region.
As you know, Your Highness, from the very outset of
the conflict surrounding Iran
,
Russia has advocated an end to hostilities and a political and diplomatic solution
.
The Islamabad Memorandum
, signed in June through Qatari and Pakistani mediation, remains relevant as a basis for advancing the negotiating process.
☝️
For our part, we are ready to do everything within our power to help resolve this complex and acute crisis
.
