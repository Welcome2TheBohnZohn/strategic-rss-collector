# 🎙 Opening remarks by Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at a meeting with Chairman of the Supervisory Board of the Serbian state-owned company Srbijagas

Published: 2026-11-11 10:33:16

🎙
Opening remarks
by Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at a meeting with Chairman of the Supervisory Board of the Serbian state-owned company Srbijagas
Aleksandar Vulin
(Moscow, November 11, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
We have known each other for a long time. We have also long known you as one of Serbia’s leading political figures.
You have a keen understanding of political developments and always keep the Serbian people’s overriding goal in sight. Your work as
Chairman of the Supervisory Board of Srbijagas
also reflects
our shared commitment to cooperation that benefits both our countries
.
Talks are currently under way on future gas supplies and a new long-term contract.
I know that Director General of Srbijagas Dusan Bajatovic was also here.
🤝
We remain fully committed to all our agreements, past and present
. We hope the new government, too, will ensure
continuity in Serbia’s strategic partnership with the Russian Federation
.
I look forward to hearing your assessment of the latest developments in your country.
