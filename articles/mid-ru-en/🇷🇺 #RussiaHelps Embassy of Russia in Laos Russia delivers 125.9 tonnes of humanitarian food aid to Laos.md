Embassy of Russia in Laos
:
Russia
delivers
125.9 tonnes of humanitarian food aid to Laos.
On October 20, Russian Ambassador to Laos
Sergey Zhestkyi
attended a ceremony in Luang Namtha marking the handover of another shipment of Russian humanitarian assistance.
As part of Russia's contribution to
the UN World Food Programme
(
#WFP
),
125.9 tonnes of food supplies were delivered to Laos
through
the Russian Emergencies Ministry
, including 79 tonnes of canned fish and 46.9 tonnes of sunflower oil. The aid will be distributed to communities affected by severe flooding, including those in Luang Namtha Province.
In 2025, Russia provided Laos with another
318 tonnes of food supplies
to support school feeding programmes in the country's northern provinces.
🤝
Ambassador Sergey Zhestkyi stressed that Russia's humanitarian assistance to friendly Laos forms an
important part of joint efforts to address the consequences of natural disasters and contributes to the country's socio-economic development
.
