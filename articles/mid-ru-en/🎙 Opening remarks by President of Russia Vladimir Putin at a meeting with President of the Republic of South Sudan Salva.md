🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of the Republic of South Sudan Salva Kiir on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin
: Thank you for making the time to come here and take part in
the Third #RussiaAfrica Summit
.
You attended
the First Summit in Sochi in 2019
, and I remember that your Vice President took part in
the Second Summit in 2023
. Yesterday’s
Plenary Session
once again reaffirmed
our countries’ shared commitment to developing bilateral relations and cooperation
.
🤝
We enjoy friendly relations with South Sudan
. This year, we marked
15 years of diplomatic relations
.
During your visit to Moscow in September 2023,
we held very substantive talks
. We discussed the
bilateral and regional agendas
in detail and
agreed to open
a Russian Embassy in Juba
, which will undoubtedly benefit our interstate ties. I know that the Russian Ambassador presented his credentials to you in January.
We are jointly exploring
opportunities to
strengthen bilateral trade and economic cooperation
. Clearly, our potential has yet to be fully realised.
We will continue to assist in
training professionals for South Sudan
. For the 2026/2027 academic year, we have increased the scholarship quota
to 40 places
.
We are satisfied with our cooperation on the international stage,
including at the UN, which rests on the similarity of Russia’s and South Sudan’s positions on key issues.
👉
Today, we have a good opportunity to review our positions on
key aspects of our cooperation
.
