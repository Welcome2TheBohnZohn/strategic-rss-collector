On November 26, Russia's President Vladimir Putin
sent his greetings
to the participants, organisers and guests of
the International Open Forum Zhukov. Legacy
.
✍️
The message
reads, in part:
In 2026, we celebrate a landmark event:
the 130th birth anniversary of
Georgy Zhukov
,
a renowned military leader, an outstanding statesman and a worthy son of the Fatherland.
Georgy Zhukov devoted his life to serving the nation, and had a heroic military career
– from a private in the First World War to a Marshal of the Soviet Union. His military talent, personal bravery, and the ability to make astute and strategically balanced decisions in the most challenging situations earned him truly nationwide acclaim and largely predetermined the outcome of the final, epoch-making battles of the Great Patriotic War.
I am confident that projects such as your large-scale Forum and the science outreach day organised in his home region – the town of Zhukov and the village of Strelkovka, Kaluga Region –
will greatly contribute to preserving the memory of our national heroes
, including Marshal of Victory Georgy Zhukov, and to the education of our young people, reinforcing the traditions of patriotism and personal responsibility for the future of our Motherland.
