🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Equatorial Guinea Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo on the sidelines of
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
(Moscow, October 27, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am very pleased
to see you in Moscow again
.
In recent years, we have
met regularly
, almost every year, and our meetings reflect
the friendly and consistently constructive nature of our relations
.
Your presence in Moscow in 2025
to mark
the 80th anniversary of our country’s Victory in the Great Patriotic War
was particularly significant.
This time, you have come to the Russian capital to participate in
two major international events
:
the Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
and
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
. We have already held the first of these today, and I would like to express my gratitude for your active contribution.
The coming days will be devoted to advancing the multifaceted
#RussiaAfrica
partnership
. I suggest that today we discuss current bilateral and international issues and outline our next joint steps.
🇷🇺
🇬🇶
We note the positive momentum in relations between Russia and Equatorial Guinea.
Dialogue continues between our Foreign Ministries.
The Russian Embassy in Malabo
resumed operations in 2024.
Groups for cooperation with Equatorial Guinea
are active in
the State Duma
and
the Federation Council
, while
a friendship group with Russia
was established in
Equatorial Guinea’s Senate
last year.
Against this backdrop, trade and economic cooperation is, of course, lagging somewhat behind, and has even seen a slight decline.
We hope that establishing an intergovernmental commission will help improve the situation.
☝️
I would also like to note that
our countries have considerable potential both to increase mutual supplies of goods in demand and to implement promising investment projects
, particularly in energy and mining.
Positive results have been achieved in cultural and humanitarian cooperation.
Every year, we provide federal funding for scholarships enabling
75 of your citizens to study at Russian universities
. This year, we also organised
two professional development programmes
for Equatorial Guinean civil servants at
the Russian Presidential Academy of National Economy and Public Administration
and
St Petersburg State University
. The group in St Petersburg completed its training just yesterday. In total,
more than 1,000 specialists
have been trained in our country over the years.
A Russian Cultural Centre
has been operating in Malabo since 2025.
🤝
We value our effective coordination with Equatorial Guinea on the international stage.
Our countries maintain good relations at international platforms and hold similar positions on key issues, advocating
a polycentric world order
and
the right of all countries to choose their own path of development independently
.
