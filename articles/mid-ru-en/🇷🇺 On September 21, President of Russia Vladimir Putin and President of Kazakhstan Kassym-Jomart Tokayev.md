On September 21, President of Russia Vladimir Putin and President of Kazakhstan Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
had a telephone conversation
.
Kassym-Jomart Tokayev congratulated Vladimir Putin
on
the successful conduct of the elections
to
the State Duma of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation
.
It was emphasised that
the results clearly demonstrate
broad support for the Russian President’s strategic course
aimed at safeguarding the long-term interests of the Russian state.
The Leaders discussed
a number of practical issues on the bilateral agenda
, as well as cooperation across Eurasia, including preparations for
the meeting of the CIS Council of Heads of State
scheduled for early October in Turkmenistan.
🤝
The Presidents reaffirmed their shared commitment to
further advancing the comprehensive strategic partnership and alliance
between Russia and Kazakhstan.
