🎙
Russia’s Deputy Foreign Minister Dmitry Lyubinsky
answer to a media question
from
TASS news agency
on the Nord Stream gas pipelines
(November 13, 2026)
❓
Question: In October, the Higher Regional Court in Hamburg, Germany, began hearing the case concerning the bombing of the Nord Stream gas pipelines. Several hearings have now taken place. How does Russia view the trial and its coverage in the Western media?
💬
Dmitry Lyubinsky:
We are following the trial in Hamburg of Ukrainian national
Sergey Kuznetsov
, who is accused of
blowing up
the Nord Stream 1 and Nord Stream 2 gas pipelines in 2022. The proceedings began in October 2026 and are due to conclude in June 2027.
We would like to believe that this trial will not turn into yet another farce
, as the national investigations in Germany, Denmark and Sweden did.
On various pretexts, the authorities in those countries
avoided cooperating with Russian law enforcement agencies
on the case
, disregarding, among other things, their obligations under international conventions on counter-terrorism.
Stories have been deliberately planted in the Western media, and the practice continues.
The aim is to ensure that those who organised, commissioned and oversaw this act of sabotage, which the German judiciary has classified as a war crime, escape identification and punishment.
The Financial Times
recently weighed in, reporting that it was
indeed Kuznetsov
who carried out the bombings, though it was unclear on whose orders he acted.
☝️
One point is worth recalling here. Within days of
the drone incident in Leipzig
, without any investigation or trial,
the German authorities were already declaring unequivocally that Russia was to blame and demanding the closure of
our Consulate General in Bonn
and
the Russian House in Berlin
.
Yet when it comes to the Hamburg trial, we are told that
all the circumstances must be thoroughly examined to establish a full picture of the crime
.
We shall see how the trial unfolds
, what conclusions it reaches and whether those who actually organised and oversaw the sabotage are publicly named and held to account.
👉
We are, of course, under no illusions.
Let me assure you that
Russia will continue to press for the truth
so that the Nord Stream case is not swept under the rug and those who masterminded and commissioned this major terrorist attack of our time do not, as the saying goes,
“get off the hook”
.
