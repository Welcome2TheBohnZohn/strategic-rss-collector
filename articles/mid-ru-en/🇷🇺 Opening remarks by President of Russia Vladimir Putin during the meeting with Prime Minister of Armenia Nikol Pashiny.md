# 🇷🇺 Opening remarks by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with Prime Minister of Armenia Nikol Pashinyan

🇷🇺
🇦🇲
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with Prime Minister of Armenia Nikol Pashinyan
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We have always supported and continue to support
the development of friendly and mutually beneficial relations with Armenia
, based on the principles of strategic partnership and alliance.
Russia is the Republic’s leading trade and economic partner
, accounting for
around 30% of its trade
, as well as a major investor in Armenia’s economy. In the first half of 2026, bilateral trade reached
$2.5 billion
.
Russian companies are among the largest taxpayers in Armenia
, creating jobs, transferring advanced technologies and strengthening the country’s energy security.
I would also note that you and I have discussed on a number of occasions
matters related to Armenia’s stated intention to join the EU
, both
by telephone
and
in person
.
☝️
You are well aware of Russia’s position:
Armenia’s aspiration to join the EU
is the natural and legitimate right of the country and its people
. Armenia is a sovereign state, and it is for the Armenian people to determine the path of their development.
At the same time, this undoubtedly creates difficulties for Armenia’s continued membership in
the Eurasian Economic Union
(
#EAEU
) and
risks nullifying the advantages
the Republic enjoys in the EAEU market and
disrupting established trade chains
.
📈
Meanwhile, over more than 10 years of participation in Eurasian integration processes,
Armenia’s GDP has nearly tripled: from $10.6 billion in 2015 to $29.2 billion in 2025
– at least according to our statistics. Exports to the EAEU countries
have increased tenfold
, and today the EAEU accounts for
around 36%
of Armenia’s foreign trade.
Therefore, of course,
the people of Armenia should clearly express their view
on the country’s future political and economic course
. At the same time, as we have discussed, any corresponding decisions should be approached strictly objectively and based on realities.
I remember that you publicly said you considered it necessary to hold a referendum on this issue
, and
I supported your position at the latest meeting of the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council
. I specifically mentioned that Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan had announced this publicly and urged our colleagues to support it as well.
👉
The sooner we determine the way forward, the better
, because we also need to understand how to develop our relations with Armenia, above all in the economic sphere.
Some regulatory matters
– agricultural products, various technical norms and standards, for example –
are fundamentally different in the EAEU and the EU
.
I hope that today, in a calm, friendly and businesslike atmosphere, we will discuss all these matters.
