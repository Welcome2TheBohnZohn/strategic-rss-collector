🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during a meeting with President of Kazakhstan Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
(Moscow, November 25, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am very pleased to see you.
We meet regularly
–
in Kazakhstan
,
in Russia
and
on the sidelines
of various international events. This time, you have agreed to come to Russia as
the guest of honour
at
the conference
on artificial intelligence organised by Sberbank.
I know that you devote considerable attention to
the development of information technology in Kazakhstan
. I am sure you will find the conference interesting. We will have an opportunity to hear from experts, interact with young people – schoolchildren and university students – and discuss cooperation between Russia and Kazakhstan in high-tech industries in general and in artificial intelligence in particular.
📈
Our bilateral trade is growing at a steady pace and is already approaching $30 billion
– just over $28 billion, I believe. Settlements are conducted in national currencies, and their share is already substantial –
over 90%.
The Intergovernmental Commission is working actively on both sides. Overall, everything is progressing
at a good pace
, and our cooperation is
at a good level
.
🤝
I believe our regular meetings also help create a favourable atmosphere and
give momentum to this entire process
, so we are very pleased to see you.
