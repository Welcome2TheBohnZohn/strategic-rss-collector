Published: 2026-09-09 12:15:54

🇷🇺
Russia's Federal Agency for the CIS, Compatriots Living Abroad, and International Humanitarian Cooperation
(
#Rossotrudnichestvo
):
On September 9, employees of
the Russian House in Berlin
returned to Russia with their families
. Following
the German authorities’ decision
to shut down the center, they were issued a harsh ultimatum – just seven days to leave the country.
💬
The first group was welcomed by Rossotrudnichestvo Head
Igor Chaika
:
“Our people are our top priority right now. We do not abandon our colleagues.
We will help them find new positions and do everything we can to ensure that their experience and expertise remain in demand”.
Rossotrudnichestvo is already working out further employment options for each staff member
– at its headquarters, other Russian Houses and new partner venues. Their future employment is under the personal supervision of the agency’s head.
Employees returning from Berlin were also welcomed with flowers and gifts in Nizhny Novgorod
. Another group is traveling by car across Europe to bring back at least some of the Russian House’s valuable property.
☝️
The Russian House
operated in Berlin for more than 40 years
. Over the past 15 years alone, it welcomed
more than 3 million visitors
.
The House may have been closed.
👉
But people, ties and decades of shared history cannot be erased by a single decision.
