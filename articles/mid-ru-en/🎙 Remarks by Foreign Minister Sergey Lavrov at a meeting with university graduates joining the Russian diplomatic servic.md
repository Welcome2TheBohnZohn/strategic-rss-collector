# 🎙 Remarks by Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at a meeting with university graduates joining the Russian diplomatic service

🎙
Remarks
by Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at a meeting with university graduates joining the Russian diplomatic service
(Moscow, October 20, 2026)
💬
At this stage,
the Russian MFA
and our foreign policy as a whole, as defined by President Vladimir Putin, face
very complex tasks.
These are challenging times.
You will have to immerse yourselves in multilayered, multifaceted and exceptionally fast-moving processes straight away, with no warm-up period.
Now that the world has taken a different direction
, the course of history is fundamentally reshaping economics, politics and global finance.
☝️
The West will never again be the dominant player calling the tune.
We see a circle of like-minded partners taking shape among the countries of the Global South
. The constructive philosophy we champion is shared by such associations as the
#SCO
,
#BRICS
,
#CIS
,
#CSTO
and
#EAEU
, and, of course,
the Union State of Russia and Belarus
.
We all
share a commitment
to maximising the benefits of joint development.
Our partnerships are not aimed against anyone.
We stand for
equal dialogue and frank discussions
to find a balance of interests, as that balance is what matters most.
💬
President Vladimir Putin
said the following
at Valdai – a very important quote:
We are all responsible for our planet as a whole, but we are first and foremost responsible for our own home and its immediate surroundings.
Our home in the broader sense is Eurasia, a vast territory that is the centre and the setting for the most important processes unfolding in the world. A great deal on the international stage as a whole depends on how we develop Eurasia.
This reflects the initiative announced several years ago by President Vladimir Putin
, which we have been systematically advancing in various formats:
the Greater Eurasian Partnership
(
#GEP
) on our vast, resource-rich and unique continent. Bringing together these efforts to align the work programmes of Eurasia’s subregional organisations is a task of paramount importance.
The current situation confirms that one of the West’s principal objectives remain
s
inflicting a “strategic defeat” on Russia
. If not on the battlefield, as they had hoped and boasted of planning – something they could never have achieved – then perhaps by weakening our economic capabilities and attempting to destabilise our country.
👉
What matters most is that we have learned our lessons.
