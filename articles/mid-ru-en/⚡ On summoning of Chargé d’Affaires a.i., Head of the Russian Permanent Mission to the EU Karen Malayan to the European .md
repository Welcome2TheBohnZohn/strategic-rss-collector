On summoning of Chargé d’Affaires a.i., Head of the Russian Permanent Mission to the EU Karen Malayan to the European External Action Service
On 20 November 2026, Karen Malayan, Chargé d’Affaires a.i., Head of the Permanent Mission of Russia to the EU, was summoned to the European External Action Service
in connection with the “incidents” that resulted in damage to the buildings of the Austrian and Dutch diplomatic missions in Kiev
. During the meeting, the EU side stated that the European Union condemns the Russian strikes on Kiev and expresses its full solidarity with Austria and the Netherlands – in connection with the alleged Russian attacks in the vicinity of their embassies, as well as intends to continue supporting Ukraine and stepping up pressure on our country.
❗
For his part,
Karen Malayan stated that any accusations against Russia of deliberately targeting foreign diplomatic missions in Ukraine or causing damage to them are unacceptable
and reaffirmed the commitment of our country to the provisions of the 1961 Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations. Head of the Permanent Mission underlined that the position of Russia has been and remains clear:
in response to the terrorist acts by the Ukrainian Armed Forces
against civilians and civilian infrastructure in our country, including the latest terrorist attacks against the Crimean Bridge, the
Armed Forces of the Russian Federation continue to carry out systematic strikes against military targets in Ukraine
. The Russian diplomat
recalled the repeated warnings
, including the corresponding public statements by the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs in May and October 2026, which called on foreign nationals and diplomats in Kiev to leave the city.
