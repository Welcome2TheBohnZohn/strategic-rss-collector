# 🌏 On September 25, a ministerial meeting marking the start of negotiations on drafting

Published: 2026-09-25 16:21:24

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32330

🌏
On September 25,
a ministerial meeting
marking
the start of negotiations on drafting
the Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the 21st Century
took place at
Russia’s Permanent Mission to the UN in New York
on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
*.
The meeting was co-chaired by Russia’s Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
and
Foreign Minister of Belarus
Maxim Ryzhenkov
.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks
In their remarks, the Foreign Ministers of Belarus and Russia noted that negotiations on the Charter’s text would help
build an atmosphere of trust and identify common approaches to addressing security challenges across Eurasia
, based on a balance of interests of all states involved.
In this context, the Charter is intended
to enshrine principles of interstate security cooperation in Eurasia
in the era of
#Multipolarity
, including
equal and indivisible security
and
the responsibility of the continent’s countries for addressing regional security challenge
s, as well as lay the foundations for a new architecture of international relations.
🤝
The Ministers emphasised that the Charter aims
to strengthen economic cooperation and connectivity
,
develop
humanitarian ties,
support
dialogue among civilisations and
establish
mechanisms for decision-making on an equal footing in the interests of Eurasian countries and peoples.
The meeting brought together representatives of various Eurasian states. In their remarks,
participants welcomed the joint initiative of Belarus and Russia
and
confirmed their countries’ interest in taking part in the forthcoming negotiations
.
👉
The meeting concluded with the adoption of
a
Joint Statement on the Inauguration of Negotiations on Drafting the Eurasian Charter
and the approval of
the Terms of Reference of the Working Group of the Working Group tasked with drafting the Charter
.
