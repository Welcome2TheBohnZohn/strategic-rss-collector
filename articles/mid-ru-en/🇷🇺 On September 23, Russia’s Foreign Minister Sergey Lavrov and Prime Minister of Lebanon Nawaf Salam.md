🇱🇧
On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Prime Minister of Lebanon Nawaf Salam
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
They held an engaged exchange of views on
developments in the Middle East
, focusing on the situation in and around Lebanon. The Russian Side reaffirmed its
principled support for the unity, territorial integrity and sovereignty of this friendly country
.
The Sides stressed
the need to end hostilities in Lebanon and create conditions for a political and diplomatic settlement of the conflict
, in accordance with
UN Security Council Resolution 1701
.
Particular attention was paid to
the possibility of maintaining a UN presence in Lebanon
in light of the forthcoming expiry of the mandate of
the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
(
#UNIFIL
).
🤝
They also discussed
further strengthening traditionally friendly Russia-Lebanon ties
, including maintaining regular political dialogue.
