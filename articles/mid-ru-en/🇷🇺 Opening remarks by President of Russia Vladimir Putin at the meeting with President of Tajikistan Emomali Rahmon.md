🇹🇯
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the meeting with President of Tajikistan Emomali Rahmon
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We maintain
regular contact
and continue to uphold the high level and status of our relations –
truly at the level of strategic partnership and alliance.
📈
Our economic ties continue to grow.
Trade
increased by 25.1%
in 2025 and
by another 25%
in the first half of this year. This is a very strong pace and something we are certainly pleased to see.
There are
320 enterprises with Russian participation
operating in Tajikistan, and
more than 96%
of all transactions are conducted in national currencies.
The Intergovernmental Commission is working actively
. We are also implementing
the 2024-2027 Economic Cooperation Programme
and are moving at a very good pace under
the Joint Action Plan to increase bilateral trade 2.5-fold by 2030
.
I would also note our close and constructive cooperation in the humanitarian sphere
.
I remember how we opened five Russian-language schools in several Tajik cities
. That was your initiative, and I remember it very well. We are grateful to you for it.
Some 6,000 children
are already studying at these schools and, as I understand it, are doing so with great enthusiasm
.
The Russian-Tajik Slavonic University in Dushanbe
is operating successfully.
More than 35,000 Tajik students
are studying at Russian universities
.
In 2025,
Days of Tajik Culture
were held with great success in Moscow and Kazan, and in May 2026,
Days of Russian Culture
took place in Tajikistan.
Naturally, we also work closely in the security sphere
. We are aware of your concerns regarding developments in neighboring Afghanistan, and we remain in close contact on these and other security-related issues.
🤝
I am very glad that we have the opportunity to meet and talk today.
