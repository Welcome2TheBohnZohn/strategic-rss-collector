# 🗓 On October 31, the meeting of the #G20 Foreign Ministers under the US chairmanship concluded in Atlanta, Georgia, USA

Published: 2026-11-02 16:01:23

🗓
On October 31,
the meeting
of the
#G20
Foreign Ministers under the US chairmanship concluded in Atlanta, Georgia, USA
.
The Russian delegation was helmed by Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
.
🎙
Sergey Lavrov's presser following the G20 Ministerial
During the meeting, the Parties held
a substantive discussion on a wide range of current international issues
, focusing on the interplay between economic challenges and geopolitical instability.
Sergey Lavrov outlined Russia’s views on the factors fuelling global tensions
, with particular emphasis on energy and food security and the smooth operation of supply chains and shipping routes on an open, non-discriminatory basis.
He stressed the importance of
strengthening
a
#MultipolarWorld
order,
reforming
global economic governance, and
countering
unfair competition and neo-colonial practices.
The participants offered
detailed assessments of destructive actions that undermine international trade
, including the interception of merchant vessels, sanctions, asset seizures and other unscrupulous methods.
☝️
Russia reaffirmed its commitment to remaining
a reliable trading partner
and
a leading supplier of energy, food and fertilisers to countries in need
.
The discussion also highlighted
Russia’s considerable transport and logistics potentia
l, including opportunities to expand the capacity of
the Northern Sea Route
.
👉
The parties underscored the unifying nature of
President Vladimir Putin’s initiative to establish a Greater Eurasian Partnership
and of efforts to build
a common security architecture in Eurasia
.
***
As is customary, Sergey Lavrov held a series of contacts on the sidelines of the event with his counterparts, including US Secretary of State
Marco Rubio
, India’s External Affairs Minister
Subrahmanyam Jaishankar
, Saudi Arabia’s Foreign Minister
Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud
, Indonesia’s Foreign Minister
Arrmanatha Nasir
and Japan’s Foreign Minister
Toshimitsu Motegi
, among others.
