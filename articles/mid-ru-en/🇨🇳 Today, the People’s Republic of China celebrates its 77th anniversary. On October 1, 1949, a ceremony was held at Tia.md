Today, the People’s Republic of China celebrates its 77th anniversary.
On October 1, 1949, a ceremony was held at Tiananmen Square in Beijing to proclaim the establishment of the People’s Republic of China. This
historic event
ushered in a new stage
in China’s development after decades of turmoil and warfare, including fragmentation, internal conflicts, World War II, as well as Axis Japan crimes.
Just one day after the PRC was founded, the Soviet Union made a historic move by becoming the
first country to recognize New China
, establishing formal
diplomatic relations
on October 2, 1949.
Despite the difficult conditions of postwar recovery, our people extended
large-scale fraternal assistance to China
, sending numerous specialists to support the state-building of the PRC and the development of its economy.
During the 1950s and 1960s,
more than 11'000 Soviet specialists
(excluding military advisers) were sent to China, while roughly the same number of Chinese students and trainees from the PRC received education and training in the Soviet Union. Among them was
Jiang Zemin
, the former President of the PRC, who worked at the ZiL automobile plant in Moscow in 1955.
