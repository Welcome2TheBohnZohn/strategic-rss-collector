Remarks and answers to media questions
by Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov following the CIS Council of Foreign Ministers
(Turkmenbashi, October 7, 2026)
💬
Today, we began with
bilateral talks with my colleague
, Deputy Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers and Foreign Minister of Turkmenistan
Rashid Meredov
. We focused primarily on
deepening our strategic partnership
, which we consider particularly important, in line with
our Leaders’ directives
.
🇷🇺
🇹🇲
Next year, we will mark the 35th anniversary of
diplomatic relations
, so today we agreed to draw up a programme of events, including exhibitions, the publication of archival documents and cultural events. We will report to
Vladimir Putin
and
Serdar Berdimuhamedov
on matters within our remit that require decisions by our Presidents.
We discussed preparations for
the
#CIS
Council of Heads of State meeting
, which will open tomorrow evening with a formal reception, followed by the main proceedings on Friday.
An environmental Summit of the five Caspian states
will take place later that day, after the CIS Summit.
🤝
Rashid Meredov and I praised our cooperation across all areas
. We attach particular importance to expanding economic, investment and trade ties.
The Intergovernmental Russian-Turkmen Commission on Economic Cooperation
is due to hold its next meeting before the end of 2026.
We share the view that historical memory must be preserved
, especially at a time when Europe is clearly taking a lax approach to the revival of Nazism in a number of countries, with the acquiescence of their ruling circles. We approved plans to erect
a Friendship monument in Ashgabat
in gratitude to the city’s residents who welcomed
people evacuated from besieged Leningrad
during the Great Patriotic War.
We discussed the international agenda, including cooperation within the CIS and in the Caspian region
. We proposed developing ties between Turkmenistan and the Eurasian Economic Union. We also invited Turkmenistan to take an active part in work on
the Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the 21st Century
.
