# 🗓 On September 20, Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister Evgeny Ivanov, Chair of the Russian MFA Commission for Organising Elections Abroad,

Published: 2026-09-20 22:00:45

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32148

🗓
On September 20, Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister Evgeny Ivanov, Chair of the Russian MFA Commission for Organising Elections Abroad,
took part in a session
at Russia’s Central Election Commission Information Centre on organising voting for Russian citizens abroad.
Key points
:
• Organising elections to
the 9th State Duma of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation
for Russian citizens abroad has been
a top priority for Russian diplomatic and consular missions
.
• As President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
noted
, these elections will demonstrate
the resilience
of the state,
the maturity
of its political system and its
ability to withstand any external threats, blackmail or aggression
. They will express the collective will of millions of people and their determination to safeguard Russia’s sovereignty and security.
• Once again, this election campaign is taking place during
the special military operation
. For our diplomatic service, it poses a serious challenge and a test, against the backdrop of
the Western “hybrid war” against our country
.
• Despite all the obstacles,
the Russian MFA
has made every effort
to ensure that our citizens abroad can fully exercise their right to vote
. A total of
326 polling stations
(288 back in 2024) have been established at Russian embassies and consulates general in
152 countries
.
•
Representatives of Russian and foreign media are present at many polling stations
. Observers are monitoring compliance with electoral legislation.
More than 120 observers
from
the Civic Chamber of Russia
,
the CIS Interparliamentary Assembly
and
the CSTO Parliamentary Assembly
are present
at 48 polling stations abroad
.
• In coordination with
Russia's Central Election Commission
,
121 early voting visits
were organised to communities with sizeable Russian populations.
More than 25,000 people took part.
•
In seven countries
(
Bahrain
,
Israel
,
Iraq
,
Yemen
,
China
,
Kuwait
and
Libya
),
all voting took place ahead of the main election day
to accommodate local work schedules and religious calendars.
•
Voting has already ended in 38 countries
, and the count is underway.
As of 7:00 pm today
, including early voting and voting over the three-day period (
Cyprus
,
Thailand
,
South Ossetia
),
more than 212,000 people had cast their ballots
(191,000 people back in 2021).
•
This shows that
our citizens remain undeterred
by attempts to obstruct our work, the hybrid war waged against Russia or the toxic coverage in Western media.
•
Russian citizens are politically engaged
,
they
understand the importance of participating in elections
under the current circumstances, and trust Russia’s electoral system.
🗳
Voting is still underway
. With the time difference, the last polling stations abroad will close
at 5:00 am Moscow time
.
