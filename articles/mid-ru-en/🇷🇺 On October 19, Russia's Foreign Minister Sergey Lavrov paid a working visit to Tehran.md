🇮🇷
On October 19, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
paid a working visit to Tehran
.
He
was received
by President of the Islamic Republic of Iran
Masoud Pezeshkian
,
held extensive talks
with Foreign Minister of Iran
Abbas Araghchi
and
held a meeting
with Secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council
Mohsen Rezaei
.
🎙
Sergey Lavrov’s remarks and answers to media questions following talks with Abbas Araghchi
In their traditionally constructive spirit,
the Sides reviewed the current bilateral agenda in detail
, focusing on strengthening
the comprehensive strategic partnership
in light of the agreements reached at the meetings between the Presidents of Russia and Iran
in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on September 1
, and
in Avaza, Turkmenistan, on October 8.
The Sides noted with satisfaction
the intensive political dialogue and close cooperation
between governments, sectoral agencies, parliaments, regions and businesses. Attention was paid to expanding trade, economic and investment cooperation, including the implementation of joint transport, logistics and energy projects.
The importance of close coordination
on the situation in the Middle East and the Persian Gulf, as well as in the South Caucasus and the Caspian region,
was emphasised
.
Sergey Lavrov reaffirmed Russia’s readiness to help resolve the situation surrounding Iran
and, in this context, briefed his counterparts on
his contacts with representatives of the Arabian Peninsula
countries in Manama, Bahrain, the previous day. The Sides expressed
mutual interest in stepping up joint efforts in multilateral forums
– the
#UN
,
#BRICS
, the
#SCO
and the
#EAEU
.
🤝
The visit
continued
the substantive political dialogue
and
helped identify practical steps to further develop bilateral relations and foreign policy cooperation
between Russia and Iran.
