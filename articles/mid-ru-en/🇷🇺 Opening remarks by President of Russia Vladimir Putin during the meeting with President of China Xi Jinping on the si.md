# 🇷🇺 Opening remarks by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with President of China Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the SCO Summit

Published: 2026-08-31 15:06:02

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31721

🇷🇺
🇨🇳
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during
the meeting
with President of China Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the SCO Summit
(Bishkek, August 31, 2026)
.
💬
President Putin:
I am sincerely glad
to meet with you again
– this time in Kyrgyzstan.
I would like to begin with
the tragic events
that recently occurred in China’s Tibet Autonomous Region, near the border with Nepal – a powerful mudslide struck the area, resulting in loss of life.
▪️
Please convey
our deepest condolences
to the families and loved ones of those who lost their lives, as well as
our wishes for a speedy recovery
to all those injured.
***
We are meeting just a few hours before the start of
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Summit
.
☝️
The SCO was established 25 years ago at the initiative of China and Russia.
I am very pleased to note that our initiative has developed so successfully, acquired a truly broad international character and grown into a full-fledged international organisation.
This year we also marked
the 25th anniversary of the fundamental
Treaty of Good-Neighbourliness and Friendly Cooperation
, signed by Comrade
Jiang Zemin
, who would have turned 100 on August 17. We remember his contribution to the development of Russia-China relations.
Today, amid a complex geopolitical environment,
the comprehensive partnership and strategic interaction between Russia and China
serve as a model of interstate relations.
🤝
They are founded on strong traditions of friendship, respect for sovereignty, equality and mutual benefit.
Cooperation is developing successfully across all areas, including the economy
. We are implementing major projects in energy, industry, space, transport and logistics. The first regular voyage by a Chinese container ship along
the Northern Sea Route
took place in mid-August.
Mutual agricultural trade
is growing rapidly – up 34%
.
Digital innovation
, including artificial intelligence technologies,
is opening up new opportunities
for the economic development of our countries
. Together with our Chinese friends, we established
the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization
in 2026. This was your initiative, which we were pleased to support, and we are committed to working together in this field.
Business and humanitarian contacts are undoubtedly facilitated by
the 30-day visa-free regime
. You announced this initiative a year ago, on September 2, during
our meeting in Beijing
.
We supported it, and the arrangement is now in effect through the end of 2027.
The results were immediate:
tourist flows increased by 43% in the first half of 2026
. We are ready to work together on
making the visa-free regime permanent
, should the Chinese Side consider this possible and beneficial.
🌐
As for current foreign policy issues,
our fundamental approaches are close or fully aligned
. In addition to the
#SCO
, we actively cooperate within the
#UN
,
#BRICS
,
#G20
and ASEAN-centred formats, and, of course, we will continue supporting
China’s chairmanship of #APEC.
