Question: The Kiev regime has stepped up the attacks on the Zaporozhskaya Nuclear Power Plant (ZNPP) and the satellite town of Energodar, which fact has significantly destabilised the operation of the ZNPP. How would you comment on these attacks?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
The Kiev authorities completely lost their minds and
intensified targeted attacks on the
#ZNPP
and the satellite town of Energodar
, which is home to the plant’s personnel, who continue to perform their duties heroically under extremely challenging conditions, as do their families.
Earlier this week,
the Ukrainian forces targeted exclusively civilian sites,
namely, an art school and the Russian Emergencies Ministry’s fire and rescue unit. Clearly, unable to strike the ZNPP directly, the Kiev regime continues its futile attempts to destabilise the plant’s operations by
intimidating its personnel.
Such criminal actions clearly show that the sole true objective of Zelensky and his junta is
to multiply the number of casualties
and turn the Zaporozhye Region into a barren land of radioactive contamination.
Having failed the test of their own competence, the former actors continue to labour under the delusion that any criticism, or even condemnation, amounts to publicity that can be monetised.
It is time for the Western patrons and sponsors of this deranged clique, to whom they have effectively given carte blanche to get away with any crimes, to realise that they are dumping their money not merely in a failed anti-Russia project, but also in
creating direct radiological threats in the heart of Europe,
including to their own citizens.
We are compelled to note that international bodies, above all the
#IAEA
,
have reacted with considerable restraint to Kiev’s provocative antics
and have yet to muster the courage to identify Ukraine directly as the culprit. Apparently, they fail to appreciate that such spineless behaviour could lead to catastrophe.
❗️
For our part,
we consistently bring detailed information about Ukraine’s outrages to the attention of the international public
, including at relevant international forums, and spare no effort to protect the ZNPP, its personnel, and the people of Energodar from Kiev’s attacks.
