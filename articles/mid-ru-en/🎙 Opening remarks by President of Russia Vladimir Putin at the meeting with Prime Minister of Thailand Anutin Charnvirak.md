# 🎙 Opening remarks by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the meeting with Prime Minister of Thailand Anutin Charnvirakul on the sidelines

Published: 2026-11-18 18:01:06

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33455

🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the meeting with Prime Minister of Thailand Anutin Charnvirakul on the sidelines
of
the 33rd APEC Summit
(Shenzhen, November 18, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am pleased to see you
again
. We last met at
the Russia-ASEAN Summit in Kazan
in June.
🇷🇺
🇹🇭
Thailand is a longstanding partner of Russia.
Next year marks
the 130th anniversary
of the establishment of
diplomatic relations
between our countries. We are committed to
further developing our wide-ranging bilateral ties
.
Our governments, parliaments and foreign ministries maintain
active contacts
.
Bilateral trade is growing
. In 2025, trade rose by
2.3%
.
In
January-August 2026
,
it grew by
37.4%
compared with the same period last year.
This is a good result.
There is considerable scope for mutually beneficial cooperation
in energy, transport, logistics, agribusiness and investment.
The Joint Commission on Bilateral Cooperation
is tasked with advancing work in these areas.
Our cultural and people-to-people ties are also growing
. Thailand successfully hosted
a series of events
this year as part of
the Russian Seasons festival
.
Education has traditionally been an important part of our cooperation
. Thailand has been allocated
50 government scholarships
for the 2026–2027 academic year, and around
109 Thai students
are currently studying in Russia.
🏝
Thailand is a very popular destination for Russian tourists
. Nearly
2 million Russians
visited Thailand as tourists in 2025. In the first nine months of 2026,
1.3 million
did so.
Our representatives coordinate their efforts in various international and regional forums
. We cooperate constructively within
#APEC
and through the
#RussiaASEAN
Dialogue Partnership.
Mr Prime Minister,
we welcome Thailand’s participation in
#BRICS
as a partner country since 2025.
🤝
Our relations are developing across a wide range of areas.
I look forward to discussing with you and your colleagues how we can build on what we have achieved so far and what steps we will take together in the near future.
#RussiaThailand
