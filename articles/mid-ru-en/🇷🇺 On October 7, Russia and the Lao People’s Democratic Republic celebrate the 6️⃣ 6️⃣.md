6️⃣
6️⃣

Bilateral ties between Moscow and Vientiane are rooted in
long-standing traditions of friendship and cooperation
and are characterised by a high level of trust.
Our country provided significant assistance to the Lao people
in their struggle for national liberation, as well as in building national statehood, developing the economy and strengthening the country’s defence capabilities.
***
🤝
#Laos
is a reliable partner of Russia in the
#AsiaPacific
.
The foundation of our bilateral relations is the Treaty on the Principles of Friendly Relations, signed on March 9, 1994. In October 2011,
Russia and Laos adopted the
Declaration on Strategic Partnership in the Asia-Pacific Region.
Russia-Lao ties are multifaceted.
Political dialogue at the highest and high levels is maintained on a regular basis.
Inter-parliamentary ties are developing
actively, while exchanges of delegations between relevant ministries and agencies are expanding.
📈
Trade and economic ties are steadily growing.
Last year, bilateral trade doubled compared to 2024. The Inter-governmental Commission operates on a regular basis.
One of the priority areas of bilateral cooperation is the development of
military and military-technical cooperation.
Interaction in the fields of security and law enforcement is also gaining momentum.
Cultural
and
humanitarian
exchanges
are being expanded, while joint initiatives in education are being promoted, including
support for the Russian language in Laos.
More than 10'000 Lao nationals have received education at Soviet and Russian universities. Many graduates have been promoted to senior positions in the country’s Party and state apparatus, the Command Staff of the Lao People’s Army and law enforcement agencies.
