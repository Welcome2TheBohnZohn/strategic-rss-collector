H.E. Jessica Alupo, Vice President of the Republic of Uganda, has arrived in Moscow to represent H.E. Yoweri Kaguta Museveni, President of the Republic of Uganda, at the Third Summit and Economic and Humanitarian Forum “Russia–Africa”, taking place on 28–29 October 2026.
Upon arrival at Domodedovo International Airport, H.E. the Vice President was received by Amb. Moses K. Kizige, Head of Mission of the Republic of Uganda to the Russian Federation and Hon. Anatoly Gennadyevich Bashkin, Director of the African Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation.
The Embassy warmly welcomes H.E. the Vice President to Moscow and looks forward to supporting Uganda’s participation in this important engagement.
