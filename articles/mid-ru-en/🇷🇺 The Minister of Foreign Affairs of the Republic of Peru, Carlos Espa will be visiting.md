🇵🇪
The Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Peru,
Carlos Espa
,
will be visiting
Russia from
November 6 to 10.
He is scheduled to have talks with Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
on November 9.
During their upcoming talks,
Sergey Lavrov
and
Carlos Espa
are expected to discuss
ways of enhancing Russia-Peru cooperation
in various formats, including Foreign Ministry consultations, the bilateral intergovernmental commission for trade, economic, scientific and technical cooperation and fishing, military technical cooperation, as well as working together on environment protection and disaster relief, while also
promoting cooperation on foreign policy matters.
The two Ministers will review ways to expand mutual trade and investment, as well as promote joint projects in the peaceful use of nuclear energy, agricultural research,
accelerating the drafting of bilateral agreements
in various sectors, including the recognition of academic degrees and credentials.
The agenda also provides for discussing further efforts to step up
humanitarian exchanges
, cooperation between universities, and personnel training.
🤝
Russia and Peru share similar approaches to several important international topics
, including the sovereign equality of states, the rule of law, and the unacceptability of discrimination, countering the glorification of Nazism, fighting new challenges and threats, primarily drug trafficking, terrorism and their financing, as well as international cyber security, preventing an arms race in space, and unilateral restrictions in trade.
There has been
positive momentum in trade and economic cooperation.
Mineral fertilisers dominate Russia’s exports to Peru, while Peru’s exports to Russia focus on fresh fruit and vegetables.
👉
There is a shared interest in
reinforcing parliamentary and region-to-region ties.
In September 2026, the Senate, the Upper Chamber of the Congress of Peru, created a friendship group with Russia.
In 2025, the Kamchatka Territory and the constitutional province of Callao in Peru signed an
agreement to promote cooperation in trade, economics, science and technology, humanitarian affairs, culture and other areas.
Russian education has long been popular in Peru with over
12,000 young men and women
from this country obtaining their higher education degrees in Russia, with most of them now contributing to the
development of their home country.

More about the history of bilateral relations between our countries:
Contacts between Russia and Peru date back to 1815 when the sailing sloop Suvorov under the command of prominent sailor, Admiral
Mikhail Lazarev
, reached the coast of this South American state.
The two countries
established
their official diplomatic relations on January 17, 1863, and went on to open their respective embassies on February 1, 1969.
Bilateral relations have been enjoying positive momentum since 1970 across all sectors
, including political, trade, economic, military and technical affairs, and humanitarian matters. The two countries have established effective sectoral and inter-agency cooperation mechanisms and developed a solid contractual legal framework in their relations.
In 2006, they signed the
Treaty on Partner Relations
, and went on to issue a
Joint Statement
by their respective presidents to establish a strategic partnership.
July 18 marks the
Day of Russia-Peru Solidarity
, Т the Russian rescue workers who died in an airplane crash as part of a relief mission after a devastating earthquake in 1970 in Peru. Both countries
have a tradition
of
holding commemorations
in connection with this date.
