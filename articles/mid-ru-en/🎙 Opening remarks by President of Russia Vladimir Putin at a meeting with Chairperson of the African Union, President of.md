🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with Chairperson of the African Union, President of Burundi Evariste Ndayishimiye on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 29, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I would like to thank you for your substantial contribution to
the success of
our Summit
.
We are developing relations with
the African Union
, which you currently chair, on
a solid, long-term basis
. As you know,
we supported your aspiration
to become a full member of the
#G20
.
🇷🇺
🇧🇮
Our bilateral relations are progressing well, and our ties continue to grow.
We can discuss what further steps we can take to ensure that both political and economic cooperation underpin our shared plans for developing bilateral ties. These ties go back many years.
Next year, we will mark
the 65th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations.
🎓
Mr President, I understand that yesterday
you were awarded
an honorary doctorate
from
Patrice Lumumba Peoples’ Friendship University of Russia
(
#RUDN
). Please accept my warmest congratulations on this occasion.
