# ⚡️ In connection with Ukrainian statements aimed at creating direct threats to civil aviation

Published: 2026-09-04 14:10:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31842

⚡️
In connection with Ukrainian statements aimed at creating
direct threats to civil aviation
, the competent Russian authorities are
in contact
with the leadership of
the International Civil Aviation Organization
(
#ICAO
) in Montreal.
An
urgent appeal
on the
matter has been sent
to ICAO Secretary General
Juan Carlos Salazar
.
❗️
The Russian Federation has issued a strong protest over Ukraine’s reckless terrorist actions, which run counter to international law.
The NOTAMs issued by the Ukrainian authorities alleging that Russian airspace is unsafe have been
categorically rejected
. They
have no legal force
,
distort the actual situation
and
mislead users of Russian airspace
.
We called on the ICAO Secretary General to condemn
the Kiev regime’s unlawful interference in civil aviation and the direct threats it is creating, and
to take targeted steps
to put an end to these actions by Ukraine.
☝️
The Russian Federation remains firmly committed to ensuring the safety of civil aviation.
