On September 22, President of Russia Vladimir Putin and President of Turkmenistan Serdar Berdimuhamedov
had a telephone conversation
.
During the call, Vladimir Putin extended
birthday greetings
to Serdar Berdimuhamedov.
🤝
The Leaders expressed satisfaction with
the high level of Russia-Turkmenistan strategic partnership relations
and reaffirmed their
shared commitment to further developing these ties across all areas
.
They also discussed preparations for the upcoming meeting of
the Council of Heads of State of the Commonwealth of Independent States
,
to be chaired by the President of Turkmenistan in early October
.
Serdar Berdimuhamedov, in turn,
congratulated Vladimir Putin
on
the successful conduct of elections
to
the State Duma of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation
.
