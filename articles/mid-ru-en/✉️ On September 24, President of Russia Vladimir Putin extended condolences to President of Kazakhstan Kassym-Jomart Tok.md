# ✉️ On September 24, President of Russia Vladimir Putin extended condolences to President of Kazakhstan Kassym-Jomart Tokayev.

Published: 2026-09-24 19:32:23

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32281

✉️
On September 24, President of Russia Vladimir Putin
extended condolences
to President of Kazakhstan Kassym-Jomart Tokayev.
✍️
Please accept my deep condolences over
the tragic incident during Kazakhstan’s naval exercises
in the Caspian Sea.
I ask you to convey my sincere sympathy and support to the families of the servicemen who lost their lives. We in Russia share their pain and grief.
Respectfully,
VLADIMIR PUTIN
