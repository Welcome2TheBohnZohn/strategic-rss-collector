On November 21, President of Russia Vladimir Putin
sent greetings
to the participants, organisers and guests of
the International Conference on Public Health in Africa
.
✍️
I welcome you on the occasion of
the international conference
being held in Addis Ababa, dedicated to the most important issues related to
ensuring the genuine sovereignty of African countries in the field of public health
.
These issues include
the development of healthcare services and sanitation infrastructure, the training of qualified personnel and the introduction of modern technologies.
🇷🇺
The Russian Federation has traditionally provided active assistance to its African partners
in protecting public health, and the outcomes of
the recently held Third Russia–Africa Summit
have created opportunities for further expanding our cooperation in this area.
Particularly close cooperation has been established
in combating epidemic diseases
under the relevant comprehensive cooperation programme, which has been extended until 2029.
Joint scientific research activities and practical exercises
are conducted on a regular basis.
Teams of Russian specialists are dispatched to Africa
to combat outbreaks of dangerous infectious diseases
, while mobile laboratories, large consignments of medicines, diagnostic equipment and preventive supplies are also provided.
Around 10,000 students from almost every African country
are currently studying medicine at Russian universities. Many of them undertake internships at our leading clinics and are regularly involved in research activities.
🤝
I am confident that the conference participants – representatives of government agencies and international organisations, medical professionals, scientists and experts – will engage in substantive discussions, exchange experience and
identify new forms and areas of constructive cooperation
aimed at protecting the health of the people of the African continent.
I sincerely wish you every success and all the best.
VLADIMIR PUTIN
