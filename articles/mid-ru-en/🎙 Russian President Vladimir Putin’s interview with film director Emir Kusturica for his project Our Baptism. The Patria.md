# 🎙 Russian President Vladimir Putin’s interview with film director Emir Kusturica for his project Our Baptism. The Patriarch

Published: 2026-11-20 20:45:28

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33508

🎙
Russian President Vladimir Putin’s
interview
with film director Emir Kusturica for his project Our Baptism. The Patriarch
(November 20, 2026)
❓
Emir Kusturica: You have repeatedly said that Russians and Ukrainians are one people dragged into a fratricidal war by their enemies. What message would you like to convey to Ukrainians? Why do you think that Russia’s victory is in their interests?
💬
Vladimir Putin:
You have come upon
a very delicate issue
. I do not know whether I should answer your question or not.
There is a well-known Latin phrase,
“divide and conquer“
. What does it mean? It is a principle of the Senate in ancient Rome. And it is very simple: if anything large, powerful, effective, and influential emerges and grows, it is seen as a threat to Rome:
then it must be divided and ruled, to serve Rome’s interests
.
The history of our country abounds in such examples.
As soon as Russia grew stronger, there immediately appeared forces that wished to divide and conquer
.
By the way,
all such invasions against Russia in modern history are
, in my view,
also connected with this
:
Napoleon’s invasion
,
World War I
,
World War II
,
our Great Patriotic War
,
the Nazi invasion
. I believe that ideological considerations played an absolutely secondary or tertiary role there. The most important factors were issues of geopolitical interest and the
“divide and conquer“
mentality. <...>
Back in Soviet times, according to
the census conducted in 1986
, if I’m not mistaken,
51 or 52 million people lived in the territory of today’s Ukraine
, making it one of the largest industrially developed regions of Europe.
And what happened after the collapse of the Soviet Union and Ukraine gaining independence?
It lost everything
; shipbuilding, aircraft and spacecraft manufacturing were gone. There was also
a decline in population
, and in a couple of decades, the population was first estimated
at 41 million people
, then
it reduced to 31 millio
n, and now, they say, there are
only about 20 million people left in the territory of Ukraine
.
👉
Why?
Because power in Ukraine was seized not by its patriots, but
by nationalists
. The difference between patriotism and nationalism, as you know, is that
patriots love their country
, while
nationalists hate other peoples
. That’s the difference. But you won’t get far with such hatred toward other peoples.
And that is why,
if we are talking about Ukrainians
– and I’d like them to hear this – no matter what is happening now, no matter what tragedies are unfolding today,
we should think about the future
, about the future of our children, and we should understand that
if we are to be strong, powerful and well-positioned for positive development, we certainly need to stand united
, that is,
we should join our efforts to achieve our common goals
.
☝️
As for
Ukrainian soldiers
, I have already mentioned, I do not want to say that they should surrender, for it sounds somewhat humiliating. After all, they are fighting, they keep on fighting.
I would like to tell them to come over to our side, because it is our shared side ‒ and the right side of history.
