# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's statement regarding Russia’s alleged plans to launch a spacecraft carrying a nuclear warhead

Published: 2026-11-27 12:29:57

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
statement
regarding Russia’s alleged plans to launch a spacecraft carrying a nuclear warhead
(November 26, 2026)
💬
The Western political and expert circles continue to repeat
allegations about nuclear weapons in space
.
The latest allegations
have been published
by the German media in the form of
a “journalistic investigation”
regarding
Russia’s alleged plans to orbit a spacecraft with a nuclear warhead
in the near future for a potential mass attack on orbital satellite constellations.
The publication of this false information during a regular session of
the Open-ended Working Group on the Prevention of an Arms Race in Outer Space in all its Aspects
(OEWG on PAROS) in Geneva, established in accordance with
UNGA Decision 79/512
, is obviously
interfering with a depoliticised substantive dialogue on a platform established to address space security issues
.
☝️
But this does not bother German Russophobes.
We reiterate that these absurd allegations regarding Russia’s activities in outer space
have nothing in common with reality
and are aimed at
justifying Germany’s destructive moves to orbit weapons in space
, as well as the allocation of multibillion-euro funding for this purpose.
This concerns, in particular,
the development of the Precision Orbital Deep Strike system
by German concerns for potential deployment in low-Earth orbit, as well as
plans
announced by German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius to invest 35 billion euros
in measures to strengthen German military space capabilities by the mid-2030s.
❗️
We have pointed out on numerous occasions that the Russian Federation is conducting space exploration and development activities
in full compliance with its international obligations, exclusively for peaceful purposes, and do not threaten other states
.
