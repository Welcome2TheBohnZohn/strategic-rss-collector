Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of the
#DRC
Felix-Antoine Tshisekedi Tshilombo on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin
: Thank you for the attention you give to
the Russia
–
Africa Summit
as
an effective means of developing
#RussiaAfrica
relations
.
As for bilateral relations, last year marked a significant milestone –
65 years of diplomatic relations
between Russia and the
Democratic Republic of the Congo.
We value the level of cooperation we have achieved
. As an honorary doctor of
the Peoples’ Friendship University of Russia
, you can see that we
remember and hold in high esteem your country’s first Prime Minister, Patrice Lumumba
,
a true champion of freedom and independence for the peoples of Africa
. His name is indeed well known in Russia.
🇷🇺
🇨🇩
The DRC is one of Russia’s promising and reliable partners on the continent
. Russian-Congolese trade ties are strengthening, and bilateral trade is growing.
We expect the newly established
Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation
to take our cooperation to a new, higher level.
Humanitarian ties are developing
. Given the strong demand among Congolese students for education in Russia, t
he scholarship quota
for citizens of your country stands at
100 places
.
More broadly,
we seek to strengthen friendly ties with African countries
, including the DRC, and are open to cooperation on an equal footing, based on mutual respect and consideration for each other’s interests.
☝️
Today, as I have said, we have a good opportunity to discuss
the current state and prospects of Russian-Congolese cooperation
, as well as regional and international issues of mutual interest.
