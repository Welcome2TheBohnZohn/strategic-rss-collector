# 🇷🇺 Embassy of Russia in Burundi On 17 November, Ambassador of Russia to Burundi Sergey Chernenko

Published: 2026-11-18 19:01:30

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33460

🇷🇺
🇧🇮
Embassy of Russia in Burundi
:
On 17 November, Ambassador of Russia to Burundi
Sergey Chernenko
took part in
an official ceremony
in Bujumbura to transfer
Russian humanitarian aid to Burundi
, with support from the country office of
the UN World Food Programme
(
#WFP
).
Burundi received
1,100 tonnes of food aid
– wheat, peas and flour – worth
$2 million
. The supplies will reach
8,000 of the most vulnerable households
across the country.
The ceremony was also attended by Acting Country Director of WFP in Burundi
Arduino Mangoni
, and Director General of the Programme for Regional Integration and International Cooperation at Burundi’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs
Adolphe Nahayo
.
🤝
In their remarks, participants
thanked Russia
for providing humanitarian aid and supporting the people of Burundi.
