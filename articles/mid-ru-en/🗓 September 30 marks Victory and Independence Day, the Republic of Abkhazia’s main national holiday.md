September 30 marks Victory and Independence Day, the Republic of Abkhazia’s main national holiday.
3️⃣
3️⃣
years ago, the Abkhaz people upheld their legitimate right to live a life of peace and freedom.
#ThatDay
in 1993 saw the end of
the Patriotic War of the people of Abkhazia
. From the very first days of the war, the courageous and brave Abkhaz people were joined by members of the Abkhaz diaspora and volunteers from the North Caucasus and other Russian regions and effectively defended their
legitimate right to freedom and independence
.
The Victory secured the right of the people of Abkhazia to self-determination and laid a
robust foundation for the Republic’s sovereignty
.
Over the years,
#Abkhazia
has made significant progress in state-building. The economy has become stronger, social welfare and living standards have improved, and major investment projects have been implemented. The republic’s international ties have expanded.
🇷🇺
Russia is supporting these processes through fundamental bilateral agreements, including the Treaty on Friendship, Cooperation and Mutual Assistance of September 17, 2008 and the Treaty on Alliance and Strategic Partnership of November 24, 2014.

On the occasion of Abkhazia’s national holiday,
President Vladimir Putin
sent
a message of congratulations to President of the Republic
Badra Gunba
(September 30, 2026)
:
Russia and Abkhazia enjoy
friendly and allied relations
. I am confident that they will continue to grow stronger for the benefit of our peoples and in the interest of ensuring regional stability and security.
Russia will continue to provide active support to its Abkhaz partners in addressing pressing issues of state-building and socioeconomic development.
On the eve of the national holiday, Sukhum hosted a ceremony to lay flowers at the Memorial to the Defenders of the Fatherland in the Park of Glory. President of the Republic of Abkhazia Badra Gunba was joined by Russia’s Chargé d’Affaires in Abkhazia Mikhail Litvinov and the Embassy staff.
