# 🇷🇺 Embassy of Russia in Iran Statement by the Embassy on circulating false claims about the evacuation of diplomatic personnel and specialists at the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant

Published: 2026-10-24 11:05:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32924

🇷🇺
🇮🇷
Embassy of Russia in Iran
:
⚡️
Statement
by the Embassy on circulating false claims about the evacuation of diplomatic personnel and specialists at the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant
(October 24, 2026)
We have noted reports by several hostile news channels alleging that
an evacuation
of Russian Embassy staff and specialists at
the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant
has begun.
❗️
We state that neither Embassy staff nor specialists at the Bushehr NPP have any plans to evacuate
.
Work continues as normal. Claims to the contrary are
disinformation
from a well-known source.
👉
We advise
unsubscribing
from news channels that spread such false information.
