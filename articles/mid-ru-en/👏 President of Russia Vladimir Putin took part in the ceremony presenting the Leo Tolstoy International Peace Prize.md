# 👏 President of Russia Vladimir Putin took part in the ceremony presenting the Leo Tolstoy International Peace Prize

Published: 2026-09-10 07:00:03

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31957

👏
President of Russia Vladimir Putin took part in
the ceremony
presenting
the Leo Tolstoy International Peace Prize
to General Secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam, President of Vietnam To Lam
(Moscow, September 9, 2026)
The award was presented to Comrade To Lam for his significant personal contribution to strengthening peace and cooperation among peoples. The ceremony was held in the St Andrew Hall of the Grand Kremlin Palace.
💬
President Putin
: This year, the award ceremony coincided with
Leo Tolstoy’s birthday
, which, in my view, is deeply symbolic.
It is universally recognized that Tolstoy’s creative legacy is part of the world’s cultural heritage
. It transcends time and ideological boundaries and has had – and continues to have – a profound influence on politicians, statesmen, public figures and generations of people across the world.
I know that Tolstoy’s literary and spiritual legacy is highly valued in Vietnam
. Vietnam’s first President,
Comrade
Ho Chi Minh
, wrote in an article published in the Soviet Literaturnaya Gazeta in 1960 that he had first encountered Tolstoy’s works in his youth. As he recalled, they made an indelible impression on him and inspired him to try his own hand at writing.
☝️
In Russia, we have always regarded with deep respect and admiration the resilience and courage of the Vietnamese people
, who endured
the severe trials of foreign oppression
, armed intervention and national division with dignity. The Vietnamese people know well the value of human life, peaceful coexistence, work and creation. This outlook on life and spirit is deeply consonant with the humanist philosophy of Leo Tolstoy.
Of course, we fully support
the unanimous decision of the multinational jury to award this year’s Leo Tolstoy Peace Prize to the Leader of Vietnam
, and through him
to the entire Vietnamese people.
Comrade To Lam is indeed
an authoritative and far-sighted statesman
,
a true patriot
who has chosen the path of dedicated service to his homeland and the defense of its national interests. As Head of State, he pursues
a balanced domestic and foreign policy
that contributes to strengthening an independent and sovereign Vietnam and to ensuring stability and security in Asia and throughout the world.
🤝
I also see this award as recognition of your substantial personal contribution to deepening friendship and mutual understanding between Russia and Vietnam
. As a principled and consistent supporter of advancing the Russia-Vietnam strategic partnership, you keep the development of direct political, economic and humanitarian ties between our countries under constant attention.
