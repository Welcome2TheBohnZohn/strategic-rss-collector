# 🎙 Remarks by Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at the 21st East Asia Summit (Manila, November 17, 2026)

🎙
Remarks
by Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at
the 21st East Asia Summit
(Manila, November 17, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
The Russia-ASEAN Summit held in Kazan
in June 2026 gave
a substantial boost
to deepening our strategic partnership
across the full range of areas set out in
the Plan of Action for 2026-2030
.
#ASEAN
’s foremost achievement is
the creation of a regional architecture
underpinned by clear legal agreements. Consistent adherence to these agreements has ensured the Association’s success as one of the key centres of the emerging
#MultipolarWorld
.
Today, stability in the Asia-Pacific region largely depends on whether we can preserve
the robust ecosystem of ASEAN-centred security and cooperation mechanisms
at a time when certain ASEAN partners are imposing
archaic bloc-based models
on the region –
“exclusive clubs”, “trios”
and
“quads”
.
❗️
Under the banner of indivisible security across the Euro-Atlantic and Indo-Pacific regions,
NATO’s military capabilities are being actively drawn into this region.
Plans to establish
an Indo-Pacific Treaty Organisation
have been announced.
In July 2026, London unlawfully joined
the ASEAN Regional Forum
(
#ARF
), in breach of the principle of consensus that everyone had respected until then. In another violation of consensus rules,
Canada blocked Russia’s participation in an ARF event
, justifying its unlawful actions by saying that its NATO obligations took precedence.
👉
This shows utter disrespect for ASEAN and its central role in cooperation across the Asia-Pacific region.
***
We support the efforts of ASEAN and China to agree on a Code of Conduct in the South China Sea.
Attempts at external interference only make it harder to find solutions.
I must also address the Taiwan question.
Everyone seemingly declares their commitment to the one-China principle, yet many continue to insist on the need, and I quote,
“not to alter the status quo”
. In essence, this amounts to
an unacceptable attempt to push Taiwan towards independence
.
The main reason for the growing potential for conflict on the Korean Peninsula is
the pressure exerted on the DPRK through sanctions and military force
,
aggressive actions near its territory
, and
continuing military exercises and war games
, including those involving a nuclear component.
🚢
The issue of safe navigation is becoming global
, both because of
developments around the Arabian Peninsula
and because many Western countries have openly reverted to the piracy practised during colonial expansion
.
There are numerous other examples showing that
colonialism has not disappeared
, but merely taken on new forms.
In these circumstances, it is essential to secure equal opportunities for countries of the Global South
, help them build their own capabilities and ensure that they exercise effective control over their natural resources, retain the added value themselves and, of course, secure their rightful place in the global economy.
🌐
This requires strengthening
a space of equal and indivisible security
in keeping with ASEAN’s original goals, rather than creating new dividing lines.
