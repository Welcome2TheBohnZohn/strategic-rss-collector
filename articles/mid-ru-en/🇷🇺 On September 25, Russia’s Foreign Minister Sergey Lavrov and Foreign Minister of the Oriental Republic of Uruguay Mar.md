On September 25, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of the Oriental Republic of Uruguay Mario Lubetkin
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Sides discussed
current issues in Russia-Uruguay relations
, including bilateral contacts at various levels and trade and economic cooperation.
The Foreign Ministers exchanged views on
the current international situation
and discussed multilateral cooperation, primarily at the UN.
They emphasised their interest in
reinvigorating the
#RussiaCELAC
political dialogue mechanism and strengthening ties between
the EAEU
and
MERCOSUR member states
, taking into account Uruguay’s rotating chairmanship of these Latin American associations.
