# 🎙 Russian Ambassador to Seychelles Kirill Sokolov-Shcherbachev’s comment regarding Russia-Seychelles cooperation in the field of tourism

Published: 2026-09-08 11:03:24

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31912

🎙
Russian Ambassador to Seychelles Kirill Sokolov-Shcherbachev’s
comment
regarding Russia-Seychelles cooperation in the field of tourism
(4 September 2026)
#Tourism
💬
Tourism is the leading area of our cooperation with Seychelles
and it is now on the rise. For Seychelles tourism is the main source of income. The tourist industry suffered a lot when the air connectivity with Gulf countries was broken because of the Middle East crisis. Even after it has been partially restored,
that is Russia which kept providing Seychelles with the tourist flow
. Aeroflot flights have never stopped, although the flight time has increased.
#Aeroflot
Moreover, this year it has been decided to make
Aeroflot
flight
all year-round
, instead of seasonal timetable (September-May). After a short period,
flights have resumed on 8 July
. Currently, Aeroflot is operating
twice a week
and it is fully booked. In the winter period they add one more flight. 2025 counts
more than 43,000 passengers
, most of which are tourists. Guests from Russia make
15%
of the whole tourist flow
. Our country is on the third place after Germany and France. We will see what are the figures by the end of this year because, according to local estimates, sometimes
our tourists are ahead
.
#Russianlanguage
There are Russian-speaking tourist operators in Seychelles.
And the use of Russian language in the field of tourism is increasing.
We see Russian-speaking personnel in hotels and travel agencies, tour guides, advertisement etc. The Seychelles Tourism Academy (STA) is providing Russian language courses for more than
100 students
studying in different areas of tourist industry. It doesn’t mean that all of them will fluently speak Russian in a couple of months, but I am sure that
this is just the beginning
and it may be useful for some of them in their future professional activity.
Recently, we agreed the management of
Savoy
and
Coral Strand hotels
, which host a large number of Russians, and the STA to launch
a project for students
to help the reception desk in welcoming Russian guests and practicing their Russian language skills. Of course, we do not expect them to literally “sing” in Russian, they are just beginners. But anyway, I think this initiative is working for the nice image of the hotel and help the young specialists to overpass the language barrier. So, the Russian tourists, in particular those, who come to Seychelles in group by Aeroflot, can be warmly welcomed by Seychellois students at the hotel.
🤝
We count on keeping up the good figures in Russian arrivals and maybe even on their rise. At the same time we will continue to work on
further improving the tourist services
, for example, in expanding the Russian language in this field.
