On September 24, 2026 H.E.
Andrey A. Pritsepov
presented his Letters of Credence as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the Russian Federation to Barbados to Lieutenant Colonel The Most Honourable
Jeffrey Davidson Bostic
, President of the Republic of Barbados.
🔸
In his speech, Ambassador Pritsepov highlighted the rapidly developing friendly relations between the two countries and the intensification of bilateral contacts, and expressed his hope for the establishment of ties in the fields of science, education and tourism. He noted that earlier that day, a productive meeting had taken place in Moscow between the Russian Minister of Finance,
Anton Siluanov
, and the Minister of Finance of Barbados, the Honourable
Ryan R. Straughn
, who discussed the digitalisation of public finances, in particular the tax administration system.
🔹
The Russian Ambassador also wished the people of Barbados further progress and prosperity on the eve of the 60th anniversary of the declaration of independence and the fifth anniversary of the country’s proclamation as a republic.
🔸
The Barbadian Head of State noted his interest in continuing the mutually beneficial course towards strengthening ties and supporting Russia’s efforts to reform the system of global governance. He thanked the Russian side for its assistance in training professional staff for the Barbadian police force.
🔹
During a brief conversation following the ceremony, the parties discussed possible areas of bilateral cooperation, including the prospects for establishing air links between Russia and Barbados, as well as cooperation projects in the oil and gas, nuclear and renewable energy sectors.
