# 🎙 Remarks by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the Plenary Session of the #APEC

Published: 2026-11-19 05:50:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33481

🎙
Remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the Plenary Session of the
#APEC
Economic Leaders’ Meeting
(Shenzhen, November 19, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
First of all, I would like
to commend China in the strongest terms
for its work as
APEC Chair
this year. We fully endorse the theme of China’s chairmanship –
Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together
.
This is an ambitious undertaking, and
APEC
, as the largest economic forum in our region,
is uniquely placed to see it through
. As our colleague from Singapore has just noted, the global economy is becoming increasingly fragmented. With challenges and threats to its stability multiplying,
APEC must bring economies together and set a constructive agenda for cooperation
.
📄
That is precisely the aim of
the draft Shenzhen Declaration
before us for approval.
Russia supports it.
It is gratifying that
the Asia-Pacific region has become one of the engines of global growth
. The region continues to grow and steadily expand its industrial, technological and logistics capabilities. Taken together, the APEC economies are forecast
to grow by 4.8%
in 2026, well above the global rate.
☝️
Let me emphasise that building constructive partnerships with Asia-Pacific economies is a strategic priority for Russia.
We are an integral part of the region and make a substantial contribution to its prosperity.
In the first eight months of this year alone,
our trade with the region’s economies grew by almost a third
, with a focus on non-energy and innovative products. Over the past ten years,
Russia’s total trade with ASEAN countries has grown by nearly 60%.
I would also like to mention the growing network of
preferential trade agreements between countries in the region and
the Eurasian Economic Union
.
Russia is actively advancing its
flagship initiative to establish a
Greater Eurasian Partnership
to create a seamless economic area across the continent. We see this partnership as
the core of regional integration
, bringing together the mechanisms of
the Eurasian Economic Union
,
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
and
ASEAN
.
Russia supplies the Asia-Pacific region with
energy resources as well as large quantities of grain and mineral fertilisers
. In doing so, we make a significant contribution to energy and food security throughout the region. Our trading partners can always count on us for
reliable, consistent supplies of high-quality products
.
🇷🇺
Russia seeks to remain open to the world while strengthening its economy.
To that end, we continue to pursue far-reaching reforms at home, upgrade our industrial base, expand our technological capabilities and keep inflation in check.
We have fundamentally changed the structure of the Russian economy.
We are giving priority to
advanced sectors
such as digital technology, artificial intelligence and nuclear energy. As a result, Russia’s GDP has grown by
more than 10%
in real terms over the past three years, and our country ranks firmly among the world’s leading economies in terms of purchasing power parity.
Russia is keen to work as closely as possible with fellow APEC economies in
knowledge-intensive industries
. One example is
the peaceful use of nuclear energy
, a field in which
Russia is a world leader
and works closely with many partners across the region.
🤝
Russia will continue to play an active part in APEC’s work.
