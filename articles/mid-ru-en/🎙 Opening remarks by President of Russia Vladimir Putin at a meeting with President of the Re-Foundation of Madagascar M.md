🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of the Re-Foundation of Madagascar Michael Randrianirina on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We enjoy
longstanding friendly ties
with Madagascar. Next year, we will mark
the 55th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations
.
Our talks in Moscow in February
gave fresh impetus to
strengthening cooperation between Russia and Madagascar
across a broad range of issues. There is, of course,
considerable untapped potential
for bilateral trade and economic cooperation. Trade remains relatively modest in absolute terms, but it is gaining momentum, and the trends are positive.
We also attach great importance to expanding humanitarian ties
. Some
5,000 students
from your country have already received an education in Russia. In April,
the University of Antananarivo
concluded a cooperation agreement with
St Petersburg State University
, and in June,
MGIMO University
and
Madagascar’s Foreign Ministry
signed a memorandum of understanding.
🎓
I understand that you have been awarded
an honorary professorship
by
the Russian Presidential Academy of National Economy and Public Administration
. Please accept my congratulations.
I would also like to highlight that, in April,
the Russian Orthodox Church was granted legal status in Madagascar
. Thank you for this. It is an important development, given that the Church has
more than 1,500 parishioners
on the island.
We certainly see your country as
an important partner in shaping a
#MultipolarWorld
order
and
a balanced and just system of international relations.
🤝
We are ready to expand our cooperation
in international forums, including, of course, the foremost among them – the UN.
