🗓
Press release on the International Day for the Prevention of and Fight against All Forms of Transnational Organised Crime
(November 15, 2026)
Every year on November 15,
the world marks
the International Day for the Prevention of and Fight against All Forms of Transnational Organised Crime
, established by
UN General Assembly Resolution 78/267
in 2024.
The date commemorates the adoption of
the UN Convention against Transnational Organised Crime
(
#UNTOC
) –
a key universal international legal instrument
in the global fight against crime.
Today, transnational organised crime is becoming
increasingly aggressive, coordinated, and technologically sophisticated
.
Criminal syndicates are rapidly embracing
information and communication technologies
and engaging in the illicit trafficking of arms and drugs, human trafficking, fraud, and money laundering.
There is also clear evidence of
growing links
between these groups and extremist and terrorist organisations.
🇷🇺
The Russian Federation has consistently stood at the forefront of international efforts to address these challenges.
Our country
helped lay the foundations
of the modern global anti-crime framework under the auspices of the UN. Also,
it was among the first to ratify
the UNTOC
and its supplementary protocols, and took the initiative in developing
the UN Convention against Cybercrime
.
Russia makes
a significant contribution
to strengthening the capacity of other countries in this area, extensively promoting cooperation among competent authorities both bilaterally and within the frameworks of the
#UN
, the
#CIS
, the
#CSTO
, the
#SCO
, and
#BRICS
, while also working
to foster broader collaboration
.
Among other forms of assistance,
Russia supports its partners through
the UN Office on Drugs and Crime
(
#UNODC
), making an
annual
voluntary contribution
of $3 million to its budget.
These funds go toward implementing specialised programmes and projects to combat
corruption, illicit drug trafficking
in Central Asia,
maritime piracy
and armed robbery
at sea off the African coast.
The scope of this technical assistance and the number of countries benefiting from it
are expected to grow in the future.
☝️
Transnational crime knows no borders.
Defeating this scourge requires
concerted, coordinated and, above all, depoliticised efforts
of the entire international community, with the UN playing a core coordinating role and all action firmly grounded in the norms and principles of international law.
Attempts by certain jurisdictions to make law-enforcement cooperation conditional on political demands or to resort to double standards for short-term political gain
only play into the hands of criminals and undermine global security
.
🤝
The Russian Federation remains firmly committed to further enhancing international cooperation on the basis of equality and mutual respect
, with the aim of ensuring reliable protection for states and their citizens against the complex threats posed by transnational organised crime.
