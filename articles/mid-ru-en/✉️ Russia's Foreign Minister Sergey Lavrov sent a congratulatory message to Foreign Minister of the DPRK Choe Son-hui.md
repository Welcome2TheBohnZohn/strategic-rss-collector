# ✉️ Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov sent a congratulatory message to Foreign Minister of the DPRK Choe Son-hui

Published: 2026-09-09 17:01:34

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31946

✉️
Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov sent
a congratulatory message
to Foreign Minister of the DPRK Choe Son-hui
(September 9, 2026)
✍️
Please accept my heartfelt congratulations on
the 78th anniversary of the founding of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea
.
Your country is
steadily advancing
along the path of strengthening its sovereignty and improving the well-being of its people. I am sincerely pleased with the successes of our Korean friends.
I note with satisfaction the strong momentum in the development of
multifaceted and comprehensive Russia-DPRK cooperation and strategic partnership
, fully in line with
the agreements reached in June 2024
by President of the Russian Federation
Vladimir Putin
and President of the State Affairs of the DPRK
Kim Jong-un
.
I am confident that
the productive joint work
of the Foreign Ministries of Russia and the DPRK will contribute to fulfilling
the tasks set by our Leaders
. Their implementation
fully serves the interests of the peoples of our two countries
and
contributes to our common goals
of building a just
#MultipolarWorld
and maintaining peace and stability on the Korean Peninsula and in Northeast Asia.
🤝
I wish you and all employees of the DPRK Foreign Ministry good health and outstanding success in your responsible work.
SERGEY LAVROV
