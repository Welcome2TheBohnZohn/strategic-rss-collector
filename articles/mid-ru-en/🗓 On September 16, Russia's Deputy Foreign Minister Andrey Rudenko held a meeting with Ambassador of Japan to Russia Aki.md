# 🗓 On September 16, Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister Andrey Rudenko held a meeting with Ambassador of Japan to Russia Akira Muto.

Published: 2026-09-16 20:00:38

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32071

🗓
On September 16, Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister Andrey Rudenko
held a meeting
with Ambassador of Japan to Russia Akira Muto.
They had a frank discussion on the most pressing
bilateral issues
.
The Russian Side stressed that
Tokyo’s continued anti-Russia policy
, including
the recent hysteria in Japan
over
President of Russia Vladimir Putin’s visit to the Southern Kuril Islands,
as well as its expanding military assistance to the Kiev regime,
pushes the prospect of normalising dialogue even further away
, despite Japan’s publicly stated interest in doing so.
The Russian Side also highlighted the dangers of Japan’s remilitarisation
, Tokyo’s increased military exercises with the US and Western allies near Russia’s Far Eastern borders, the deployment of intermediate-range missile systems on Japanese territory and other such steps.
☝️
All these actions pose a direct threat to Russia’s national security and to peace and stability in Northeast Asia
. Should such activities continue, Russia will take the necessary counterbalancing measures to maintain an adequate level of defence capability.
