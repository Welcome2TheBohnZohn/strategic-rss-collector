On September 7, RT India launches a new Hindi-language digital news platform,
RTHindi.com
.
RT India's broadcasting debut took place in December 2025 and over the past year the channel has gained popularity among a wide audience thanks to its objective coverage of news. The launch of RT Hindi will allow more viewers to receive reliable and timely information without Western censorship.
The studio's first guest was
DCM & Minister Counsellor of the Russian Embassy in India Roman Babushkin
.
🎤
In an interview in Hindi with Runjhun Sharma, RT India's Head of News, the diplomat described the launch of a Hindi-language news platform as necessary and timely. Roman Babushkin outlined that Indian viewers will now be able to receive objective and reliable information about Russia and the world in their native language.
