# 🇷🇺 Russia's President Vladimir Putin received in the Kremlin Special Envoy of the President of the United States of America

Published: 2026-09-05 22:00:40

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31860

🇷🇺
🇺🇸
Russia's President
Vladimir
Putin
received in the Kremlin
Special Envoy of the President of the United States of America
Steve
Witkoff
and entrepreneur, founder of Affinity Partners
Jared
Kushner
(Moscow, September 5, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Colleagues, I heartily welcome you to Moscow.
The situation we are to sort out today is, of course, a difficult one.
But before we begin, I would like to ask you to convey my
best wishes and words of gratitude
to the President of the United States,
Donald Trump.
While he realizes that he is facing a difficult situation,
he nevertheless does not stop his efforts to resolve
it and to contribute to the settlement of the
Russian‑Ukrainian conflict.
Today, we will discuss all its components and all its aspects, and for our part, we are also ready to inform you about our view of the situation.
As you informed us, I know that you are planning to go to Kiev after visiting Moscow. In any case, of course, no matter what happens next,
contacts of this kind are certainly always beneficial.
And here, of course, the quality of mediation and trust in those who are engaged in it are very important. I’d like to tell you that
we find it comfortable to work with you,
and, of course, we have a very high level of trust during this work.
🤝
We are glad to see you. Welcome.
***
We received the request through official channels — both through the intelligence services and through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs — to stop the strikes on Kiev.
I issued the respective order,
and since midnight on September 5, these
strikes on Kiev have not been carried out.
I would like to note that the Ukrainian side has also significantly reduced — by an order of magnitude — the strikes on the territory of the Russian Federation, including Moscow.
The Ukrainian authorities have published their call for a broader three‑day truce, but we have never agreed on this with them.
Yet we will do everything in our power to ensure the
safety of the negotiation process
and the mediators, represented by you and, perhaps, by those accompanying you.
