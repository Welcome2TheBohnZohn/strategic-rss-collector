# ✉️ Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s greetings to His Holiness Patriarch Kirill of Moscow and All Russia on his 80th birthday

Published: 2026-11-20 05:59:58

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33496

✉️
Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
greetings
to His Holiness Patriarch Kirill of Moscow and All Russia on his 80th birthday
(November 20, 2026)
✍️
Your Holiness,
I offer you my most heartfelt greetings on the occasion of your birthday.
Your long-standing primatial ministry has earned
the deepest recognition both within our country and far beyond its borders.
Your tireless pastoral labours and Christian selflessness help people find spiritual support in life and contribute to strengthening the moral foundations of Russian society.
Your immense
personal contribution to instilling patriotic values in the younger generation
commands particular respect. Your support for those taking part in the special military operation, and for their families, is truly invaluable.
🤝
I greatly value our close and fruitful cooperation.
I look forward to continuing this partnership for the sake of strengthening the standing of Russia and the Orthodox world
, and for advancing international relations grounded in the enduring principles of equality, truth, goodness, and justice.
Wishing you the best of health, many more years, and new achievements for the benefit of the Church and the Fatherland.
SERGEY LAVROV
