On National Unity Day, Russia's President
Vladimir Putin
took part in several official events, including
a ceremony to lay flowers
at the monument to Kuzma Minin and Dmitry Pozharsky on Red Square.
The ceremony was attended by representatives of religious denominations and members of non-governmental and youth organisations.
The President
also spoke
with children of special military operation heroes who were in Red Square.
