Statement by a Russian representative at the 63rd session of the UN Human Rights Council during the Interactive dialogue on Secretary-General report on cooperation with the United Nations:
📍
September 23, 2026, Geneva
👋
I greet you on behalf of the Human Rights Commissioner of the Russian Federation,
Yana Lantratova
. I have to speak from the placard "Russian Federation" rather than "National Human Rights Institution" (NHRI), a fact that
clearly illustrates the problems within the system of international NHRI recognition
, as the Russian institution was stripped of its "A" status accreditation. The grounds for this decision are
opaque
and raise
serious questions
.
🇷🇺
Russia is consistently developing
its own national model for human rights protection
, based on the Constitution, norms of international law, historical experience, and the spiritual and moral values ​​of the Russian people.
👤
At the heart of this model lies
human
–
his dignity, rights, and freedoms
. We view civil and political rights as inextricably linked to social, economic, and cultural rights. Human dignity
cannot be protected selectively
.
❗️
The international human rights agenda is frequently used as
a tool to promote double standards.
Such an approach undermines trust in international mechanisms and hinders equitable dialogue.
👉
The primary mission of our institution is
to restore the violated rights of the particular person
. Civil society, based on solidarity and responsibility, remains a key partner.
🤝
The Commissioner
is ready to develop cooperation
with the UN and foreign counterparts based on mutual respect and equal partnership. We advocate for a
joint search for solutions in the interests of the people
– free from politicization and pressure.
