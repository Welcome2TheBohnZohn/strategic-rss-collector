🌍
On 29 October 2026, Moscow hosted the main events of the
Third Russia–Africa Summit!
#Day2
• The plenary session of the Third
#RussiaAfrica
Summit
took place
at the Grand Kremlin Palace, co-chaired by Russian President
Vladimir Putin
and African Union Chair, President of Burundi
Évariste Ndayishimiye
.
🤝
The motto:
“For Peace, Security and Development.”
• At the close of the 3rd Russia – Africa Summit,
Vladimir Putin
and the Chairperson of the African Union, President of the Republic of Burundi
Évariste Ndayishimiye,
held
a joint press conference.
• The Summit adopted
the Declaration of the Third Russia–Africa Summit,
the Declaration on Cooperation in the Field of Civilian Applications of Artificial Intelligence, and the Russia–Africa Partnership Forum Action Plan for 2027–2029.
On the sidelines of the Summit,
Vladimir Putin
also held a series of bilateral meetings:
🇳🇦
with Namibian President
Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah;
🇨🇬
with President of the Republic of the Congo
Denis Sassou Nguesso;
🇧🇮
with Burundian President
Évariste Ndayishimiye;
🇰🇪
with Kenyan President
William Samoei Ruto.
***
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Holding such large-scale events has become a good tradition and clearly demonstrates the firm determination of both Russia and African states to further strengthen and develop multifaceted ties in the spirit of genuine partnership and mutual respect for the sake of universal growth and prosperity.
