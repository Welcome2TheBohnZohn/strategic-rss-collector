🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Tajikistan Emomali Rahmon
(Dushanbe, November 20, 2026)
On November 20, Russia's President
arrived
in the Republic of Tajikistan on
a working visit
.
During their talks, Vladimir Putin and Emomali Rahmon discussed key issues in the further development of the strategic partnership and alliance between Russia and Tajikistan, as well as current international and regional issues.
***
💬
Vladimir Putin:
At
our last meeting
, we agreed to meet again after
the APEC Summit in Shenzhen
. I will, of course, brief you on the outcome. Developments there are
relevant to Tajikistan and the wider region
, as many of the countries involved are also your neighbours.
Today, outside
the CSTO framework
, we will have an opportunity to discuss
regional security
, the operation of our
201st Military Base
and, most importantly,
economic cooperation
.
📈
In 2025,
our bilateral trade
grew by more than 25%
, and in the first nine months of 2026
it
rose by almost 19%
. That is a good pace of growth, and I want to thank you for the attention you give to developing relations between Russia and Tajikistan.
We’re making headway on a range of issues.
A large number of companies in Tajikistan have Russian investment.
More than 70 Russian regions cooperate with districts across Tajikistan
and have built up a wide network of partners. Then, of course, there are our cultural and humanitarian ties.
We greatly appreciate the attention you give to
promoting the Russian language
.
Mr President, thank you for the invitation. I am sure we will have
a substantive discussion
. I have asked several colleagues from Moscow to join us, and they will be here shortly. They include
Marat Khusnullin
, the Russian co-chair of
the Intergovernmental Commission
, and representatives of
the Ministry of Defence
, so that we can discuss military cooperation.
