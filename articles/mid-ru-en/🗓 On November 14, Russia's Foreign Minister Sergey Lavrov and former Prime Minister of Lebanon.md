On November 14, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
and former Prime Minister of Lebanon
Saad Hariri
held a meeting
in Abu Dhabi on the sidelines of
the 17th Sir Bani Yas Forum on Peace and Security
.
They exchanged views on
developments in the Middle East
, focusing on the situation in and around Lebanon.
The Russian Side reaffirmed its
principled support for Lebanon’s unity, territorial integrity and sovereignty
.
🤝
They also discussed
prospects for strengthening the traditionally friendly relations
between Russia and Lebanon.
