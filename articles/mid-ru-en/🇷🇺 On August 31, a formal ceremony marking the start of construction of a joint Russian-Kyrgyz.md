On August 31,
a formal ceremony
marking the start of construction of
a
joint Russian-Kyrgyz
Research Center for Infectious Disease Studies in the Kyrgyz Republic
was held in Bishkek via videoconference.
The Center is being
established by Rospotrebnadzor
pursuant to an instruction issued by the President of Russia on November 24, 2021, and under the Agreement between the Government of the Russian Federation and the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic dated March 7, 2023.
The Center will operate on the premises of
the Republican Center for Quarantine and Particularly Dangerous Infections
under the Kyrgyz Ministry of Health in Bishkek.
The design and construction of the facility are financed by the Russian Federation.
Preparatory engineering surveys and design and cost-estimate work have been completed
, and the project has received a positive assessment from
Russia’s Federal Autonomous Institution Glavgosexpertiza
. The facility has also been entered into Bishkek’s register of buildings under construction, while general contractors authorized to carry out the work have been designated by both the Russian and Kyrgyz Sides.
✅
Construction is expected to be completed in Q4 2027.
