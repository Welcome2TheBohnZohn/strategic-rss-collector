On September 24, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Deputy Prime Minister, Minister of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates of Jordan Ayman Safadi
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The discussion focused on priority tasks for further strengthening
traditionally friendly Russia-Jordan relations
.
Particular attention was paid to the outcomes of the
7th meeting of the Russia-Jordan Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation
, held in Moscow on September 9-10.
The Sides exchanged views on key Middle Eastern issues, focusing on
the consequences of the unprecedented military escalation in the region
in the context of
the US-Israeli aggression against Iran
. They also discussed the situation in
the Palestinian-Israeli conflict zone
.
