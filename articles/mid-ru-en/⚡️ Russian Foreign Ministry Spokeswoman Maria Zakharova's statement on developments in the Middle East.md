# ⚡️ Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's statement on developments in the Middle East

Published: 2026-11-17 16:03:23

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33414

⚡️
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
statement
on developments in the Middle East
(November 17, 2026)
We note with regret
another surge in tensions in the Persian Gulf
, where the opposing sides have exchanged
further strikes
in recent days. Hits were recorded on
Iran’s Qeshm Island
and on
the territory of Bahrain
.
Such attacks
constitute a blatant violation of the sovereignty and security of states in the region
and, to our deep regret, cause casualties and material damage.
❗️
Russia once again calls on all parties to the current confrontation
, above all the US,
which initiated it
,
to cease hostilities immediately.
We consider attacks on civilians and any civilian facilities, including strikes on the territory of the Arab states of the Persian Gulf,
utterly unacceptable
.
Russia consistently advocates resolving differences in this strategically important region
at the negotiating table, taking into account the interests of every coastal state without exception.
🤝
W
e stand ready to provide the necessary assistance towards this goal
, including on the basis of the proposals set out in
Russia’s Concept of Collective Security in the Persian Gulf Region
, updated in May.
