Published: 2026-10-05 12:59:58

⚡️
Prosecutor General’s Office of the Russian Federation
:
Following an application by the Prosecutor General’s Office of the Russian Federation,
Baltic nationalists
have been recognised by a court
as accomplices of Nazi Germany in war crimes and crimes against humanity committed against the Soviet people during the Great Patriotic War
– a finding of legal significance.
Recognition of this fact is not only
an evident conclusion
stemming from
the principal legal findings of the Nuremberg Trials
, but also
a historical duty
that society and the state owe to those who fought in the war, its veterans and
its victims
.
⚖️
It has been established that the Nazi invaders committed atrocities with the direct involvement of their accomplices –
Latvian, Lithuanian and Estonian nationalists
(
13 individuals and five organisations
).
According to records from the archives of
Russia’s Federal Security Service
,
Foreign Ministry
and
Defence Ministry
, as well as
the State Archive of the Russian Federation
, following
Germany’s attack on the USSR
,
the Nazis recruited Baltic nationalists from among local residents to do their “dirty work” in the occupied territories
. They carried out
punitive operations
against civilians and Soviet POWs.
Through their actions, the territories of the Baltic countries –
Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia
– became killing grounds during the Great Patriotic War for POWs, Soviet officials and their families, and people suspected of sympathising with the Red Army.
▪️
In total, Baltic collaborators killed more than 1.5 million people during the war and the post-war years.
***
Since the early 1990s, the authorities of today’s Baltic countries have pursued
a deliberate state policy of distorting the USSR’s role in the Great Patriotic War and
its Victory over Nazism
. Collaborators associated with Nazi criminals, along with their organisations, are being
glorified
.
This
contradicts
the Verdict of the Nuremberg Tribunal
regarding responsibility for
the Second World War, its causes and its outcomes
, as well as the provisions of the UN General Assembly resolution
“Combating glorification of Nazism, neo-Nazism and other practices that contribute to fuelling contemporary forms of racism, racial discrimination, xenophobia and related intolerance”
.
In effect, it serves to justify Nazi crimes.
❗️
Countering these destructive trends is fundamental to preserving historical memory, preventing the falsification of history and protecting the national interests of the Russian Federation.
