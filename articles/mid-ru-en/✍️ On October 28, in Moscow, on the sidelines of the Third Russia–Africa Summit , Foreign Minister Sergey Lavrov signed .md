On October 28, in Moscow, on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
, Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov signed a substantial package of bilateral documents with the heads of the executive bodies of African regional integration organisations.
These included:
– Memorandums of understanding
between
the Government of the Russian Federation
and
the East African Community
,
the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa
, and
the Secretariat of the African Continental Free Trade Area
,
– Action plans
with
the African Union Commission
for 2027-2029,
the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa
,
the Southern African Development Community
, and
the Economic Community of Central African States
.
🤝
This marked another step towards developing the legal framework for cooperation with leading African organisations
, helping to further deepen the partnership with the continent and implement the decisions of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
.
