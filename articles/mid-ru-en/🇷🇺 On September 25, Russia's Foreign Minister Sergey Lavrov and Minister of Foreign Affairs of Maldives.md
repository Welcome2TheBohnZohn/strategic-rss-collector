Sergey Lavrov
and Minister of Foreign Affairs of Maldives
Iruthisham Adam
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Foreign Ministers discussed prospects for
further developing the friendly Russian-Maldivian relations
.
During the meeting, the Sides expressed their
mutual interest in maintaining regular political dialogue
and expanding practical cooperation, including in tourism.
