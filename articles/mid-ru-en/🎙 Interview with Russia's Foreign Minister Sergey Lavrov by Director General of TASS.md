# 🎙 Interview with Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov by Director General of TASS

Published: 2026-11-16 19:02:21

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33385

🎙
Interview
with Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov by Director General of
TASS
Andrey Kondrashov
(Moscow, November 16, 2026)
Key points
:
• When I met President of Finland
Alexander Stubb
in New York during
the UN General Assembly Session
, he said something had to be done:
“We are heading down the wrong path, and this will take us somewhere we do not want to go. It will not end well”
.
I replied
:
“Listen, if that is how you feel,
take some meaningful action
. Do not just say that Ukraine will die for us, and that this is how we will defend our values”
.
• At the same
#UNGA81 Session
, I
asked
German Foreign Minister
Johann Wadephul
a very simple question. They had found
a drone at Leipzig Airport
carrying an explosive charge that had failed to detonate.
Nobody proved it was a Russian drone.
In just three days, they simply declared that the Russians were responsible and that
Russian diplomats and the Russian cultural centre would be expelled
. Against that backdrop,
I asked whether
their investigation into Nord Stream
was taking rather too long
. Johann Wadephul replied that
these were different issues
🤷‍♂️
.
•
Understandings on Ukraine that could be put down on paper
were reached in Anchorage
.
They were not simply announced on the spur of the moment. US Special Envoy Steve Witkoff
visited a week before the Anchorage Summit
in August 2025 and was received by President
Vladimir Putin
. We saw that some elements of the proposal he brought involved
compromises
. But since any lasting agreement rests, to some extent, on compromise,
we accepted the American proposal
.
• Then
various interpretations
emerged: supposedly, there had been no deal, no agreement, only an initiative.
But if you propose something and I accept it, in Russia that means we have reached an agreement.
•
Europe has had numerous opportunities to act as a mediator
, starting in February 2014, when
France, Germany
and
Poland
acted as guarantors of the agreements between Ukraine’s then President
Viktor Yanukovich
and the opposition. Incidentally, Poland’s guarantor was its current Foreign Minister
Radoslaw Sikorski
, who also attended
the international forum in Abu Dhabi
where I went to speak. I recalled this there and said:
“Here is one of the guarantors”
. The morning after that agreement,
a coup took place
.
•
Dialogue on removing irritants in
#RussiaUS
relations is making slow progress
, even though I raise the issue every time I meet US Secretary of State
Marco Rubio
. We met
in New York
during the high-level week of the 81st UN General Assembly Session, and later
in Atlanta
at the G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting.
Both Sides have an interest in resolving these issues.
•
There is no reason to hail Washington’s lifting of sanctions on Russian diesel supplies as some bold move.
The Americans proudly announced that Venezuelan oil was now under their control, rather than China’s or Russia’s. We did not control anything there, nor did the Chinese: we were partners.
We are being pushed out of every energy market.
A doctrine of dominance over global energy markets has been approved and is now being put into practice.
•
We need to take a sober view of things. We must talk to the Americans.
But trying to do so while positively gushing with enthusiasm does not serve our interests. The Americans do not respect such behaviour.
• The world is not getting any simpler. Remember, we have two allies:
our Army and Navy
. Now we also have our
Aerospace and Nuclear forces
.
