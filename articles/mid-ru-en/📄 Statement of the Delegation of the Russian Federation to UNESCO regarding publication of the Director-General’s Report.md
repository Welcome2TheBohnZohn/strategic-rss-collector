# 📄 Statement of the Delegation of the Russian Federation to UNESCO regarding publication of the Director-General’s Report on the Safety of Journalists and the Issue of Impunity

Published: 2026-11-08 06:15:20

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33244

📄
Statement
of the Delegation of the Russian Federation to UNESCO regarding
publication of the Director-General’s Report on the Safety of Journalists and the Issue of Impunity
(Paris, 2 November 2026)
💬
The Russian Federation is fully committed to
the UNESCO Constitution
, which establishes the
intergovernmental nature
of the Organization and stresses the importance of human dignity, equality and
mutual respect
among peoples, as well as the importance of the
free exchange of ideas and information
.
We strongly believe that
disrespect to these principles puts UNESCO’s values at risk
.
We regret to note that
the Director-General’s report to the IPDC
prepared by the Secretariat has once again become
an example of a failure to respect the UNESCO’s values
.
First of all, I would like to recall that the
General Conference resolution of 1997
and
the 216th Executive Board session decision of 2023
require the Director-General to condemn the killing of journalists without any distinction.
The 2024 decision of the IPDC Intergovernmental Council
also stresses the importance of including official information provided by Member States to the Director-General’s report.
❗️
I have to note with deep regret
, that the lists attached to the report
once again ignore
a significant number of journalists killed
, whose cases were officially reported by the Russian side. These journalists are also excluded from the statistics presented in the report.
❌
The “methodology” that the Secretariat constantly refers to when deciding which information to include in the report
is fundamentally untenable
. I
t is based on biased criteria
and the opinions of politically motivated NGOs. At the same time,
it ignores information
provided directly by the Member States concerned.
This “methodology” goes against the intergovernmental nature of UNESCO. The Secretariat’s continued refusal to take information from Member States into account raises serious questions about whether it is carrying out its duties in good faith.
This approach leads to a
serious contradiction
: the number of journalists killed according to UNESCO’s “verified” data is many times lower than the real number.
In this regard,
we would like to propose
that the Director-General
correct the mistakes of the previous leadership
of the Organization and ask the Secretariat to revise the “methodology” in cooperation with Member States, to ensure that it follows the decisions of UNESCO’s Governing bodies, and
present it to the next session
of the IPDC Intergovernmental Council in 2028.
We call on the Director-General to condemn
the killing of each journalist whose case has been reported to his attention by Member States since he took office.
In conclusion, I would like to stress that crimes against journalists
have no time limits
. Journalists killed during armed conflicts are civilians, with all the legal consequences that follow from this status.
🇷🇺
Russian Federation will not give up its efforts to ensure that
all the killed journalists are properly included in UNESCO’s statistics
and their killings are duly condemned.
Thank you for attention.
