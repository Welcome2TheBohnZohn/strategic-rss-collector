Comment by the Permanent Mission of Russia to the EU, 2 September 2026
On 2 September 2026, Chargé d'Affaires a.i. of the Permanent Mission of Russia to the EU and Euratom Karen Malayan was summoned to the European External Action Service (EEAS) in connection with claims by the German Government of Russia’s alleged involvement in the “incident” involving a UAV at Leipzig Airport on the night of 4–5 August 2026.
Against the backdrop of a
new wave of anti-Russian information campaign that is fuelled in EU Member States at the instigation of Berlin
, the EU “strongly condemned” the afore-mentioned drone “incident”, expressed its “full solidarity” with Germany and confirmed its plans to take “appropriate measures” against Moscow. It was also reiterated that the
EU intends to continue providing comprehensive support to Kiev
.
☝
The Head of the Russian Mission to the EU
firmly rejected the new unfounded allegations of the EU
. He noted that the new drone provocation, this time in Leipzig, was intended to
divert the attention of the Europeans from the increasingly deteriorating socio-economic situation in the EU and the crisis of confidence in relation to its political elites, particularly in Germany.
❗
In light of the public remarks made by EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas in Ireland on 2 September 2026, at an informal meeting of EU Foreign Affairs Ministers, regarding the alleged existence of “signs of state-sponsored terrorism” in relation to Russia,
it was explicitly pointed out to the EU that the Kiev regime is of terrorist nature
. Drawing on the thematic reports for July–August 2026 by Ambassador-at-Large of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs Rodion Miroshnik,
Karen Malayan resolutely condemned the crimes committed by the Armed Forces of Ukraine, that have resulted in numerous civilian casualties in our countr
y, as well as in the destruction of Russian civilian infrastructure. It was emphasized that the
actions of terrorists, their accomplices and sponsors will not be left without due legal consequences.
