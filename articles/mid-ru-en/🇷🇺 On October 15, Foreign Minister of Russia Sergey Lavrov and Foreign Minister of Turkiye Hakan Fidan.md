On October 15, Foreign Minister of Russia Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Turkiye Hakan Fidan
held a meeting
on the sidelines of
the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia
(
#CICA
)
Summit
in Baku
.
The Ministers exchanged views on
current priorities for strengthening trade, economic and energy cooperation
, with a focus on progress in implementing key bilateral projects.
They reaffirmed
the importance of coordination between Russia and Turkiye on the international stage
, including efforts to promote a just settlement of the crisis surrounding Ukraine.
The Ministers also reviewed
the schedule of upcoming contacts
at the highest and other senior levels.
