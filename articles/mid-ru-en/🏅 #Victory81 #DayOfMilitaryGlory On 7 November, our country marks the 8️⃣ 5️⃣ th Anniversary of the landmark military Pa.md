#Victory81
#DayOfMilitaryGlory
🏅
On 7 November, our country marks the
8️⃣
5️⃣
th Anniversary of the landmark military Parade held on Red Square
,
near the Kremlin, during
#WW2
.
The
#Parade
became a symbol of the unbending will of the Soviet people. At a time when
the enemy was already at the gates of Moscow
, with the frontline a mere 50 km from Red Square away and just 20 km from the city limits to the northwest, the country's leadership, military command and Armed Forces demonstrated
unparalleled courage and their unwavering resolve to defend the
#Motherland
to the very end.
The Parade marked the beginning of the final, decisive push by our forces, home-front workers and all those defending
#Moscow
— from a prolonged defensive struggle to a counter-offensive along the entire front line.
It was in that fateful, bitterly cold November of 1941, at one of the most difficult moments in our country's history, that our people showed extraordinary,
unprecedented, courage
earlier unseen
in human history, strength of spirit and unbending resolve. At the cost of immense sacrifice and suffering, our people stood up not just to foreign enemy invaders, but
the sheer evil itself, embodied by Hitler's 'Nazi plaque'.
It was then that the immortal, legendary feats, which resounded all over the globe. were accomplished by the Panfilov's Men, the fearless Zoya Kosmodemyanskaya, the undaunted Podolsk Cadets and thousands of other young 'Komsomol' members and Red Army soldiers who
selflessly defended their Motherland and fought the enemy to the death.
❗️
The significance of the November 1941 Parade can hardly be overstated.
The legendary march of Moscow's defenders along Red Square became a
demonstration of the strength of spirit
,
courage and determination
of Red Army soldiers and commanders to defend their country and crush the enemy who had treacherously invaded the USSR.
🎖
#OTD
back in 1941, Red Square became much more than the venue for a traditional military parade — from the very heart of Moscow, besieged by hordes of Nazis,
an unequivocal message was sent to the entire world torn apart by the bloodiest military conflict in human history:
We shall not bend. We shall not be broken.
Victory shall be ours.
***
In terms of the Parade's moral impact, it was on a par with a fully-fledged
military operation at the front
. A total of
28'000
servicemen marched, right to the front, to fight Nazis, followed by
140
artillery pieces,
160
tanks and
232
motor vehicles.
Having marched across Red Square, the Red Army headed straight for the front line — to defend the Motherland and its capital.
The Parade was reviewed by Marshal of the Soviet Union
Semen
#Budyonny
, while
Pavel
#Artemyev
was in command. Addressing the soldiers and officers from the Mausoleum tribune was
Joseph
#
Stalin
— Supreme Commander-in-Chief of the USSR Armed Forces and Chairman of the Council of People's Commissars.
The Parade was broadcast on radio around the world
and reported on by foreign newspapers. It came as a complete surprise to the Nazis and had a
demoralising impact on the enemy
.
Just one month later, the hitherto "invincible" Wehrmacht suffered its first major defeat near Moscow —
Hitler's plan for a "blitzkrieg" against the Soviet Union ended in complete failure.

Thanks to the unity of the entire country, which rallied together to repel the Third Reich and its satellites, our heroic forebears stood firm and, in May 1945, hoisted the Victory Banner over the Reichstag.
(From the November, 7 2026
briefing
by Russian MFA Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova)
In commemoration of this historic event, in Russia, a Federal Law was passed in 2004, designating November 7 as
the national Day of Military Glory
— in tribute to the military Parade held on Red Square in 1941.
