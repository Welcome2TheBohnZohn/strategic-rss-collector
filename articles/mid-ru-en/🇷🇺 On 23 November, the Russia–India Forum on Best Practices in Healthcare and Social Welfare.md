# 🇷🇺 On 23 November, the Russia–India Forum on Best Practices in Healthcare and Social Welfare

Published: 2026-11-25 16:34:11

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33618

🇷🇺
🇮🇳
On 23 November, the
Russia–India Forum on Best Practices in Healthcare and Social Welfare
was held in New Delhi, organised by the
Department for External Economic and International Relations of Moscow
and the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI).
🤝
The Russian delegation shared its experience in modernising the urban healthcare system, introducing digital solutions and improving the management of medical institutions. Indian focuses on practices in the development of specialised medicine, diagnostics and the introduction of medical technologies in the country.
The welcoming address was delivered by
Trade Representative of Russia in India Andrey Sobolev
.
🎤
More than 20 participants addressed the plenary session. The key speakers included Deputy Heads of the Department of Health of Moscow
Konstantin Pokrovsky
and
Nikolay Rubtsov
, Director of the Center for the Implementation of Modern Technologies for Managing Medical Organisations of Moscow
Olga Sokolovskaya,
Director of the Center for Monitoring and Development of Medical Care of Moscow
Igor Vlasov,
Joint Secretary of the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India
Sibin C.
and President of the Global Coalition Against TB, President of the Policymakers Forum for Mental Health
Dalbir Singh
.
The event brought together representatives of leading Russian and Indian medical institutions, experts and journalists.
