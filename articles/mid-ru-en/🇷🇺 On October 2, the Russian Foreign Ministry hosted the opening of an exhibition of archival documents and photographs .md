On October 2, the Russian Foreign Ministry
hosted the opening
of
an exhibition of archival documents and photographs marking the 35th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Russia and Brunei
.
In their welcoming remarks, Russia’s Deputy Foreign Minister
Andrey Rudenko
and Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of Brunei Darussalam to Russia
Husaini Alauddin
reaffirmed their countries’ commitment to
further strengthening bilateral cooperation in a spirit of friendship, trust and mutual respect
.
The Sides expressed their
determination to expand political dialogue and deepen ties
in defence and security, trade and humanitarian exchanges. They also noted that
Russia and Brunei share identical or similar positions on key international and regional issues
.
The event was also attended by Heads of diplomatic missions of other
#ASEAN
member states in Moscow, representatives of Russian ministries and agencies, civil society and academia, and journalists.
