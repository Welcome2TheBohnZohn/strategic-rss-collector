On August 27, continuing a good tradition, the staff of the Embassy of Russia visited
Bophelong Children’s Home
in Mamelodi, Pretoria.
The children were treated to traditional Russian sweets, including blini (Russian pancakes) and khvorost, and received a selection of gifts from the Embassy: sporting equipment, school and arts-and-crafts supplies, hygiene products and sweets.
The children were also presented with Russian caps featuring Russian symbols - a particularly practical gift as the South African summer approaches.
Addressing the children and staff of the Home,
Ambassador of Russia to South Africa
Roman Ambarov
stressed the importance of sport, teamwork and perseverance:
“
Among the items we are donating are arts and crafts supplies and sporting equipment, including footballs. These soccer balls are more than sporting equipment - they are symbols of teamwork, discipline and perseverance.
South Africans recently had every reason to celebrate the remarkable performance of Bafana Bafana at the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The team’s success inspired millions across the country and showed what can be achieved through hard work, unity and belief in one another. Perhaps one of these footballs will one day be kicked by a future Bafana Bafana player.”
For the Embassy, such visits are not about symbolic gestures or formal occasions.
They are about sincere care, human warmth and the friendship we wish to share with our South African friends.
As Nelson Mandela once said:
“There can be no keener revelation of a society’s soul than the way in which it treats its children.”
And sometimes, friendship is expressed in the simplest things: playing games together, sharing a cup of tea, talking about the little things — and, most importantly, knowing that you are not forgotten.
