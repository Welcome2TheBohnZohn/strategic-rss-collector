On September 30, 2022, following
referendums
, the Donetsk and Lugansk People’s Republics and the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions officially became part of the Russian Federation.
✍️
Looking back at #HowItHappened
(Signing of the treaties on the accession of the DPR, LPR, Zaporozhye and Kherson regions to Russia)
In the
referendums
held on
September 23-27, 2022
, residents of the four regions made a historic decision that determined their future.
International observers
from Italy, Germany, Venezuela, Latvia and other countries
(133 in total)
who monitored the voting
recognised the results as legitimate.
In 2014
, the Kiev regime unleashed
full-scale hostilities against the civilian population of Donbass
, while neo-Nazis and radicals intimidated and attacked people in Zaporozhye and other regions as well. For eight long years, people endured relentless shelling, terrorist attacks and persecution.
Despite shelling and numerous provocations by the Kiev regime,
people went to the polls and expressed their will.
🗳
Four years ago, the people voted in favour of joining Russia:
•
DPR – 99.23%
, with a turnout of 97.5%;
•
LPR – 98.42%
, with a turnout of 92.6%;
•
Zaporozhye Region – 93.11%
, with a turnout of 85.4%;
•
Kherson Region – 87.05%
, with a turnout of 76.9%.
Behind these figures are the lives of millions of people
and their hopes for safety, for the freedom to speak their native language,
to honour their ancestors
– true
heroes of the Great Patriotic War
(instead of
Hitler’s henchmen
) – to preserve their culture and raise their children without fearing for their lives.
❗️
Russia’s position remains unchanged:
the people of Donbass and Novorossiya exercised their
legitimate right to self-determination
, enshrined in
the UN Charter
,
the 1966 International Covenants on Human Rights
and
the 1975 CSCE Helsinki Final Act
.

Sergey Lavrov’s
address

#TOGETHER
