# ✉️ On September 30, Vladimir Putin sent greetings to President of the Republic of Abkhazia Badra Gunba on the occasion of Victory and Independence Day.

Published: 2026-09-30 11:01:15

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32465

✉️
On September 30, Vladimir Putin
sent greetings
to President of the Republic of Abkhazia Badra Gunba on the occasion of Victory and Independence Day.
✍️
The message
reads, in part:
“Please accept my heartfelt congratulations on Victory and Independence Day. This glorious holiday symbolises
the resilience, courage, and unity
of the people of Abkhazia, who upheld their legitimate right to a free and peaceful life.
Relations between Russia and Abkhazia are marked by
friendship and alliance
. I am confident that they will continue to strengthen, for the benefit of our peoples and in the interests of ensuring regional stability and security.
Russia will continue to assist its Abkhaz partners
in every way in addressing key tasks relating to state-building and socioeconomic development”.
Russia's President wished the Leader of Abkhazia sound health and every success, and to all citizens of the Republic, happiness and well-being.
