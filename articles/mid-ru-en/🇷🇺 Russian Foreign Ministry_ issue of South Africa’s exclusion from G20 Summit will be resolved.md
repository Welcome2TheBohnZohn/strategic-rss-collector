# 🇷🇺 Russian Foreign Ministry: issue of South Africa’s exclusion from G20 Summit will be resolved

Published: 2026-08-29 16:00:02

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31677

🇷🇺
🇿🇦
Russian Foreign Ministry: issue of South Africa’s exclusion from
#
G20
Summit will be resolved
Russian Ambassador-at-Large
Marat Berdyev
has described South Africa’s exclusion from the upcoming
#G20
Summit as an “egregious violation” and expressed confidence that the issue will be resolved promptly.
South Africa’s exclusion is an egregious violation. The G20 is a collegial format that cannot be usurped by anyone,” Berdyev told TASS on the sidelines of the third meeting of APEC Senior Officials in Dalian, China.
We have no doubt that, ultimately, the issue will be resolved.
We are working with our partners on additional steps to ensure that Pretoria takes its rightful place at the negotiating table as soon as possible.
☝️
The Russian diplomat noted that Russia, like the majority of G20 members, has made its position clear:
“South Africa should return to the fold as soon as possible.”
Any restrictions in this regard are “unacceptable and only undermine the working process.”
📍
The G20 Leaders’ Summit is scheduled to take place on
14-15 December in Doral, near Miami, Florida
.
