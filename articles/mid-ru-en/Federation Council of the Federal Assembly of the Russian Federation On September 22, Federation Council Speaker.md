On September 22, Federation Council Speaker
Valentina Matviyenko
met with President of the Senate of Zimbabwe
Mabel Chinomona
.
Russia's Federation Council Speaker
thanked Zimbabwean parliamentarians
for monitoring
the State Duma elections
.
💬
Valentina Matviyenko noted that
the traditionally friendly relations between Russia and Zimbabwe
date back to
Zimbabwe’s struggle for independence
:
It is gratifying that Zimbabwe remembers the support our country provided during its difficult struggle for national liberation. We stand ready to continue working closely together to combat
neocolonialism
.
💬
Russia's Federation Council Speaker emphasised that
our countries share common views
on the contemporary world order:
We oppose interference in the affairs of sovereign states and uphold the leading role of the UN and the primacy of international law.
