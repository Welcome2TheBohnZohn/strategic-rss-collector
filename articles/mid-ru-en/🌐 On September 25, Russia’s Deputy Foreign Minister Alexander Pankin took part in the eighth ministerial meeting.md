# 🌐 On September 25, Russia’s Deputy Foreign Minister Alexander Pankin took part in the eighth ministerial meeting

Published: 2026-09-26 12:05:28

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32382

🌐
On September 25, Russia’s Deputy Foreign Minister
Alexander Pankin
took part in
the eighth ministerial meeting
of
the Group of Friends in Defence of the UN Charter
, held on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
In his remarks, Alexander Pankin highlighted
the Group’s vital role in upholding the principles of
the UN’s founding document
,
advancing the legitimate aspirations of the Global South and East, and helping build a truly
#MultipolarWorld
order.
He noted the Group’s effective efforts in key areas such as
combating colonialism and its modern manifestations, opposing unilateral coercive measures and preventing the politicisation of human rights
. In this context, he stressed the importance of the Group putting forward
a new anti-colonial resolution
to further consolidate this issue’s place on the UN agenda.
🤝
The Group’s members emphasised
the importance of strengthening
coordination
,
broadening
the thematic and geographical scope of its activities, and
improving
its working methods.
They also discussed
priority and promising areas
of the Group’s work for the coming year.
