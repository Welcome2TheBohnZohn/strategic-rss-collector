# 🏰 On September 11, 1812, the Russian settlement of Fort Ross was established on the northwestern coast of the continental United States.

🏰
On September 11, 1812, the Russian settlement of
Fort Ross
was established on the northwestern coast of the continental United States.
It was
the first European settlement
and a fortress on the coast of Northern California located 80 km north of San Francisco. It marked
the southernmost point in North America
reached by Russian pioneers.
In the 18th century, Russian industrialists, hunters and farmers began exploring Alaska and the Aleutian Islands. Settlements began to spring up all along America’s northwestern coast. These places would later become known as Russian America.
🧭
The harsh climate of Alaska made farming difficult, and food shortages began to be felt. To address this problem,
Alexander Baranov
(1790-1818), the first chief administrator of the Russian settlements in America, sent several exploratory expeditions led by his closest aide and associate
Ivan Kuskov
(1765–1823) to California.
Ivan Kuskov spotted a plateau 30 km north of Rumyantsev Bay (present-day Bodega Bay), which was separated from the surrounding area by deep ravines and surrounded by valuable timber forests and grazing land. Kuskov purchased the plot from the Native Americans for three blankets, three pairs of trousers, two axes, three hoes, and several strings of beads. He named the nearby river the Slavyanka (the Russian River).
🇷🇺
From March to September 1812, construction work was carried out on the newly acquired land, and a fortress and a settlement were built. On August 30 (September 11), the flag of the Russian-American Company –
the Russian tricolour
bearing the imperial double-headed eagle - was raised at Fort Ross.
In a short time, orchards and vegetable gardens were planted, and farmers began raising livestock and poultry. Our ancestors hunted sea otters and sea lions that inhabited the coastal waters. Fort Ross thus became
the main source of food supplies
for the Russian settlements in Alaska.
Maritime industries also developed. The fortress had a shipyard and a boatbuilding workshop. California’s windmills and shipyards
appeared first
at Fort Ross.
The population was ethnically diverse: Russians lived side by side with Native Americans and Aleuts. The settlers not only got along with the local population, but also provided them with
basic education
and
professional skills
, and many Native Americans at Fort Ross became carpenters, blacksmiths, and shipbuilders.
☦️
The fortress was also home to
California’s first Orthodox church building
, namely,
the Chapel of the Holy Trinity
, which remains an important historical, cultural, and religious centre for San Francisco’s Russian-speaking community.
By 1841, the food supply problem in Alaska had been resolved by other means, and Fort Ross was deemed operating at a loss. The colony’s lands were sold to the “Emperor of California,” American businessman and landowner
John Sutter
. The last Russian settlers returned home in 1847, and in 1867 the United States purchased Alaska.
🕰
Today, a museum dedicated to the history of the Russian colony and the daily life and traditions of its settlers is open on the site of the former fortress. It also displays a replica of the flag presented by
Emperor Paul I
to the Russian-American Company. Located on the picturesque Pacific coast, Fort Ross attracts visitors from around the world.
These historic sites also inspired the plot of the famous opera
“Juno and Avos”
by
Alexey Rybnikov
, inspiring future generations to create artistic works, including the well-known
portrait of Concepción (Conchita) Argüello
by Russian artist
Anatoly Sokolov
, which was donated by the artist’s family to the State Historical Museum in Moscow in September 2001.
This year, the Fort Ross Historical Park hosted a
Russian cultural heritage festival
for the first time in six years
. The programme included performances of folk songs, Russian food tasting, and demonstrations of traditional crafts, as well as a traditional Russian round dance to the music played on ancient instruments.
