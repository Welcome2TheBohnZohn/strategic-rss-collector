Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
statement
on the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes against Journalists
(
November 2, 2026)
💬
November 2 marks
the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes against Journalists
,
established in 2013
by a
#UN
General Assembly resolution.
This observance aims to draw global attention to
the need to protect journalists as they carry out their professional duties
and to reinforce the principle that
atrocities committed against them must inevitably be punished
.
❗️
We have to acknowledge that the actual situation regarding journalists’ safety has not improved and
continues to steadily deteriorate.
Over these years,
the media landscape has turned into a full-fledged theatre of war
, where the countries of the “collective West”, clinging to their former hegemony, use every means available to suppress dissent and impose on the
#GlobalMajority
the “only correct” view of world events.
Repressive actions by the West and its protégés
in
Ukraine
,
Moldova
and t
he Baltic states
against Russian media outlets and their staff have still not received an adequate response from the relevant international organisations –
the UN Human Rights Office
,
UNESCO
,
the OSCE
and others.
Emboldened by unchecked impunity, the Kiev regime is openly conducting a relentless hunt for members of the Russian media community.
War correspondents on the front line know better than anyone that
the blue “PRESS” patch
on a bulletproof vest, which once offered protection against targeted fire, has become
a convenient target for Ukrainian Banderites
– visible from afar and used to direct deliberate attacks.
Such attacks constitute
war crimes
under
international humanitarian law
, yet the relevant international bodies fail to direct criticism at those responsible.

Another regrettable consequence of the toxic politicisation of journalists’ protection is that many of the Russian correspondents named above were
once again omitted
from the newly published
UNESCO Director-General’s Report on the Safety of Journalists for 2024-2025

Although, at our insistence, a link to official Russian data on all such crimes was finally included in an annex to the report

We consider it necessary to recall that
the 1997 General Conference resolution
and
the 2023 Executive Board decision
require UNESCO’s leadership
to condemn the killing of journalists and acts of physical violence
against them without distinction.
