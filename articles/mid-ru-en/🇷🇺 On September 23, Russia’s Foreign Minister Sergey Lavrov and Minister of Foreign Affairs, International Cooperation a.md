🇪🇬
On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Minister of Foreign Affairs, International Cooperation and Egyptian Expatriates Badr Abdelatty
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly.
The Ministers reviewed
key issues of multifaceted Russia-Egypt cooperation
, focusing on maintaining the strong momentum of political dialogue, expanding trade and economic cooperation, and implementing major bilateral infrastructure projects. They also discussed preparations for
upcoming joint events
.
They held
an in-depth exchange of views on the crisis in the Persian Gulf
caused by
the unprovoked US and Israeli aggression against Iran
. Both Sides stressed
the importance of restoring a lasting and sustainable peace in the region through political and diplomatic means
.
🤝
The Ministers reaffirmed the need for and continued relevance of
close foreign policy coordination between Moscow and Cairo
, including at the UN and in other international venues.
