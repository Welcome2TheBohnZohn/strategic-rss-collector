On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Minister of Foreign and CARICOM Affairs of Trinidad and Tobago Sean Sobers
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Ministers exchanged views on
key global issues and ways to expand bilateral cooperation in areas of mutual interest
, as well as cooperation at the UN and within
the Gas Exporting Countries Forum
.
Following
Trinidad and Tobago’s election
as
a non-permanent member of the UN Security Council for 2027-2028
, the Russian Side expressed its
readiness for productive dialogue
aimed at finding collective responses to threats to international peace and security.
