# 🇷🇺 #Announcement On October 8-9, President of Russia Vladimir Putin will pay a working visit to Turkmenistan

Published: 2026-10-06 19:02:57

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32593

#Announcement
🇷🇺
🇹🇲
On October 8-9, President of Russia Vladimir Putin will pay
a working visit to Turkmenistan
.
The Russian President will take part in
a meeting of the CIS Council of Heads of State
and
the Caspian Environmental Summit
, both to be held in Turkmenistan’s Avaza National Tourist Zone.
At the meeting of the CIS Council of Heads of State, the Leaders will
review cooperation
among the member states as the Commonwealth marks its 35th anniversary this year,
set priorities
for further joint work and
exchange views
on current international and regional issues.
✍️
Documents are expected to be adopted
to expand cooperation in law enforcement, culture, humanitarian affairs and information, as well as to strengthen foreign policy coordination among the CIS countries.
At the Caspian Environmental Summit, the Presidents of Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan will discuss
practical aspects of environmental cooperation among the Caspian littoral states
, including measures to prevent the decline in the Caspian Sea’s water level and preserve its biological resources.
🤝
During his visit to Turkmenistan, Vladimir Putin will hold
a series of bilateral meetings
.
