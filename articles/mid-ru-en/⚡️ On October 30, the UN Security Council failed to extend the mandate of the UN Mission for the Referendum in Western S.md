# ⚡️ On October 30, the UN Security Council failed to extend the mandate of the UN Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara

Published: 2026-11-03 12:59:54

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33207

⚡️
On October 30, the UN Security Council
failed to extend the mandate
of
the UN Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara
(
#MINURSO
).
❌
The Russian delegation was compelled
to vote
against
the relevant draft resolution prepared by the US.
China, Pakistan and Somalia
abstained
.
Apparently, the authors assumed that we would not block the extension of a UN operation headed by a Russian national and included
provisions unacceptable to us and several other UN Security Council members
.
These provisions sought to enshrine a single option for resolving the Western Sahara issue, denying the people of Western Sahara
the right to choose their own future
and that of
the land they have inhabited since the end of Spanish colonial rule in North Africa
.
👉
Such an outcome would run counter to the principles and purposes of
the UN Charter
, including
the principle of self-determination
.
The Russian Federation does not support
solutions imposed from outside through ultimatums. Any plans for a Western Sahara settlement must be developed
through negotiations
aimed at reaching
mutually acceptable agreements
, based on the relevant UN Security Council resolutions.
We are
disappointed
that US diplomats failed to distinguish between their own national position recognising Western Sahara as part of Morocco and their role as the informal lead on the Western Sahara file in the UN Security Council, which
requires them to act as an “honest broker”
.
Recognising the importance of MINURSO’s work, we submitted
an alternative short draft resolution
to the Security Council that
would have extended the Mission’s mandate for one year without any changes
, including to the parameters of the Western Sahara settlement. Unfortunately, only China and Somalia supported our draft.
The US voted against it
, while the remaining members abstained.
☝️
As a result, our proposal was not adopted.
We believe that
full responsibility
for winding down the UN presence in Western Sahara, which has long had a stabilising effect on the situation in the region,
lies with the US and the countries that sided with it.
