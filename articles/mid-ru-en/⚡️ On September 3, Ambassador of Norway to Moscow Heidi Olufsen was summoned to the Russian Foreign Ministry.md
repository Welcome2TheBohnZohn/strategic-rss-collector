# ⚡️ On September 3, Ambassador of Norway to Moscow Heidi Olufsen was summoned to the Russian Foreign Ministry

Published: 2026-09-04 07:20:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31831

⚡️
On September 3, Ambassador of Norway to Moscow Heidi Olufsen
was summoned
to the Russian Foreign Ministry
.
The Ambassador was issued a strong protest
over
the Norwegian authorities’ September 2 arrest of the Russian research vessel Professor Molchanov
, which was in the port of the Russian settlement of Barentsburg on the Spitsbergen (Svalbard) archipelago.
The vessel, arrested at Ukraine’s request, belongs to the Northern Directorate for Hydrometeorology and Environmental Monitoring of
Roshydromet
. It was making
a scheduled voyage from Murmansk to the Russian settlement of Barentsburg
, transporting, among others, employees of
the Arktikugol Trust
and scientists. The Professor Molchanov also delivers supplies essential for the functioning of the Russian settlements of
Barentsburg
and
Pyramiden
.
Against the backdrop of Norway’s restrictive measures against Russia on Spitsbergen, introduced in violation of international law,
Russian organisations and citizens operating on the archipelago have effectively been placed under blockade
.
❗️
Such discrimination against Russians constitutes a direct violation by Norway of
the 1920 Svalbard Treaty
.
The Ambassador was told that the Russian Side categorically rejects
not only the arrest of the Professor Molchanov, but also Norway’s recently intensified actions aimed at restricting Russia’s lawful presence on the archipelago.
Moscow demands that Oslo unconditionally comply with international law
, including both the provisions and the spirit of
the Svalbard Treaty
.
☝️
The Russian Foreign Ministry and the relevant Russian authorities will seek the lifting of the vessel’s arrest.
The Russian Foreign Ministry
,
the Embassy of Russia in Norway
and
the Russian Consulate General in Barentsburg
remain in close contact
with representatives of Russian organisations, as well as with the Norwegian authorities.
The vessel’s crew and passengers are receiving the necessary
consular, legal and other assistance
.
