.
Despite the efforts of law enforcement authorities across South-East Asia, human trafficking for the subsequent involvement of victims in the activities of criminal organisations remains
one of the most serious transnational threats in the region
.
In this regard, we would once again urge Russian citizens to
exercise caution when approached by strangers seeking to establish contact via social networks and messenger
s, as well as when responding to online advertisements for highly paid positions in tourism, administration, translation and modelling.
☝️
At the same time, we would like to stress that travelling to South-East Asian countries, including Myanmar and Thailand, for tourism purposes does not in itself pose a threat, provided that the above-mentioned security rules are observed.
