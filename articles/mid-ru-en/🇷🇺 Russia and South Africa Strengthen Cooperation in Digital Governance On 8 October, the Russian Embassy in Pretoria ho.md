Russia and South Africa Strengthen Cooperation in Digital Governance
On 8 October, the Russian Embassy in Pretoria hosted the graduation ceremony for 43 South African public officials who completed the advanced training programme “
Digital Transformation of the Public Administration System
”, jointly organised by the Russian Presidential Academy of National Economy and Public Administration (RANEPA) and South Africa’s National School of Government.
🎙
Addressing the graduates, Russian Ambassador
Roman Ambarov
emphasised that education is not merely about developing skills, but a foundation for
sustainable national development, intellectual and technological sovereignty, and stronger people-to-people ties.
☝️
He reaffirmed
Russia’s commitment to educational cooperation based on equality, mutual respect and due regard for national priorities,
stressing the importance of translating the considerable human potential of both countries into practical outcomes for public administration.
The Ambassador also highlighted
RANEPA’s extensive cooperation with BRICS partners,
including more than 50 cooperation agreements with institutions across BRICS countries and over 400 students from these countries studying at the Academy.
The programme marks the first initiative between RANEPA and South Africa’s National School of Government, opening new opportunities for professional development, knowledge exchange and closer institutional cooperation.
“If we want to sustain a dynamic and forward-looking partnership between our countries, we must invest not only in institutions, but also in the people who will shape its present and future. Invest in you,”
Ambassador Ambarov told the graduates.
South African participants praised the programme’s relevance to the country’s public administration priorities, describing it as “
intensive and very effective.”
🤝
The initiative underscores the shared commitment of Russia and South Africa to developing expertise, advancing digital transformation and strengthening their strategic partnership through education.
