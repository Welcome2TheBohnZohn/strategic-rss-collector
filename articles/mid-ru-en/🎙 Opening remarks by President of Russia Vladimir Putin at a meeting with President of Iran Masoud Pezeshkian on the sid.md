Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Iran Masoud Pezeshkian on the sidelines of
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
(Moscow, October 27, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am pleased
to meet with you again
– this time on the sidelines of
the Gas Exporting Countries Forum
.
I believe today’s event was quite productive
. Our colleagues are also holding many bilateral meetings, which is very useful.
As you know, we have just discussed the situation in the region one-on-one.
It is certainly a cause for concern
, not only for us, but particularly for us as a country that works closely with Iran.
☝️
We are neighbours, we have many shared interests, and we are working to advance them all.
We hope the situation will develop in a positive direction.
For our part,
we are fulfilling all our commitments across all our joint projects
. I would like to note that
Russian-Iranian trade grew by 10.4%
in the first eight months of 2026. Construction of
the Bushehr nuclear power plant
continues.
We are also developing humanitarian ties
. For example, the number of Iranian students at our universities has approached
10,000
. In late September and early October, a series of vibrant events took place in Russian cities as part of
the Iranian Culture Festival
–
in Moscow, Kazan and Astrakhan
.
🤝
We are always pleased to welcome you to Moscow and to Russia
, Mr President, and we value the opportunity to discuss current issues and exchange views on our bilateral agenda.
