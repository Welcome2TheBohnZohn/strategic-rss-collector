# ✉️ Russia's President Vladimir Putin sent a message of congratulations to President of Turkmenistan Serdar Berdimuhamedov on the occasion of the country’s Independence Day

Published: 2026-09-27 11:00:25

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32413

✉️
Russia's President Vladimir Putin
sent a message of congratulations
to President of Turkmenistan Serdar Berdimuhamedov on the occasion of the country’s Independence Day
(September 27, 2026)
✍️
Under your leadership, Turkmenistan has been steadily generating positive momentum in advancing its social and economic development.
Ashgabat’s consistent commitment to
the policy of positive neutrality
has helped strengthen
security and stability in Central Asia
and the Caspian region.
🇷🇺
🇹🇲
Our two countries have forged
a deep strategic partnership
in their relations. Russia and Turkmenistan have been proactive in working together across various domains, and
have been
effectively cooperating
when addressing major international issues.
Of course,
we will maintain
our constructive dialogue and our joint efforts
to further strengthen our bilateral relations in all their aspects in the interests of our friendly nations.
🎉
I am sending you
my heartfelt wishes of good health and success
in performing your state duties, while wishing
happiness and prosperity
to all your compatriots.
