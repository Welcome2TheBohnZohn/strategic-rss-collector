On September 24, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Co-Foreign Minister of Nicaragua Valdrack Jaentschke
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Ministers praised
the momentum of political dialogue and the allied nature of cooperation between Moscow and Managua
on the international stage.
The Sides expressed their mutual readiness
to further expand the full range of bilateral ties
in the spirit of strategic partnership.
Particular attention was paid to
the situation in and around Nicaragua
in the context of the electoral reform process launched in the country.
Sergey Lavrov reaffirmed
Russia’s unwavering support
for the Nicaraguan Government’s efforts
to defend national sovereignty and the right of the brotherly Nicaraguan people to follow their own path of development.
