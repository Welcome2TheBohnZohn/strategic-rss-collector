International Festival of the Russian Language and Culture Underway in Islamabad
On November 23, the International Festival of the Russian Language kicked off at Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU), with the support of Ural State Pedagogical University (USPU) and the Embassy of Russia in Pakistan.
🎓
The opening ceremony was attended by representatives of AIOU and USPU, as well as
Gleb Shubin, Cultural Attaché of the Russian Embassy
,
Roman Porozov and Anton Dolgov,
faculty members of the Russian university.
🇷🇺
🤝
🇵🇰
Speakers highlighted the importance of cultural and educational initiatives in strengthening humanitarian ties between Russia and Pakistan.
📖
The first day of the festival was dedicated to the Russian language and its history.
USPU Associate Professor
Svetlana Eremina
delivered a lecture on linguistic contacts and borrowing among the peoples of Russia. She also highlighted similarities between certain Russian and Urdu words, including those related to culinary traditions.
The event also featured remarks by USPU administrators and faculty members, including
Acting Rector Margarita Kusova,
Director of the Institute of Philology and Intercultural Communication
Irina Semukhina
,
Professor Natalia Zavyalova
, and other university representatives.
🎭
The first day concluded with a lecture by Pakistani Professor Dr. Abdul Aziz Sahir titled
“Pushkin and Iqbal: A Dialogue Between Two National Poets.”
His presentation explored the literary legacies of the two great poets and their contributions to promoting humanist principles and values.
🎨
On the second day, USPU faculty members Roman Porozov and Anton Dolgov conducted a workshop titled
“The Richness of Russian Culture”
for primary school students at the Russian Open Education Center at AIOU.
The children learned about traditional Russian ornamental patterns and had the opportunity to try their hand at decorative arts and crafts.
🌐
The festival will continue through November 26, with events taking place at Allama Iqbal Open University and the Federal Urdu University of Arts, Science and Technology.
