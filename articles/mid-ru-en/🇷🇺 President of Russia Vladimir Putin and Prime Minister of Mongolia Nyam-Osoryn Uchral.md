# 🇷🇺 President of Russia Vladimir Putin and Prime Minister of Mongolia Nyam-Osoryn Uchral

Published: 2026-09-02 13:39:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31799

🇷🇺
🇲🇳
President of Russia Vladimir Putin and Prime Minister of Mongolia Nyam-Osoryn Uchral
held a meeting
on the sidelines of
the Eastern Economic Forum
(Vladivostok, September 2, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Welcome to
Vladivostok
. I am pleased to greet you and all our Mongolian friends.
You have already visited Russia this year
, when you served as Chairman of the State Great Khural. I know that during that visit you met with
the Speakers of the Upper House of the Russian Parliament
and
the State Duma
.
I am confident that the participation of the Mongolian delegation led by you in
the Eastern Economic Forum
will contribute to
further Russian-Mongolian cooperation
. Tomorrow, we will take part together in
the Forum’s Plenary Session
.
🤝
The traditionally friendly and good-neighbourly relations between our countries have reached the level of a comprehensive strategic partnership
. We have longstanding traditions of friendship and, without exaggeration, a special relationship.
We have maintained diplomatic relations for 105 years
, and our interstate ties were, in the fullest sense of the word, cemented by the blood of our ancestors. They fought together for Mongolia’s freedom and independence during
the Great Patriotic War
and
at Khalkhin Gol
.
The foundations of Mongolia’s economy were laid with the direct participation of our country.
Yesterday in Bishkek, on the sidelines of
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Summit
, as you know, we had
a substantive meeting with President of Mongolia Ukhnaagiin Khurelsukh
.
We discussed
the strong momentum in bilateral contacts
between ministries and agencies, political parties and public organisations, as well as
cooperation in science, culture, cross-border and interregional ties
.
📈
We also noted the work of the Intergovernmental Commission, which deals with the full range of practical cooperation issues.
We were pleased to note the growth in bilateral trade – by 4.5% in 2025 and by another 18.1% in the first half of 2026.
More than 70 regions of the Russian Federation maintain regular ties with Mongolian partners
, covering a wide range of areas, including the economy, culture, education, sports and youth exchanges.
