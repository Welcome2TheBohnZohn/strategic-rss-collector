# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's response to a media question

Published: 2026-09-09 06:03:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31939

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
response to a media question
regarding the IAEA Director General’s report “Nuclear Safety, Security and Safeguards in Ukraine”
(September 8, 2026)
❓
Question: Ahead of the IAEA Board of Governors session, which opened on September 7, the Agency published another report by the Director General entitled
“Nuclear Safety, Security and Safeguards in Ukraine”
. How would you comment on it?
💬
Maria Zakharova
: We regard this document as
politically biased and failing to reflect the actual situation
surrounding
the Zaporozhskaya Nuclear Power Plant
(
#ZNPP
).
This conclusion can be drawn from the very fact that
the report ignores that Europe’s largest nuclear power plant is Russian territory.
The document speaks of a
“serious deterioration of the situation”
at the ZNPP, while IAEA Director General
Rafael Grossi
continues
, with persistence that would be better applied elsewhere,
to avoid
directly acknowledging the Kiev regime’s responsibility for its
reckless attacks
on ZNPP infrastructure,
the killing of plant personnel
, psychological pressure on their families and loved ones,
attempts to disrupt the plant’s power supply
and other crimes.
The report also fails to mention
the disruption,
through the fault of the Zelensky regime
, of
Rafael Grossi’s planned August 20-21 visit
to the ZNPP and the nuclear workers’ city of Energodar
, which was intended to coincide with another rotation of IAEA Secretariat experts at the plant. The rotation itself also could not be carried out in August.
❗️
We expect the Agency’s Secretariat and leadership to take a more responsible approach to drafting such reports in the future.
