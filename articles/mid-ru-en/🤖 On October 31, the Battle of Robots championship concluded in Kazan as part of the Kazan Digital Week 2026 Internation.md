# 🤖 On October 31, the Battle of Robots championship concluded in Kazan as part of the Kazan Digital Week 2026 International Forum

Published: 2026-11-04 11:04:28

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33217

🤖
On October 31,
the Battle of Robots championship
concluded in Kazan as part of
the Kazan Digital Week 2026 International Forum
.
The final bouts took place at
the Kazan Expo International Exhibition Centre
. Teams showcased robots they had designed and built themselves, demonstrating their piloting skills in two weight categories –
up to 1.5 kg
and
up to 110 kg
.

In 2026, the championship brought together 144 teams from 11 countries
: Russia, Belarus, Brazil, India, Iran, China and Tunisia, as well as four countries represented for the first time – Saudi Arabia, the US, Chile and Ecuador.
#BattleOfRobots
is one of the most spectacular and fastest-growing competitions in the world of robotics
. Held annually since 2023, the championship promotes engineering, supports technical creativity and advances modern digital technologies.
Preparations for and the running of the championship are coordinated by
the Russian Ministry of Digital Development
,
Communications and Mass Media
. The final stage was held with support from
the Government of the Republic of Tatarstan
. The championship is operated by the Engineers and Robots autonomous non-profit organisation. Its general technology partner is
VK
, and its general industrial partner is
X Holding
.
