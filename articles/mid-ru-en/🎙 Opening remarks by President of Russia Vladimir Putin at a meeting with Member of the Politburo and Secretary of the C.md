🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with Member of the Politburo and Secretary of the Central Committee of the Workers’ Party of Korea Jo Yong-won
(Moscow, November 16, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Comrade Secretary, dear friends, I am very pleased to see you!
First of all, I would like to convey
my very best wishes
to President of the State Affairs of the DPRK
Comrade Kim Jong-un
. I greatly value
the businesslike and friendly relationship
we have developed, which undoubtedly helps us address issues and advance relations between our countries.
Your delegation has come to Moscow to take part in
the Forum of Supporters of the Struggle Against Modern Practices of Neocolonialism “For the Freedom of Nations”
.
You have a busy programme in Moscow
, including meetings with the leadership of
the United Russia party
,
Chairman of the State Duma
and, tomorrow,
Speaker of the Federation Council
. I wish you every success in your work.
🇷🇺
🇰🇵
For many decades, our countries have been bound by strong friendship, good-neighbourly relations and brotherhood in arms.
The Treaty on Comprehensive Strategic Partnership
is in force.
Last year, we marked
several anniversaries
:
Victory in the Great Patriotic War
,
the liberation of Korea
,
victory over militarist Japan
and
the end of World War II
, as well as the founding of the Workers’ Party of Korea.
⚔️
The participation of Korean People’s Army units
in
the liberation of the Kursk Region
has opened a new chapter in our countries’ brotherhood in arms.
We will never forget the heroism of the Korean soldiers
who fought shoulder to shoulder with Russian servicemen.
By the end of this year,
we plan to unveil a sculptural monument in Kursk
honouring the shared feat of the soldiers of Russia and the DPRK.
In addition, Korean military engineers and construction units have made
an important contribution
to restoring infrastructure and bringing peaceful life back to the Kursk Region.
We greatly appreciate this.
🤝
Once again, please convey
my very best wishes and
friendly greetings
to President of the State Affairs
Comrade Kim Jong-un
.
