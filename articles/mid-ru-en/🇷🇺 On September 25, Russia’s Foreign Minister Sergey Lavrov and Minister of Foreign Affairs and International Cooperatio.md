On September 25, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Republic of Sudan Mohieldin Salem Ahmed Ibrahim
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The discussion focused on
the situation in Sudan
in the context of the ongoing armed confrontation between the regular army and the rebel Rapid Support Forces.
Mohieldin Salem Ahmed Ibrahim
thanked Sergey Lavrov for Russia’s principled position
in support of Sudan’s unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity.
The Sides praised their
close bilateral foreign policy coordination
at the UN.
They also discussed
further strengthening Russia-Sudan relations
, with an emphasis on expanding trade, economic and humanitarian cooperation.
