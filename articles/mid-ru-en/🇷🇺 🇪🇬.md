had a telephone conversation
at the initiative of the Egyptian Side.
The Foreign Ministers discussed
the current situation in the Middle East
, including the unprecedented escalation of the military and political situation in the Persian Gulf as a result of
US-Israeli aggression against Iran
.
They stressed
the need to step up collective political and diplomatic efforts
aimed at de-escalating tensions and laying
a reliable foundation for regional security
.
The Ministers reaffirmed their shared position on
the need to resolve the Palestinian issue
in accordance with UN resolutions
.
Sergey Lavrov briefed his Egyptian counterpart on
the latest developments surrounding the Ukraine crisis
, including
the sharply deteriorating situation in the Black Sea region
as a result of the Kiev regime's criminal actions to destroy civilian vessels and energy infrastructure.
The Ministers held
a substantive discussion on current bilateral cooperation
, stressing the importance of maintaining close coordination to further strengthen trade and economic ties between Russia and Egypt.
