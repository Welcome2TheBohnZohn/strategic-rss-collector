# ⚡️ Statement by the Russian Ice Hockey Federation (November 25, 2026) The Russian Ice Hockey Federation (RIHF) acknowledges the statement by the International Ice Hockey Federation

Published: 2026-11-26 07:03:50

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33652

⚡️
Statement by the Russian Ice Hockey Federation
(November 25, 2026)
:
The Russian Ice Hockey Federation (RIHF) acknowledges the statement by the International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF) regarding its intention to appeal the decision of the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) to the Swiss Federal Tribunal.
The actions of the
IIHF once again demonstrate a lack of intent to respect and comply with the ruling of an international arbitration body
, as well as the agreement concluded with the RIHF.
The RIHF notes that filing an appeal does not take away the IIHF’s obligation to comply with the decision. It is evident that the IIHF is failing to fulfill this obligation, which, in our view, violates not only the ruling itself, but also the law.
The RIHF considers the arguments presented in the IIHF’s press release for challenging the CAS decision to be
unfounded and contrived.
Furthermore, the RIHF maintains that the IIHF’s actions are
violating the agreement
with the RIHF with regard to the immediate implementation of the decision.
The CAS ruling in favour of the RIHF was reached
unanimously,
with the concurrence of the arbitrator appointed by the IIHF. Earlier this May, a three-member panel of the IIHF Disciplinary Board had likewise determined that the banning of Russian teams from international competitions was both
unfounded and unlawful.
Throughout the entire arbitration process, the IIHF raised no objections concerning the independence or impartiality of the arbitrators. It only submitted a request to CAS to disqualify two of the arbitrators two weeks after the unfavourable ruling was issued - a move which violates fundamental procedural rules and is based upon the same contrived arguments. Moreover, the information that the IIHF cites to substantiate its baseless accusations was already publicly available well before the arbitrators were even appointed to hear this case. CAS rightly and lawfully refused to consider this untenable argument provided by the IIHF.
❗️
In light of the above, the RIHF considers the arguments presented by the IIHF in its press release - put forward in order to justify its intention to appeal the CAS decision - to be legally baseless.
The entire sporting community can see that the IIHF is resorting to any means necessary, including the abuse of legal rights, in an attempt to cast doubt on the legitimacy of a final and binding arbitral decision, which was unanimously rendered in favour of the RIHF. These actions represent an attempt to evade compliance with the arbitral decision and to discredit the institution of independent sporting justice.
The RIHF
will continue to consistently and resolutely uphold the principles of Olympism and non-discrimination in sport
, and will defend the rights and legitimate interests of Russian athletes while using all available legal routes.
