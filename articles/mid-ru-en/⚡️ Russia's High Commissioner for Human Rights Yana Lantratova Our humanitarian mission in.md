⚡️
Russia's High Commissioner for Human Rights Yana Lantratova
:
❗️
Our humanitarian mission in
#Alyoshki
has concluded
.
The convoys are now heading to
their
respective destinations
.
Those who decided
to leave for Ukraine
have been given the opportunity to do so. We will provide accommodation and the necessary support to residents
staying in Russia
.
We
delivered
all the essential supplies and arranged safe passage out of the city
. However, people still have much to resolve – medical treatment, housing, documents and reunification with their loved ones. We will help them. We also remain in contact with those who stayed in Alyoshki to keep track of their further needs.
***
❗️
I propose
making humanitarian missions coordinated by US First Lady Melania Trump a regular practice
.
Our experience in Alyoshki
and our joint work with my counterpart Dmitry Lubinets have shown that
such agreements make it possible to help people even in the most difficult conditions
.
It is important to continue this work
wherever civilians are waiting for support.
I thank
President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
,
our Defence Ministry
, the relevant services and our military personnel for helping carry out the mission and ensuring people’s safety.
🙏
Special thanks go to Melania Trump for her assistance and coordination.
