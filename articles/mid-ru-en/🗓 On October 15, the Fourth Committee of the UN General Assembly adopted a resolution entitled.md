On October 15,
the Fourth Committee
of the UN General Assembly
adopted
a resolution entitled
“Addressing Contemporary Forms of Colonialism”
.
The draft was prepared by
the Group of Friends in Defence of the UN Charter
, with Russia’s active participation.
19 states co-sponsored the resolution
.
🌐
The resolution is non-confrontational and unifying in nature.
It stresses the need to eradicate neocolonial practices
, including interference in internal affairs, unilateral coercive measures, the imposition of unfair international treaties and restrictions on technological development.
These practices are among
the most serious obstacles to achieving the purposes and principles of
the UN Charter
and are a root cause of numerous problems on the UN agenda.
✅
The document was supported by 85 Member States,
with 49 voting against and 23 abstaining.
Significantly, its supporters included
#BRICS
countries
and states across
Africa
,
Asia
and
Latin America
, while its opponents were predominantly countries of the “collective West”, many of them
former colonial powers
. The latter refused to support the document, despite having participated in drafting it and having some of their amendments incorporated into the final text.
The voting results clearly demonstrate that the emergence of a
#MultipolarWorld
is well underway
. Freeing interstate relations from all forms of pressure, subordination and dependence is an integral part of this process.
The Global Majority is unwilling to accept the monopoly held by a small group of countries.
For these states, combating neocolonialism is no abstract notion: it is a matter of pursuing sovereign domestic and foreign policies, strengthening their security and preserving their national identity.
☝️
We expect the adopted resolution
, which builds on two previous thematic resolutions of the General Assembly,
to gain even broader support when it is considered shortly at a plenary meeting of the
#UNGA
.
This will help firmly establish
the fight against all manifestations of colonialism
as one of the UN key priorities.
