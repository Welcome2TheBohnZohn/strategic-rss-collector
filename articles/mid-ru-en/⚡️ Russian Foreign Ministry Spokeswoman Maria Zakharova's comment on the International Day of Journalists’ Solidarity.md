Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's comment on the International Day of Journalists’ Solidarity
(September 8, 2026)
💬
September 8 marks the International Day of Journalists’ Solidarity.
The date was established in 1958 in memory of
Czechoslovak anti-fascist journalist Julius Fucik
, who was executed by the Nazis in 1943. It serves as a reminder of the importance of freedom of speech and professional journalism, while also drawing attention to the safety of media workers and the need for solidarity and mutual support within the global journalistic community.
In recent years, these fundamental principles have been increasingly eroded by the West’s policy of
purging the media landscape of unwelcome viewpoints in pursuit of narrow political goals
.
👉
Russian media outlets abroad have faced
systematic harassment and various forms of repression
in countries whose leaders routinely proclaim their commitment to press freedom while grandly portraying themselves as a “flourishing garden”.
In practice, under far-fetched pretexts, they have
blocked
Russian television channels,
shut down
correspondent offices and
expelled
journalists.
The Kiev regime has launched an outright campaign against Russian journalists reporting truthful, first-hand information.
At the same time,
the neo-Nazi junta’s openly declared intent to kill civilians
– a category that under international law includes media workers – has drawn not the slightest criticism from its Western patrons.

It is deeply troubling that these crimes and acts of repression have failed to elicit an adequate response from the Western media community
. Mainstream outlets in those countries, guided by political expediency and in defiance of the principles of journalistic solidarity,
chose to remain silent
and act as though the issue did not concern them. In doing so, they abandoned professional ethics and the very idea of solidarity among journalists.
Those who nevertheless found the courage to defend Russian media workers or honour the memory of those who died for the truth were met with
a barrage of criticism and accusations
, including from fellow journalists.
