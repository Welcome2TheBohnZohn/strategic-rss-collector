🇹🇷
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the meeting with President of Turkiye Recep Tayyip Erdogan
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am very glad to meet with you again. It has indeed been quite some time since
we last met in person
, although we continue to keep our bilateral relations
under close review
.
Turkiye is one of our privileged partners both in foreign policy and in the economic sphere
. The Intergovernmental Commission is working actively. Thanks to your personal attention to the development of our ties, economic cooperation remains at a high level.
Major joint projects are continuing
. One of the best known is
the Akkuyu Nuclear Power Plant
. It is being built in Turkiye, and you tasked our company
Rosatom
with launching the first unit this year – and that is exactly what will happen.
The company is on schedule
.
There is also considerable potential for expanding cooperation in the cultural and humanitarian sphere
. The number of Russian tourists visiting Turkiye continues to grow. In 2025, the figure reached
nearly
7 million people
.
🤝
Today we have an opportunity to discuss these and all other issues of mutual interest.
