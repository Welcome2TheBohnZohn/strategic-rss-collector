# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s answer to a media question

Published: 2026-11-03 20:34:11

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33213

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
answer to a media question
on the situation on the Korean Peninsula
(November 3, 2026)
❓
Question: Tensions on the Korean Peninsula, including around the Demilitarised Zone, have escalated sharply, and Seoul is once again trying to shift the blame onto Pyongyang. How do you see the situation in this subregion developing?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
Regrettably, the situation on the Korean Peninsula shows
no sign of stabilising
and
continues to deteriorate
.
Of particular concern is that the situation is deteriorating amid
ongoing large-scale military exercises
conducted by the US and the Republic of Korea near the DPRK border.
These manoeuvres are overtly provocative
. By the participants’ own admission, they are designed to rehearse
pre-emptive strikes against the DPRK
, including with nuclear weapons.
☝️
These exercises are growing in frequency and scale
and now extend across land, air, maritime and cyber domains.
For example, October saw
the US-ROK Iron Mace command-post exercise
, which involved rehearsing scenarios for the deployment of US strategic assets to the Korean Peninsula;
the ROK Armed Forces’ Hoguk exercise
;
US military training in airborne operations
behind the lines of a simulated adversary; and the second round this year of
the joint US-ROK Freedom Flag air exercise
, which is scheduled to continue until November 6. In addition, Seoul, Washington and Tokyo practised combat interoperability during
the trilateral, multidomain Freedom Edge exercises
held in September.
❗️
In these circumstances, we regard the measures Pyongyang is taking to strengthen its security and defence capabilities as
entirely lawful and justified
, in full accordance with
the right to self-defence
enshrined in
the UN Charter
.
We urge Seoul to immediately cease its dangerous military games and provocations
, particularly in the Demilitarised Zone, rather than seeking far-fetched pretexts for accusing the DPRK or making empty declarations about peace, and
to respect Pyongyang’s sovereign right to ensure its own security by all means available
.
