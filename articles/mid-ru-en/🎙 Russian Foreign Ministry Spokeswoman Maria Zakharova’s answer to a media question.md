🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
answer to a media question
on the complicity of Dutch company Destinus in the attack on the Crimean Bridge
(November 20, 2026)
❓
Question: How would you comment on
a statement
by the Dutch private arms manufacturer Destinus that the Kiev regime used its Ruta missiles and drones in the November 17 attack on the Crimean Bridge?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
We have noted that
public opinion in the Netherlands has become considerably divided
in response to this admission – or, more accurately,
this self-exposure.
Those Dutch citizens whose minds have been
thoroughly poisoned by unbridled Russophobia
greeted the news that Dutch weapons had been used in an attack on civilian infrastructure in the Russian Federation with almost primitive glee.
However,
there were also many voices of reason
, pointing to
the Netherlands’ irresponsible policy and the risks
posed by the continued supply of weapons to the Kiev regime, which has repeatedly demonstrated its terrorist nature.
Against this backdrop,
the reaction
of Dutch Foreign Minister
Tom Berendsen
is particularly telling. On the one hand,
he confirmed
the statement by Destinus about the Netherlands’ de facto involvement in the terrorist attack against Russia. On the other,
he continues to make complacent assertions that bear no relation to reality
, claiming that the Netherlands is not a party to the conflict surrounding Ukraine.
Let us recall that just over a month ago, Dutch Defence Minister
Dilan Yesilgoz-Zegerius
publicly described
our country as an
"enemy"
, a position subsequently endorsed by the entire Cabinet of Ministers of the Kingdom. Earlier,
reports
emerged that
retired Dutch military pilots
had taken part in combat operations against Russia, flying F-16 fighter jets transferred to Kiev.
❗️
All of this
, together with the Netherlands’ thoughtless support for the neo-Nazi Kiev regime through arms supplies and funding (amounting to
at least 12.4 billion euros
since 2022), at the expense of addressing its own domestic problems,
represents an undisguised advance along the path of military escalation
.
The Russian Side is documenting all such actions and taking them into account in its military planning.
