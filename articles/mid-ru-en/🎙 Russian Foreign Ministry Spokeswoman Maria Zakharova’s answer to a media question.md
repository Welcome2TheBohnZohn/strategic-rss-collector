# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s answer to a media question

Published: 2026-10-02 16:59:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32536

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
answer to a media question
regarding the withdrawal of foreign troops from Iraq
(October 2, 2026)
❓
Question: On September 30, the US-led Anti-ISIS Coalition announced the completion of its troop withdrawal from Iraq. How does Russia view this event?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
Indeed, on September 30,
the twelve-year presence in Iraq of the so-called International Anti-ISIS Coalition came to an end.
All remaining bases of the US Armed Forces and their allies in the country – mainly within Iraqi Kurdistan – have been fully transferred to the control of the Iraqi military.
The decision by the Iraqi Parliament, adopted in January 2020 and fully
reflecting the aspiration of its people to restore and consolidate state sovereignty
after the prolonged presence of foreign forces, has thus been implemented.
That presence had effectively continued since 2003 – from the moment of the American invasion under a completely contrived pretext, which triggered decades of instability across the wider region.
The withdrawal of troops from Iraq, which
we certainly welcome
, demonstrates that the Iraqi army and security forces are capable of independently addressing the task of strengthening the state’s defence capability and ensuring national security. They are capable, among other things, of countering the terrorist threat emanating from ISIS and other such organisations.
We are convinced that
the Iraqi people will be able to successfully withstand any internal and external challenges that confront them.
The guarantee of this lies in neighbourly relations and coordination with neighbouring countries; in this regard, we would like once again to draw attention to
the Collective Security Concept for the Gulf Region proposed by Russia.
☝️
It proceeds from the understanding that it is precisely the states located there, such as Iraq, that
play the leading role in maintaining stability in this strategically important region through close interaction among themselves.
