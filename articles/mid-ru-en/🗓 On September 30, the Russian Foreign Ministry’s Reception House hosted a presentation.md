# 🗓 On September 30, the Russian Foreign Ministry’s Reception House hosted a presentation

Published: 2026-10-01 09:00:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32486

🗓
On September 30,
the Russian Foreign Ministry’s Reception House
hosted
a presentation
for the diplomatic corps of
the
International Sports Forum “Russia: Country of Sports”
– one of the major events in international sports cooperation in 2026.
Deputy Prime Minister of Russia
Dmitry Chernyshenko
, Minister of Sport and President of the Russian Olympic Committee
Mikhail Degtyarev
, and Deputy Foreign Minister
Alexander Pankin
delivered welcoming remarks to members of the diplomatic corps and representatives of Russian and foreign media.
The Forum
will take place in
Krasnoyarsk on October 21-23
and will once again serve as a leading platform for dialogue and the exchange of experience in promoting physical education and sport.
