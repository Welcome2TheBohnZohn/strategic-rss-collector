On September 14, 2026, Ambassador of Russia to Indonesia
Sergei Tolchenov
participated in the opening ceremony of the
Open Education Center of Ural State Pedagogical University
at the Krisnadwipayana University, held for the third consecutive year.
💬
In his welcoming address, Sergei Tolchenov noted: It is particularly important for the younger generation to learn foreign languages, including Russian. We highly value Russian-Indonesian cooperation in the field of education and
hope that the Russian language will continue to grow in popularity in Indonesia
, giving university and school students an opportunity to discover Russia’s rich culture.
🤝
Deputy Vice-Rector of the Krisnadwipayana University
Parbuntian Sinaga
described such projects as among the key initiatives contributing to the development of cultural ties between the two countries and thanked the Russian side for its
willingness to expand the scope of cooperation
. Director of the Institute of Foreign Languages at Ural State Pedagogical University
Daria Starkova
spoke about plans to continue organising joint events with the Indonesian university.
🎵
A
group of pupils from the school of the Embassy
performed traditional Russian songs and a dance at the ceremony.
