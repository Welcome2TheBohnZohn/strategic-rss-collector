# ✍️ To all who have honoured the memory of Alexander Darchiev, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the Russian Federation to the United States of America

Published: 2026-11-23 23:31:46

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33570

To all who have honoured the memory of Alexander Darchiev, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the Russian Federation to the United States of America
✍️
On behalf of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation, I would like to express my gratitude to all those
who have sent us messages of condolence and support
in connection with the untimely passing of the Russian Ambassador to the United States Alexander Nikitich Darchiev.
For the staff, for the team of the Foreign Ministry, his passing is
an irreplaceable loss.
In Alexander Nikitich, we have lost not only a professional of the highest caliber, a renowned expert on American affairs, and an unwavering patriot of our country, but also a dear friend, a sensitive and attentive mentor, and a colleague who was always ready to assist and lend a helping hand.
Alexander Darchiev devoted his entire life to selfless service to his homeland.
May his memory live on!
All condolences received have been conveyed to the family and loved ones of the deceased.
SERGEY RYABKOV, Deputy Foreign Minister of Russia
