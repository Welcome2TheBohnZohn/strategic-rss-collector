🇩🇿
On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
and Algeria’s Minister of State, Minister of Foreign Affairs, National Community Abroad and African Affairs
Ahmed Attaf
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Ministers had a thorough discussion of
practical steps to further strengthen the enhanced strategic partnership between Russia and Algeria
, including the schedule of upcoming contacts at various levels.
They expressed satisfaction with
the spirit of trust in their political dialogue and their well-established cooperation
, both bilaterally and in multilateral forums.
🤝
Following an exchange of views on current regional issues, the Ministers reaffirmed
their shared interest in continuing effective coordination to resolve ongoing conflicts through political and diplomatic means
, based on the universally recognised principles of international law and
the UN Charter
.
