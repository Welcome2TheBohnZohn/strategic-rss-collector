# ⚡️ On November 11, Moldova's Ambassador to Russia Lilian Darii was summoned to the Russian Foreign Ministry.

Published: 2026-11-11 13:32:45

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33309

⚡️
On November 11,
Moldova's Ambassador to Russia
Lilian Darii
was summoned
to the Russian Foreign Ministry.
The Ministry issued a strong protest
over the Moldovan authorities’
decision
on October 23 to declare a staff member of
the Russian Embassy in Chisinau
persona non grata. The authorities cited as a pretext the alleged discovery of
debris from a drone carrying explosives
on Moldovan territory.
The Ministry stressed that
the lack of evidence
that the drone was of Russian origin shows that
the decision was based on a contrived pretext
and constitutes yet another unfriendly act by Moldova.
The move’s real aim is
to further erode the centuries-old ties between Russia and Moldova
and
to obstruct the Russian diplomatic mission’s work
in Chisinau at every turn.
❗️
The Moldovan Ambassador received a diplomatic note informing him that,
in accordance with the principle of
reciprocity
, Russia has declared a staff member of the Moldovan Embassy in Russia persona non grata.
