✉️
President of Russia Vladimir Putin
sent a message of greetings
to participants and guests of
the Peoples of Russia International Week
(November 2, 2026)
💬
Welcome to
the Peoples of Russia International Week
.
This year’s Forum has brought together representatives of government, business, academia and the expert community to discuss a wide range of issues related to
making state ethnic policy more effective
and
strengthening international humanitarian cooperation.
I would like to emphasise that
we deeply cherish Russia's rich historical, cultural and spiritual heritage
and make it a priority to preserve the languages, distinctive traditions and customs of the peoples living in Russia.
☝️
It is particularly important that
indigenous peoples’ associations and communities, civic organisations and volunteer groups
play an active part in this extensive, constructive work.
I would especially like to highlight
The Soul of Russia
, an undoubtedly multifaceted project. Its widely supported initiatives and educational and outreach programmes
help
develop and promote
renowned traditional arts and crafts, national sports, and new and exciting tourist destinations.
I am confident that your Forum, held during
the Year of Unity of the Peoples of Russia
, will be
a major event
in the public and cultural life of our country and other states,
helping to strengthen international humanitarian dialogue and professional and personal ties.
🤝
I wish you success, fruitful exchanges and all the very best.
VLADIMIR PUTIN
