#Announcement
🗓
On October 28-29,
the Third Russia – Africa Summit
will take place in Moscow under
the chairmanship
of Russia's President
Vladimir Putin
and Chairperson of the African Union, President of Burundi
Evariste Ndayishimiye
.
Representatives of almost 50 African countries will take part in
the Summit
, including Heads of State and Government as well as Leaders of the continent’s integration associations.
The focus will be on
the further development of Russia’s multifaceted ties with African nations
in politics, trade and the economy, investment, science and technology, and humanitarian and cultural affairs.
✍️
The Summit
is expected to adopt:
• a final declaration;
• the next Russia – Africa Partnership Forum Action Plan for 2027-2029;
• the Declaration of Cooperation in the Civilian Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies;
• a substantial package of intergovernmental and interdepartmental agreements covering cooperation between Russia and Africa in a wide range of areas.
On October 28,
the Leaders of Russia and Burundi will address
the Russia – Africa Economic and Humanitarian Forum
, which will bring together Russian and African officials, representatives of government agencies, business and expert communities.
In addition, Vladimir Putin will hold
a meeting with Leaders of Africa’s regional organisations
.
🤝
Russia's President will hold
a series of bilateral talks
with heads of African states on the sidelines of
the Summit
.
