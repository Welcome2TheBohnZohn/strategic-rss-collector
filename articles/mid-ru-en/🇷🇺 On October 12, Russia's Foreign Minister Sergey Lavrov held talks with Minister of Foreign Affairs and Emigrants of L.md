# 🇷🇺 On October 12, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov held talks with Minister of Foreign Affairs and Emigrants of Lebanon Youssef Rajji

Published: 2026-10-12 18:55:34

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32704

🇷🇺
🇱🇧
On October 12, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
held talks
with Minister of Foreign Affairs and Emigrants of Lebanon Youssef Rajji
.
The Ministers had
an extensive discussion
of the developments in Lebanon.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks
The Russian Side expressed
serious concern regarding the continuing hostilities in southern Lebanon
. It is crucial to cease fire as soon as possible, withdraw the Israeli forces, and create conditions for a political and diplomatic settlement of the conflict according to
UN Security Council Resolution 1701
.
☝️
Sergey Lavrov confirmed Russia’s consistent support of Lebanon’s sovereignty, independence, unity, and territorial integrity.
The parties specifically covered
the completion of the current mandate of
the UN Interim Force in Lebanon
and possible preservation of international presence in a different form.
When discussing the prospects of strengthening bilateral Russian-Lebanese cooperation, the Ministers noted
the importance of maintaining regular trusted political dialogue
and
increasing delegation exchanges
.
🤝
They stressed
the importance of developing business partnerships
to ensure steady positive dynamic of mutual trade and reaffirmed mutual interest in expanding cultural and humanitarian links.
The Ministers had
a substantive exchange of opinions on the regional agenda
with focus on the crisis in the Gulf region caused by
the US and Israeli aggression against Iran
. The parties stressed that the only acceptable option is to resolve the disagreements
by political and diplomatic means
while considering the legitimate interests of the states involved in the conflict.
As concerns
the Middle East settlement
, Sergey Lavrov and Youssef Rajji confirmed the importance of swift and full implementation of
the agreement between Israel and Hamas on Gaza ceasefire
and
immediate humanitarian assistance to the civilian population
of the Palestinian enclave, as well as
normalisation of the situation in the West Bank.
The Ministers expressed support for Syria’s unity, territorial integrity, and sovereignty
. They stressed the importance of consolidating international efforts in the interests of the country’s post-conflict recovery.
Sergey Lavrov informed his Lebanese counterpart about the latest developments with respect to
the Ukrainian crisis
.
