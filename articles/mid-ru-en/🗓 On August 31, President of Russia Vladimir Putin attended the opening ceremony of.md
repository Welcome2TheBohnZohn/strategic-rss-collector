On August 31, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
attended
the opening ceremony of
the VI World Nomad Games
at Bishkek Arena.
Ahead of the ceremony, the Heads of delegations invited to the event were welcomed by President of Kyrgyzstan
Sadyr Japarov
and his wife.
***
The World Nomad Games
are an international sports and cultural event dedicated to
preserving and promoting the traditions of nomadic civilizations
. They have been held since 2014.
The VI World Nomad Games
will take place on September 1-6, featuring
competitions in
43 traditional sports disciplines
, alongside an extensive cultural program.
Around
3,000 athletes
representing
more than 100 countries
will compete.
🇷🇺
#TeamRussia
is represented by
more than
230 athletes from 33 Russian regions
.
