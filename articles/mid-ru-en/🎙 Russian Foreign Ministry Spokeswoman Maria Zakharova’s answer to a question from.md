🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
answer to a question
from
RT France
regarding media reports on the presence of French military personnel in Ukraine
(November 17, 2026)
❓
Question:
RFI has published an article
on the direct involvement of French military personnel in technical support and training activities in Ukraine. It notes that their deployment bypassed the law and parliament. What measures does the Russian MFA intend to take in light of these revelations?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
RFI’s report
on the presence of French military personnel in Ukraine as part of
“Operation Volya”
came as neither a surprise nor a revelation to us
.
Paris’s direct involvement in the Ukraine conflict has long been an open secret.
We have repeatedly pointed to the presence of Western military experts, instructors and mercenaries in Ukraine, including from France.
In October 2025,
Russia’s Foreign Intelligence Service also reported
on French plans to deploy
a military contingent
to Ukraine. President
Emmanuel Macron
himself has repeatedly spoken of the possibility, or even the necessity, of such a move.
As is known,
large-scale
Coalition 2.0
exercises
are currently taking place in Poland to prepare for the deployment of multinational forces to Ukraine under the
“coalition of the willing”
, with French personnel forming their core.
This confirms that Paris and the other coalition capitals are quite serious about their intentions.
Given the scale of the assistance Paris provides to the AFU, including deliveries of technologically sophisticated weapons and the establishment of production facilities in Ukraine,
it would be naive to assume that French military specialists are not involved on the ground
.
☝️
We have no doubt that the extent of France’s direct or indirect involvement in hostilities will eventually become public knowledge.
If
RFI’s report
that the French parliament was not informed of military personnel taking part in an overseas operation is accurate, this would be
further evidence of the French authorities’ disregard for the views of their own citizens
.
Far from everyone in France supports the current leadership’s militarist course.
Many understand that it
does nothing to strengthen national security
and
creates unprecedented risks
of a direct confrontation with Russia. Indeed, French officials have long openly acknowledged that this is precisely what they are preparing for.
While France is reeling from
yet another wave of protests
, its authorities continue to act as though Ukraine were their overriding priority. Instead of addressing pressing social and economic problems,
Paris is becoming ever more deeply embroiled in the Ukraine conflict
, sparing no expense and seemingly prepared to risk the lives of its own military personnel.
As for measures on our part, these will be taken by
the Defence Ministry
and the General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
, rather than
the Foreign Ministry
.
As President
Vladimir Putin
has stated
,
foreign troops
, if deployed to Ukraine,
will become legitimate targets for the Russian military.
👉
We hope Paris understands this.
