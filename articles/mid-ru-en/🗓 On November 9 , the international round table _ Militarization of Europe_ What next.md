# 🗓 On November 9 , the international round table " Militarization of Europe: What next?

Published: 2026-11-10 06:44:58

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33278

🗓
On November 9
, the international round table "
Militarization of Europe: What next?
" was held in Vienna, organized by the
Delegation of the Russian Federation to the Vienna Negotiations on Military Security and Arms Control
.
The event focused on analyzing
the current situation in Europe, marked by the unprecedented militarization
under the pretext of countering the "Russian threat".
#NATO
and the
#EU
are openly preparing for war against Russia
, predicting that it could break out within the next 3–5 years.
Participants discussed
the consequences of the massive increase in mil spending
by European countries, the
expansion of military infrastructure
in "frontline states" bordering Russia, and
NATO's provocative plans
against our country.
❗️
In 2025, military spending by NATO states already amounted to ~ $1.6 trln. In 2025, spending by the 27 EU states reached EUR 418 bln and is projected to rise to EUR 800 bln by 2030.
❗️
💊
Particular emphasis was placed on the
harmful effects of the intensive information and propaganda campaign
conducted by European countries to condition their own populations to accept the idea of an "inevitable" conflict with Russia.
The "Russian threat" is used to justify everything citizens are expected to sacrifice
in the struggle against our country. The increase in European military spending is taking place against a backdrop of
economic stagnation and will have serious social consequences.
💊
An important topic of discussion was the existing mechanisms for
de-escalation
, including those between Russia and NATO. It was noted that their use has become increasingly difficult, while bilateral de-escalation channels remain virtually frozen, including dialogue under
agreements on the prevention of incidents on and over the high seas, as well as on the prevention of dangerous military activities
.
💊
Special attention was paid to the Ukrainian crisis and its connection with European militarization.
Ukraine is being systematically integrated into the defense industries of European countries, while the battlefield experience of the Armed Forces of Ukraine is being used
to prepare for a conflict with our country.
The round table opened with a
video address by Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation S.A. Ryabkov
. The event brought together representatives of the Vienna diplomatic corps, international and regional organizations, including the UN and the OSCE, as well as prominent experts and journalists.
🎙

☝️
Following the round table,
we reiterate that Russia does not seek confrontation with NATO and does not want war with Europe
. European countries must abandon their dangerous course, which gives them the illusion of protection against external threats but will, in reality, lead to catastrophic consequences.
