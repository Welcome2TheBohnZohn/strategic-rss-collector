# 🎙 Remarks by Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at the UN Security Council meeting on Ukraine

🎙
Remarks
by Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at
the UN Security Council meeting on Ukraine
(New York, September 23, 2026)
💬
Had
the 2015 Minsk agreements
, reaffirmed in Paris in 2019 – with Zelensky already in office – been implemented as required by the UN Security Council,
Ukraine would still be within its 1991 borders, except, of course, for Crimea
.
That issue has long been settled
, as everyone fully understands. <...>
Russia’s December 2021 proposals
to reach an agreement based on mutual respect for the security interests of Russia and the countries of Europe and North America were
arrogantly rejected by NATO and the Biden administration
.
In practice, preparations were being made for Kiev
to seize by force the territories of Donbass beyond its control
. To prevent this Western scenario from unfolding, we were compelled to launch
the special military operation
in February 2022. Just a week later, the Ukrainian side requested
negotiations
. We agreed.
After several rounds in Belarus and Istanbul, the Ukrainian side proposed key principles for a settlement,
which our negotiators accepted
. Crucially, these enshrined Ukraine’s
neutral status
, recognised
the realities on the ground
and provided
collective security guarantees
offering reliable protection against any recurrence of armed provocations or attempts at aggression.
The document was
initialled
by the Ukrainian and Russian delegations,
but everyone knows what happened to it
. <...>
☝️
Had that
initialled agreement
been formalised in a treaty and implemented,
Ukraine would, once again, have retained its 1991 borders, but without Crimea and part of Donbass.
<...>
Russia
– as President
Vladimir Putin
has
repeatedly reaffirmed
–
is ready for negotiations to achieve precisely such a lasting and just peace
. The tasks that must be accomplished to achieve this goal were clearly outlined in
President Vladimir Putin’s keynote address in June 2024
.
❗️
However, having learned from
bitter experience
,
we will not suspend the special military operation for the duration of negotiations, whatever their format.
Yet this is precisely what Europe wants, with the same simple aim of
securing a respite and replenishing the depleted arsenals of the Kiev regime
, which, out of desperation, has long since resorted to
openly terrorist methods
.
***
As we can see,
Greenland is neither Crimea nor Donbass
, and “never the twain shall meet”. We eagerly await the next episode of this “series”. It will be interesting to hear the Secretariat’s response to any questions journalists may ask about the plans of
Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
.
We hope that the next Secretary-General will bring the statements of Secretariat officials
back into line with international law
.
👉
The Declaration on Principles of International Law concerning Friendly Relations and Cooperation among States in accordance with the Charter of the UN
clearly stipulates that the principles of sovereignty and territorial integrity apply to s
tates that respect the principle of equal rights and self-determination of peoples
and have
a government representing the whole people belonging to the territory
, without distinction as to race, creed or colour.
I believe it is obvious to everyone that
the Kiev regime cannot be regarded as a government representing the interests of the people of Crimea, Donbass and Novorossiya
, whom it has time and again branded
“terrorists, subhumans and specimen”
.
