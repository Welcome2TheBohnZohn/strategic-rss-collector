# 🇷🇺 #Announcement At the invitation of President of the People’s Republic of China Xi Jinping

Published: 2026-11-16 16:01:09

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33383

#Announcement
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
At the invitation of President of the People’s Republic of China
Xi Jinping
, President of the Russian Federation
Vladimir Putin
will pay a working visit to China
on November 18-19 and attend
the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Summit
(
#APEC
) in Shenzhen.
On November 18,
Vladimir Putin will address the APEC CEO Summit
, and on November 19,
he will take part in the APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting
, where he will outline Russia’s priorities for trade and economic cooperation in the Asia-Pacific region.
Full-scale
#RussiaChina
talks will take place
, during which
Vladimir Putin
and
Xi Jinping
will discuss prospects for further strengthening relations of comprehensive partnership and strategic cooperation, as well as international and regional issues.
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
🇺🇸
A trilateral meeting between Vladimir Putin and the Leaders of China and the US is planned.
A separate meeting between
Vladimir Putin
and
Donald Trump
is also envisaged.
In addition,
the Russian President will hold bilateral meetings
with the Leaders of several other countries.
During his stay in Shenzhen,
Vladimir Putin will also visit
Shenzhen MSU-BIT University
, jointly established by
Lomonosov Moscow State University
and
Beijing Institute of Technology
, and
address students and faculty
.
