Government of the Russian Federation
:
On September 22, ahead of
the XII St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
, Culture Minister
Olga Lyubimova
met with her counterparts from the DPRK and Thailand.
🇷🇺
🇰🇵
During her meeting with DPRK Culture Minister, Comrade
Hyon Un-chol
, Olga Lyubimova discussed
cooperation in filmmaking and exchanges between museums.
The ministers also reviewed cultural cooperation in 2025, including the participation of Russian performing arts groups in
the April Spring Friendship Art Festival
in Pyongyang.
🇷🇺
🇹🇭
Olga Lyubimova’s meeting with Thailand's Culture Minister
Sabida Thaised
focused on
the flagship international cultural project
Russian Seasons
, which is currently enjoying a successful run in the Kingdom. Another key topic was preparations to celebrate
the 130th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Russia and Thailand
in 2027.
