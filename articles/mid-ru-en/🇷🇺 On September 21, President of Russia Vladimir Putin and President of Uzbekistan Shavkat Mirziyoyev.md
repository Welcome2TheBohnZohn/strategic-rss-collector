On September 21, President of Russia Vladimir Putin and President of Uzbekistan Shavkat Mirziyoyev
had a telephone conversation
.
Shavkat Mirziyoyev congratulated Vladimir Putin
on
the successful conduct of the elections
to
the 9th State Duma of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation
.
It was noted that
the high turnout and voting results confirmed Russian citizens’ unequivocal support
for the President’s course aimed at advancing the country’s socioeconomic development and safeguarding national interests.
Vladimir Putin and Shavkat Mirziyoyev also discussed current issues related to
further strengthening the alliance between Russia and Uzbekistan.
