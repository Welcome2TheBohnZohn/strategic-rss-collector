# 🗓 October 7 marks the 24th anniversary of the signing of the Charter of the Collective Security Treaty Organisation

Published: 2026-10-07 14:02:44

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32612

🗓
October 7 marks
the 24th anniversary of the signing of
the Charter of the Collective Security Treaty Organisation
(
#CSTO
), the instrument that established the CSTO as an international regional association.
Its
stated purposes
– the strengthening of peace and stability and the collective defence of the independence, territorial integrity and sovereignty of the member states –
remain the cornerstone of mutually beneficial cooperation in a true spirit of alliance
.
On this basis,
the Russian Federation assumed the CSTO
chairmanship
in 2026
under the motto
‘Collective Security in a
#MultipolarWorld
: A Common Goal, a Shared Responsibility’.
In a short span by historical standards, the Organisation, which today brings together six states –
Russia, Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan
– has made substantial headway and established itself as
a key mechanism for safeguarding regional security
.
The CSTO systematically
addresses the prevention and settlement of crises
and
counters the many challenges and threats to stability
. It continually enhances the combat readiness of the organisation’s Collective Forces and
sustains intensive foreign-policy coordination
.
Drawing upon its considerable experience and advanced security instruments,
the CSTO continues to evolve
, adapting flexibly to changing circumstances.
🛡
In order to strengthen the Organisation’s security capabilities,
the unique combat experience of
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
is being drawn upon
– including in
exercises involving the CSTO Collective Forces
held in the Chelyabinsk Region in September and October 2026.
Cooperation in the emerging fields of biological and information security
is likewise gathering pace. Operations conducted by the law-enforcement agencies and security services of the member states to combat
terrorism, illicit drug trafficking, illegal migration and cybercrime
continue to deliver substantial results.
🤝
The development of ties with other international organisations is a key strand of the CSTO member states’ systematic efforts
. Since 2004, the Organisation has held
observer status
at the UN General Assembly.
Priority is being given to deepening cooperation with the CIS and the SCO
, and the roadmap for cooperation among the three organisations in areas of mutual interest continues to be implemented.
A joint conference of the CSTO, CIS and SCO
on security issues in Central Asia and Afghanistan will be held in Moscow on October 15-16.
The memory of
the Soviet Union’s Victory in the Great Patriotic War
remains a cornerstone of our allied cooperation and of our shared enduring values
. The CSTO member states are joining forces to preserve the truth about the heroism of the Soviet people and to prevent any rewriting of history or any distortion or revision of the war’s outcome.
Prospects for expanding multifaceted allied cooperation will be discussed at the forthcoming
session of the CSTO Collective Security Council,
and at
the joint meeting of the Organisation’s Council of Foreign Ministers, Council of Defence Ministers and Committee of Secretaries of Security Councils
. Both will be held in Moscow on November 10-11. Decisions aimed at strengthening the CSTO collective security system are to be signed.
❗️
The further expansion of comprehensive cooperation with the CSTO member States is
a priority of the Russian Federation’s foreign policy
.
