# ✉️ On September 17, President of Russia Vladimir Putin sent a message to Prime Minister of India Narendra Modi, extending heartfelt congratulations on his birthday

On September 17, President of Russia Vladimir Putin
sent a message
to Prime Minister of India Narendra Modi, extending heartfelt congratulations on his birthday
.
✍️
Please accept my heartfelt congratulations on your birthday.
You rightfully command
great respect
among your compatriots and enjoy
considerable standing
on the world stage.
🇷🇺
🇮🇳
Your personal contribution to strengthening
the special and privileged strategic partnership
between India and Russia
can hardly be overstated.
I would like to express
my gratitude
for the cordial welcome and generous hospitality extended to me and the Russian delegation during
our visit to New Delhi
last week.
Our talks
were undoubtedly
substantive, frank and highly productive.
As always,
I sincerely enjoyed our conversations
, which once again affirmed your exceptional political wisdom and foresight.
We will, of course, continue working constructively together on pressing bilateral and international issues
for the benefit of the friendly peoples of our countries.
🤝
I sincerely wish you, my dear friend, sound health, happiness, well-being and every success in your service to the nation.
VLADIMIR PUTIN
