On September 24, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Sides discussed current issues relating to the Organisation’s work,
focusing on the record of the Portuguese diplomat’s tenure as UN Secretary-General
.
Sergey Lavrov stressed the importance of
carefully calibrated UN reform
to adapt the Organisation to the realities of a
#MultipolarWorld
order, ensure significantly broader representation of nationals of
#GlobalMajority
countries within the Secretariat, and ensure that
UN staff perform their duties impartially and without favouring any side
.
Russia's Foreign Minister also emphasised
the inadmissibility of eroding
the Organisation’s intergovernmental character
or disrupting
the “division of labour” among its principal organs.
Sergey Lavrov presented Antonio Guterres with a copy of the report
“Heirs of Goebbels: Falsification of the Events in Bucha and Bloody Staged Performances of the Kiev Regime”
, prepared by the International Public Tribunal led by its Chairman, member of the Civic Chamber of Russia
Maxim Grigoryev
.
📄
The report
is devoted to exposing
the false claims
spread by the Kiev regime about
the Bucha hoax
. The Minister demanded that the UN Secretariat conduct
a detailed examination of the facts surrounding the provocation staged by Kiev and its NATO sponsors
.
