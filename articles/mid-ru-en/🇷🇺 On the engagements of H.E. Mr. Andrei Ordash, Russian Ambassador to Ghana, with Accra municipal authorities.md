On the engagements of H.E. Mr. Andrei Ordash, Russian Ambassador to Ghana, with Accra municipal authorities
In the beginning of September 2026, H.E. Mr. Andrei Ordash, Ambassador of Russia to Ghana paid courtesy visits to Hon. Michael Kpakpo Allotey, Mayor of Accra, as well as Municipal Chief Executives of the La Dade-Kotopon Municipal Assembly, Hon. Alfredos Nii Anyetei, and the Ayawaso West Municipal Assembly, Hon. Dr. Michael Mensah.
The Ambassador expressed a readiness to engage with municipal authorities as part of efforts to deepen bilateral cooperation at the level of municipal self-governance.
