photographic evidence of another Ukrainian war crime
– the destruction of St George’s Church, a historical and cultural heritage site in the Rylsk district.
According to
Alexander Khinshtein
, the church stood in the village of Kozino, one kilometre from the state border.
The wooden church was built in 1844, but its history goes back much further: the earliest mention dates to 1648, in a tax record. In 1662, the chapel was ravaged. In 1699, it received a new antimension and was consecrated. Over the centuries, the church on this site has perished and risen again more than once.
