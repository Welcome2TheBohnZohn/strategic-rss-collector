Ambassador-at-Large of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs
Rodion Miroshnik
:
💬
On September 1,
a volunteer humanitarian convoy
– four vehicles carrying food and medicines –
was attacked by Ukrainian drones
while approaching the city of Alyoshki in the Kherson Region.
For several months, Ukrainian militants have been using
drone strikes and remote mining
to obstruct civilian traffic, including ambulances, travelling to and from Alyoshki.
According to eyewitnesses,
a Ukrainian drone hovered in front of the lead vehicle
, forcing the volunteer group to stop. The drone then circled all the vehicles, giving its operator ample opportunity to verify that they were carrying only civilians and humanitarian cargo.
The first vehicle was then struck by a Ukrainian UAV.
Further strikes on the civilian vehicles followed one after another until
the cars were badly damaged
and
the aid cargo destroyed
. The drivers and volunteers accompanying the shipment managed to leave the vehicles and take cover in a nearby wooded area.
According to preliminary information
,
one volunteer was injured
.
❗️
An attack on civilian vehicles and civilians is unequivocally a war crime.
This attack is further aggravated by the fact that the volunteers were carrying out a humanitarian mission to the city, which was deliberately disrupted.
