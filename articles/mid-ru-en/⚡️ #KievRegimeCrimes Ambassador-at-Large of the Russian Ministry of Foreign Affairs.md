Ambassador-at-Large of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs
Rodion Miroshnik
:
💬
Ukrainian militants
deliberately struck
ICRC trucks and a humanitarian aid warehouse in Donetsk.
On October 6,
Ukrainian B-2 fixed-wing drones struck the warehouse premises twice
, first at 3:00 a.m. and then at 5:45 a.m.
According to local DPR authorities, the attacks
damaged three ICRC trucks bearing Red Cross emblems
and
destroyed construction materials
stored both outside and inside the warehouse.
(See photo)
☝️
The Regional Delegation of the International Committee of the Red Cross in Russia and Belarus
confirmed the strike
on the organisation’s property in Donetsk.
The ICRC warehouse premises were marked with
protective Red Cross emblems
in full compliance with international humanitarian law. The Ukrainian side had also received
the necessary notifications
regarding the warehouse’s location and operations. The Kiev authorities must therefore have known that it stored
food, medical supplies and construction materials
used to assist Donbass residents in need.
👉
This, however, did not deter them from deliberately attacking the facility.
The attack is particularly cynical given that
these construction materials are used to restore civilian facilities damaged by Ukrainian shelling
.
This crime underscores Kiev’s disregard for international humanitarian law
, which grants special protection to ICRC personnel and property.
❗️
These actions by Kiev’s militants and the Ukrainian authorities must receive an unequivocal response from states that support the ICRC’s work and respect international humanitarian law, which is binding on all countries.
