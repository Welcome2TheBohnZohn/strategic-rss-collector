# 🇷🇺 Ambassador of the Russian Federation to Belize with residence in Mexico Nikolay Sofinskiy

Published: 2026-09-18 23:34:51

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32105

🇷🇺
🇧🇿
Ambassador of the Russian Federation to Belize with residence in Mexico
Nikolay Sofinskiy
is participating in
Diplomatic Week
, organized by the Government of Belize for members of the diplomatic corps accredited to the country in connection with the
upcoming 45ᵗʰ anniversary of Belize’s Independence.
🤝
On September 18, Ambassador
Sofinskiy
held meetings with Prime Minister of Belize
Hon. John Briceño
and Minister of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Trade
Hon. Francis Fonseca.
During the talks, the parties discussed key issues on the bilateral agenda, particularly in the context of
the 35ᵗʰ anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between Russia and Belize
, which the two countries commemorate this year, and exchanged views on current international and regional matters.
✉️
Ambassador
Sofinskiy
presented Minister
Fonseca
with a personal congratulatory message from the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation,
Sergey Lavrov
, on the occasion of
the anniversary of Belize’s Independence
. In his message, Minister
Lavrov
noted that Russia highly values the ties of friendship and mutual respect between the two countries and remains committed to further developing constructive political dialogue and mutually beneficial cooperation.
During his stay in Belize, Ambassador
Sofinskiy
also held a number of other official and business contacts.
🎉
The Embassy of the Russian Federation in Mexico
extends its warmest congratulations to the Government and people of Belize on the occasion of the anniversary of Independence and expresses its appreciation to the Belizean Party for its hospitality.
The Embassy is also grateful to the Honorary Consul of the Russian Federation in Belize,
Ms. Lauren Reardon Smith
, for her organizational support during the visit.
