# 🎙 Address by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the Plenary Session of the 11th Eastern Economic Forum

Published: 2026-09-03 10:20:34

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31820

🎙
Address
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the Plenary Session of
the 11th Eastern Economic Forum
(Vladivostok, Russky Island, September 3, 2026)
The event
was also attended by President of Indonesia
Prabowo Subianto
, Prime Minister of Mongolia
Nyam-Osoryn Uchral
, Vice President of Myanmar
U Nyo Saw
, and Vice Premier of the State Council of China
Ding Xuexiang
.
Key points
:
•
Russia is an active participant in investment and trade cooperation in the Asia-Pacific
. We engage with our partners and the region’s leading integration associations on an equal footing.
•
Our strategic objective is to ensure that the Far East remains a region of cooperation and dynamic transformation
in the economy and infrastructure, technological development, workforce training and, of course, the social sphere.
•
Over the past 11 years, around 25 trillion rubles in capital investment
has been attracted to Russia’s Far Eastern regions
. As a result, the Far East’s gross regional product has more than tripled over this period – a very strong pace of growth.
•
The Far East’s key macroeconomic indicators are growing faster than the national average
. This is precisely what we sought to achieve when we declared the region’s development a strategic priority for Russia throughout the 21st century.
•
It is important to continue creating
convenient and predictable conditions for investors
, taking into account the needs of businesses and the regions while proceeding from Russia’s national interests.
•
For investors in such projects
,
and for those launching new enterprises in the Far East and the Arctic
,
it is important to ensure stable demand for their products
. Offset contracts, which are being developed with the support of
State Development Corporation VEB.RF
, should be used for this purpose as well.
• In today’s challenging global environment, amid risks to international shipping and trade,
the Northern Sea Route
is intended to provide greater flexibility and security for international deliveries
, open up new opportunities for transit and coastal shipping, and ensure uninterrupted supplies to Arctic regions.
•
Our goal is to make navigation along the Trans-Arctic Transport Corridor year-round
. To achieve this, we are building icebreakers and ice-class vessels, expanding our service and rescue fleet and satellite constellation, and strengthening port infrastructure.
• We expect that by the end of this decade,
freight traffic along the Trans-Arctic Corridor will reach at least 70 million tonnes per year
– at least double the current level.
• To expand international capital participation in our Far Eastern and Arctic projects,
we need to make use of the potential of
the Eastern Exchange
. As I have already said, we plan to establish a modern financial centre on its basis.
•
We need to develop
new cross-border routes with Asia-Pacific,
#BRICS
and
#EAEU
countries
. We are open to cooperation with all our partners in this area.
