# 🗓 On November 17, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov took part in the 21st East Asia Summit

Published: 2026-11-17 15:01:19

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33411

🗓
On November 17, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
took part
in
the 21st East Asia Summit
(
#EAS
) in Manila
.
In his remarks
, the Minister stressed
the importance of strengthening the international legal foundations
underpinning the work of
the Association of Southeast Asian Nations
(
#ASEAN
) and the region-wide mechanisms built around it.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's full remarks at the EAS
Sergey Lavrov drew attention to the risks of undermining the ASEAN-centred system as Euro-Atlantic actors encroach on regional fora
, violating ASEAN’s established rules and operating principles and introducing confrontational issues onto their agendas. The Minister also highlighted
the militarisation of the region
and the growing network of military blocs facilitating NATO’s expansion into Asia.
Russia reaffirmed its consistent commitment to
fostering a secure, conflict-free environment in the Asia-Pacific
conducive to sustainable growth and cooperation on an equal footing. It also expressed its interest in maintaining
an open, depoliticised dialogue within the EAS
, with a focus on practical cooperation.
Regional partners were briefed on
Russia’s proposals for a seamless architecture of security and joint development across Eurasia
, including by bringing together the capabilities of multilateral mechanisms such as the
#SCO
,
#ASEAN
and
#EAEU
, which pursue
a broad, positive agenda
.
