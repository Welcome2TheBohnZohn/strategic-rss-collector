🇲🇳
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the meeting with President of Mongolia Ukhnaagiin Khurelsukh
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Russia-Mongolia relations of strategic partnership are based on
long-standing traditions
of good-neighborliness, friendship and mutual understanding of each other’s interests.
This year, on November 5, we will mark
the 105th anniversary of the establishment of
diplomatic relations
. That is a significant milestone – 105 years.
📈
In 2025, bilateral trade increased by 4.5%
. We are also seeing positive results this year: in the first half of 2026,
trade grew by
more than 18%
. That is a strong indicator.
The Russian-Mongolian Intergovernmental Commission on Trade and Scientific and Technical Cooperation is working
actively
and, in my view,
very intensively and effectively
.
I hope that
the provisional trade agreement between the Eurasian Economic Union and Mongolia
, which entered into force in July 2026, will provide
preferential conditions for supplies of traditional Mongolian exports
throughout the customs territory of our integration association.
We cooperate constructively within multilateral organisations and forums
, since our positions on many pressing international issues coincide or are close.
Today,
the Summit of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
was held
successfully and productively
. Mongolia took part as an observer and, under the decision adopted today, will now receive
partner status
.
🤝
We remain in
regular contact
. Whenever necessary, we can always speak by phone – I am always at your disposal.
