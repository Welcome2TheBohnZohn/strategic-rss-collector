# 🎙 Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s answer to a media question at a joint news conference with Peru’s Foreign Minister Carlos Espa

Published: 2026-11-09 20:03:38

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33276

🎙
Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
answer to a media question
at a joint news conference with Peru’s Foreign Minister Carlos Espa
(Moscow, November 9, 2026)
❓
Question: What are your expectations for the upcoming talks in Abu Dhabi? Speaking about the talks earlier, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said that the conflict would have to be resolved before discussions could begin on security guarantees for Ukraine and Europe. We noticed that the Russian delegation has been expanded this time to include Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister Sergey Ryabkov. Does this mean there will be a separate negotiating track?
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
I heard US Secretary of State
Marco Rubio’s
remarks
about the growing importance of security issues, both at the Abu Dhabi talks and in the broader context of the Ukraine conflict.
In fact, the first item on the agenda in Abu Dhabi is
the security of Russia, Ukraine and the region as a whole
. We can only welcome this approach.
Let me remind you what we did to avert what would otherwise have been inevitable.
We know what plans the Kiev regime was making in late 2021 and early 2022
to resolve the Donbass issue by force. In December 2021, acting on instructions from President Vladimir Putin,
we presented the US and NATO members with
two draft treaties
aimed at strengthening the foundations of equal and indivisible security.
Several meetings followed
. A Russian interagency delegation met with our NATO counterparts.
I then met in Geneva with Antony Blinken
, who was US Secretary of State at the time. We explained to him in detail why
it was essential to do everything in our power to put the principles of indivisible security into practice
. These principles had been proclaimed loud and clear at the highest level of the OSCE,
👉
yet they were being swept under the carpet
.
One of them states that
no organisation in the Euro-Atlantic area has the right to claim military and political dominance
. NATO was doing precisely the opposite.
That principle was endorsed
as far back as 1999 at
the Istanbul Summit
and reaffirmed in 2010 at
the Astana Summit
.
After
the talks
between the Russian and US delegations, during which we pressed these points,
Antony Blinken
asked to speak to me in private.
He told me we would get nowhere
. The US had already made its decision, he said. By then, the Americans
had withdrawn
from
the Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces Treaty
and were preparing to deploy these missiles in various parts of the world.
☝️
Antony Blinken said the only assurance they could give us was that there might be limits on the deployment in Ukraine of intermediate- and shorter-range missiles aimed at Russia
. As for a guarantee that Ukraine would stay out of NATO, he said that was out of the question. They would never make such a promise.
In other words,
the administration of then US President Joe Biden could not have cared less about “European security”
, particularly the security of Russia, which is our primary concern.
We can only welcome the fact that the current US negotiators, acting on instructions from President
Donald Trump
, have proposed
putting collective security at the top of the agenda
for the talks on a settlement in Ukraine.
👉
Everyone understands that the purpose of these talks is
to agree on the principles to be endorsed at the conclusion of negotiations on a comprehensive settlement
.
It is entirely natural that another of my deputies,
Sergey Ryabkov
, should join
Mikhail Galuzin
in the delegation. His responsibilities cover
North Atlantic affairs
and
arms control
in the broadest sense. He has also spent a considerable part of his career working on
OSCE matters
.
