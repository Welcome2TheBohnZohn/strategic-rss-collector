# 🇷🇺 On October 16, Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov met with Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Serbia

Published: 2026-10-16 17:49:57

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32798

🇷🇺
🇷🇸
On October 16, Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
met
with Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Serbia
Ivica Dačić
in Moscow.
The Parties discussed the current state of the
Russian-Serbian cooperation
and its future prospects across key areas.
Regarding international issues, particular emphasis was placed on the
situation in the Balkans
.
The Ministers underscored the fundamental importance of
UN Security Council Resolution 1244
for the
settlement of the Kosovo problem
, as well as their mutual interest in maintaining stability in the region.
