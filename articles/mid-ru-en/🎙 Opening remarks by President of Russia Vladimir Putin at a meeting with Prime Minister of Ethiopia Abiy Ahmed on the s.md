🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with Prime Minister of Ethiopia Abiy Ahmed on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 28, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Once again, congratulations on
your election as Prime Minister
.
Ethiopia actively participated
in
the Russia–Africa Summits in Sochi in 2019
and
St Petersburg in 2023
. Our talks today are taking place during your visit to attend what is now
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
. I would like to thank you for finding the opportunity to come to Moscow once again.
🤝
I value the friendly relations between Russia and Ethiopia
, as well as our personal contacts, which are always substantive and constructive.
And, of course, I recall
our recent talks
at
the #BRICS Summit in New Delhi
in September, when we compared notes on
the key areas of bilateral cooperation
.
📈
I would like to highlight
the substantial growth in bilateral trade
, which
exceeded $1.3 billion
in January-September this year. This is
more than double the figure
for the whole of 2025. We have
many promising large-scale projects
across a wide range of areas, from energy, agriculture and industrial cooperation to the peaceful exploration of outer space, science, healthcare and education.
Russia and Ethiopia stand together in international affairs
. We maintain dialogue at the
#UN
, within
#BRICS
and in other multilateral forums. We know that Addis Ababa is scheduled to host
the UN Climate Change Conference in 2027
. We wish you every success in hosting this major international forum.
Russia fully supports Africa’s emergence as one of the key centres of the new
#MultipolarWorld
order
. We will pay particular attention to expanding trade and economic cooperation with African countries, ensuring food and energy security, and developing healthcare.
At the conclusion of
the Summit,
we will adopt
its Declaration
and
a new Action Plan of
the Russia–Africa Partnership Forum for 2027–2029.
🇷🇺
🇪🇹
We now have a good opportunity to continue discussing
the main tasks for further strengthening the multifaceted ties between Russia and Ethiopia
, as well as current international and regional issues. We have already begun exchanging views on some of these in our one-on-one conversation.
