On October 12-15, Secretary-General of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (
#ASEAN
) Kao Kim Hourn
will pay a working visit to Moscow
.
On October 13, he will hold talks with Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
.
Meetings are scheduled at the Presidential Executive Office,
Russia's EMERCOM
,
Ministry of Science and Higher Education
,
Ministry of Agriculture
,
Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media
, and
Ministry of Energy
.
Kao Kim Hourn
is also scheduled to meet with
Bakytzhan Sagintayev
, Chairman of the Board of
the Eurasian Economic Commission
.
The programme includes meetings with business representatives and experts, as well as addresses at
a thematic session of
the Russian Energy Week International Forum
(
#REW2026
) and
the conference “Russia and ASEAN in the Asia-Pacific Region”
at
MGIMO University
.
🌐
#ASEAN
is one of the largest regional integration organisations
, bringing together
11 Southeast Asian countries
with a combined population of
684 million
, a total GDP of
$3.9 trillion
and foreign trade turnover of
$3.8 trillion
.
The Association will mark its 60th anniversary in 2027.
Over the years, ASEAN has established itself as
a cornerstone of the security and cooperation architecture
in the Asia-Pacific region.
It serves as the hub for multilateral cooperation frameworks involving leading world powers
–
the East Asia Summit
(
#EAS
),
the ASEAN Regional Forum
(
#ARF
) and
the ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting Plus
(ADMM-Plus).
#RussiaASEAN
***
🤝
Since our first contacts with the Association in 1991,
our dialogue has developed into a strategic partnership
, established in 2018.
Strengthening this partnership is
one of Russia’s priorities in the Asia-Pacific region
, as reaffirmed by
the Russia-ASEAN Summit held in Kazan
on June 17-18 this year to mark
the 35th anniversary of relations
.
Kao Kim Hourn’s upcoming visit to Moscow is one of the key events of this anniversary year
. Discussions will focus on implementing the tasks set by our leaders to advance cooperation under
the Comprehensive Plan of Action to Implement the Russia-ASEAN Strategic Partnership (2026-2030)
.
