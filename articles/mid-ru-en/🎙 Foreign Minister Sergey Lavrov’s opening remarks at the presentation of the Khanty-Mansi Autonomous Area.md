# 🎙 Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s opening remarks at the presentation of the Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area

Published: 2026-11-26 22:00:22

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33673

🎙
Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov’s
opening remarks
at the presentation of the
Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area
(Moscow, November 26, 2026)
💬
Mr Kukharuk, Ladies and gentlemen, colleagues and friends,
I am delighted to welcome you to the presentation of the
Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area – Yugra
, a distinctive region of the Russian Federation about which you have just been given a brief but, to my mind, highly vivid account. I am convinced that the remainder of the presentation will spark even greater interest among you.
Unfortunately, I have visited Khanty-Mansiysk only once. That was long ago, in 2007, when I took part in the
Conference on the Development of International Regional Ties of the Constituent Entities of the Russian Federation
and attended the opening of the local branch of the Russian Commission for UNESCO there. Almost 20 years have passed, and today it is particularly gratifying for me to highlight the successes achieved by this region of the Russian Federation.
☝️
It is an industrially developed area with immense economic, investment and tourism potential
and a most interesting history, culture and traditions. Today, I promised Governor of the Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area – Yugra Ruslan
Ruslan Kukharuk
that I would certainly visit next year, all the more so as there will be great reasons to come back. We will certainly follow through – we will continue our close cooperation.
The diversification of the regional economy and the creation of an innovative economic architecture, driven by the leadership of Yugra, make it possible to achieve a
synergistic effect
from the harmonious combination of vast mineral resources, modern technologies, and high-quality human capital. As a result, the Khanty-Mansi Area
occupies a prominent place
among the regions with a high share of exports and among the leading contributors to the federal budget. <...>
Yugra’s unique experience in preserving the culture and traditional way of life of the
indigenous peoples of the North – the Khanty, Mansi and Nenets
– as well as its efforts to preserve biodiversity, which is so vital for our planet now, deserves particular attention. Representatives of the region’s indigenous peoples are traditionally involved in the work of Russian delegations at specialised UN fora in
New York, Geneva and Nairobi
.
<...>
🤝
On the instructions of President
Vladimir Putin
, an International Friendship Club has been established in the region, bringing together participants from
44 foreign countries
. Moreover, the Club’s key project – the International Arctic Volunteer Corps – has been included in the national Action Plan for the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development.
At the end of last year, the Khanty-Mansi Area
ranked first among Russian regions in volunteer activity
, charity and the non-profit sector, and second in the national rating for the effectiveness of youth policy implementation. All this is in addition to the leading positions the Area occupies in the economy.
I strongly believe that today’s presentation will not only provide an account of Yugra’s achievements but
will also serve as a starting point
for new, interesting and promising joint initiatives involving partners from the countries represented here.
I have no doubt that you will recognise the true worth of the advantages and opportunities Yugra offers and will report on them to your governments and economic operators. Our
ministry
will do everything it can to help establish ties and advance promising, mutually beneficial projects.
