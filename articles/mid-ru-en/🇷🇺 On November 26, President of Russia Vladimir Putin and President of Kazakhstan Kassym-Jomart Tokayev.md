On November 26, President of Russia Vladimir Putin and President of Kazakhstan Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
took part
in the plenary session of
the Artificial Intelligence Journey conference
.
Ahead of the plenary session,
Vladimir Putin
and
Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
toured an exhibition
showcasing
the application of AI technologies in Russia and Kazakhstan across various sectors
, including education, healthcare, public administration, industry and transport.
💬
Excerpts from President of Russia
Vladimir Putin’s
remarks
at the plenary session of the conference:
We will strive to ensure that
AI is increasingly widely adopted in Russia, encompassing every sector of the economy and every aspect of people’s lives.
Why is this task of
fundamental importance
?
Almost ten years ago – allow me to recall this –
I said
that if any country achieved leadership in developing artificial intelligence,
that country would rule the world.
The situation has indeed changed since then, and radically so. Merely possessing even the latest generation of artificial intelligence
no longer guarantees either technological superiority or sustainable, long-term development
.
To be among the leaders, it is not enough simply to develop cutting-edge solutions.
We need to ensure that people and autonomous systems work together as seamlessly as possible, reshaping management and production processes, the economy and the social sphere accordingly.
With precisely this approach in mind, I propose
updating Russia’s National AI Strategy through 2036
and adopting
a National Action Plan in this field
.
Following the event,
Vladimir Putin
and
Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
met with schoolchildren and university students
from
Russia
,
India
and
Kazakhstan
– young talents in the field of AI.
