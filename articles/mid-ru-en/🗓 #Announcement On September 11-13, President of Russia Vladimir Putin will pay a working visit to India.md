# 🗓 #Announcement On September 11-13, President of Russia Vladimir Putin will pay a working visit to India

Published: 2026-09-10 13:14:57

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31962

#Announcement
🗓
On September 11-13, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
will pay
a working visit to India
to attend
the XVIII BRICS Summit
. The event will take place in New Delhi as
#BRICS
marks its 20th anniversary
.
On September 11,
Vladimir Putin
will address
the Plenary Session of the annual
BRICS Business Forum
, which traditionally precedes the Summit and serves as an important platform for dialogue among business representatives from BRICS countries.
On the same day, Russia's President will hold
a bilateral meeting with Prime Minister of India Narendra Modi
and join him for a visit to
the Innoprom exhibition
, organised by the two countries’ Ministries of Industry and Trade.
The main Summit programme will begin on September 12 with
a meeting of BRICS Leaders
to discuss initiatives put forward by
the Indian Chairship
and prospects for further strengthening the BRICS strategic partnership in politics and security, economics and finance, and cultural and humanitarian ties. The Leaders will also exchange views on current international and regional issues.
On September 13,
an expanded BRICS meeting
will bring together the Heads of delegations from BRICS member and partner countries, as well as invited countries and Heads of international organisations.
✍️
The key agreements reached are expected to be reflected in
the Summit’s final document
.
During the events in New Delhi,
Vladimir Putin
will also hold
a number of other bilateral meetings
.
