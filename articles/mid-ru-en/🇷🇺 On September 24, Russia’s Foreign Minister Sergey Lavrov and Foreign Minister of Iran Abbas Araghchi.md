On September 24, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Iran Abbas Araghchi
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Sides discussed current regional and international issues, focusing on
developments surrounding Iran
.
❗️
They stressed that
there is no alternative to a diplomatic settlement of the crisis
provoked by
the unlawful actions of the US and Israel
.
Attention was also paid to
implementing the bilateral agreements reached at
the meeting
between the two Presidents
in Bishkek on September 1, 2026.
The Ministers reviewed
the schedule of upcoming Russia-Iran contacts
at various levels.
