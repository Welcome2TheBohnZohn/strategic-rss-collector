On October 13, President of Russia Vladimir Putin and President of the United Arab Emirates Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan
had a telephone conversation
.
With this year marking
the 55th anniversary of
diplomatic relations
between Russia and the UAE
, the Presidents discussed
ways to expand strategic and mutually beneficial bilateral cooperation.
During their exchange of views on international affairs, they discussed
the agreements reached during Vladimir Putin’s
telephone conversation
with US President Donald Trump
regarding
Russian diesel fuel supplies
, as well as
prospects for resolving the crisis in the Persian Gulf region
.
🤝
The Presidents agreed to continue their personal contacts.
