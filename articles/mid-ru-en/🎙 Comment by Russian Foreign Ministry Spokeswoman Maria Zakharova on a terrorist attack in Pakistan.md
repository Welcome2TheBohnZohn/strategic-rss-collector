# 🎙 Comment by Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova on a terrorist attack in Pakistan

Published: 2026-09-19 02:59:53

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32107

🎙
Comment
by Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova on a terrorist attack in Pakistan
(September 18, 2026)
💬
We were concerned to learn of the terrorist attack by a group of terrorists, including a suicide bomber, on a police station in Kohat, Pakistan, on September 18,
which left 16 people dead and more than 50 injured.
❗️
We strongly condemn every form and manifestation of terrorism
and consistently advocate for all countries to join efforts to effectively combat this common threat.
🕯
We extend our deepest condolences to the families and loved ones of those who died in the attack and wish the injured a speedy recovery.
