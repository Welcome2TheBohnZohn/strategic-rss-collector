Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with Chairman of the Presidential Council of Libya Mohamed Yunus al-Menfi on the sidelines of
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
(Moscow, October 27, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We are pleased
to see you again
and welcome you to Moscow.
Libya is our longstanding partner in North Africa.
Last year marked
the 70th anniversary
of diplomatic relations.
Cooperation at multilateral platforms
, particularly within
the Gas Exporting Countries Forum
and
OPEC+
,
enables us to jointly develop measures to ensure global energy security.
Tomorrow, we will begin work at
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
, and I am confident that this, too, will provide
an opportunity for interesting and useful discussions
.
📈
Despite the difficult international political and economic situation, bilateral trade continues to grow
. The co-chairs of our joint Intergovernmental Commission
held their first meeting in Moscow in April
. We are, of course, interested in seeing the Commission resume its full operations as soon as possible.
Russian universities continue to train specialists for Libya
. This year,
we increased the number of state-funded places
for your students to 100. In total,
1,500 Libyan students are studying at Russian universities
. In 2025-2026,
25 diplomats from Tripoli and Benghazi
completed professional development courses at
the Diplomatic Academy of the Russian MFA
.
Today, we have a good opportunity to exchange views on
prospects for expanding bilateral cooperation
and discuss t
he most pressing international and regional issues
.
