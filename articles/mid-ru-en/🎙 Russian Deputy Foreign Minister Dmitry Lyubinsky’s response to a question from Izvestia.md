# 🎙 Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Dmitry Lyubinsky’s response to a question from Izvestia

Published: 2026-11-19 08:01:43

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33488

🎙
Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Dmitry Lyubinsky’s
response
to a question from
Izvestia
on the possible resumption of Russian gas supplies to Germany
(November 19, 2026)
❓
Question: The trial over
the Nord Stream pipeline bombings
began in Germany on October 14. On October 21, Russian Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said it was technically possible to supply gas to Germany through the remaining intact pipeline. Is Germany sharing information with Russia on the progress of the investigation? Under what conditions would Russia be ready to resume gas flows through that pipeline?
💬
Dmitry Lyubinsky:
In the four years since
the terrorist attack
on the Nord Stream 1 and Nord Stream 2 pipelines,
the German authorities have consistently avoided cooperating with Russia
, in breach of their international obligations under agreements on mutual legal assistance in criminal matters and counter-terrorism conventions.
Russia and several other countries called for
an independent, open and objective international investigation
under UN auspices.
That proposal was rejected.
It is therefore
very difficult
to regard either the German judicial authorities or the findings of the investigation as impartial.
☝️
Resuming gas supplies requires
, first and foremost,
political will and a readiness to act on the part of the German authorities.
👉
There is no sign of either.
