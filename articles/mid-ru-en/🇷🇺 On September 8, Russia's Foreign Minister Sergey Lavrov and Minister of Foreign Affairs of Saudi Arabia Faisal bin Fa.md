On September 8, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Minister of Foreign Affairs of Saudi Arabia Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud
held talks in Moscow
.
👉
Opening remarks by Sergey Lavrov
👉
Joint press conference of the Ministers
The Foreign Ministers spoke in favour of maintaining regular political dialogue and expanding exchanges of delegations.
They stressed the need to deepen business cooperation in order to ensure sustained positive momentum in bilateral trade.
❗️
The Ministers conducted a thorough exchange of views on key international and regional issues.
Priority attention was given to the escalation of tensions in the Persian Gulf as a result of
US-Israeli aggression against Iran
.
Sergey Lavrov briefed his Saudi counterpart in detail on
the updated Russian Concept of Collective Security in the Persian Gulf
. He reaffirmed Russia’s readiness for constructive engagement with all interested parties with a view to building a comprehensive security architecture and promoting interstate cooperation in this strategically important region.
The Ministers
expressed mutual concern over rising tensions in the south of the Arabian Peninsula and in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.
They noted that the path towards lasting stabilisation in Yemen lies through strict observance of the ceasefire and the earliest possible launch of an inclusive national dialogue involving all of the country’s political forces under UN auspices.
The Sides expressed a shared position in support of preserving Syria’s unity, territorial integrity and sovereignty
. They reaffirmed the need to consolidate international efforts in support of its post-conflict recovery.
Sergey Lavrov outlined Russia’s assessments of the evolving geopolitical situation, including the situation in Ukraine.
He reaffirmed Russia’s commitment to a comprehensive and lasting settlement of the Ukrainian crisis, which can only be achieved through the complete and irreversible elimination of its
root causes
.
