The world, particularly Europe, is still reeling from the news of the “diesel deal” between Russian President Vladimir Putin and US President Donald Trump. Yesterday, the US President
announced

Regarding the status of this agreement,
we can refer to Zelensky’s reaction
. When asked the same question, he said he had not heard of such an agreement, would welcome clarification and was ready for a ceasefire.
He has said this many times. For many months now, the slogan of a “ceasefire” has replaced that of
“inflicting a strategic defeat on Russia”
in the rhetoric of both Zelensky and his Western “handlers”.
The aim is the same and very simple – to buy time and secure a respite.
His feigned surprise at President Donald Trump’s
statement
reveals just one thing: he is eager to seize on any words, initiatives or ideas that enter the public domain
to pursue, by fair means or foul, his goal of securing a respite
.
❗️
I do not think Zelensky has earned a respite. I do not think we will once again fall for assurances and promises.
President
Vladimir Putin
has made it very clear
that
we continue to pursue the objectives of
the special military operation
