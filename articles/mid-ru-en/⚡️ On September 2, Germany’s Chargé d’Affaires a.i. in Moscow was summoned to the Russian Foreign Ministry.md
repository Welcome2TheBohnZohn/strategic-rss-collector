⚡️
On September 2, Germany’s Chargé d’Affaires a.i. in Moscow
was summoned
to the Russian Foreign Ministry.
The German diplomat
was told
that
the Russian Side
categorically rejects
Berlin’s completely fabricated allegations
of Russia’s involvement in the purported incident at Leipzig Airport.
It was stressed that
the German Government’s decision
to use this contrived pretext to close
the Russian Consulate General in Bonn
and
the Russian House of Science and Culture in Berlin
is viewed as
yet another step towards the complete dismantling of the bilateral relationship
painstakingly built by generations of Russian and German politicians and diplomats.
It was emphasised that
Berlin deliberately resorted to an outright provocation
aimed at escalating Russian-German relations
.
❗️
The actions of the German authorities will be met with a tough response.
