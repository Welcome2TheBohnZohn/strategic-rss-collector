Declaration of the APEC Economic Leaders 2026
(Shenzhen, November 19, 2026)
Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together
✍️
We, the Economic Leaders of
#APEC
,
gathered in Shenzhen
, China, on November 18 to 19, 2026.
Decades after APEC’s establishment, our sustained efforts have helped make our region
a key engine of global growth and a champion of regional economic cooperation
.
Under the theme
“Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together”
, we have built on our work throughout the year with China’s APEC 2026 priorities
“Openness, Innovation, Cooperation”
, and advanced our shared objectives toward realizing an open, dynamic, resilient, and peaceful Asia-Pacific community, guided by
the Putrajaya Vision 2040
.
The world today is changing rapidly
, with new opportunities emerging alongside
complex challenges
. Technological breakthroughs are contributing to the economic growth, resilience, and dynamism of our region. We endeavor to work together
to maintain
macroeconomic stability,
promote
productivity and dynamism, and
boost
economic growth for the benefit of all our people.
APEC has long been at the forefront of regional economic cooperation
, and its enduring role remains relevant in the current economic context.
🤝
We reaffirm our commitment to APEC as the premier forum for regional economic cooperation
, guided by its voluntary, non-binding and consensus-building principles, and to making APEC
a more modern, efficient, and effective incubator of ideas
.
