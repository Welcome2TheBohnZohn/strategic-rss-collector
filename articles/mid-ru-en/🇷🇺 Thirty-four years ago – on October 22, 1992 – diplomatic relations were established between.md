Thirty-four years ago – on
October 22, 1992
– diplomatic relations were established between
the Russian Federation
and
the Republic of Kazakhstan.
The peoples of our countries are bound together by closely aligned cultural, spiritual and moral values, a centuries-old history of coexistence within a single state, and shared achievements and victories.
Russian-Kazakhstani relations are grounded in strong historical ties of friendship, mutual trust and respect for each other’s interests, and are underpinned by a
strategic partnership and alliance.
🤝
The leaders of our countries, who have developed close and cordial personal ties over years of collaboration,
hold regular meetings and telephone conversations
to exchange views on both current bilateral matters and issues on the regional and global agendas.
At the end of May this year, President of the Russian Federation
Vladimir Putin
paid a state visit to Kazakhstan.
Following talks with President of the Republic of Kazakhstan
Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
, a number of bilateral documents were signed, including
the Joint Statement on the Seven Foundations of Friendship and Neighbourliness between the Peoples of Russia and Kazakhstan.
In October 2025, Foreign Minister of the Russian Federation
Sergey Lavrov
held talks
with Foreign Minister of the Republic of Kazakhstan
Yermek Kosherbayev
during his first official foreign visit to Moscow. On April 30, 2026, Sergey Lavrov, in turn,
paid an official visit to Kazakhstan.
On 25 September, on the sidelines of the High-Level Week of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly,
Sergey Lavrov
met
with Minister of Foreign Affairs of Kazakhstan
Mukhtar Tileuberdi.
📈
Russia is one of Kazakhstan’s leading trade and economic partners.
At the end of 2025, bilateral trade volume
totalled $27.4 billion.
Our country also remains the largest investor in the Kazakhstani economy.
Russian capital is present in numerous sectors, including metallurgy, mechanical engineering, energy, and pharmaceuticals.
Russia and Kazakhstan play an important role in advancing integration processes in the post-Soviet space. Our countries were among
the founders of the Eurasian Economic Union
(
#EAEU
) and today cooperate actively within the
#CIS
, the
#CSTO
and the
#SCO
.
💬
From
Sergey Lavrov
’s
opening remarks
during talks with Foreign Minister of the Republic of Kazakhstan
Yermek Kosherbayev
(Astana, April 30, 2026)
:
Kazakhstan and Russia are
close neighbours and allies.
Our people are bound by centuries-long traditions of friendship and mutual respect. These relations are enshrined in numerous interstate documents.
One of the most important aspects of the quality of our relations is our shared memory of Victory in the Great Patriotic War. We always celebrate the anniversaries of the Great Victory together and hold special events in both Kazakhstan and the Russian Federation.
🎉
We offer our warmest congratulations to our Kazakh colleagues and friends on our shared holiday. We reaffirm our confidence in the further dynamic and steady development of friendly relations and the expansion of Russian-Kazakhstani allied ties.
