# ⚡️ Russia’s Minister of Sport Mikhail Degtyarev The Court of Arbitration for Sport #CAS

Published: 2026-10-30 11:01:34

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33104

⚡️
Russia’s Minister of Sport Mikhail Degtyarev
:
The Court of Arbitration for Sport
(
#CAS
) has ordered
the International Ice Hockey Federation
(
#IIHF
) to admit Russian national teams to international competitions
.
CAS
overturned
all five resolutions adopted by the IIHF Council
and ordered that Russian teams be allowed to compete in the IIHF events covered by those resolutions.
CAS thus
upheld the key claims
of
the Ice Hockey Federation of Russia
and
overturned the IIHF decisions
that prevented Russian teams from participating in international competitions.
❗️
Under
the ruling
,
the IIHF must ensure Russian teams’ participation in the relevant tournaments in the 2026/27 season.
The CAS ruling settles this issue, as
it is final and cannot be appealed
.
Challenging work lies ahead
as we work with the IIHF to implement the CAS ruling and secure Russian teams’ return to competitions held under its auspices.
🏒
The next event is
the World Junior Championship
, which will take place in the Canadian cities of Edmonton and Red Deer.
