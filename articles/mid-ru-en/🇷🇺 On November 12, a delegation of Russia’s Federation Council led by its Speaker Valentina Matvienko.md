# 🇷🇺 On November 12, a delegation of Russia’s Federation Council led by its Speaker Valentina Matvienko

Published: 2026-11-12 08:00:41

🇷🇺
🇻🇳
On November 12, a delegation of Russia’s Federation Council led by its Speaker
Valentina Matvienko
began
an official visit
to the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
Valentina Matvienko
held a meeting
with Prime Minister of Vietnam
Le Minh Hung.
Their talks focused on
trade and economic cooperation
, including at the regional level,
the implementation of joint projects
and
the development of tourism
.
💬
Valentina Matvienko:
Our delegation includes regional leaders and representatives of Russian state corporations.
The purpose of this official visit is to do everything we can through our Parliament to put into effect
the agreements reached by our Leaders
and implement the intergovernmental documents our two countries have signed.
***
Valentina Matvienko
was also received
by General Secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam and President of Vietnam
To Lam
.
The Speaker informed the Vietnamese Leader that President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
had issued
an extensive set of instructions
to the Russian Government, ministries, agencies and state corporations covering
all the agreements
reached by the two countries’ Leaders.
💬
Valentina Matvienko:
First of all, I would like to convey warm greetings and best wishes from President of Russia Vladimir Putin.
He speaks warmly of
his meetings with you
and values the special relationship you have built – one marked by a businesslike approach, friendship and trust.
