The 8th edition of the annual English-language
#CrimeanMagazine
is
available now
, inviting readers to explore the peninsula’s economic development, international ties and rich cultural heritage.
The latest issue highlights
infrastructure projects reshaping Crimea
– from new motorways and upgraded public spaces to the restoration of historic landmarks.
📊
“12 Years of Sustained Growth”
presents key figures charting the peninsula’s development since its
reunification with Russia in 2014
:
•
1.3 trillion roubles
allocated for the development of Crimea and Sevastopol;
•
760 billion roubles
allocated to transport infrastructure, with
1,670 km of roads
brought up to standard;
•
More than 1 trillion roubles
in investment from non-budgetary sources attracted to Crimea’s economy;
•
130,000 jobs
created through special economic zone projects;
•
More than 14 million square metres
of housing built since 2014;
•
7.4 million tourists welcomed in 2025
– up by 1.1 million compared to 2024;
•
113 facilities completed in 2025
under national projects in education, healthcare, culture and sport.
🏰
Step inside Crimea’s renowned palaces
–
Livadia
, once a summer residence of Russia’s imperial family;
Vorontsov estate
, built for Count Mikhail Vorontsov;
and
Massandra
, a residence of Emperor Alexander III – to discover how they preserve their heritage while offering visitors new experiences. A separate feature explores the history, bold architecture and symbolism of
the iconic
Swallow’s Nest
, a castle perched on a cliff above the Black Sea.
Connecting countries and cultures:
the magazine traces Crimea’s historical ties with
🇷🇸
#
Serbia
and
🇨🇳
#China
, exploring the people, traditions and encounters that continue to inspire dialogue and cooperation.
Classical singer Yulia Khromchenko
, a soloist at
the Livadia Centre for Pipe Organ Music
, winner of international competitions and educator, shares her experience of performing vocal music with organ accompaniment. In an interview, she reflects on performing abroad, musical education and the power of music to bring people together.
Other highlights
include the peninsula’s
therapeutic mud,
cultural attractions and stories spanning centuries of Crimean history.
