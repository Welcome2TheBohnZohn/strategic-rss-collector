On September 19, Russian citizens living or staying currently abroad continued to actively participate in the voting.
Today, Russian citizens cast their ballots at polling stations in
China
,
Iraq
,
Yemen
and
Libya
, and elsewhere.
From oil workers at the
Sarkala oil field
to an
Aeroflot
crew at the
Consulate General in Guangzhou
, Russian diplomats are doing their best to ensure
every Russian citizen has the opportunity to exercise their right to vote
. And do that with arms wide open.
Voting abroad began on September 18 at a number of Russian diplomatic missions. The two of the three voting days took place in
Cyprus
, Thailand (
Phuket
)
and
South Ossetia
, while voting was also held in
Kuwait
and
Bahrain
.

, as well as on the
