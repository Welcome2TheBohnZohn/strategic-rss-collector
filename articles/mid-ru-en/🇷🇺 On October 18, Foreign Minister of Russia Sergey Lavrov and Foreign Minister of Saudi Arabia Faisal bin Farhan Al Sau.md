On October 18, Foreign Minister of Russia Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Saudi Arabia Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud
held a meeting in Manama
on the sidelines of the ninth Ministerial Meeting of the Russia-Gulf Cooperation Council (
#GCC
) Strategic Dialogue
.
The Ministers discussed
priority issues
in the multifaceted Russia-Saudi Arabia relationship.
Both Sides expressed their commitment to
maintaining regular, frank political dialogue
and
expanding trade, economic, investment, cultural and humanitarian cooperation
.
They reaffirmed the importance of
continuing effective cooperation between Russia and Saudi Arabia
within the
#OPECplus
framework.
The Ministers considered
current international and Middle Eastern issues
, including the situation in the Persian Gulf. They
expressed concern
over tensions in the southern Arabian Peninsula and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.
Both Sides emphasised
the importance of ensuring the safety of navigation and the unimpeded passage of vessels
through all international waterways.
