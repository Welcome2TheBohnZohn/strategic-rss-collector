Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with President of Kazakhstan Kassym-Jomart Tokayev on the sidelines of the SCO Summit
(Bishkek, August 31, 2026)
.
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am very pleased
to see you again
– this time on the sidelines of events related to
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
.
Before we begin, I would like
to personally
congratulate you
on the results of the Kurultai parliamentary elections.
I would also like to note the high voter turnout and, most importantly, the election results, which confirm that
the people of Kazakhstan support the course you have chosen for the country’s further development,
strengthening its domestic political stability, advancing the economy and, on that basis, addressing a broad range of social issues and improving people’s living standards.
☝️
That is what people voted for.
Our bilateral relations are developing dynamically across virtually all areas.
Trade figures were solid in 2026, and trade has grown further this year.
