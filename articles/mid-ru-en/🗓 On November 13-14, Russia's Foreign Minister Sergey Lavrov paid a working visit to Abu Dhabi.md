# 🗓 On November 13-14, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov paid a working visit to Abu Dhabi

Published: 2026-11-15 13:01:50

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33362

🗓
On November 13-14, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
paid a working visit to Abu Dhabi
to attend
the 17th Sir Bani Yas Forum on Peace and Security
.
🇷🇺
🇦🇪
During the visit, he held talks with UAE Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs
Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan
.
The Ministers discussed
key areas for further developing the traditionally friendly Russia-UAE relations,
which constitute a strategic partnership.
They noted that the two countries approach
the 55th anniversary of
diplomatic relations
on December 8 with
impressive achievements
in trade, economic, cultural and humanitarian cooperation.
The Sides reaffirmed Moscow and Abu Dhabi’s commitment to maintaining
an active political dialogue
and
strengthening multifaceted cooperation
based on agreements
reached at the highest level
.
The Ministers held an in-depth exchange on key global and regional issues, focusing on the continuing tensions in the Persian Gulf
. They stressed that differences must be resolved through
political and diplomatic mean
s, in strict compliance with international law and with full regard for the legitimate interests of all states involved in the conflict.
They also highlighted the need to ensure
stable, unimpeded and safe navigation through the Strait of Hormuz
, which is vital to the global economy.
Sergey Lavrov
once again drew attention to
Russia’s
updated Concept for Collective Security in the Persian Gulf
. He reaffirmed Russia’s readiness to engage constructively with all interested countries and organisations to build a comprehensive security architecture in this strategically important region.
The Ministers exchanged views on
the Palestinian-Israeli conflict and developments in Lebanon, Sudan and Libya
. Russia and the UAE supported continued concerted efforts to find mutually acceptable political solutions based on the relevant UN Security Council resolutions.
👉
Sergey Lavrov outlined Russia’s assessment of developments surrounding Ukraine.
He reaffirmed Russia’s unwavering commitment to a comprehensive and lasting settlement of the Ukraine crisis, which can only be achieved by fully and irreversibly eliminating
its root causes
.
