# 🎙 Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s remarks at the 8th meeting of the Moscow Format Consultations on Afghanistan

Published: 2026-10-05 13:59:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32572

🎙
Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
remarks
at the 8th meeting of the Moscow Format Consultations on Afghanistan
(Moscow, October 5, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
It is a pleasure to open
the latest meeting of the Moscow Format Consultations on Afghanistan
.
This dialogue mechanism of high standing has been in operation since 2016 meaning that this particular meeting marks
its 10th anniversary
and brings together countries that have a stake in establishing and maintaining equal and mutually beneficial partnership with the Afghan people. It offers an opportunity to hold
a substantive discussion of matters related to providing international assistance to Afghanistan
and, importantly, to do so in conjunction with Kabul.
☝️
August 2026 marked the fifth anniversary of Afghanistan’s liberation from
two decades of Western occupation
. The vast military machine of the US and its NATO allies crumbled in the face of the Afghan people’s fighting spirit and determination, forcing the occupiers to flee. A national government led by the Taliban movement came to power and established control over the country. I
t must be acknowledged that it has governed with a fair degree of success.
Afghan security forces are waging an uncompromising fight against ISIS and other terrorist groups
. Hundreds of militants have been eliminated, and a number of high-profile terrorist attacks, including those targeting foreign nationals and diplomatic missions, have been foiled. Importantly, helping strengthen Kabul’s counterterrorism capabilities by providing material and expert assistance and maintaining contacts between intelligence and law enforcement agencies
meets the interests of all of us
.
The measures the Afghan government is taking to combat illicit drug trafficking produce results
. According to expert assessments by
the UN Office on Drugs and Crime
, opiate production in that country
has declined by 90%
since 2022.
The Afghan economy continues to grow despite the tough sanctions imposed by Western countries
, which, under the pretext of protecting human rights, have cut the Islamic Emirate off from the international financial system and seized its national banking assets. This is what the West is practicing on a regular basis.
Unfortunately, the humanitarian situation in the country remains difficult
.
According to the UN
,
more than 17 million Afghans
- nearly half the population -
will face food shortages this coming winter
, and the total number of people in need of humanitarian aid will reach
22 million
. Clearly, relevant international agencies need to step up, and bilateral efforts need to be deployed to address the situation at hand.
Russia will continue to provide regular humanitarian aid
, and
another batch
will arrive in Afghanistan in the coming months.
❗️
The deployment of NATO military infrastructure in Afghanistan and neighbouring countries under any pretext whatsoever is categorically unacceptable
. As we know from history, presence of this kind carries the risk of destabilisation and heightened tensions and conflict. We have seen no other outcome from this kind of presence over the past several decades.
I am confident that your meeting will be
constructive
and contribute to
Afghanistan’s further development as an independent and sustainable state
, and help promote peace and stability throughout the region.
