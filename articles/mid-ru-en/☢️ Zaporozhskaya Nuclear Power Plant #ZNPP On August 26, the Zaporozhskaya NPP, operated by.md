Zaporozhskaya Nuclear Power Plant
(
#ZNPP
):
On August 26, the Zaporozhskaya NPP, operated by
Rosatom
, came under
a Ukrainian drone attack
targeting its dry spent nuclear fuel storage facility.
No significant damage was recorded and no personnel were injured
.
No environmental impact was detected,
while radiation levels at the plant and in the surrounding area remain within normal limits.
❗️
An attack on a spent nuclear fuel storage facility is an extremely dangerous and unacceptable act
.
Damage to the infrastructure ensuring the safe storage of spent nuclear fuel
could have serious consequences
and poses
a potential threat
to nuclear and radiation safety.
ZNPP personnel are taking
all necessary measures
to ensure the plant’s safe operation.
