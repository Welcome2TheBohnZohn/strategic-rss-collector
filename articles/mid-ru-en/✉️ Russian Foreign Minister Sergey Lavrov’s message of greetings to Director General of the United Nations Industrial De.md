# ✉️ Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s message of greetings to Director General of the United Nations Industrial Development Organisation Gerd Müller

Published: 2026-11-24 08:30:41

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33571

✉️
Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
message of greetings
to Director General of the United Nations Industrial Development Organisation Gerd Müller
(November 23, 2026)
✍️
Please accept my sincere congratulations on
the 60th anniversary
of
the United Nations Industrial Development Organisation
(
#UNIDO
).
Over the past six decades, UNIDO has come a long way, establishing itself as
the leading specialised agency within the UN system in the field of industrial development
.
It makes
a significant contribution
to strengthening
the productive capacities of developing countries,
modernising
industry, and
promoting
the dissemination of advanced technologies.
Its efforts to promote
industrialisation in African countries
, which account for more than half of the Organisation’s project activities are particularly noteworthy.
☝️
R
ussia and UNIDO have a long history of constructive and mutually beneficial cooperation dating back to the Soviet era.
Our country was among the Organisation’s founding members and has consistently supported efforts to strengthen its capabilities.
We highly value
the practical results
achieved through our joint efforts over the years.
With Russia’s support, projects have been implemented in such areas as
industrial modernisation, improving labour productivity at enterprises across Eurasia and Latin America, facilitating trade, and promoting high-quality products on international markets.
New opportunities for cooperation are emerging within the
#BRICS
framework
, including through the establishment of a BRICS Centre for Industrial Competencies with UNIDO’s participation.
We look forward to further developing our partnership
with a view to building a fair and sustainable system of international industrial cooperation based on the principles of equality, mutual respect and due consideration for the national priorities of states.
🤝
I wish the leadership and staff of UNIDO sound health, wellbeing, and continued success in fulfilling the Organisation’s mandate.
SERGEY LAVROV
