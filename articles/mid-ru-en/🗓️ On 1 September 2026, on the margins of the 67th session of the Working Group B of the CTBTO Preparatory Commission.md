# 🗓️ On 1 September 2026, on the margins of the 67th session of the Working Group B of the CTBTO Preparatory Commission

Published: 2026-09-02 08:03:36

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31791

🗓️
On 1 September 2026, on the margins of
the 67th session of the Working Group B of the CTBTO Preparatory Commission
, an event was held with the support of the Permanent Mission of the Russian Federation, focusing on
the potential application of national artificial intelligence (AI) technologies
in support of the Organization’s activities
The event brought together
more than 120 participants
, including representatives of CTBTO Preparatory Commission Member States - diplomats and national experts - as well as officials of the Organization’s Provisional Technical Secretariat
🇷🇺
In his opening remarks, Deputy Permanent Representative
Roman Ustinov
emphasized that any potential introduction of AI into the work of the CTBTO Preparatory Commission should be approached with particular caution, taking into account, inter alia, the need to protect sensitive information; preserve human responsibility and the decisive role of human judgement in the use of AI; ensure transparency of the models employed; conduct a prior assessment of associated risks; and maintain accountability to the Organization’s policymaking organs
A central element of the event was a presentation by Professor
Evgeny Burnaev
, Vice President for AI Development and Director of the Skoltech AI Center, who outlined potential pilot projects demonstrating the capabilities of
Russian AI technologies
for possible use in support of the CTBTO Preparatory Commission
The solutions presented by Professor Burnaev are intended
to complement the Organization’s existing working mechanisms with tools
for intelligent data analysis and forecasting
The event generated
considerable professional interest among the diplomatic and expert communities
. In view of the importance and complexity of substantive discussions on this topic, consideration has been initiated regarding the possible establishment of
a dedicated expert group on the application of AI
in the activities of the CTBTO Preparatory Commission
