Russia and South Africa Strengthen Cooperation on Human Rights
On 2 September 2026, the latest round of
Russia–South Africa consultations on human rights
was held in Pretoria.
During the meeting between
Grigory Lukyantsev,
Director of the Russian MFA’s Department for Humanitarian Cooperation and Human Rights, and Ambassador
Xolisa Mabhongo,
Deputy Director-General of the South African Department of International Relations and Cooperation, both sides reaffirmed their commitment to building a
trust-based dialogue grounded in mutual respect and consideration of national interests.
Particular attention was paid to
coordinating efforts in international fora, primarily the
#UN
, as well as countering the politicisation of human rights and its use as a tool of geopolitical pressure and unfair competition.
The sides stressed the importance of maintaining the
impartiality of international institutions
, strictly adhering to the
UN Charter
, and eliminating
double standards
in assessing the global human rights situation.
☝️
A special focus was placed on combating
racism and racial discrimination
, including mutual support for Russian and South African initiatives at the UN and marking the
25th anniversary of the World Conference against Racism
, held in Durban, South Africa.
🤝
The sides also agreed to continue cooperation in connection with the
2026 anniversaries of the International Covenants on Civil and Political Rights and on Economic, Social and Cultural Right
s
, as well as the
UN Declaration on the Right to Development
.
The meeting demonstrated a
high level of mutual understanding
and opened new prospects for strengthening the partnership between Russia and South Africa on the international stage.
