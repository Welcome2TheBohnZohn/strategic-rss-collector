On November 23, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
held a meeting
with the Moscow-based Ambassadors of
the Association of Southeast Asian Nations
(
#ASEAN
) Member States.
The participants reviewed the results of the programme of events marking
the 35th anniversary of Russia-ASEAN relations
, celebrated this year.
They discussed progress in implementing
the decisions
of
the Russia-ASEAN Anniversary Summit
(Kazan, June 17-18, 2026).
🤝
Both Sides expressed their shared commitment to
stepping up efforts to develop new areas of cooperation
.
A frank exchange of views
took place on the situation in the Asia-Pacific region and developments within
ASEAN-centred mechanisms
, including
the East Asia Summit
,
the ASEAN Regional Forum
and
the ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting Plus
.
The participants emphasised the need to uphold
the fundamental principles of equality and consensus
underpinning these mechanisms, as well as their focus on practical cooperation aimed at strengthening
the regional architecture of security and cooperation
.
The meeting took place in a traditionally friendly atmosphere.
