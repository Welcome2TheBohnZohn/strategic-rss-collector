# 🇷🇺 On 3 October 2000, #RussiaIndia Declaration on Strategic Partnership was signed,

🇷🇺
🇮🇳
On 3 October 2000,
#RussiaIndia
Declaration on Strategic Partnership
was signed,
marking the beginning of a new era in Moscow–New Delhi relations and setting out the key areas for their further development.
The
Document was signed
during President of the Russian Federation
Vladimir Putin’
s state visit to India, which took place on 2–5 October 2000.
Since then,
Russian-Indian cooperation has reached an even higher level
— that of a
Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership
.
🤝
#India
is a long-standing and reliable partner of Russia.
For decades, our nations have maintained friendly and trusting relations, grounded in the
equal consideration of each other’s interests,
mutual respect and a shared commitment to advancing an inclusive and non-confrontational international agenda.
🌐
We are committed to shaping a
#MultipolarWorld
order
and advocate the democratisation
of international relations. Russia and India are enhancing their cooperation in multilateral formats, first and foremost within the
#G20
,
#UN
,
#BRICS
and
#SCO
.
Political dialogue
between Moscow and New Delhi at the highest and high levels, as well as through foreign ministries and other government agencies, remains dynamic.
💬
President of the Russian Federation
Vladimir Putin
in his
statement to the media following Russian-Indian talks
(New Delhi, 5 December 2025):
Russia and India pursue an independent and sovereign foreign policy.
Together with our BRICS partners and other countries of the Global Majority, we are promoting the
emergence of a fairer and more democratic multipolar world
order and upholding the fundamental principles of international law enshrined in the
#UNCharter
.
These include the right of every country to pursue its own path of development and preserve its cultural and civilisational identity, respect for sovereignty, and a carefully calibrated balance of interests of all members of the international community.
🎉
We extend our warmest congratulations to our Indian colleagues and friends on this shared milestone!
