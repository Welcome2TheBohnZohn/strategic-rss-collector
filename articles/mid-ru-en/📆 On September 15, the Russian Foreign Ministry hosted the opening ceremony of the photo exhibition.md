# 📆 On September 15, the Russian Foreign Ministry hosted the opening ceremony of the photo exhibition

Published: 2026-09-15 13:15:26

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32040

📆
On September 15, the Russian Foreign Ministry hosted
the opening ceremony
of the photo exhibition
“The Road to Friendship: Sun Yat-sen, Song Qingling and Russia”
, organised by
the Foundation of St Andrew the First-Called
and
the
Song Qingling Foundation
.
In his welcoming remarks, Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister
Andrey Rudenko
highlighted
Sun Yat-sen’s
and
Song Qingling’s historic contributions
to the Chinese people’s revolutionary struggle, their enduring interest in and affection for Russia, as well as their considerable personal efforts to strengthen friendship and mutual understanding between the peoples of the two countries.
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
Today, under the leadership of President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
and President of China
Xi Jinping
, these relations have reached
their highest level in the history of our two countries
.
The initiative by
the Foundation of St Andrew the First-Called
and
the Song Qingling Foundation
was highly praised for raising awareness of key figures and events in Russia-China relations. It also ties in naturally with
the Russia-China Years of Cooperation in Education in 2026-2027
.
