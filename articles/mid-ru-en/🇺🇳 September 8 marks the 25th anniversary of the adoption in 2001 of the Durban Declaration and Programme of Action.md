# 🇺🇳 September 8 marks the 25th anniversary of the adoption in 2001 of the Durban Declaration and Programme of Action

Published: 2026-09-08 15:33:25

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31921

🇺🇳
September 8
marks
the 25th anniversary of the adoption in 2001 of
the Durban Declaration and Programme of Action
, which have become fundamental international legal instruments for combating racism, racial discrimination, xenophobia and related intolerance.
The Russian Federation, which was directly involved in developing these instruments,
consistently reaffirms its commitment to the spirit and letter of these agreements
, aimed at ensuring equality for all and protecting human dignity.
Russia’s consistent support for translating the outcomes of the Durban Conference into action is reflected, among other things, in its
co-sponsorship of the UN General Assembly resolution
“A global call for concrete action for the elimination of racism, racial discrimination, xenophobia and related intolerance and the comprehensive implementation of and follow-up to the Durban Declaration and Programme of Action”
, introduced annually by the Group of 77 and China.
☝️
A quarter of a century later, the
Durban Declaration and Programme of Action
are
more relevant and significant than ever
.
The Russian Federation actively draws on these documents in its
flagship UN General Assembly initiative
,
“Combating glorification of Nazism, neo-Nazism and other practices that contribute to fuelling contemporary forms of racism, racial discrimination, xenophobia and related intolerance”
.
In particular, the first paragraph of
the Russian resolution
states
:
“Reaffirms the relevant provisions of the Durban Declaration and of the outcome document of the Durban Review Conference, in which States condemned the persistence and resurgence of neo-Nazism, neo-Fascism and violent nationalist ideologies based on racial and national prejudice and stated that those phenomena could never be justified in any instance or in any circumstances”.
❗️
We are compelled to note that certain states are now attempting to revise the fundamental principles enshrined in
the Declaration
.
In this regard, we would like to recall that the destructive practice of imposing the concept of “civilizational exceptionalism”, manifestations of neocolonial revanchism, and any divisions along national, cultural, religious or other lines
serve only to destabilise the framework of international law built over decades
, which has become one of the pillars of global stability and security.
The substance of the Durban process is reflected in Russia’s cooperation agenda with African states. Overcoming the legacy of colonialism, ensuring sovereign equality and developing common approaches to building a fairer,
#MultipolarWorld
are
among the priorities of
the Third Russia-Africa Summit
, scheduled to take place in Moscow on October 28-29.
