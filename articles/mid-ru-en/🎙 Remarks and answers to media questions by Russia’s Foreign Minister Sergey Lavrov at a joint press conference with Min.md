Remarks and answers to media questions
by Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at a joint press conference with Minister of Foreign Affairs and Cooperation of Timor-Leste Bendito Freitas
(Moscow, October 6, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
Our talks took place in a friendly atmosphere and were
very productive
.
Before today’s meeting, we had met on the sidelines of international events in previous years. Today, we thoroughly discussed
the current state and prospects of our bilateral relations
, as well as
the international agenda
.
We share an interest in developing and expanding cooperation
, preferably as swiftly and effectively as possible, in line with
the agreements
reached by President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
and Prime Minister of Timor-Leste
Xanana Gusmao
during their meeting in Kazan this June, on the sidelines of
the #RussiaASEAN anniversary Summit
marking 35 years of our partnership with the Association.
We have made very little progress in trade and economic ties so far, but today’s discussions confirmed some promising prospects.
Both Sides are keen to explore joint investment projects in energy, mining, fisheries and tourism.
We discussed dialogue between our ministries on foreign policy issues
. The Minister proposed holding regular consultations, and we supported this proposal. We will draw up a plan for these contacts, which I believe will be very useful.
I briefed my colleague on the latest developments surrounding Ukraine
. I emphasised the need for a lasting settlement of this crisis by addressing its
well-known root causes
.
***
Key points from the answers to media questions
:
• When we issued
a very clear statement
in response to
threats by the Baltic states
and other NATO members that had “got out of hand” to attack the Kaliningrad Region,
we were immediately assured
– by both NATO Secretary General
Mark Rutte
and US Permanent Representative to NATO
Matthew Whitaker
–
that the bloc had no plans to attack Russia
and could not possibly have any, since NATO was supposedly a defensive alliance.
• We have heard many times that NATO is a defensive alliance
, but that did not save
Yugoslavia
,
Afghanistan
or
Libya
.
• NATO members are now trying to extend their military presence to the Far East, Southeast Asia and Northeast Asia. My colleague and I discussed this today. We hear what they say, but, as the Russian saying goes, we
“keep our ears pricked”
. Above all, we are working to fulfil the objectives set by President
Vladimir Putin
for
the special military operation
–
to prevent any threats to the Russian Federation from our western neighbours.
• At
the Valdai Forum
, President
Vladimir Putin
gave an unambiguous answer to the question of what would happen if the Kaliningrad Region were attacked.
He made it clear that if the Kaliningrad Region or any other region of the Russian Federation were attacked, the response would be the same.
