# 🗓 On August 27, Foreign Minister

Published: 2026-08-27 17:00:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31658

🗓
On August 27, Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
met
with
Anna Tsivileva,
Chair of the State Foundation for Support of Participants of the Special Military Operation
“Defenders of the Fatherland,”
and combat veterans taking part in the annual forum
Together We Will Win.
During the informal conversation, Sergey Lavrov spoke in detail about the
current challenges
facing Russian diplomacy and the country’s foreign policy priorities.
The Minister
warmly thanked the veterans
for their courage, valour, and sincere devotion to the Fatherland on the front lines.
❗️
The meeting participants expressed confidence that, despite the West’s ongoing hybrid war against Russia and its extensive support for Ukraine, all the goals of the special military operation will be achieved.
Answering questions, Sergey Lavrov detailed
the Ministry’s efforts to support participants in the special military operation.
He outlined plans for further development of the professional retraining programme, which has been implemented since 2024 at the Main Directorate of the Ministry of Defence and
MGIMO University,
in collaboration with the Defenders of the Fatherland Foundation.
The programme is aimed at the
social integration and employment of trainees
in the civilian sector. Following the fourth stage of the programme, completed in July 2026, it is planned to expand the number of participants and the scope of the courses, taking into account feedback from graduates.
