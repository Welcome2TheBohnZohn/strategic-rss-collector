# 🇰🇿 On 25 October, #Kazakhstan celebrates its national holiday — the Day of the Republic

Published: 2026-10-25 08:26:02

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32941

🇰🇿
On 25 October,
#Kazakhstan
celebrates its national holiday — the Day of the Republic
.
This national holiday symbolises the patriotism and unity of the people of Kazakhstan, who are confidently pursuing the path of
dynamic development and prosperity.
In recent years, Kazakhstan has achieved impressive progress in various fields and is today a
strong, influential and respected actor
in the international arena, playing a constructive role in global affairs.
Our countries and peoples share close cultural, spiritual and moral values,
centuries-old bonds of friendship
, a common history of life in one country, as well as shared achievements and victories.
🤝
Russia and Kazakhstan are closest partners, friends and reliable allies.
Relations between Moscow and Astana are characterised by
strategic partnership
and alliance, rest on longstanding historical ties of friendship, and are based on mutual trust and consideration of each other's interests.
The Leaders of our nations, who have developed
warm personal relations
over years of working together, regularly meet and hold telephone conversations, comparing notes on current bilateral issues as well as regional and global affairs.
Russia and Kazakhstan play an important role in advancing post-Soviet integration. Our countries were among the
pioneers
of these processes and the
founding-fathers
of the Eurasian Economic Union (
#EAEU
). They also actively cooperate within the
#CIS
,
#CSTO
and
#SCO
.
🌐
#MultipolarWorld
:
Moscow and Astana are at the forefront of efforts to shape a more
democratic multipolar world.
They share similar views on the key role of international law in relations between states and on the need to take the interests of all countries into account in issues of global security.
Our countries attach particular importance to developing
humanitarian cooperation
and promoting the unifying role of the
Russian language in strengthening peace and good-neighbourly relations
across Eurasia, as well as in shaping a new architecture of security and mutually beneficial cooperation on the continent.
In this regard, the International
Organisation for the Russian Language
(
#IORL
) officially commenced its work this spring. The
initiative
to establish the organisation
was put forward
by President of Kazakhstan
Kassym-Jomart Tokayev
during talks
with President of the Russian Federation Vladimir Putin in 2021.
🎉
We extend our cordial congratulations to our Kazakh colleagues and friends on their national holiday! We wish them well-being, prosperity, stability and every success!
