Opening remarks
by Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov during talks with Deputy Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Turkmenistan Rashid Meredov on the sidelines of the CIS Council of Foreign Ministers
(Turkmenbashi, October 7, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
It is a pleasure to be here.
Every visit
leaves me with new impressions of Turkmenistan’s development.
These visits invariably involve
productive bilateral and multilateral international events
, as will be the case over these three days – meetings within the
#CIS
framework, both at the Foreign Minister level and of the Collective Security Council at the highest level, followed by the environmental Summit of the five Caspian states. This, too, is a very important event that we have long awaited.
There will also be
a series of other sideline events
in
the Avaza National Tourist Zone
this week.
I would like to express
my sincere appreciation for our close cooperation
across various forums and emphasise the importance of
the agreements reached by our Leaders
on developing bilateral cooperation and coordinating our foreign policy efforts.
I hope that today we
will discuss all these matters in detail
and o
utline further steps
to ensure that our Foreign Ministries play an increasingly important role in achieving the corresponding results.
In closing,
I would like to congratulate you
on the anniversary celebrated on September 27 –
35 years of Turkmenistan’s independence
.
🤝
Once again, I would like to emphasise
our satisfaction with the current state and prospects of our enhanced allied partnership
.
