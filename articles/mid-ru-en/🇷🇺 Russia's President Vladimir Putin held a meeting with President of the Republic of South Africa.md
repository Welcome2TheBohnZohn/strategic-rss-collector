# 🇷🇺 Russia's President Vladimir Putin held a meeting with President of the Republic of South Africa

Published: 2026-09-12 10:01:45

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31981

🇷🇺
🇿🇦
Russia's President
Vladimir Putin
held a meeting
with President of the Republic of South Africa
Cyril Ramaphosa
on the sidelines of the BRICS Summit in India
(New Delhi, September 11, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin
: Mr President, friends, I am very glad to see you.
Our
previous discussions
also took place at the BRICS summit, in Kazan,
yet we remain in constant contact
– as do our respective teams.
Last year, we had the opportunity
to compare positions
on the full spectrum of current issues with your Vice-President, who, at your instruction, participated in
the St Petersburg International Economic Forum
.
🤝
Relations between the Russian Federation and the Republic of South Africa
are developing successfully
. Just last week, on September 5, we marked the
20th anniversary of our basic treaty
, signed in 2006 –
the Treaty on Friendship and Partnership between our states
.
Political dialogue and inter-parliamentary ties are developing, with the heads of our Foreign Ministries maintaining regular contact.
Trade may not be increasing at a rapid pace, but it continues to grow and
remains at a solid level
.
We have traditionally developed cooperation in the cultural and educational spheres, particularly in personnel training and education. For the 2026–27 academic year, the state quota has been increased to
100 scholarships
for your young people to study in Russia.
We note
the similarity of our approaches
to key international and regional issues, and we are grateful for South Africa’s support for Russian initiatives at the United Nations.
Naturally, Russia attaches great importance to the comprehensive development of relations with African countries as a whole.
We are preparing, in a robust manner, for
the Third Summit Russia-Africa
, which will take place in Moscow
from October 28 to October 29
. Among the main subjects are digital technologies, artificial intelligence, peaceful use of freedom, and food security. And we do expect to have a representative delegation from your state.
