Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with President of Kyrgyzstan Sadyr Japarov
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Russian-Kyrgyz relations of strategic partnership and alliance are developing successfully on the basis of
long-standing traditions of friendship, mutual respect and consideration for each other’s interests
.
You and I personally oversee the key issues of bilateral cooperation
. We will have another opportunity to meet later this year at
the Collective Security Treaty Organization Summit
and at
the traditional year-end meeting of CIS Leaders
– hopefully in St Petersburg, as has traditionally been the case, including
last year
.
📈
Russia is among the Republic’s leading trade partners
, accounting for
more than 26%
of its foreign trade. In 2025, bilateral trade
increased by 25.9%
and
grew by another 36%
in January-June 2026. This is a strong result.
Around 98%
of all payments are made in national currencies.
Accumulated Russian investment in Kyrgyzstan’s economy
has exceeded $1.2 billion
.
More than 2,000 companies with Russian capital operate in the country
. The Intergovernmental Commission is working effectively, and the 2022-2026 economic cooperation programme is being implemented.
In August, Issyk-Kul hosted
the Russian–Kyrgyz Interregional Conference
and
the Russian–Kyrgyz Economic Forum
.
More than 80 regions of Russia maintain ties with all seven regions of the Republic
.
🤝
We greatly appreciate your attention to supporting the Russian language
, which has official status in the Republic.
In 2023, you and I launched the construction of nine secondary schools in Kyrgyzstan
with Russian as the language of teaching. We expect the first three to open by September 1 of the next academic year, in 2027.
More than 10,000 Kyrgyz citizens
are studying in Russia, while
another 8,500 students
attend the Kyrgyz-Russian University in Bishkek.
We pay particular attention to security cooperation
. The Joint Russian Military Base stationed in the Republic is an important element in ensuring stability in the region.
We now have a good opportunity to discuss all these and other important issues.
