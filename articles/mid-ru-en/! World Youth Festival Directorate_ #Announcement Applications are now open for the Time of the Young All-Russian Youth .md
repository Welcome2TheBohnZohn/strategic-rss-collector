# ! World Youth Festival Directorate: #Announcement Applications are now open for the Time of the Young All-Russian Youth Achievement Award

Published: 2026-10-20 20:01:05

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32872

World Youth Festival Directorate:
#Announcement
Applications are now open for
the Time of the Young All-Russian Youth Achievement Award
!
Two international nominations
are open for participation of young compatriots and youth organizations abroad:
•
Friend of Russia
— for Russian citizens and compatriots aged 18 to 35 who live and work abroad, bring young people together, implement socially significant initiatives, and strengthen humanitarian ties with Russia. Applicants must have at least three years of project experience.
•
Ambassador of Russia
— for international organizations developing cooperation with Russia, as well as Russian organizations engaged in international activities. This nomination recognizes projects that promote Russian culture, language, and values, and strengthen international youth and humanitarian cooperation.
💬
Deputy Prime Minister of Russia Dmitry Chernyshenko:
The Time of the Young Award, part of the Youth and Children national project, is one of the main awards in Russia’s youth policy.
Over the past five years, it has recognized the achievements of
more than 1,200 people
: those who create opportunities for young people, serve society, develop socially significant projects, and show that Russia is a country of limitless opportunities.
The winners have been able to scale up their projects, receive informational support, and share their experience with others.
This year, the Award is held in nominations across seven thematic areas.
I invite you to take part!
Deadline for application submission:
November 9, 2026
🏆
The awards ceremony will take place on December 19, 2026, at
the 'RUSSIA' National Centre in Moscow
, bringing together more than 1,000 participants.
