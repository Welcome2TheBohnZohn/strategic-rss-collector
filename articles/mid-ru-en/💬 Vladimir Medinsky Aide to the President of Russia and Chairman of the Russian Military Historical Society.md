# 💬 Vladimir Medinsky Aide to the President of Russia and Chairman of the Russian Military Historical Society:

Published: 2026-09-05 16:01:24

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31854

💬
Vladimir Medinsky
,
Aide to the President of Russia and Chairman of the Russian Military Historical Society:
🌟
On September 4 in Khabarovsk, on the high bank of the Amur River,
the Russian Military Historical Society
and Khabarovsk Region Governor
Dmitry Demeshin
unveiled
Russia’s largest memorial dedicated to
the Victory over militarist Japan and the end of World War II
.
A 15-meter high bronze Soviet soldier is the centrepiece of the composition
. With the stock of his rifle, he smashes a border post bearing a chrysanthemum – not only the emblem of Japan’s Imperial House, but also a symbol of
the militarism responsible for
monstrous crimes
.
In August-September 1945,
Marshal Alexander Vassilevsky
commanded Soviet forces from Khabarovsk. In just three weeks, they
defeated Japan’s million-strong Kwantung Army
and
brought World War II to an end
.
PS. In terms of the ratio between duration, losses and results, the operation to defeat the Japanese army in the Far East was the most effective military operation in history.
