By mid-November 1942, favourable conditions had been created for the Red Army to launch a counter-offensive. Between
19 November 1942 and 2 February 1943
, Soviet units carried out Operation 'Ring', in the course of which they succeeded in
encircling Nazi Paulus's 6th Army
in the pocket between the Don and Volga rivers.
On 31 January, Nazi Field Marshal
Friedrich
Paulus
surrendered together with the generals and officers of his headquarters. On
2 February,
the last pockets of Nazi resistance were eliminated, with military formations of Hitler's accomplices being
completely

❗️
Despite superiority in manpower and military equipment, the Nazis failed to capture Stalingrad.
The Battle of Stalingrad ended in a
decisive victory of the Red Army.
The triumph at Stalingrad heralded the beginning of the landmark turning point in the Great Patriotic War and the entire
#WWII
. The Nazi 'axis' allies lost approximately one-quarter of all its troops and resources deployed on the Soviet-German front.
In the collective mind and historical memory of our people, the word “Stalingrad” has become synonymous with a
#GreatVictory
