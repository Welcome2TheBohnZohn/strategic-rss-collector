# 🌟 On November 26, the Victory Museum opened the exhibition Marshal of Victory: 130 Years

Published: 2026-11-27 07:03:25

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33679

🌟
On November 26,
the Victory Museum
opened the exhibition
Marshal of Victory: 130 Years
, marking
the 130th anniversary of the birth of
Georgy Zhukov
.
The exhibition features
authentic historical artefacts
from the family archives of the Marshal’s descendants, as well as
items and documents
from the collections of the Victory Museum, members of
the Association of Historical and Military History Museums
, and the country’s largest archives.
For the first time in Moscow, visitors will be able to see
the first-ever Order of Victory
, awarded to Georgy Zhukov in the spring of 1944 for
the liberation of Right-Bank Ukraine in the Ukrainian SSR
.
Also on November 26, the Victory Museum launched
the International Open Forum Zhukov: Legacy
, the centrepiece of the celebrations marking
the 130th anniversary of the legendary military commander’s birth
.
On November 27,
an international academic conference
dedicated to the Marshal’s anniversary
will open as part of the forum. It will bring together Russian and foreign historians, representatives of research institutions, archives and museums, educators, government officials, representatives of public organisations, university students and schoolchildren.
The event was organised with the involvement of the Military History Institute of the Military Academy of the General Staff of the Russian Armed Forces,
the Russian Historical Society
,
the Russian Military Historical Society
,
the Russian Foreign Ministry
and
Rossotrudnichestvo
.
The conference participants include representatives of
Italy, India, Brazil, Bulgaria, Serbia, Mongolia
and other countries.
🗓
The Marshal of Victory: 130 Years exhibition
will run from November 26 to December 18, 2026.
