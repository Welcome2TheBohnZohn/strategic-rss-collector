# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s response to a media question

Published: 2026-09-10 17:02:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31964

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
response to a media question
regarding statements by members of Israel's Cabinet on Palestine
(September 10, 2026)
❓
Question: In recent days, several members of the Israeli Government have threatened the physical elimination of the leadership of the Palestinian National Authority and the complete annexation of the Palestinian territories, with the Arab population expelled from them. How would you comment on this?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
We have taken note of the statements you mentioned.
We link the increasingly tough rhetoric from senior Israeli officials to the electoral campaign gathering momentum
ahead of
Israel’s parliamentary elections
scheduled for October 27.
However, we believe that
the race for votes cannot justify provocative statements
that further fuel an atmosphere of mutual hostility and intolerance between Palestinians and Israelis.
❗️
We call on the parties to the conflict to act responsibly and refrain from steps that could lead to further escalation.
We consistently advocate constructive approaches
aimed at creating the necessary conditions for the resumption of direct Palestinian-Israeli dialogue on all outstanding issues.
👉
We remain convinced that there is no alternative to a comprehensive and just settlement of
the
Palestinian issue
on the well-known international legal basis
, providing for the establishment of an independent, viable and territorially contiguous
State of Palestine
, coexisting with Israel in peace and security.
Only the implementation of this formula can guarantee lasting peace for all peoples of the Middle East.
