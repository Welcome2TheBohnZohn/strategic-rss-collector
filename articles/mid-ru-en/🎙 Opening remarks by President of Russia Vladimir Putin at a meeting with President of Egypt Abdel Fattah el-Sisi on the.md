Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Egypt Abdel Fattah el-Sisi on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 28, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We are grateful to you for coming to
the #RussiaAfrica Summit
. You and I were instrumental in establishing this format, holding
the First Russia–Africa Summit
in Sochi in 2019.
I remember very well how it all began. I remember asking you to take the lead in this process.
You responded so favourably and agreed to do so.
With your encouragement, one might say,
the process gained momentum
and has developed quite actively and productively.
Since then, we have achieved a great deal through our joint efforts
. The main indicator is the growth in trade between Russia and Africa. Since 2022, its
total volume has increased by 50% to exceed $27 billion
. Egypt accounts for a substantial share of this trade.
On September 11, on the sidelines of
the BRICS Summit in New Delhi
, we had
a useful exchange of views
on
the situation in the region
and
the further development of friendly ties between Russia and Egypt
.
🇷🇺
🇪🇬
The historic relations between our countries rest on the principles of mutual respect, consideration for each other’s interests and mutual assistance
. They continue to develop successfully in accordance with
the Treaty on Comprehensive Partnership and Strategic Cooperation
.
Despite regional tensions, bilateral trade continues to grow steadily.
In the first eight months of this year, it has already
increased by 14%
. In 2025, it reached a record high,
exceeding $10 billion
for the first time.
Our Joint Intergovernmental Commission is working productively
, and preparations are underway for its 16th meeting, which will take place in Egypt in 2027.
⚛️
Our flagship project to build
the El Dabaa Nuclear Power Plant
in Egypt is progressing on schedule
. On July 9,
a ceremony was held
to mark the installation of the reactor vessel at Unit 2.
More than 1.2 million Russian tourists visited Egypt in the first six months of 2026
, compared with around 1.5 million in the whole of 2025.
🤝
Today, we have a good opportunity to continue discussing
promising areas of long-term bilateral cooperation
. We will also address other issues of mutual interest.
