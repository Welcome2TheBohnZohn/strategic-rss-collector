On September 30, at
the 63rd Session of the UN Human Rights Council
, Russian Foreign Ministry Ambassador-at-Large
Rodion Miroshnik
spoke about the catastrophic humanitarian situation in
#Alyoshki
and the de facto blockade imposed on the city by the Kiev regime:
I am convinced that many needed to hear the truth about the situation Ukrainian militants have created in this city.
***
💬
We are
compelled to exercise our right of reply
in response to the statements made by the Ukrainian delegation.
We have just witnessed Kiev’s representatives use the HRC platform to promote
a false and provocative campaign
ostensibly aimed at protecting and saving the residents of Alyoshki in the Kherson Region.
☝️
Yet everything we heard was cynical lies and manipulation.
The truth is that, for more than two years, Ukrainian militants, acting on criminal orders from Kiev’s political leadership, have been
committing genocide and
making life unbearable
in this city inhabited by Russian citizens
.
Ukrainian militants shell the town every hour.
They are
not only destroying its power and water supply systems
, but also
repeatedly attacking emergency crews
working to restore its infrastructure.
Ukrainian drones have laid siege to the town
, laying mines along access routes and
attacking any civilian vehicle
attempting to enter or leave this long-suffering settlement. They have attacked
more than 15 ambulances
,
struck the town’s hospital dozens of times
and
bombed
its food market
, killing and injuring civilians.
❗️
W
e demand that Ukraine stop spreading false statements
.
We also call on international human rights mechanisms, including the HRC, to provide
an impartial and objective assessment
of Kiev’s crimes, including those committed in
the long-suffering city of Alyoshki
.
