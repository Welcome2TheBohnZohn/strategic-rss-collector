🎙
Opening remarks
by Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov during talks with Minister of Foreign Affairs and Emigrants of Lebanon Youssef Rajji
(Moscow, October 12, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
As agreed in New York
during our meeting
with Prime Minister of Lebanon
Nawaf Salam,
you are making
your first visit to the Russian capital
.
This is already the second Russian-Lebanese meeting in the past month.
I hope we will maintain this momentum.
It is also the first visit to Moscow by a Lebanese Foreign Minister in five years
. I hope we will no longer allow such lengthy gaps in our contacts.
I would like to express
my sincere appreciation
for your making time to visit Russia while Lebanon and its people face
a very severe crisis as a result of Israel’s military operations and escalation against your country
.
☝️
In this regard, I wish to reaffirm clearly and unequivocally Russia’s principled support for Lebanon’s sovereignty, unity and territorial integrity.
Our bilateral relations have traditionally been friendly
. We have always been ready to support our Lebanese friends, including in the current situation, with
almost a million Lebanese citizens
having suffered severely as a result of the hostilities. Their rights have been violated.
The humanitarian situation in Lebanon remains dire
.
In April this year,
we sent humanitarian aid
to those in need in your country.
We will continue to provide humanitarian supplies.
❗️
The situation in Lebanon cannot be considered in isolation from the other dramatic and tragic developments unfolding in the region
.
I hope that today we will discuss all these developments and seek to formulate
a joint approach
to strengthening positive trends and bringing together those who favour a return to normality, primarily through agreements among the countries of the region themselves.
