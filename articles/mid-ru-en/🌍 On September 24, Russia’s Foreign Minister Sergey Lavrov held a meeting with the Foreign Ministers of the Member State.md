On September 24, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
held a meeting
with the Foreign Ministers of the Member States of
the Confederation of Sahel States
(
#AES
) in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
:
🇧🇫
Foreign Minister of
#BurkinaFaso
Karamoko Traore
;
🇲🇱
Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of
#Mali
Abdoulaye Diop
;
🇳🇪
Minister of Foreign Affairs, Cooperation and Nigeriens Abroad of the Republic of
#Niger
Bakary Yaou Sangare
.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks
The Sides discussed
a wide range of issues in priority areas
of political, economic, financial, transport, logistics and information cooperation within the Russia-AES dialogue, with a view to preparing for and holding the events of
the Third Russia-Africa Summit
, scheduled for October 28–29 in Moscow
.
They held a frank exchange of views on
the situation in the Sahara-Sahel region
and reaffirmed their commitment to
strengthening foreign policy coordination at the UN and other international platforms
.
