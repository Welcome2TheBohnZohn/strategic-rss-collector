• September 3rd marks the 81st Anniversary of the End of World War II.
The Allies’ victory in World War II laid the foundation of the United Nations
that was established in 1945 with the aims, as stipulated at its Charter, “to save succeeding generations from the scourge of war”.
• Nearly 80 percent of the world’s population were drawn into the fiery orbit of hostilities. As the primary theaters of military operations in Europe and Asia, the Soviet Union and China bore the brunt of World War II. Altogether their casualties accounted for over half of the deaths worldwide. Most losses were among civilians who suffered greatly from the Axis powers’ war crimes.
Lest we forget the Chinese and Soviet people’s sacrifice for the victory as well as the contribution of other Allies, including New Zealand, to crushing Nazism and fascism and fighting for a peaceful future!
• During World War II, Japanese militarists’ war of aggression and innumerable crimes inflicted immense suffering on the people of China, Russia and elsewhere across Asia, resulting in the deaths of tens of millions of innocent people
. 80 years have passed, it is painful to see that Japan has not fully reflected on its past crimes.
Instead, shielded and encouraged by outside forces, it now attempts openly to whitewash its aggression and even to return openly to a militarist path.
• Annually, the respective UN General Assembly resolution – sponsored by Russia and co-sponsored by China and 43 other countries
– is supported by the overwhelming majority of member-states
. Last year, 119 nations voted for this document. Unfortunately,
New Zealand is not among them being in the minority, although Kiwis were actively involved in the fight against Nazism
and won fame on the battlefields of World War II.
• Back in the days of World War II, New Zealand and many other nations were on the right side of the history and their cause was just. Russia and China are determined to
stand firmly on the side of international justice and prepared, together with the peace-loving peoples of all nations, to resolutely uphold the post-war world order for the sake of building a bright future for all mankind
.
