On November 17, the United Nations Industrial Development Organisation (UNIDO)
marked
the 60th anniversary.
In 1966, on the initiative of the Soviet Union, UNIDO was
established
as an autonomous organisation within the
#UN
, and in 1985, thanks to the efforts of the USSR, it was granted the status of a UN specialised agency.
☝️
Over the past six decades, the Organisation has promoted
inclusive and sustainable industrial development across its member states
, particularly in Africa, strengthening their productive capacities and integration into the global economy.
As a global forum, it facilitates international dialogue on promoting industrialisation, bringing together representatives of the public and private sectors alongside non-governmental and academic organisations.
🤝
The Russian Federation is a long-standing and reliable partner of the Organisation.
As an active participant and key donor to international efforts in sustainable industrial development, Russia attaches particular importance to multilateral cooperation within the relevant bodies of the UN system.
During the forthcoming
54th session of the Industrial Development Board
, scheduled for November 23–25, a number of commemorative events are scheduled to mark the Organisation’s anniversary, including a thematic seminar entitled
“Russia and UNIDO: 60 Years Together”
.
