# 🎙 Excerpts from Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s answers to media questions

Published: 2026-11-25 17:33:57

🎙
Excerpts from Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
answers to media questions
following the joint meeting of the boards of the Russian and Belarusian Foreign Ministries
(Gomel, November 25, 2026)
❓
Question: The European Commission
has refused
to hold bilateral consultations with Russia on European security. Prior to that, Europe spent a long time discussing who should be appointed as its negotiator. How can this contradictory position be explained?
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
We have never had any contacts with the European Commission regarding negotiations on the Ukraine crisis. The European Commission regularly makes
various statements
.
However, the fact that these are
not accompanied by any concrete proposals
conveyed to the Russian Side through diplomatic channels confirms
the lack of seriousness in the approach taken by Brussels
,
particularly by the European Commission, which engages in megaphone diplomacy.
There have been some interesting statements. Polish Prime Minister
Donald Tusk
said
that
no one intended to consult Russia
on what the European dimension of the upcoming negotiations should look like.
EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy
Kaja Kallas
still holds her post, so
her statement
must also be taken into account. She said that
Europe already had a security architecture
– NATO and the EU – and that no one should try to reshape it. The main objective, she said, was
to integrate Ukraine into NATO and the EU
. She also added that
the EU must not allow a settlement that takes Russia’s interests into account.
👉
So we know full well what the position voiced by the Europeans is worth.
There has recently been discussion of
statements
coming out of Brussels
claiming that they had agreed on a negotiating team
(they had been asked about their negotiator) – the European "Five" (meaning France, Germany, Britain, Italy and Poland, plus representatives of the EU and NATO). That makes
seven
already.
They publicly stated that
this "Seven" should insert itself
into
the negotiation process that has now resumed in Abu Dhab
i involving Russia, the US and Ukraine.
☝️
Our position is that we must first understand what role Europe intends to play in the negotiations and what objectives, if any, it has set for itself.
If those objectives are reflected in the statements I have just quoted – that NATO and the EU alone will shape Europe’s security architecture and that Russia’s interests must not be accommodated –
then what is the point of holding such negotiations?
We therefore asked representatives of European countries
to come and see us or convey their position to us in some other way
. They say:
"No, we do not want to explain anything to you. We want to sit down at the negotiating table straight away"
.
With such manners and intentions,
they will sit down at the negotiating table only to immediately put their feet up on it.
❌
We have no need for that.
