# 🎙 Statement by Permanent Representative of the Russian Federation to the International Organizations in Vienna

🇷🇺
Statement by Permanent Representative of the Russian Federation to the International Organizations in Vienna
Mikhail Ulyanov
at the Seventh Session of the UN Conference on the Establishment of the Middle East Zone Free of Nuclear Weapons and Other Weapons of Mass Destruction
📍
New York, 16 November 2026
💬
We are compelled to note that, since our
last meeting
, the international security and nuclear non-proliferation landscape
has not undergone any significant improvement
.
The escalation in the Middle East has not subsided. Following the
unlawful and unprovoked
US-Israeli aggression
against Iran’s nuclear facilities,
which were under IAEA safeguards
, in June 2025, the United States and Israel embarked on a new and unprecedented escalation in February-April this year.
The war is still ongoing and has affected a number of other countries in the region. Unfortunately, despite the efforts being made, a sustainable peaceful settlement has yet to be achieved.
👉
The threat of a resumption of armed hostilities remains.
<…>
❌
The difficult situation prevailing today in the field of nuclear non-proliferation was also reflected in the fact that the Eleventh Review Conference of the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT), held here in New York this spring, concluded
without the adoption of a final document.
***
Now, more than ever, there is a pressing need for collective political and diplomatic efforts
aimed at de-escalating tensions and maintaining dialogue
across the entire range of issues related to regional security.
📄
A
sufficient number of relevant initiatives
are already on the table, covering either the entire Middle East or individual parts of the region. One such initiative is Russia’s
Concept of Collective Security in the Persian Gulf Region
, which was most recently updated in 2026.
***
For seven years now, the UN Conference on the Establishment of a Middle East Zone Free of Nuclear Weapons and Other Weapons of Mass Destruction has been building on the
substantial results
achieved in its earlier work.
In addition to the aforementioned consensus rule, we have a substantive political declaration; representatives of the IAEA and the OPCW Secretariats regularly participate in the forum’s proceedings; meetings of the intersessional Working Committee are held, and, more recently, have also been hosted by countries of the region; and the fifth session of the Conference, in 2024, was held in a review format for the first time.
☝️
All this provides a
solid foundation for further progress
.
