#ELECTIONS2026
🇷🇺
On September 20,
voting concluded
in the elections to
the 9th convocation of the State Duma of the Russian Federation
. The last polling station at a Russian diplomatic mission abroad closed today
at 5:00 am Moscow time
.
Through the efforts of
Russia’s Central Election Commission and Foreign Ministry
,
all necessary arrangements were made
to enable Russian citizens residing or temporarily staying abroad to exercise their right to vote
– from
the far reaches of the Arctic
to
the most remote corners of the globe
.
🌐
A total of
326 polling stations
were set up at Russian diplomatic missions – embassies, permanent missions and consulates general – in
152 countries
.
In seven countries
(
Bahrain
,
Israel
,
Iraq
,
Yemen
,
China
,
Kuwait
and
Libya
),
all voting took place ahead of the main election day
to accommodate local work schedules and religious calendars.
Russian diplomatic and consular missions organised
121 early voting sessions
for employees of
Russian companies
and
enterprises
working far from home
, as well as
Russian military personnel
safeguarding the security and sovereignty of our allies.
In unfriendly countries, where the local authorities encouraged
persistent attempts to intimidate Russian citizens
ahead of the election campaign,
our compatriots stood firm and voted responsibly despite the psychological pressure exerted on them.
