“From Cairo to Cape”: Vladimir Shubin’s Memoirs Presented in Johannesburg
On 29 September, the “
From Cairo to Cape”
, newly released memoirs by renowned
Russian Africanist Professor Vladimir Shubin,
were presented at the Nelson Mandela Foundation in Johannesburg.
The event brought together
more than 160 guests
, reflecting the strong interest in Professor Shubin’s work and in the history of Russia-Africa relations.
Among the distinguished guests were former President of South Africa and former Deputy President of the African National Congress
Kgalema Petrus Motlanthe
, as well as former Ministers of International Relations
Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma, Lindiwe Sisulu and Naledi Pandor
.
The audience also included veterans of the African National Congress who witnessed and participated in the historic events described in the book, members of the diplomatic corps, representatives of business, academia, education, and journalists.
📖
From Cairo to Cape
is both a historical study and a deeply personal memoir.
Drawing on his first-hand experience, Professor Shubin tells the story of the people and events that shaped
Africa’s struggle for liberation and examines the role played by the Soviet Union in supporting national liberation movements.
The choice of the
Nelson Mandela Foundation
as the venue was particularly symbolic, recalling South Africa’s long struggle for freedom and the importance of international solidarity in the fight against apartheid and colonialism.
🤝
The Embassy sincerely thanks Professor Vladimir Shubin for this
important and personal contribution to preserving the historical memory of Africa’s liberation
and the development of Russia-Africa relations.
