Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
interview
for the Our Baptism: The Patriarch project
(Moscow, November 20, 2026)
❓
You have said on several occasions – and this is clear to many people around the world – that Ukrainians, for the most part, and Russians are essentially one people, drawn into a grave conflict by our enemies’ malicious designs. Why should the victory of Russian arms and the triumph of truth ultimately be a victory for the people of Ukraine, who are currently suffering under the lies and violence of a regime led by these temporary rulers?
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
This is a broad topic, which includes the question of
the unity and indivisibility
of the Russian and Ukrainian people.
There is much debate on this subject, including discussions about
how exactly the Ukrainian SSR was formed
. When the Soviet leadership incorporated the western territories of present-day Ukraine after
World War II
, they were deeply concerned that
the “contamination” by nationalism
and, to some extent,
even neo-Nazism
, given that
Bandera’s followers and their associates
were among the principal perpetrators of Nazi crimes,
would spread.
👉
They decided to neutralise this newly acquired territory by
incorporating historically Russian regions into Ukraine
: lands that had never been outside the Russian Empire or, following the Great October Socialist Revolution, the Soviet Union. <...>
I would hesitate to claim that the entire territory of present-day Ukraine is inhabited by a single, unified people. In the west of the country,
many people hold cultural values that are fundamentally at odds with our own
. Ukrainian leaders, from Leonid Kuchma to Viktor Yanukovych, had to take into account
the powerful influence of radical nationalist ideologies
, not only in the west of the country but in other regions as well. So they chose
to “flirt” with these forces
. <...>
☝️
Southeastern Ukraine and much of central Ukraine, including Kiev, are undoubtedly historically Russian lands
. The people living there were
baptised in the same font as we were
. It is the very source from which we all emerged. I believe that
our greatest victory
, beyond achieving
the specific goals of the special military operation
, and indeed as an objective inseparably linked to those goals,
will be to defeat the primitive, visceral Russophobia being forcibly instilled in Ukraine,
or rather, in the territory currently known as Ukraine. <...>
It is crucial to defeat this Russophobic fervour
, which the West is doing everything in its power to keep boiling. You must understand that the majority of people there
still speak Russia
n. Even among the agitators and propagandists who appear on various online platforms making anti-Russia statements,
many speak Russian
. This includes members of Zelensky's administration.
They have not succeeded in
completely subjugating
the Ukrainian Orthodox Church
. Yes, there have been criminal raids on churches; priests have been killed; and clergy have been forcibly taken to “territorial recruitment centres”. Yet
the Ukrainian Orthodox Church
, a sister church to the Russian Orthodox Church, c
ontinues to hold its ground
. Long-standing attempts to impose a surrogate – the
“Orthodox Church of Ukraine”
. created by the
“leader”
in Constantinople –
have failed to make any significant impact
.
I would like to share another quotation with you, a recent one. In 2023, Swedish Chief of the Defence Staff
Michael Claesson
remarked
:
“The Russians have an unpleasant habit of actually doing what they say”
.
When we say that we will defend the rights of Russians and Russian speakers in Ukraine, that we will not abandon them or their language, culture or religion,
the West should take note and consider what it is trying to achieve
.
When the West responds that Russia’s
“red lines”
keep shifting – here today, somewhere else tomorrow, and further still the day after –
it should listen to the Swedish general
.
❗️
Unlike the West, we keep our promises.
