# 🎙 Comment by Aide to the President of Russia Yury Ushakov following the telephone conversation between the Presidents of Russia and the US

Published: 2026-10-09 23:00:38

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32674

🎙
Comment
by Aide to the President of Russia Yury Ushakov following the telephone conversation between the Presidents of Russia and the US
(October 9, 2026)
*
💬
Yury Ushakov:
On the afternoon of October 7, we spoke over the phone with US Presidential Special Envoy
Steve Witkoff
. <...>
He also said that
the US President would like
to congratulate
Vladimir Putin personally by telephone
[on his birthday], and we began looking for a suitable opening in the two Presidents’ schedules. <...>
The telephone conversation
took place
late in the evening on October 9.
Naturally,
Donald Trump immediately wished Vladimir Putin a happy birthday
, offering his best wishes for success and well-being. He also conveyed greetings from his wife,
Melania
.
Vladimir Putin thanked his American counterpart
for his congratulations and kind words. He stressed that
we value the attention that the US President and his team devote to dialogue with Russia
, despite their busy agenda of domestic and Middle Eastern affairs.
***
One notable moment in the conversation [between the Presidents of Russia and the US], which I would like to highlight, was
our President’s expression of gratitude to the US First Lady for her active humanitarian work
, particularly her contribution to efforts
to reunite children with their families
separated during the Ukrainian conflict.
Regarding the concern expressed over
the humanitarian situation
in Alyoshki, Kherson Region
, it was agreed that Human Rights Commissioner
Yana Lantratova
would remain in regular contact with Melania Trump’s office
.
🤝
The two Leaders discussed in detail
preparations for major economic initiatives, including possible supplies of Russian petroleum products to the US and global markets
.
A special statement
has already been issued on this matter.
Naturally, considerable attention was paid to
prospects for resolving the conflict in Ukraine
. <...>
It was stressed that
Kiev’s attempts to halt the advance of Russian troops along the entire line of contact by striking deep inside our country have no prospect of success
.
❗️
Our Armed Forces
hold the initiative across the entire front and are steadily advancing
. At the same time, Vladimir Putin made it clear that
our position on the goals and objectives of the special military operation remains unchanged
, as does our readiness for a political and diplomatic settlement aimed at achieving a lasting, sustainable peace.
It was particularly stressed that, while
the Presidents’ previous conversation
had addressed
the possibility of resuming trilateral talks involving the US and Ukraine
immediately after
the State Duma elections
,
the situation has now changed
.
In its attempts to disrupt
our elections
by launching an exceptionally large-scale drone attack on Moscow, other cities and polling stations, as well as specific individuals involved in organising the elections, on the main day of voting,
the Kiev regime effectively prevented an immediate resumption of the negotiating process
. As the President said,
we will now consider when these talks can resume
.
Donald Trump said
he would instruct his team to convey the relevant messages to the Ukrainian delegation
, which is currently in Miami for contacts with American representatives.
☝️
Vladimir Putin particularly stressed that
the scope for diplomacy on Iran is far from exhausted
. An agreement with Tehran is both possible and necessary.
The President mentioned that yesterday, in Turkmenistan, he had
discussed the current situation in detail
with Iranian President
Masoud Pezeshkian
. He noted that, despite the harsh public statements made from time to time, the Americans continue their indirect dialogue with the Iranians through Qatar. <...>
Notably, Donald Trump acknowledged this point,
welcoming Moscow’s involvement in efforts to resolve the situation surrounding Iran
. <...>
The substantive, friendly conversation lasted
an hour and a half
.
