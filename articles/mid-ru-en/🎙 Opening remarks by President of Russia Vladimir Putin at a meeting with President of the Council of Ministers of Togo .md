Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of the Council of Ministers of Togo Faure Essozimna Gnassingbe on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin
: I would like to thank you for
your attention to developing
#RussiaAfrica
ties
and for taking part in an event that, in my view, was very successful.
We appreciate the attention Togo’s leadership gives to cooperation with Russia
. In November 2025,
we held talks in Moscow
, and we have also had opportunities to work together in various other settings.
🇷🇺
🇹🇬
We have enjoyed very good, fruitful cooperation with Togo for many decades
. It has now been
66 years
since
we established diplomatic relations
.
In September this year, a key bilateral document,
the Agreement on the Foundations of Relations
, entered into force. In August,
the Russian Embassy in Lome
resumed its operations. Preparations are underway to open
Togo’s Embassy in Moscow
.
Trade and economic cooperation is also developing
. Bilateral trade totalled
$852 million
in 2025. The
intergovernmental commission
began its work in 2026. We expect to give fresh impetus to mutually beneficial cooperation, including in energy, transport and logistics, geological exploration and agriculture.
We pay particular attention to humanitarian matters
. Every year, more young people from your country apply to study at Russian universities. In total,
more than 900 Togolese nationals
have received an education in Russia.
Over 440 young people
are currently studying here. Work is underway to open
two partner Russian Houses
in Lome under the auspices of the Centre for Public Diplomacy.
🤝
Thank you for supporting Russian initiatives at the UN
and other international platforms. We see and greatly appreciate this.
