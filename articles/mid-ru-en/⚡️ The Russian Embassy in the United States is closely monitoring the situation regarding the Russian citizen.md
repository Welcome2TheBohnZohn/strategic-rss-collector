# ⚡️ The Russian Embassy in the United States is closely monitoring the situation regarding the Russian citizen

Published: 2026-11-22 16:01:31

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33535

⚡️
The Russian Embassy in the United States is closely monitoring the situation regarding the Russian citizen
Igor Andreevich D'iu
, who was detained by U.S. authorities on October 8 in Palo Alto, California, and is currently being held at the Santa Clara County Jail.
According to publicly available criminal case files, Igor D'iu is being charged with acting as a foreign agent, unlawful solicitation, and fraud involving the use of forged documents.
He has not been charged with espionage.
The case is being heard in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California. The next hearing is scheduled for November 24, 2026.
The Embassy remains in contact with our citizen and is providing him with consular and legal assistance.
