Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Ghana John Dramani Mahama on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Relations between our countries rest on foundations laid during
Ghana’s emergence as a sovereign, independent State
and its struggle against
the legacy of colonialism
.
We were among the first to establish diplomatic relations with Accra – our ties date back to 1958
. We helped build industrial facilities and social infrastructure and trained national professionals. Today, in a rapidly changing world, we strive to preserve and build on this shared legacy.
We note with satisfaction the increase in contacts at various levels
. A mechanism for political consultations is in place, and interparliamentary and interparty dialogue is developing.
📈
Ghana ranks twelfth among African countries in terms of trade with Russia.
However, it is one of our largest trading partners in sub-Saharan Africa. Bilateral trade
grew by 6%
in January-August, so the overall trend is positive.
We will, of course, continue to support Ghana in education and help train national professionals
. Currently,
649 young people
from Ghana are studying in Russia. I understand that, during your visit, you have been awarded an honorary doctorate by a Russian university.
We are interested in strengthening coordination with Ghana in international forums.
We appreciate your longstanding support for Russian resolutions at the UN.
🤝
We also welcome
Accra’s independent stance under your leadership
,
Mr President,
in
combating neocolonialism
and in pursuing its foreign policy more broadly.
