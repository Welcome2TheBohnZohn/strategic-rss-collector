🇲🇳
November 5 marks
1️⃣
0️⃣
5️⃣
years since
the establishment of diplomatic relations between Russia and Mongolia.
In 1921, our country became the first state in the world to officially recognise Mongolia’s independence. Since then, Russian-Mongolian relations
have steadily developed and strengthened.
☝️
For a long time,
the USSR remained the guarantor of Mongolia’s sovereignty and security
and, in 1939, came to its aid in repelling Japanese aggression at Khalkhin Gol.
Today, relations between Russia and Mongolia are comprehensive and are rooted in traditions of friendship and neighbourliness, close ties in the economic and humanitarian spheres and aligned approaches to pressing issues on the international and regional agenda.
✍️
An important milestone in the development of bilateral ties was the signing in 2019 of the open-ended
Treaty on Friendly Relations and Comprehensive Strategic Partnership between Russia and Mongolia.
Political dialogue is steadily advancing, particularly at the highest level. In September this year, on the sidelines of the SCO Summit, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
met
with President of Mongolia
Ukhnaagiin Khürelsükh.
Shortly thereafter, during the 11th Eastern Economic Forum in Vladivostok, the Russian leader
held talks
with Prime Minister of Mongolia
Nyam-Osor Uchral.
📈
Mongolia remains an important trade partner for Russia. By the end of 2025, trade between our countrie
s reached $2.8 billion,
and in the first half of this year it
increased by 18.1 percent.
The Russia-Mongolia Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation remains highly active. In December 2025, its 27th session was held in Ulaanbaatar.
A
visa-free regime
is in place between Russia and Mongolia, and our citizens are reaping benefits and tourism is expanding.
Cooperation in
the Mongolia-EAEU
format remains an important area of Russia-Mongolia interaction. In June 2025, on the sidelines of
the meeting of the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council in Minsk,
the Interim Trade Agreement
between the Eurasian Economic Union and its member states, on the one hand, and Mongolia, on the other hand, was signed. The document entered into force in July this year.
Russia and Mongolia carefully preserve and build upon their invaluable traditions of friendship, neighbourliness and mutual assistance, maintain active interaction in international affairs, are developing a number of new promising projects in the economic and industrial sectors and are expanding cooperation in the cultural and academic spheres.
💬
President of Russia
Vladimir Putin:
The traditional friendly and neighbourly relations between our countries have reached the level of a comprehensive strategic partnership. We share longstanding traditions of friendship and special relations, without exaggeration.
Diplomatic ties have now been maintained for 105 years, and, in the fullest sense of the word, our interstate relations have been cemented by the blood of our ancestors. Fighting side by side, they stood for the freedom and independence of Mongolia during the Great Patriotic War and at Khalkhin Gol.
(Excerpt from
remarks
during talks with Prime Minister of Mongolia
Nyam-Osor Uchral
