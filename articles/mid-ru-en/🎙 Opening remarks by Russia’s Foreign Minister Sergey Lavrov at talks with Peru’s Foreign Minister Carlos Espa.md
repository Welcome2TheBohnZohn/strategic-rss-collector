🎙
Opening remarks
by Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at talks with Peru’s Foreign Minister Carlos Espa
(Moscow, November 9, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
We are glad to welcome you. I am particularly pleased to meet you in person, Mr Minister.
We attach great importance to your visit.
This is the first time in many years that the Foreign Ministers of Russia and Peru have met in person.
We have great respect for Peruvian diplomacy
, which draws on the knowledge and experience your country has gained over the millennia.
Like Russia, Peru is a unique civilisation shaped by many peoples and cultures
. People in Russia know your country well and appreciate its distinctive traditions, music and cuisine.
Relations between our countries go back
almost 165 years
. July 18 has become an unofficial day of Russian-Peruvian solidarity. On that day in 1970, Soviet medical personnel were on their way to help Peru after a devastating earthquake when they lost their lives in
a plane crash over the Atlantic
.
We are grateful to the people of Peru for honouring their memory.
🤝
Your visit to Moscow today on behalf of President Keiko Fujimori gives us
a good opportunity to review the full range of our bilateral relations
and
exchange views on international and regional issues
, as well as on our cooperation at the UN and in other international organisations.
