# : Government of the Russian Federation On September 7, a ceremony marking the completion of construction of a road bridge across the Tumannaya River, connecting Russia and the DPRK

Published: 2026-09-07 17:00:53

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31884

Government of the Russian Federation
:
🇷🇺
🇰🇵
On September 7, a ceremony marking the completion of construction of a road bridge across the Tumannaya River, connecting Russia and the DPRK,
was held on the border between the two countries
.
The agreement on implementing the project
was signed in 2024 in the presence of President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
and Chairman of the State Affairs Commission of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea
Kim Jong-un
.
Construction began on April 30, 2025
, with Prime Minister of Russia
Mikhail Mishustin
and Premier of the Cabinet of the DPRK
Pak Thae-song
giving the official start to the work.
🤝
The construction of the bridge symbolises
the commitment to strengthening friendly, good-neighbourly relations
and
expanding interregional cooperation between Russia and the DPRK
.
Despite sharing a common border,
the two countries previously had no road connection
. The capacity of the existing rail route via the Friendship Bridge across the Tumannaya River was insufficient to meet transport needs.
The new bridge will provide year-round road traffic
.
The new route will enable businesses to significantly increase freight volumes and reduce transport costs
, while ensuring reliable and stable supplies of various goods. This will contribute to expanding trade and economic cooperation between the two countries.
💬
Russia's Prime Minister
Mikhail Mishustin:
Relations between Russia and the DPRK have today reached an unprecedentedly high level of comprehensive strategic partnership. Our countries are actively cooperating in virtually all areas and coordinating their efforts to ensure regional security and stability.
It is symbolic that the new bridge will bear the name of Red Army officer
Yakov Novichenko
. His feat, accomplished many years ago, resonates with
the unparalleled courage of the Korean heroes
who only recently fought shoulder to shoulder with our servicemen and defended Russian soil.
I am convinced that the expansion of cross-border transport infrastructure will give a powerful impetus to the further development of trade and economic, scientific and technological, and cultural cooperation between Russia and the DPRK.
ℹ️
The total length of the bridge crossing is
almost 5 km
, while the bridge itself is
around 1 km long
. The bridge has
two traffic lanes
.
More than 70 people
and
over 30 pieces of equipment
were involved in its construction.
