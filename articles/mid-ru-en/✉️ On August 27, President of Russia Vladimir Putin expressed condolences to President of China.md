# ✉️ On August 27, President of Russia Vladimir Putin expressed condolences to President of China

Published: 2026-08-27 11:31:04

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31656

✉️
On August 27, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
expressed condolences
to President of China
Xi Jinping
.
✍️
I would like to express my deepest condolences over the tragic consequences of the mudslides in China’s Tibet Autonomous Region.
Please convey my sympathy and support
to those who lost their family members and loved ones in this natural disaster, as well as
my wishes for a speedy recovery
to all those injured.
Respectfully,
VLADIMIR PUTIN
