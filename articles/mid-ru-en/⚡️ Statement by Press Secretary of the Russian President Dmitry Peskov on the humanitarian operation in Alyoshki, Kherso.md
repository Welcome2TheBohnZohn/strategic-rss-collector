# ⚡️ Statement by Press Secretary of the Russian President Dmitry Peskov on the humanitarian operation in Alyoshki, Kherson Region

Published: 2026-10-23 17:31:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32919

⚡️
Statement by Press Secretary of the Russian President Dmitry Peskov on the humanitarian operation in Alyoshki, Kherson Region
*
(October 23, 2026)
Russia welcomes the efforts of US First Lady Melania Trump
, who has made a substantial contribution to securing
agreement on a humanitarian operation in
Alyoshki
, Kherson Region.
To implement the agreements reached by the Presidents of Russia and the US,
Russian Commissioner for Human Rights
Yana Lantratova
and her Ukrainian counterpart
have carried out extensive work with Ms Trump’s direct involvement.
Representatives of the defence ministries are coordinating
the timing of a ceasefire
(from 04:00 Moscow time on October 27, 2026) to enable the humanitarian mission in Alyoshki.
❗️
Those wishing to leave the city will be able to do so.
The mission requires
extensive and complex preparations
involving a wide range of representatives.
Melania Trump is playing a coordinating role
. This issue was discussed during
the recent telephone conversation
between Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump.
