Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with Interim President of Mali Assimi Goita on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We appreciate that, despite the difficult situation in the region,
you found the time to visit us
and participate in
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
.
In May,
a key interstate agreement on the foundations of our relations entered into force
. This month, we marked
the 66th anniversary of the establishment of
diplomatic relations
between our countries
in October 1960.
We greatly value
your personal commitment to advancing our partnership with Russia
in all areas
.
We remain in
regular contact
.
📈
Trade and economic cooperation is gaining momentum
. Although volumes remain modest, we hope that the intergovernmental commission, which began its work last year, will help us make progress in these areas.
We also provide humanitarian assistance.
Given the growing interest among young Malians in pursuing higher education in Russia, we have, as you know,
increased the quota for the 2026/27 academic year to 300 scholarships.
🇷🇺
Learning Russian has become popular in Mali,
and we are grateful for the attention you give to this.
Six Russian language and culture centres
known as “Russian Space” and two privately run partner “Russian Houses” are operating in the country. Ties in culture and sport are expanding.
Thank you for your
firm support for Russian initiatives
at the UN and in other international forums.
We work together in solidarity on international affairs.
🇷🇺
