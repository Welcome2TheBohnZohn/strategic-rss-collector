# 🗓️ On November 17, 2026 , Russian Ambassador to Indonesia Sergei Tolchenov attended the opening of the Russia-ASEAN Dialogue Forum of Women in High-Tech Industries in Jakarta, held

Published: 2026-11-18 20:03:28

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33464

🗓️
On
November 17, 2026
, Russian Ambassador to Indonesia
Sergei Tolchenov
attended the opening of the Russia-ASEAN Dialogue Forum of Women in High-Tech Industries in Jakarta, held under the theme “Unity in Diversity: Women for Technology, Energy and Inclusive Prosperity.”
The Forum was organised by the Rosatom Women’s Community and the Indonesian Nuclear Society (HIMNI), with the support of Rosatom State Corporation, the Council of the Eurasian Women’s Forum, the International Council for the Support and Development of Women in Industry and Technology, the ASEAN Confederation of Women’s Organisations, and the Indonesian Business Women’s Association (IWAPI).
🤝
Discussions focused on
expanding opportunities for women in high-tech industries
, including nuclear energy, advancing engineering education, workforce training, technology entrepreneurship, and implementing joint projects between Russia and ASEAN countries.
🎙️
In his remarks,
Sergei Tolchenov
noted that the strategic partnership established between Russia and Indonesia in June 2025 reflects growing interest in cooperation across various sectors of the economy, particularly high technology. He also stressed the importance of expanding opportunities for women in technical fields, science and management, supporting their professional development, and encouraging their participation in a wide range of projects.
🇮🇩
Indonesia’s Vice Minister of Higher Education, Science and Technology
Stella Christie
highlighted women’s contribution to global scientific progress and expressed support for cooperation between Russian and Indonesian women’s organisations in advancing technological innovation.
✔️
Participants’ proposals for strengthening international cooperation in high-tech industries will be reflected in the Jakarta Action Plan - 2026 "Women in Technology".
