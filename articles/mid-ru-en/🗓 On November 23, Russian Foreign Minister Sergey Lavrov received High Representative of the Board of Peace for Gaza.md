# 🗓 On November 23, Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov received High Representative of the Board of Peace for Gaza

Published: 2026-11-23 17:02:36

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33566

🗓
On November 23, Russian Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
received
High Representative of the Board of Peace for Gaza
Nikolay Mladenov
, who is in Moscow on a working visit.
The discussion focused primarily on the Middle East settlement
. The Russian Side reaffirmed its firm commitment to
the two-state solution to the Palestinian-Israeli conflict
, based on the universally recognised international legal framework and relevant UN Security Council and General Assembly resolutions.
☝️
Russia expressed its readiness to facilitate the parties’ return to negotiations on a lasting settlement
, with the aim of establishing
an independent Palestinian State
, ensuring territorial contiguity between Gaza and the West Bank, with East Jerusalem as its capital.
The unacceptability of resorting to terrorism and unilateral steps
that prejudge the status of the Palestinian territories was emphasised.
The Minister separately stressed
the responsibility of external actors
and the need to coordinate their efforts while fully taking into account the positions of regional states.
It was emphasised that
there is no alternative to resolving disputes through political means and negotiations
as the fundamental prerequisite for ensuring the security of all Middle Eastern countries without exception.
👉
Attention was drawn to
Russia’s Concept of Collective Security in the Persian Gulf
, the provisions of which could be applied to resolving other crises in the Middle East.
On the same day, Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister
Georgy Borisenko
held extensive consultations with
Nikolay Mladenov
.
