# 🎙 Remarks by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the Plenary Session of the Third Russia–Africa Summit

Published: 2026-10-29 12:04:45

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33078

🎙
Remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the Plenary Session of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 29, 2026)
Representatives of around 50 African countries, including Heads of State and government, as well as Leaders of the continent’s integration associations, are taking part in
the Summit
.
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Russia and Africa are united by truly
strong bonds of friendship and close partnership
.
We are natural allies in building a genuinely democratic
#MultipolarWorld
order
– one free from diktat and double standards, based on respect for international law and the fundamental principles of
the UN Charter
in their entirety and interrelationship.
☝️
Let me recall
the words
of
Julius Nyerere,
an outstanding Leader of the anti-colonial movement and the first President of Tanzania, –
“independence means self-reliance”
.
Guided by this approach,
our country has always stood by the peoples of Africa
, assisting their struggle against
colonial oppression
and providing comprehensive and selfless support to the continent’s states in their pursuit of freedom and genuine independence, sovereign development, and their own political and socioeconomic models.
I would like to emphasise that Russia has always been, and remains, interested in seeing
African countries defend their sovereignty, grow steadily, and strengthen their economies and social sectors.
We stand ready to continue working together with you in all these areas to achieve these goals.
Russia is a reliable supplier of food and agricultural products to African markets, making a tangible contribution to food security across Africa
. In particular, we supplied
more than 18 million tonnes of grain
to Africa in 2025 and
another 13 million tonnes
in the first nine months of 2026 alone. Russian mineral fertiliser supplies totalled
around 1.5 million tonnes
in 2025 and have already
exceeded 2.2 million tonnes
in 2026.
Our country also makes
annual contributions to
the UN World Food Programme’s
budget
for projects supporting the agricultural sector in African countries and regularly provides humanitarian aid free of charge to African states in need.
***
🌐
It is gratifying that the positions of Russia and African countries on the most pressing issues in world politics are largely similar or identical
. We advocate dialogue among equals and consideration for the interests of all states.
Our country seeks to do everything possible to strengthen Africa’s role in key multilateral forums
. We champion fairer African representation in global decision-making, including in
the UN Security Council
and international financial institutions.
We supported
the resolution
adopted by the UN General Assembly last December establishing
December 14 as
the International Day against Colonialism
. And, of course, we welcome
the UN’s decision
in March this year
to recognise the transatlantic slave trade as
the gravest crime against humanity
.
Russia makes a substantial contribution to resolving existing conflicts and preventing new ones on the African continent.
In doing so, we consistently uphold the fundamental principle that you yourselves proclaimed:
“African solutions to African problems
”.
We have intergovernmental agreements on military and military-technical cooperation with 45 African countries
. African military personnel and law enforcement officers receive training at Russian educational institutions.
Of course, I must also mention Russia’s systematic efforts to expand its diplomatic presence on the continent
. Since
the previous Summit in 2023
,
eight new Russian embassies
have opened in African countries, bringing
the total to 48
. We are also developing a network of trade missions. There are currently
only six
, but their activities cover
16 African countries
.
🤝
For our part, we provide all necessary assistance to African diplomatic missions operating in Russia.
Their number is also growing. We are aware of plans by
Botswana, Seychelles and Togo
to open embassies in Moscow in the near future and, naturally, support these plans.
