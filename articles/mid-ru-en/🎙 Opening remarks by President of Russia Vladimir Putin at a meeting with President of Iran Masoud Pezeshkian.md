🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Iran Masoud Pezeshkian
(Turkmenbashi, October 8, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We met quite recently
in Bishkek at
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Summit
.
Today, we have an opportunity to discuss
the development of our relations, the situation in the region
and how these processes are unfolding.
As for bilateral relations, I would like to note that they are
developing positively
on the basis of
our fundamental Treaty
, signed early last year.
📈
Progress in the economic sphere is certainly positive.
Trade grew by more than 21% in 2025,
and this trend continued in the first seven months of this year.
Our
humanitarian cooperation
, I believe, also sets a good example, with numerous events taking place in both Russia and Iran.
🤝
Overall, our relations are growing stronger
. I would ask you to convey
my very best wishes
to Iran’s Supreme Leader for his well-being and every success.
Of course, the most pressing issue today is
the situation in the region
. We know that
Iran is making concrete efforts to end the conflict
that broke out in the region
through no fault of its own
. Iran has once again put forward
new proposals
to resolve the situation by peaceful means.
You know our position, Mr President.
For our part, we are ready to do everything in our power to help you resolve this situation
.
We have an opportunity to discuss all these issues.
