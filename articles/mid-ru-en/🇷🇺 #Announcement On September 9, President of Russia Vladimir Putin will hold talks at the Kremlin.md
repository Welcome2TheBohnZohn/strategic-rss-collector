On September 9, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
will hold talks at the Kremlin
with General Secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam
To Lam
, who will visit Russia on a state visit.
The Leaders will discuss the current state and prospects for the further development of the Russia-Vietnam Comprehensive Strategic Partnership.
Key topics will include trade and economic cooperation, scientific and educational ties, humanitarian exchanges, as well as cooperation in security and defence.
The Sides will also exchange views on
current international and regional issues
.
Following the talks,
a package of bilateral documents
is expected to be adopted.
