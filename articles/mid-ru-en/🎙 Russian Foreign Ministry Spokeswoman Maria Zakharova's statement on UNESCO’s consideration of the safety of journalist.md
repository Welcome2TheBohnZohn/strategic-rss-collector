# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's statement on UNESCO’s consideration of the safety of journalists

Published: 2026-11-19 06:15:23

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33482

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
statement
on UNESCO’s consideration of the safety of journalists
(November 19, 2026)
💬
The 35th session
of the Intergovernmental Council of the International Programme for the Development of Communication
(
#IPDC
) opens on 19 November. The Council is expected to consider
the biennial report
by UNESCO Director-General
Khaled El-Enany
on the safety of journalists in 2024-2025.
❗️
Since its publication, the report has drawn
strong criticism from the journalistic community
. It
omits information
on Russian correspondents killed in terrorist attacks by the Kiev regime –
Semyon Yeryomin
,
Yulia Kuznetsova
,
Alexander Martemyanov
and
Nikita Goldin
.
The names of
many other Russian media workers
omitted from the 2022–2023 report are
still missing
, even though Russia formally notified the UNESCO Secretariat of these crimes through diplomatic channels.
👉
These omissions stem from the persistent refusal of Audrey Azoulay of France
, who served as UNESCO Director-General until November 2025,
to condemn these crimes and record the deaths of Russian media workers in UNESCO’s statistics.
Regrettably, the current Director-General’s report has thus inherited these distortions, making it
an unreliable source of data on the safety of journalists
.
Two years ago,
the controversial report
drew
objections from countries of the Global South and East
. For the first time, the Council
failed to approve it by consensus
and merely took note of it following a vote.
Russia is currently outside the IPDC Intergovernmental Council. At the upcoming session, we nevertheless intend to raise with the Director-General
the need to take due account, without distinction, of official information provided by member states on the killings of journalists
.
We will insist that UNESCO correct the errors made by its previous leadership and include in its statistics every Russian media worker who was killed. We will call on UNESCO to condemn the crimes committed against them, as provided for in the decisions of
the 1997 General Conference
and
the 2023 Executive Board
, among others.
☝️
We will press for
a review of the so-called methodology for recording the killings of media workers
, which member states have yet to approve, and for international officials to be held properly accountable.
Given
the widespread public and media attention
the report has received, we will draw the attention of the Secretariat and IPDC member states to the numerous appeals that news outlets and professional associations have sent to the Director-General
demanding justice for their fallen colleagues
.
We are convinced that this situation is
procedurally and legally unacceptable
and
raises serious moral questions
for an organisation with universal membership within the UN system whose remit includes culture, humanitarian affairs and the development of ethical standards in these areas.
We will continue to press our legitimate demands after the session
. We owe this to our profession and to
the journalists whose lives were cut short
.
🕯
We have a sacred duty to honour their memory and keep it alive.
