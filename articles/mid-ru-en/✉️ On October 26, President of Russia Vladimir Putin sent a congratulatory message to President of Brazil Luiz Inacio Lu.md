# ✉️ On October 26, President of Russia Vladimir Putin sent a congratulatory message to President of Brazil Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva

Published: 2026-10-26 10:59:57

✉️
On October 26, President of Russia Vladimir Putin sent
a congratulatory message
to President of Brazil Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva
.
✍️
Please accept my heartfelt congratulations on
your re-election as President of the Federative Republic of Brazil
.
The election results fully reaffirmed
your fellow citizens’ recognition of your outstanding leadership qualities
and
their support for your policies
aimed at ensuring the country’s sustainable socioeconomic development and defending its sovereignty and legitimate interests on the world stage.
Your personal contribution to strengthening
the strategic partnership between Brazil and Russia
cannot be overstated.
🤝
We will, of course, continue working closely together
to expand
our fruitful bilateral cooperation across all areas
and
our constructive engagement
within the
#UN
,
#BRICS
, the
#G20
and other multilateral frameworks.
I would also like to extend
my heartfelt congratulations on your birthday
and wish you sound health, happiness, well-being and continued success in your work for the benefit of the friendly Brazilian people.
Kind regards,
VLADIMIR PUTIN
