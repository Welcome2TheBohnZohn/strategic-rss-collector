On November 11, President of the Russian Federation Vladimir Putin
held a meeting
with Secretary General of the Collective Security Treaty Organisation (CSTO) Taalatbek Masadykov at the Kremlin
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am delighted to see you.
You assumed the post of Secretary General of the Collective Security Treaty Organisation while
Russia is holding the
#CSTO
chairmanship
and overseeing its operations.
I would like to note that
you have considerable experience, enjoy respect
and have a deep understanding of the Organisation’s sphere of operation.
You have been Deputy Secretary General of the CSTO since 2024, and there is
no doubt that under your guidance
the CSTO Secretariat will work smoothly
and stay on the right track, doing everything necessary to
strengthen
the
security
of our countries.
You have held meetings with the Leaders of CSTO countries. You also maintain contacts with the Leaders of other international organisations, and had an opportunity to attend exercises held within the CSTO framework.
I would like to assure you that
we will do everything necessary to assist you
with any organisational matters, and that I am personally at your disposal, as are
Mr Ushakov and the Security Council of Russia
, of course, should you need to elevate any issues to the level of the heads of state.
Please feel free to ask for help.
However, I think that you will be able to manage without us.
You have been Deputy Secretary General for several years, so you know the ropes and are deeply involved in every aspect of our collaboration.
