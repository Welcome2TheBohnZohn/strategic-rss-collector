Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the trilateral meeting with President of China Xi Jinping and President of the US Donald Trump
(Shenzhen, November 19, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
First of all, I would like to express
my gratitude
to our Chinese hosts and to President Xi Jinping for organising today’s meeting.
Today’s event
is being held in this format for the first time
and brings together the Leaders of three permanent members of the UN Security Council – Russia, the People’s Republic of China and the United States of America.
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
🇺🇸
Our three countries are united
by our status as
victorious powers in the Second World War
and, above all, by
our shared responsibility for maintaining international peace and security
.
We stood at the origins of
the United Nations
, therefore
the task of preserving and strengthening the system of international law
that took shape with the founding of this Organisation
falls to us
. That system includes
the UN Charter
and the principles enshrined in it.
We should, of course, also discuss
global and regional economic ties
, as well as
food and energy security
.
Our countries
share many interests when it comes to resolving regional conflicts
, including, of course, the conflict in Ukraine. President Xi Jinping and I discussed this
yesterday
. Today, at President Trump’s initiative, we devoted much of
our bilateral meeting
to the issue.
We also have a common interest in tackling the threats and challenges of our time
, including international terrorism and transnational organised crime.
🤝
For my part, I am convinced that
Russia, China and the US, the peoples of all three countries, can make a significant contribution to global security and stability
if we cooperate constructively as partners on the basis of mutual respect, equality and due regard for the balance of interests.
