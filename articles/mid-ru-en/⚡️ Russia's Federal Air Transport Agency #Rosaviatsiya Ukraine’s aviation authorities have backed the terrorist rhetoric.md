# ⚡️ Russia's Federal Air Transport Agency #Rosaviatsiya Ukraine’s aviation authorities have backed the terrorist rhetoric of their leadership

Published: 2026-09-02 19:00:46

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31807

⚡️
Russia's Federal Air Transport Agency
(
#Rosaviatsiya
)
Ukraine’s aviation authorities have backed the terrorist rhetoric of their leadership
aimed at disrupting the operation of Russia’s civil aviation and issued an illegitimate NOTAM alleging that Russian airspace is unsafe.
In response, Rosaviatsiya
issued a NOTAM
stating that all documents published by Ukraine concerning Russian airspace have
no legal force
and violate
the Convention on International Civil Aviation.
❗️
Russia’s air transport system and ground infrastructure continue to operate as normal, receiving and dispatching flights, including those of foreign airlines.
Rosaviatsiya, in coordination with other federal and regional authorities, is implementing
additional measures
to ensure the safe use of Russian airspace and strengthen the security of transport infrastructure on the ground.
