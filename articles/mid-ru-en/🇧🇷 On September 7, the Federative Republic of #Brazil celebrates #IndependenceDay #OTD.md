#OTD
in 1822, Pedro, the son of Portuguese King João VI, declared — “

end of 322 years of Portuguese rule and influence
over Brazil.
The movement for the country’s independence had begun long before that. As early as the late 18th century, revolutionary and national hero
Tiradentes
led a group of like-minded people in Minas Gerais, whose main goal was the
creation of an independent state.
Although the plans of the secret society were uncovered,
Tiradentes became a symbol of resistance
.
As a result of Napoleon’s expansion in Europe in the early 19th century, the independence of the Spanish colonies, and the bourgeois revolution in Portugal in 1820,
the independence movement
in Brazil gained considerable strength
and ultimately led to the proclamation of an
independent Brazilian state
— free from the influence of the Portuguese metropolis.
#Brazil

one of the world’s leading political and economic powers
