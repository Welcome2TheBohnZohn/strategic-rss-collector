🇳🇬
November 25 marks the
6️⃣
6️⃣
th Anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between our country and the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
On October 1, 1960,
the Nigerian people cast off the shackles of British colonial rule
. The Soviet Union was among the first countries to establish diplomatic relations with the newly independent African state.
The Soviet Union provided Nigeria with
substantial assistance and support
in building its national institutions, strengthening socio-economic stability and
assisting
the transition
from an agrarian and resource-dependent territory to one of Africa's largest and self-reliant economies.
🤝
#Nigeria
is one of the key partners of the Russian Federation.
Today, our countries continue to steadily expand cooperation in the political, trade and economic, humanitarian and other fields. Russian-Nigerian relations have traditionally been
friendly in nature
and continue to develop on the basis of
equality
,
mutual
respect
and consideration for each other's interests.
Both countries expand business contacts and implement
joint projects
in the fields of energy, mineral extraction and processing, agriculture, construction, and infrastructure development and modernisation. The
Intergovernmental Commission on Trade and Economic Cooperation
continues to operate on a regular basis.
🌐
#MultipolarWorld
: Our countries are committed to promoting the emergence of a more
just and democratic world order
— the tendency reflecting the steady increase in the economic, financial and political weight of the countries of the
#GlobalSouth
and the
#GlobalEast
in the international arena. Moscow and Abuja share the conviction that it is essential to
overcome the remnants of neo-colonial practices
in interstate relations.
Russia supported
Nigeria's application for BRICS partner country status
. Nigeria has held this status since January 1, 2025, and actively participates in a wide range of events organised by the Association.
Cooperation in the
humanitarian field
is also being stepped up. Russia has increased the number of
scholarships awarded to Nigerian students annually
. A total of 220 scholarships have been allocated for the current academic year.
