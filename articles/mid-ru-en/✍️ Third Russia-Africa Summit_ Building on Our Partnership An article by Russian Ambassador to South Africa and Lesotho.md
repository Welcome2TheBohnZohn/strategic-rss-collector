Third Russia-Africa Summit: Building on Our Partnership
An article by Russian Ambassador to South Africa and Lesotho
Roman Ambarov,
published in
IOL
, The Lesotho Tribune and The Public Eye newspapers (12-13 November 2026)
🔴
Strong African participation
The Third Russia-Africa Summit and Economic and Humanitarian Forum
, held in Moscow on 27–29 October, brought together representatives
of 51 out of 54 African countries,
alongside business leaders and experts.
For many African participants, particularly first-time visitors, the summit offered an opportunity to
discover the “real Russia”
and form their own opinions beyond the narratives often presented in Western media.
🔴
Listening to Africa
For Russia,
listening to our African partners is just as important as presenting our own ideas.
The best results come when we identify shared interests and work out solutions together.
🔴
Equality and mutual respect
Russia and African countries are committed to developing relations based on sovereign equality, mutual respect and practical results.
🔴
Investment, not exploitation
Russian companies are encouraged to invest in African economies, establish local production facilities, share expertise, cooperate with local businesses and create jobs.
African countries should have greater opportunities to
process their own natural resources, expand industrial capacity and move beyond dependence on raw-material exports.
🔴
Important Agreements
and
a Roadmap until 2029
The Summit concluded with
a comprehensive 101-point Moscow declaration outlining
priorities for Russia-Africa cooperation across politics, security, economic development, humanitarian affairs, environmental protection and climate issues.
🔴
Addressing the legacy of colonialism
The Declaration supports developing legal mechanisms to assess and compensate for colonial-era damage inflicted on African countries, as well as the return of cultural property taken from the continent.
🔴
A practical roadmap for 2027
-2
029
The Russia-Africa Partnership Forum Action Plan provides a framework for implementing Summit agreements and advancing cooperation between high-level meetings.
🔴
Expanding trade and investment
Russia-Africa trade
exceeded US$27 billion
in 2025, with
significant potential for further growth and diversification
.
Around 85% of commercial transactions between Russia and African countries are conducted in
national currencies,
including the Russian rouble.
🔴
Energy and nuclear cooperation
Russia is ready to
strengthen cooperation in oil and gas, hydropower and nuclear energy
.
Rosatom signed three agreements with Togo, Rwanda and Uganda during the Summit, advancing potential nuclear energy projects.
🔴
Food security and agricultural development
Russia exported
over 18 million tonnes of grain
to Africa in 2025 and another
13 million tonnes
in the first nine months of 2026.
We will
continue
supplying grain, agricultural products and fertilisers.
🔴
Technological sovereignty
Russia is ready to
share its technological expertise and digital solutions with
African partners.
☝️
The objective is to help African countries develop their own technological capabilities, knowledge and skills, reducing dependence on external providers.
🔴
From commitments to implementation
The Summit established priorities and frameworks for expanding cooperation.
❗️
The next step is to
translate agreements into concrete projects and maintain regular engagement with African partners.
