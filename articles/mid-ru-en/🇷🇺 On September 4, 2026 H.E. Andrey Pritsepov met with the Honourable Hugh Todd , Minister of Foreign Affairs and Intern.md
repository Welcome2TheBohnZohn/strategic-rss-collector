🇬🇾
On September 4, 2026 H.E.
Andrey Pritsepov
met with the Honourable
Hugh Todd
, Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of Guyana, at his request.
❗
The Minister thoroughly outlined Georgetown's foreign policy priorities, emphasizing principles of multipolarity and respect for the interests of all stakeholders. Guyana positions itself as bridge for developing fruitful cooperation between developed and Global South countries.
❗
The Guyanese Minister paid special attention to the upcoming UN Secretary General election and Guyana's candidate
Carolyn Rodrigues Birkett
. H.Todd noted she has extensive experience in international politics and if elected to this high position will be guided by principle of impartial mediation in resolving international issues.
❗
In response Ambassador emphasized that for Russia competence and impartiality remain the candidates’ key qualities.
🇷🇺
🇬🇾
They discussed upcoming meeting between Russian Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
and Hugh Todd at the 81st session of the UN GA.
