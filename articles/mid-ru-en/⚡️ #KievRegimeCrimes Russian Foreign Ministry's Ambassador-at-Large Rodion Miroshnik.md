The Ukrainian Armed Forces (UAF) attacked a group of Ukrainian civilians just as they were evacuating from the Kupyansk-Uzlovoye town to areas controlled by the Russian Federation.

Of the 22 civilians, only 15 managed to reach safety.
On the morning of November 10, a group of 22 civilians left their temporary shelters in Kupyansk-Uzlovoye and set out toward territories controlled by Russian armed forces. To leave the combat zone, the group of civilians had to travel more than 20 km.
Engineering units of the 153rd Tank Regiment of the 47th Tank Division prepared the evacuation routes, surveyed the terrain, and cleared the route of mines.
Halfway to the safety zone, a group of civilians carrying white flags and armbands was first subjected to a drone attack by the Ukrainian Armed Forces (UAF), then an explosive device planted by a Ukrainian drone detonated in the immediate vicinity of the group of refugees. This horrifying crime is depicted on the video.
As a result of these criminal actions by the Ukrainian Armed Forces,
seven civilians were killed.
Among the dead were a child and his mother. All civilians participating in the evacuation were Ukrainian citizens, residents of the Kharkov region. Ukrainian Armed Forces units stationed in the area had been informed of the humanitarian evacuation but carried out a
deliberate criminal attack
on a group of unarmed civilians attempting to leave the combat zone.
All those who managed to make their way out of the danger zone were picked up by Russian soldiers and taken to safe locations, where they have received all necessary assistance.
