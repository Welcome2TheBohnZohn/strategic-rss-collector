On August 31 2026, at the personal initiative of staff members of Russian diplomatic missions and representative offices in Jakarta and in response to a request from residents of Nagekeo, East Nusa Tenggara Province,
humanitarian assistance for communities impacted by the earthquake on Flores Island
was delivered
to Indonesia’s National Agency for Disaster Management
.

rice, cooking oil, instant noodles, and other essential food and household supplies intended to support local families in need
.
🤝
The initiative
reflects genuine solidarity
with those affected by the earthquake and
aims to support local communities
during this difficult time.
