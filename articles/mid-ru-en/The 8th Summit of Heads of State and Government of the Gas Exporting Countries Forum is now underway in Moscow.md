The 8th Summit of Heads of State and Government of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum is now underway in Moscow
Prior to the start of the GECF Summit, the leaders gathered for a group photo.
Together, the GECF member countries control 70 percent of the world's proven natural gas reserves and 38 percent of its production.
