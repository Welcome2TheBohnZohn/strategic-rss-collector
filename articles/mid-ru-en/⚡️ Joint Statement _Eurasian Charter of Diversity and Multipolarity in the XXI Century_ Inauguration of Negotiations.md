# ⚡️ Joint Statement "Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the XXI Century: Inauguration of Negotiations"

Published: 2026-09-25 18:31:22

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32343

⚡️
Joint Statement "Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the XXI Century: Inauguration of Negotiations"
(New York, September 25, 2026)
✍️
We, the representatives of the Republic of Belarus, the Islamic Republic of Iran, the Republic of Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic, the Lao People's Democratic Republic, the Republic of the Union of Myanmar, the Russian Federation, the Republic of Tajikistan, the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam, meeting on 25 September 2026 on the margins of the high-level week of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly,
Reaffirming
our dedication to the purposes and principles enshrined in
the UN Charter
and international law, such as sovereign equality, political independence and territorial integrity of all States, non-interference in their internal affairs, the inviolability of State borders, and the peaceful settlement of disputes,
Maintaining
international peace and security, friendly relations among States, sovereign equality of States, and refraining from the threat or use of force,
Guided
by the shared commitment to strengthen international peace, security, and stability on the Eurasian continent and worldwide,
Recognizing
the inherent diversity of the world, including the diversity of civilizations, cultures, traditions, and models of development, as a fundamental principle of international relations,
Reafirming
that such diversity should be respected in full conformity with international law, including respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms for all,
Convinced
of the need to promote a more inclusive, democratic and equitable international order,
Emphasizing
that the emerging
#MultipolarWorld
order must be based on international law, the central coordinating role of the UN and equal opportunities for all States, regardless of their size, economic potential or military capabilities,
Convinced
of the need to promote a genuine multipolar world order that ensures equal and indivisible security, mutually beneficial cooperation, and sustainable development for all states,
Recalling
the idea of a Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the XXI Century (hereinafter – the Charter), first articulated at
the International Conference on Eurasian Security in Minsk
in October 2023, and noting the subsequent discussions on the Charter that have taken place in various venues and formats since then,
Desiring
to translate the idea into a political framework for pan
-
Eurasian cooperation,
Have agreed as follows:
1.
We hereby
officially launch a negotiation process
to develop and adopt
the Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the XXI Century
.
Given the Eurasian character of the initiative, the negotiation process shall be
open to all interested Eurasian States
on the basis of the principles of
sovereign equality, inclusiveness, transparency and equal participation
.
All decisions on substantive and procedural issues, including the approval of the draft Charter, the determination of its legal nature, format and procedure for adoption, shall be taken
only on the basis of concensus
.
***
2.
The purpose of this process is to develop a political document
that sets out the principles of cooperation, reaffirms commitment to international law, and defines common approaches to ensuring equal and indivisible security in the Eurasian space and fostering economic partnerships, promoting cultural and humanitarian ties, and facilitating civilizational exchanges in a multipolar era.
***
3.
To this end,
we establish
a Working Group
to develop the Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the XXI Century
, which will conduct the negotiations on the Eurasian Charter in accordance with the Terms of Reference of the Working Group attached to this Joint Statement.
