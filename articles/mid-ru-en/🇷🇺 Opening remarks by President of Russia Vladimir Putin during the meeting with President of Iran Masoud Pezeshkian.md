🇮🇷
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during the meeting with President of Iran Masoud Pezeshkian
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
I am very pleased
to see you again
, this time here at the SCO Summit in Kyrgyzstan. As you know, we use
every opportunity
to meet, talk and hold consultations.
Friendly Russia-Iran relations continue to develop steadily across all areas,
in keeping with the letter and spirit of
the Treaty on Comprehensive Strategic Partnership
.
☝️
As I have said on many occasions, Russia stands in solidarity with the people of Iran
. We admire your courage and resilience in defending your national interests. Please convey my greetings and sincere words of support to Supreme Leader of the Islamic Republic
Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei.
We welcomed the conclusion in June of this year of the Iran-US ceasefire memorandum
, but unfortunately
the ceasefire proved unstable and overly fragile
.
During this difficult period, we have been trying to provide you with the assistance you need. We have discussed this with you, and
we are supplying the necessary goods
.
Despite the military and political situation,
we have maintained last year’s momentum in trade, economic ties and cooperation
. This is due first and foremost to the effective work of the intergovernmental commission, which meets regularly and held another session quite recently.
The free trade agreement between the EAEU and Iran
is also, in my view,
an important factor.
Considerable attention is also being paid to expanding humanitarian cooperation
: more and more Iranian students are choosing to study in Russia, which we are very pleased to see. A large number of future medical professionals are studying in Russia. In the 2025-2026 academic year,
the number of Iranian students in Russia approached 9,000
, including 802 studying at the expense of the Russian federal budget.
🤝
We have many issues to discuss
. I am very pleased to have this opportunity to meet with you and your team today.
