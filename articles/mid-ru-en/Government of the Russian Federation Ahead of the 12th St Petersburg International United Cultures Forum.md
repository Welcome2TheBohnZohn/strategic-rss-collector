# : Government of the Russian Federation Ahead of the 12th St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum

Published: 2026-09-23 12:46:04

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32192

Government of the Russian Federation
:
🎙
Ahead of
the 12th St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
, Russia's Deputy Prime Minister Tatyana Golikova, who chairs the Forum’s organising committee,
gave an interview to TASS Director General
Andrey Kondrashov.
📺
Full video
(in Russian)
Tatyana Golikova noted considerable interest in the forum
: representatives of
70 countries
are expected to attend, with
44 official foreign delegations
confirmed. Around
2,500 delegates
will attend the main programme, while registrations for the forum’s first-ever youth programme are approaching
1,500
.
Speaking about international participation, Tatyana Golikova noted that representatives would also be coming
from countries Russia designates as unfriendly
.
💬
Russia's Deputy Prime Minister
Tatyana Golikova:
If we are talking about a thaw in relations, we can see that attempts to cancel Russian culture and isolate our country have clearly failed.
As for our forum, after 2022 we reassessed the approaches we had followed before the pandemic and even changed its name to reflect Russia’s unifying role as a spiritual and cultural guide and a champion of traditional values.
***
The 12th St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
will take place in St Petersburg on September 24-26, 2026. Its main theme is “United Cultures – Shared Values”.
