Russia–South Africa Strengthen Cooperation on Consumer Protection
On 29 October, on the sidelines of the
Third Russia–Africa Summit
in Moscow, Head of Rospotrebnadzor
Anna Popova
and Acting Commissioner of South Africa’s National Consumer Commission
Hardin Ratshisusu
signed a Memorandum of Cooperation.
The agreement provides for closer institutional ties, the exchange of expertise and information, and the development of joint approaches to protecting consumer rights, including within the
#BRICS
framework.
The Memorandum represents a practical step towards translating dialogue into concrete cooperation.
❗️
Russia remains committed to sharing its extensive experience, knowledge and best practices with African countries
, supporting the development of effective consumer protection systems and strengthening partnerships based on mutual respect, trust and the exchange of expertise.
