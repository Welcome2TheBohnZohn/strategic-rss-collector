# ❗️ 270 Russian servicemen returned from Kiev regime's territory In exchange, 270 AFU prisoners of war were transferred.

Published: 2026-10-23 16:00:30

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32917

❗️
270 Russian servicemen returned from Kiev regime's territory
In exchange, 270 AFU prisoners of war were transferred.
Currently, the returned Russian servicemen are on the territory of the Republic of Belarus. Yana Lantratova, the Commissioner for Human Rights in Russia, is working with them.
Upon receiving first aid and psychological assistance, all the released servicemen will be sent to the Russian Federation for treatment and rehabilitation at medical institutions of the Russian Ministry of Defence.
