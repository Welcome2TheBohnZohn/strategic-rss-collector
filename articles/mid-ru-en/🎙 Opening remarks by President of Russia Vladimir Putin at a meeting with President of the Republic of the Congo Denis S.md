Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of the Republic of the Congo Denis Sassou Nguesso on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 29, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We worked together at both
the First
and
Second Russia–Africa Summits
, held in Sochi and St Petersburg. Today, together with our other African friends, we have completed
the Third Summit
.
In my view,
the outcome
is very satisfactory indeed.
We have achieved virtually all the objectives we set ourselves.
🇷🇺
🇨🇬
Our bilateral relations are developing successfully.
We maintain
regular political dialogue
, expand trade and economic ties, and strengthen scientific, technical and humanitarian cooperation.
Last year, we concluded
an important intergovernmental agreement on the promotion and reciprocal protection of investments
. Russia
has already completed the domestic procedures
required for its entry into force.
The eighth meeting of the intergovernmental commission
took place
in Brazzaville on September 28-30
. As we agreed, a plan for the comprehensive development of cooperation between the Russian Federation and the Republic of the Congo for 2026-2028 has been signed.
Currently,
863 students from the Republic of the Congo are studying at Russian educational institutions
. This cooperation rests on a solid historical foundation.
Cultural ties are also growing stronger
. In mid-July, Brazzaville hosted
a three-day festival of Russian language and culture
entitled
“We Are Together”
, which concluded with an open day dedicated to Russian education.
🤝
Mr President, we will certainly
continue to move forward
at the same pace in all these areas.
