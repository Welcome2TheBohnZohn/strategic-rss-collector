#GeographicalDictation2026
🌍
In a great annual tradition,
the Russian Geographical Society
invites you to take part in
the 2026 Geographical Dictation
.
Launched at the Society’s initiative with the support of
the President of Russia
,
MFA Russia
and
Rossotrudnichestvo
, this annual international educational event encourages people around the world
to explore Russia
and
discover its beauty that stretches from the Baltic to the Pacific
.
The questions range from familiar facts to puzzles that call for logic, a keen eye and creative thinking
. You can test your knowledge, learn something new about Russia and see geography in a different light.
🗓
The online Dictation is available from
November 22
to
December 10
, 2026
(12 pm local time). You can take part at any time during this period.
☝️
No registration is required.
Choose from four versions:
▪️
Main version
– 30 questions in Russian.
▪️
International version
– 20 questions in English and in other languages where volunteer translations are available.
▪️
Extra version
– 30 questions in Russian and English. You can take this version as many times as you like.
▪️
Geodictation. Advanced Level
– 20 questions for those who want to test their geography knowledge at expert level.
🌐
In 2025, the Dictation set a new record with 10,375 venues across 120 countries.
Oman, Jamaica and the Dominican Republic took part for the first time. 2025 marked the 180th anniversary of
the Russian Geographical Society
, the 80th anniversary of
Victory in the Great Patriotic War
and 500 years of exploration of
the Northern Sea Route
.
Over six years, geographers from around the world have submitted
more than 4,500 questions
to the organisers through the Dictation’s question-writing competition.
👉
Take part on the official Geographical Dictation website
.
