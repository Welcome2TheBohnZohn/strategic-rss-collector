# 🗓 On October 30-31, Atlanta (USA) will host the G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting under the US Presidency.

Published: 2026-10-29 09:32:55

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33077

🗓
On October 30-31, Atlanta (USA)
will host
the G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting
under the US Presidency.
Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov will, as usual, head the Russian delegation
. Most G20 members are expected to be represented at a high level.
The upcoming Foreign Ministers’ Meeting marks
the conclusion of this year’s series of G20 sectoral ministerial meetings
and the start of the final stage of
the US Presidency
, which will culminate in
the Summit in Miami on December 14-15
.
☝️
At the meeting in Atlanta, Russia intends to set a clear course towards substantive and balanced agreements among G20 leaders
, reached by consensus and fully reflecting the priorities of the countries of the
#GlobalMajority
.
The US’s stated goal of returning the G20 to its original purpose
– ensuring sustained, inclusive growth and maintaining financial stability –
broadly aligns with Russia’s position on the need to uphold the forum’s economic mandate and depoliticise its work
. The G20 should serve as an effective platform for dialogue among equals, rather than deepen dividing lines.
❌
In this context, Russia opposes Washington’s unilateral decision to exclude South Africa from the G20 process
. Moscow has consistently called for Pretoria’s rights as a G20 member to be restored as soon as possible.
Russia expects the Foreign Ministers’ Meeting to proceed in a constructive and impartial manner
. Substantive discussions are anticipated on the impact of global geopolitical instability on the world economy, topical aspects of multilateral energy cooperation and the effective functioning of supply chains.
***
In his remarks, Sergey Lavrov will outline Russia’s assessment of the main drivers of international tensions, focusing on
:
• the refusal of certain powers to acknowledge the rise of new centres of power;
• the use of economic “weapons” against opponents, including sanctions, trade wars and disruptions to supply chains;
• the persistent North-South imbalances resulting from this destructive policy.
🌐
Russia will reaffirm its position that prosperity for all peoples can only be achieved by maintaining an open world economy
, including by curbing unfair competition and the neocolonial practices widely employed by the West.
