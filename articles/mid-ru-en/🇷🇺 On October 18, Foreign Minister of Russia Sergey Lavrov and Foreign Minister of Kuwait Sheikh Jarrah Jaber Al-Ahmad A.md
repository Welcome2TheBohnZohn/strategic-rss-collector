🇷🇺
🇰🇼
On October 18, Foreign Minister of Russia Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Kuwait Sheikh Jarrah Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah
held a meeting in Manama
on the sidelines of the ninth Ministerial Meeting of the Russia-Gulf Cooperation Council (
#GCC
) Strategic Dialogue.
The Foreign Ministers discussed
the further development of traditionally friendly Russia-Kuwait relations
.
🤝
They reaffirmed the shared commitment of Moscow and Kuwait City
to maintaining intensive political dialogue
and
strengthening mutually beneficial trade, economic, cultural and humanitarian cooperation
.
The Sides exchanged views on current international and regional issues,
focusing on the situation in the Persian Gulf
. They stressed
the need to find ways to de-escalate tensions as soon as possible
in this strategically important region.
❗️
The Foreign Ministers underscored that
there is no alternative to resolving existing differences through political and diplomatic means
, with full respect for the interests of all countries in the region involved in the conflict.
