# ⚡️ In response to the unfriendly actions of the German side in closing the Consulate General of Russia in Bonn

Published: 2026-09-07 15:02:51

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31882

⚡️
In response to the unfriendly actions
of the German side in closing
the Consulate General of Russia in Bonn
and
the Russian House of Science and Culture in Berlin
, on September 7 the Chargé d’Affaires a.i. of Germany in Moscow
was informed
of
the following retaliatory measures
:
The Russian Federation is
withdrawing its consent
to the operation of the Consulate General of the Federal Republic of Germany in St Petersburg, which must cease its activities
no later than September 18, 2026
, with all its staff required to leave Russia by that date.
The Goethe-Institut cultural centres in Russia
must cease their activities
.
Their seconded staff have been instructed to leave Russia
no later than September 13, 2026
.
The head of the diplomatic mission was presented with
the relevant notes
of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
❗️
It was stressed that it was the German authorities that once again provoked a new round of escalation in bilateral relations. Full responsibility for the consequences rests entirely with the German Government.
