Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
response
to a question from
RBC
(RosBusinessConsulting)
on the US deployment of a launcher for intermediate- and shorter-range missiles in Norway
(September 7, 2026)
❓
Question: How does Moscow assess the US deployment of a launcher for intermediate- and shorter-range missiles in Norway, including in the context of the stalled strategic stability dialogue? Is any response planned? If so, what kind?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
We regard the appearance in Europe of US systems capable of launching intermediate- and shorter-range missiles as
yet another step in the endless series of acutely destabilising actions by NATO countries.
In pursuing plans to deploy Western ground-launched intermediate- and shorter-range missiles in the region, NATO not only makes no secret of its intention but
openly flaunts its ambition to acquire a substantial capability to strike deep into Russia’s territory
.
At the same time,
NATO is seeking to bring vast maritime areas of the region within its sights
, establishing dominant control over sea routes of critical military, commercial and transport importance.
In this context, we note that the US missile systems equipped with
Mk 41 launching systems
, which are being brought into Europe and have now been deployed in Norway,
are capable of launching a wide range of missiles against both land and maritime targets
.
☝️
We also take into account that these systems can be used to launch
long-range Tomahawk cruise missiles
, while their deployment in Norway puts
a substantial part of Russia’s European territory, including Moscow, within range
.
Thus, these missile systems could be used in aggressive actions by NATO countries against our country to accomplish
strategic-level objectives
.
Those in Washington and Oslo who made the decision to deploy these systems in Norway should therefore not be surprised that, as part of Russia’s response measures, the deployment sites of the US systems, their command and control facilities, as well as the infrastructure supporting them,
are becoming a particular focus for Russia’s strategic deterrence forces
. This includes planning for the combat employment of the relevant strike assets in the event of an armed conflict.
***
As for the dialogue with the US on strategic stability, we would like to recall that it did not simply “stall”.
It was unilaterally suspended by the Joe Biden administration.
The current US authorities, meanwhile, are
unrealistically discussing the launch of some kind of “multilateral” negotiations with China’s mandatory participation
, even though Beijing has repeatedly and unequivocally stated that it has compelling reasons not to become involved in such work at this stage.
❗️
For our part, we have repeatedly explained in detail that
the conditions for a substantive and productive strategic dialogue with the US are not in place
.
Steps such as the deployment in Europe of US ground-launched intermediate- and shorter-range missiles that threaten Russia will, to put it mildly,
eliminate any basis for a constructive strategic dialogue with the US
.
