# 🇷🇺 On September 4, 2026, Ambassador of Russia to Indonesia Sergei Tolchenov met with Minister of Health of Indonesia

Published: 2026-09-05 22:34:30

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31861

🇷🇺
🇮🇩
On September 4, 2026, Ambassador of Russia to Indonesia
Sergei Tolchenov
met with Minister of Health of Indonesia
Budi Gunadi Sadikin
.
🤝
The parties discussed prospects for
strengthening bilateral cooperation in the field of healthcare
, including the exchange of experience in public health, the development of medical technologies, cooperation in the pharmaceutical sector, as well as contacts between specialized institutions of the two countries.
✅
The sides expressed their mutual interest in expanding practical cooperation and
maintaining a constructive dialogue
on issues of common interest.
