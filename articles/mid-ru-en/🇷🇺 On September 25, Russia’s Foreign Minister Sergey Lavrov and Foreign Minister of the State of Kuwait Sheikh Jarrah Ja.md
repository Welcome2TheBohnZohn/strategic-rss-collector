Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks

further developing Russia-Kuwait cooperation
.
They reaffirmed
Moscow and Kuwait City’s mutual commitment to strengthening traditionally friendly ties
, including maintaining close coordination at the UN.
The Sides also compared their positions on
key international and regional issues
, including escalating tensions in the Persian Gulf.
