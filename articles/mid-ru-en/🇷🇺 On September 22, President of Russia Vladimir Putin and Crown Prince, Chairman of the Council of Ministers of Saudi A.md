On September 22, President of Russia Vladimir Putin and Crown Prince, Chairman of the Council of Ministers of Saudi Arabia Mohammed bin Salman Al Saud
had a telephone conversation
.
Vladimir Putin congratulated the Crown Prince on
the upcoming Saudi National Day
, noting that 100 years ago,
the Soviet Union became the first foreign state to recognise the Saudi Kingdom
.
📈
Both Sides expressed
satisfaction with the level of cooperation achieved
in political, trade, economic and humanitarian affairs.
The Leaders held
an in-depth exchange of views on developments in the Middle East
, particularly the Persian Gulf. They stressed that there is
no alternative to political and diplomatic efforts
to resolve the current crisis, with due regard for the interests of all parties.
Given the deteriorating military and political situation in
Yemen
, they reaffirmed
the need to end hostilities as soon as possible
and create
conditions for constructive dialogue among Yemeni parties
under UN auspices.
They also emphasised the importance of ensuring
safe and unimpeded passage for vessels through the region’s international waterways
, including the Bab el-Mandeb and Hormuz straits.
🤝
The Leaders
agreed to maintain contacts
at various levels.
