# 🗓 On August 31 – September 1, 2026, President of Russia Vladimir Putin visited Bishkek

Published: 2026-09-02 09:59:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31796

🗓
On August 31 – September 1, 2026,
President of Russia Vladimir Putin
visited Bishkek
, where he took part in the events of the
anniversary Summit of
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
(
#SCO
), marking the Organisation’s 25th anniversary.
Key events of the anniversary Summit:
👏
Opening ceremony of the VI World Nomad Games
🎙
Meeting of the SCO Council of Heads of State
🎙
SCO Plus meeting
💬
From
Vladimir Putin’s remarks
at the meeting of the SCO Council of Heads of State:
Over the past 25 years, the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation has come a long way, becoming one of the independent and influential centres of the emerging
#MultipolarWorld
.
Today, the SCO is effectively the largest regional association not only on our common Eurasian continent, but in the world as a whole.
Its member states account for almost half of the world’s population and more than one-third of global GDP.
✍️
Following the meeting,
a package of 28 documents
was adopted, including
the Bishkek Declaration on the 25th Anniversary of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
.
