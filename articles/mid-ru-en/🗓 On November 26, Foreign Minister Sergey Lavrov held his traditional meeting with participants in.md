On November 26, Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
held his traditional meeting
with participants in
the Dialogue for the Future programme
, an annual research and educational initiative organised by the
Alexander Gorchakov Public Diplomacy Fund
.
In an informal, interactive session with the head of Russian diplomacy, young representatives of the academic, research and expert communities of Russia and other countries exchanged views on
various aspects of international relations
. The discussion focused on key developments and trends of 2026.
Participants in the programme examined, through the lens of the Russian school of diplomacy, the challenges of
conducting negotiations amid political turbulence
, as well as mechanisms for
strengthening interstate dialogue
.
