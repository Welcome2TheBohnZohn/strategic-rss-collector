Comment of the Embassy of Russia on the disinformation campaign held by the Embassy of Ukraine in Jakarta
The Embassy of Ukraine in Jakarta has recently spread the comment claiming that at least eight Indonesian citizens who visited Russia allegedly joined Russian Armed Forces – is a
propaganda-driven disinformation campaign
. The copies of e-visas and migration card issued for tourist trips, which were published by the Ukrainian Embassy, certainly
do not constitute any evidence
of joining military service.
At the same time,
Ukraine itself is actively recruiting foreign mercenaries
into the ranks of its military formations. This is due to their significant losses on the battlefield – over 700,000 killed and 1 million wounded. The Ukrainian Ministry of Defense has stated that, in the long term, its army should consist of 30–50% foreigners. Kiev employs
deceptive methods
and makes use of diplomatic missions and affiliated organizations abroad to recruit them. Tens of thousands of citizens from the other countries are currently serving in the Ukrainian army.
Several thousand people died, Indonesian citizens were also among them.
The Ukrainian Embassy’s allegations about Kiev’s adherence to international humanitarian law and the humane treatment of prisoners of war are
extremely cynical
. The majority of the prisoners of war who returned from Ukraine as part of prisoners’ exchanges reported torture, abuse, and cruel treatment while in captivity.
It is obvious that the anti-Russian disinformation campaign held by the Ukrainian Embassy was timed to coincide with the participation of President of Indonesia Prabowo Subianto in
the XI Eastern Economic Forum
in Vladivostok and
aimed at damaging the Russian-Indonesian relations
.
