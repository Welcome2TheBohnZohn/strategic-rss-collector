# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova on the anniversary of the tragic events in Ukraine:

Published: 2026-11-21 08:02:27

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33511

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman
Maria Zakharova
on the anniversary of the tragic events in Ukraine:
💬
We need to talk about the origins of everything we are witnessing in Ukraine today. On November 21, Ukraine will mark
the 13th anniversary of the so-called “revolution”
.
In reality, it was
yet another Western-orchestrated “colour revolution”
, aimed at subjugating Ukraine, turning it into a permanent hotbed of instability and a springboard for an offensive against Russia, against our country.
Generously funded from abroad
,
skilled provocateurs lured crowds into anti-government street protests
with promises of a better “European” life, visa-free travel to EU countries, democracy, freedom of speech, human rights and the fight against corruption.
☝️
Even then,
the protesters were being incited to adopt anti-Russian and nationalist attitudes
, encouraged towards aggression, hatred of humanity and, quite literally, dehumanisation. It was no coincidence that
Bandera-inspired chants
calling for the killing of Russians
(“Hang the Moskals!”)
could be heard among the demonstrators.
Today, it would be appropriate to ask:
what exactly has this “Euromaidan” brought Ukraine?
Has its cherished dream of integration into the European family come true?
👉
The answer is obvious.
The West viewed Ukraine as
an instrument
, and that is exactly how it continues to treat the country.
Yes, every now and then, they dangle something soft and fluffy before people's eyes in the form of
yet more promises
of a bright future. Then they proceed, in the most brutal fashion, to do everything possible
to destroy Ukrainian citizens.
And it is the West that is doing this through Zelensky.
First and foremost, they are killing Ukrainians
. They are clearing Ukraine of its indigenous population.
Once a
self-sufficient, industrially developed and populous
republic of the former Soviet Union, Ukraine has been reduced to
an impoverished, corruption-ridden territory facing demographic collapse.
It has lost its independence as a state.
👉
Where is the dignity they spoke so much about?
What good is a visa-free regime in a country that
has closed its borders
and does not even allow its own men to travel abroad, while at home conducting the most brutal,
cynical manhunt against its own people?
Those who have stolen millions, even billions and seek to move their fortunes abroad are the ones reaping the benefits. But
they are a small minority
, while the vast majority of Ukrainians have far more pressing concerns than visa-free travel.
What about freedom of speech under a
single nationwide television marathon
, where only one officially approved opinion is permitted?
Or perhaps freedom of conscience, amid the authorities' blatant interference in church affairs,
interference that has gone so far as to destroy the Church itself?
Their drive
to ban canonical Orthodoxy
and force millions of people to renounce their faith?
***
Can
the events of 13 years ago and everything that followed
really be considered cause for celebration?
Well, the Zelensky regime could not care less. As though nothing had happened, they will mark yet another anniversary of the
euromaidan
.
They will be celebrating the genocide of their own people.
They are simply being paid to do so.
(From
the briefing
by Russia's Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova on November 20, 2026)
