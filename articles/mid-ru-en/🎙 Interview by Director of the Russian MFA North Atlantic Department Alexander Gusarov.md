# 🎙 Interview by Director of the Russian MFA North Atlantic Department Alexander Gusarov

🎙
Interview
by Director of the Russian MFA North Atlantic Department
Alexander Gusarov
with
International Affairs magazine
(October 22, 2026)
Key points
:
•
The North Atlantic Department is considered one of
the Ministry’s
most distinguished and well-established departments
, with a history dating back to the 1920s. Its formal founding date, regarded as its birthday,
is October 1, 1925.
•
We regard regular consultations between the Russian and US leaderships, as well as through other working channels, as
an important stabilising force in bilateral relations and the entire international security architecture.
•
Restoring a constructive approach to
#RussiaUSA
relations does not automatically resolve all the problems and disagreements
that have accumulated between our countries over the years. Clearly,
a great deal of painstaking work still lies ahead.
•
We have no doubt that US President Donald Trump is sincere in seeking mutually acceptable terms for a lasting and sustainable political settlement.
We appreciate the efforts made by
him personally
and
by his representatives
.
•
The current Republican majority in both houses of Congress
did not prevent the adoption of the rather
damaging “Lindsey Graham Russia and Iran Sanctions Act
”.
•
There is no reason to expect positive changes in Russia-UK relations following the formation of a new government in London.
We are convinced that London has no interest in normalising relations.
•
Russia-Canada relations remain at a near-zero level.
This stems from the Canadian leadership’s irrational obsession with anti-Russian illusions and Ottawa’s commitment to supporting the neo-Nazi Kiev regime.
•
Canada’s history contains
many controversial chapters
involving
Nazi criminals and collaborators who settled in the country after 1945.
•
We remain open to restoring normal relations with Ireland
, despite its drift towards the anti-Russian consensus in Brussels and London.
