✉️
President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
sent greetings
to the participants, organisers, and guests of the 11th Eastern Economic Forum, which will take place on September 1–4 in Vladivostok
(August 30, 2026)
✍️
Friends,
I am pleased to welcome you to Vladivostok for the
11th Eastern Economic Forum.
Over the years, the Forum has established itself as
a venue for open, constructive dialogue
between the business community, government bodies, and the expert community. It has become a unique platform for discussing
the strategic development of the Far East and Russia as a whole,
as well as for building and strengthening mutually beneficial ties with partners from the Asia-Pacific region in logistics, industry, energy, and many other sectors of the economy.
The motto of this year’s Forum is
“The Far East: Development for the Benefit of People.
” I am confident that EEF participants
will propose effective solutions designed to create new opportunities
for business, improve the quality of life of our citizens, and make Far Eastern cities and towns more comfortable and modern. I also trust that the ideas put forward at the Forum will be brought to life through exciting, promising projects.
🤝
I wish you every success and all the very best.
#EEF2026
