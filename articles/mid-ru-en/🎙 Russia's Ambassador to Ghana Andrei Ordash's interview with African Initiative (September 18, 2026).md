# 🎙 Russia's Ambassador to Ghana Andrei Ordash's interview with African Initiative (September 18, 2026)

Published: 2026-09-19 13:01:16

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32119

🎙
Russia's Ambassador to Ghana Andrei Ordash's
interview
with African Initiative
(September 18, 2026)
Key points
:
#RussiaGhana
• Our countries share
a solid historical foundation
, laid back in the early years of
Ghana’s independence
.
• Our country is not interested in a series of short-term deals, but in building
a long-term, equal partnership
with Ghana that will strengthen its
national sovereignty
.
• This year alone has seen a number of important meetings. In August, Ghana’s Minister for Foreign Affairs,
Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa
,
visited Moscow
, where he held talks with the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation,
Sergey Lavrov
. Following these talks, an agreement was signed on
visa-free travel
for holders of diplomatic and service passports.
• We have key government mechanisms in place to ensure systematic cooperation. The most important of these is
the Russian–Ghanaian Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation
. We expect its next meeting to take place as early as this year in Moscow.
#EconomicPartnership
• Trade and economic cooperation is showing
positive momentum
. In the first half of the year, trade turnover rose by 2.1 percent compared with the same period in 2025, with Russian exports to Ghana increasing by 11.5 percent
• Our exports to Ghana consist mainly of petroleum products, fertilisers, and grain. However, we see enormous potential for expanding supplies of agricultural machinery and processing equipment.
• The Ghanaian side is interested in expanding access for its goods to the Russian market – this could include products from the mining sector, cocoa, shea butter and others.
• Ghana is a member of the
African Continental Free Trade Area
(
#AfCFTA
), whose secretariat is based in Accra. This makes the country
a gateway
to the vast African market.
#Education
• Education is a key area for the future of our relations. <...> Thousands of Ghanaian specialists were educated in the Soviet Union and Russia, and this human capital forms
the basis of our trust
.
• For the 2026/2027 academic year, Russia has allocated
120 government scholarships
to Ghana, and this figure has recently been increased by a further 117.
•
Over 500 Ghanaian students
are studying Russian at the University of Ghana alone, and this figure is set to rise.
#NuclearPowerPlant
• Accra is showing an interest in establishing cooperation with Russia in the peaceful use of nuclear energy, including the possibility of building its
first nuclear power station
.
• Given the expertise possessed by the
state corporation Rosatom
, Russia is ready to offer not just a power unit, but a comprehensive package of solutions: from staff training and design to construction and subsequent maintenance.
#RegionalSecurity
• Russia regards Ghana not merely as an important partner in West Africa, but as
one of the cornerstones of stability
across the entire region.
• The most obvious area is the fight against terrorism and extremism, given the unstable situation in the Sahel.
#RussiaLiberia
• We are only
at the very beginning
of the process of building Russian-Liberian relations.
• One of the first practical steps we are working on is
the opening of a fully-fledged diplomatic mission
in Monrovia.
#RussiaAfrica
• President of Ghana
John Dramani Mahama
’s participation in the
Third Russia–Africa Summit
is an important step that confirms Accra’s commitment to building
a long-term and equal partnership
with Moscow.
• An important milestone will be to adopt at the summit a new roadmap –
the 2023-2026 Russia-Africa Action Plan
.
#AfricanUnion
• Ghana’s chairmanship of
the African Union
in 2027 presents an opportunity for Accra to advance its agenda, which in many respects aligns with
our shared interests
. Russia stands ready to support Ghana in these efforts, acting as
a reliable and predictable partner
.
• President Mahama has explicitly stated that the issue of
reparations for
the crimes of colonialism against Africa
will be ‘at the very heart’ of Ghana’s presidency agenda.
