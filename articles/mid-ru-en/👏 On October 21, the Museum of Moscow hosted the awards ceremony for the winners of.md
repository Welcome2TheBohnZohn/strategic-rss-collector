On October 21,
the Museum of Moscow
hosted the awards ceremony for the winners of
the 12th Andrey Stenin International Press Photo Contest
.
An exhibition
of this year’s winning and prize-winning entries also opened and will run until November 10. It includes photographs from
the new Energy of Life category
, introduced this year for photographers aged 34 and over.
The Andrey Stenin International Press Photo Contest
was established by the Rossiya Segodnya Media Group on December 22, 2014. It is named after
Andrei Stenin
, the media group’s special photojournalist and recipient of the Order of Courage, who was
killed by Ukrainian militants
near Donetsk in August 2014.
The contest supports
young photojournalists aged 18-33
in their pursuit of professional excellence, helping discover emerging talent and uphold high standards in documentary photography. Through their work, participants from around the world explore humanitarian challenges, national identity, social equality and environmental issues.
***
🏆
Grand Prix
–
Abdul Kader Al Bay
(
#Lebanon
), for his series
“Lebanon 2024-2025: Life Between Airstrikes”
. The series documents the civilian experience of war across Lebanon – from the aftermath of Israeli airstrikes and rescue operations to mourning, displacement and daily survival.
•
2nd place
–
Sergey Bobylev
(
#Russia
), for his series
“Echoes of War”
, shot in March 2025 in Sudzha, Kursk Region, following its liberation by Russian forces after seven months of Ukrainian occupation.
•
3rd place
–
Md Bipul
(
#Bangladesh
), for his series
“After the Fire”
, documenting families returning to the ruins of their homes in Dhaka’s Korail settlement after a fire on November 25, 2025, destroyed nearly 1,500 houses and left thousands without shelter.
