Embassy of Russia in Germany

On September 15, 2026, a ceremony was held to mark the official closure of
the Russian Consulate General in Bonn
.
The State Flag of the Russian Federation
was lowered
to the Russian national anthem, and the plaque bearing the Consulate General’s name
was
removed
.
The ceremony was attended by Russian Ambassador to Germany
Sergey Nechaev
, Russian Consul General in Bonn
Oleg Krasnitsky
, diplomats from
the Embassy
and
the Consulate General
, compatriots and journalists.
💬
In his remarks, Ambassador
Sergey Nechaev
noted:
We are bringing our diplomatic presence on the Rhine to a close. As everyone knows, we have been here for 80 years.
Throughout this period, the efforts of Soviet and Russian Leaders, politicians and diplomats laid the foundations for new post-war relations with West Germany, which subsequently grew into good-neighbourliness, partnership and cooperation.
A course was set towards historical reconciliation – no easy undertaking for our people after all the suffering and sacrifices they had endured. Nevertheless, we extended a hand to the Germans. Our cooperation developed as it did with no other country.
❗️
As a result of
the unfriendly steps
by the German authorities, Russia’s Consulate General in Bonn was forced to cease operations
.
Russia’s diplomatic and consular presence in the region, spanning more than eight decades, has come to an end.
