✉️
Russia's President Vladimir Putin
sent greetings
to the participants, guests and organisers of
the International Collective Security Forum
(November 10, 2026)
✍️
I am pleased to welcome you to Moscow for the opening of
the International Collective Security Forum
, held as part of Russia’s chairmanship of
the Collective Security Treaty Organisation
(
#CSTO
).
The Forum has brought together representatives of government bodies, defence ministries and the defence industry, as well as scientists and experts. During the plenary sessions and roundtable discussions, you will
assess the impact of today’s challenges on the geopolitical situation in Eurasia
and
explore ways to strengthen regional security and stability
.
The CSTO Member States have gained
considerable experience
in working together constructively. At the same time, given the continuing international tensions, it is clear that
we must further strengthen the Organisation’s capabilities
and
work more closely together
to address the tasks before it.
👉
Our priorities include defence, countering terrorism and extremism, and combating cybercrime, drug trafficking and illegal migration.
I am confident that the Forum will take place in
a constructive atmosphere
and that its proposals and initiatives will find
practical application
for the benefit of the peoples of our countries.
🤝
I wish you every success and all the very best.
VLADIMIR PUTIN
