#Announcement
🗓
On November 24-25, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
will pay
a working visit
to Gomel, Belarus, to attend
the annual joint meeting of the boards of
the Russian Foreign Ministry
and
the Belarusian Foreign Ministry
.
The two boards have met annually since 2000 under
the Treaty on the Establishment of the Union State
, which the two countries signed in December 1999.
The boards will review the implementation of
the Programme of Coordinated Foreign Policy Action for 2024-2026
of the states parties to the Treaty on the Establishment of the Union State. Under the programme, Russia and Belarus
closely coordinate
their positions,
support
each other and
jointly advance
their shared interests on the international stage.
The boards will also discuss
how to align the two countries’ approaches to work at the
#UN
and across the UN system
, how their diplomatic services can help Russian and Belarusian regions strengthen interregional and cross-border cooperation, and how the two countries can respond jointly to crises and emergencies.
Following the meeting, the two Sides will sign
a new Programme of Coordinated Foreign Policy Action for 2027-2029
and submit it to the Supreme State Council of the Union State for approval. They will also sign
a joint resolution of the two boards
and
a plan for consultations
between the Foreign Ministries in 2027.
On the sidelines of the meeting,
Sergey Lavrov
will hold talks with Foreign Minister of Belarus
Maxim Ryzhenkov
on current issues in bilateral cooperation and on further integration within the Union State. The Ministers will also exchange views on key international issues.
Sergey Lavrov’s visit will include
a series of commemorative events
. Together with his Belarusian counterpart,
he will lay flowers at the Eternal Flame
in memory of those who defended Belarus during
the Great Patriotic War of 1941-1945
. He will also attend the unveiling of a bust of
Nikolai Rumyantsev
, Foreign Minister of the Russian Empire, who was buried 200 years ago in Gomel’s Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul. The commemorations will also honour Soviet Foreign Minister
Andrey Gromyko
, who was born 117 years ago in the Gomel district.
