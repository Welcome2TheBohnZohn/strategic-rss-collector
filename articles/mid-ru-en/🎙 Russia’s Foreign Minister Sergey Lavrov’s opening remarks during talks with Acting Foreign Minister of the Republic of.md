# 🎙 Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s opening remarks during talks with Acting Foreign Minister of the Republic of South Ossetia Akhsar Dzhioev

Published: 2026-10-01 13:30:00

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32506

🎙
Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
opening remarks
during talks with Acting Foreign Minister of the Republic of South Ossetia Akhsar Dzhioev
(Moscow, October 1, 2026)
💬
It has become a good tradition for us to meet shortly after
the commemorative dates
celebrated by our South Ossetian allies, including
the anniversary of Russia’s recognition of the Republic of South Ossetia’s independence
on August 26, the anniversary of
the establishment of diplomatic relations
on September 9 and, of course,
South Ossetia’s national holiday – Republic Day on September 20
. Congratulations on all these occasions.
Today, we have a good opportunity to discuss
the current state and prospects of our bilateral relations
following another event that also took place in September. On September 18, your country held
an early presidential election
, won by Acting Head of State and Prime Minister
Marat Kambolov
, who took office on September 30.
A few days before his inauguration, on September 19, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
spoke with him over the telephone
. We share the assessment of the many international observers that
the election was legitimate and free of any violations
.
We will continue working closely with President
Marat Kambolov’s
Government to deepen our allied relations.
📈
Our relations are developing positively. Bilateral trade grew by almost 15% in 2025
.
South Ossetia’s State Programme for Socioeconomic Development
is being implemented with support from the Moscow Government. New agreements have been reached on cooperation in modernising transport infrastructure, including the Nizhny Zaramag checkpoint on the Russia-South Ossetia border, and on healthcare, education and cultural projects.
All of this helps strengthen humanitarian ties
, which the peoples of Russia and South Ossetia are keen to develop.
We welcome your country’s openness to cooperation with
all international partners willing to engage constructively
. Representatives of Belarus, Nicaragua, Venezuela, Mexico, Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, South Africa, Türkiye and Germany took part in observing South Ossetia’s presidential election.
My prepared remarks say
“even Germany”
, but the explanation is clear:
in all these countries
, as in others,
there are public figures and non-governmental organisations that have no interest in bringing ideology into international relations
. They want to develop good-neighbourly ties – and broader ties beyond their immediate neighbours – with countries that share this interest.
We understand and welcome this.
Of course, the participation of representatives from so many countries shows that
the Republic of South Ossetia is known internationally
. We will continue to provide
diplomatic support
to help your country translate this recognition and visibility into concrete economic and humanitarian projects involving a wider range of foreign partners.
🤝
Today, following
the telephone conversation between our Presidents
,
we will review our bilateral relations
. We will also take stock of developments on the external front, which I have already touched upon. Then, together with
Abkhazia’s Foreign Minister Oleg Bartsits
, we will discuss matters related to
the International Discussions on Security and Stability in the South Caucasus
, which concern the interests of all three of our countries.
