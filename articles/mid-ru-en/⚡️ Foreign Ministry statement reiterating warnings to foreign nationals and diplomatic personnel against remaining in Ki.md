# ⚡️ Foreign Ministry statement reiterating warnings to foreign nationals and diplomatic personnel against remaining in Kiev or near military facilities in Ukraine

Published: 2026-10-03 12:15:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32545

⚡️
Foreign Ministry statement reiterating warnings to foreign nationals and diplomatic personnel against remaining in Kiev or near military facilities in Ukraine
(October 3 , 2026)
The Russian Foreign Ministry
reiterates that,
in response to terrorist attacks by the Armed Forces of Ukraine against Russia’s civilian population
and infrastructure,
the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
continue to carry out systematic and sustained strikes against military targets in Kiev
and other Ukrainian cities.
The Russian side
has previously called on foreign nationals and diplomatic personnel located in Kiev to leave the city
and refrain from travelling to Ukraine. Russian authorities have also urged Ukrainian residents to stay away from military and administrative facilities.
These warnings were conveyed in the
statement
issued by the Russian Foreign Ministry on May 25, 2026, as well as through
diplomatic notes
addressed to foreign partners. They were also reiterated by Deputy Foreign Minister
Mikhail Galuzin
during a briefing for members of the diplomatic corps on May 27, 2026.
The authorities of a number of countries, including
the United States, the United Kingdom, European Union member states, Norway, and Switzerland
, have repeatedly advised their citizens against travelling to Ukraine, most recently in August of this year.
We note with concern that
not everyone has taken these warnings into account and that some individuals are knowingly placing themselves in mortal danger
. A number of foreign embassies continue to operate in Kiev, and foreign officials continue to visit Ukraine.
The Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
will continue to carry out large-scale retaliatory strikes.
❗️
The Russian Foreign Ministry once again urges foreign nationals, including personnel of diplomatic missions, to refrain from remaining in or travelling to Ukraine.
Responsibility for any possible adverse consequences
lies entirely with those who choose to disregard this warning
.
