Remarks and answers to media questions
by Russia's Minister of Foreign Affairs Sergey Lavrov during a joint press conference with Minister of Foreign Affairs of Saudi Arabia Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud
(Moscow, September 8, 2026)
Key points
:
🇷🇺
🇸🇦
#RussiaSaudiArabia
: We discussed in detail the key aspects of trade and economic cooperation.
We are pleased that
bilateral trade increased significantly in 2025, by almost 40%
. We share an interest in doing everything possible to sustain this momentum.
•
We reaffirmed our commitment to continued close coordination within OPEC+
in order to maintain a balance between supply and demand on the global energy market. This is particularly important under the current circumstances. As co-chairs of this group of countries, Russia and Saudi Arabia will consistently pursue coordinated approaches.
•
There are good prospects for cooperation in tourism
. Saudi tourists enjoy visiting Russia. In 2025, their number
increased by 35%
compared with the previous year. The number of Russian tourists visiting Saudi Arabia is growing at a similar pace. This has been facilitated by
the Agreement on visa-free travel for our citizens
, which entered into force in May 2026.
•
We discussed the regional agenda, with particular attention to the situation in the Persian Gulf
. We in Russia regret that the countries of the Arabian Peninsula have been drawn, through no choice of their own, into
the military confrontation between the US and Israel on the one hand and Iran on the other
. Together with our Arab partners, we believe that all emerging differences must be resolved
exclusively through
political and diplomatic means
.
•
We paid special attention to the situation in the Palestinian territories, above all in the Gaza Strip
. Unfortunately, despite the existence of an agreement presented as a “plan” by US President Donald Trump, there has been no progress. Both Moscow and Riyadh proceed from the understanding that only
a comprehensive and all-encompassing settlement of
the Palestinian issue
can guarantee lasting peace for all peoples of the region.
•
We noted the importance of ensuring the security and freedom of commercial navigation in all parts of the World Ocean
, including waters adjacent to Yemen.
•
We see the path towards sustainable normalisation in Yemen in the earliest possible launch of direct dialogue among all influential Yemeni political forces
, possibly under UN auspices. At the same time, it is essential to preserve the foundation provided by the “road map” developed at Saudi Arabia’s initiative and agreed with both the internationally recognised authorities of Yemen and representatives of the Ansar Allah movement.
