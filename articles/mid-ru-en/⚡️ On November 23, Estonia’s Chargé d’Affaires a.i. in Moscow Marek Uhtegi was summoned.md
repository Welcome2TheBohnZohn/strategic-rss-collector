# ⚡️ On November 23, Estonia’s Chargé d’Affaires a.i. in Moscow Marek Uhtegi was summoned

Published: 2026-11-23 15:00:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33563

⚡️
On November 23, Estonia’s Chargé d’Affaires a.i. in Moscow
Marek Uhtegi
was summoned
to the Russian Foreign Ministry, where he was
issued a strong protest over Estonia’s unfounded accusations against Russia
in connection with a failed arson attack on a defence industry facility near Tallinn and the Estonian government’s
subsequent decision to close border crossing points
on the Russian-Estonian border from December 1.
The Estonian diplomat was told that such hasty accusations,
entirely unsupported by evidence
, once again
expose the Estonian regime’s deeply Russophobic nature
.
Tallinn appears to have given
little serious thought
to the consequences of this move. Closing the state border will primarily
deal another blow to the Estonian economy and backfire on Tallinn itself
, as well as its EU and NATO allies.
This latest anti-Russian stunt by Estonia adds to the long list of
hostile actions
undertaken by this Baltic state over the years.
Estonia continues to actively support the Kiev regime,
covertly allowing Ukrainian drones to use its airspace to carry out attacks on northwestern Russia
, something the Russian Side has repeatedly documented with objective evidence.
☝️
The Estonian diplomat was once again warned that such provocative and extremely dangerous actions are
on the brink of triggering an open war with Russia.
Estonia’s hostile actions do not end there.
Tallinn is attempting to obstruct the movement of Russian cargo vessels in the Baltic Sea
, seeking to inspect, seize and detain them with the aim of imposing a naval blockade on our country.
In October, Estonia introduced
a complete ban on the transit of Russian and Belarusian grain
through its territory.
Estonia is stepping up
repressive measures against Russian-speaking and dissenting residents
who refuse to blindly follow the authorities’ official narratives aimed at comprehensively vilifying Russia.
To resolve the so-called
"Russian question"
, Tallinn has resorted not only to
banning the Russian language at all levels
, but also to criminal prosecution and deportation of those who sympathise with our country and seek to preserve their right to access their native language and culture.
In pursuing their police-state methods, the authorities do not even shy away from
coercive measures and threats to dissolve the Estonian Orthodox Christian Church
altogether.
The head of the Estonian diplomatic mission was informed that this unjustified policy of
fostering all-encompassing hostility towards our country
will continue to destabilise the situation in the region and adversely affect
both the well-being of Estonia’s residents
and
the already dire economic situation in this Baltic state
.
❗️
During the meeting, it was emphasised that
Russia reserves the right to take a comprehensive range of political and diplomatic measures
to protect its national interests and the legitimate rights of Russian citizens.
