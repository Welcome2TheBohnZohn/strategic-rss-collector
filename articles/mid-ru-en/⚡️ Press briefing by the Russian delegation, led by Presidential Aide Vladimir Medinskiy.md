# ⚡️ Press briefing by the Russian delegation, led by Presidential Aide Vladimir Medinskiy

Published: 2026-11-11 20:00:02

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33320

⚡️
Press briefing by the Russian delegation, led by Presidential Aide
Vladimir Medinskiy
, following
the talks in Abu Dhabi
(November 11, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Medinskiy:
First of all, on behalf of the entire Russian delegation,
I would like to thank the leadership of the UAE and Abu Dhabi
for the excellent organisation of the trilateral talks and for creating a friendly, businesslike atmosphere.
***
Answers to media questions:
❓
Question: How did the discussion on shaping Europe’s future security architecture proceed?
💬
Vladimir Medinskiy:
We believe it is right to raise the question of what will happen after the inevitable end of the conflict in Ukraine.
❗️
Russia
has repeatedly raised
the issue of European security architecture and the need to establish a new, comprehensive and fair security system in Europe.
We did so
in 2008
, when we proposed
the relevant draft European Security Treaty
, and
in 2021
, when we sent the relevant documents to NATO, the US and European countries.
But, as the saying goes, it is easier said than done.
Many years have passed, and this issue has finally gained attention,
with a growing recognition that it must be addressed
.
The conflict in Ukraine has shown that
the entire European security system in place since
Yalta
and
Potsdam
functions very poorly
. In essence, the entire body is diseased, and Ukraine is simply where
the abscess has burst
.
With the US Side participating, we exchanged views. We found common ground on some points, but not on others.
This discussion will certainly continue.
☝️
The world needs a new, modern, renewed security system
that is fair and does not come at the expense of individual countries. We will work towards that.
❓
Question: EU representatives were also in Abu Dhabi. Did you speak to them, and are any meetings with them planned?
💬
Vladimir Medinskiy:
We did not speak to them.
We are not quite sure in what capacity they are here
. The talks are being held in the agreed trilateral format.
As for the stance taken by the leadership of a number of European countries, they are, in essence,
collectively deceiving the Ukrainian people
. They finance and supply weapons to one of the warring parties.
In effect, they are contributing to civilian deaths on a massive scale.
We are not quite sure what there is to discuss with them
. We have not received a single peace initiative or a single concrete peace proposal from European countries. Therefore, it is unclear what there is to discuss.
They would do well to learn
, for example,
from the American envoys
when it comes to
common sense, realism and a desire to achieve peace
.
❓
Question: Were humanitarian issues raised during the meeting? In previous rounds held in Turkiye and Switzerland, you managed to reach agreements with the Ukrainians on POW exchanges, for example. Was such an agreement reached in Abu Dhabi?
💬
Vladimir Medinskiy:
We have agreed in principle
on
a new large-scale prisoner exchange
. The specific details will be announced shortly.
❓
Question: How do you assess the role of Steven Witkoff and Jared Kushner, and the Trump administration more broadly, in the rounds of talks? What role do you believe they can play in reaching agreements?
💬
Vladimir Medinskiy:
The US Side played
an active role
in the talks. The role of individuals in history matters here. President Donald Trump’s envoys are certainly
sincere, effective people
and, most importantly,
focused on results
, rather than empty talk.
What matters is not how we assess them, but
how many lives will be saved
through their active mediation efforts.
We are therefore
very grateful
to them as well.
❓
Question: Under what conditions could the Europeans become full participants in the talks? What conditions must they meet to be admitted to the negotiating table?
💬
Vladimir Medinskiy:
I think
as soon as their conscience awakens
, they can become participants in peace talks.
Until then, as I have said,
they are taking part in the collective deception of the Ukrainian people
and are, in effect, complicit in
civilian deaths
.
