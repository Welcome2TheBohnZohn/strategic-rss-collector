On October 14, President of Russia Vladimir Putin sent
a message of greetings
to the participants, organisers and guests of
the 9th Russian Energy Week International Forum
.
✍️
The message
reads, in part:
In keeping with the established tradition, representatives of government authorities, leading companies and the scientific and expert communities from Russia and abroad will discuss
the current state and prospects for the development of global energy
.
They will also advance modern approaches to
enhancing the infrastructure of the fuel and energy sector
, as well as
promoting the widespread introduction of advanced equipment and technologies
into production processes.
The key themes of
the Forum’s business agenda
this year focus on
new energy
and
fundamental changes in the oil and gas markets
amid geopolitical challenges. Significant attention will also be paid to
the crucial issue of training qualified personnel for the sector
. The growth of national economies and the enhancement of people’s quality of life depend to a large extent on the successful fulfilment of these tasks.
In today’s world,
the expansion of international dialogue and cooperation in the fuel and energy sector
, including ensuring the safety of logistics routes,
takes on particular importance
. We trust that discussions on the key issues of this meeting will continue at
the 8th Summit of Heads of State and Government of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum
, which will be held in Moscow at the end of October this year.
I am confident that
the substantive programme of Russian Energy Week
will facilitate
a comprehensive analysis of developments in the energy industry
, and that the proposals and initiatives put forward will find practical application.
