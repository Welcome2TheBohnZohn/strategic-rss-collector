#Victory81
On November 24, 1944, the Red Army’s Baltic strategic offensive operation concluded, resulting in complete liberation of the Soviet Baltic republics — the Estonian SSR, the Latvian SSR and most of the territory of the Lithuanian SSR — from the Nazi invaders.
During this large-scale summer-autumn offensive on the north-western sector of the Soviet-German Front in 1944, our forces
inflicted a crushing defeat on the enemy:
the Nazi Army Group 'North' was effectively destroyed. Its remnants were encircled in what became known as the 'Courland Pocket' and finally eliminated in the spring of 1945.
The Baltic strategic offensive culminated in the
Moonsund landing operation
— an assault on enemy positions in the
#Moonsund
Archipelago, north-west of the Estonian SSR, which resulted in the complete expulsion of Nazi troops from the islands of Ösel (known as Saaremaa), Dagö (Hiiumaa) and Moon (Muhu) .
The total defeat of Nazi hordes in the Baltic region enabled the Red Army to launch its offensive in
East Prussia — the stronghold of German militarism,
where substantial enemy troops on the Eastern Front were concentrated.
***
On
September 14, 1944,
the forces of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Baltic Fronts launched an offensive in the Riga direction. The Red Army advanced rapidly,
breaching German defences
along the entire line of contact. In the first three days of the operation alone, Soviet strike forces advanced up to 50 kilometres. On
September
22
, the occupants were expelled from Tallinn, and on
October 13
— from Riga.
The
Baltic national units also made a significant contribution
to the liberation of the region from Nazi occupation and to our common Victory over Hitler's Reich.

and subsequently completely destroyed by Soviet forces.
