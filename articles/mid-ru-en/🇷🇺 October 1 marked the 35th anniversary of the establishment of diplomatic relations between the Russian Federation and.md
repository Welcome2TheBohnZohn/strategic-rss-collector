October 1
marked
the 35th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between the Russian Federation and
#BruneiDarussalam
.
✉️
To commemorate this anniversary, the two countries’ Heads of State and Foreign Ministers
exchanged congratulatory messages
.
In his message,
Sergey Lavrov
noted, in particular, that the development of the multifaceted Russia-Brunei partnership
serves the long-term interests of both countries
and contributes to strengthening peace and stability in the Asia-Pacific region and the world as a whole.
Today, Russia and Brunei maintain a constructive political dialogue
. In June, President of the Russian Federation
Vladimir Putin
and Sultan of Brunei Darussalam
Hassanal Bolkiah
held a bilateral meeting
on the sidelines of
the anniversary Russia-ASEAN Summit in Kazan
.
Cooperation between the two countries’ Foreign Ministries is well established. In March, Brunei’s Second Minister of Foreign Affairs
Erywan Yusof
visited Moscow.
In September, Russia’s Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
and Foreign Minister of Brunei Darussalam Prince
Abdul Mateen
held a meeting
on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the #UNGA81
.
Security ties are also developing rapidly
. In August, Secretary of the Security Council of the Russian Federation
Sergey Shoigu
held consultations
in Moscow with Brunei Darussalam’s Coordinating Minister for National Security, Minister at the Prime Minister’s Office and Second Minister of Defence
Halbi Yussof
.
The two countries also
cooperate effectively in multilateral forum
s, primarily at the
#UN
and within the
#RussiaASEAN
dialogue partnership.
🤝
Russia and Brunei remain committed to peace, stability and prosperity in the Asia-Pacific region and globally
, and share a determination to further strengthen their multifaceted cooperation.
