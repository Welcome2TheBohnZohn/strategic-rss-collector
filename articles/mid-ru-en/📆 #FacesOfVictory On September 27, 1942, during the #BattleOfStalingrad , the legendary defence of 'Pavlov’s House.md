In the autumn of 1942,
amid fierce defensive battles for Stalingrad,
a reconnaissance group led by Guards Sergeant Yakov Pavlov reached a four-storey residential building on Lenin Square in the city centre, near the right bank of the Volga, during the night of September 27.
The building seized by Pavlov’s men was, in fact,
the forward defence point
on the entire front.
It was an ordinary residential building made of just brick and timber and was hardly suitable for establishing a stronghold capable of holding back the Nazi hordes.
The group of Sergeant Yakov Pavlov, which was being pushed back towards the river by the enemy,
had to make a stand in that building
, drawing Wehrmacht units advancing towards the Volga into the fighting and
buying time to prepare the Red Army’s counteroffensive near Stalingrad.
Within just days, Pavlov’s men
managed to equip the building for long-lasting defence.
When the Germans launched their offensive, the 'Pavlov’s House' defenders —
around just 30 (!) soldiers vs. hordes of well-equiped and armed Nazis
— mounted a determined all-round defence, moving between the floors depending on the direction of the enemy attacks.
In response, the Germans unleashed the firepower of its artillery and tank armada on 'Pavlov’s House'.
The enemy subjected the building to
direct-fire bombardment
from effectively all sides simultaneously.
It looked like Pavlov’s men were doomed.
🌟
Yet, despite the increasingly intense fire of the Nazi artillery, the defenders of the 'Pavlov's House took
#NoStepBack
!
They heroically held their positions for almost
two months
(!), steadfastly repelling endless enemy attacks and preventing the Germans from breaking through to the Volga.
#OurHeroes

After the Great Patriotic War, 'Pavlov’s House' was rebuilt. Today, this memorial serves as a
symbol of the heroism of the defenders of Stalingrad.
🎖
The Heroes of 'Pavlov’s House' were duly honoured by their Motherland.
Yakov Pavlov was awarded the Star of
#HeroOfTheSovietUnion
,
while his other soldiers who fought for the legendary House received the Orders of the Red Star,
the Orders of the Patriotic War, 1st and 2nd Class,
as well as the Medal “For the Defence of Stalingrad”.
