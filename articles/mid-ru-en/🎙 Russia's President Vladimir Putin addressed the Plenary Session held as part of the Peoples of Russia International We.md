# 🎙 Russia's President Vladimir Putin addressed the Plenary Session held as part of the Peoples of Russia International Week

Published: 2026-11-04 22:33:48

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33232

🎙
Russia's President Vladimir Putin
addressed
the Plenary Session held as part of the Peoples of Russia International Week
(November 4, 2026)
💬
I would like to welcome the participants in
the Peoples of Russia International Week
and congratulate you and all citizens of the country on
National Unity Day
.
This holiday symbolises our sincere, deep love for the Motherland, our duty to the Fatherland for the fate of our people, for the fate of the Motherland.
We proudly turn to the origins of this holiday, when people of different ethnicities and faiths put
an end to civil strife and internal turmoil
, restored statehood and legitimate authority. <...>
Today, centuries later, we are even more acutely aware of the values and traditions passed on to us by our ancestors, and we feel that
we need to cherish the living spiritual heritage
.
Special attention has always been paid to interethnic relations in Russia
, as this area is very sensitive and delicate, and it must be borne in mind that life does not stand still, and new, often
extremely dangerous challenges are emerging
.
We must stay alert,
prevent any attempts to undermine social stability
, incite discord between people, and remember that harmony, mutual respect between the peoples of Russia, and their unity are
the mainstay of our country’s sovereignty and independence
, while caring about the preservation of culture, customs, and languages of each people is the foundation of our national policy.
***
🇷🇺
Historically, Russia has developed through the interaction of peoples and ethnic groups, languages and customs. We have not just rallied over the centuries and epochs. It is no exaggeration to say that
our cultures have literally grown into each other
. This kinship, the soul of the people, is reflected in our thousand-year-old history, in the military chronicle of the Fatherland, and in its great achievements.
And here is what I consider important to note: our ancestors, compatriots, people of various ethnicities
became known throughout the world
primarily as great Russian trail-blazers, workers, warriors, artists, scientists, and inventors. And the indigenous art of all the peoples of our country is also Russian for the whole world.
Let me stress once again:
the Russian language and the Russian people have been and remain the foundation of the Russian statehood
. With its unifying, creative mission, it is the guarantor of the preservation and progress of all other peoples of the Russian Federation, the preservation of their freedom and identity in harmony with unity.
Therefore, support of the Russian culture, Russian language is strategically important
. We have a big country, and I would like to reiterate once again: this diversity of cultures, traditions, and customs is
our strength, a huge competitive advantage, and a potential
. We must constantly strengthen it, protect the multinational harmony which our common heritage.
Friends,
The powerful, all-conquering spirit of unity of the peoples of Russia has been passed down from generation to generation
. We are still standing shoulder to shoulder and we are confident that we will achieve our goals.
Representatives of
more than a hundred peoples of Russia
have been awarded high state decorations for their bravery and heroism displayed on the fronts of the special military operation.
Once again, I wish you all a happy holiday. I wish all participants and guests of the Peoples of Russia International Week continued joint and fruitful work for the benefit of the peoples of our Motherland.
🎉
Thank you very much. Happy holiday!
