✉️
President of Russia Vladimir Putin
sent greetings
to participants, organisers and guests of
the 12th St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
(September 24, 2024)
✍️
Over the years,
the Forum
has earned considerable prestige, bringing together prominent writers, artists, creative professionals and experts from many countries around its objectives. Today, it is rightly regarded as
a major event in the cultural life of Russia and the world
.
The theme of this year’s Forum,
“United Cultures – Shared Values”
, holds particular significance.
Culture has always provided a solid foundation for mutual understanding between people and nations
, helping to preserve historical memory and spiritual and moral values and pass them down from generation to generation. Respect for one another’s distinctive identities and traditions, together with a commitment to open dialogue on an equal footing, enables us
to find common ground even in the most difficult circumstances.
I am confident that
the 12th St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
will take place in
a constructive and creative atmosphere
, helping to strengthen international humanitarian cooperation and launch promising new joint projects.
And, of course,
the extensive business and cultural programme
will give the Forum’s participants and guests vivid and unforgettable memories of their stay in
St Petersburg
– a city with a unique historical and cultural heritage
.
🤝
I wish you productive discussions, success and all the very best.
VLADIMIR PUTIN
