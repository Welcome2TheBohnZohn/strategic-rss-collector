September 20 – voting in elections to
Russia’s State Duma
begins at Russian diplomatic missions around the world, with polling stations opening at 8:00 am local time.
As the day moves across time zones, our diplomats in many countries are already welcoming voters, while their colleagues elsewhere are opening the doors to polling stations for
Russian citizens eager to exercise their constitutional right

Polls close at 8:00 pm local time.
Votes will then be counted and the results transmitted to
Russia’s Central Election Commission
.
***
Voting abroad to
the 9th convocation of the State Duma of the Russian Federation
is taking place on September 18-20, 2026, at Russian embassies and consulates general in 152 countries. A total of 326 polling stations have been set up.
Information on the locations and opening hours of polling stations is best available on the
digital platforms of Russian diplomatic missions
.
