# ✍️ On October 12, 2026, President of Russia Vladimir Putin signed Decree No. 730 on temporary entry and exit procedures for citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam

Published: 2026-10-13 12:00:48

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32722

✍️
On October 12, 2026, President of Russia Vladimir Putin
signed Decree No. 730
on temporary entry and exit procedures for citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam
.
🇷🇺
🇻🇳
The Decree introduces
visa-free travel to Russia for Vietnamese citizens holding ordinary passports
, allowing
stays of up to 30 days
for private and business visits, tourism and participation in scientific, cultural, socio-political, economic and sporting events. It also permits visa-free transit.
The Decree will remain in place
through October 15, 2027, inclusive
. It does not apply to entry for employment, including journalism, education or residence.
📄
The Decree
*
reads:
Until October 15, 2027, inclusive, citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam shall be entitled to enter the Russian Federation and stay in the Russian Federation for no more than 30 days for private or business visits, tourism, or participation in scientific, cultural, socio-political, economic and sporting events, as well as to transit through the territory of the Russian Federation and leave the Russian Federation without visas, using an ordinary passport issued by the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
The entry and exit procedures established by this Decree shall not apply to citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam entering the Russian Federation for employment, including journalistic activities, education or residence in the Russian Federation.
The conditions governing the stay in the Russian Federation of citizens of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam entering under the procedures established by this Decree shall be determined by the legislation of the Russian Federation.
*Unofficial translation
❗️
The Decree
enters into force on October 15, 2026
.
