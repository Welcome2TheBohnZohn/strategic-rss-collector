# 🗓 #Announcement The St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum will take place on September 24-26, 2026.

Published: 2026-09-23 20:45:10

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32210

#Announcement
🗓
The St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
will take place on September 24-26, 2026.
Held since 2012, the Forum has established itself as
a unique platform for cooperation in culture and the arts,
as well as for open and substantive discussions of issues arising as the contemporary cultural landscape evolves.
The Forum
will be one of the key events of
the Year of Unity of the Peoples of Russia
, proclaimed by
Presidential Executive Order No. 962 of December 25, 2025
.
The event
is expected to bring together representatives of
70 countries
, with
44 official foreign delegations
confirmed, around
2,500 delegates
set to attend the main programme and nearly
1,500 participants
already registered for the Forum’s first-ever youth programme.
💬
Russia's Deputy Prime Minister, Chair of the Forum’s organising committee
Tatyana Golikova
said to TASS Agency in the run-up
to the Forum
:
We can see that attempts to cancel Russian culture and isolate our country have clearly failed.
The St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum reflects Russia’s unifying role as a spiritual and cultural guide and as a champion of traditional values.
