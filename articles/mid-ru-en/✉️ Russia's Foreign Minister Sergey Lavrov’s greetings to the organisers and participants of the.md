✉️
Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s
greetings
to the organisers and participants of the
6th ASEAN-Russia Youth Summit
(October 22, 2026)
✍️
I extend my warmest greetings to the organisers and participants of the
6th ASEAN-Russia Youth Summit,
which, following a lengthy hiatus, is
hosted by the hospitable and beautiful city of
#Vladivostok
— Russia’s Pacific outpost with a rich history and a major centre of education, science and culture.
In 2026, we mark the
35th Anniversary of relations between Russia and ASEAN
, which have rightfully attained the high status of
#StrategicPartnership
. Our wide-ranging and robust ties bring
substantial benefits to socio-economic development
and serve as an important factor in maintaining regional stability.
The mutual commitment to
strengthening multifaceted cooperation
was clearly demonstrated at the commemorative
ASEAN-Russia Summit held in Kazan this June.
The Leaders devoted particular attention to creating favourable conditions to facilitate and intensify contacts among young people.
The energy of the youth, their openness to innovation and progress are essential to
ensuring progressive development
of our countries and preserving an atmosphere of friendship and trust between our peoples.
The implementation of the
Summit’s comprehensive agenda
will also contribute to achieving these important objectives.
It is encouraging that the programme, alongside thematic discussions and round tables, features an extensive cultural component.
🤝
I wish you all fruitful work, positive impressions of your stay in the Russian land of
#Primorye
and every success.
SERGEY LAVROV
#RussiaASEAN
