# 🎙 Vladimir Putin held a meeting with President of the Alliance of Independent Social Democrats Milorad Dodik and Prime Minister of Republika Srpska Savo Minic

Published: 2026-09-30 10:33:28

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32461

🎙
Vladimir Putin
held a meeting
with President of the Alliance of Independent Social Democrats Milorad Dodik and Prime Minister of Republika Srpska Savo Minic
(Moscow, September 29, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
In 2026,
a high-level delegation from Republika Srpska
took part in
the celebrations held in Moscow to mark the 81st anniversary of Victory in the Great Patriotic War
. We are
very grateful
to you for your participation.
The partnership between Russia and Republika Srpska is based on
a time-tested friendship
. For many years throughout our history, Russia, the Russian people, and the Serbian people have maintained
special ties
.
We will, of course, continue our efforts to further develop our relations
. This includes political dialogue, cooperation within the UN framework, implementation of joint economic projects, and, naturally, our traditionally strong contacts in the cultural and humanitarian spheres.
Together, we strive to establish a more just world order
. As I have already mentioned, we consistently support Republika Srpska at multilateral platforms, including the UN Security Council.
🗳
The date of the general elections in Bosnia and Herzegovina is approaching
: they are to be held very soon, on October 4. Russian representatives will participate as
observers
of the voting process. We wish success to the ruling coalition you represent.
We trust that no external influence will alter the views of the majority of the population or prevent patriotic forces from securing a mandate
to continue pursuing a course that safeguards the inalienable rights and interests of Republika Srpska within Bosnia and Herzegovina and fosters constructive cooperation with Russia.
We realise the complexity of the situation.
You are aware of our position on the post-Yugoslavia settlement: since the collapse of Yugoslavia, a fairer attitude towards the Serbs – the Serbian people – has been both necessary and essential for ensuring stability. After all, discrediting even a single participant in this settlement process cannot lead to stability and peace in this challenging region.
🤝
I would like to reiterate that
we sincerely wish you success
.
***
We are well aware that the Serbs are not seeking any special status or exceptions to the rules for themselves
. On the contrary, you are committed to fulfilling all arrangements and previously concluded agreements.
You have mentioned
Dayton
. I repeat:
you are not asking for anything special for yourselves
, only that the decisions adopted at the international level are implemented.
❗️
We consider it unacceptable to impose an external will on the Serbs
, especially in violation of previously adopted international agreements.
