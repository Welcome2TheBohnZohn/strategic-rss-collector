Statement by the Delegation of the Russian Federation under Agenda Item 3 of the Technical Assistance and Cooperation Committee of the IAEA Board of Governors
“Evaluation of Technical Cooperation Activities in 2026”
📍
Vienna, November 16, 2026
💬
We have reviewed
the report of the Office of Internal Oversight Services on the evaluation of technical cooperation (TC) activities in 2026
and have no objection to taking note of it.
As a matter of principle, we support the work of OIOS, which performs an important function by
providing an assessment of the actual state of affairs across various areas of the Agency’s activities
. This is particularly valuable when the Office succeeds in identifying systemic or localised problems, as their timely detection makes it possible to take corrective action and strengthen the Agency’s performance.
In this regard, we note that,
for the third consecutive year, the Office has highlighted significant shortcomings in the planning, implementation and reporting of TC projects, as well as in ensuring the sustainability of their results
. Disruptions in equipment deliveries and other related issues have also been identified. The report rightly points out that these problems have an adverse impact on the efficiency, effectiveness and sustainability of the assistance provided by the Agency.
We urge the Secretariat to take a more responsible approach to the management of the TC Programme
, which continues to serve as the Agency’s principal mechanism for promoting nuclear energy, science and technology, as well as ensuring unhindered access to their benefits for all interested countries. Every effort should be made to address the identified shortcomings as soon as possible. At the same time, it is important to develop a systemic solution that would ensure that these problems are addressed on a long-term basis.
As regards the Office's work plan for next year,
we consider it appropriate to conduct an evaluation of the Agency’s assistance to Palestine
, bearing in mind the need to implement the resolution adopted by the 69th session of the IAEA General Conference entitled “Extension of the Technical Cooperation Programme to the Gaza Strip in the State of Palestine”.
