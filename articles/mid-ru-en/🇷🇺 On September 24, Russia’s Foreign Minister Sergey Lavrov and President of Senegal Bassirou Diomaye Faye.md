On September 24, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and President of Senegal Bassirou Diomaye Faye
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks
The discussion focused on further strengthening
traditionally friendly Russia–Senegal relations
, with an emphasis on expanding cooperation in trade, the economy, investment and humanitarian affairs, as well as enhancing the legal framework for bilateral cooperation.
The Sides exchanged views on key international and regional issues, including in the context of
Senegal’s current chairmanship of
the Economic Community of West African States
(
#ECOWAS
).
They reaffirmed that Moscow and Dakar
share similar principled approaches to most global challenges
and
agreed to continue coordinating their efforts
at the UN and other multilateral platforms.
