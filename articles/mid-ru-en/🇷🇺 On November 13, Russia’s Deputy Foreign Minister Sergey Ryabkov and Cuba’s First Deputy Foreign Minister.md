On November 13, Russia’s Deputy Foreign Minister
Sergey Ryabkov
and Cuba’s First Deputy Foreign Minister
Gerardo Penalver
had a telephone conversation
at the Cuban Side’s initiative.
They discussed current
bilateral cooperation issues
.
Sergey Ryabkov reaffirmed Russia’s principled position that
economic and military pressure on Cuba is unacceptable and that
the US trade, fuel and financial blockade
of the island must end immediately
.
🤝
He expressed
unwavering solidarity with and support
for the leadership and people of the fraternal country in these difficult times, and reiterated
Russia’s readiness to continue providing Havana with the necessary political and material assistance.
