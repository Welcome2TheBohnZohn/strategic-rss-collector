On September 25, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Guatemala Carlos Ramiro Martinez
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Foreign Ministers expressed their commitment to
developing Russia-Guatemala ties in bilateral and multilateral formats
and reaffirmed their interest in
deepening dialogue between Russia and regional integration associations in Latin America and the Caribbean
.
