Energy security is not a new topic in Russian–Thai dialogue. We collaborated before all these geopolitical turbulences. #EAEU

Thailand is not only interested in and ready to sign such an agreement — it has also conducted a study of the potential benefits to the Thai economy if such an agreement were signed. Of course, for the EAEU, it is a matter of consensus. Russia's position is quite
positive
and
very constructive
. We therefore also recommend that our Thai colleagues discuss this matter with the other members of the Eurasian Economic Union.

Thailand is interested in increasing cooperation with Russia
