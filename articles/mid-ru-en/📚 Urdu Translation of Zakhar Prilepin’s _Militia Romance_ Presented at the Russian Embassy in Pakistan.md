Urdu Translation of Zakhar Prilepin’s "Militia Romance" Presented at the Russian Embassy in Pakistan
On November 17, the Embassy of Russia in Pakistan hosted the presentation of the Urdu translation of "Militia Romance", a collection of short stories by renowned Russian writer Zakhar Prilepin.
🤝
The event was attended by
Ambassador Albert P. Khorev
; Federal Minister of National Heritage and Culture
Aurangzeb Khan Khichi
; MNA and Parliamentary Secretary
Farah Naz Akbar
; Director General of the National Language Promotion Department
Dr. Muhammad Saleem Mazhar
, as well as Ambassadors of friendly countries and representatives of Pakistan’s intellectual community.
🎥
In a video address to the participants, the author,
Zakhar Prilepin
, expressed his gratitude to the Russian Embassy and Pakistani colleagues for their assistance in translating and publishing the book in Urdu.
💬
In his opening remarks,
Ambassador Albert P. Khorev
spoke about Zakhar Prilepin’s life and literary career. He emphasized the importance of the book for
understanding the root causes of the conflict in Ukraine
, the history of the struggle of the people of Donbass and their determination to preserve their historical, cultural and spiritual identity.
Addressing the Ukraine crisis, the Ambassador drew attention to the
extensive Western military and financial support to
Kiev, which contributes to prolonging the conflict.
He also highlighted the
Western disinformation campaign aimed at demonizing Russia
through the manipulation of humanitarian and cultural issues.
❗️
Ambassador Khorev reaffirmed
Russia’s commitment to a political and diplomatic settlement of the crisis:
“We are not fighting for territory. We are fighting to protect our borders and to establish a stable European security architecture based on the principles of equal and indivisible security.”
🙏
In conclusion, the Ambassador thanked the translator,
Dr. Najam Sahar Butt,
for his initiative and outstanding professionalism, which made it possible to preserve the philosophical depth of the original work.
🤝
Federal Minister of National Heritage and Culture Aurangzeb Khan Khichi highlighted the
strength of Russia-Pakistan diplomatic, trade and economic ties
and emphasized the considerable potential for expanding bilateral cooperation in culture, science and education.
