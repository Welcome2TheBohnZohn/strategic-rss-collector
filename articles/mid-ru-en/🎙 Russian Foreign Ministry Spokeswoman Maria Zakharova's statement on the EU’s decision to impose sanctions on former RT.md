# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's statement on the EU’s decision to impose sanctions on former RT France Editor-in-Chief Ksenia Fedorova

Published: 2026-09-25 11:06:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32319

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
statement
on the EU’s decision to impose sanctions on former RT France Editor-in-Chief Ksenia Fedorova
(September 25, 2026)
💬
On September 24,
the EU imposed sanctions
on
Ksenia Fedorova
, former Editor-in-Chief of RT France and a journalist working for French media outlets
CNews
,
Europe 1
and
JDNews
.
The EU Council justified its
illegitimate restrictions
by describing Ksenia Fedorova as
“a Russian media figure who is active in dissemination of pro-Russian narratives in France and the EU”.
There is no doubt that this latest act of political censorship and anti-Russian hysteria by Brussels, fuelled with ferocity across the West, was driven from the outset by
Paris’s desire to lend greater “legitimacy” to its recent shameful decision to expel Ksenia Fedorova from France
on equally fabricated grounds.
The journalist
, caught in the gears of repression of this
“progressive European democracy”
,
has not worked for Russian media for several years
. During that time, she has become a correspondent for several French outlets and gained popularity as a journalist and commentator on the national media scene.
☝️
Her only “offence” in the eyes of the Élysée Palace, which authorised this campaign of harassment, is that
she refused to abandon the ideals and ethics of journalism
, as the current French leadership demands, and
continued to express her views honestly
on events and developments in France and beyond.
In the imitation of democracy that France is becoming under its current leadership, this was enough
to set
the locomotive of vindictive censorship
in motion. The EU, hopelessly mired in Russophobic policies, in turn had no hesitation in endorsing this flawed logic.
The violation of Ksenia Fedorova’s legitimate professional rights –
a disgrace for the “enlightened” West and a direct breach of the international commitments
of Paris and Brussels
– once again exposes the absurdity of the “collective West’s” claims to set the standard for protecting democratic freedoms.
❗️
We call on all reasonable members of the international community to condemn this act of intimidation against honest journalism and truly independent-minded media. We demand that the relevant international bodies, including the leadership of
the Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights
(OHCHR),
UNESCO
and
the OSCE
, properly assess the actions of France and the EU against their own international commitments to uphold freedom of access to information and freedom of expression.
For our part, we make clear that
the Russophobic attacks
by the countries concerned and the aggressive blocs to which they belong
will receive
an appropriate response
.
