Russian Ambassador to India Denis Alipov extended his greetings to our Indian friends on Diwali!
In English:
Warm Diwali wishes to all my dear friends in India!
The lamp of Diwali conveys a beautiful message: sharing light never diminishes it; rather, it causes it to grow.
I believe that this very spirit—mutual respect, harmony, and trust— will continue to strengthen the friendship between India and Russia.
May this auspicious festival of Diwali bring new hope, happiness, peace, and prosperity into your lives.
Happy Diwali to you all!
