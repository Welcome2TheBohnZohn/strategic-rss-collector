On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov
held a meeting
with Deputy Prime Minister, Foreign Minister of the UAE Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
They discussed current issues in the further development of
the multifaceted Russia-UAE strategic partnership
.
🤝
Both Sides reaffirmed Moscow and Abu Dhabi’s
commitment to
strengthening the full range of bilateral ties
, including maintaining close foreign policy coordination at the UN.
They exchanged views on
key international and regional issues
, focusing on the situation in the Persian Gulf and the Palestinian territories.
