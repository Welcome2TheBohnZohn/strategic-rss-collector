State Protocol Department of the Russian Foreign Ministry
:
🚀
On November 12, 2026, the Russian Foreign Ministry’s State Protocol Department and the International Cooperation Department of
Roscosmos
jointly
organised a meeting
between
Roscosmos Director General Dmitry Bakanov and Heads of foreign diplomatic missions
. The meeting took place at
the National Space Centre
(
#NSC
), named after
Valentina Tereshkova
, the first woman in space.
The meeting brought together ambassadors and senior diplomats from
more than 40 countries
.
Russia’s Deputy Foreign Minister
Sergey Ryabkov
delivered
opening remarks
to the foreign guests.
The centrepiece of the meeting was a presentation by
Dmitry Bakanov
on
the national project
Development of the Russian Federation’s Space Activities to 2030 and Beyond to 2036
. He noted that Russia was preparing to launch
its own space station
. Unlike
the International Space Station
, the new station would
welcome cooperation with any country
.
Roscosmos
is ready to discuss cooperation with foreign partners
on the supply of equipment and engines, the development of space launch systems, and the construction and modernisation of national satellite communications systems, Earth observation systems and navigation infrastructure. Discussions could also cover
phased technology transfer and the localisation of production
.
During the Q&A session,
Dmitry Bakanov
spoke about work to develop
a nuclear power plant on the Moon
and about ongoing cooperation with a number of countries, including several in Africa.
🛰
The programme also included a tour of
the National Space Centre’s permanent exhibition
, which features unique exhibits tracing the milestones of Russian space exploration alongside examples of technology in use today.
