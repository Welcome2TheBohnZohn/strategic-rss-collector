# 🇷🇺 On November 24-25, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov paid a working visit to Gomel

Published: 2026-11-26 06:03:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33645

🇷🇺
🇧🇾
On November 24-25, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
paid a working visit to Gomel
, Republic of Belarus, where he took part in
the annual joint meeting of the boards of the Russian and Belarusian Foreign Ministries
and
held talks
with his Belarusian counterpart
Maxim Ryzhenkov
.
During their discussions, the Ministers
reviewed current bilateral issues
, including diplomatic support for integration processes within the Union State, and
exchanged views on international affairs
.
They agreed to continue close foreign policy coordination
, mutual support at multilateral forums and jointly advance their common interests on the global stage.
The agenda for the meeting included matters relating to the implementation of
the Programme of Coordinated Actions in Foreign Policy for 2024-2026
and the adoption of
a new Programme for 2027–2029
, interaction at the
#UN
and its affiliated organisations, diplomatic support for interregional and cross-border ties, and mutual assistance in crisis and emergency situations.
✍️
Following the meeting, the Sides adopted
a resolution
, summarising the discussions and agreements reached, as well as
a Plan of Consultations between the Foreign Ministries for 2027
.
The Programme of Coordinated Actions in Foreign Policy for 2027-2029
was also signed and will be submitted for approval to the Supreme State Council of the Union State.
The next joint meeting of the boards of the Russian and Belarusian Foreign Ministries will be held
in the Russian Federation in the fourth quarter of 2027
.
