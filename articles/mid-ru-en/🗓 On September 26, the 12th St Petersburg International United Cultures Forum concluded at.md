🗓
On September 26,
the 12th St Petersburg International United Cultures Forum
concluded at
the State Hermitage Museum
. The Forum became
a major event dedicated to promoting intercultural dialogue
and strengthening understanding between countries and peoples, while preserving their linguistic, cultural and spiritual identities.
The Forum
brought together
4,500 participants
, including
45 official delegations
, with
representatives from
73 countries
. Delegations from
more than 10 countries
were led by their Ministers of Culture.
💬
The Forum’s central event was
an address
by President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
:
Culture can build bridges between people
. It is a vital and irreplaceable means of communication and dialogue where political and economic ties have been frozen or brought to a halt by disagreements, sanctions and restrictions.
Russia has historically developed as a crossroads of peoples, languages and traditions
. For us, unity means solidarity built on nurturing the distinct identity and development of every people and ethnic group.
Culture plays a key role in this process
, just as it does in our complex world, with its conflicts, profound technological and social transformations, and competition between countries.

The Forum featured
84 events
across ten thematic tracks
, covering museums, theatre, music, film, cultural sovereignty, education, artificial intelligence and other subjects.
✍️
Participants signed 33 agreements
to develop cooperation in culture, museums, theatre, education and the preservation of cultural heritage.
Plans were set in motion to develop a
concept for theatre development through 2035
and expand cooperation with professional associations in
Vietnam, Arab countries and Iran
. Russia also signed a number of bilateral documents with friendly countries.
💬
Russia’s Minister of Culture
Olga Lyubimova
:
Once again, our Forum has confirmed its standing as
a respected platform for open and free dialogue
, bringing together representatives of different countries, peoples and cultures around a common goal: identifying the challenges facing world culture and finding ways to address them.
