We, the Ministers of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Armenia, the Republic of Belarus, the Republic of Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic, the Russian Federation, the Republic of Tajikistan, and the Republic of Uzbekistan, hereby declare the following.
The CIS Member States conduct activities relating to exploration and use of outer space
in full compliance with
the Charter of the United Nations
,
the 1967 Treaty on Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space, including the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies
, and other regulations and principles of international law,
in the interests of promoting international cooperation and mutual understanding and maintaining international peace and security
.
The Parties advocate broad joint efforts by the international community
to improve the relevant international legal framework
.
❗️
The CIS Member States express serious concern over the real prospect of an arms race in outer space and its transformation into an arena of military confrontation
,
which undermines global stability.
The Parties note that
a ban on placing weapons of any kind in outer space
, as well as on the use or threat of force against space objects or through the use of such objects,
could prevent the emergence of a serious threat to international peace and security
. The Parties also emphasise
the unacceptability
of using space systems and their associated ground infrastructure for purposes that are incompatible with maintaining international peace and security and strengthening trust among states.
The CIS member states reiterate
the exceptional importance of taking immediate practical measures to prevent an arms race in outer space
(
#PAROS
).
The most effective of these remains
the negotiation by the international community of a multilateral, legally binding instrument
that would provide reliable guarantees for preventing an arms race in outer space and prohibiting the placement of weapons there. This instrument should be based on
the Russian-Chinese draft treaty on preventing the placement of weapons in outer space, the threat or use of force against outer space objects
, and
the substantive report of the Group of Governmental Experts on PAROS
, established pursuant to
UN General Assembly Resolution 77/250
and adopted by consensus in 2024.
The Parties reaffirm the key role of the Conference on Disarmament
, which is the single multilateral negotiating forum on disarmament, in negotiations on an international agreement to prevent an arms race in outer space.
The CIS Member States welcome the launch of a discussion on the substantive elements of a legally binding international instrument on PAROS
, as well as
on non-binding measures
, within the framework of
the UN Open-Ended Working Group established by UN General Assembly Decision 79/512
. These discussions include, in particular, preventing the placement of weapons in outer space. The Parties look forward to the Working Group achieving substantive results.
☝️
The Parties share the view that transparency and confidence-building measures help prevent the placement of weapons in outer space
. At the same time, they emphasise that any possible non-binding measures on PAROS must, directly or indirectly, contribute to
a comprehensive ban on placing weapons in outer space
and
on the use or threat of force against space objects or through the use of such objects
. Such measures must complement, rather than replace, legally binding agreements on PAROS.
