# ✉️ On August 27, President of Russia Vladimir Putin expressed condolences to President of Nepal

Published: 2026-08-27 10:40:38

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31655

✉️
On August 27, President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
expressed condolences
to President of Nepal
Ram Chandra Paudel
and Prime Minister of Nepal
Balendra Shah
.
✍️
Please accept my deepest condolences
over the heavy loss of life and serious destruction caused by mudslides and flooding in the northeastern regions of your country.
We share the grief of the friendly people of Nepal.
Please convey my sincere sympathy to the families and loved ones of those who lost their lives, as well as my wishes for a speedy recovery to all those injured in this natural disaster.
Respectfully,
VLADIMIR PUTIN
