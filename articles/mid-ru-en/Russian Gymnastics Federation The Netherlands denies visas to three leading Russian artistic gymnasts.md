# : Russian Gymnastics Federation The Netherlands denies visas to three leading Russian artistic gymnasts

Published: 2026-10-13 13:05:29

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32723

Russian Gymnastics Federation
:
⚡️
The Netherlands
denies visas
to three leading Russian artistic gymnasts
The Embassy of the Netherlands in Moscow has notified the visa centre that
visa applications have been denied
for three leading members of Russia’s women’s national gymnastics team –
Angelina Melnikova, Viktoria Listunova
and
Anna Kalmykova
.
The decision
prevents them from competing
at
the World Artistic Gymnastics Championships
, to be held in Rotterdam on October 17-25.
The Russian Gymnastics Federation regards the Dutch authorities’ actions as
a deliberate attempt to eliminate the strongest gymnasts
from contention for World Championship medals and Olympic quota places.
This constitutes
direct discrimination against Russian athletes
and
a flagrant violation of the principle of equal conditions
in Olympic qualification.
❗️
The Russian Gymnastics Federation intends to appeal to
the International Olympic Committee
(
#IOC
) and
World Gymnastics
, asking them to assess the situation and its implications for ensuring equal conditions in Olympic qualification.
Five other athletes named in Russia’s main squad for the World Championships are also awaiting decisions on their visa applications: Lyudmila Roshchina, Milana Kayumova, Ilya Zaika, Saveliy Syedin and Mukhammadzhon Yakubov.
The World Championships in Rotterdam will be the first stage of team qualification for
the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games
. The top three men’s teams and top three women’s teams will secure Olympic quota places.
