On September 25, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Kazakhstan Mukhtar Tileuberdi
held a meeting
on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
The Sides discussed
key areas of joint work between their Ministries
to deepen bilateral relations of comprehensive strategic partnership and alliance.
The Foreign Ministers exchanged views on
cooperation at regional and international platforms
and coordinated their positions on current global issues.
The meeting took place in
the traditionally friendly atmosphere
characteristic of Russia-Kazakhstan relations.
▪️
Sergey Lavrov
expressed condolences
over
the tragic incident
during Kazakhstan’s naval exercises in the Caspian Sea.
