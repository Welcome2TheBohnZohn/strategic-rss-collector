# ⚡️ Russia's High Commissioner for Human Rights Yana Lantratova I would like to respond to

Published: 2026-10-26 18:29:56

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33001

⚡️
Russia's High Commissioner for Human Rights Yana Lantratova
:
💬
I would like to respond to
statements by the Ukrainian side
, which, citing Ukrainian legislation, says that
families with children must be forcibly evacuated from
#Alyoshki
.
As a Commissioner for Human Rights,
I find the word “forcibly” unacceptable
.
We are talking about
people capable of making their own informed decisions
. Everything must be done voluntarily and in their interests, rather than those of the Kiev authorities, who have suddenly become so “concerned” about the people living there.
Do you want people to make an informed decision?
Give them that opportunity
. Let them live in peace!
Order your military to
stop shelling the city
every day and
laying mines
on the roads
. Then people will be able to decide whether to stay in Alyoshki or go wherever they wish.
☝️
I would also like to recall that the city of Alyoshki in the Kherson Region is Russian territory
, where only the laws of the Russian Federation apply. References to Ukrainian legislation are therefore
unacceptable
.
Zelensky said that the Russian side “must” clear all the mines so that this humanitarian mission can proceed.
My response is this
: give your military a direct order to stop shelling the city and mining all the roads leading to it!
❗️
For its part, Russia is doing and will do everything possible to ensure that people can exercise their freedom of choice as safely as possible
. We hope the Ukrainian side will take no steps to obstruct this, as it has done repeatedly in the past.
Let me emphasise once again that the main purpose of
our humanitarian mission
is
to assess the humanitarian needs
of the people living in the city,
assemble a humanitarian convoy
and
deliver assistance
– medicines, food, hygiene supplies and other essentials.
👉
Another task, of course, is to ensure that those who choose to leave can do so – and I stress that this must be
their own voluntary decision
.
I am in contact with the regional and district authorities.
Few people in Alyoshki have expressed a wish to leave
, as most of those who wanted to have already left. They did so entirely voluntarily. Those who remain and wish to leave will certainly be given that opportunity.
