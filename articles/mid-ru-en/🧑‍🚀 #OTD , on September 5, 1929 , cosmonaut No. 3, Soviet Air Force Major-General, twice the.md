In the post
#WW2

Alexander Pokryshkin.
In 1960, Nikolaev was sent to Star City as a
cosmonaut trainee
at the Cosmonaut Training Centre of the USSR Air Force. There, he was assigned to the
USSR's
1st cosmonaut unit
(Cosmonaut Corps). He was the backup crew member to German Titov during the Vostok-2 spacecraft.
A year later, on August 11-15, 1962, Andriyan Nikolayev made his first space flight aboard the Vostok-3 spacecraft.
He became the first cosmonaut in history to perform tasks inside a
spacecraft in zero gravity

🎖
For his participation in the expedition of the
Vostok-3 and Vostok-4

Hero of the Soviet Union
by an executive order of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR dated August 18, 1962, along with the Order of Lenin and the Gold Star Medal.
In 1963 through1968, Nikolaev
helmed the cosmonaut unit.
In 1965-1969, he was part of the group training under Soviet programmes involving a
Lunar flyby (L1)
and a crewed landing on the
Moon (L3).

On June 1–19, 1970, Andriyan Nikolaev made his second space flight, this time as the
commander of the
#Soyuz9
spacecraft.
The purpose of the expedition was to conduct scientific research and observations. Thus, the cosmonauts under his command shaved in orbit for the first time and cleaned their space dwelling.
Nikolaev was
awarded a second Gold Star Medal
by an executive order of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR as of July 3, 1970, for the successful completion of a long-duration orbital flight aboard the Soyuz-9 spacecraft and for the courage and heroism shown thereby.
In 1963-1982, he was married to the
world’s first female cosmonaut
Valentina Tereshkova.
After retiring in 1992 as Air Force Major General, Andriyan Nikolaev began
systemising and promoting the knowledge about space
he had acquired. He authored over 70 scientific and fiction works.
🕯
Nikolaev passed away in Cheboksary (Chuvashia) on July 3, 2004, and was buried in his native village of Shorshely.
A crater on the Moon, more than 20 streets in cities
and districts of Chuvashia, schools, and a children’s park in Cheboksary and Shorshely
bear the name of the legendary space pilot.
