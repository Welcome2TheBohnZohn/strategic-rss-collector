On October 27, Foreign Minister of Russia Sergey Lavrov and Minister of State, Minister of Foreign Affairs, National Community Abroad and African Affairs of Algeria Ahmed Attaf
held a meeting in Moscow
.
Ahmed Attaf arrived as part of the delegation led by Algerian Prime Minister
Sifi Ghrieb
to attend
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
and
the Third #RussiaAfrica Summit
.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks
The Ministers held substantive discussions on
practical steps to strengthen the enhanced strategic partnership
between Russia and Algeria.
They expressed satisfaction with the trust underpinning their political dialogue and their
well-established cooperation, both bilaterally and at multilateral platforms
, including the UN.
Following an exchange of views on developments in North Africa and the Sahara-Sahel region, the Sides reaffirmed their
shared interest in continuing effective Russian-Algerian coordination to resolve ongoing conflicts through political and diplomatic means
, based on universally recognised principles of international law and
the UN Charter
.
