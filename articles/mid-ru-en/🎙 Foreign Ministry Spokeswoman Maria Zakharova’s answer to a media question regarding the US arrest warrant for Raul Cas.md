# 🎙 Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s answer to a media question regarding the US arrest warrant for Raul Castro

Published: 2026-10-26 14:59:56

🎙
Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
answer to a media question
regarding the US arrest warrant for Raul Castro
(October 26, 2026)
❓
Question: The Russian Foreign Ministry’s
statement
of October 20 regarding the arrest warrant issued by a US judge for Cuba’s historic leader Raul Castro refers to
UN Security Council Resolution 1067 of July 26, 1996
. Could you explain in more detail how that decision was reached?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
Following the well-known incident involving private US aircraft on February 24, 1996,
intensive consultations
took place among UN Security Council members.
Permanent Representatives of Russia and the US to the UN –
Sergey Lavrov and Madeleine Albright
– played the most active role in these consultations.
Their efforts made it possible to agree on
a statement
acceptable to all partie
s – both Russia and, especially importantly, the US – which was issued by the President of the UN Security Council on February 27, 1996.
I would like to draw attention to the fact that
it contained no accusations
against any individual
, let alone calls for measures to hold anyone accountable.
The approach proposed in that statement subsequently formed the basis of
UN Security Council Resolution S/RES/1067 (1996) of July 26, 1996
.
This effectively meant that all Council members agreed that this dimension of the incident was closed.
❗️
We recall this episode of Russia-US cooperation at the UN precisely to underscore that
the accusations now being made against Cuba’s historic leader Raul Castro are legally null and void
.
We clearly stated this in
the Russian MFA statement
of October 20.
