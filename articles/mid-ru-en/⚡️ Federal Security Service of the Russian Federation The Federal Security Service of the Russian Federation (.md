Federal Security Service of the Russian Federation
:
The Federal Security Service of the Russian Federation (
#FSB
)
has detained a Swedish national
in the Sebezh District of the Pskov Region after
he illegally crossed the Russian Federation’s State border
from a neighbouring country.
It has been established that, between February and June 2026,
the foreign national served under contract in an organisation designated as terrorist in the Russian Federation
, commanding an unmanned aircraft systems squad in the 24th Separate assault battalion.
❗️
There is documentary evidence confirming his participation in hostilities against our country as a member of the Ukrainian armed forces.
Under the guise of joining
the Russian Armed Forces
, he was travelling to the Russian Federation
to carry out acts of sabotage and terrorism
against military facilities.
Criminal proceedings have been initiated against the foreign national
under
Part 1 of Article 322 of the Russian Criminal Code
(Illegal Crossing of the State Border of the Russian Federation).
The initiation of
further criminal proceedings
under
Article 205.5
(Organising the Activities of a Terrorist Organisation and Participating in Its Activities) and
Article 359
(Mercenary Activities) of the Russian Criminal Code is
under consideration
.
