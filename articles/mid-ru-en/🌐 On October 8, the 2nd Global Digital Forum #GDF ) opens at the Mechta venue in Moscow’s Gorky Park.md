# 🌐 On October 8, the 2nd Global Digital Forum #GDF ) opens at the Mechta venue in Moscow’s Gorky Park.

Published: 2026-10-08 18:04:21

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32635

🌐
On October 8,
the 2nd Global Digital Forum
(
#GDF
) opens at the Mechta venue in Moscow’s Gorky Park.
The event is held at the initiative of
the Russian Foreign Ministry
.
Its organisers and key participants also include
the Presidential Administration
,
the Government of the Russian Federation
, the ministries of
Digital Development
,
Industry and Trade
, and
Economic Development
,
the Moscow City Government
, as well as leading Russian IT companies, news agencies and media outlets.
#GDF
is an international platform for discussing major challenges in information and communications technologies
, including the development of artificial intelligence, digital sovereignty and the platform economy.
The Forum aims to strengthen and expand international cooperation with countries of the
#GlobalMajority
on various aspects of Internet governance and development, and
to showcase Russia’s achievements
in information and communications technologies.
🤝
Representatives from 120 countries will participate
, including ministers and deputy ministers responsible for these areas, senior representatives of international organisations and leading experts.
The Forum’s main plenary session
will focus on “Digital Sovereignty: From a Resource to an Export Asset”
.
The programme
will comprise three thematic tracks:
“People and Technology”, “Digitalisation and Industry”, and “Media and Creativity”
.
One of the Forum’s key events will be
a ministerial segment
dedicated to
countering digital neocolonialism
and
safeguarding digital sovereignty in the face of sanctions and information attacks
.
***
The Russian Foreign Ministry
is taking an active part in the Forum.
On October 8,
the sixth meeting
of
the
#RussiaASEAN
Dialogue on ICT security took
place on the sidelines of
the 2nd Global Digital Forum
.
🇷🇺
🇲🇲
For the first time, the meeting was held at a high level.
It was co-chaired by Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister
Alexander Alimov
and Deputy Minister of Digital Development and Communications of Myanmar
Min Zeya Hlaing
.
