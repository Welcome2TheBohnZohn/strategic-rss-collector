Statement by the Russian Ice Hockey Federation
:
The International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF) has decided to host the 2027 World Junior Championship in Canada without the participation of the junior Russian national team.
The Russian Ice Hockey Federation
(RIHF) expresses its strong protest against this
unacceptable
and
unlawful
decision.
The IIHF’s decision
was made solely due to political reasons
, in violation of a binding and effective ruling by the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) — which stated that the IIHF must allow Russian national teams to participate in international competitions — as well as an agreement between the IIHF and RIHF, under which the international federation committed to implementing the CAS ruling with immediate effect.
The exclusion of the Russian national team
violates fundamental legal principles
, including that of Olympism, the principle of non-discrimination, equality among athletes and the integrity of international tournaments.
In conjunction with the Russian Olympic Committee, the RIHF will appeal to the International Olympic Committee to resolve this unacceptable situation.
The RIHF will pursue all available legal avenues to ensure the enforcement of the CAS ruling, the restoration of the rights of Russian athletes and reparation for the harm caused.
