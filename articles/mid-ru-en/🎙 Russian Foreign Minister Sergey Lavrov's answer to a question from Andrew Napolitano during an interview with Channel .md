🎙
Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov's
answer to a question
from Andrew Napolitano during an interview with Channel One’s
The Great Game programme
(Moscow, September 8, 2026)
❓
Question: Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner recently visited Moscow and met with President of Russia Vladimir Putin. What can you tell us about the outcome of this visit?
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
Much of the opening part of these talks
was broadcast on television
: President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
’s remarks, followed by statements from
Steve Witkoff
and
Jared Kushner
. Presidential Aide Yury Ushakov, who took part in the conversation,
later gave a detailed comment
.
In our diplomatic tradition,
we generally limit ourselves to what we have decided to make public
.
I do not want to emulate
those who are now beginning to speculate
about
“what Mr Witkoff and Mr Kushner brought to Moscow and what they subsequently took to Kiev”
. A member of Ukraine's parliament has already said that what they brought back was far worse than what Ukraine had previously rejected.
❗️
There is a great deal of speculation.
As for further diplomatic steps,
the American Side has said nothing
.
The French, however, have already announced that they were there and that the Americans supposedly said that,
following the elections to Russia's State Duma
, direct talks involving the US would definitely resume.
I leave all of this on the conscience of the Élysée Palace
, especially given that it has repeatedly gained notoriety for
less-than-ethical conduct
when it comes to disclosing the substance of telephone and other conversations.
The substance of what was discussed is based – and I can say this with confidence – on
the position President Donald Trump outlined on Ukraine
immediately after returning to the Oval Office.
☝️
First, he said that
any attempts to draw Ukraine into NATO should simply be forgotten.
He recalled that this had been clear long before President Vladimir Putin, that the matter was closed and there was no reason to try to reopen it.
👉
The second element of the US position is
recognition of the realities on the ground.
I would particularly stress that
this is not about territories
.
When we speak of realities,
we are talking about people
who, immediately after
the February 2014 coup
, were branded “subhuman”, “specimen” and terrorists. When they rejected the results of
the illegal coup
, the army, combat aircraft and artillery were deployed against them – something categorically prohibited under international humanitarian law.
They were simply not treated as human beings.
Shortly after being elected president in 2014, Poroshenko said that their children – the children of “real Ukrainians” – would go to clean schools and kindergartens, while the children of those living in Donbass would rot in basements
.
So how do the Europeans intend to return these people to the rule of that regime when they call for restoring the 1991 borders?
Simply to hand the territory over to Nazis? Or do they want to punish these people for wanting to preserve their freedom, their culture and their belonging to the Russian world?
I have spoken about this
many times
.
I would like to stress that
the realities on the ground
, which President Donald Trump and his team recognise – as far as we can judge, they continue to recognise them, just as they did in Alaska –
are realities connected with people
,
with the views they expressed in referendums
: in Crimea, in the Donetsk and Lugansk People’s Republics and, of course, in the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions.
The fact that the Americans proceed from these realities in all their contacts with us, above all in their contacts with President Vladimir Putin, naturally elicits
a positive response from us and strengthens our readiness to continue talking with them
.
