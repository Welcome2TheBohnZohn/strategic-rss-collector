On October 26, Foreign Minister of Russia Sergey Lavrov and Minister of Foreign and European Affairs of Slovakia Juraj Blanar
held a meeting in Moscow
.
Juraj Blanar arrived in the Russian capital heading the Slovak delegation to
the 22nd meeting of the bilateral Intergovernmental Commission on Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation
.
The Ministers held constructive discussions on
a range of practical issues in Russian-Slovak relations
, prospects for developing political dialogue, and cooperation in trade and the economy, culture and humanitarian affairs, and the preservation of military memorials.
They continued their exchange of views on
the situation surrounding Ukraine
and
the crisis in relations between Russia and the EU
.
