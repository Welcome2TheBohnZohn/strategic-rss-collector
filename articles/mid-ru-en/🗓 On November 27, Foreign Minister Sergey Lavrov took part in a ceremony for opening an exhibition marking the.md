On November 27, Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
took part
in a ceremony for opening an exhibition marking the
5️⃣
5️⃣
th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Russia and the United Arab Emirates (December 8, 1971) at the Foreign Ministry.
The exhibition featured materials from the Foreign Policy Archives of the Russian Federation and the UAE National Archives, ranging
from copies of the first credentials to photographs of recent meetings and talks.
These documents vividly illustrate the key milestones of multifaceted Russian-Emirati cooperation, which has now reached the level of a strategic partnership.
In his welcoming remarks,
Sergey Lavrov
emphasised that regular contacts between President
Vladimir Putin
and UAE President
Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan
provide the main boost for bilateral cooperation. The two leaders discussed in detail current bilateral issues and global and regional issues during
the talks in Moscow
on January 29, 2026.
The Minister spoke highly of
close coordination with Abu Dhabi
through the Foreign Ministries, including at international fora, as well as the
intensive exchange of delegations
at the level of executive authorities.
🤝
Sergey Lavrov
highlighted the dynamic
development of trade and economic ties,
with the Russian-Emirati Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic and Technical Cooperation playing a coordinating role. He underscored the important contribution of the Russian-Emirati Business Council to strengthening cooperation between the two countries’ business communities.
Sergey Lavrov drew attention to
the significant expansion of bilateral contacts
in the cultural, humanitarian and educational spheres, as well as growing tourism ties. He noted
the successful work
of the Sheikha Fatima Centre for International Cooperation with the UAE, which was officially opened at the Yevgeny Primakov Regional Gymnasium in October 2024 in the presence of the two countries’ leaders. He also noted the high-profile Russian Culture Days held in Abu Dhabi in November 2025 and the UAE Culture Days held in Moscow in August 2026.
The event was attended by UAE Ambassador to Russia
Mohammed Al Jaber,
heads of diplomatic missions accredited in Moscow, members of both chambers of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation, and representatives of Russia’s academic and expert communities.
