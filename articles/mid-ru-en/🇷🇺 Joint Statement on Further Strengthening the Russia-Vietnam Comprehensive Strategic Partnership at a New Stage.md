Joint Statement on Further Strengthening the Russia-Vietnam Comprehensive Strategic Partnership at a New Stage
(Moscow, September 9, 2026)
The Leaders of Russia and Vietnam held
an in-depth exchange of views
on the current state and prospects of Russia-Vietnam relations and
agreed on steps to further advance the comprehensive strategic partnership between the two countries
.
✍️
Following
the visit
, the Russian Federation and the Socialist Republic of Vietnam
declare the following
:
FOUNDATIONS, GOALS AND PRINCIPLES FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF RUSSIA-VIETNAM RELATIONS
1
. Relations between Russia and Vietnam, established in 1950, have always been characterised by
fraternal friendship
,
deep trust
and
a readiness to assist each other
. These qualities were particularly evident during the heroic Vietnamese people’s struggle for freedom and independence.
In 2001, these relations were elevated to
a strategic partnership
, and in 2012 – to
a comprehensive strategic partnership
.
4
. Russia and Vietnam base their cooperation on mutual trust, long-term planning and effective engagement, in-depth strategic coordination, the fundamental principles of international law and
the UN Charter
, and equal and mutually beneficial cooperation.
They seek to maintain close cooperation across all areas of bilateral relations and within multilateral frameworks
.
KEY AREAS OF BILATERAL COOPERATION
• Politics and diplomacy
• Defence and security
• Trade and economic cooperation
• Fuel and energy
• Transport
• Industry and agriculture
• Science and technology, education and professional training, healthcare
• Humanitarian cooperation
KEY AREAS OF INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION
34
. The Parties
consistently reaffirm
the outcomes of World War II
as enshrined in
the UN Charter
and
oppose attempts to distort and falsify the history of World War II
. They emphasise the importance of patriotic education, maintaining continuity between generations and preserving the memory of the struggle against Nazism, fascism and militarism.
The Parties also
attach
great importance
to
treating the shared history of the two countries with care and respect.
38
. The Parties stand ready
to help unite the international community’s efforts to counter traditional and emerging security challenges
, including terrorism, extremism and radicalisation, transnational crime, armed conflicts, the illicit production and trafficking of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their precursors, externally instigated coups d’état, climate change, natural disasters and epidemics.
44
. The Parties
reaffirm their intention to strengthen cooperation and coordination within
the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation
(
#APEC
)
Forum
as the key economic platform in the Asia-Pacific region
, operating on a depoliticised and non-discriminatory basis.
Russia
fully supports
Vietnam’s efforts to successfully host APEC in 2027
. Vietnam
welcomes and supports
Russia’s bid to host APEC in 2035. The Parties call on APEC members to give favourable consideration to bids on the basis of consensus to ensure the forum’s continued effective functioning.
48
. Russia highly appreciates
Vietnam’s balanced and objective stance on the Ukrainian crisis
and welcomes its readiness to join efforts to find ways to achieve a political and diplomatic settlement and restore regional stability, taking into account the legitimate security interests of all sovereign states.
Moscow and Hanoi are committed to international law and the principles of
the UN Charter
in their entirety and interrelationship.
They seek to help resolve the conflict around Ukraine by fully addressing its root causes in pursuit of a lasting peaceful solution
. The Parties express deep concern over civilian casualties and call on members of the international community
to refrain from supplying weapons to the conflict zone
.
