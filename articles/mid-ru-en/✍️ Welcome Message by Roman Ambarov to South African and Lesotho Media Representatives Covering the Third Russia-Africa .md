Welcome Message by Roman Ambarov to South African and Lesotho Media Representatives Covering the Third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow
.
It is my great pleasure to welcome representatives of the South African and Lesotho media to Moscow for the
Third Russia-Africa Summit and the events of Africa Week
.
Your participation reflects the
growing interest in relations between Russia and the African continent.
We highly value your presence and see it as an important contribution to strengthening mutual understanding and expanding cooperation between our countries.
We attach great importance to direct dialogue with media professionals and welcome the opportunity to share Russia’s perspectives on key international issues.
We are particularly pleased by the broad representation of South African media, including young journalists, bloggers and digital content creators, as well as the participation of Lesotho media representatives for the first time at an event of this scale. We hope this visit will foster new professional contacts and closer cooperation among our media communities.
🤝
I am confident that your coverage of the Summit will help
deepen understanding of Russia-Africa relations and bring our peoples closer together
.
I wish you a productive and successful stay in Moscow, engaging discussions and new professional opportunities.
Welcome to Moscow and the Third Russia-Africa Summit!
