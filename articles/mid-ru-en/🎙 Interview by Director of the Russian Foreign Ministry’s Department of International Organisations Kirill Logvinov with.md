Key points
:
• The schedule of events that members of the Russian delegation will attend on the sidelines of
the #UNGA High-Level Week
will be
subject to change
until the last moment,
as invitations are still coming in.
•
Sergey Lavrov
already has a busy schedule of bilateral meetings
, primarily with representatives of friendly countries, as well as other major global players.
• Sergey Lavrov is scheduled to address
the General Debate
of the 81st Session of the UNGA
on
Saturday, September 26
. That same day, the Head of the Russian delegation will hold his
traditional
press conference
to conclude his visit to New York.
•
Dialogue with the US on removing irritants in bilateral relations
, including restrictions on staff at Russia’s Permanent Mission to the UN,
continues
.
❗️
Russia's Deputy Foreign Minister
Alexander Alimov
, who oversees UN affairs,
has not been issued a visa
. Failure to issue a visa constitutes
a serious violation of US obligations
as the host country of the UN Headquarters.
• Russia continues to press for proceedings to be initiated over the
ongoing US violations of its obligations as the host country of the UN Headquarters
. This is not a demand of our own, but a specific requirement reiterated year after year in the relevant UNGA resolutions.
• Regrettably, the current UN Secretary-General has consistently
evaded implementing these resolutions
and the relevant provisions of
the 1947 UN Headquarters Agreement
. Under Guterres,
the UN Secretariat has effectively admitted its inability to fulfil its duty
to safeguard normal working conditions for permanent missions to the UN.
•
We will, of course, continue to insist that this issue be resolved as soon as possible
. We expect
the next Secretary-General
to initiate arbitration proceedings and, more broadly, to discharge his duties conscientiously and in full accordance with the UN Charter. We would not want to hear the future head of the Secretariat resort to Guterres-style excuses:
“We could, but why bother”
⁉️
• Guterres’ tenure as UN Secretary-General will be remembered for
repeatedly overstepping his mandate
and
departing from the fundamental norms and principles
of
the UN Charter
, which the Secretary-General is called upon to uphold. This has been reflected in politically biased statements, actions taken without consulting member states, and reliance on decisions that lack the broadest possible support.
•
Ultimately,
Guterres’ actions have seriously undermined trust in the UN
. These failings have been most blatant in the context of the Ukraine crisis.
• These issues will also be raised at
the UN Security Council meeting on the situation in Ukraine on September 23
. This will provide Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
with another opportunity
to explain in detail the goals and objectives of
the special military operation
, including ensuring that the rights of Russians and Russian-speaking people in Ukraine are respected.
•
We have plenty to say to those in the West who support Kiev with money and weapons
, and to UN Secretariat officials who seek to “prioritise” certain principles of
the UN Charter
over others. In the presence of delegations from our allies in the anti-Hitler coalition, we will highlight
the ongoing Nazification of Ukraine
.
