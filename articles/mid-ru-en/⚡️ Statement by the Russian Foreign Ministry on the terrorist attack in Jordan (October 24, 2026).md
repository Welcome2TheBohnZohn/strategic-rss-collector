⚡️
Statement by the Russian Foreign Ministry on the terrorist attack in Jordan
(October 24, 2026)
On October 23,
an armed attack
targeted people attending a memorial church service in the village of Hammoud, in Karak Governorate in western Jordan.
According to preliminary reports,
seven people were killed and another 40 sustained injuries
of varying severity.
ISIS
has claimed responsibility for the attack.
❗️
We strongly condemn this cynical terrorist act
, clearly aimed at undermining the interfaith peace and harmony established in Jordanian society.
We consider attacks on religious sites and worshippers
unacceptable
. We reaffirm
our unwavering solidarity with friendly Jordan
in combating terrorism and extremism in all their manifestations.
🕯
We extend our sincere condolences
to the leadership and people of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan and to the families and loved ones of those killed. We wish the injured
a speedy recovery
.
