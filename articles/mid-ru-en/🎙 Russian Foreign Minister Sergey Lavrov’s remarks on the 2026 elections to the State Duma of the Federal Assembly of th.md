🎙
Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov’s remarks on the 2026 elections to the State Duma of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation
(Moscow, September 18, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
Dear friends,
Voting is now underway
to elect
the ninth State Duma
of the Federal Assembly of the Russian Federation.
🗳
On September 18-20, we are all called upon to make a choice of great importance to our country.
In
his address to Russian citizens
,
President of Russia Vladimir Putin
stressed the significance of these elections for Russia’s future
, its continued development, and the protection of its security and sovereignty.
The President urged all of us
to exercise our constitutional right and make a responsible choice
, so that the election results demonstrate the maturity and resilience of Russian society.
👉
This will be our collective response
to those who seek to weaken Russia and deny us the right to determine our own destiny.
