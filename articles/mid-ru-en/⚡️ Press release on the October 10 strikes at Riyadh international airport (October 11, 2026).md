⚡️
Press release on the October 10 strikes at Riyadh international airport
(October 11, 2026)
According to incoming information, on October 10
the King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh was subject to a massive missile strike.
▪️
The attack resulted in the death of 12 people
, including citizens of Saudi Arabia, Bangladesh, Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Palestine, Sudan and the United States.
More than 300 people sustained injuries of varying severity.
According to preliminary reports from
the Embassy of Russia in Saudi Arabia
there are
no Russian compatriots among the victims.
Moscow expresses its
deep condolences
to the families and friends of those killed and
wishes speedy recovery
to the wounded.
❗️
Russia condemns this attack and calls on the parties to the conflict on the Arabian Peninsula to completely give up strikes against civilian infrastructure facilities
.
Russia’s position remains unchanged
: there is no alternative to settling any crises in the region by political and diplomatic means while ensuring the interests of all the states in this region without exception.
