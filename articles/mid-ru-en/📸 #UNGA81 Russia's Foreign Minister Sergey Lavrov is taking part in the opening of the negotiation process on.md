📸
Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
is taking part in the opening of the negotiation process on
the draft
Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the 21st Century
.
📍
New York, September 25
***
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
Today, we plan to officially announce
the start of work on drafting
the Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the 21st Century
.
To this end, we propose that you
consider and approve the Joint Statement
. It is a very straightforward document that simply announces the launch of this work.
We have proposed
a new Eurasian security architecture
as a foundation for the peaceful, steady development of all the peoples of our continent – the world’s largest and richest, and home to several of its oldest civilisations.
In putting forward this initiative, we took into account the fact that
the rise of multipolarity will remain the most enduring trend in global affairs for many years to come
.
We also recognised t
he need to respond to the rapid spread and escalation of conflicts across Eurasia
, which, of course, divert resources and attention from constructive endeavours and claim human lives.
We asked ourselves
what mechanisms could help ensure predictability
in the development of our vast continent. We concluded that we need to bring together
the broadest possible circle of like-minded partners
who share a common understanding of the need to develop principles for coexistence among all Eurasian states, based on mutual respect and mutual benefit, in the new era of
#Multipolarity
.
🇧🇾
🤝
🇷🇺
This initiative was originally put forward by
our Belarusian friends
. Russia fully supported it.
We are pleased to have the opportunity today to hear from our friends on
the ways they could contribute
to this joint effort.
