🎙
Opening remarks
by Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov during talks with Minister of Foreign Affairs and Cooperation of Timor-Leste Bendito Freitas
(Moscow, October 6, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov
: Mr Minister,
Сolleagues,
We are sincerely pleased to welcome you to Moscow on
the first official visit to Russia
by a Minister of Foreign Affairs and Cooperation of Timor-Leste in
the history of our diplomatic relations
.
This is undoubtedly
a historic moment
.
Today, we will have a
n opportunity to review the full scope of our relations
, both bilateral cooperation and engagement on international issues, in the spirit of
the fundamental agreements
reached by President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
and Prime Minister of Timor-Leste
Kay Rala Xanana Gusmao
on the sidelines of
the anniversary Russia-ASEAN Summit in Kazan
this June.
☝️
We remember the early days of Timor-Leste’s statehood.
Russia played an active role
, primarily through the UN, i
n providing humanitarian and other assistance to your citizens
. In the early 2000s, we deployed Russian police officers to
the UN Mission of Support in East Timor
. This was another contribution we made to building your country’s statehood.
We have
a number of initial ideas
on how to implement
the fundamental agreements
on developing our relations, and I hope we will discuss these in detail today.
