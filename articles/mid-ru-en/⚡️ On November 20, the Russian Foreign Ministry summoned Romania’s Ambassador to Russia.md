# ⚡️ On November 20, the Russian Foreign Ministry summoned Romania’s Ambassador to Russia

Published: 2026-11-20 14:32:54

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33507

⚡️
On November 20, the Russian Foreign Ministry
summoned
Romania’s Ambassador to Russia
Cristian Istrate
.
The Ministry issued
a strong protest over Romania’s unjustified refusal to issue entry visas to the Russian national team
, which was due to compete in
the European Taekwondo Championships
for boys and girls aged 12–14 in Bucharest in late November.
Moscow regards this violation of the young athletes’ rights as
a shameful act of discrimination on grounds of nationality
that contravenes fundamental human rights standards and undermines the foundations of international cooperation in sport.
❗️
Politicising decisions on whether Russian athletes can compete in international competitions is absolutely unacceptable.
Hostile acts against Russia
will not go unanswered
and will have lasting negative consequences for those responsible.
