Opening remarks
by Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov during talks with Minister of Foreign Affairs of Saudi Arabia Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud
(Moscow, September 8, 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
We are pleased to welcome you to Moscow.
We highly appreciate that, despite the difficult situation in the region, you found the time to come to Moscow.
This underscores the special nature of our relations
, which are not dependent on the current political climate and are focused on implementing the agreements reached by our Leaders.
We particularly value this visit and
the opportunity to hold substantive talks
, especially given that this year marks
the centenary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between Russia and Saudi Arabia
.
This is a good opportunity to take a detailed look at the state of Russia-Saudi relations.
They continue to expand year by year in trade and the economy, investment, culture and humanitarian cooperation. We will also discuss regional affairs.
🤝
Russia is sincerely interested in helping advance efforts to calm the situation
, as well as in addressing other regional issues of mutual interest.
