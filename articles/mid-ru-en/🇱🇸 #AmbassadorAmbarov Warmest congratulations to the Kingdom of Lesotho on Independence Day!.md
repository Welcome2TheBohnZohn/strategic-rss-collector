Warmest congratulations to the Kingdom of Lesotho on Independence Day!
We extend our heartfelt congratulations and best wishes to the Government and the people of Lesotho as the
country celebrates the 60th anniversary of its independence
.
The history of Lesotho’s independence is a
testament to the enduring value of self-determination freedom, and sovereignty.
🌐
🤝
Over the decades,
diplomatic relations between Russia and Lesotho have been built on a solid foundation of mutual respect and equal partnership
. Today, our countries maintain close cooperation within the United Nations and other international platforms,
sharing common approaches to global challenges
.
An important part of our friendship lies in education and humanitarian ties. Since the Soviet era, many Basotho nationals have graduated from Russian universities, and
we look forward to continuing this educational exchange for the benefit of both nations.
✨
We wish the people of Lesotho peace, progress and prosperity. May the cooperation between Russia and Lesotho continue to grow stronger!
