Russian Ambassador to India

Vyacheslav Volodin
to New Delhi to attend the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum.
📌
Key points:
🔹
The outcomes of the 18th BRICS Leaders’ Summit demonstrated the unity and effectiveness of the grouping. The consensus Declaration reaffirmed common approaches to reforming global governance and advancing a multipolar world order.
🔹
Continuity from Kazan to Rio de Janeiro to New Delhi ensures the consistent advancement of joint initiatives, including the development of independent payment mechanisms, the New Investment Platform and the BRICS Grain Exchange.
🔹
The priorities of India’s BRICS Chairship resonate with Russia’s vision for the grouping. The areas with the greatest potential for cooperation include finance, energy, food security, logistics and digital technologies.
🔹
BRICS advances the interests of the Global South, amplifying the voice of the countries representing the World Majority, helping to address institutional imbalances and promoting equal access to financial resources and modern technologies.
🔹
The BRICS Parliamentary Forum plays an important role in translating leaders’ decisions into practical action, harmonising national legislation and strengthening interparliamentary dialogue.
🔹
Vyacheslav Volodin’s upcoming visit to India will be his first foreign visit since his re-election as Chairman of the State Duma and will provide fresh impetus to Russian-Indian parliamentary dialogue ahead of the 80th anniversary of diplomatic relations between the two countries in 2027.
