Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
sent greetings
to the organisers, participants and guests of the
Dialogue of Young Diplomats of the Greater Eurasian Partnership
(August 29, 2026)
✍️
I extend my warmest welcome to the organisers, participants, and guests of the
Dialogue of Young Diplomats of the Greater Eurasian Partnership
countries in Kazan.
The current stage of global development, marked by the
strengthening of multipolar principles
in international affairs, presents vast opportunities for building new and more
equitable mechanisms
of global economic cooperation.
Russian President Vladimir Putin’s initiative to establish a
#GreaterEurasianPartnership
is intended to
consolidate integration processes across the continent,
facilitate the removal of barriers in trade and investment, and foster more sophisticated,
balanced, and reliable economic ties
throughout the vast
#Eurasia
.
Young foreign policy professionals will have a vital role to play in this ambitious endeavour. Before long, it will be your responsibility to coordinate the strategies and plans of your respective countries in
pursuit of the wellbeing and prosperity
of our shared continent.
I am confident that today’s meeting will provide a
constructive platform for dialogue
, enable the establishment of meaningful professional contacts, and contribute to further
strengthening the
bonds of friendship and trust
among our countries.
🤝
I wish you fruitful discussions and all the very best.
Sergey Lavrov
