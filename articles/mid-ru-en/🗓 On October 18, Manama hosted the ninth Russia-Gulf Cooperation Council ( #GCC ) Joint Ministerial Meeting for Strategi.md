On October 18, Manama
hosted
the ninth
Russia-Gulf Cooperation Council (
#GCC
) Joint Ministerial Meeting for Strategic Dialogue, co-chaired by Foreign Minister of Russia Sergey Lavrov and Foreign Minister of Bahrain, the current GCC Chair, Abdullatif bin Rashid Al Zayani.
The meeting
was attended by the Foreign Ministers of
Kuwait
and
Saudi Arabia
, Ministers of State of
Qatar
and
the UAE
, Deputy Foreign Minister of
Oman
, and
GCC Secretary-General Jasem Albudaiwi
.

In his remarks at the Plenary Session
,
Sergey Lavrov
noted that, over its 15 years, the
#RussiaGCC
Strategic Dialogue has established itself as
an effective and valuable mechanism
for constructively comparing assessments of key regional and international issues and discussing priorities in trade, economic, investment, cultural and humanitarian cooperation.
The Minister also stressed that advancing Russia’s multifaceted partnerships with the member states of this influential organisation is
one of our key foreign policy priorities
.
Sergey Lavrov noted
the continued intensity of political dialogue with the GCC Member States at the highest and high levels
, particularly the frank and trusting contacts between President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
and the Leaders of the Council’s Member States. He expressed
satisfaction with deepening business cooperation
and the steady growth in trade and mutual investment.
🤝
In this context, Sergey Lavrov and his counterparts from the Gulf states
stressed their shared interest in further diversifying mutually beneficial ties
between Russia and the GCC countries
, drawing on the coordinating role of intergovernmental commissions.
The participants held substantive discussions on t
he situation in the Middle East and North Africa
, including military and political tensions in the Persian Gulf. They reaffirmed that
there is no alternative to resolving existing differences through political and diplomatic means
, in strict compliance with international law and with full regard for the legitimate interests of all states involved in the conflict.
They stressed
the imperative of ensuring safe, secure and unimpeded navigation through international waterways
, including the Strait of Hormuz and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.
Deep concern was expressed over rising tensions in the southern Arabian Peninsula
. The participants noted that normalising the situation in Yemen requires
the prompt launch of direct dialogue among all influential Yemeni political forces under UN auspices
, based on the settlement roadmap developed with Saudi Arabia’s direct involvement.
In discussing the Palestinian-Israeli conflict, the participants stressed
the need to create conditions for a lasting ceasefire in the Gaza Strip
and provide immediate humanitarian assistance to the enclave’s civilian population. They emphasised the importance of normalising the situation and ending illegal settlement activity in the West Bank. They reaffirmed
their shared support for a comprehensive settlement of
the Palestinian question
on the established international legal basis.
Sergey Lavrov set out in detail Russia’s assessments of developments surrounding Ukraine
. He reaffirmed Russia’s firm commitment to a comprehensive and lasting settlement of the Ukrainian crisis, which is possible only through
the complete and irreversible elimination of its
root causes
.
👉
It was agreed to hold the tenth Ministerial Meeting of the
#RussiaGCC
Strategic Dialogue in Russia in 2027.
