On
October 24, 2026,
Russian Ambassador to Indonesia
Sergei Tolchenov
delivered a speech at a seminar entitled
“From the Russian Language to Cultural Connections: How Language Opens Doors to Russia”
, held as part of the International Cultural Exhibition at the University of Indonesia (UI).
🇷🇺
In his remarks,
Sergei Tolchenov
emphasized the importance of promoting the Russian language, literature and traditions abroad, highly commended cooperation with UI in this field, and encouraged students to actively participate in international youth events held in Russia.
🖼️
The seminar was organized with the support of UI’s Faculty of Humanities and focused on
fostering international dialogue
in the fields of culture, student exchanges and interuniversity cooperation.
