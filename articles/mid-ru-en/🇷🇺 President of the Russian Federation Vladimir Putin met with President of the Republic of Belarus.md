It seems that we saw each other not so long ago, but, as I always say in such situations, the
dynamics of our relationship

main area of cooperation, trade and economic relations
,

further 12,5% percent

4.5 billion in direct investment from Russia,

namely,
high
technology

Belarus has preserved and built upon much of what was created back in the days of the Soviet Union

We are very glad to see you. I know that you were already quite busy yesterday working within the
Government of the Russian Federation,
with your colleagues and mine from the Russian Government. Now, in the course of our discussion today, we shall summarise some of the outcomes. If there is anything that needs to be added or amended, we shall do just so.
