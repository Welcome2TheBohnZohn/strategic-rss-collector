# : Russia's Ministry of Finance On November 23, Moscow hosted the 12th Russia-China Financial Dialogue

Published: 2026-11-23 18:05:42

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33568

Russia's Ministry of Finance
:
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
On November 23, Moscow hosted
the 12th Russia-China Financial Dialogue
, co-chaired by Russia's Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance
Anton Siluanov
and China's Minister of Finance
Lan Fo’an
.
🤝
The Sides exchanged views on key areas of bilateral economic and fiscal cooperation
. They discussed the macroeconomic situation, ways to ensure fiscal sustainability under current conditions, shared experiences in introducing modern technologies in public finance management, and outlined steps to coordinate their efforts at multilateral financial forums.
💬
Anton Siluanov
, opening the meeting:
Good relations between Russia and China are a valuable asset that must be further strengthened. Over the past 10 months, bilateral trade has grown to nearly $240 billion.
More than 90 joint investment projects are being implemented in energy, transport, industry and high-tech sectors. Tourism, cultural and scientific exchanges are also expanding.
On the sidelines of the Financial Dialogue,
Anton Siluanov
and
Lan Fo’an
also held
a bilateral meeting
to discuss cooperation on matters of mutual interest.
📄
Following the 12th Russia-China Financial Dialogue,
a joint statement
was adopted
.
