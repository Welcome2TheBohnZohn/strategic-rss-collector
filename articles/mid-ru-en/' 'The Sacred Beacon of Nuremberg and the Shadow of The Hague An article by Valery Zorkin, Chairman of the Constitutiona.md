✍️
80 years ago, the International Military Tribunal in
#Nuremberg
delivered its verdict against 24 top-ranking leaders of Nazi Germany.
Most defendants were found guilty of the terrible crimes
against peace and humanity, as well as war crimes. Twelve Nazi leaders —
sentenced
to
death
by hanging, while the others
sentenced
for
life
or lengthy terms of imprisonment.
It was the very
unprecedented nature of Nazi ideology
that made it necessary for taking equally unprecedented political and legal measures — the establishment of the
Nuremberg and Tokyo Trials.
<...>
❗️
It was the 'Judgment of Mankind' on Hitler's Nazism
— the most dangerous manifestation of ideological, political and military barbarism.
A 'Sacred Beacon' in Nuremberg served as bearing the
Promethean flame of international criminal justice
. Yet, imperfect, with the retroactive application of law, it nevertheless shone,
illuminating human path
towards the ideals of universal justice.
<...>
The verdict of the Nuremberg Trials was based on an
immense body of irrefutable evidence
, firmly established and thoroughly examined. A vast archive of testimony was compiled.
The voices of the victims were heard loud and clear
— that was the enduring ethical significance of Nuremberg, which was both an act of justice and an act of remembrance. <...>
The Nuremberg Trials appeared before the world like Athena, born fully armed from the head of Zeus. The
four Victorious Powers
set about establishing a new legal order amid the
ruins of defeated statehood
and bringing the vanquished to justice.
The legitimacy of the Nuremberg proceedings and the
binding force of its verdict was based on the collapse of the Nazi state,
as well as on the Allied law established by the
Instrument of Surrender.
The Tribunal's legitimacy, meanwhile, was rooted in
natural law and universal moral values,
above all, justice. It was founded on the conviction that certain
crimes are so monstrous that they violate the very idea of humanity
(
delicta juris gentium
), and that such crimes must be punished under all circumstances.
👉
That fundamental principle made it possible not only to
cut through the Gordian knot of fascist evil
and punish those responsible without departing from the bounds of the law, but also to establish a hierarchy of values in which law stands above force and crimes against humanity are subject to
#NoStatuteOfLimitations
. <...>
Nuremberg was an act of retribution
and a defining moment in establishing the legal foundations of the post-war world. The Tribunal became a hammer brought down upon a criminal Nazi statehood and its legal system.
***
❗️
It was for the first time in history, categories of crimes were established that would become the 'alpha' and 'omega' of international criminal law.
Article 6 of the Charter of the Nuremberg International Military Tribunal (IMT)
defined three categories of crimes
:
1. Crimes against Peace;
2. War Crimes;
3. Crimes against Humanity.
A war of aggression was declared the "supreme international crime".
The events in Nuremberg were perceived by the international community as the
restoration
of justice from the chaos of violence
and arbitrariness. In the eyes of millions of victims and billions of people around the world, Nuremberg became
a ritual of purification,
one that united humanity. <...>
👉
Punishment was inescapable
<...>. All this created a sense of the triumph of law and moral catharsis. The deeper meaning of Nuremberg can be traced to Plato's understanding of punishment — a criminal is held accountable not by the right of force, but by the force of law.
