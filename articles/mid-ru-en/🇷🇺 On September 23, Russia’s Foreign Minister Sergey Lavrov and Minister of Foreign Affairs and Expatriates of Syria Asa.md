On September 23, Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Minister of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates of Syria Asaad Al-Shaibani
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
👉
Sergey Lavrov's opening remarks
The Ministers discussed in detail
the development of Russia-Syria relations
, including maintaining regular political dialogue and expanding mutually beneficial practical cooperation across various fields.
The Russian Side
reaffirmed its principled support for Syria’s unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity
. It stressed the need to coordinate international efforts to advance Syria’s post-conflict recovery and assist its leadership in overcoming the challenges of the transition period.
The Ministers also discussed
current regional issues of mutual interest
, focusing on unresolved crises along Syria’s borders.
The Sides agreed to maintain contacts at various levels, including in multilateral forums.
