🎙
Remarks and answers to media questions
by Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov following
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
(New York, September 26, 2026)
Key points
:
• Without a doubt, the UN needs to be adapted to the new realities, including through
reform of the Security Council
by adding
representatives from Asia, Africa and Latin America
.
We cannot envisage any additional seats for Western countries.
We cannot allow that, because
the West is already overrepresented on the UN Security Council
.
• We expect
the new Secretary-General
to
represent the interests of all humanity, not just the “golden billion,”
and to meet all the criteria set out in
the UN Charter
, including
impartiality, objectivity and proper performance of the functions
of the Organisation’s chief administrative officer.
•
I have
repeatedly set out
the sequence of events in the development of the Ukraine crisis
. There is no lack of trust on our part. Moreover, we continued
the bilateral talks in Istanbul
, and
trilateral talks were also held in Abu Dhabi
with US participation.
None of these rounds of negotiations were terminated by us
, but by those who now claim that
“the Russians are avoiding contacts and have nothing to say”
.
• We believe that
the Islamabad Memorandum
was
, as they say,
a fair deal
. Since it was signed at the highest level, it was presumably done consciously.
So why not return to it?
•
As for drafting
the Eurasian Charter of Diversity and Multipolarity in the 21st Century
, we are not setting any artificial deadlines
. At the same time, we certainly would not want this work to drag on, given that
our Eurasian continent badly needs a continent-wide framework
open to all countries and organisations of the continent without exception and free from any external interference.
•All the damage being inflicted on commercial shipping is
the result of Ukrainian terrorist attacks
, not only in
the Black Sea
but also in
the Caspian Sea
.
• The trend towards
outright Nazism
is evident not only in Europe’s support for the Kiev regime, but also in what I mentioned in my statement. German chancellor
Friedrich Merz
has
said
– more than once, so this was no slip of the tongue – that his goal is to ensure that Germany
“once again becomes the leading military power in Europe”
. Does he understand the meaning of the words
“once again”
in that context? These are
very dangerous trends
, prompting many analysts to speak of
attempts to create a “Fourth Reich”
.
• We maintain
a regular dialogue
with the administration of US President Donald Trump in various formats, and
that alone is already a sign of normality
. If relations are reduced to issuing propaganda statements every morning condemning Russia and demanding that Russia suffer a
“strategic defeat,”
then
that is not diplomacy
.
• In today’s world,
no country
– not even one as powerful as the US –
can solve all global problems single-handedly, especially at the expense of others
.
👉
I am confident that reality will teach our American partners to start thinking in terms of
#Multilateralism
. They are not the only economic and military power in the world, nor the only independent actor on the international stage.
• I am frankly tired of discussing this issue [European leaders calling for preparations for war with Russia]. We have stated our position many times, so I have nothing to add. President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
has recently reaffirmed it in St Petersburg
:
we are not going to wage war against Europe.
The very idea is absurd and nonsensical
.
☝️
Yet it is Europe that is preparing for war with us.
