🎙
Remarks and answers to media questions
by Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov following
the 21st East Asia Summit
(Manila, November 17, 2026)
Key points
:
• I believe today's event was
useful
. It is mainly
European countries, Australia
and
Japan
that are politicising this forum.
•
An entire architecture has been built around
#ASEAN
–
the ASEAN Regional Forum
,
the ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting Plus
(ADMM-Plus), etc –
and everyone has always recognised ASEAN’s centrality within it
. Our Western colleagues are now trying
to undermine and dismantle
this inclusive, open and consensus-based architecture. They are trying to draw individual ASEAN countries into
exclusive military blocs
.
•
NATO members are actively expanding their presence in this region
, making little secret of their goal:
to contain the People’s Republic of China
. Everyone understands this. Beyond moving elements of NATO’s military infrastructure here, they have even devised a new concept: establishing
an Indo-Pacific Treaty Organisation
modelled on NATO.
•
Nothing good has ever come of NATO’s presence.
The Alliance’s
invasions of Iraq
,
the former Yugoslavia
and
Libya
, and its many other ventures, speak for themselves. Whenever NATO has operated beyond its members’ territory, these have been
military missions of a distinctly aggressive nature
. Nowhere has NATO’s armed intervention improved the situation. On the contrary,
it has left deep crises everywhere
, with neighbouring countries bearing the brunt of dealing with the consequences.
•
We sensed a desire among our ASEAN friends to uphold the founding principles
underpinning both the Association itself and its mechanisms for engagement with partners, including the East Asia Summits. If everyone treats the founders of this process –
the ASEAN countries
– with respect, all these meetings will be far more productive.
• Japanese PM
Sanae Takaichi
has already announced that she is
no longer satisfied
with the three non-nuclear principles
enshrined in
Japan’s Constitution
.
This will trigger a chain reaction
. There could be a domino effect. Many understand this, including ASEAN members. We will work with them
to prevent these adverse trends from materialising
.
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
🇺🇸
[
The trilateral meeting of the Leaders of Russia, China and the US
, scheduled for November 19 in Shenzhen],
is useful under any circumstances
, because these are three nuclear powers. Two of them are also the world’s largest economies. These countries have wide-ranging interests across the globe and interesting initiatives to offer.
Simply listening to one another is useful in itself
, particularly as no meeting in this format has ever been held before.
• As for
the agenda
of the meeting between
Vladimir Putin, Xi Jinping
and
Donald Trump
, it has been agreed.
I will not comment
on what the other participants are saying about the substance of the forthcoming meeting.
That is not our practice
. It is broadly clear what the Leaders will discuss. They will have an opportunity for a free exchange of views. Be patient.
There is very little time left to wait.
• Why do the Europeans want a ceasefire in Ukraine? They want to deploy “stabilisation forces” immediately. They have been warned a thousand times: President
Vladimir Putin
, I and many other officials
have said
that
any deployment of “stabilisation forces” would mean Europe’s direct and unequivocal entry into the war.
• On the Ukraine crisis, I would like to reiterate what President
Vladimir Putin
recently emphasised
when commenting on the
“territorial issue”
: we are not fighting for territory,
we are fighting for people who have been deprived of the basic rights guaranteed by numerous documents.
