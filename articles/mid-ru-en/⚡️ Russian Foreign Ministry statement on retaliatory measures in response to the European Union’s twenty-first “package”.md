⚡️
Russian Foreign Ministry statement on retaliatory measures in response to the European Union’s twenty-first “package” of anti-Russian sanctions
(September 18, 2026)
💬
The EU continues to pursue a policy of unilateral restrictive measures that is illegitimate under international law
. On July 23, the EU Council approved its twenty-first “package” of anti-Russian sanctions aimed at inflicting damage on our country.
In response to the EU’s unlawful decisions,
Russia has substantially expanded the list of representatives
of EU institutions, EU member states and several other European countries supporting Brussels’ anti-Russian policy
who are
banned from entering our country
under
Federal Law No. 114-FZ of August 15, 1996, “On the Procedure for Exit from and Entry into the Russian Federation”
.
🚫
The list includes individuals:
• involved in decisions to provide
military assistance to the Kiev regime
;
• engaged in activities aimed at
undermining the territorial integrity
of the Russian Federation;
• responsible for
imposing anti-Russian sanctions
;
• responsible for
damaging Russia’s relations
with third countries;
• responsible for
obstructing maritime shipping
serving Russia’s interests;
• involved in
persecuting Russian citizens on fabricated grounds
;
• involved in establishing
pseudo-legal anti-Russian “mechanisms”
within the Council of Europe;
• advocating the
unlawful seizure of Russian state assets
or the use of profits generated by them.
Russia has also imposed restrictive measures on “civil activists” and academics in European countries
who
take a hostile anti-Russian stance
, as well as members of EU national parliaments and the European Parliament
who voted for anti-Russian resolutions and legislation
.
Despite Brussels’ destructive policy,
Russia will remain firmly committed to its foreign policy course
, aimed at defending national interests, safeguarding the rights and freedoms of Russian citizens, and helping shape a fair
#MultipolarWorld
.
❗️
Any further moves by the EU to expand its unilateral anti-Russian sanctions will continue to meet with an appropriate response from Russia.
