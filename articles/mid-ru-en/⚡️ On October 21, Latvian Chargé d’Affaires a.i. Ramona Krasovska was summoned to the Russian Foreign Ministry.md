# ⚡️ On October 21, Latvian Chargé d’Affaires a.i. Ramona Krasovska was summoned to the Russian Foreign Ministry.

Published: 2026-10-21 12:15:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32886

⚡️
On October 21, Latvian Chargé d’Affaires a.i. Ramona Krasovska
was summoned
to the Russian Foreign Ministry.
A strong protest was expressed
over the Latvian MFA demand to dismiss all staff of the social welfare office within
the Consular Section of the Russian Embassy in Riga
.
This jeopardises pension payments and social support for
nearly 6,000 Russian military pensioners and their family members
residing in Latvia.
It was stressed that these actions by the Latvian authorities
violate the country’s obligations
under
the 1994 Russian-Latvian intergovernmental agreement on social protection for Russian military pensioners and their families
.
Russia has always fulfilled its obligations
to Russian military pensioners and their family members living in Latvia, rigorously and in full.
❗️
The Latvian authorities bear full responsibility for this artificially created crisis in military pension payments.
The Latvian side was strongly urged
to strictly observe the letter and spirit of
the intergovernmental agreement
. A corresponding note from the Russian Foreign Ministry was delivered.
