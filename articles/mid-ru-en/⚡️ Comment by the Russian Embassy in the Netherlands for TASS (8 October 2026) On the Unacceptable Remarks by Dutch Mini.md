🔹

we will not tolerate attempts to address Russia in the language of threats and ultimatums. Any encroachment upon the security and sovereignty of the Russian Federation will be met with an immediate, swift and crushing response.

We expect the Dutch authorities to provide official and unequivocal clarification regarding the belligerent and irresponsible rhetoric of their Defence Minister.
