# 🎙 Statement by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the restricted-format meeting of the CIS Council of Heads of State

Published: 2026-10-09 10:31:58

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32656

🎙
Statement
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at the restricted-format meeting of the CIS Council of Heads of State
(Turkmenbashi, October 9, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
Over the past three and a half decades,
the Commonwealth of Independent States
(
#CIS
) has come a long way, establishing itself as
a respected regional integration association
.
It is thanks to the CIS that we have managed to preserve, and in many areas even strengthen,
the economic, social, cultural and humanitarian ties our peoples
developed over centuries of living in a single state.
The economy has always been, and remains, one of our priorities for cooperation.
The CIS countries work closely together in trade, investment and high technology. Of course, we would like to move faster and work more effectively in this area. That is certainly true. Nevertheless,
progress is being made.
📈
Notably,
Russia’s trade with its CIS partners exceeded $110 billion in 2025 and grew by a further 12% in the first half of this year
.
Our countries have also established effective coordination in tackling
financial and economic offences, money laundering and corruption
. We are implementing
joint programmes
to combat the criminal use of digital technologies, organised crime, drug trafficking, terrorism and extremism. Our law enforcement agencies and special services maintain
close operational contacts
.
A number of useful new decisions on
criminal justice, cybercrime prevention and border protection
have been submitted for approval at this Summit, and
Russia supports the
m.
🌐
I am pleased to note that
the CIS countries unanimously support the establishment of a fair
#MultipolarWorld
order
based on the universally recognised principles of international law and the central role of the UN.
Humanitarian ties are an important part of our partnership within the CIS
. We maintain intensive exchanges in science and education, culture and tourism, healthcare and environmental protection, sport and youth policy.
As for the environment,
thanks to our hosts
, who are chairing this meeting, we will also discuss
the situation in the Caspian Sea today
– a pressing issue for many of us.
🇷🇺
The Russian language is a powerful unifying force across the vast CIS region
, as has also been noted today. In 2026,
the International Organisation for the Russian Language
began its work.
Its first ministerial meeting
took place in Moscow, setting out the main areas of work to preserve our shared Russian-language cultural and humanitarian heritage and promote the Russian language worldwide.
I have repeatedly stressed
the importance of joint efforts by the CIS countries to protect our shared history and prevent its falsification
. Young people must know the truth about our peoples’ achievements during the Soviet era and the heroic feats of our fathers and grandfathers during
the Great Patriotic War
.
More broadly, promoting a positive image of the CIS among the public, in the media and on the international stage is a particularly important task
. We expect the relevant decision being approved at today’s meeting to give fresh impetus to this work.
🤝
Following our established tradition, dear friends,
I would like to invite you all to St Petersburg on December 21
. We traditionally gather for
a friendly meeting of CIS Leaders
ahead of the New Year, giving us an opportunity to discuss current issues and future plans in an informal setting.
