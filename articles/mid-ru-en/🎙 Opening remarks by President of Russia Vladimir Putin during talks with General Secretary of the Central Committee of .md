Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin during talks with General Secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam, President of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam To Lam
(Moscow, September 9, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We are very pleased to welcome you and your distinguished delegation to Moscow.
Quite recently, on September 2,
the Vietnamese people celebrated Independence Day
. I would like to note that it was precisely during Vietnam’s struggle for sovereignty that the special ties of friendship, mutual understanding and cooperation between our peoples were forged.
In this regard, I would also like to note that over the years of independence and full sovereignty,
Vietnam has achieved impressive results under the leadership of your Party and the country’s leadership
. This means that those who fought for Vietnam, for its independence and sovereignty, did not fight in vain, and their sacrifices were not in vain.
☝️
Building on the sovereignty it won,
the Vietnamese people continue to achieve
, I would stress once again,
impressive results
.
We are developing our bilateral ties
. Trade continues to grow: in 2025, it increased by
nearly 6%
. In the first half of 2026, the pace of trade growth
virtually doubled
.
We also continue to work across all other areas
, including culture, education and security. We have time-tested projects, as well as new and promising initiatives.
Comrade General Secretary, Comrade President,
we are very pleased to see you
. I know that yesterday
you had a very busy working day
, with many substantive meetings. I was briefed on some of them just now on the way to this hall.
I am confident that today’s work and the signing of the relevant documents will provide
a solid foundation for the further development of our special strategic relations
.
