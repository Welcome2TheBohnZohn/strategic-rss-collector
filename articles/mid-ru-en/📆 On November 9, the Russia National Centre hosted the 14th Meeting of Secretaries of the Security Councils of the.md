On November 9,
the Russia National Centre
hosted the 14th Meeting of Secretaries of the Security Councils of the
#CIS
Member States.
Secretary of Russia’s Security Council
Sergey Shoigu
chaired the meeting.
✉️
President of Russia
Vladimir Putin
sent
a message of greetings
to the participants:
Today, with the geopolitical situation complex and conflicts intensifying in several regions of the world, it is important to make full use of your councils’ considerable capacity for analysis and forecasting to strengthen the security and sovereignty of the
#CIS
Member States and maintain stability throughout the Commonwealth.
🤝
The participants discussed
current security challenges and threats across the CIS
and
joint measures
they could take to address them. They also reviewed the outcomes of
the Peoples of Russia International Week
.
💬
Secretary of Russia’s Security Council
Sergey Shoigu
:
The countries of the Global South and East are playing an increasingly influential role in world politics and the global economy, shaping the course of international relations and the contours of the security architecture. In the current circumstances, it is important to work together to put mechanisms in place so that we are ready to deal with the consequences of international crises as they arise.
Russia has proposed that
#BRICS
and the
#SCO
establish emergency stockpiles to help people in partner countries in the event of an emergency.
The proposal is to establish regional hubs at key points in the logistics network to hold vital reserves, primarily food and energy supplies, critical materials, medicines and other essential goods.
