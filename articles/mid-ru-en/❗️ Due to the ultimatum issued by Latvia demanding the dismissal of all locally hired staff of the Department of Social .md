# ❗️ Due to the ultimatum issued by Latvia demanding the dismissal of all locally hired staff of the Department of Social Welfare at the Consular Department of the Russian Embassy in

Published: 2026-11-24 18:01:17

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33583

❗️
Due to the ultimatum issued by Latvia demanding the dismissal of all locally hired staff of the Department of Social Welfare at the Consular Department of the Russian Embassy in Riga (hereinafter the DSW),
on November 23, the Russian Foreign Ministry
summoned
Latvia’s Chargé d’Affaires ad interim Ramona Krasovska.
The Foreign Ministry explained the nature of the problems that will arise for the diplomatic missions of both countries, to the diplomat –
the main problem being the inability to continue paying military pensions
as they are processed by the DSW staff as per the Agreement Between the Government of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Republic of Latvia on the Social Protection of Russian Military Pensioners and Their Families Residing in the Republic of Latvia, signed on April 30, 1994 (hereinafter the “Agreement”).
The diplomat received
a corresponding note detailing Russia’s initiatives
regarding the resumption of talks on the employment of locally hired staff by the Latvian Embassy in Moscow in strict compliance with Russian law.
It was also proposed to the Latvian side that
consultations may be conducted on potential amendments to the Agreement and clarification of the status of the DSW staff
, provided that their dismissal would make it impossible to continue with the mechanism introduced by the Agreement and would considerably hinder the fulfilment of the obligations to
almost 6,000 military pensioners
and their family members (both Latvian and Russian citizens) residing in Latvia.
☝️
The official Riga was addressed with
an appeal to evaluate the humanitarian consequences
of the step it has requested, considering that
the pension is the only source of income
for many of the beneficiaries, and it is important to prevent any serious social issues in the lives of the elderly residents who belong to one of the most vulnerable population categories.
