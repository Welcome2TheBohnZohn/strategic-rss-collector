Published: 2026-10-17 11:02:02

✉️
Russia's President
Vladimir Putin
extended his greetings
to President of Indonesia Prabowo Subianto on his 75th birthday
(October 17, 2026)
💬
You have earned the
well-deserved respect
of your compatriots and a high standing on the international stage as an experienced and far-sighted politician who has made
a significant contribution to Indonesia’s development
and protecting its interests worldwide.
We highly value your principled commitment to further strengthening
friendly relations
between Russia and Indonesia.
Our
productive contacts
help foster
constructive cooperation
across key areas of the partnership between Moscow and Jakarta within the framework of the
#UN
,
#BRICS
, the
#RussiaASEAN
Dialogue, and other multilateral forums.
🤝
Of course,
we will continue our consistent joint efforts
to address current issues on the bilateral, regional and global agendas, for the benefit of our nations and in the interests of international stability and security.
Vladimir Putin
