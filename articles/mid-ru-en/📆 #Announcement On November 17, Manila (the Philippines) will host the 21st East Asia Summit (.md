#Announcement
📆
On November 17, Manila (the Philippines)
will host
the 21st East Asia Summit (
#EAS
). The Russian delegation will be headed by Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
.
Established in 2005,
the EAS serves
as a forum for dialogue among leaders of Asia-Pacific countries
on a broad range of political and economic issues of common interest, as well as ways to advance practical cooperation across various fields.
Since its inception,
the East Asia Summit
has identified
six priority areas of cooperation:
• Energy
• Finance
• Emergency response
• Healthcare
• Education
• Regional connectivity
☝️
However, substantive work has been carried out in only some of these areas.
***
🌐
The EAS
brings together the members
of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations
(
#ASEAN
) – Brunei, Vietnam, Indonesia, Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste and the Philippines – and
ASEAN’s dialogue partners
: Russia (since 2010), Australia, India, China, the Republic of Korea, New Zealand, the United States and Japan.
The ASEAN Chair also chairs the East Asia Summit
: the Philippines in 2026 and Singapore in 2027.
👉
The agenda of the 21st EAS will focus on
current issues in regional development
and
prospects for expanding practical cooperation
.
Russia advocates
broadening the EAS’s tools for practical cooperation,
with an emphasis on implementing Leaders’ decisions.
Our initiatives include
coordinating region-wide efforts to combat pandemics
, with the possibility of establishing a collective response mechanism for epidemic threats, and
setting up a standing consultation channel on tourism
. These proposals are reflected in relevant Leaders’ documents.
At
the 14th EAS Foreign Ministers’ Meeting
(Vientiane, July 27, 2024),
Russia proposed establishing cooperation on the development of remote areas.
Our country has extensive experience in this field, both in implementing national projects and in multilateral cooperation, including within the
#SCO
and
#APEC
.
Russia supports building a lasting network of links among Eurasian platforms
by bringing together the development potential of key regional mechanisms – the
#SCO
,
#ASEAN
and the
#EAEU
– which share a common commitment to constructive cooperation.
Mutual interest in establishing contacts between
#ASEAN
countries and
#BRICS
is growing
. Inviting Brazil’s leadership to ASEAN summits is becoming
a welcome tradition
: as last year, President of Brazil
Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva
will attend the ASEAN Leaders’ meeting as a guest of the Chair.
🤝
The BRICS presence within the EAS continues to grow
, encompassing Russia, India, China, Indonesia, which joined BRICS in January 2025, and Vietnam, Malaysia and Thailand, which have gained partner-country status.
