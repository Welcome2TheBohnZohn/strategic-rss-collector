Comment by Ambassador of Russia to Canada Oleg Stepanov to The Canadian Press News Agency on recent Prime-Minister Mark Carney's
remarks
(
Ottawa, September 3, 2026
)
❗️
I
am compelled to correct Prime Minister Carney.
Russia was, remains and will remain a member of the Group of Twenty.
As for the fate of the G8 format, it ceased to exist in 2014. Let me recall how this came about. In 2014, Moscow held the presidency of the group.
Following Crimea’s reunification with Russia
, the other seven members decided, for political reasons, to withdraw from the G8 format.
In other words, they excluded themselves.
☝️
The G8 no longer exists. And that is perhaps for the best.
We have no interest in reviving it
.
In contemporary international affairs, it is
new-type formats such as the
Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
and
BRICS
, as well as the aforementioned Group of Twenty, that command the greatest authority.
The G7, by contrast, has long since become a grouping that exists on paper only, perpetuated merely for its own sake. Whatever prestige it may once have enjoyed no longer carries any weight in contemporary international relations.
Such is the reality.
