# On November 13 and 16, a book of condolences was opened at the Russian Embassy in memory of the late Ambassador

Published: 2026-11-19 01:03:03

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33467

On November 13 and 16, a book of condolences was opened at the Russian Embassy in memory of the late Ambassador
Alexander Darchiev.
From the moment the doors opened until the very last minute people kept coming to sign the book and share their precious memories of the Ambassador.
State Department officials left messages of condolences, noting that Alexander Darchiev or, as he was lovingly called by his family and colleagues, Alexander Nikitich (using his patronymic), was an outstanding diplomat of highest qualification.
Many members of the diplomatic corps, our Russian compatriots and American friends visited the Embassy and shared genuine words of sorrow. Dozens of e-mails streamed in each with warm reminiscences and thoughts. Russian flag has been put on display on the triumphal arch in Atlanta (Millennium Gate Museum) in memory of the Ambassador at the initiative of President of the U.S. Commission of Fine Arts Rodney Mims Cook-Jr.
We thank everyone who voiced and left their condolences for the Ambassador’s passing.
The book with all the messages will be presented to the family of Alexander Nikitich.
May his memory live on!
