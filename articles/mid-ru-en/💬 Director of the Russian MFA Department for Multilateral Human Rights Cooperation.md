Director of the Russian MFA Department for Multilateral Human Rights Cooperation
Grigory Lukyantsev
:
On November 19 in New York,
the Third Committee
of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
adopted
the annual resolution
put forward by the Group of 77 and China, entitled
“A global call for concrete action for the elimination of racism, racial discrimination, xenophobia and related intolerance and the comprehensive implementation of and follow-up to the Durban Declaration and Programme of Action”.
The resolution
seeks to ensure
full implementation of
the Durban Declaration and Programme of Action
and to prevent politically motivated attempts to revise it. The Declaration and Programme of Action was the outcome document of
the World Conference against Racism
, held in Durban, South Africa, in 2001.
The voting pattern
was similar
to that on Russia’s UN General Assembly
resolution
on combating the glorification of Nazism.
✅
122 votes in favour
❌
16 against
🟨
42 abstentions
The vote once again confirms
what we have noted before
– Western countries find certain issues
particularly “uncomfortable”
.
As in previous years,
Russia supported the initiative and co-sponsored the resolution.
❗️
In its statement, the Russian delegation called on those States that had once again chosen to stand aside or vote against the resolution
to abandon double standards and demonstrate in practice their readiness to combat all forms of racial discrimination and intolerance.
