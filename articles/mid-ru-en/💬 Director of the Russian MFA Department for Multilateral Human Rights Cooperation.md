Director of the Russian MFA Department for Multilateral Human Rights Cooperation
Grigory Lukyantsev

On October 7,
the 63rd session of the UN Human Rights Council
(
#HRC
)
concluded
in Geneva.
Over four and a half weeks,
the Russian delegation
, participating as an observer,
actively promoted
our approaches to the human rights agenda,
advocated
constructive dialogue and
opposed
the politicisation of the Council’s work.
A highlight was
the Joint Statement
initiated by Russia
on behalf of 11 countries to mark this year’s
60th anniversary of the adoption of
the International Covenants on Human Rights
.
⚠️
The Session once again demonstrated
the HRC’s continued degradation
at the hands of a familiar group of Western countries and
its transformation into an instrument for pressuring “unwelcome” states
that dare to pursue independent domestic and foreign policies.
Russia’s firm footing and continued prominence on the international stage remain
a source of frustration for certain “beacons of democracy”
, which seek to harm our country by any means available.
At
the 63rd Session
, Western countries focused on pushing through
an anti-Russian resolution extending the mandate of the Special Rapporteur on the situation of human rights in Russia
. The EU initiative was overtly anti-Russian and bore no relation to reality.
👉
As a result, a majority of HRC members
–
26 countries – did not support it
.
In their desire to punish Russia,
our opponents did not hesitate to misuse the HRC’s agenda.
Once again, the Council adopted a resolution on technical assistance to Georgia aimed at advancing territorial claims to Abkhazia and South Ossetia.
***
Against this backdrop,
the events organised by the Russian delegation
during the Session met a clear need for reliable information about the situation in Russia and in
the special military operation
zone.

,
Ambassador-at-Large of the Russian Foreign Ministry for
#KievRegimeCrimes

his statements

, presenting
photographic and video evidence

•
Mikhail Melekh
,
Russian Foreign Minister's Special Representative for International Cooperation in Ensuring the Right to Freedom of Religion

to

Deputy Chairman of the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Crimea

manifestations of racism by the Kiev regime

Yana Lantratova
