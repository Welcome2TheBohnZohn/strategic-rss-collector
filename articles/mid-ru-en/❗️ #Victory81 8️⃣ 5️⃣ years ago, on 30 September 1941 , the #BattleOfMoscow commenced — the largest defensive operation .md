#Victory81
8️⃣
5️⃣
years ago, on
30 September 1941
, the
#BattleOfMoscow
commenced — the largest defensive operation carried out by the Red Army during the initial stage of the
#GreatPatrioticWar
to
defend the capital of our Motherland
against the Nazi invaders.
The Nazi troops, which sought to seize the city within the shortest possible timeframe, were completely destroyed by the Red Army.
One of the key battles of
#WW2
ended in a
major and decisive victory for our people.
Despite their considerable superiority in manpower and military hardware, the
Nazi invaders suffered a crushing defeat
, which played a major role in paving the way for the eventual collapse of the Third Reich.
❗️
It was precisely near Moscow that the myth of the invincibility of the Nazi war machine was shattered once and for all.
Hitler’s blitzkrieg strategy
, which had previously been successfully employed by the aggressor in the occupation of a number of European countries (Poland, Denmark, the Netherlands, France and Belgium),
completely failed on the Soviet soil.
***
By the end of September 1941, the Nazis had
occupied the entire Baltic region, Belarus
and
Moldova
, as well as large parts of
Soviet
Ukraine
; they had
sieged Leningrad
and reached the approaches to Moscow. Given the strategic and political importance of the Soviet capital,
Hitler committed a powerful force — 1.8 million Wehrmacht soldiers and officers
, 1,700 tanks and around 1,000 aircraft. The Nazi command planned to seize Moscow within the first three to four months and completely
destroy its population.
The situation on the Eastern Front in September 1941 was extremely difficult. Nazi troops attacked Red Army positions along several axes simultaneously. In just two days,
Guderian’s tank group advanced 80 kilometres
, breaking through the front and encircling a considerable number of Red Army units in the areas of Bryansk and Vyazma.
In these circumstances, a state of siege in Moscow and the surrounding areas was put in effect.
Preparations for urban combat were intensified throughout the city,
while key government and industrial facilities were mined. Ordinary residents of Moscow and cadets of military schools joined Red Army personnel in
defending the city
. For two weeks, Soviet troops held back around twenty Nazi divisions in fierce fighting, making it possible to strengthen the defensive line and bring reserve forces up to Moscow.
❗️
During
#WW2
, no other capital had yet put up such determined resistance to Nazism.
In early December, when the Wehrmacht’s forces had been exhausted, the
Red Army was able to launch a counter-offensive.
The formations of the Western, Kalinin and Southwestern Fronts routed the strike groups of the enemy’s Army Group Centre.
German hordes were pushed back 100–350 kilometres
from the capital, and the Moscow, Tula, Kalinin and Ryazan regions were completely liberated from the enemy.
It was near Moscow that Hitler suffered his first major defeat of the Second World War.
Around one million defenders of the capital of our Motherland were awarded the Medal “For the Defence of Moscow”, while 110 people were awarded the title of
#HeroOfTheSovietUnion
.
🎖
In May 1965, on the occasion of the 20th Anniversary of the Victory, Moscow was awarded the honorary title of
#HeroCity
.
