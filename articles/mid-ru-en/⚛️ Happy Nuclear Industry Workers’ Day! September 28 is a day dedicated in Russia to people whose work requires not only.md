Happy Nuclear Industry Workers’ Day!
🇷🇺
September 28
is a day dedicated in Russia to people whose work requires not only profound knowledge and the highest level of responsibility, but also genuine dedication to their profession.
The nuclear industry is not only about reactors, technologies and highly sophisticated scientific research.
Above all, it is about people
- scientists, engineers, designers, power engineers, construction specialists, radiation safety professionals, doctors, educators, diplomats and thousands of other dedicated professionals whose daily work advances science, energy and industry.
Behind every new project, scientific breakthrough and technological achievement stand
years of hard work, the experience of several generations and a responsibility towards the future
.
Special words of gratitude go to
the veterans of the nuclear industry
, who laid the foundations for its development, and to the young professionals who will carry this work forward and create the technologies of tomorrow.
We wish everyone who has devoted their lives to the nuclear industry
good health, well-being, professional success and new achievements!
May the power of the atom
always serve peace, progress and development
.
