# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's statement on the note of protest issued to Japan on September 3

Published: 2026-09-18 13:00:01

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32100

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
statement
on the note of protest issued to Japan on September 3
(September 18, 2026)
💬
On September 3, a note of protest was issued to the Japanese side in Moscow and Tokyo over the deployment on Kyushu of
US Typhon ground-based systems designed to launch intermediate- and shorter-range missiles
.
The note stressed that
Tokyo had once again taken this step despite repeated Russian demarches explaining our concerns
about the threats posed to national security, regional peace and stability by the deployment of these weapons on the Japanese archipelago.
The note emphasised that Japan’s forward deployment of these systems on its territory is
unacceptable under any pretext
, regardless of the duration or manner of their deployment.
☝️
The Japanese side was required to end this practice and remove the systems from its territory
.
The note also stressed the need for the Japanese leadership
to fully recognise
the outcomes of World War II
, including their territorial aspects
relating to the Southern Kuril Islands, and
abandon its policy of remilitarisation
.
On September 16, during
the meeting with Japanese Ambassador to Moscow Akira Muto
, Deputy Foreign Minister of the Russian Federation
Andrey Rudenko
urged Tokyo to take the contents of the earlier note with
the utmost seriousness
.
Our Chinese partners recently delivered a similar note to the Japanese Foreign Ministry
. They, too, have expressed concern over Tokyo’s policy of remilitarisation, including the deployment of Typhon systems on the Japanese islands.
