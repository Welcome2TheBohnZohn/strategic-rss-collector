# ⚡️ The Presidential Press and Information Office’s Department of Accreditation is now accepting applications for media coverage

Published: 2026-09-18 12:01:40

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32099

⚡️
The Presidential Press and Information Office’s Department of
Accreditation is now accepting applications for media coverage
of the 8th Summit of Heads of State and Government of the
Gas Exporting Countries Forum
(
#GECF
), the third
Russia–Africa Summit
and the
Russia–Africa Economic and Humanitarian Forum
.
The accreditation badge grants access to the international press centre and is valid for the following events:
•
October 27
: Gas Exporting Countries Forum;
•
October 28–29
: Russia–Africa Economic and Humanitarian Forum;
•
October 28–29
: Russia–Africa Summit.
Journalist accreditation is handled via the website
www.kremlin.ru
(
президент.рф
).
👉
To obtain an accreditation badge, applicants must
complete the registration form
.
Accreditation applications are accepted from Russian media outlets registered with Roskomnadzor and from foreign journalists.
❗️
The accreditation deadline is 12:00 pm Moscow time on September 30, 2026.
