# 📃 Following the meeting of the Council of Heads of State of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation,

Published: 2026-09-01 12:45:36

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31754

📃
Following
the meeting
of the Council of Heads of State of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation,
the Bishkek Declaration on the 25th Anniversary of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
was signed
(Bishkek, September 1, 2026)
🤝
We, the Leaders of the Member States of
the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
(
#SCO
), gathered for a regular meeting of the Council of Heads of State and marking the 25th anniversary of the SCO,
reaffirm our firm commitment
to the goals and principles enshrined in
the SCO Charter
, as well as to further strengthening good-neighbourliness, friendship and effective multifaceted cooperation, and
declare
:
Since its establishment, the Organisation has
successfully passed
through a historic stage of institutional and conceptual development and
established itself as a widely recognised and dynamically developing multilateral association
, the role of which in international and regional affairs continues to grow steadily.
Over the past quarter-century,
a solid legal foundation for the SCO’s activities and effective mechanisms of cooperation
have been established, while strategic directions for its further development have been defined.
The Member States will continue their efforts
to further consolidate the SCO
in implementing the tasks and achieving the goals set out in
the SCO Development Strategy until 2035
.
***
Key provisions
:
✍️
The Member States
•
Reaffirm their unwavering commitment to the universal norms and principles of international law enshrined in
the UN Charter
and stress the need to strengthen the UN’s role as a guarantor of international peace and security, sustainable development, and the establishment of a representative, democratic and just
#MultipolarWorld
. They are making a growing contribution to collective efforts to improve the global system of governance.
•
Advocate ensuring sustainable international peace
and
call for joint efforts
to counter traditional and emerging security challenges and threats.
• Expressing concern over growing information security risks,
strongly oppose the militarisation of the information and communications technology (ICT) sphere
and the creation of threats to critical information infrastructure.
•
Reaffirm the need to ensure the safe and responsible use of artificial intelligence technologies
, including the inadmissibility of their use to deliberately cause harm to human life and health, as well as to the national security, territorial integrity and sovereignty of states.
•
Oppose unilateral coercive measures that run counter to
the UN Charter
and other norms of international law
, as well as the rules and principles of
the World Trade Organization
, and which undermine international security interests, including food, energy and humanitarian security, and adversely affect the global economy.
•
Support reform of the international financial architecture
aimed at increasing the representation and strengthening the role of developing countries in the governing bodies of international financial institutions, including
the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development
and
the International Monetary Fund
.
• Underscoring the significance of the 200th anniversary of
Leo Tolstoy’s
birth and the 100th anniversary of
Chingiz Aitmatov’s
birth,
support initiatives to hold SCO events in 2028 dedicated to the memory of these outstanding writers
, who made an invaluable contribution to world literature, the advancement of humanistic values and the strengthening of cultural and humanitarian ties between peoples.
