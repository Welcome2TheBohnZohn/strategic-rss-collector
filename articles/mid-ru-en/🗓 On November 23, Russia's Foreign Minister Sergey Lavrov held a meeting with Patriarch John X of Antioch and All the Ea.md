# 🗓 On November 23, Russia's Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov held a meeting with Patriarch John X of Antioch and All the East

Published: 2026-11-23 12:59:57

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33558

🗓
On November 23, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
held a meeting
with
Patriarch John X of Antioch and All the East
, who is in Moscow to attend events marking
the 80th birthday of Patriarch Kirill of Moscow and All Russia
.
They exchanged views on the situation in the Middle East,
with a focus on the Christians living there.
Russia
reaffirmed its commitment to working closely with the Orthodox Church of Antioch on a range of issues
and its readiness to provide Christian communities with the assistance they need as the military and political situation deteriorates in their historic homelands.
☝️
They also discussed
the Western-inspired church schism in Ukraine
and
the ongoing persecution
of the canonical Ukrainian Orthodox Church.
***
🎙
Sergey Lavrov's
opening remarks
:
💬
We are pleased to see you, especially at a time when your See is at the heart of
major developments in the Middle East
.
In Syria, efforts to bring the nation together continue. We very much hope that, despite numerous provocations,
genuine national unity can be achieved
with the participation of all ethnic and religious communities.
In Lebanon,
the situation remains deeply worrying and shows no sign of improvement.
Much to our regret, the Israeli army’s continued operations are increasing the risk of another military confrontation, which we want to do everything we can to avoid. We believe it is essential to
preserve the structure of the Lebanese state
and safeguard the delicate balance among its three main communities.
In
our discussions
with Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa,
we have welcomed his commitment to a similarly inclusive approach.
One example is the formation of the People’s Assembly, where Orthodox Christians serve, including in leadership positions. Orthodox Christians are also represented in the Government of Syria.
🤝
Your Beatitude,
we greatly value the efforts of your Church and your Patriarchate to foster unity across the region
and preserve its historical roots.
I would welcome your assessment of the situation and any ideas we could actively support
to help restore stability throughout the region
.
