🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of the Central African Republic Faustin-Archange Touadera on the sidelines of
the Third Russia–Africa Summit
(Moscow, October 30, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin
: I am pleased
to meet with you again
. First of all, let me once again thank you for taking part in
the Third #RussiaAfrica Summit
. You have attended all three summits, including those
in Sochi
and
St Petersburg
.
🤝
We appreciate your personal commitment to strengthening the friendly ties between our countries
. In December, we will mark
66 years
of diplomatic relations.
There are promising opportunities
, yet to be fully realised,
to expand trade and economic ties
in energy, geological exploration, transport and logistics. In September, the intergovernmental commission established this year held
its first meeting
.
We also provide humanitarian support to your country
. A growing number of your citizens are applying to study at Russian universities. For the 2026/2027 academic year, we have increased the government-funded scholarship quota for students from your country
from 85 to 100 places
.
An association of graduates of Soviet and Russian universities
has been established in your capital, which also hosts
the
Russian House cultural centre
and
a Centre for Open Education in Russian
. Mr President, we greatly appreciate the attention you give to these matters.
👉
Russia and the Central African Republic share common positions on the international stage
. We appreciate your
support
for Russian initiatives at the UN and other multilateral platforms
You are always a welcome guest here
. I am pleased to have this opportunity to discuss current issues on the agenda we have been developing together for many years.
🇷🇺
