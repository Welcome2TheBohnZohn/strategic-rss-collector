Interview
with Russia's Permanent Representative to the UN Vassily Nebenzia for the programme
“America with Valentin Bogdanov”
on
Rossiya 24
(New York, September 20, 2026)
Key points
:
#Ukraine
Negotiating efforts are underway
. Our American partners genuinely want to help,
unlike the Europeans
.
I think it would be fair to say that I sense fatigue over this issue at the UN, including among Kiev’s sponsors
. They feel obliged to keep it on the agenda as a matter of ritual, holding at least one meeting a month so that no one forgets.
The UNGA High-Level Week
is approaching. Naturally, the centrepiece of the French presidency will be a meeting on Ukraine with Zelensky taking part.
But I think
everyone has grown rather tired of this subject
.
#Iran
The impression is that the US administration does not know how to get out of
this conflict
without losing face.
Goals were announced, but
they have not been achieved
.
I
ran has not proved to be another easy victory for them
, as elsewhere, and has demonstrated its capacity to resist. It is a vast nation, an entire civilisation.
#MultipolarWorld
The unipolar world
– a world of
colonial and postcolonial domination
–
is disappearing
. New centres of power are emerging, as we know.
We hope that out of this chaos a new world will emerge
, with new relations between states, different from those we see today.
#UNSG
We would welcome
the election of a Secretary-General
who understands what it means to serve as the Organisation’s chief administrative officer – someone who, in accordance with Article 100 of
the UN Charter
, demonstrates
the highest levels of competence and impartiality
, along with the other qualities expected of an international civil servant.
☝️
This must not be someone
who is in anyone’s pocket
, i
ncluding Russia’s
. The Secretary-General must be objective and serve all Member States.
