# ⚡️ Russian MFA statement on the adoption of the UNGA resolution Necessity of Ending the Economic, Commercial and Financial Embargo Imposed by the US against Cuba

Published: 2026-10-30 18:00:47

⚡️
Russian MFA statement on the adoption of the UNGA resolution Necessity of Ending the Economic, Commercial and Financial Embargo Imposed by the US against Cuba
(October 29, 2026)
On October28,
the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
adopted
the resolution Necessity of Ending the Economic, Commercial and Financial Embargo Imposed by the US against Cuba
, traditionally tabled by the Republic of Cuba.
The resolution received
130 votes IN FAVOUR
, with 17 against and 25 abstentions.
✅
Russia
, together with the overwhelming majority of the international community,
consistently supported the document and
delivered a statement
to that effect
.
We are aware that this time
many countries came under intense
pressure aimed at forcing them to withhold their support for the resolution. However, despite this pressure and blackmail, the vote once again demonstrated
the international community’s virtually unanimous and unequivocal condemnation
of
the unlawful and inhumane embargo
against Havana.
It is clear that the US is failing in its attempts to turn unilateral sanctions into an instrument for coercing governments it disapproves of.
Cuba provides a particularly clear illustration of the pernicious nature of this destructive practice, which the White House employs to pursue its short-term objectives, in defiance of the consolidated position of the overwhelming majority of the international community and in complete disregard of the universally recognised principles and norms of international law.
The unprecedented escalation
this year of economic and military pressure on Havana
, compounded by an aggressive energy embargo,
has pushed ordinary Cubans to the brink of survival
. We are witnessing unlawful pressure against Cuba that has gone far beyond all accepted standards of human morality and decency.
The ultimate aim of such flagrant interference in the internal affairs of a sovereign country is clear to all
: to destabilise its domestic political situation, bring about regime change and impose on the Cuban people a different path of development dictated from abroad.
The inclusion of the Caribbean republic on
the US list of State Sponsors of Terrorism
serves the same purpose.
This designation remains one of the main obstacles facing Havana in conducting foreign trade and carrying out financial transactions
.
☝️
At the same time, it seems that those who are doing everything in their power to overthrow the current regime fail to appreciate
the devastating consequences
this could have:
a mass exodus of Cubans to neighbouring countries
in the event of socioeconomic collapse, thereby triggering a major migration crisis in those countries.
We admire the resilience and courage of the Government and the people of Cuba
. Despite the enormous pressure exerted by Washington, the Republic continues to demonstrate its principled and unwavering commitment to defending its sovereignty and independence, pursuing an independent foreign policy and keeping its socioeconomic situation under control.
❗️
Russia’s principled position on the resolution remains unchanged.
Together with the friendly people of Cuba and the overwhelming majority of members of the international community,
we will continue to demand the earliest possible, complete and unconditional lifting of the embargo against the Island of Freedom
.
Russia will always stand shoulder to shoulder with Cuba
in opposing unlawful unilateral restrictive measures and interference in the internal affairs of sovereign states.
