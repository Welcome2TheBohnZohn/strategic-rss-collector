# ⚡️ On October 13, the Belgian Ambassador to Russia, Michel Gerebtzoff, was summoned

Published: 2026-10-14 06:31:18

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32748

⚡️
On October 13, the Belgian Ambassador to Russia, Michel Gerebtzoff,
was summoned
to the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
The Belgian diplomat was informed that
the Russian Side resolutely rejects
the increasingly frequent and wholly unfounded assertions by the Kingdom’s military and political leadership concerning growing hybrid and sabotage threats, alongside direct military threats to Belgium and Europe purportedly emanating from Russia.
It was emphasised that
the overtly aggressive statements
by certain representatives of the Belgian authorities, who deliberately portray Russia as an enemy, contribute to a further escalation and increase the risk of the confrontation they are stoking spiralling out of control.
❗️
The Belgian Side was urged to consider the consequences of such statements and to rein in its anti-Russian rhetoric
, which is fuelling a further rise in tensions on the European continent.
