🎙
Opening remarks
by President of Russia Vladimir Putin at a meeting with President of Mozambique Daniel Francisco Chapo on the sidelines of
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
(Moscow, October 27, 2026)
💬
Vladimir Putin:
We greatly appreciate your personal, active participation in
the #RussiaAfrica Summit
.
It is important that Mozambique was also represented at the highest level at
the previous Russia–Africa forums
, which, in my view, laid
a solid foundation for developing Russia’s ties with the entire continent.
Today, we worked productively together at
the 8th Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit
.
🇷🇺
🇲🇿
We fully share your commitment to strengthening the multifaceted ties between our countries.
Russia and Mozambique enjoy longstanding bonds of friendship and cooperation dating back to
the Mozambican people’s struggle for independence
. Last year, we marked a significant milestone –
the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations
.
Political contacts and interparliamentary ties are developing
. In September, delegations of Mozambican parliamentarians took part in the election observation mission for
Russia’s State Duma elections
.
📈
Trade and economic cooperation is growing stronger.
Bilateral trade
nearly doubled
in January-August this year. Our
Intergovernmental Commission
is actively working and held its fourth meeting in Moscow recently, on October 1.
We traditionally enjoy close ties in education
. This year, we welcomed Mozambican civil servants for training for the first time. The quota for students from your country to study at Russian universities at the expense of the federal budget
continues to grow steadily
.
We will also continue to provide humanitarian assistance to Mozambique.
As you know, this spring,
Russia’s EMERCOM delivered humanitarian aid
to help address the aftermath of flooding caused by Cyclone Gezani.
🤝
Our cooperation at multilateral international platforms, above all the UN, is constructive.
We are grateful for Mozambique’s support for many Russian initiatives and its rejection of outrageous Russophobic actions.
