# 🎙 Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s remarks in response to a media question about real threats to freedom of navigation for civilian vessels and global food security

Published: 2026-10-13 19:02:47

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32743

🎙
Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova’s
remarks
in response to a media question about real threats to freedom of navigation for civilian vessels and global food security
(October 13, 2026)
:
❓
Question: On October 6, crude oil tanker Aframax RIO en route from Novorossiysk to India was attacked by unmanned surface vessels in the Black Sea off the coast of Sochi. The Zelensky regime is deliberately escalating tensions in an attempt to disrupt navigation in the Black Sea and to undermine international economic cooperation. Why has the problem exacerbated at this particular point and is there any chance to resolve it by agreeing on an energy ceasefire or negotiating a new Black Sea deal?
💬
Maria Zakharova:
Indeed, the Kiev regime is deliberately carrying out such attacks in order to destabilise global energy and food markets.
This is just
another terrorist attack carried out by the Ukrainian military against civilian merchant vessels
, which is apparently intended to compensate for the failures of Ukrainian militants on the battlefield. Since January, the Ukrainian military has carried out
over 250 indiscriminate strikes against vessels carrying crude oil, grain, and other non-military cargoes
. <...>
In response to Kiev’s relentless terrorist activity
, the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation
are implementing
a comprehensive set of systematic measures to suppress the Ukrainian military combat capabilities
, including by striking vessels and port infrastructure involved in military logistics.
I would like to emphasise that, as long as only the Russian-flagged vessels and foreign vessels operating in the interests of Russian economic operators were targeted in the Black Sea,
freedom of navigation was not widely covered either by the West or the relevant international organisations
. <...>
Our
counterattacks
which targeted exclusively facilities linked to the Kiev regime’s military-industrial complex have come as
a painful blowback for Kiev
. This explains why Zelensky and his handlers are seeking ways to mitigate the intensity of Russian military strikes. Their objective is clear: under the formal pretext of concern for global food security,
they seek to secure a protected maritime route for supplying the Ukrainian military with weapons
and to create yet another instrument for exerting political and economic pressure on Russia. <...>
As we know from our own experience, concluding memorandums and declarations without establishing effective implementation mechanisms is unlikely to meaningfully improve the situation.
Kiev regularly violates the agreements it signs
. <...>
Consider, finally, the Black Sea Grain Initiative of 2022-2023, which
NATO countries used as a cover for supplying weapons to the Ukrainian military
. <...>
Those now calling for achieving normalised navigation in the Black Sea are overlooking the key point: the current food security challenges are
a direct consequence of Kiev’s actions
, while its sense of impunity and the absence of consequences stem from the West’s irresponsible policies. <...>
Russia is a responsible and, without any exaggeration, critical participant in the global food market.
Ukraine’s share of that market, by contrast, should not be overstated
. To give just two examples, Russia accounts for around 26 percent of global wheat exports, compared with just 6 percent for Ukraine, and 20 percent of barley exports, compared with 5 percent for Ukraine.
☝️
Moreover, up to 70 percent of Ukrainian agricultural products go
primarily to the EU and other Western countries
that are by no means poor. <...>
Our friends and partners from the Global Majority countries know that
Russia has never shied away from substantive dialogue on promoting global food security
.
We are convinced that, at this stage, the most effective immediate measure would be to exert collective international pressure on Kiev as the main source of instability. This could produce more tangible results in the near term.
