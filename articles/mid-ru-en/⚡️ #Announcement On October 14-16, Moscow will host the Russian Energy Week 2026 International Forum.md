# ⚡️ #Announcement On October 14-16, Moscow will host the Russian Energy Week 2026 International Forum

Published: 2026-10-13 08:31:52

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32706

#Announcement
⚡️
On October 14-16, Moscow will host
the Russian Energy Week 2026 International Forum
.
#REW
is Russia’s leading international platform for discussing
key issues in the development of the fuel and energy sector
, interregional cooperation and international energy partnerships.
Over the years,
the Forum
has established itself as
one of the most prominent international venues
for discussing pressing issues facing the fuel and energy industries.
This year’s programme
features
more than 30 panel sessions and discussions with over 100 speakers
, organised into seven thematic tracks:
•
Oil Market Transformation
•
Gas Market Development
•
Investment in the Electric Power Industry
•
New Energy
•
Environment and Energy Efficiency
•
The Energy of International Cooperation
•
Education and Workforce Development
💬
Deputy Prime Minister of the Russian Federation
Alexander Novak
:
The goal of
Russian Energy Week 2026
is to make dialogue within the industry as practical as possible.
The business programme
is structured around the key segments of the fuel and energy sector: oil and gas, electric power, renewables and technological modernisation.
The technology and industry strategy tracks have also been expanded, giving participants clear guidance for planning and implementing solutions. Particular attention will be paid to international projects and investment.
🌐
The REW 2026 agenda includes oil production in the Arctic
,
economic and energy development in the digital age, the “3+3” platform and energy cooperation in the South Caucasus
.
Particular attention will be paid to
#RussiaASEAN
energy cooperation
. A meeting of
the UN Economic Commission for Europe
(
#UNECE
)
working group on digitalisation in energy
will also take place.
💬
Minister of Energy of the Russian Federation
Sergey Tsivilev
:
Russian Energy Week provides a platform for developing solutions that are crucial to the industry’s operations.
I would also like to highlight
Youth Day
. It offers young professionals an opportunity to speak directly with industry leaders, put forward their ideas and explore career prospects in the fuel and energy sector.
This is the generation that will shape Russia’s energy industry in the decades ahead.
