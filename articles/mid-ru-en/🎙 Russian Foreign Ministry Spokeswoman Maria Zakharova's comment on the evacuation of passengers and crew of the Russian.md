# 🎙 Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's comment on the evacuation of passengers and crew of the Russian vessel Professor Molchanov from the Spitsbergen archipelago

Published: 2026-09-09 04:02:05

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31935

🎙
Russian Foreign Ministry Spokeswoman Maria Zakharova's
comment
on the evacuation of passengers and crew of the Russian vessel Professor Molchanov from the Spitsbergen archipelago
(September 8, 2026)
💬
On September 8,
a group of passengers and crew members
(69 people in total) from the Russian research vessel Professor Molchanov,
arrested on September 2 in Spitsbergen
at the request of Ukrainian companies,
crossed the Russia-Norway border and is now en route to Murmansk.
The Russian nationals were evacuated with assistance from the Norwegian authorities through
close coordination
between
State Trust Arktikugol
,
Embassy of Russia in Oslo
and the Russian Consulates General in
Spitsbergen
and
Kirkenes
.
☝️
The Russian nationals have received the necessary assistance and support.
Following their border crossing, the Murmansk regional authorities are helping arrange their onward journey to Murmansk.
