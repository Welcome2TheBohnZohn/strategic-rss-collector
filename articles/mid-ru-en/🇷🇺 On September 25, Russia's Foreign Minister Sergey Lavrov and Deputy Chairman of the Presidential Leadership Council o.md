🇷🇺
🇾🇪
On September 25, Russia's Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
and Deputy Chairman of the Presidential Leadership Council of Yemen
Dr. Abdullah Al-Alimi Bawazeer
held a meeting
in New York on the sidelines of
the High-Level Week of the 81st Session of the UN General Assembly
.
During the meeting, the Sides discussed
current issues related to the development of the traditionally friendly Russian-Yemeni relations
.
They reaffirmed their
mutual commitment to further strengthening cooperation
, including maintaining close coordination at the UN and other international platforms.
The Sides also held
an in-depth exchange of views on developments in and around Yemen
amid escalating tensions in the Persian Gulf and the Red Sea.
The Russian Side emphasised
the importance of stepping up international support for efforts to establish a sustainable intra-Yemeni national dialogue under UN auspices
, aimed at finding mutually acceptable compromises on outstanding issues and bringing the positions of the opposing sides closer together.
