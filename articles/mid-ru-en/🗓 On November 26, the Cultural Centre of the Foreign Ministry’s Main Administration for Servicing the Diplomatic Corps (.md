# 🗓 On November 26, the Cultural Centre of the Foreign Ministry’s Main Administration for Servicing the Diplomatic Corps (GlavUpDK)

Published: 2026-11-27 01:02:16

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/33674

🗓
On November 26, the Cultural Centre of the Foreign Ministry’s Main Administration for Servicing the Diplomatic Corps (GlavUpDK)
hosted a presentation
of the investment, economic and tourism potential of the
Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area – Yugra
, attended by Foreign Minister
Sergey Lavrov
, Governor of the Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area – Yugra
Ruslan Kukharuk
and representatives of the diplomatic corps, the business community, and Russian and foreign media.
In his remarks,
Sergey Lavrov highlighted
the achievements of the Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area – Yugra
and the experience it has gained through its
active engagement with foreign partners
, particularly in the extraction and supply of
energy resources and in tourism
. He made special mention of the rich
cultural and historical heritage
of this constituent entity of the Russian Federation.
During the event, those present were introduced to the Area’s natural
diversity and distinctive traditions
, its unique experience in preserving the way of life of the indigenous peoples of the North and the projects under way in the region, including in
digital technologies
.
The participants expressed confidence that the presentation would provide
further impetus
for deepening cooperation
and diversifying the international ties of the Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Area – Yugra, above all with
friendly countries
, in the economic, educational, scientific and cultural spheres.
