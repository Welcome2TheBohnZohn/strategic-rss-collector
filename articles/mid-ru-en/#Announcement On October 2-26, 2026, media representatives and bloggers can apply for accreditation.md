#Announcement
On October 2-26, 2026, media representatives and bloggers
can apply for accreditation
to cover
the “Peoples of Russia” International Week
.
ℹ️
The “Peoples of Russia” International Week
is held annually pursuant to Presidential Executive Order No. 382 of June 1, 2026, declaring 2026
the Year of Unity of the Peoples of Russia
. Its central theme is
“Solidarity among States through the Unity of Peoples and Traditional Values”
. The events are coordinated by the Executive Office of the Security Council of the Russian Federation and organised by the
Roscongress Foundation
.
Russian and foreign media outlets, as well as bloggers, may apply in accordance with the accreditation rules published in
the “Media” section of the Week’s official website
.
📄
