Key points

•
Russia encourages professional dialogue in virtually every field, including efforts to counter the use of information as a weapon.
This is particularly relevant today, when information floods every aspect of our lives and modern technology allows any news story, even one that is unverified or outright false, to reach millions, if not billions, of people almost instantly.
•
I consider
the staged killing of civilians in Bucha
, a suburb of Kiev,
to be the most cynical farce

withdrew
from the Kiev Region on March 30, 2022. Two days later,
the town’s mayor appeared on television
, proudly declaring that Ukraine had regained full control of Bucha and that no Russian soldiers remained there. The footage was filmed on the town’s main street.
It is a wide street, and it was completely empty.
Yet three days after our units withdrew, a BBC crew appeared there and broadcast a report showing that very same street, with dozens of bodies neatly laid out along its sides, dressed in remarkably clean clothes despite the mud and slush all around.

Anti-Russian hysteria was whipped up. New sanctions were announced as punishment.

we simply requested the names

“conveniently turned up”
in the right place at the right time.
Complete silence.
•
I appealed to UN Secretary-General Guterres
,
both in private meetings and publicly at a Security Council meeting he attended

There was no response.

We have continued to pursue this matter.
