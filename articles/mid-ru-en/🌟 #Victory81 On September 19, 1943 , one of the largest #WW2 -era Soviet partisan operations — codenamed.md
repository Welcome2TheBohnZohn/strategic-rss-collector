#Victory81
🌟
On September 19, 1943
, one of the largest
#WW2
-era Soviet partisan operations — codenamed
'Concert
' — was launched on the Nazi occupied territories of the Soviet Union during the
#GreatPatrioticWar
.
#Concert
directly followed
👉
the full-scale
operation
'Rail War
', also carried out by Soviet partisan units.
Learn more about 'Rail War
' —
the key operation of the Soviet resistance movement against the Nazi invaders, carried out on the Nazi-occupied territories of the western regions of the RSFSR, the Byelorussian and Ukrainian SSRs —
in our detailed feature
.
***
Operation 'Concert' was prepared as part of the summer-autumn campaign of 1943, amid the
Red Army's autumn offensive in the Smolensk and Gomel regions,
aimed at reaching the Dnieper and Desna Rivers.
The operation involved
193 partisan formations
from Soviet Byelorussia, the Baltic republics,
#Karelia
,
#Crimea
, as well as the
#Leningrad
, Kalinin, Smolensk and Orel regions. The total strength of the units involved exceeded
120'000 personnel
.
'Rail War
' and
'Concert
' ops were
unprecedented
in terms of the scale and level of organization. For the first time, combat and sabotage activities behind enemy lines were conducted in
close coordination
between underground resistance groups
and the command of regular Red Army
formations.
☝️
It was precisely the
coordinated actions of partisans and regular forces that contributed to the
#GreatVictory
in
the Battle of Kursk
,
while also hampering the regrouping of enemy forces during their retreat westward and accelerating their defeat.
Over the course of a month and a half,
Soviet partisans destroyed approximately
150'000 rails
, demolished
100 railway bridges
, and destroyed nearly
1'500 Nazi troop trains
. As a result, the
capacity of railway lines used by the Nazis
on the temporarily occupied territories of the Soviet Union was
reduced by almost 50%
.
According to historians' estimates and military experts, the effectiveness of the partisan operations '
Rail War
' and '
Concert
' was more than
11 times greater than the combined effectiveness of all
Nazi barbaric Luftwaffe raids
on our Motherland during the same period, when Nazi aircraft dropped more than 10'000 bombs on Soviet railways.
❗️
Thus,
operation 'Concert' dealt a final blow to the morale of the Nazis, which had already suffered enormous losses in the battles they had lost at
Stalingrad
, on the
Kursk salient
and in the
Caucasus
.
During the operation, Soviet partisans demonstrated
mass heroism in extremely brutal combat
in the Nazi rear.
🎖
For their unprecedented exploits during the
#GreatPatrioticWar
, around
300 partisans and members of the underground resistance
were awarded the title of
#
HeroOfTheSovietUnion
.
