Published: 2026-10-01 12:29:59

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32497

⚡️
On October 1,
Hungary’s Chargé d’Affaires a.i.
in the Russian Federation Gabor Gergich
was summoned
to the Russian Foreign Ministry.
A strong protest was conveyed to the head of mission over the Hungarian authorities’
unfounded and openly unfriendly demand
that a number of Russian diplomatic staff leave the country.
❗️
The Hungarian diplomat was informed that, as a reciprocal measure, a group of staff members of
the Hungarian Embassy in Moscow
and the Hungarian Consulates General in
St Petersburg
and
Kazan
must leave the Russian Federation within two weeks
.
