🎙
Remarks
by Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov at the opening of an exhibition marking the 55th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between Russia and the United Arab Emirates
(Moscow, 27 November 2026)
💬
Sergey Lavrov:
The UAE is
a truly strategic partner
for Russia. Our cooperation rests on equality, mutual respect and regard for each other’s national interests. It offers
a good example
of how states can work together as equals in a
#MultipolarWorld
.
The close friendship between our countries goes back many years. On 8 December 1971, just days after the formation of the UAE was announced,
the Soviet Union recognised the new state and expressed its readiness to establish diplomatic relations with it
.
Since then,
our ties have developed steadily
, and the pace of cooperation across a wide range of fields has picked up year by year.
The Declaration on Strategic Partnership between the Russian Federation and the United Arab Emirates
, signed in Moscow on 1 June 2018, provides
a firm foundation
for this cooperation.
It is hard to overstate the importance of the regular contacts and mutual trust between our Presidents,
Vladimir Putin
and
Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan
. On 29 January, the UAE President paid
another visit to Moscow
, where the two Leaders
discussed the full range of bilateral issues in detail
, along with key issues on the global and regional agenda.
Our Foreign Ministries coordinate closely
, including in international forums. Other government agencies also regularly exchange delegations. I recently took part once again in the prestigious
Sir Bani Yas Forum
in Abu Dhabi, where
I held talks
with my colleague and friend
Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan
.
📈
Our trade and economic ties are growing rapidly
. Last year,
bilateral trade rose by 56%
, reaching an all-time high.
The Russia-UAE Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic and Technical Cooperation
plays the leading role in coordinating efforts to expand bilateral cooperation. Its next meeting is due to take place in Moscow
on December
8
. The Russia-UAE Business Council has also proved effective in forging closer ties between our business communities.
I should also mention that
the UAE is one of the most popular overseas holiday destinations for Russians
. In 2025, Russian citizens made
more than 2 million trips to the UAE
, while Emirati citizens made
more than 70,000 trips to Russia
. I am confident these figures will continue to rise. With visa-free travel in place, we have every reason to expect more tourists to travel in both directions.
🤝
We will continue to work energetically alongside our Emirati friends to take our cooperation further in every field.
