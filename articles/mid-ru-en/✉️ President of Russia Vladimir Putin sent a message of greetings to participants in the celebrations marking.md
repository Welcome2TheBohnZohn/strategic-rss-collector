Published: 2026-09-08 18:03:03

✉️
President of Russia Vladimir Putin sent
a message of greetings
to participants in the celebrations marking
the Day of the Languages of the Peoples of the Russian Federation
(September 8, 2026)
💬
It is symbolic that this new holiday, timed to coincide with the birthday of
Rasul Gamzatov
– a great son of Dagestan and of our multinational Fatherland – is being celebrated for the first time during
the Year of Unity of the Peoples of Russia
.
Our country is home to
representatives of nearly 200 peoples and ethnic groups,
living together in friendship and harmony
.
Their languages, distinctive traditions and customs are our common heritage and Russia’s true wealth.
This is why the preservation of our ethnocultural and linguistic diversity is a state priority. Specialised textbook series are being developed, literature in national languages and schools of literary translation are being supported, while much-needed educational, information, outreach and creative projects are being implemented.
☝️
I would like to emphasise that, while preserving our distinctive languages, it is important to remember that the Russian language serves as a unifying bond for all our peoples.
It was instrumental in shaping Russia as a single multinational state and, for centuries, has facilitated the mutual enrichment of cultures and fostered traditions of good-neighbourliness and mutual understanding among our peoples.
I am confident that the current celebrations will take place in a festive atmosphere and will be long remembered by participants and guests alike.
🤝
I wish you every success and all the very best.
VLADIMIR PUTIN
