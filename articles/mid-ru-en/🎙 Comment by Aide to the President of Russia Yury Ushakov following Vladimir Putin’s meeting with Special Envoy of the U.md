# 🎙 Comment by Aide to the President of Russia Yury Ushakov following Vladimir Putin’s meeting with Special Envoy of the US President Steven Witkoff

Published: 2026-10-21 17:00:23

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/32891

🎙
Comment
by Aide to the President of Russia Yury Ushakov following Vladimir Putin’s meeting with Special Envoy of the US President Steven Witkoff
(October 21, 2026)
💬
Yury Ushakov
: As always,
today’s discussion
was
substantive and frank
, lasting almost three hours.
Following up on
Vladimir Putin’s
telephone conversation
with
Donald Trump
on October 9, the exchange of views continued on
ways to achieve a lasting and sustainable peace
.
Russia's President
praised the mediation efforts
of the US Administration, Donald Trump personally and his special envoys to find mutually acceptable solutions.
☝️
It is important to note that the possible resumption of trilateral talks involving representatives of Russia, the US and Ukraine was discussed in specific terms
.
As you know,
the Ukrainian regime itself thwarted their planned resumption
immediately after
the State Duma
elections by launching massive strikes on the main voting day against Moscow, other cities, polling stations and election commission staff directly, some of whom were killed.
The situation was reviewed again,
taking into account, among other things, the signals received from the Americans.
Our President informed Steve Witkoff that
we are
, in principle,
ready to resume these trilateral talks between November 10 and 19
, focusing on
👉
the central issue: building a security framework for Russia and Ukraine after the conflict ends
. The American representative said that President Trump would undoubtedly welcome this decision with great satisfaction.
Vladimir Putin asked Steve Witkoff to convey
his gratitude to US First Lady Melania Trump
for her
humanitarian efforts
, particularly in
reuniting children with their families
, as well as for her mediation in resolving
the grave humanitarian situation in the Russian town of Alyoshki
in the Kherson Region.
***
There was also an exchange of views on
the situation in the Middle East
, primarily, of course,
developments surrounding Iran
. Russia reaffirmed its readiness to continue helping to find
political and diplomatic ways
to resolve the Iranian issue.
🇷🇺
🇨🇳
🇺🇸
Naturally, today’s discussion also touched on a matter attracting considerable attention worldwide:
the possible first-ever meeting of the leaders of Russia, China and the US
during
the APEC Summit in Shenzhen
, China, this November.
None of the three capitals
– Moscow, Washington or Beijing –
has rejected this idea
.
Our Chinese friends
,
as hosts of the APEC Summit
,
are already undertaking preparations for it
. If circumstances are favourable, they will, of course, include this meeting in the programme of events in Shenzhen.
We put forward
several practical suggestions
regarding the substance of the possible trilateral talks.
