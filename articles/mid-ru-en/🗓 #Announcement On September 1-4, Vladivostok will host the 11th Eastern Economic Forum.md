# 🗓 #Announcement On September 1-4, Vladivostok will host the 11th Eastern Economic Forum

Published: 2026-08-31 13:59:58

Source: https://t.me/MFARussia/31711

#Announcement
🗓
On September 1-4, Vladivostok will host
the 11th Eastern Economic Forum
(
#EEF
).
The Forum
is a leading platform for
advancing the development of the Russian Far East
and
expanding international cooperation across the Asia-Pacific Region
(
#APR
).
Participants will gain
direct access
to partners, investors and regulators,
opportunities to enter new markets
and
a chance to help shape the development agenda
of Russia’s largest macroregion.
#EEF2026
will feature
a plenary session, thematic sessions, business dialogues, roundtables and expert discussions
bringing together government, business and academic representatives from
more than 80 countries.
📄
The business programme
is structured around five thematic tracks
covering international cooperation, technological development, improving quality of life, attracting investment and implementing infrastructure projects:
• Living and Working in the Far East;
• The Far East – A Pole of International Cooperation;
• Technologies of the Present and the Future;
• Mechanisms for Attracting Private Investment;
• Logistics and Infrastructure for Economic Growth.
✍️
President of Russia Vladimir Putin’s
greetings
to the participants, organisers and guests of
the 11th Eastern Economic Forum
(August 30, 2026)
:
“Over the years,
the Forum
has established itself as a platform for open and constructive dialogue among representatives of the business community, government authorities and experts.
It has become a unique venue for discussing strategic priorities for the development of the Far East and Russia as a whole, as well as for establishing and strengthening mutually beneficial ties with partners from the Asia-Pacific region in logistics, industry, energy and many other sectors of the economy”.
